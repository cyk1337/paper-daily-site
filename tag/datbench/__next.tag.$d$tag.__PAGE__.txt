a:T43a,An end-to-end curation pipeline that composes multiple levers instead of isolating one:
- Joint multimodal deduplication: removes redundancy across image-text pairs (not just text-only or image-only), targeting higher effective sample diversity.
- Joint image-and-text filtering: quality filtering based on learnability and alignment (e.g., caption-image consistency, OCR readability, low-information captions, noisy/watermarked images).
- Mixture design via target-distribution matching: adjusts the training mix to better cover capability axes (OCR/docs, charts, math, counting, spatial/3D, branding, captioning, VQA, grounding, etc.), mitigating the “generic web caption” bias.
- Synthetic data: both task-agnostic and task-specific generation to fill long-tail phenomena and to shape supervision for harder axes.
- Strict controls: fixed backbone/vision encoder (Qwen3 LM + SigLIP2), fixed compute and recipe; baseline uses the same source dataset without curation.
