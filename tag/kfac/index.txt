2) KFAC-SVD: starts from Fisher-weighted SVD where the loss increase is approximated by a second-order expansion with the Hessian approximated by the Fisher matrix, then uses a Kronecker-factored approximation F ≈ A ⊗ G to obtain a Frobenius objective in whitened coordinates (G^{1/2} W A^{1/2}). The key change is how A and G are estimated: token-wise statistics rather than sequence-wise aggregation. The motivation is that sequence-level aggregation yields low effective rank under limited samples (rank collapse), while token-wise accumulation increases statistical support and stabilizes the Kronecker factors, improving the truncated SVD fidelity.
