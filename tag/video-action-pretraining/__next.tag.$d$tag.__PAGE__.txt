9:T571,LingBot-VA 2.0 has two central pretraining components. The first is a semantic visual-action tokenizer. Instead of optimizing only VAE-style reconstruction, it aligns visual latents with a frozen visual foundation model and learns latent actions through self-supervision, placing world states and actions in a shared latent space. This lets unlabeled web video provide action-relevant supervision. The second component is a causal diffusion transformer. Conditioned on language instructions via cross-attention to a frozen pretrained language model, it predicts future visual latents and the latent actions connecting them. The causal next-latent objective is chosen instead of retrofitting a bidirectional video generator, because robot control unfolds forward in time. The backbone uses sparse MoE feed-forward layers to keep capacity while reducing active compute. The training objective adds multi-chunk prediction, supervising several future chunks at once so that the model learns trajectory-level dynamics rather than copying adjacent-frame appearance. For deployment, Foresight Reasoning runs future-latent prediction asynchronously with action execution and re-grounds predictions on the latest real observation through a learned forward-dynamics step.
