18:T481,The methodology is simple but well structured. First, the authors analyze failed trajectories from SLMs and frontier models using a shared failure taxonomy. This establishes that the dominant failure modes are similar across tiers—step repetition, weak verification, and specification violations—so the gap is mostly quantitative rather than qualitative. Second, they evaluate the three capability axes separately. For solvability, they use repeated sampling and report pass@k, with each attempt executed in a fresh environment and judged by the task verifier. For verification, they study outcome verification: given a pool of completed candidate trajectories, the model must select a successful one. For confidence, the model sees only the task description and outputs a verbalized probability of success, which is evaluated with overconfidence and Brier score. A useful detail is the distinction between process verification during execution and outcome verification after generation; the main verification experiment targets the latter.
