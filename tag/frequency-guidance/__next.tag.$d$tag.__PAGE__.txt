5:T77e,The method inherits the multi-time forcing formulation from Latent Forcing, but replaces the semantic auxiliary stream with a frequency-based one derived directly from the data. The main pixel branch remains standard linear flow-matching interpolation, so endpoints, trajectory, and compatibility with vanilla flow-matching pipelines are preserved. A separate auxiliary branch receives a low-frequency scratchpad extracted from the image, and its time schedule is advanced relative to the pixel branch so that it matures earlier and provides structural guidance during denoising. The key point is that frequency information is not imposed by redefining the flow coordinate; it is introduced as an earlier-maturing conditioning stream.

Two design choices matter. First, the frequency basis is learnable. Instead of fixing Haar, Laplacian, or another hand-designed basis, the paper learns a wavelet-packet analysis filter from data, regularized by sparsity and quadrature-mirror-filter constraints to obtain a sparse and approximately orthogonal basis. This addresses a real limitation of fixed bases: what counts as “low frequency” is otherwise determined by the designer rather than the data distribution. Second, the architecture is path-preserving. Unlike K-Flow, it does not move the process into a transformed amplitude space or reinterpret flow time as frequency scale. That preserves compatibility with standard flow matching and makes composition easier. The paper explicitly shows that a frequency stream can be combined with a DINO semantic stream, which highlights that forcing is modular at the representation level.

Methodologically, the contribution is not a new generative objective. It is a new interface for injecting generation order: soft frequency guidance through an auxiliary self-forcing stream, instead of a hard transformed-space constraint or an externally distilled semantic scratchpad.6:T5b5,From the available full-text content, the experiments support three main claims. Relative to Latent Forcing, it avoids dependence on a large pretrained semantic encoder by using a lightweight wavelet-packet-derived self-forcing signal.
