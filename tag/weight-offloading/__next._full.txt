19:T608,AcceptMoE is a verifier-side expert selector. It leaves the draft mechanism intact, but restricts the set of target experts eligible during verification of a speculative block.

The first component is commitment-weighted expert demand. For each draft position, the method uses an offline estimate of the probability that a node at that position will be committed to the final output. It multiplies this commitment probability by the target router’s natural top-k probabilities, then aggregates the weighted demand over the verification block. Positions likely to survive receive more weight; branches likely to be discarded receive less. This differs from MoE-Spec, which aggregates router mass more uniformly and requires a fixed expert budget B.

The second component is self-sizing. AcceptMoE always preserves the root token’s natural top-k experts as anchors. It then ranks non-anchor experts by weighted demand. The number of selected experts is derived from the effective rank of the demand distribution, computed as the exponential of entropy. Concentrated demand yields a smaller set; diffuse demand yields a larger set. This removes the need to tune a fixed budget for every model-task pair.

The third component is residency-aware pruning for offloading. Among selected nonresident experts, the method prunes low-demand experts first, subject to a rerouting budget and a minimum set size.
