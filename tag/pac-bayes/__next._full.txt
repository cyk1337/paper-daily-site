19:T4fe,D-RPC (Distillation through Reasoning Path Compression) builds and maintains a “reasoning path bank” (cookbook) of high-level canonical solution strategies.
Stage 1: initialize the bank by clustering teacher-solved seed problems by intent/strategy to extract reusable reasoning-path skeletons.
Stage 2: for each new training question, retrieve the most relevant path(s) from the bank and condition the teacher to follow the retrieved path when generating a detailed rationale. If the teacher produces a novel valid strategy not represented in the bank, it is buffered and periodically merged back, allowing the bank to grow.
Key positioning: unlike Buffer-of-Thought / ReasonFlux-style prompting methods, D-RPC aims to bake consistent structure into the student weights so no retrieval is needed at deployment; unlike student-side consistency regularizers, it directly constrains the teacher’s output distribution upstream.
They provide a PAC-Bayes generalization analysis that makes the bank-size trade-off explicit: smaller banks reduce supervision entropy but risk under-coverage; larger banks improve coverage but increase variance. A completion is any stronger consistent statement that extends the current one, and weakness is the number of such completions. Intuitively, a weak learned function leaves many compatible extensions and is less over-committed.
