1a:T945,方法名是 AdaPrefix-GRPO。核心设计有四步。第一，作者先分析 GRPO 信号与组内成功率 k/G 的关系。采用未做 std-normalization 的 centered advantage 时，正向学习信号随 p(1-p) 变化，在 p≈0.5 附近最大；这也是他们把目标 solve rate 设在约 50% 的原因。这里有一个细节：如果用按组标准差归一化的 advantage，极低成功率时单个正确样本的 advantage 会被放大，数值上更不稳定，而未归一化形式更适合把“中等成功率最有信号”这个结论直接落到控制目标上。

第二，作者把参考解前缀注入 prompt，作为 assistant message 的部分答案，让模型只补全后半段；前缀 token 不参与梯度，reward 仍按完整输出的最终答案计算。这样做的好处是实现层面几乎不改 trainer，只改数据管线和 loss mask。与直接做 trace SFT 不同，它没有把整条参考解当监督目标压给模型，而是只在条件分布上降低问题难度，保留了 RL 的探索空间。

第三，作者不是给每题固定一个 prefix ratio，而是做“全局基准前缀 + 每题静态偏移”的两级控制。训练前先做一次 cold-start calibration，根据 base model 在不同题上的条件成功率，给每个样本估一个 difficulty-dependent offset；训练时再维护一个全局 base prefix length，用 secant 或 binary search 这类 root-finding 更新规则，根据最近 batch 实际观测到的平均 k/G，把全局前缀往目标 solve rate 推。这样既避免逐题在线搜索的高成本，也比完全固定的 per-sample 前缀更能跟踪策略随时间的变化。

第四，训练后期把所有前缀 anneal 到 0，保证部署时模型不依赖提示拐杖。这个 annealing 很关键。如果一直带前缀训练，模型学到的是“条件补全参考解”的能力，而不是无辅助求解；逐步撤掉前缀，相当于把课程从 teacher-assisted completion 过渡到独立解题。

和并行工作相比，差别主要在控制论视角。已有 prefix-based RL 方法通常在训练开始前选几个固定前缀长度，要求 base accuracy 落在“还行”的范围；AdaPrefix 的主张是，非零信号不够，应该把样本尽量维持在接近 50% 成功率的高梯度区，而且这个点会随训练漂移，所以必须在线调节。1b:T8b1,The method, AdaPrefix-GRPO, has four key pieces. First, the paper analyzes how GRPO signal depends on the within-group success rate k/G. With unnormalized centered advantages, the aggregate positive learning mass scales like p(1-p), peaking near p=0.5. That is why the controller targets roughly a 50% solve rate. The choice to avoid std-normalized advantages matters here: normalization can make rare successes numerically dominant when p is tiny, while the unnormalized form preserves a cleaner and more stable signal-shape argument.

Second, the method prepends a correct prefix from a reference solution as an assistant message and asks the model to complete the rest. Gradients are masked on the prefix tokens, while reward is still computed from the final full answer. This is an important design choice. It lowers conditional difficulty without turning the whole trace into a supervised target, so it preserves more of the exploration behavior that plain trace SFT tends to suppress.

Third, the controller is not fully per-sample online and not fully static either. The paper uses a two-level scheme: a global base prefix length plus a fixed per-sample offset derived from a one-time calibration. During training, the system observes the realized batch mean k/G and updates the global base prefix using a root-finding style rule such as secant or binary search, pushing the observed solve rate toward the target. The per-sample offsets account for persistent difficulty differences, while the global term tracks the non-stationarity induced by policy improvement.

Fourth, all prefixes are annealed to zero by the end of training, so the deployed model solves problems unaided. This is crucial. Without annealing, the policy may become good at conditioned continuation of partially solved traces rather than independent problem solving.

Relative to concurrent prefix-based RL work, the distinction is the control perspective. Prior methods typically choose a few fixed prefix lengths so that the base model has a “reasonable” conditioned accuracy. AdaPrefix argues that merely making the signal non-zero is not enough; one should keep examples near the high-signal regime, and that regime drifts during training.1c:T77f,The experiments focus on hard math and are explicitly run under matched training FLOPs, which is important because otherwise gains could be explained by extra sampling or longer training. Baselines include vanilla GRPO and a fixed-prefix variant. The headline result is that AdaPrefix-GRPO more than doubles pass@1 over vanilla GRPO on held-out hard problems, while also beating the fixed-prefix baseline. At the same time, pass@16 does not collapse, suggesting the method is not simply trading diversity for a narrow pass@1 gain. The paper also reports that the gains grow as model size shrinks, which matches the mechanism: smaller models have a larger dead zone, so converting impossible examples into learnable ones yields larger marginal benefit.

Several ablations are especially informative. Targeting an intermediate solve rate works better than aiming too low or too high, supporting the p(1-p) analysis. Fixed prefixes help early but drift toward the too-easy regime as the policy improves, reducing learning signal; the adaptive controller keeps the observed batch solve rate near the target. Annealing prefixes to zero is necessary for strong unaided test-time performance. The one-time calibration cost is treated as model-level preprocessing rather than run-specific training cost, since it can be reused across runs on the same model and dataset.

A useful quantitative anchor from the paper is that vanilla GRPO solves only about 35% of problems at pass@16 on the hard split, indicating a substantial dead zone in the training distribution. AdaPrefix’s benefit comes from moving a meaningful fraction of that region into a non-degenerate regime where group-relative learning signal exists. The excerpt does not expose the full parameter table or every absolute number, but the evaluation setup appears careful about compute matching, model-size stratification, and the no-prefix deployment constraint.1d:T4dc,Start with the Introduction and Section 4.1. For figures, prioritize the plots showing how solve rate changes with prefix length and the training curves showing batch-mean k/G being regulated near the target. Those are the core validity checks.
