9:T486,The paper connects MH acceptance ratios to Bradley–Terry (BT) choice models. Under BT, the odds of preferring x over y equal exp(u(x)-u(y)), which matches the unnormalized density ratio needed by MH for a Gibbs target. The obstacle is that the algorithm cannot query the comparison probability itself—only draw Bernoulli preference samples. The authors prove an impossibility result: any fixed-budget plug-in estimator of the acceptance ratio (estimate win probabilities then substitute into MH) cannot implement the oracle acceptance rule and can arbitrarily distort the stationary distribution. Pref-MH avoids estimating probabilities and instead designs a randomized accept/reject mechanism whose marginal acceptance probability exactly matches the Hastings ratio implied by the preference odds (plus proposal correction). With this construction, detailed balance holds in expectation, yielding an exact stationary distribution while using only a fixed number of binary comparisons per transition.
