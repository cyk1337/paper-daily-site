9:T4d4,Two core modules. First, a Sorting-and-Truncation (S&T) pipeline: the intractable combinatorial global rank optimization is reduced to scoring all singular components across every weight matrix, sorting them, and truncating to a total rank budget. Each component receives a product score: (1) local singular energy ratio, i.e. normalized squared singular value, measuring structural contribution to reconstructing the original weight matrix per Eckart-Young-Mirsky; (2) global functional importance, proxied by layer-wise input-output cosine similarity computed on a small calibration set. Under pre-norm residual structure and scale-invariant RMSNorm, semantics are encoded purely in vector direction — high input-output cosine similarity implies the layer transform is either near-identity (singular values ≈ 0) or linearly correlated with input features (effective rank ≈ 1), justifying a lower assigned rank. Second, Rank-Preserving Fine-Tuning (RPFT): the pre-allocated rank k of each decomposed module is split into a frozen part (k - k_LoRA) and a tunable part (k_LoRA).
