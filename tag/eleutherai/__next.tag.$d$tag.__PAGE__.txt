b:Ta9a,Bergson 的核心设计是把数据归因拆成一条可组合的数据管线，而不是把每种方法写成彼此隔离的脚本。管线大致包括：训练或加载目标模型、收集训练样本梯度、可选的 Hessian/Fisher 近似、梯度归一化与预条件、随机投影压缩、建立磁盘索引、对查询样本做 top-k 影响检索，或者直接在线流式打分。这样的拆法有几个好处。

第一，它统一了 influence-style 方法的实现接口。库里支持 K-FAC、EK-FAC、经验 Fisher、autocorrelation 等 Hessian 近似，也支持 Adam/Adafactor 二阶矩、unit norm 等 normalizer。很多近年的经验发现是，影响函数效果往往不只取决于 Hessian 近似，还强依赖梯度归一化和预条件方式。Bergson 把这些组件显式模块化，方便做 ablation，而不是把方法名当黑盒。

第二，它把存储和查询问题当成一等公民。大模型归因的瓶颈常常不是算一次梯度，而是如何保存、压缩、索引、检索海量 per-example 或 per-token 梯度。Bergson 支持 on-disk gradient store，按模块存储，可选 attention head 级拆分，支持多种精度；查询时可用精确 top-k，也可接 FAISS 做近似最近邻。对于训练集极大、但查询集合固定的场景，作者还提供 on-the-fly scoring：不保存全量梯度库，而是流式扫描训练数据，只累计对预定义 query 的影响分数，以空间换可扩展性。

第三，它覆盖了从近似到精确的训练轨迹归因。论文强调三种此前缺少开源实现的方法：MAGIC、SOURCE、TrackStar。MAGIC 是对完整训练过程做可微分回传，属于精确 unrolled differentiation。作者实现了文献中的 O(N log N) 计算、O(log N) 磁盘 checkpointing 方案，并额外提出一个 O(N) 计算、O(√N) 磁盘的新折中。SOURCE 则是在少量 checkpoint 上重建训练轨迹，做近似 unrolled differentiation。TrackStar 是可扩展 influence approximation，结合经验 Fisher 与优化器状态做归一化。把这三类方法放在同一库里，意味着研究者可以按预算和精度需求选择：想要更准，就走 MAGIC；想要更便宜，就走 influence/TrackStar；想在两者之间折中，就用 SOURCE。

第四，系统层面支持 replicated 和 sharded 并行，目标规模到 405B 参数。这里的意义不是单纯“能跑更大模型”，而是很多归因行为只在大模型上才出现，小模型 proxy 往往失真。库里还强调可复现性和可观测性：每一步产物都序列化，配套 YAML 可重建整条管线，这对归因研究尤其重要，因为不同预处理和近似细节会显著改变结果。c:T479,Its main contribution is infrastructure. In pretraining work, many questions about data quality, duplication, memorization, and the origins of reasoning eventually reduce to one practical question: which training examples mattered for this behavior? That question is often approximated with lexical overlap or embedding similarity, but the paper explicitly argues that such signals are poor proxies for causal influence. Bergson matters because it puts several major attribution families into one scalable framework: checkpoint-based influence-style methods, approximate multi-checkpoint unrolled differentiation, and exact backpropagation through training via MAGIC. This has two concrete benefits. First, researchers can compare methods under the same model, query set, and data pipeline instead of inheriting incomparable engineering choices. Second, it pushes attribution from a small-scale analysis tool toward something usable in large-model training workflows. For people working on data mixture, filtering, continual pretraining diagnostics, or synthetic data quality, that is more useful than yet another isolated attribution algorithm.d:Tc5d,The core design choice is to represent attribution as a composable pipeline rather than a collection of disconnected scripts. The pipeline includes training or loading the target model, collecting per-example gradients, optionally applying Hessian or Fisher approximations, normalizing or preconditioning gradients, compressing them with random projections, building an on-disk index, and then either retrieving top-k influential examples for a query or scoring influence on the fly. This decomposition is important for several reasons.

First, it unifies influence-style methods under a common interface. The library supports K-FAC, EK-FAC, empirical Fisher, and autocorrelation-based Hessian approximations, as well as Adam and Adafactor second-moment normalization, unit normalization, and identity variants. In practice, attribution quality often depends not only on the Hessian approximation but also on how gradients are normalized and preconditioned. Bergson exposes these as modular components, which makes ablations and method comparisons much cleaner.

Second, it treats storage and retrieval as first-class problems. For large models, the bottleneck is often not computing a gradient once, but storing, compressing, indexing, and querying massive per-example or per-token gradient collections. Bergson provides on-disk gradient stores organized by module, with optional attention-head splitting and support for multiple precisions. Queries can use exact top-k search or FAISS-based approximate nearest-neighbor retrieval. For cases where the training set is huge but the query set is fixed, the library also supports on-the-fly scoring: instead of materializing a reusable gradient database, it streams through the training data and accumulates influence scores only for predefined queries, trading storage for scalability.

Third, it spans the full accuracy-cost spectrum of trajectory-based attribution. The paper highlights open-source implementations of three methods that previously lacked them: MAGIC, SOURCE, and TrackStar. MAGIC performs exact differentiation through the training process. The implementation includes the checkpointing strategy from prior work with O(N log N) compute and O(log N) disk, and adds a new trade-off with O(N) compute and O(√N) disk. SOURCE reconstructs training from a small number of checkpoints and performs approximate unrolled differentiation. TrackStar is a scalable influence approximation that combines empirical Fisher structure with optimizer-state-based normalization. Putting all three in one library means researchers can choose based on budget and fidelity: MAGIC for higher faithfulness, TrackStar or related influence methods for lower cost, and SOURCE as a middle ground.

Fourth, the systems layer supports both replicated and sharded parallelism, with a stated target scale up to 405B parameters. That matters because many attribution phenomena only become meaningful at large scale; small-model proxies are often misleading. The library also emphasizes reproducibility and observability: each stage emits serialized artifacts and a YAML configuration sufficient to reconstruct the full pipeline.e:T5ae,This is primarily a systems and tooling paper, so the experiments focus on scalability, method coverage, and case studies rather than a single benchmark score. Second, method coverage: it provides the first open-source implementations of MAGIC, SOURCE, and TrackStar, while also supporting common influence-style Hessian approximations and normalizers.
