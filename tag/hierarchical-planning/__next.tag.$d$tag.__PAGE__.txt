9:T483,HIPIF trains an agent end to end around explicit subgoals. A conventional ReAct-style policy conditions each decision on the entire accumulated trajectory. HIPIF instead keeps a compact working context composed of the current subgoal, the necessary local interaction history for that subgoal, and a folded summary of completed progress. The model proposes an initial subgoal, executes actions under that subgoal, and decides when the subgoal is completed or should be terminated. Once a subgoal ends, its execution history is folded into a progress summary, and the model proposes the next subgoal. To make this stable, the method adds hierarchical reflection for judging subgoal completion, correcting subgoal content, and deciding whether to continue execution or replan. Training uses GRPO for subgoal-centric decisions and introduces subgoal-oriented process rewards for both subgoal generation and within-subgoal execution.
