18:T4ea,Glide is a guided, layerwise non-uniform hybrid attention scheme combining sliding-window softmax attention with a linear recurrent aggregation path.

Motivation via measurement:
- They sweep softmax-to-linear ratios across layers and observe strong depth dependence: early layers suffer large accuracy drops when linearized; deeper layers tolerate linearization.

Mechanism:
- Partition layers into early / middle / late stages.
- Early: full softmax (δ = 0).
- Late: full linearization (δ = w).
- Middle: partial linearization parameterized as δ = α·w with a single scalar α ∈ [0, 1].

Key design choice:
- Reduce a high-dimensional per-layer configuration problem to a 1D knob (α), making the search/tuning and deployment much easier.
- Designed to integrate with existing retention-based hybrid architectures (e.g., Liger, LoLCats), rather than requiring a fully new model from scratch.

Difference vs prior work:
- Uniform hybrid methods apply the same policy across layers; Glide allocates softmax budget where it matters (early layers) and linearizes where redundancy is higher (late layers).
