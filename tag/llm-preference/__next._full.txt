17:T4e9,The approach is a three-way alignment between (i) corpus exposure, (ii) an external popularity proxy, and (iii) model judgments.
1) Full-corpus exposure measurement: the authors index the complete OLMo training corpora (pretraining, midtraining, long-context) with Infini-Gram. The combined data is ~27TB / 7.4T tokens; indexing is sharded (128 shards) and yields ~50TB of indexed artifacts, enabling exact entity/alias counting at scale.

3) External popularity signal: Wikipedia pageviews are used as a real-world popularity proxy.
4) Model elicitation: two protocols on OLMo-3-7B and OLMo-3.1-32B—Direct (ask for popularity) and Pairwise Comparison (which entity is more popular). The paper treats pairwise comparison as a more stable behavioral readout, less sensitive to calibration and free-form generation artifacts.
