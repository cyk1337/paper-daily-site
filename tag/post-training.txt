1a:T8bc,方法核心是 CROP：Counterfactual Relevance for On-Policy Distillation。流程分三层。第一层，离线为每个原始 prompt 构造一个 matched triplet：原始输入 x、语义等价的 paraphrase x_para、以及只改动一个关键任务条件的 counterfactual x_cf。这里的设计意图很明确：paraphrase 用来估计模型对表述改写的敏感度，counterfactual 用来估计模型对任务语义变化的敏感度。第二层，在学生当前策略生成的同一条 rollout y 上做重打分，而不是让不同 prompt 各自重新采样响应。也就是固定原始 rollout 的前缀，分别计算在 x、x_para、x_cf 条件下，各 token 位置的下一词分布差异。这样做的好处是把比较集中在“同一 realized trajectory 上的条件分布变化”，减少由不同采样路径带来的噪声。第三层，用 counterfactual sensitivity 减去 paraphrase sensitivity，得到 token 级 relevance score，再在 batch 级固定预算下做 hard selection，只对得分最高的一部分 sampled tokens 施加教师蒸馏损失，teacher target 和 OPD 目标本身不变。

分布差异的具体实现上，论文使用 top-K JSD，并带 residual bucket。这样做是为了避免全词表 JSD 的高成本，同时又不至于因为只看 top-K 而丢掉剩余概率质量。这个设计比直接比较 argmax 或 top-1 logit 更稳，因为 relevance 关心的是整个局部分布如何随语义变化而变化，而不是单一 token 是否翻转。

和最接近的 CREDIT 相比，区别主要有三点。其一，CREDIT 用 unrelated batch queries 做对比，语义变化和其他 nuisance factors 混在一起；CROP 用 paraphrase/counterfactual 配对，把“语义不变的表面改写”和“关键条件改变”分开。其二，CROP 在固定 rollout 上比较条件分布，强调 controlled contrast；其三，最终分数是 counterfactual sensitivity 减 paraphrase sensitivity，显式做了语义校准，而不是把任何输入敏感都当成高价值监督。论文还强调，CROP 不是在估计严格因果意义上的 token causal effect，而是在预算受限 OPD 里构造一个可操作、模型内部、对比受控的 relevance proxy。1b:T7e8,CROP, short for Counterfactual Relevance for On-Policy Distillation, builds a token relevance score from controlled prompt contrasts. For each original prompt x, the method constructs an offline matched triplet: the original prompt x, a meaning-preserving paraphrase x_para, and a counterfactual prompt x_cf that changes one task-relevant condition. The student first generates a rollout y from its current policy on the original prompt. CROP then keeps that realized rollout fixed and rescoring is performed under all three prompts on the same response prefixes. This is an important design choice: the comparison is over conditional next-token distributions along the same trajectory, rather than over independently sampled responses, which reduces variance from trajectory mismatch.

At each token position, CROP measures how much the model’s next-token distribution changes under the counterfactual prompt and subtracts how much it changes under the paraphrase prompt. Intuitively, sensitivity to the counterfactual captures dependence on task semantics, while sensitivity to the paraphrase captures dependence on superficial wording. The resulting score is a calibrated relevance proxy rather than a generic input-sensitivity measure. Tokens are then selected globally under a fixed batch-level budget, and only those selected sampled tokens receive the teacher distillation loss. The teacher target and the OPD objective remain unchanged; only the allocation of supervision changes.

Distribution shift is computed with a top-K JSD plus a residual bucket. This is a practical compromise: full-vocabulary divergence is expensive, but top-K alone can ignore tail mass. Compared with CREDIT, the closest prior work discussed by the authors, CROP uses controlled original/paraphrase/counterfactual triplets instead of unrelated batch queries, and explicitly subtracts paraphrase sensitivity from counterfactual sensitivity. That makes the signal more targeted to semantic dependence rather than generic prompt variation.1c:T5e7,The empirical setup includes two teacher-student settings evaluated within the same budgeted OPD framework. The headline result reported in the paper is that CROP improves aggregate performance by 1.92 and 2.96 points over the strongest non-CROP selector. For a token-selection method inside distillation, that is a meaningful gain.

The most convincing evidence comes from matched token-selection controls. Under the same supervision budget, selecting the highest-relevance tokens consistently outperforms random selection, while selecting the lowest-relevance tokens performs substantially worse. This matters because it validates the ranking induced by the relevance score itself, not just the end-to-end training recipe.

Ablations show that counterfactual sensitivity is the primary signal and paraphrase calibration adds further benefit. In other words, semantic sensitivity already identifies useful supervision locations, and subtracting paraphrase sensitivity removes positions that react to wording changes rather than task content. The paper also adapts CREDIT into the same selector framework for a cleaner comparison.

The main limitations are also visible from the setup. The method depends on constructing and validating prompt triplets offline, which introduces extra pipeline cost. It also assumes that a meaningful task-relevant counterfactual can be defined for the prompt. That assumption is easier to satisfy in structured QA-style tasks than in open-ended generation or long-form dialogue.1d:T871,方法分两部分：测量协议和缓解方法。

先看测量。作者在训练环中每 20 step 对 OOD evaluation set 做一次双 judge 评估：一个是训练时使用的 proxy judge，另一个是更强、且跨家族的 gold judge。这里“跨家族”很关键，目的是降低与训练 judge 同源偏差带来的假阳性。如果只是 judge 有固定偏差，gold 曲线应整体平移；而作者观察到的是 proxy 持续上升、gold 先升后降，这更符合 reward hacking 而不是静态偏差。实验用了两个独立 train→eval 配对：RubricHub-Medical → HealthBench-Hard，以及 RubricHub-Science → ResearchQA。

缓解方法是 Rubric Dropout。训练时对每个 prompt 的 rubric，随机丢弃一部分 criterion，再用剩余 criterion 计算 reward。这样策略在不同 step 面对的是不断变化的子 rubric，无法稳定利用某个固定 criterion，尤其是那些跨 prompt 重复出现的模板化 criterion，比如“结构清晰”“语言明确”。这个设计的优点是简单：只改 reward function 一行，不增加 judge 调用次数，只有一个超参数 f，即 dropout fraction。

关键实现点在于和 GRPO 的兼容。GRPO 依赖同一 prompt 的多条 rollout 之间 reward 可比较。如果每条 rollout 用不同子 rubric，group-relative advantage 就会被污染。作者因此采用 group-shared masking：同一个 rollout group 共享一张 dropout mask。附录里给出推导，说明在这种设置下 reward normalizer 会在 advantage 中抵消；在 group standardization 之前，dropout 主要表现为对期望 advantage 的缩放，以及对依赖单一 criterion 的更新起到方差正则作用。这个解释很重要，因为它说明 Rubric Dropout 不是在“加噪声碰碰运气”，而是在系统性削弱对单一可利用 criterion 的过拟合。

作者还把 Rubric Dropout 与 POW3R 风格的 criterion reweighting 做了对比。后者试图按训练 usefulness 重加权 criterion，但在这里反而更容易把优化压力集中到少数可利用 criterion 上，因此可能加剧 hacking。全文实验也支持这一点。1e:T866,The method has two parts: an in-loop measurement protocol and a mitigation.

For measurement, every 20 training steps the model is evaluated on an OOD benchmark using two judges: the training-time proxy judge and a stronger gold judge from a different family. The cross-family choice is important because it reduces the chance that the observed gap is just shared bias. If the proxy judge were merely biased, the gold curve would be shifted but not necessarily turn downward while the proxy keeps improving. The paper uses two independent train-to-eval pairs: RubricHub-Medical to HealthBench-Hard, and RubricHub-Science to ResearchQA.

The mitigation is Rubric Dropout. At each training step, a random fraction f of the rubric criteria is dropped before computing reward. The policy therefore never sees exactly the same rubric twice, which makes it harder to exploit any fixed criterion, especially generic templates repeated across prompts such as “well-organized” or “clear language.” The appeal is operational simplicity: one line in the reward function, one hyperparameter, and no extra judge calls.

The crucial systems detail is GRPO compatibility. In GRPO, multiple rollouts for the same prompt must be scored on a comparable basis. If each rollout were graded with a different sub-rubric, the group-relative advantage would be corrupted. The paper solves this with group-shared masking: one dropout mask per rollout group. The appendix shows that under this scheme the reward normalizer cancels out of the advantage, and before group standardization the main effect of dropout is to rescale expected advantage while acting as a variance regularizer against updates that rely too heavily on any single criterion. That makes the method more than just noise injection; it is a targeted way to reduce overfitting to exploitable proxy features.

The paper also compares Rubric Dropout with POW3R-style criterion reweighting. The argument is that reweighting can inadvertently concentrate optimization pressure on a smaller set of exploitable criteria, which may worsen hacking rather than reduce it. On the science setup evaluated on ResearchQA, the gold score drops by 22 points from its peak while the proxy score continues to rise. That is strong evidence that the standard rubric-RL recipe can over-optimize the proxy out of distribution.

In the fixed three-way comparison, the paper evaluates base training against Rubric Dropout with f = 30% and f = 50%.
