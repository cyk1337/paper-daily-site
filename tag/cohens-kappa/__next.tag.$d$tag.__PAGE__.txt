Second, it evaluates them on MT-Bench, JudgeBench, and RewardBench. MT-Bench contributes 2,391 pairwise comparisons with expert human labels; JudgeBench contributes 350 items across math, coding, creative writing, and analysis with objective correctness labels; RewardBench contributes 2,981 chosen-versus-rejected pairs with per-item position randomization. Third, it uses three protocols. The agreement protocol reports Cohen’s κ, Krippendorff’s α, and tie-excluded exact match. The consistency protocol runs each item independently 3–5 times with response caching disabled and with both AB and BA orderings, measuring test-retest reliability, self-consistency, and position flip rate. The bias-audit protocol uses paired AB/BA presentations and response-length analysis to estimate position and verbosity bias.
