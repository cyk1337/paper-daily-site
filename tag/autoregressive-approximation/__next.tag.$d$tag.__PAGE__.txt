For draft position t+i, the module takes the drafter hidden state and the embedding of the previous token, passes them through a SwiGLU MLP, and produces a modified hidden state. The verifier’s output embedding is then reused to produce q'(x_{t+i}|x_{≤t}, x_{t+i-1}). This gives a local autoregressive dependency without turning the drafter into a sequential model. To avoid sequentially applying the approximator across draft depth, the method uses a two-stage tree construction. First, it builds a top-M-ary tree using the original DFlash marginals. Second, it applies the AR-approximator in parallel over the nodes of this tree and then uses OPT-Tree selection to form the final draft tree under budget B. Since only M unique previous tokens appear at each depth, the modified distributions require M·γ parallel evaluations and preserve O(1) drafting depth.
