The first is a finite-precision transformer with polylogarithmic width and rightmost-unique hard attention. It emits reasoning tokens at the level of Word RAM instructions instead of simulating Turing-machine tape movements. For a t-step Word RAM execution with word size w = Θ(log(n+t)), the construction uses O(w^2) tokens per instruction, or O(t log^2(n+t)) tokens overall.
