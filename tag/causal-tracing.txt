19:T836,The core method adapts activation patching to the vision encoder and measures how much a single layer-token activation restores the final image-text similarity score. The procedure has three runs. In the clean run, the model sees the original image and produces a similarity score s_clean. In the corrupted run, the image is perturbed with Gaussian noise and yields s_corr. In the patched run, the corrupted image is used again, but one intermediate activation at layer l and token t is replaced by its clean counterpart, producing s_patch. From these three scores, the paper defines a causal contribution Γ(l,t), interpreted as the fraction of the clean-corrupted gap recovered by restoring that activation. This is stronger than attention visualization or gradient saliency because it measures functional recovery, not just correlation.

The second key step is to connect causal importance to spatial grounding. Since ViT tokens correspond to fixed image patches, each token is mapped back to its patch region Pt, and the overlap with the ground-truth object box B is measured using IoU(Pt, B). For each layer, the paper computes the correlation between Γ and IoU, denoted Corr_l. If Corr_l is high, causally important tokens tend to fall on the target region. If it is low, the model may still perform well while relying on evidence outside the queried object. This layerwise analysis is important because it asks whether spatial grounding emerges progressively through the encoder or never becomes causally dominant.

The full text indicates that the analysis starts with CLIP as a controlled setting and then extends to larger VLMs, multiple corruption settings, and different generations of visual encoders, including ViT-based and SigLIP-based systems. The paper also probes whether models preserve visual structure when appearance cues are removed. The answer is nuanced: visual cues are indeed used for structural understanding, but that does not imply localized causal grounding. This is a meaningful distinction from prior work that relies mostly on attention maps or attribution heatmaps.1a:T6d2,The clearest findings in the provided full content are threefold. First, on CLIP, the authors run token- and layer-level causal tracing over 100 samples and find only a weak relationship between causal contribution and target-box IoU. The qualitative conclusion is explicit: highly causal tokens often do not lie inside the target region. In other words, the activations that recover the image-text score are frequently outside the object referred to by the text.

Second, this is not presented as a CLIP-specific artifact. The same pattern is reported in larger VLMs from 2023 onward and under different corruption procedures. So increased model scale and stronger multimodal benchmark performance do not automatically produce better spatial causal alignment. The excerpt does not include the full numeric tables, but the textual claim is clear that the phenomenon persists across models.

Third, the paper studies structure understanding under reduced appearance cues and finds that models do use visual information to infer structure. That matters because it rules out the simplest interpretation that the models are ignoring the image altogether. The issue is subtler: they use visual evidence, but the evidence that is causally important is not necessarily localized where a human would expect it to be.

From an experimental-design perspective, the useful details are the layerwise Corr_l analysis, reporting mean and standard deviation across seeds, and comparisons across encoder families. When reading the full paper, it is worth extracting the actual correlation values, where the layerwise peaks occur, whether different corruption types change the conclusion, and whether SigLIP and ViT differ systematically in middle or late layers.1b:T595,Start with the definition of Γ in the method section. That is the central quantity: it measures how much of the clean-to-corrupted performance gap is recovered by restoring one activation. Then read the part that maps tokens back to image patches and computes IoU and Corr_l. This is what makes the paper a causal grounding analysis rather than a standard saliency study.

In the results, focus on three kinds of figures. First, token heatmaps on individual examples, which make the off-target causal tokens visually obvious. Second, layerwise Corr_l curves, which show whether spatial grounding emerges anywhere in the encoder. Third, cross-model comparisons, which tell you whether the effect is specific to CLIP or more general. If time is limited, the introduction, method, the main CLIP experiment, and the cross-model generalization section are enough to form a solid view in about 25 to 35 minutes.

The most interesting follow-up questions are practical. Would the conclusion hold under occlusion, patch dropout, or frequency-domain corruption instead of Gaussian noise? Does the same mismatch appear when the target is a generative VLM next-token logit rather than an image-text similarity score? And can region-level alignment losses, object-centric crops, or stronger visual discretization constraints materially increase Corr_l during pretraining? Those are the places where this paper connects back to model design.1c:Ta7d,技术路线分成三步，逻辑很完整。

第一步是把 locate-then-edit 框架尽量原样迁移到 MDM。作者比较了两类 AR 模型（LLaMA、Qwen）和两类 MDM（LLaDA、Dream），并尽量做 matched scale 对比。方法基础仍然沿用 ROME / MEMIT 的解释：Transformer MLP 的 down-projection 矩阵可视为线性关联记忆，last subject token 处的 up-projection activation 作为 key，value 编码 relation-object 信息；编辑就是在选定层的 MLP 权重上加入低秩或解析更新，使同一个 key 映射到新的 object 表征。对 MDM 的改动只限于适配 masked prediction，而不是重写编辑算法，这样实验结论更能回答“原有假设是否成立”。

第二步是做 causal tracing 和 edit-site sweep，区分“知识位置”和“编辑成效”两个问题。作者先用 causal tracing 看事实回忆对哪些层、哪些 token 位置最敏感，再系统扫描不同 token position 和 layer range 上的编辑效果。结果显示，即便 MDM 是双向建模、所有 token 都能看完整 subject，事实回忆最关键的位置仍然是 early-to-mid layer 的 last subject token MLP。这一点很关键，因为它说明 locate-then-edit 的位置假设并不是 AR 专属产物，而更像是 Transformer 在实体-关系检索上的一个稳定机制。

第三步是诊断并修复 MDM 的 multi-token breakdown。作者发现单 token target object 上，MDM 和 ARM 的编辑效果接近；但一旦目标 object 跨多个 token，MDM 性能明显下降。论文给出的解释很有说服力：标准 locate-then-edit 只在一个“理想状态”上拟合编辑，也就是模型在预测目标 token 时的某个内部表示。AR 解码是左到右的，生成时会自然经过这个被拟合的状态，所以编辑能生效。MDM 则从 fully masked 输出开始，经过多轮去噪，依次经历一系列不同的 partial-mask states；原始编辑更新没有针对这些中间状态做鲁棒化，因此多 token object 的后续 token 会在不匹配的状态分布下被生成，导致编辑逐步失效。

为了解决这个问题，作者提出一个简单修正：让编辑更新对去噪过程中的中间 partial-mask states 也保持有效。全文摘要没有给出全部公式细节，但从描述看，核心不是换新的编辑位点，而是在构造目标 value / 优化约束时，把多种 mask 状态纳入编辑拟合，使同一事实更新在不同去噪阶段都能维持一致方向。这个修正的价值在于，它直接对应了 MDM 与 ARM 的生成机制差异，而不是把失败归因于“MDM 不适合知识编辑”这种过宽泛的结论。1d:T431,This matters for pretraining research in three ways. The objective is to find the subset of components whose joint intervention optimizes the chosen metric.

The main challenge is combinatorial explosion. The proposed solution is to relax discrete subset selection into a continuous optimization problem.
