It predicts the target visual representation in continuous ViT-embedding space. Training follows a masked generative modeling setup: target ViT tokens are randomly masked, and a lightweight ViT-embedding decoder on top of the MLLM reconstructs the missing target embeddings from contextual hidden states. At inference, the planner progressively fills in the full target representation starting from masked tokens. The renderer is a DiT trained with flow matching over VAE latent tokens. It conditions on the planner’s semantic embeddings through cross-attention, also uses text features, and for editing tasks receives source VAE features to preserve low-level appearance and temporal consistency. The paper also introduces Segment-Aware 3D RoPE, which adds segment-index-dependent phase modulation to spatiotemporal rotary embeddings so that multiple visual sources can coexist in one sequence.
