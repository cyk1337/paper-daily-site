9:T453,The analysis considers two-task continual regression. After training on Task A, the model is at θ_A; Task B training starts from this point. The model is linearized around θ_A, and the Task-A prediction shift induced by Task-B training is derived in closed form: Δf_A ≈ -K_AB (K_BB + λI)^-1 r_B. K_AB is the cross-task NTK between Task-A probe inputs and Task-B training inputs, K_BB is the Task-B NTK, and r_B is the Task-B residual evaluated at θ_A. The important operational detail is that all terms are computed before Task-B optimization. In frozen-backbone linear-head PEFT, the model is exactly linear in the trainable parameters, so the predictor is exact up to numerical precision. For LoRA, adapters, and full fine-tuning, it becomes a local NTK approximation. The paper then decomposes the shift in the eigenbasis of K_AA and shows that the forgetting vector lies in the image of the Task-A Jacobian.
