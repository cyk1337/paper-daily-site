18:T419,The paper compares scalar PTQ against vector PTQ. Scalar quantization maps each weight to one of a few discrete levels; at 2 bits this representational set is too limited, and error-aware methods such as GPTQ-style objectives, SpinQuant, or QuaRot can still fail. Vector quantization maps a small weight vector to an entry in a learned codebook, which preserves correlations among dimensions that scalar grids discard. The study uses VPTQ and YAQA under a shared rotated frame. On the data side, calibration affects the Hessian used to weight quantization error, while recovery data is used for short-budget distillation after quantization. A useful engineering idea is to precompute Hessians for individual domains and sweep calibration mixtures by linearly reweighting those Hessians. Recovery freezes the quantized backbone and trains only quantization parameters, such as codebooks or scales, using a full-precision teacher with a mixture of cross-entropy and temperature-scaled KL distillation. This avoids the optimizer-state cost of full QAT.19:T4cb,The experiments cover six dense and MoE models from 3.8B to 32B, including Phi-4-mini, Llama-3.1-8B, Qwen3-30B-A3B, Qwen3-8B, OLMoE-1B-7B, and Qwen3-32B. Four results stand out. First, representation is the dominant factor at 2 bits: the tested scalar PTQ pipelines fall to near-random performance, while vector PTQ at about 2.3 effective bits retains 89%–97% of full-precision question-answering accuracy without training, using 39–304 times less compute than trained scalar methods. Second, generative degradation is model-dependent. QA retention is relatively stable, but HumanEval/code-generation retention ranges from about 45% for Phi-4-mini and Qwen3-8B to 81% for Qwen3-MoE. Third, calibration and recovery data interact. For Phi-4-mini, generation accuracy moves from 11.1% with web calibration to 45.7% with math calibration plus web recovery, and the best recovery data changes with the calibration choice across all three dense models studied. Fourth, the recommended low-budget recipe is vector representation, task-aligned calibration mixtures, and a short codebook-only recovery stage.
