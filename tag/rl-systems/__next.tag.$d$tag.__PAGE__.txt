9:T5cd,Arctic-SWE is a model–system co-designed RL framework built on a fully asynchronous pipeline:
- Asynchronous architecture: on a single 8×B200 node, 2 GPUs train the policy while 6 GPUs run independent vLLM inference engines that keep generating rollouts while training proceeds. Weights are refreshed after every update, so a single multi-turn trajectory can span multiple policy versions.
- Off-policy control: a max_off_policy_steps threshold bounds policy staleness; rollouts exceeding it are dropped.
- Consistency constraints: align training and inference engines (tokenization, numerical/logprob paths) and maintain consistent cached state across policy updates to prevent importance ratios from being corrupted by implementation mismatch.
- RL objective/optimizer: CISPO-style token-level policy gradient with importance weighting; the importance ratio is detached and clipped (bounded at 2.0) to reduce variance and stabilize learning under asynchrony.
- Debugging via overfitting: filter a small solvable pool (160 tasks where Qwen3.5-4B passes tests at least once in 4 tries) and train to sustained memorization; failures (early reward gains that later collapse, etc.) are treated as signals of feedback/execution loop issues.
