9:T441,QVAC Genesis III contains 191.43B synthetic STEM tokens across 19 domains and three difficulty levels, rendered in four styles: textbook, web article, QA, and dialogue. The pipeline samples seed passages from FineFineWeb, filters them with the Ultra-FineWeb quality classifier, and keeps roughly 500k high-quality seeds. QwQ-32B is then used to generate self-contained multiple-choice questions. A weak edge-scale student answers the questions, and an answer-extraction step decides whether the student matches the gold label. Wrong answers trigger a Failure Analysis pipeline, where a teacher model diagnoses the misconception and writes a targeted corrective explanation. Correct answers trigger an Option-Level reasoning pipeline, where the teacher explains the correct option and each distractor. This differs from standard synthetic textbook generation because the student response controls the form of supervision.
