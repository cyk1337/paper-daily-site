For each object token, the method records image-token attention for all 1,024 attention heads in LLaVA-1.5-7B and ranks heads by the drop in image attention around hallucinated object words relative to grounded ones. Second, the strongest candidates are screened by single-head ablation, measuring the change in hallucination-token log probability. This yields a fixed set of 32 heads spanning 19 transformer layers. Third, two interventions are applied only to this set: a head-sliced LoRA adapter on the corresponding Q/K/V head dimensions, enforced with gradient masking, and an inference-time grounding controller that penalizes generation when the selected heads attend weakly to image tokens.
