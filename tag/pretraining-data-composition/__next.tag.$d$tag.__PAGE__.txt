The architecture uses a learnable class token, pre-norm Transformer blocks, SwiGLU feed-forward layers, and no positional encodings because the input is a set. Four pretraining subsets are compared: QCD only, QCD plus res2p, QCD plus res34p, and the union of all three groups. The downstream task is 10-class jet classification on JetClass.
