3. 没有观察到 stereotype amplification。作者专门做了 stereotype direction 检验，结果显示量化没有把输出更系统地推向预设刻板集合。换言之，量化放大的不是“偏向某个敏感类别”的方向性偏差，而是“在有效答案空间里更集中地重复少数选项”的确定化倾向。

4. token entropy 上升，但 semantic entropy 下降。这个结果很有意思，也最容易被误读。若只看 token 分布，量化后表面上似乎更随机；但从实体选择、语义替代空间来看，模型实际上更集中。作者据此认为，量化带来的不是简单的 distribution sharpening，而更像是“语义备选减少，表面措辞变化增加”。这解释了为什么传统 token-level 指标可能漏掉部署行为变化。

1. At 8B, int4 reduces diversity. In practical terms, the model becomes more likely to repeat default answers.

2. This content-level concentration largely disappears by 14B and remains absent at 32B. Larger models show stylistic drift rather than a stable collapse in country or brand choice. This scale dependence is the central empirical result.

3. There is no evidence of stereotype amplification. The dedicated stereotype-direction analyses do not show quantization pushing outputs more strongly toward predefined stereotype sets.
