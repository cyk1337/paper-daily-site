18:T927,方法上不是提出全新量化格式，而是把近一年 NVFP4 训练 recipes 的关键设计轴拆开做 controlled comparison，并在此基础上拼出一个组合配方 Mosaic NVFP4。核心技术点集中在三块：

1）选择性高精度层（selective high-precision layers）
- 论文明确把它当作“必要条件”来检验：即便其它量化细节都优化，仍需要把一小部分敏感层（通常是 embedding / output / normalization / 或少数关键投影层，具体以实现为准）保留在更高精度（如 BF16/FP8）以避免规模上去后的不稳定与尾段质量差。
- 这里的关键不是“混精度”本身，而是它在 FP4 噪声很大的 regime 里承担了类似数值锚点（anchor）的作用：减少误差在深层堆叠与长训练中的累积。

2）反向量化：无偏梯度估计 + 降低 backward quantization error
- 论文把“梯度估计偏差会在长 horizon 累积”作为主线，比较了不同 backward-pass 处理：包括 MS-EDEN（Quartet II 提到的 unbiased estimator）与其它随机舍入/随机旋转类方案。
- 结论是：在“全层量化”的设定下，选择性地对梯度使用 MS-EDEN，再对其余 backward tensors 用 Four Over Six 的 scale 选择，能得到测试中最小的 loss gap。这说明：无偏并不需要对所有张量一刀切，成本/收益可以分层配置。

3）前向/反向 scale 选择与 1D/2D 权重量化的耦合
- 现有 recipes 的分歧点之一是：NVIDIA ’25 recipe 倾向 2D 权重量化以保持 forward/backward 权重表示一致；而 Quartet II / 随机旋转类方法更自然地配 1D 量化（沿 contraction 维度），并通过“forward 权重 dequantize 后再按 backward 维度 re-quantize”来近似一致性。
- 论文的 Mosaic NVFP4 选择把这些拼起来：在 backward 两个 GEMM 上都做 Random Hadamard Transform（降低量化误差/偏差的结构性相关），梯度用 MS-EDEN 保无偏，其余张量用 Four Over Six 做更好的 scale 选择，同时保留选择性高精度层。

整体上，这篇的创新不在某个单点技巧，而在于把“前向表示质量”和“反向无偏/低偏梯度”两条路线的冲突点（1D/2D、权重一致性、旋转的放置位置）用统一实验回答：哪些组合在 1T tokens 的尾段仍站得住。19:T6d6,Rather than proposing a new numeric format, the paper decomposes NVFP4 training into major design axes and evaluates combinations systematically, then proposes a composite recipe (“Mosaic NVFP4”). The approach has three pillars:

1) Selective higher-precision layers
Ablates whether leaving a subset of sensitive layers in BF16/FP8 (e.g., embeddings/output/norm or other critical projections depending on implementation) is necessary. The paper argues this acts as a numerical anchor in the FP4 noise regime, preventing instability and late-stage degradation that persists even with otherwise strong quantization tricks.

2) Backward pass: unbiased gradient estimation + reduced quantization error
The backward pass is treated as the main long-horizon risk: biased gradient estimators can accumulate over training. The study compares unbiased estimators (MS-EDEN from Quartet II) and related techniques, and finds that selectively applying MS-EDEN to gradients while using Four Over Six scale selection for remaining backward tensors yields the smallest observed loss gap when all layers are quantized.

3) Coupling between scale selection and 1D/2D weight quantization
Existing recipes disagree on 2D quantization (to keep forward/backward weight representations consistent) versus 1D schemes (more natural for rotation-based bias reduction). The paper highlights the practical workaround used by 1D schemes—dequantize forward-quantized weights and re-quantize along the backward contraction dimension—and builds Mosaic NVFP4 by combining: selective high-precision layers, Random Hadamard Transforms on both backward GEMMs, MS-EDEN for unbiased gradients, and Four Over Six scale selection for other tensors, while staying kernel-compatible.1a:T562,Key experimental choices are “medium scale + long horizon + unified stack”:
- Models: dense 8B and 30B-A3B MoE (Nemotron family). The dense setting includes a hybrid Mamba-Transformer variant (24 Mamba-2 layers, 4 attention layers, 24 FFN layers).
- Training horizon: up to 1T tokens, explicitly to surface late divergence.
- Data: phased blending (diverse data for the first 600B tokens, higher-quality thereafter).
- Ablation axes: selective high-precision layers; forward/backward scale selection; 1D vs 2D weight quantization; backward bias mitigation (random rotations/Hadamard, unbiased estimators); and their interactions.
- Reported outcome-level findings (directional from the provided full text excerpt):
  (1) omitting selective higher-precision layers can trigger instabilities and prevents full BF16 recovery at larger scale/medium horizons;
