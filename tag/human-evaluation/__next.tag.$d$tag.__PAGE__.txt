1) Measurement: the same writers produce political opinion paragraphs with and without AI assistance; writers can edit and finalize the assisted version. Independent reader panels then blindly rate the paragraphs on 29 socially salient perception dimensions. 2) Acceptability: the authors probe whether writers object to the induced persona shifts and whether disclosure about distortions changes adoption of AI suggestions.
3) Mitigation: using the large-scale paragraph–perception-rating dataset, they train reward models that score outputs for stance/persona faithfulness and use these rewards to steer the assistant’s suggestions.
