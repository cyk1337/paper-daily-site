9:T4a4,GAE uses a two-stage pipeline. In stage one, the DA3 geometry encoder is frozen, and a learned bottleneck is inserted between its four-level feature hierarchy and the original geometry head. The bottleneck compresses all feature levels into one latent and reconstructs the full hierarchy in a single pass. The geometry head remains frozen when decoding depth, camera rays, and point maps, which prevents the decoder from adapting around information lost during compression. An RGB head is trained from the same bottleneck, forcing appearance and geometry to share the latent. The latent is further shaped for generation through two constraints: token-wise alignment to co-located C-RADIO features improves semantic organization and smooth transport, while pairwise similarity matching to DINOv2 features restores relational and spatial structure. In stage two, the codec is frozen and a DiT-style conditional flow is trained in the standardized GAE latent space.
