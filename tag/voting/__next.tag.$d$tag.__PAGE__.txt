a:T409,The proposed method is Confidence Dynamic Gain, or CDG. Token-level confidence is defined using the KL divergence between the next-token distribution and the uniform distribution, so sharper distributions correspond to higher confidence. Each reasoning trace is partitioned into position-normalized bins. The method compares the average confidence near the tail with the average confidence near the head, producing a tail-minus-head confidence gain. During answer aggregation, CDG combines three signals: the number of traces supporting an answer, the mean confidence of those traces, and the dynamic confidence gain. This differs from prior confidence-weighted voting methods, which usually compress an entire trace into a static confidence score.
