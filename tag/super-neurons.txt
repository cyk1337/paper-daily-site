19:T44f,The proposed mechanism starts with causal self-attention. The initial token attends only to itself, while later tokens aggregate values from an expanding context. This averaging reduces the variance of later token representations, leaving the first token as a high-variance outlier. The attention output projection preserves this outlier, and the FFN then amplifies it through super-neuron activations. In SwiGLU FFNs, channel-sparse down-projections create a dimension disparity in the token representation. Residual connections and normalization layers propagate rather than fully remove this disparity. The distorted representation then dominates query-key dot products, making the first token a structural anchor. The causal claim is tested with two interventions: modifying attention masks to isolate aggregation effects, and increasing the variance of arbitrary token representations. Both can induce attention sinks at chosen positions.
