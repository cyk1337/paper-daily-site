9:T44f,The protocol represents each behavioral evaluation as a set of conditions with measured rates. A condition may be an MMLU subject in a sycophancy test, a decision-topic and demographic cell in a discrimination evaluation, or an agentic scenario. A prediction method outputs one rate per condition, and performance is scored by correlation and calibration on frozen splits. The paper varies three axes independently: information available to the model, from abstract descriptions to exact items and behavior history; the subject of the prompt, including “you,” a named model, and “capable AI agents in general”; and the question format, either direct rate prediction or forced choice. The crucial design choice is the subject control. If self-directed prompts perform no better than generic-agent prompts, positive correlations cannot be interpreted as privileged self-knowledge.
