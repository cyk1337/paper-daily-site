9:T50f,Two complementary, weight-frozen, inference-time interventions are studied as both control mechanisms and causal interpretability probes.

(1) Neuron-level steering: Following a LAPE-style selection (Tang et al., 2024), the method computes per-dialect activation probabilities for each MLP neuron (ReLU-on frequency) and measures selectivity via the entropy of the normalized across-dialect activation distribution (low entropy = selective). Neurons are selected if they are highly active for at least one dialect (top n%) and highly selective (bottom m% LAPE). During decoding, activations of target-dialect neurons are multiplicatively amplified (α), while MSA-associated neurons are suppressed (γ), optionally also suppressing competitor dialect neurons (γ_comp).

(2) Distributed direction steering: A dialect direction is extracted from contrastive dialect–MSA pairs in activation/residual space, then injected during inference to bias generation. This targets a global, distributed representation rather than a small neuron subset.

Key claim: dialect information is both sparse and distributed.
