18:T4e8,A controlled head-to-head comparison between two supervision channels with careful confound control.
- Filtered SFT: sample teacher solutions once, filter by final-answer correctness (and separately analyze completeness filtering), then train via token-level cross-entropy (behavior cloning).
- OPD: run student rollouts; at each step the teacher provides token-level supervision (distribution/score) on the student’s prefixes; train the student to match the teacher on those visited states (DAgger-style).
Key design choices:
1) Nested data budgets with fixed update counts per method to avoid “more steps” artifacts.
2) Training-time logging of token-role divergence and explicit completion diagnostics.
3) Error decomposition: separate performance gaps into (a) completion rate differences and (b) correctness conditional on completion.
4) Mechanism probing: analyze teacher behavior on student-generated prefixes and test five OPD variants to see whether the completion collapse is robust to objective tweaks.
A notable empirical claim is that the completion collapse is an attractor induced by the on-policy feedback distribution: starting from an SFT-initialized student, continued OPD quickly erases the previously learned completion behavior.19:T4c1,Setup: Qwen3-VL 2B student distilled from an 8B teacher; tasks are ChartQA and Geometry3K.
Main findings:
- Geometry3K (teacher fails to finish ~22% within the token budget): with 900 training problems, OPD reaches ~63% test completion vs ~76% for SFT, and trails SFT by 7.9 accuracy points (about -10.5 to -5.3). About 6.6 points of the gap come from instances where only one method produces a concluded answer.
- SFT → OPD regression: continuing OPD from an SFT-trained student drives the rollout termination/completion back to the plain-OPD level within ~40 training steps; final test completion ends around ~62%, close to zero-shot.
- ChartQA (more reliable teacher completion): OPD shows a small, non-robust data-efficiency edge. OPD with 300 questions gets 0.826 accuracy, within 1.4 points of SFT trained on 3,000 (0.840). Under a fixed SFT training-length protocol, OPD leads same-budget SFT by 2.7 points (per-seed interval roughly +0.6 to +5.8). With tuned SFT training length, the OPD advantage shrinks to ~1.1–1.5 points with intervals including zero.
