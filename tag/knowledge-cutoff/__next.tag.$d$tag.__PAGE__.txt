A valid answer must avoid facts from R_post. The diagnostic section shows that leakage is highly sensitive to prompt surface form and placement: explicit instructions such as “use only information available before date X” work better than implicit historical framing, and prefix constraints reduce leakage more than suffix constraints. The proposed method, TCFT, trains temporal critique rather than answer imitation. Given a query, a cutoff, and a candidate response, a teacher model produces a critique that identifies post-cutoff facts, explains boundary violations, and gives an admissibility judgment. A student model is fine-tuned on these critiques, learning to verify candidate answers against the cutoff.
