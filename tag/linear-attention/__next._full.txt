18:T97e,方法分三段。第一段是架构转换。作者从 Qwen3-0.6B-Base 出发，保留每 4 层中的 1 层原生 GQA，共保留 7 层，其余 21 层替换为 KDA。KDA 配置包括 16 heads、head dim 128、短卷积 k=4、q/k L2 norm、低秩 forget gate、beta write gate、gated RMSNorm、NoPE。这样做不是纯线性模型，而是混合结构：75% 层用 O(1) recurrent state，剩余层仍保留线性增长 KV cache。作者还详细分析了 forget gate 初始化，指出几乎不遗忘会让 recurrent state 爆炸，遗忘过强又会比随机还差，最终采用 g6 初始化，forget half-life 中位数约 6.3 tokens。

第二段是两级蒸馏。先做 layer-wise hidden alignment：每个 KDA 层对齐对应 teacher hidden state，teacher 冻结，误差跨层 detach，只训练 KDA 参数 193.6M，学习率 1e-4 到 1e-5，batch 为 8192 tokens/step。这个阶段把 CE 从 9.48 拉到 4.125，但 C-Eval 仍只有 25.5%。随后做 end-to-end forward KL distillation，在预训练文本上最小化 teacher-student next-token 分布的 forward KL，不混 CE；训练范围扩大到 KDA + 全部 MLP + LayerNorm，共 457.9M 参数，embedding、lm_head 和保留的 7 个 attention 层冻结。为适配单卡显存，作者实现了 chunked fp32 KL，避免物化全量 logits。7000 steps、总计 57.3M tokens 后，valid CE 到 2.9385，teacher 为 2.8108，gap 仅 +0.128 nats，valid KL 0.160，但 C-Eval 只有 28.8%。

Stage 1 is architecture conversion. From Qwen3-0.6B-Base, every fourth layer remains native GQA, leaving 7 full-attention layers and replacing the other 21 with KDA. The KDA stack uses 16 heads, head dimension 128, causal short convolution with k=4, L2-normalized q/k, low-rank forget gates, beta write gates, gated RMSNorm, and NoPE. This yields a hybrid model rather than a pure linear-attention model: 75% of layers use O(1) recurrent state, while the retained GQA layers still have linearly growing KV cache. A notable engineering point is gate initialization. The paper shows that near-total retention destabilizes the recurrent state, while excessive forgetting performs worse than random; their adopted g6 initialization gives a median forget half-life of about 6.3 tokens.

Stage 2 is two-step distillation. The first step is layer-wise hidden-state alignment: each KDA layer is trained against the corresponding teacher hidden state, with the teacher frozen and errors detached across layers. Only KDA parameters are updated in this phase, 193.6M trainable parameters total, at 8,192 tokens per step. This reduces CE from 9.48 to about 4.13, but C-Eval stays at chance. The second step is end-to-end forward-KL distillation on pretraining text. The objective is pure forward KL between teacher and student next-token distributions, without mixing in CE. Trainable parameters now include KDA, all MLPs, and all LayerNorms, 457.9M in total, while embeddings, tied lm_head, and the 7 retained attention layers remain frozen. To fit a single 32GB GPU budget, they implement chunked fp32 KL instead of materializing full logits. After 7,000 steps over 57.3M tokens, validation CE reaches 2.9385 versus the teacher’s 2.8108, a gap of only +0.128 nats, with validation KL 0.160, yet C-Eval is still only 28.8%.

Stage 3 is diagnosis and repair. The diagnostic is a four-permutation multiple-choice test: 161 clean four-option questions are constructed from the training corpus with zero benchmark overlap, and each question is evaluated under four cyclic rotations of option labels. A content-following model should track the correct option as labels rotate; a model that simply forgot should fail roughly uniformly. Instead, the converted model predicts “A” 81.06% of the time, and 106 of 161 questions keep the same predicted label under all four rotations. The average margin on the correct option is negative, -0.117 nats. This points to label-interface collapse rather than uniform forgetting. The repair stage then runs 1,000 steps of completion-only KL on teacher-verified MCQ permutations, poetry, and translation QA, explicitly targeting the question-option-answer interface. Second, layer-wise hidden alignment reduces CE from 9.48 to roughly 4.13, but C-Eval remains around the 25% random baseline, demonstrating that hidden-trajectory matching is insufficient for restoring decision behavior.
