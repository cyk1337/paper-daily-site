b:T547,Let z be a latent reasoning trace and y the final answer segment. The base model induces an answer marginal p(y|x)=\sum_z p(z,y|x). The method defines an inference target that sharpens this marginal: q_\alpha(y|x) \propto p(y|x)^\alpha for \alpha>1, amplifying answers with broad support across many plausible traces.

A key result is that for integer \alpha, the powered marginal admits a multi-trace decomposition: p(y|x)^\alpha can be expressed via products over multiple independent trace samples. This enables a practical approximation with ordinary AR models:
1) sample K full completions (traces + answers);
2) discard the idea of selecting one completion; instead, decode a single answer whose token probabilities are aggregated across the K traces in a way motivated by the sharpened-marginal objective (intuitively akin to token-level product/voting, but derived from the marginal target rather than an ad-hoc heuristic);
3) optionally apply a sequential importance sampling (SIS) correction to better match q_\alpha, at extra cost.

Contrast to power sampling: power sampling sharpens p(z,y|x) at the sequence level, favoring individually likely transcripts and making alternative traces compete even when they support the same answer.
