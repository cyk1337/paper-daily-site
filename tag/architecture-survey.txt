1) Unified formalization: MoE as a sparse combination of expert functions where routing selects an expert set K_t per token. The survey highlights the dual objective—grow total capacity while bounding per-token active compute—while noting practical deviations (shared experts, attention/embedding overhead, heterogeneous widths).
2) Four control planes forming a closed loop:
- Expert Topology: expert granularity and structural choices (incl. shared paths), slow-changing.
- Routing: token-level discrete decisions (top-k, routing freedom, visibility constraints).
- Balance: windowed load statistics n_i and feedback knobs (router bias, auxiliary loss strength, capacity), plus non-loss mechanisms like runtime replication and expert placement.
- Expert Parallelism (EP): mapping experts to devices and scheduling communication/compute (all-to-all, fan-out, kernel shapes), which constrains feasible routing.
