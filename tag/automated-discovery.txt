A watermark uses pseudorandom vectors U derived from a context hash and a private key, and applies a logits transformation f(U, p_t) to produce a watermarked distribution. Detection recovers token-level scores U_{a_t, token_t} at retained positions, deduplicates repeated seed-token pairs, and relies on those scores being i.i.d. under the null hypothesis. The autoresearch harness isolates the agent, lets it propose and implement schemes, and submits them to a private verification pipeline.
