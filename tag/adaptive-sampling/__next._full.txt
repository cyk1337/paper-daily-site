19:T430,Formulates adaptive sampling as a finite-horizon MDP over rounds of candidate generation and majority-vote aggregation.
- State: constructed purely from the current set of extracted final answers. Features include sorted top-K answer counts, total sample count |D_t|, and entropy of the count vector as a proxy for convergence/dispersion.
- Actions: discrete choices—stop (return current majority vote) or sample k_l additional candidates in parallel.
- Reward: correctness of the final aggregated answer as positive reward, with penalties for additional samples (compute) and additional rounds (latency). Implemented as a weighted sum and interpreted as a Lagrangian relaxation of maximizing accuracy under compute/latency budgets.
- Policy: a small 4-layer MLP trained with RL; runs on CPU; wraps around any black-box LLM sampler.
