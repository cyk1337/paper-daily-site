9:T66b,Ask-to-Distill (A2D) treats a prompt as a distribution elicitor: it determines which prefixes the student visits, hence where teacher token distributions become available for OPD.

Two-stage pipeline:
1) Train a prompt synthesizer before OPD.
- Initialize the synthesizer from the student.
- Optimize it with RL while keeping the scoring student and the teacher frozen.
- Crucially, the method avoids an expensive inner loop: it does not update the student per candidate prompt to estimate value.

2) A no-answer, distributional surrogate reward for prompt value.
- The reward aggregates token-level signals along student rollouts.
- Instead of rewarding pure disagreement (which can be dominated by support-mismatch), it uses a support-aware notion of teachability: combine teacher–student discrepancy with the teacher’s probability mass on the student’s candidate tokens, aiming to capture “corrective preference within student support” rather than “divergence outside support”.

Anti-gaming terms:
- Length control via sublinear length normalization plus a soft response-budget penalty to prevent inflating reward by longer outputs.
- A batch-level diversity term to prevent mode collapse into a narrow prompt family.

Curation:
- After training, prompts are format-filtered and lexically/semantically deduplicated; a frozen Qwen3-Embedding-4B is used only for semantic deduplication, not for reward computation.

Compared to synthetic SFT data, A2D does not need reference answers; compared to task-generation via success rewards, it optimizes the usability of teacher distributional supervision rather than task correctness.a:T8ba,论文的技术路线是把“数据选择”当成完整 pipeline 来评估，而不是只比一个打分函数：

- LongPPL 的核心是定义 key token：该 token 在长上下文条件下的 log-likelihood 相对短上下文显著提升（超过阈值 α，同时满足 β 等条件）。文档分数是 key token 的密度（比例）。直觉是：密度高的文档包含更多需要远距离信息才能预测的监督信号。

- Repacking constructs 128K sequences by concatenating shorter texts, satisfying length but typically lacking cross-section dependencies.
- Random-long samples genuine long documents, preserving delayed references and distributed evidence.
- The study finds random-long performs better at longer evaluation lengths, suggesting that real long-range structure matters more than mere sequence length.

2) Controlled selector comparison within a fixed candidate pool
Selectors fall into three families:
- Generic quality: perplexity, gzip compressibility.
- LLM-judge: a model-based “is this good long-context training data?” scorer.
- Dependency/likelihood-aware: LongFilter, Prospector, and LongPPL-based key-token density.
All natural-long selectors operate on the same candidate pool to avoid confounding from corpus differences.

3) LongPPL key-token density and truncLongPPL
- LongPPL defines “key tokens” whose log-likelihood improves substantially when conditioning on long context vs short context (thresholded by parameters like α and β). Document score is the density (fraction) of such tokens, aligning selection with documents that actually require long-range context.
- Full-span LongPPL scoring is expensive at scale. truncLongPPL computes key-token density on a truncated prefix (fraction r of the reference span) and uses it as a proxy for full-span ranking.
- Empirically, scoring only the first half of the reference span at 128K yields downstream performance close to full-span scoring, with an end-to-end scoring speedup of about 2.28×.

Compared with SHIFT, LIFT uses partial likelihood lift instead of hidden-state shifts from full trajectories.
