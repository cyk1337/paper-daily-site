第一，FARS 的跨格式概念性是存在的。作者在 24 个 generative models 上，用 10 个互不重叠概念重新抽取子空间，跨格式 retrieval 达到 62% 到 100%。这说明“按 concept centroid 提取共享子空间”这个程序本身有迁移性，不只是某一组概念上的偶然现象。

第二，FARS 与 readout 主方向的重合普遍很低。四种 activation-derived concept estimator 的平均 readout energy 只有 0.38% 到 0.80%。相比之下，final-layer PCA 的平均值是 3.56%，并且在 26 个模型里有 25 个超过 FARS；same-layer next-token control 在 25/25 个 depth-matched 比较里也超过 FARS。这个结果很关键：它说明 FARS 更像上游计算子空间，而不是最终 logits 读出子空间。

第三，单靠干预掉点不能证明 readout 接近。作者在四个模型、三个随机种子上做了更细的 intervention comparison。source-directed patching 的效果依赖模型，而且明显弱于 full-vector replacement；同时随机子空间 patch 也可能保留 argmax，因此“argmax preserved”不是强证据。论文据此强调，几何分离和干预敏感性都不足以单独推出 causal sufficiency。

第四，作者做了 rank 和 layer 敏感性分析。k=10 是经验上边际收益趋缓的工作点，不是声称 reasoning subspace 的真实内在维度就是 10。论文还限定 readout 分析主要在 30%–75% depth band 内、且 concept-RSA 达峰的层进行，以避免把太浅层的 form signal 或太深层的 readout leakage 混进来。

整体看，最有信息量的数字不是 retrieval，而是那组 0.38%–0.80% 对 3.56% 的 readout energy 对比，以及 25/26、25/25 这两组模型计数结果。19:T6f9,The method has two layers. First is FARS extraction. The authors build a tightly controlled stimulus inventory where reasoning content is held fixed while surface form varies. There are 18 reasoning concepts, 3 instances per concept, and 6 forms: English, Chinese, French prose, Python code, mathematical notation, and step-by-step structure, for 324 total stimuli. For each layer they collect last-token hidden states, use RSA to identify layers where concept structure dominates form structure, then average activations within each concept across forms and instances to obtain 18 concept centroids. After mean-centering those centroids, they run PCA and keep the top k=10 directions as the FARS basis. The key design choice is to average by concept before PCA, which explicitly suppresses form variation instead of hoping PCA will ignore it.

Second is the readout-orthogonality test. Given an extracted rank-k basis B and the model’s unembedding matrix W_U, they compute the top-k right singular vectors of W_U as a proxy for the dominant output-readout directions. They then measure how much energy of B lies in that top-k readout span. Once the basis is extracted, this diagnostic uses only model weights and requires no additional forward passes. To calibrate the test, they compare FARS against multiple rank-matched controls: final-layer PCA, same-layer next-token controls, FormPCA, probe-weight constructions, and other estimators, all normalized to k=10 so Grassmann and energy comparisons are meaningful. They also run subspace patching, ablation, and amplification interventions, with random orthonormal, norm-matched random, Full-PCA, and full-vector replacement controls, to show that geometric separation from readout and intervention sensitivity are distinct claims.1a:T76d,The evaluation spans 26 models from 0.4B to 70B parameters, including dense decoders, MoE models, state-space models, reasoning-tuned variants, and one encoder MLM. Several results stand out.

First, the extraction procedure does recover cross-format concept structure. When re-extracted on 10 disjoint concepts, cross-format retrieval reaches 62% to 100% on 24 generative models. This supports the extraction procedure itself, not transportability of one fixed basis.

Second, activation-derived concept subspaces are consistently weakly aligned with readout. Across four activation-based concept estimators, mean energy in the top-10 readout span is only 0.38% to 0.80%. Final-layer PCA reaches 3.56% on average and exceeds FARS in 25 of 26 models. A same-layer next-token control also exceeds FARS in 25 of 25 depth-matched comparisons. This is the central empirical result: FARS appears much more upstream than output-proximal.

Third, intervention effects do not rescue a stronger interpretation. In a four-model, three-seed intervention study, source-directed patching is model-dependent and substantially weaker than full-vector replacement. Random subspace patches can also preserve argmax, so argmax preservation is not a strong indicator of semantic specificity. The paper uses this to argue that neither geometric separation nor intervention sensitivity alone establishes causal sufficiency.

Fourth, the authors examine rank and layer sensitivity. The choice k=10 is presented as an empirical operating point where marginal gains flatten, not as a claim about intrinsic dimensionality. Readout analyses are concentrated around concept-RSA peaks within the 30%–75% depth band to avoid shallow form-dominated layers and trivial late readout leakage.

The most informative numbers are the readout-energy gap—0.38%–0.80% versus 3.56%—and the model-count comparisons of 25/26 and 25/25.1b:T493,Start with the argument, not the extraction details. The key claim is that ablation drops cannot distinguish between a subspace that computes an answer and one that carries an already-computed answer toward logits. Once that framing is clear, move to the stimulus construction and the concept-centroid PCA procedure. After that, read the definition of the readout-orthogonality test and the positive controls carefully; that is the real contribution.

For figures and tables, prioritize the FARS extraction schematic, the estimator-by-estimator readout-energy comparisons, the per-model comparison between FARS and final-layer PCA, the depth-matched next-token control results, and the intervention-control plots.
