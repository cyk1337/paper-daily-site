18:T5e9,The method has three main components. First is the milestone-plan-act workflow. Instead of planning at the finest step granularity from the start, the orchestrator first decomposes a complex query into milestones. This reduces low-level execution noise and gives the policy a more stable state abstraction. Within each milestone, it builds a dependency-aware execution graph and can run independent steps in parallel across executors. Second is explicit executor profiling. Each executor comes with textual descriptions of capability and cost, so the orchestrator reasons jointly over task requirements and executor profiles rather than routing by query features alone. This differs from standard learned routers that often rely on shallow signals and fixed executor sets. Third is end-to-end RL with tree-structured rollout. Starting from an initial trajectory, the system expands selected milestone or plan nodes into alternative branches, producing multiple strategies under comparable contexts. Rewards combine task correctness, execution efficiency, and trajectory completeness. The completeness term is important because otherwise the policy may exploit partial execution or early stopping. Rewards are normalized at both node and query levels to make signals more comparable across branches and tasks and to reduce variance.
