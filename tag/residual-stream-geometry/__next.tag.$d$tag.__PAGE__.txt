b:T5ab,The paper uses a controlled task: addition of three (or more) 10-digit integers, analyzing residual-stream activations at every generated digit position. It defines column-wise Raw Sum r_p, propagated carry c_p, and ground-truth digit s_p, and introduces model-side latent counterparts (predicted raw sum / predicted carry).

Method components:
- Probing: lightweight probes (logistic regression, small MLPs) decode multiple variables from a single activation vector, establishing “probe versatility.”
- Geometry: UMAP visualizations (primarily final-layer activations) reveal an Iso-Raw-Sum Trajectory (IRST): continuous fibers of constant raw sum threading through a macro-structure of digit “basins,” with micro-structure tied to carry states.
- Mechanistic model: the Noisy Quantization Model posits a continuous Carry Potential internally estimated and then discretized; noise can push it across quantization thresholds, causing “geometric slippages” across digit-basin decision boundaries.
- Validation via intervention: an inference-time dual-stream consistency method decodes local (raw-sum) and global (carry-potential) signals and enforces consistency to self-correct, supporting the claim that correct components exist latently even when output is wrong.
