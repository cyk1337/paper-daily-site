A transition-rate matrix defines the forward noising process and a family of marginal distributions p_t. The reverse process requires concrete scores, i.e. pairwise ratios p_t(y)/p_t(x). SEDD learns these ratios with the score-entropy loss, a weighted Bregman divergence D(a,b)=a/b-1-log(a/b), where the weights are induced by the transition structure.

The main contribution is a minimax treatment of this estimation problem. The paper first derives a lower bound for estimating a single concrete score function under the score-entropy risk. It then constructs a simple estimator, essentially a smoothed or clipped empirical plug-in estimator for the relevant probability ratios, and proves that its risk nearly matches the lower bound. The single-time result is then connected back to SEDD by controlling the time-averaged score-estimation term that appears in KL guarantees for the generated distribution.
