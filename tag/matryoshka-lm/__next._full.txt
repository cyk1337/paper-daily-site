19:T51e,The architecture enforces strict parameter nesting: all parameters of smaller sub-models are contained within larger sub-models, while each sub-model uses its own hidden dimension (width) and layer count (depth) for flexible size design. A parameter-free junction mechanism connects outputs from smaller sub-models to the inputs of larger sub-models: the first D_m dimensions of the larger sub-model’s hidden state are populated directly from the smaller sub-model’s output, while dimensions added for the larger sub-model are filled with corresponding token embeddings. A simple normalization step resolves activation magnitude mismatch between Transformer outputs and input embeddings that would otherwise destabilize training. The training objective combines standard next-token cross-entropy at every sub-model exit with an in-line distillation loss that uses the largest model’s logits as soft labels for all smaller sub-models, providing per-step distillation with no extra forward pass cost. At inference, any sub-model can be extracted and served independently with its own KV cache footprint.
