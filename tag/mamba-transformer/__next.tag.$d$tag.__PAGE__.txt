a:T828,模型是 550B 总参数、每 token 激活 55B 参数的 Mixture-of-Experts hybrid Mamba-Attention LM。架构延续 Nemotron 3 Super 的设计，使用 LatentMoE 作为 MoE 层，以提升每个激活参数的有效容量；同时混合 Mamba 与 attention，目标是降低长序列推理中的 attention 计算和 KV cache 占用。模型还原生加入 Multi-Token Prediction。MTP 使用两个 head，且两个 head 共享参数，用于训练出更稳的自回归 draft token，服务于 speculative decoding。每个 MTP head 由一个 attention layer 和一个 MoE layer 组成。

预训练使用 20T text tokens，采用 Warmup-Stable-Decay 学习率计划。数据分两阶段：前 15T tokens 强调多样性和领域覆盖，后 5T tokens 强调高质量数据，用于提升模型精度。这种安排接近大规模训练中的“先覆盖、后打磨”：前段用广覆盖保证能力底座，后段用更干净、更高价值的数据降低噪声并改善 benchmark 表现。

低精度训练是报告的重点之一。Nemotron 3 Ultra 使用 NVFP4 预训练，NVFP4 层采用 E2M1 数据类型，对权重做二维 block quantization；wgrad 输入使用 Random Hadamard Transform；梯度使用 stochastic rounding。为了稳定性，最后 15% 网络，也就是 16 层，以及 Mamba output projection、latent projection、QKV 和 attention projection、MTP layers、embedding layers 保持更高精度。这个选择反映了一个工程判断：不是所有矩阵都适合压到 FP4，靠近输出端、注意力投影和嵌入相关层对数值误差更敏感。

后训练路径包括 curated SFT、统一 RLVR、多教师 on-policy distillation。RLVR 覆盖 reasoning、agentic、code、safety、usability、chat 等多类环境。团队还训练了十多个领域教师模型，包括沿专门 agentic SFT 路径训练的教师。MOPD 在学生自己生成的 rollout 上提供 dense token-level guidance，把多个教师的能力合并到 Ultra。模型还支持 reasoning effort control，用于在推理时调节准确率和计算量之间的取舍。b:T81d,Nemotron 3 Ultra is a 550B total-parameter MoE language model with 55B active parameters per token. It follows the Nemotron 3 Super hybrid Mamba-Attention design, scaled up and combined with LatentMoE. The motivation is direct: MoE improves accuracy per active parameter, while replacing part of full attention with Mamba-style sequence modeling reduces inference cost and KV-cache footprint for long contexts and long outputs.

The model includes native Multi-Token Prediction with two shared-parameter heads. The heads are trained to produce autoregressive draft tokens and are used for speculative decoding. Each MTP head consists of one attention layer followed by one MoE layer.

Pretraining uses 20 trillion text tokens with a Warmup-Stable-Decay learning-rate schedule. The first 15T tokens emphasize diversity and broad domain coverage. The last 5T tokens emphasize higher-quality data to refine accuracy. This is a practical data schedule: broad coverage first, lower-noise refinement later.

A central technical point is NVFP4 pretraining. NVFP4 layers use the E2M1 datatype with two-dimensional block quantization on weights. Random Hadamard Transforms are applied to inputs for weight-gradient computation, and gradients use stochastic rounding. The recipe keeps numerically sensitive parts in higher precision: the final 15% of the network, corresponding to 16 layers, plus Mamba output projections, latent projections, QKV and attention projections, MTP layers, and embeddings. This selective precision policy is likely as important as the FP4 datatype itself.

Post-training starts with curated SFT, then applies unified RLVR over reasoning, agentic, code, safety, usability, and chat environments. More than ten specialized teacher models are trained, including agentic teachers from a dedicated agentic SFT path. Multi-teacher On-Policy Distillation then gives dense token-level supervision on rollouts generated by the student model. The final model also supports reasoning effort control, allowing inference-time adjustment of the compute-accuracy trade-off.c:T4e7,The main scale numbers are 550B total parameters, 55B active parameters, 20T pretraining tokens, and 1M-token context extension. The paper reports 5.9×, 4.8×, and 1.6× higher inference throughput than GLM-5.1-754B-A40B, Kimi-K2.6-1T-A32B, and Qwen-3.5-397B-17B respectively under an 8K input / 64K output setting, while maintaining broadly comparable accuracy.

For NVFP4 training health, the team branches from checkpoints at 5T, 10T, and 16T tokens, switches tensors to BF16, and continues training for 74B tokens. The relative training-loss gap between the NVFP4 run and the BF16 continuation averages below 0.4%. This is the key evidence that the FP4 recipe tracks a high-precision training trajectory closely enough at this scale.

The base model is reported to outperform several public base models, including DeepSeek v3.2, Mistral Large 3, Kimi-K2, and GLM-4.5, though the provided text does not include the full benchmark tables. One concrete data ablation is given: adding the Nemotron-Pretraining-Legal-v1 synthetic legal datasets to Nemotron 3 Nano improves a proxy LegalBench average from 64.6 to 74.7.
