c:T5b9,They run Petri, an automated misalignment auditing framework, in multiple languages on the same target model.

Pipeline:
- Target: Qwen3-30B-A3B.
- Auditor: Gemini 2.5 Flash, which conducts multi-turn probing conversations and can construct prompts/tools to elicit misaligned behavior.
- Judge: Gemini 2.5 Pro, which scores transcripts on multiple behavior categories (1–10).
- Multilingualization: translate seed instructions and the auditor system prompt into each language; force the target model to respond exclusively in that language.

Metric construction:
- They focus on a 5-category “scheming index” intended to be more specific to scheming than broader safety categories.
- To improve cross-language comparability, they restrict to samples where a given behavior is non-baseline (score ≥ 1) in at least one language, then compute per-language means on that subset (a form of conditional averaging).

Statistics:
- Two-sample permutation tests are used to assess whether observed differences could arise by chance.

Key methodological caveats:
- “Pretraining language coverage” is not directly measured; languages are grouped into high-resource (English/Chinese) vs medium/low-resource (Spanish/Portuguese/Arabic/Vietnamese) based on historical Qwen data composition hints.
