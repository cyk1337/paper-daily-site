在选择保留哪些专家时，HodgeCover 不是直接最小化全局 KL，而是把 harmonic-critical edges 和 triplet-critical triangles 当作需要“覆盖”的结构单元。具体做法是：先取 top-p% 的高权重 harmonic 边和 triplet 三角形，再用带 saliency 权重的 greedy submodular coverage 选出 k 个 survivor experts，使这些关键结构尽量被 survivor 集覆盖。未保留专家则通过 router redirect 映射到最近的 survivor。这里的 saliency 权重借鉴了 REAP，但选择目标已经从 pairwise ranking 变成 higher-order coverage。之后作者再叠加 Wanda，对 survivor 专家内部做非结构化剪枝，形成 HodgeCover+Wanda。这个组合的逻辑很清楚：HodgeCover 处理 expert axis 的压缩，Wanda 处理权重轴的稀疏化，两者基本正交。

和已有方法相比，REAP、REAM、MC-SMoE、STUN 主要都停留在 pairwise 或基于路由统计的专家筛选；有些方法虽然会看 triplet 信息，但只是把 triplet 聚合成 pairwise surrogate 或 binary veto，没有把“不可约循环残差”单独抽出来作为选择目标。HodgeCover 的新意就在这里：不是多加一点高阶特征，而是把高阶冲突作为主导压缩决策的对象。19:T7bf,The method builds a 2D simplicial complex for each MoE layer. Experts are vertices. Pairwise merge barriers live on edges. Triplet merge barriers live on triangles. A pairwise barrier is defined by merging two experts with weights proportional to their routing frequencies on a small calibration set, then measuring the KL cost induced by replacing the pair with the merged expert. Triplet barriers are defined analogously for three experts.

The main step is to treat the edge-barrier signal as a 1-cochain and apply Hodge decomposition using the simplicial 1-Laplacian, L1 = ∂1^T∂1 + ∂2∂2^T. This decomposes the signal into gradient, curl, and harmonic components. The harmonic part is the crucial object: it captures residual cyclic inconsistency that cannot be explained away by local pairwise variation or by triangle-boundary terms. The paper interprets this harmonic residue as the true higher-order obstruction to expert mergeability.

HodgeCover then turns this diagnosis into a selection objective. Instead of directly minimizing a global KL objective, it identifies top-p% harmonic-critical edges and triplet-critical triangles, and selects k survivor experts by greedy submodular coverage of those critical structures, weighted by a saliency term adapted from REAP. Non-survivor experts are redirected to the nearest survivor through router remapping. The hybrid HodgeCover+Wanda then adds unstructured Wanda pruning on the surviving experts. The division of labor is clean: HodgeCover compresses along the expert axis, while Wanda sparsifies weights inside the retained experts.

Compared with REAP, REAM, MC-SMoE, and STUN, the difference is not just “using more features.” Those methods are still fundamentally pairwise or routing-statistic driven. Even methods that consume triplets usually collapse them into pairwise surrogates or veto rules. HodgeCover isolates the irreducible higher-order residue itself and uses it as the primary compression signal.1a:T5a0,The experiments cover three sparse MoE families, including models such as Mixtral, OLMoE, and Qwen 3.5 at different scales. The first important result is diagnostic: the harmonic component is not negligible. It accounts for about 29% to 62% of per-layer barrier energy. That matters because the whole topological framing would be weak if the harmonic residue were tiny. The paper shows it is substantial in real pretrained MoE layers.

The main comparison is at 66% expert reduction. Under that setting, HodgeCover+Wanda achieves the best perplexity on every tested model. Relative to STUN+Wanda, downstream accuracy gains reach as high as +12.6 percentage points. There is also a key ablation on Qwen 3.5-35B: removing the Hodge step and keeping a matched non-Hodge variant costs 5.74 percentage points. That ablation is the strongest evidence that the gain is not merely from saliency weighting or from Wanda, but from the higher-order selection objective itself.

The paper is not mainly about pushing the most extreme compression ratio. It is about showing that in a meaningful compression regime, higher-order structure-aware selection is more reliable than pairwise heuristics. Another practical point is that the method only needs a small calibration corpus and a single forward-pass style analysis, with no recovery training. That makes the engineering implications more direct than many compression methods that require retraining.1b:T418,This is a skim paper.
