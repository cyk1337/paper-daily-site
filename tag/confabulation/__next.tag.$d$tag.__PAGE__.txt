9:T503,The framework is called Detect-Classify-Compare. It first segments a generated CoT into reasoning steps using a rule-based parser over newlines, transition markers, and sentence boundaries; the reported parsing F1 is 0.87. At each step boundary, it estimates internal answer commitment with a logit-lens proxy. Hidden states from each layer are projected through the unembedding and layer normalization, and the probability assigned to the ground-truth answer token, p_ans, is tracked. A step is treated as internally committed when p_ans exceeds τ=0.3. The visible-trace label is constructed by checking whether the cumulative CoT text has already exposed the ground-truth answer string or a normalized variant. Belief-CoT Agreement measures whether these two binary states match over time. Mismatches are then classified into step-level patterns, with confabulated continuation being the dominant one: the answer proxy is already stable while the model keeps writing deliberative-looking text. The paper also compares architecture-matched Qwen2.5 and DeepSeek-R1-Distill model pairs to isolate the effect of reasoning distillation.
