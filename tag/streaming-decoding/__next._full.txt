At each prefix, the system keeps a draft answer from the previous prefix. When a new sentence arrives, one forward pass over the updated request concatenated with the old draft scores each draft token under the new context. The algorithm keeps the longest leading prefix whose token probabilities exceed a threshold τ, discards the rest, and regenerates from that accepted prefix. At final submission, the residual cost is one verification pass plus decoding of the rejected suffix. The leading-prefix rule is natural for autoregressive models because generation cannot insert or repair arbitrary middle tokens without changing the conditioning of later tokens. For ReFusion-style slot-based diffusion, the same rule keeps verification and regeneration simple.
