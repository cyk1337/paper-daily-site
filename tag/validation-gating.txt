18:T450,SkillOpt treats the skill file as the optimization state. At each step, the frozen target model runs a batch of tasks with the current skill. A separate frontier optimizer model inspects successful and failed trajectories and proposes structured add, delete, and replace edits. These edits are aggregated and ranked under a textual learning-rate budget, so a new skill version cannot move too far from the previous one. Candidate skills are evaluated on a held-out selection split before acceptance. Rejected edits are retained as negative feedback for later optimizer calls, and an epoch-level slow or meta update carries stable editing directions across steps. The exported artifact is a compact best_skill.md file, while the target model and execution harness remain fixed. Compared with generic prompt optimization such as TextGrad or GEPA, the method adds explicit controls over evidence quality, update magnitude, validation, rejected updates, and long-horizon refinement.
