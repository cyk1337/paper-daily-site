c:T40b,Two main components:
1) Cross-Lingual Mapping (CL) Task for continued pre-training: jointly optimize standard Next-Token Prediction (NTP) and a CL objective that performs bi-directional language mapping in the model’s embedding space, encouraging direct cross-lingual correspondences while retaining monolingual fluency via NTP. The key design choice is to make alignment an intrinsic pre-training objective rather than a downstream-only alignment step.
2) Language Alignment Coefficient (LAC): to overcome the unreliability of mean cosine similarity across layers, LAC is defined as the mean cosine similarity of cross-lingual sentence-pair embeddings divided by the standard deviation of these similarities over a selected layer subset (statistically equivalent to inverse coefficient of variation). Embeddings are taken from the last token hidden states at layers in L_sub (e.g., {5,10,15,20,25}).
