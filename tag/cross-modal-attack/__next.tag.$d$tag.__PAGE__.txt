9:T46d,CrossMPI changes the optimization target from the embedding space to the hidden-state space. The authors argue that an LVLM integrates visual evidence and textual intent in internal representations, so optimizing only visual embeddings is insufficient for steering the interpretation of the text instruction. This creates a much larger optimization problem. In a 7B MiniGPT-4 example, the visual embedding space is around 10^5 parameters, while the hidden-state space can reach around 10^7 parameters. The method controls this space in two ways. First, it selects fusion-critical layers through layer-wise probing: layers that are stable under surface-level wording or syntax changes, but sensitive to task-semantic changes, are treated as the layers where multimodal fusion is most active. Second, it assigns perturbation budgets in a distance-decremental manner over the image prompt, concentrating optimization on positions more likely to affect fusion states.
