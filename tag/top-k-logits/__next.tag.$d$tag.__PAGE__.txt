9:T5ae,The first component is offline top-K logit distillation. For each token position, the teacher’s top-K probabilities and indices are cached once, with K=100 in the main setup. Importantly, the retained mass M is not renormalized to 1. Instead, the forward KL is rewritten exactly for a truncated teacher distribution: the loss decomposes into a teacher-entropy term, a cross term over the retained support, and an M log Z term that depends on the student’s full-vocabulary log-normalizer. This means the implementation only needs to gather student logits on the top-K support plus compute a scalar logsumexp over the vocabulary, rather than materializing dense teacher and student distributions. The paper discusses three mathematically equivalent offline variants, differing only in how densely they instantiate tensors. The second component is a fused, chunked KL loss. Borrowing from recent fused CE kernels, the authors fuse the output projection with the loss and chunk along the sequence dimension so that dense B×S×V tensors are never materialized. Because the target is a sparse teacher rather than a one-hot label, the gradient differs from standard CE: support tokens subtract teacher mass, while off-support tokens only enter through the truncated mass M times the student softmax.
