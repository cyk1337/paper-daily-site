1a:T7a6,方法设计很干净。作者构造了一个 2×2×3 的因子实验。前两个维度分别是：1）结构：Atomic vs Holistic。Atomic 先把回答拆成 claim，再逐条判断，只要任一 claim 提供可执行的危险信息就判 harmful；Holistic 则直接按 rubric 做整体判断。2）框架：Identity vs Neutral。Identity 给 judge 一个“资深 AI safety evaluator”式 persona；Neutral 则只给任务说明，不设专家身份。第三个维度是每个 cell 下再写 3 个独立 wording 版本，用来测量同一条件下纯表面改写的影响。这样总共得到 12 个 judge prompts。

主实验把这 12 个 prompt 全部交给同一个 judge model（Claude Sonnet 4-6），对 6 个 target models 在 HarmBench 400 个行为上的输出做判定。target models 包括 Claude Sonnet 4-6、GPT-4.1、DeepSeek V3、Kimi K2.5、MiniMax M2、GLM-5。所有 target response 都在 temperature 0 下生成，每个行为只取一次响应。judge 输出统一为 JSON，字段含 verdict 和 rationale；Atomic 额外返回 claims。作者定义了几类指标：overall swing（同一 target model 在 12 个 prompts 下 harmful rate 的最大差值）、within-condition swing（同一结构/框架条件下 3 个 wording 版本的最大差值）、pairwise Cohen’s kappa、ranking stability 的 Kendall’s tau，以及按 HarmBench 语义类别分解的 category sensitivity。

技术上最重要的不是 prompt engineering 本身，而是实验控制。作者刻意把 judge model 固定，避免了“prompt 变了同时 judge 也变了”的混杂；又用 within-condition swing 把“结构变化”与“纯 wording 变化”拆开。补充实验再引入多 judge setting，观察 judge model choice 叠加后方差会怎样扩大。文章也明确承认一个边界：他们证明了不同 judge 配置会显著分歧，但没有证明哪一种更准确，因为没有人工 gold labels 作为最终锚点。1b:T926,主实验共得到 28,812 次判定，去掉 4.6% parse error 后保留 27,485 个有效 judgment。核心结果有几条。

1）单 judge、只改 prompt wording，就足以大幅改写 harmful rate。最显眼的例子是 DeepSeek V3，在 12 个 prompts 下 harmful rate 从 13.3% 到 37.5%，overall swing 达 24.2 个百分点，bootstrap CI 为 [20.4, 28.7]。四个 target models 的 overall swing 都超过 13pp。

2）更值得注意的是，绝大部分波动不是来自 Atomic/Holistic 或 Identity/Neutral 这种“概念上较大”的 prompt 设计差异，而是来自同一条件内的表面改写。作者报告平均 within-condition swing 为 10.8pp，而平均 overall swing 为 13.4pp。也就是说，很多看起来语义等价、只是在措辞上不同的 judge prompt，就足以造成接近整体波动量级的大幅偏移。

3）条件均值层面，Holistic prompt 往往给出略高的 harmful rate，Neutral 也略高于 Identity，但这些 between-condition 差异并不大，且经常被 cell 内部的 wording 方差淹没。这一点很关键：如果只比较“原子式 rubric vs 整体式 rubric”，会低估真正的问题，因为更大的不稳定性藏在 wording 层。

4）模型排序整体上还算稳定，但中间名次会翻转。作者给出的 pairwise Kendall’s tau 平均约为 0.89，区间大致在 [0.80, 0.96]。这说明最安全和最不安全的模型通常还能分开，但中间梯队的相对顺序并不牢靠。对于 leaderboard 或 checkpoint selection，这种不稳定已经足够影响结论。

5）不同伤害类别的敏感度差异很大。文中指出 copyright 类别的 swing 最高，可达 39.6pp；harassment 则几乎为 0pp。这个结果说明 judge prompt 敏感性不是均匀噪声，而是与任务边界是否模糊、rubric 是否容易被不同措辞触发有关。版权、误导、非法协助这类边界更依赖 judge 对“可执行性”“帮助程度”的解释，因此更容易受 prompt wording 影响。

6）补充的 multi-judge 分析显示，换 judge model 会进一步增加方差，但即便不换 judge，仅 prompt wording 已经足够造成大幅波动。这个结论让文章的主张更扎实：问题不是“不同 judge 模型各有偏好”，而是“同一个 judge 也会被不同提示词推着走”。1c:T788,The experimental design is the main strength. The authors build a 2×2×3 factorial setup. The first axis is evaluation structure: Atomic versus Holistic. Atomic prompts ask the judge to decompose the response into claims, assess each claim, and mark the whole response harmful if any claim provides actionable dangerous information. Holistic prompts ask for a single overall judgment using the same harm rubric. The second axis is framing: Identity versus Neutral. Identity prompts assign an expert safety-evaluator persona; Neutral prompts use task-oriented instructions without persona anchoring. The third axis is surface wording: three independently written prompt variants per cell. This yields 12 judge prompts in total.

In the primary analysis, all 12 prompts are run through a single judge model, Claude Sonnet 4-6, over responses from six target instruction-tuned models on 400 HarmBench behaviors. The target models are Claude Sonnet 4-6, GPT-4.1, DeepSeek V3, Kimi K2.5, MiniMax M2, and GLM-5. Target responses are generated once per behavior at temperature 0. The judge outputs structured JSON with a verdict and rationale; Atomic prompts also return extracted claims. The authors then compute overall swing, within-condition swing, pairwise Cohen’s kappa, ranking stability via Kendall’s tau, and category-level sensitivity.

What is technically notable is not prompt engineering itself but the control strategy. By fixing the judge model, the paper removes a major confound. By separating overall swing from within-condition swing, it distinguishes conceptual prompt changes from pure wording effects. A supplementary multi-judge setup then shows how judge-model choice adds further variance. The paper is also careful about scope: it demonstrates disagreement and instability, but it does not establish which judge configuration is most accurate because there is no human gold standard anchoring the labels.1d:T923,The primary experiment produces 28,812 judgments; after removing 4.6% parse errors, 27,485 valid judgments remain. Several results stand out.

First, prompt wording alone can move the harmful-response rate by a large margin even when the judge model is fixed. The clearest example is DeepSeek V3, whose harmful rate ranges from 13.3% to 37.5% across the 12 prompts, an overall swing of 24.2 percentage points with a bootstrap CI of [20.4, 28.7]. Four of the six target models show overall swings above 13 points.

Second, most of the variance is not driven by large conceptual changes such as Atomic versus Holistic or Identity versus Neutral. It is already present within a single condition through surface rewording. The mean within-condition swing is 10.8 points, compared with a mean overall swing of 13.4 points. Third, at the condition-mean level, Holistic prompts tend to yield somewhat higher harmful rates than Atomic prompts, and Neutral framing tends to be slightly harsher than Identity framing. But these between-condition effects are modest relative to the within-cell variance. This means that focusing only on prompt-template families would miss the larger instability coming from wording.

Fourth, model rankings are fairly stable at the extremes but not fully reliable in the middle. The average pairwise Kendall’s tau is about 0.89, with reported values roughly in the [0.80, 0.96] range. So the safest and least safe models are often separated consistently, but mid-ranked models can swap positions depending on the judge prompt.

This suggests the variance is not uniform noise.
