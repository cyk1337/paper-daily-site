9:T456,The central design is a shared rollout pipeline. For a fixed agent and environment, evaluation, data collection, and RL training reuse the same observation processing, context construction, and action execution stack. This reduces harness drift. The platform covers mobile, computer, and web environments, manages provisioning, isolation, reset, and recovery, and reports scaling to 20,000 parallel environment instances. On the data side, MAI-Gym uses two synthesis layers. At the app level, coding agents build mocked apps with resettable backends, including safe clones of real services and novel interfaces. At the task level, cross-app world construction first creates a coherent world from a persona and scenario, populates multiple applications with consistent files and records, and then generates tasks that require retrieval and integration across apps. Verifiers are generated only after task requirements are fixed, and are checked on initial, completed, and reset states. The system also uses failure-driven synthesis, where failed trajectories are diagnosed and converted into targeted new tasks.a:T448,The contribution is platform design and maintenance, not a one-off dataset.
