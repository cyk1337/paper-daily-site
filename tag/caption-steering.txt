18:T405,GEASS (Gated Evidence-Aware Selective Steering) is plug-and-play and parameter-free:
- Two-path inference: run the model with the original prompt (clean path) and with an appended self-caption (caption path), producing two logit streams.
- Logit-level modulation using three signals:
  1) Confidence gate: uses the clean-path max probability as a proxy for whether external evidence is needed.
  2) Evidence-aware weight: measures caption usefulness via entropy reduction induced by the caption (information gain proxy).
  3) Selective disagreement penalty: when the two paths disagree, raise the threshold for letting the caption override, encoding the prior that captions mostly omit and occasionally fabricate (high-cost).
- Design choice: do not improve caption quality (no stronger captioner or external verification); instead, control the anchoring effect by regulating caption influence at the logits.
