c:T568,Distributionally Robust Token Optimization (DRTO) builds on Reinforced Token Optimization (RTO): a PPO-style actor update driven by a preference-learned token-wise reward. DRTO robustifies the actor loss via Distributionally Robust Optimization (DRO) by constructing an f-divergence ambiguity set over the empirical minibatch distribution and optimizing the worst-case (upper-bounded) token/trajectory-wise objective. The goal is to reduce sensitivity to minibatch randomness and reward perturbations, yielding more stable policy-gradient updates under distribution shift.

Key design choice: DRO is applied at the minibatch level (local ambiguity set per update) within an on-policy token-level RLHF pipeline.

Two practical instantiations:
1) KL-DRTO: KL-divergence ambiguity set leads to an entropic-risk / exponential-tilting objective, smoothly upweighting high-loss trajectories within the minibatch.
2) χ²-DRTO: χ²-divergence yields a tractable variance-sensitive surrogate, effectively combining minibatch mean loss with a standard-deviation-dependent term, encouraging both low average loss and low loss dispersion.

Compared to prior robust preference optimization (e.g., DrDPO, χ²-PO) that mainly targets offline preference learning, DRTO directly stabilizes on-policy token-level actor updates, while largely preserving RTO’s training pipeline and compute profile.d:T53c,【skim】
