Under this framework, SSMs correspond to the extreme of dense full-slot writes with global gradual decay, while SWA corresponds to the extreme of deterministic round-robin single-slot writes with hard eviction (unselected slots retain content until overwritten on their rotation). New content is only written to selected slots, and learnable decay is applied exclusively to these updated slots, while unselected slots retain their content indefinitely without any update or decay.
