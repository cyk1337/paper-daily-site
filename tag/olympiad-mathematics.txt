The SFT checkpoint is trained from the GA model with a maximum sequence length of 425,984 tokens and the standard per-token cross-entropy objective. The SFT corpus is built by a multi-stage synthetic pipeline. Starting from 15,879 difficult AoPS proof problems, DeepSeek-V4-Pro in Max inference mode generates multiple initial proof attempts with up to 400K tokens. Unsolved problems go through up to three refinement rounds conditioned on earlier attempts and verifier feedback. In parallel, verifier trajectories score candidate proofs with labels in {0, 0.5, 1}, and meta-verifier trajectories judge the reliability of self-evaluations and verifier outputs. The filtering is strict: incomplete, malformed, empty, length-capped, and structurally invalid samples are removed; proof and refinement examples must contain non-empty Solution and Self Evaluation sections; verification examples must contain a parseable final score and evaluate the visible proof. To avoid an SFT distribution dominated by wrong proofs, all valid 0.5 and 1.0 verifier traces are retained, while 0-score traces are deterministically subsampled. The final SFT corpus has 414,890 filtered examples over 15,818 unique problems: 58,543 proof-generation traces, 67,971 refinement traces, 236,360 verification traces, and 52,016 meta-verification traces. The SFT run uses 512 GB200 GPUs with TP=8, CP=32, EP=64, PP=1, global batch size 64, micro-batch size 1, and roughly 1.6K optimizer steps for one pass over packed data. AdamW is used with β1=0.9, β2=0.95, and weight decay 0.1.
