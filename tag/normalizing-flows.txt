第一步是构造连续 CoT 目标。作者假设训练数据里有显式文本 CoT d1:L 和最终答案 x1:N。先用一个 encoder 把文本 CoT 压缩成长度为 K、维度为 D 的连续表示 e1:K。这里的 e-space 更像“压缩后的思维表示”，适合承载语义，但未必适合直接做 prompt-conditioned AR 生成。

第二步是引入一个更适合自回归建模的 LLM-facing thought space u1:K。作者不直接对 e 建模，而是通过可逆变换 Fθ 把 e 映射到 u。u-space 服从因果高斯分布：每个 thought token ui 的均值和方差由 prompt 以及前面的 thought tokens 因果决定。这样一来，u 可以像文本 token 一样左到右采样，并且整条 thought sequence 的 likelihood 可精确计算。e 和 u 之间用 normalizing flow 连接，利用变量替换公式把连续 thought 的精确密度写出来。这里的关键不是“用了 flow”这么简单，而是 flow 被用来桥接“语义压缩空间”和“易于 AR 生成的空间”。这比直接在 encoder latent 上做 diffusion 更贴近语言模型接口。

First, the model defines a continuous target for chain-of-thought. Given supervised examples with prompt q, explicit textual CoT d1:L, and answer x1:N, an encoder compresses the textual rationale into a continuous sequence e1:K of length K and dimension D. This e-space is optimized for semantic compression, but it is not necessarily easy to model autoregressively conditioned on the prompt.

Second, the paper introduces an LLM-facing thought space u1:K that is easier to sample and score causally. Instead of modeling e directly, NF-CoT maps e to u through an invertible transformation Fθ. In the u-space, the thought sequence follows a causal Gaussian density: each latent thought token is generated left-to-right with mean and variance conditioned on the prompt and previous thought tokens. Because the transformation is invertible, the exact density of the original continuous thought can be computed via the change-of-variables formula. This is the key design choice. The flow is not used merely as a generic density estimator; it bridges a semantically meaningful compressed space and an autoregressively tractable space.

Third, the model jointly generates latent thoughts and textual answers using a shared causal LLM backbone. On top of the backbone, one head models the continuous thought density and another head models the answer tokens. Training maximizes the joint likelihood of latent thoughts and answers. Since the thought likelihood is tractable, the same interface also supports policy-gradient optimization directly in the continuous reasoning space.

Relative to prior work, the distinctions are sharp. Compared with Coconut-style methods, NF-CoT is stochastic and likelihood-based rather than deterministic hidden-state rollout. Compared with diffusion-based latent reasoning, it supports one-pass left-to-right sampling instead of iterative denoising. First, multi-noise training. Standard TARFlow trains by maximum likelihood on noisy inputs and derives a score for denoising from the gradient of the learned log-density with respect to the input. iTARFlow no longer commits to one fixed noise level. Instead, it trains the same Transformer autoregressive flow across a range of noise scales, so the model explicitly learns the noisy data distribution at multiple corruption levels. Second, iterative inference. Sampling starts from a high-noise autoregressive generation step, preserving the sequential generation structure of the flow. Then, instead of applying a single Tweedie-style cleanup, the model performs iterative denoising across the trained noise levels.
