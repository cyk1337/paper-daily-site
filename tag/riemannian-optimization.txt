18:T4c7,The paper starts from TRPO, where policy improvement is constrained by a KL trust region, and views PPO-Clip as a cheap first-order approximation to that idea. The flaw is that PPO clips the importance ratio r=πθ(a|s)/πold(a|s) with a fixed interval such as 1±ε. For a categorical policy, the local KL expansion is approximately 0.5·Σ Δp(a)^2/p(a). Written in terms of ratio changes, the KL contribution of one action is roughly 0.5·p_old(a|s)·(r−1)^2. A fixed ratio bound therefore gives low-probability actions very small absolute movement and high-probability actions disproportionately large movement. Riemannian Isometric Policy Optimization changes the clip boundary as a function of the local old-policy probability. Rare actions get a wider ratio range; frequent actions get a tighter one. The intended effect is an approximately isometric update under the policy manifold geometry. The paper further argues that this geometry-aware clipping induces a more homoscedastic update signal and improves the bias-variance trade-off.
