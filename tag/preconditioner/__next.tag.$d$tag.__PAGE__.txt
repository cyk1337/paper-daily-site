9:T72d,The paper rewrites Muon as an explicit metric/preconditioning method and then analyzes the statistics of that preconditioner at initialization.

1) Preconditioner form of orthogonalization: for a matrix parameter W∈R^{m×n}, Muon replaces the momentum/gradient matrix V with its orthogonal polar factor D=(V V^T)^(-1/2)V (computed via a fixed-point polar iteration / thin SVD). Vectorizing yields vec(D)=((V V^T)^(-1/2)⊗I_n)vec(V), i.e., Muon performs descent in a Kronecker-structured metric determined by P=V V^T (row-space Gram).

2) Structural alignment target: prior work reports that neural-network layer Hessians are row-block diagonally dominant when blocks are indexed by output neurons (rows of W). Muon’s preconditioner also only mixes along rows (left-multiplication by (V V^T)^(-1/2)), so if P is close to diagonal, Muon inherits the same row-block structure as the Hessian.

3) Main theory: at initialization (V0=G0), prove for four canonical models—symmetric matrix factorization, deep linear networks, two-layer ReLU networks, and single-layer linear attention—that the diagonal of E[G G^T] outgrows the off-diagonal as hidden dimensions increase, with explicit width-dependent rates (Theorems 1–4, summarized in Table 1). Technically, this is a correlation decay result: gradient rows corresponding to different output neurons become weakly correlated due to averaging in the wide limit.

4) Empirical support: simulations reproduce the predicted scaling trends; initialization-time measurements on real LLMs (Qwen3.5, Llama-3.2, Granite-4.0) show stronger diagonal dominance with larger width.

- Construction: the forward pass induces layerwise perturbation dynamics via linearization of activations with respect to parameter perturbations; the chosen geometry defines quadratic costs. Cross-layer coupling is retained through the dynamics and cost-to-go, rather than via an explicitly formed dense curvature matrix.
- Exact solution: Riccati recursions recover the exact geometry-aware update (used as a reference).

Crucially, the block structure is imposed on the learned inverse preconditioner after deriving the coupled LQR objective, not by replacing the curvature model upfront.
