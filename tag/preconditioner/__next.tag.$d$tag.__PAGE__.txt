- Construction: the forward pass induces layerwise perturbation dynamics via linearization of activations with respect to parameter perturbations; the chosen geometry defines quadratic costs. Cross-layer coupling is retained through the dynamics and cost-to-go, rather than via an explicitly formed dense curvature matrix.
- Exact solution: Riccati recursions recover the exact geometry-aware update (used as a reference).
- Scalable relaxation (LLQR): parameterize the update as a preconditioned gradient Δθ = -U g with U block-diagonal across layers (each block can be diagonal, Kronecker-factored, etc.). Crucially, the block structure is imposed on the learned inverse preconditioner after deriving the coupled LQR objective, not by replacing the curvature model upfront.
