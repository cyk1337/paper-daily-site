The labeling protocol stabilizes at around ten paraphrases.a:T601,The technical contribution has three layers. First, the paper reframes the notion of knowledge in OPD. Standard OPD samples trajectories from the student and minimizes reverse KL to a frozen teacher at every token. Existing analyses mostly track Overlap@k, the agreement between student and teacher top-k token sets, which captures only how to branch. The authors introduce entropy shape: the sequence of next-token entropies along a trajectory. High-entropy positions correspond to forks where the model keeps options open; low-entropy positions correspond to committed continuation. This gives a concrete representation of where to branch. Second, they define Entropy Shape Correlation (ESC), which measures alignment between student and teacher entropy shapes on the same trajectory. ESC is about the positional pattern of uncertainty, not token identity. Third, they provide both observational and causal evidence that this signal matters. Observationally, in successful OPD runs, ESC rises during training together with student performance; in unsuccessful runs, ESC starts high and quickly plateaus, suggesting little remaining uncertainty gap to learn from. Causally, they build counterfactual teachers by transplanting the entropy shape of one teacher onto another while preserving token preferences as much as possible. This isolates where to branch from how to branch. The resulting experiments show that a successful teacher’s entropy shape alone can improve distillation, and that ESC can change independently of Overlap@k.b:T59f,The experiments are designed to validate mechanism rather than maximize benchmark scores. First, the authors test whether OPD gains simply come from the teacher solving more training problems. The answer is no: gains are largely unaffected by whether the teacher is correct on those specific trajectories, which suggests that the student is learning a general reasoning behavior rather than just answer knowledge. Second, in training dynamics, successful OPD runs show a steady increase in ESC alongside student improvement, while failed runs begin with high ESC and saturate early, with limited downstream gain. This is an important result because it turns “distillability” into an observable quantity. Third, the counterfactual-teacher experiments are the strongest part of the paper: by transplanting entropy shape, the authors show that changing the uncertainty pattern alone can change distillation outcomes, even when token preferences remain largely fixed. ESC can move independently of Overlap@k, supporting the claim that it is not a byproduct of token overlap. Finally, in the teacher-selection setup, pre-OPD ESC outperforms baselines based on teacher size, teacher raw performance, and Overlap@k.
