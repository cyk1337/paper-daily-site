1a:T651,LongCrafter reframes long-context instruction synthesis as structured crafting guided by (a) a hierarchical task taxonomy and (b) evidence-graph constraints.

1) Task taxonomy (32 fine-grained types): organizes long-context understanding into local/shallow vs global/deep, and groups tasks by capability (e.g., retrieval, ordering, tracking, multi-evidence reasoning). The taxonomy is used as a global prior to steer generation toward broad coverage and to upweight scarce but important deep tasks.

2) Three-stage pipeline:
- Long Context Construction: group corpus by source; match documents with task types. For multi-document tasks, pick a primary document and retrieve complementary documents using hybrid BM25 + dense retrieval, then filter for relevance/task alignment.
- Evidence-Constraint Graph Construction: parse documents into an evidence graph by extracting candidate evidence spans, selecting task-relevant spans as nodes, and adding dependency edges capturing cross-paragraph relations (chains/trees/graphs).
- Instruction–Response Pair Synthesis: generate instructions and responses strictly conditioned on the evidence graph. Instructions are constrained to require all key nodes (to prevent local shortcuts). Responses are produced step-by-step, with each step citing the corresponding evidence verbatim, providing explicit faithfulness supervision.
