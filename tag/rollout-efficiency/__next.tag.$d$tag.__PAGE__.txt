9:T584,1) Factor decomposition: acceptance degradation during RL could come from (a) higher policy entropy for exploration, making drafts harder to predict, and (b) distribution mismatch because the policy updates while the MTP module is frozen. The authors’ analysis and experiments conclude entropy is dominant, mismatch is negligible (Fig.3).
2) Entropy bound insight:
- Under common greedy target-only verification (draft proposes argmax tokens; verification uses only target probabilities without caching draft probs), acceptance is upper-bounded by max_y p(y). As entropy increases, max probability drops, producing a near-linear acceptance decline.
- With probabilistic rejection sampling, acceptance becomes the distributional overlap sum_v min(p(v), q(v)), which is much less sensitive to entropy shifts because it depends on global overlap rather than peak mass.
3) Training objective alignment: CE/KL-trained MTP drafts are not optimized for rejection-sampling acceptance since they only indirectly affect overlap. The paper proposes an end-to-end TV (total variation) loss that directly optimizes the multi-step overlap relevant to rejection sampling, improving acceptance and keeping it entropy-invariant.
