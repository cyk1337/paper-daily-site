9:T6ee,PS-PPO replaces full-trajectory updates with stochastic prefix updates. In standard critic-free PPO for language models, a completion of length T receives a broadcast advantage, and the PPO objective sums token-level terms across all timesteps. The authors observe that on reasoning tasks, the probability of eventual success conditioned on the current prefix often saturates before the sequence ends. This suggests that much of the suffix may be redundant for learning.

The method therefore defines a prompt-conditioned cutoff distribution and samples a cutoff c for each rollout. Only the prefix up to c is used in the policy-gradient computation; tokens after c are skipped for that update. The main technical issue is bias: naively truncating the sequence would change the objective because the original update sums over all tokens. To fix this, the paper derives an inclusion-probability reweighting estimator. Each retained timestep is weighted by the inverse of its probability of being included under the cutoff distribution, so that the expectation over cutoff sampling recovers the corresponding full broadcast update. In other words, PS-PPO does not redefine the target objective; it constructs a cheaper stochastic estimator of the same critic-free update.

Another important design choice is that the cutoff distribution is prompt-conditioned rather than fixed. It is driven by a prefix uncertainty signal: examples that appear effectively decided early can be truncated more aggressively, while uncertain examples keep longer prefixes. The paper explicitly acknowledges late recovery and self-correction, so it uses stochastic truncation instead of deterministically discarding all suffix tokens. That is the main robustness-efficiency tradeoff in the design.a:T4ca,The experiments support two claims. First, the paper validates the underlying phenomenon by analyzing prefix-conditioned success rates. On the reasoning tasks they study, success often saturates before the end of the completion, which supports the idea that later tokens can be temporally redundant for optimization. Second, on mathematical reasoning and RLHF benchmarks, PS-PPO reduces training-time compute and peak GPU memory while maintaining accuracy relative to strong critic-free baselines.

第一部分是层次化 Monte Carlo。作者观察到一次样本贡献通常包含两层随机性：外层是昂贵的上游状态构造，例如 renderer / encoder / generator forward；内层是便宜的 diffusion noising 与 teacher denoising。如果每次都把两层一起重采样，会把大量预算浪费在外层。于是他们提出 amortized resampling：缓存昂贵上游结果，只对便宜的 diffusion noise 重采样。这样做保持无偏，但能在固定预算下显著增加内层样本数。论文把这种收益用 ECM（effective compute multiplier）来量化，本质是在比较“达到同等方差需要多少额外算力”。

第二部分是 timestep importance sampling。扩散 teacher 的目标里本来就有显式 timestep weight，作者不是沿用 teacher 训练时继承下来的时间分布，而是针对下游梯度估计重新选 proposal q(t)。理论上最优 proposal 与条件二阶矩有关，实际不可得，于是他们用任务相关的可计算 proxy 来近似，并给出一个很直接的 drop-in 选择 q∝p·w。这里的关键判断是：下游要估计的是 teacher-induced gradient，不是 teacher training loss，所以最优采样分布应围绕梯度贡献而不是训练损失本身。

第三部分是 stratified inverse-CDF sampling。单纯 importance sampling 仍会受有限样本波动影响，尤其在 timestep 贡献高度不均时。作者把 stratification 和 importance sampling 结合，用 inverse-CDF 构造分层采样，使每个 batch 对时间轴覆盖更均匀，同时保留重要区域的高采样密度。文中把它描述为在无偏分配里接近最优的方案。

第四部分是 compute-aware variance accounting。论文没有停在“某个 trick 有效”，而是系统测量不同随机源、不同成本项、不同采样策略对总方差的贡献。这个框架的价值在于，它解释了为什么某些直觉上常见的做法收益有限：如果主导方差的是 timestep 和 noise，而主导成本的是 renderer/encoder，那么继续增加完全独立样本并不划算，应该优先做分层与重用。d:T9bd,The central idea is CARV, a compute-aware variance-accounting framework for gradients induced by frozen diffusion teachers. The framework decomposes randomness sources, attaches explicit cost to each source, and then derives estimator choices that improve variance per unit compute.

The first component is hierarchical Monte Carlo with amortized resampling. A typical sample contribution contains an expensive outer random component, such as rendering, encoding, or generator forward computation, and a cheaper inner random component from diffusion noising and denoising. Resampling both layers independently wastes budget on the expensive outer layer. CARV instead caches the expensive upstream computation and resamples only the cheap diffusion noise. This keeps the estimator unbiased while increasing the number of effective inner samples under the same budget. The paper quantifies the gain using ECM, the effective compute multiplier.

The second component is timestep importance sampling. Rather than inheriting the teacher’s original timestep distribution, the paper chooses a proposal distribution q(t) tailored to the downstream gradient estimator. The theoretical optimum depends on a conditional second moment and is not directly available, so the authors use tractable proxies. A simple drop-in proposal q proportional to p times the explicit teacher weight already gives measurable gains. The important conceptual point is that the right proposal should target downstream gradient contribution, not the original diffusion training loss.

The third component is stratified inverse-CDF sampling over timesteps. Importance sampling alone still suffers from finite-sample fluctuation, especially when contribution mass is concentrated in a narrow timestep region. The paper combines stratification with inverse-CDF construction so that each batch covers the timestep axis more evenly while still allocating more samples to important regions. The authors argue this is close to optimal among unbiased allocations.

The fourth component is the variance-accounting framework itself. Instead of presenting isolated tricks, the paper measures how different randomness sources and cost terms contribute to total variance. This explains why some common heuristics underperform: if variance is dominated by timestep and noise randomness while cost is dominated by rendering or encoding, then fully independent samples are a poor use of budget compared with reuse plus structured sampling.e:T6ef,The experiments cover three settings: diffusion-teacher-guided text-to-3D optimization, one-step distillation, and data attribution.

In practical terms, CARV reaches comparable variance at about one-third to one-half of the original cost. This is the main empirical claim of the paper and the most important number to retain.

For timestep sampling, the explicit-weight importance proposal improves variance by about 1.2× over uniform timestep sampling at equal per-iteration cost. Hierarchical resampling is presented as the dominant source of gain. Reusing expensive upstream computation and resampling only cheap diffusion noise drives most of the ECM improvement.
