第一块是合成任务 S5-Retrieval。这个任务同时要求不可解群状态跟踪和 in-context retrieval，单纯 Transformer 或单纯 recurrent 模型都难以胜任。作者用它证明中层时间递归确实能表达“跨步持续的抽象状态”，不是只在自然语言任务上靠数据偏置取巧。摘要里没有给出具体数值，但正文结论是 shallow T^2MLR 能解这类标准架构失败的组合任务，这对机制层面的说服力很重要。

第二块是自然语言预训练。作者在 data- and parameter-matched 设置下比较 T^2MLR 与标准 Transformer，结果是 perplexity 更低，且下游 NLP benchmark 也更好。一个很关键的消融是：最好的收益出现在只对约 20% 的中间层做 recurrence/looping，而不是全层递归。这说明收益并不来自“多加一条状态通路”本身，而是来自把递归放在合适的层段。论文还把规模扩到 361M 和 1B 参数、50B pretraining tokens，收益仍然存在，说明不是小模型特有现象。

第三块是下游推理微调。作者在 GSM-Aug、ProsQA、HotPotQA、Variable Assignment 上都报告了优于参数和数据匹配基线的结果。这里最有信息量的观察不是单个 benchmark 提升多少，而是 middle-layer recurrence 变体持续优于 full-layer recurrence。也就是说，论文的核心架构判断在多个任务上都被重复验证了。

第四块是 retrofit 实验，这也是最接近实际采用场景的一部分。作者把递归路径插入到预训练好的 SmolLM2-1.7B-Instruct，再做短暂数学数据微调。结果 GSM8K 从 35.8 提升到 39.9，MATH500 从 12.8 提升到 18.0，且对照组是“同样微调但没有递归改造”的基线。这个结果说明 T^2MLR 不一定要求从头预训练，至少在已有模型上加一条中层递归支路也能带来可见收益。

成本方面，论文给出的推理额外开销最多约 8% per-token，这一点很关键。因为很多 latent reasoning 或 depth recurrence 方法的主要问题不是无效，而是推理成本太高，难以进入真实 serving。T^2MLR 的 trade-off 是把更多复杂性放在训练端，换取较低的在线开销。a:T9f0,The core idea is temporal middle-layer recurrence. The model caches a representation from a deeper middle layer at token t-1 and injects it into an earlier middle layer at token t through a gated fusion pathway. This creates a continuous latent channel across decoding steps, but places that channel inside the part of the network where abstract computation is believed to happen.

Two design choices matter. First, the recurrent signal does not re-enter through the embedding layer. It is fused directly into the middle of the computation graph, so the previous token’s abstract state can continue evolving in the current token’s middle-layer processing. Second, recurrence is localized to a subset of middle layers rather than applied everywhere. The rationale is functional: early layers are more lexical/syntactic, late layers are closer to vocabulary projection, and middle layers are the best place to preserve reasoning state.

Architecturally, T^2MLR defines a write location and a read location. A hidden state from the write layer is cached, then consumed at the read layer of the next token. A gating module controls how much of the current token’s local representation should be mixed with the carried-over latent state. This is more flexible than a plain residual addition because the model can decide contextually when to rely on persistent latent state and when to ignore it.

The training story is a major part of the contribution. Exact temporal recurrence would introduce token-to-token dependencies under teacher forcing and break standard sequence-parallel training. The paper proposes an approximate temporal-parallel training scheme that preserves scalable parallelism while still training the recurrent pathway. This is the practical enabler: without it, the architecture would behave more like an RNN-style serial model and would be hard to pretrain at scale. The approximation introduces a train-time compromise between recurrence fidelity and throughput, and that trade-off is central to evaluating the method.

Relative to prior work, the distinctions are clean. Compared with latent-recurrent methods, recurrence is placed in the middle layers rather than at the input side. Compared with looped/depth-recurrent Transformers, extra computation is propagated across time instead of repeatedly re-running the full stack for one token, so inference overhead stays small. Compared with a standard Transformer, the model adds an explicit continuous hidden-state pathway across tokens, reducing the token bottleneck.b:T84e,The empirical section is broad and well structured.
