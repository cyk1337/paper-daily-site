18:T419,Starting from the properties of KL divergence, the work compares the mode-seeking property of reverse KL and the mode-covering property of forward KL, pointing out that forward KL minimization is the theoretically optimal solution to avoid mode collapse. However, directly optimizing forward KL is computationally intractable, as it requires sampling from a global reward-weighted distribution over trajectories. Accordingly, DMPO (Distribution-Matching Policy Optimization) is proposed to approximate forward KL minimization at the group level: for each group of G trajectories sampled per query, a Boltzmann distribution is constructed as the target distribution based on rewards. A distribution-matching regularization term is added to the standard GRPO objective to align the policy distribution with this intra-group target distribution. This method is naturally compatible with GRPO's existing group-level advantage normalization logic, and can be implemented with only minor code modifications, requiring no additional model or data overhead.19:T46a,Evaluation benchmarks include two categories.
