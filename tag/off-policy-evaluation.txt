18:T991,方法核心是 OTROPE：Optimal Transport-based Robust Off-policy Evaluation。整体结构可以看成“PPI + 语义空间重加权”。

第一步，论文把目标量写成 target policy 下的平均人工评分，手头有两类数据：behavior policy 生成且带人工标签的样本，以及 target policy 生成但只有 proxy 预测、没有人工标签的样本。若没有分布漂移，PPI 会用“proxy 在未标注集上的均值 + 标注集上人工标签与 proxy 的残差均值”来校正 proxy 偏差。但一旦标注集来自 behavior、未标注集来自 target，这个残差均值不再可直接迁移。

第二步，作者不用 token likelihood 做 importance weighting，而是先把回答映射到语义 embedding 空间。这里的动机很明确：序列级重叠可能很差，但语义级重叠往往好很多。比如两个模型表述不同但语义接近，token ratio 会接近 0，而 embedding 邻近性仍然存在。

第三步，在 behavior 标注样本和 target 未标注样本之间求一个 optimal transport plan。OT 的 cost 由语义距离定义，transport matrix 给出每个带标签 behavior 样本对 target 样本的匹配权重。作者据此构造 reweighted residual estimator：不是平均 behavior 样本上的人工-代理残差，而是按 OT 权重把这些残差搬运到 target 分布附近。

第四步，形成 doubly-robust-style 估计器：一部分来自 target 未标注样本上的 proxy prediction，另一部分来自 OT 重加权后的 labeled residual correction。这样做的好处是，只要 proxy predictor 足够准，或者 OT 重加权足够好，估计都能收敛。论文给了相应的一致性和收敛率分析。

理论部分有两块值得注意。其一，作者明确推导了 PPI 在 distribution shift 下的偏差项，说明偏差来自 residual expectation 在 behavior 与 target 分布下不相等。其二，他们分析了 DR/IS 在 LLM 场景的有限样本退化：长文本导致 sequence-level ratio 极小、方差爆炸，弱 overlap 时 correction term 近乎失效。这个论证虽然不新奇，但和 LLM API 场景贴得比较紧。

方法上的取舍也很清楚：它牺牲了经典 OPE 中基于真实行为概率的严格校正，换来黑盒可用性和数值稳定性。前提是 embedding 空间确实保留了与人工评分相关的语义邻近结构；如果 embedding 失真，OT 可能把残差搬错地方。19:T8fe,OTROPE can be read as a semi-supervised evaluator that combines proxy predictions with transport-weighted residual correction.

Start from the standard PPI decomposition: estimate the target-model mean score using proxy predictions on many unlabeled target samples, then correct proxy bias using the average residual between human labels and proxy outputs on a small labeled set. This works only when labeled and unlabeled samples come from the same distribution. Here they do not: labeled samples come from the behavior model, while unlabeled samples come from the target model.

The paper avoids token-level importance weighting altogether. Instead, it maps responses into a semantic embedding space, motivated by the fact that sequence overlap may be poor while semantic overlap remains meaningful. Two responses can have near-zero token-level overlap yet still express similar content and receive similar human judgments.

In that embedding space, the method computes an optimal transport plan between labeled behavior samples and unlabeled target samples. The OT cost is defined by semantic distance. The resulting transport weights are then used to move residual information from the behavior distribution toward the target distribution. Concretely, the residual term is no longer an unweighted average over labeled behavior samples; it becomes an OT-reweighted correction aligned to target samples.

The final estimator has a doubly-robust flavor: one term comes from proxy predictions on target samples, and the other from OT-corrected labeled residuals. The theoretical claim is that consistency can be obtained when either the proxy predictor is good enough or the transport-based reweighting converges appropriately.

Two theoretical analyses are worth noting. First, the paper explicitly derives the bias term of PPI under behavior-target shift. Second, it analyzes why DR/IS degenerates in LLM settings: sequence-level ratios become tiny or unstable for long outputs, overlap is weak, and finite-sample variance explodes. The trade-off is also clear: the method gives up exact likelihood-based correction in exchange for black-box applicability and better numerical behavior. Its success depends on whether the embedding space preserves the semantic structure relevant to human evaluation.1a:T775,The experiments include both synthetic setups and real LLM evaluation tasks. The main empirical pattern is consistent: OTROPE outperforms PPI, importance-weighting, and DR-style baselines when human labels are limited and behavior-target shift is nontrivial.

The synthetic experiments are used to isolate failure modes. As shift increases, PPI becomes increasingly biased. Classical DR/IS suffers from severe variance and finite-sample degeneration under weak overlap. OTROPE remains more stable because it corrects in embedding space rather than through sequence probabilities.

The real-task experiments focus on a realistic pipeline: calibrating weak LLM judges with a small amount of human supervision, then using them to evaluate newer models. The paper reports that OTROPE allows ensembles of weaker evaluators to approach, and in some settings surpass, stronger LLM judges. If robust, that is the most practically relevant result: evaluation quality depends not only on judge strength, but also on whether distribution shift is corrected.

The gains appear largest when the target model differs substantially from the behavior model and when the human-label budget is small. If shift is mild, plain PPI is often sufficient. If the proxy is already very strong and enough target labels are available, the marginal benefit of OT shrinks. Another important practical point is that the method does not require logits or likelihoods from the evaluated model, which makes it suitable for API-only comparisons.

Then jump to the estimator construction and focus on the structure: proxy mean plus OT-weighted residual correction.
