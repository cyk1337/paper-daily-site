9:T97f,ORL 的方法可以拆成算法和系统两部分。

算法上，第一步是把 residual learning 明确成 on-policy rollout。训练时不再依赖离线预生成的 residual states，而是用当前模型在线生成 denoising trajectory：每一步模型接收当前 partially denoised block 以及上一步 residual context，输出新的 token 分布和新的 residual，再继续 rollout。这样训练看到的状态与推理时一致，避免 exposure mismatch。论文强调，若用离线 residual 监督，模型学到的是“如何利用别的策略生成的 residual”，而不是“如何利用自己会生成的 residual”。

第二步是稳定化 residual pathway。作者观察到在共享优化器下，residual 分支比 backbone 更容易快速漂移，导致训练不稳。ORL 因此对 residual pathway 做了 rescaling，使其更新节奏与 backbone 更接近；同时引入基于 rollout entropy 的 supervision gating：当某些位置已经足够 confident，就不再继续强行 sharpen，避免过度收缩导致错误被放大。这里的设计不是简单 regularization，而是在控制“哪些状态值得继续 refinement”。

第三步是提出 adjacent entropy-contraction objective。仅用最终 token 监督，模型知道每一步应该朝最终答案靠近，但不知道每一步应该推进多少。作者发现很多基线在 denoising steps 上 entropy 几乎不下降，因此虽然最终能收敛到正确 token，单步信息增益却很小，TPF 提升有限。ORL 直接要求相邻步骤之间的预测分布熵收缩，也就是后一步要比前一步更确定，但这种收缩又受前述 entropy gating 约束，避免在已经低熵的位置继续盲目压缩。这个目标抓住了 DLM 的核心：每次前向不仅要“更对”，还要“更敢提交”。

系统上，ORL 不是把 on-policy 训练当成概念验证，而是实现了可扩展的 trajectory pipeline。论文描述了异步捕获 residual states、根据显存压力决定保留还是 offload、把不规则长度和不同阶段的轨迹状态打包成稠密 batch，以及用 compute-aware scheduler 平衡 GPU 上 rollout 与训练的负载。文中还提到专门的 staircase attention/packing 设计，用来处理逐步解 mask 带来的非规则计算图。这部分很重要，因为没有这些工程设计，exact on-policy residual learning 很难在大模型上跑起来。a:T87a,论文的实验重点围绕三个问题展开：on-policy 是否必要、entropy-contraction 是否真的提升 refinement speed、以及系统实现是否能把这种训练跑到足够大规模。

从定性现象看，作者先展示了 residual predictions 在中间步骤经常已经提前包含最终 token 的强信号，这说明 residual context 不是噪声缓存，而是可学习的计算再利用通道。随后他们给出一个关键负面观察：如果只做常规中间态监督，denoising 过程中 entropy 基本持平，tokens-per-forward 几乎不增长。这直接支持了“最终正确率监督不足以优化并行推进速度”的论点。

主实验里，ORL 相比离线 residual 学习、无 on-policy rollout 的变体、以及不带 entropy-contraction 的版本，都能得到更好的 speed–quality Pareto。摘要中提到 DiffusionGemma 一类 residual DLM 已可达到 20+ TPF、单卡 H100 约 1230 tok/s；ORL 的贡献不是单纯刷新一个吞吐数字，而是在相近或更高质量下，把 residual context 真正训练成能持续收缩不确定性的机制。全文强调的指标包括不同 forward budget 下的质量曲线、平均 TPF、各 denoising step 的 entropy 变化，以及 rollout 训练稳定性。消融显示：去掉 on-policy 训练后，推理时 residual 轨迹分布偏移，质量和 TPF 都下降；去掉 residual rescaling，训练更容易震荡；去掉 entropy gating，会出现过度 sharpen；去掉 adjacent entropy-contraction，则中间步骤的熵下降幅度明显变小，说明模型没有学会更激进但可控的 refinement。

系统实验则证明 exact on-policy residual learning 不是只能在小规模玩具设置上成立。作者展示了异步轨迹采集、状态搬运和 batch packing 后，训练吞吐与资源利用率可以维持在可接受范围，说明这种训练范式具备继续放大到更大 DLM 的工程可行性。由于给出的全文片段没有完整数表，这里不硬填具体增益数字；但从文中叙述看，提升是沿 speed–quality frontier 展开的，而不是只在单一质量点或单一步数预算上偶然有效。b:T551,This is a must-read for researchers interested in non-autoregressive language modeling. Its contribution is not just another diffusion decoding heuristic. It reframes residual context as a training paradigm: the learning distribution must follow the model's own rollout states, and the objective must explicitly reward uncertainty reduction across steps rather than assuming final-token supervision will automatically produce faster refinement. That matters because DLMs differ from AR LMs in a fundamental way. They are not only learning what token should appear, but also how much progress should be made per forward pass. If that second part is left implicit, a model may reach decent final quality while making very little information gain per step, which defeats the point of parallel generation.

From a pretraining perspective, the paper is valuable for three reasons. First, it extends on-policy learning beyond RL or post-training into the generative modeling objective itself. Second, it introduces entropy contraction as a direct proxy for refinement speed, which is closer to the real optimization target of parallel decoding than plain token-level supervision. Third, it treats trajectory-dependent training as a joint algorithm-systems problem, which is likely necessary for any serious scaling attempt in diffusion or any-order language modeling.c:T9a0,ORL has two tightly coupled components: an on-policy learning algorithm and a trajectory-aware training system.

On the algorithmic side, the first move is to train on exact model-generated residual trajectories. Instead of relying on offline residual states, ORL rolls out the current model online. At each denoising step, the model takes the current partially denoised block together with the residual context from the previous step, produces a new predictive distribution and a new residual state, and continues the rollout. This aligns the training distribution with the inference distribution. The key claim is that learning to consume someone else's residuals is not the same as learning to consume your own.

The second ingredient is stabilization of the residual pathway. The authors observe that under a shared optimizer, the residual branch can drift much faster than the backbone. ORL therefore rescales the residual pathway so that its effective update pace better matches the backbone. It also applies entropy-based supervision gating: positions that are already sufficiently confident are not pushed to sharpen further. This prevents over-contraction and reduces the risk of amplifying early mistakes.

The third ingredient is the adjacent entropy-contraction objective. Final-token supervision tells the model where each intermediate state should end up, but not how much progress each step should make. The paper shows that under standard supervision, entropy often stays nearly flat across denoising steps, and tokens-per-forward barely improves. ORL explicitly encourages the predictive distribution at step t+1 to be lower-entropy than at step t, subject to the gating mechanism above. This directly optimizes refinement speed: each forward pass should not only be more correct, but also more decisive.

On the systems side, ORL implements exact on-policy residual learning at scale. It asynchronously captures residual states off the decoding critical path, uses memory-adaptive transport to decide whether states stay resident or are offloaded, packs irregular trajectory states into dense training batches, and uses a compute-aware scheduler to balance rollout and training workloads across GPUs. The paper also mentions specialized staircase-style attention and packing to handle the irregular structure induced by progressive unmasking. These details are not peripheral; they are what make the training recipe practical beyond toy settings.d:T9ba,The experiments are organized around three questions: whether on-policy learning is necessary, whether entropy contraction improves refinement speed, and whether the resulting training pipeline is scalable.

The paper first provides a useful qualitative observation: intermediate residual predictions often already contain strong signals of the final tokens before commitment. That supports the idea that residual context is a meaningful computational state rather than a noisy byproduct. It then presents a key negative result: with ordinary intermediate supervision alone, entropy remains almost flat across denoising steps and tokens-per-forward barely increases. This is one of the most important empirical findings in the paper, because it shows that final correctness does not automatically translate into faster parallel refinement.

In the main comparisons, ORL improves the speed–quality Pareto over offline residual learning, non-on-policy variants, and versions without entropy contraction. The abstract situates the work in the context of residual DLMs such as DiffusionGemma, which already reaches 20+ tokens per forward and around 1,230 tok/s on one H100. ORL's contribution is not merely a single throughput number; it is that residual context becomes a learned mechanism for sustained uncertainty reduction across steps while preserving or improving quality. The paper emphasizes metrics such as quality under different forward budgets, average tokens per forward, entropy trajectories across denoising steps, and training stability.

The ablations are especially informative. Removing on-policy rollout leads to a train-test mismatch in residual trajectories and hurts both quality and TPF. Removing residual rescaling makes optimization less stable. Removing entropy gating causes over-sharpening. Removing the adjacent entropy-contraction loss substantially weakens entropy reduction across steps, showing that the model does not naturally learn aggressive but controlled refinement from final supervision alone.

The systems experiments show that exact on-policy residual learning can be run with acceptable throughput and utilization once asynchronous trajectory capture, state transport, and dense packing are in place.
