18:T538,The announcement describes several prototypes. Hypothesis Generation is built with Co-Scientist. A researcher defines a research challenge, then a multi-agent “idea tournament” generates, debates, and evaluates hypotheses, with claims supported by clickable citations. The important design choice is iterative critique and evidence checking, not one-shot ideation. Computational Discovery is built with AlphaEvolve and ERA. It is described as an agentic research engine that generates and scores thousands of code variations in parallel, allowing researchers to explore modeling approaches for areas such as solar forecasting and epidemiology. Literature Insights is built with NotebookLM. It searches scientific literature, organizes results into tables with user-defined searchable attributes, supports grounded chat over a curated corpus, and produces artifacts such as reports, slides, infographics, and audio or video overviews. Science Skills connects more than 30 life-science databases and tools in Google Antigravity, including UniProt, AlphaFold Database, AlphaGenome API, and InterPro, so that agents can perform structural bioinformatics and genomic workflows.
