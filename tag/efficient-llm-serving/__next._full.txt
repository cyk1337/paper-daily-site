18:T8bd,方法核心是 EpiKV。作者提出用 token 生成时引起的内部表示变化来衡量其重要性，而不是看它被多少后续 token attend 到。具体地，对每一层 l、每个生成位置 t，计算相邻 token 的 hidden state 差分幅度 g_l(t)，也就是 residual stream 在该层的 L2 change。直觉是，如果某个 token 让模型内部状态发生明显跃迁，它更可能对应一个中间结论、推理步骤收束、搜索方向切换，或者某种“epiphany token”；这类 token 更值得保留在 cache 里。

全文里最有意思的发现是层带结构。作者在一个 32 层 reasoning model 上，用 counterfactual occlusion labels 做相关性分析，发现第 7–13 层的 hidden-state change 与 token 重要性正相关，称为 Band A；第 18–25 层则负相关，称为 Band B。于是最终分数不是单层信号，而是两段层带的组合：保留 Band A 高、Band B 低的 token。这个结果说明不同深度的表示变化含义不同，中层更像“引入有用状态更新”，更深层的某些大幅变化反而可能对应局部收束、表面生成或可丢弃内容。

作者还发现原始差分信号带有明显的位置趋势：在同一条推理轨迹里，某些层的 g_l(t) 会随位置单调变化，导致分数混入“早晚位置”而不只是“内容重要性”。这是全文很重要的 methodological finding，因为很多看似有效的相关性其实部分来自跨样本位置结构。为此他们引入 causal rolling z-score，把每层当前差分与过去窗口内的均值和方差比较，得到局部异常程度，再用 z_10(t)-z_21(t) 一类组合分数做排序。这样做的意义是把绝对幅值变成相对局部偏离，尽量去掉位置污染。

工程上，EpiKV 的设计边界很明确：只使用标准 forward pass 已经暴露的 hidden states，以及现成缓存中的 K/V，不需要训练额外分类器，不需要 custom kernel，也不需要 attention matrix。因此它可以直接跑在 FlashAttention inference stack 上。作者把这种性质单独定义为 FA2-compatible，这一点比单纯的 eviction 精度更重要，因为它决定了方法是否真能在长推理部署里落地。19:T856,实验主要围绕两件事：一是 token 重要性代理是否靠谱，二是在真实推理任务上的 eviction 效果和系统收益。作者先构造了 counterfactual importance labels，方法是对缓存中的 token 做遮蔽/移除，观察对后续生成或任务结果的影响，用它作为近似真值来评估不同打分信号。基于这些标签，他们发现 attention-based 信号不稳定，而 hidden-state change 在特定层带上与重要性有更强相关。Band A 正相关、Band B 负相关的层分布，是全文最关键的分析结果之一。

任务评测使用 MATH-500 和 AIME-2024，面向长链式推理场景。结果上，在可部署的 cache budget 下，attention-matrix-free 的 EpiKV 与最强 attention-based baseline 持平或更好。摘要里没有给出完整精度表，但明确指出在两套数学推理基准上都达到 match or exceed strongest attention-based baselines。系统层面，EpiKV 在相同 budget 下可达到最高 2.8× 的速度提升，而且不会触发 attention matrix 的内存墙。作者给出一个很直观的工程对比：在 80GB A100 上，若开启 output_attentions=True，eager kernel 会在远短于多数 reasoning trace 的长度上耗尽显存；而 FlashAttention 路径可以把可处理长度再推高一个数量级，文中表述为可扩展到 16× 更长的上下文范围。

另一个值得注意的实验点是位置去趋势。作者展示了如果直接用原始 hidden-state diff，整体相关性看起来不错，但在单条轨迹内排序质量会被位置偏置破坏；引入 causal rolling z-score 后，eviction 质量明显恢复。这说明他们不是只报一个有效结果，而是把信号失真的来源拆开分析了。

局限也比较清楚。层带结构是在特定 32 层 reasoning model 和竞赛数学数据上发现的，能否迁移到不同架构、不同深度、不同任务域，还需要更多验证。另一个问题是 counterfactual importance label 本身只是代理真值，不等于真实最优缓存策略；但在这类问题上，作者已经给出了相对扎实的验证路径。1a:T8ec,The method, EpiKV, scores tokens by the change they induce in the model’s internal representation rather than by how much later tokens attend to them. For each layer l and decode position t, the paper computes a hidden-state difference magnitude g_l(t), essentially the L2 change in the residual stream between adjacent generated tokens. The intuition is that tokens causing large state transitions are more likely to correspond to meaningful reasoning events: a committed intermediate result, a step boundary, or a shift from exploration to convergence. These are called epiphany tokens.

A particularly interesting finding from the full paper is the two-band layer anatomy. Using counterfactual occlusion labels as a proxy for token importance, the authors find that hidden-state change in layers 7 to 13 is positively correlated with importance, while layers 18 to 25 are negatively correlated, in a 32-layer reasoning model. They call these Band A and Band B. The final score therefore combines signals from both bands, favoring tokens with high Band-A change and low Band-B change. This suggests that representation dynamics at different depths carry qualitatively different meanings.

The paper also identifies a positional confound in the raw signal. Within a single reasoning trace, some layerwise change magnitudes drift monotonically with position, so absolute scores partly track whether a token is early or late rather than whether it is truly important. This is one of the strongest methodological points in the paper. To remove that confound, the authors apply a causal rolling z-score: each token’s layerwise change is normalized relative to a trailing window, and the eviction score is formed from these locally normalized deviations. This converts absolute magnitude into local surprise and substantially improves within-trace ranking quality.

From an engineering standpoint, the design is intentionally minimal. EpiKV uses only hidden states already exposed by the standard forward pass and the existing cached K/V tensors. It requires no training, no auxiliary classifier, no custom kernel, and no attention matrix. That makes it directly compatible with FlashAttention-based inference stacks, which is arguably the paper’s most important practical property.1b:T852,The experiments focus on two questions: whether the proposed importance proxy is meaningful, and whether it improves real decoding under cache budgets. The authors first build counterfactual importance labels by removing or masking cached tokens and measuring the effect on downstream generation or task outcomes. Against this proxy, they show that attention-based signals are weaker and less stable than hidden-state-difference signals from selected layers. The Band-A positive correlation and Band-B negative correlation pattern is one of the central empirical findings.

Task-level evaluation is conducted on MATH-500 and AIME-2024, both chosen to stress long reasoning traces. At deployable cache budgets, the attention-matrix-free EpiKV matches or exceeds the strongest attention-based baselines on both benchmarks. The abstract does not expose the full accuracy table, but the paper’s claim is clear: removing the attention matrix does not require giving up eviction quality. On the systems side, EpiKV runs up to 2.8x faster than attention-based eviction at equal budget and avoids the memory blow-up caused by materializing attention. The paper also highlights a practical scaling gap: enabling output_attentions on an 80GB A100 exhausts memory at lengths shorter than most reasoning traces, while the FlashAttention path scales much further, reported as up to 16x longer context.

Another strong experimental point is the detrending analysis. The authors show that raw hidden-state differences can look correlated with importance in aggregate while still ranking poorly within a single trace because of positional drift. The causal rolling z-score fixes much of that problem. This makes the paper more convincing than a simple benchmark win.

The limitations are also clear. The layer-band structure is discovered on a specific 32-layer reasoning model and competition-math tasks, so transfer to other architectures, depths, and domains remains open.
