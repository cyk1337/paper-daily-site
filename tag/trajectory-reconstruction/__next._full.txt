(2) Decoupled optimization with black-box trace capture: the harness is treated as an opaque rollout engine. A serving proxy at the model boundary intercepts every model call. (3) Training–inference consistency and stability: a black-box token-in/token-out mechanism aims to preserve faithful token streams, and token-level importance-sampling rollout correction is used to mitigate bias from mismatches between the inference/serving engine and the training engine.
