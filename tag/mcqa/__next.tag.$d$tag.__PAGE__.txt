b:T41e,The authors build a controlled testbed with 1B, 3B, and 8B decoder-only LLaMA-style models. English, Spanish, and back-translated English variants are trained to contain the same underlying corpus knowledge while differing in language or wording. This setup is meant to remove common confounders such as data coverage and training budget. They then analyze standard MCQA scoring, where the selected option is the one with the highest log P(a_i | q), optionally normalized by characters or tokens. The flaw is that this probability is computed over one arbitrary answer string. ParaEval changes the evaluation protocol by generating multiple paraphrases for every option, including both correct answers and distractors. Each option receives the score of its most favorable paraphrase, and the model then chooses among options. The same idea is also applied to generative evaluation by expanding the reference set.
