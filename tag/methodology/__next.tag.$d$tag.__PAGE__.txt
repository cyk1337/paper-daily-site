9:T54f,A metric/estimand analysis plus reporting guidelines.
- Binary collapse: for non-degenerate 2×2 confusion matrices, Pearson r, Spearman ρ, Kendall τ_b, phi (ϕ), and MCC coincide for binary vectors; reporting several of them is not independent evidence.
- Cohen’s κ differs mainly via a marginal-sensitive normalization (chance agreement correction), so it answers a slightly different question than pure correlation.
- Symmetry caveat: most symmetric metrics (accuracy/κ/ϕ/MCC/correlations) are invariant to swapping FP and FN, so they cannot distinguish over-prediction vs under-detection; precision/recall are directional, while F1 ignores TN and depends on which class is treated as positive.
- Abstention as selective prediction: different ways to treat CANNOT_ASSESS correspond to different estimands (conditional-on-coverage performance vs counting abstentions as errors vs introducing a third class). Once a third label is admitted, the binary equivalences no longer hold.
- Multi-judge extension: Fleiss’ κ, Krippendorff’s α, and average pairwise ϕ are nearly the same up to small finite-sample corrections, so “metric stacking” remains uninformative.
