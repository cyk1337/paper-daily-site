第一层是表示。作者把离散 token 序列嵌入到每个位置一个 V 维 simplex 顶点的连续空间里，整条序列是 (R^V)^L 中的点。数据分布 μ1 支持在 one-hot 顶点上，先验 μ0 是连续高斯。目标是学一个从连续空间到离散数据流形的 transport。

第二层是动力学。传统 flow matching 学的是随时间变化的速度场 bt(x)，采样要积分 ODE；flow map 学的是两时刻算子 X_{s,t}，但 off-diagonal 项通常需要 teacher 或 bootstrap。DBTM 改学 autonomous，也就是 time-independent 的速度场 b(x)，对应自治 ODE dX_s/ds = b(X_s)。在这个设定下，流映射满足半群性质，且作者利用 time-independent flow 的几何性质构造一个 transport map T(x)，它把任意 ambient point 沿自治流送到某个固定点，最终落在 simplex 顶点支撑的数据流形上。

第三层是训练。关键不是直接监督 T 的终点，而是利用固定点性质导出守恒方程 b·∇T = 0。直观上，沿着流线前进时，最终 transport destination 不应改变，因此 T 在流方向上的导数为零。作者最重要的贡献是把这个 PDE 残差变成可从数据最小化的训练目标，从而不需要 teacher flow。全文里还给出一个可操作的近似训练法：用冻结副本的当前模型作为 bootstrap target，再加一个 Euler correction，迭代回归到更接近真实 transport map 的目标。论文声称，部分训练好的 Tθ 并不是无意义的半成品，而是对应自治流上的中间点；因此在推理时可以反复应用 Tθ 做 iterative refinement，逐步逼近固定点。

另一个关键设计是 partial-context interpolant。它不是简单在全序列上做连续插值，而是保留部分上下文、对其余位置做插值/传输学习。这样做有两个作用：一是更适合条件生成和 reasoning，因为 prompt 或已确定 token 可以作为锚点；二是允许训练和推理时基于置信度或质量做 selective refinement，而不是每一步都全量重写整条序列。

The first layer is representation. A discrete token sequence is embedded into a continuous space where each token is a vertex of the V-dimensional simplex. A full sequence is therefore a point in (R^V)^L. The data distribution lives on one-hot vertices, while the prior is continuous Gaussian noise. The modeling goal is to learn a transport from the ambient continuous space to the discrete data manifold.

The second layer is dynamics. Standard flow matching learns a time-dependent velocity field b_t(x) and requires ODE integration at sampling time. Flow-map methods learn a two-time operator X_{s,t}, but the off-diagonal terms are usually learned either from a teacher or through unstable bootstrapping. DBTM instead learns an autonomous, time-independent velocity field b(x), defining an ODE dX_s/ds = b(X_s). In this setting, the flow has a semigroup structure, and the paper constructs a transport map T(x) that sends any ambient point along the autonomous flow to a fixed point on the simplex-supported data manifold.

The third layer is training. Rather than supervising the terminal point directly, the paper derives a conservation equation, b · ∇T = 0. Intuitively, as one moves along a flow line, the final transport destination should remain unchanged, so T is constant in the flow direction. This is the central technical contribution: the PDE residual is turned into a trainable objective that can be minimized from data, removing the need for a teacher flow. The practical training procedure uses a frozen copy of the current model as a bootstrap target plus an Euler correction, iteratively regressing toward a better approximation of the true transport map. A partially trained T_theta is not treated as a failed endpoint predictor; instead, it is interpreted as mapping to an intermediate point along the autonomous flow. That makes iterative application of T_theta meaningful at inference time.

Another important design is the partial-context interpolant. Instead of interpolating the entire sequence uniformly, the method preserves part of the context and learns transport on the remaining positions. This is important for two reasons: it makes the framework suitable for conditional generation and reasoning, where prompts or already-decided tokens should act as anchors; and it enables confidence- or quality-based selective refinement instead of rewriting the whole sequence at every step.

Relative to prior work, the distinctions are clear. Compared with discrete diffusion, DBTM does not rely on a time-indexed denoising chain. Compared with flow matching, it does not learn b_t(x, t). Compared with flow-map distillation, it does not require a converged teacher to supervise off-diagonal transitions.
