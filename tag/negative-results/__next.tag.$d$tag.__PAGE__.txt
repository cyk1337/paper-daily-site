1. Telemetry-based triage system: Abandons GPU utilization, uses B300-calibrated board power bands combined with three dimensions — cross-rank symmetry and duration — to rapidly classify compute (~700W+), communication (~640W, uniform power across all ranks), data starvation (~575W, scattered cross-rank power), checkpoint/deadlock (~190W; checkpoint only drops power on rank 0 and recovers within 2 minutes, deadlock shows zero power on one rank with full load on others and no recovery), and idle states. It is paired with a one-line monitoring command and decision workflow, requiring no profiler attachment to locate faults.
2. Controlled negative results: Validates that three common optimizations are ineffective at the 16-GPU 32B full fine-tuning scale: local NVMe caching brings no throughput gain over NFS (the bottleneck is tokenization and multi-tenant CPU contention, not storage media), enabling gradient checkpointing reduces throughput by 1.6x (recomputation is pure waste given B300’s sufficient memory), and corrects an earlier misdiagnosis of a power anomaly.
3. Calibrated scaling reference: Provides full-epoch throughput, GPU-hour, and memory footprint measurements for 4/8/16-GPU B300 setups.
4. Failure case and hardening: Identifies an end-of-epoch NCCL deadlock caused by packing imbalance (unequal block counts across ranks after packing; ranks that finish first enter the next collective communication wait early, triggering a permanent deadlock) — a scenario not covered by PyTorch Join or drop_last.
