The value transform uses value statistics and sensitivity from the output-projection blocks. The value-side transform can be folded into model weights, so it adds no online transform cost. The key-side transform and query compensation are applied after headwise normalization, when present, and after RoPE, matching the actual attention-score path. The transform is independent of the scalar quantizer and can be paired with clipped per-token quantizers.
