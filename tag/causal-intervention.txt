18:T40b,HEAL first applies causal noise intervention to multi-head attention outputs. Instead of inferring head importance from attention weights or output projection weights, it perturbs head outputs directly and measures the resulting change in the layer representation. This is a better intervention point because a head with small projection weights can still matter if its activation is large, and a head with large weights can be inactive in a given context. After filtering causally redundant heads, the method uses counterfactual Difference-in-Differences to disentangle visual and language information at the head level. The remaining heads are categorized as redundant, visual, language, or synergy heads. The central observation is that hallucination is associated with distributional drift inside synergy heads, not simply with weak visual heads or strong language heads. HEAL then introduces a dynamic calibration rule with an equilibrium factor α to adjust the visual-language information balance during autoregressive decoding.19:T441,The split-prefill experiments cover four tasks: key-value retrieval, list indexing, language following, and persona. For exact retrieval and indexing, KV-only retains 64% to 98% of full-model accuracy, while recurrent-only collapses to zero. The collapse remains on minimal short-dictionary and short-list versions, so the result is not simply a capacity issue. On behavioral tasks, the direction reverses. Recurrent-only retains 70% to 80% of full language-following accuracy, while KV-only drops to about 1%, a 60 to 80 times gap. For persona, recurrent-only is 3 to 5 times stronger than KV-only. State-swap provides a clean causal test: when two contexts contain different dictionary values and different output-language instructions, the generated answer retrieves the value from the KV-source context and uses the language from the recurrent-source context, with both properties correct on at least 99% of items in both models. A further control shows that KV-only can name the requested language but still answers in English, while recurrent-only better carries the behavioral mode.1a:T6fd,The paper uses Distributed Alignment Search (DAS) within the causal abstraction framework, instead of standard probing or sparse feature decomposition. The motivation is clear. Probes can read out information that is not actually used by the model, while SAE/CLT-style methods may surface interpretable features but still leave faithfulness and stability concerns. DAS searches for low-dimensional, causally relevant subspaces by intervening on internal states between paired examples and identifying the rotations and substitutions that best explain behavioral changes.

The experimental design is unusually important here. The authors build templated minimal pairs for three syntactic constructions across fifteen languages. Coverage is intentionally uneven where typology demands it: Chinese is excluded from number agreement because it lacks grammatical number, and German/Dutch require special handling in filler–gap templates because embedded verbs are clause-final. This matters because the paper is trying to compare mechanisms, not just outcomes, so template alignment and minimal-pair control are central to validity.

The workflow is: verify that the model behaviorally handles the construction, localize a causally implicated subspace within a source language, then transplant that intervention into the corresponding target-language position and measure whether behavior changes in the expected direction. The paper also includes controls such as random-label tests and mismatched interventions to show that the effect is not a generic perturbation artifact. A further step links transfer strength to typological similarity, leading to the claim that multilingual syntactic mechanisms are organized around typological hubs rather than arbitrary language-specific partitions.1b:T59b,The study spans 4 multilingual LMs, 15 languages, and 3 syntactic phenomena. The models differ in family, size, and training setup, which helps the authors argue that the findings are not tied to a single architecture or checkpoint. Before any interpretability analysis, they run behavioral validation to ensure the models actually exhibit the target syntactic competence on the constructed stimuli.

The main empirical findings come in three layers. First, cross-lingual mechanism transfer is consistently observed across all three constructions, not just in a few closely related language pairs. Second, transfer is graded rather than binary: stronger transfer tends to occur between typologically closer languages. Third, some languages behave like hubs, with interventions localized in those languages transferring more broadly to others. That suggests multilingual models may organize syntactic abstractions around a small number of reusable structural centers.

The controls are important. Random-label and other non-specific intervention settings substantially weaken the effect, which supports the claim that the discovered subspaces are causally tied to the target syntactic behavior rather than reflecting generic representation damage. This is not a benchmark-improvement paper, so the key numbers are intervention effect sizes, transfer success rates, and their relation to typological structure, not downstream task gains.1c:T7b8,SCIT，Suffix Cache Interchange Test，本质上是一个 cache-level counterfactual intervention protocol。给定 recipient 问题 r 和 source 问题 s，先构造一个精确可验证的反事实：source 提供某个中间变量的替代值，把它与 recipient 其余变量组合后，得到已知正确的 counterfactual answer。然后让 latent-CoT 模型分别在 r 和 s 上 rollout 到某个 latent step t，在这个 patch 点把 source 的某个缓存切片插入 recipient，对比 recipient 的答案是否朝 source 定义的反事实移动。

方法不是只做一次 patch，而是做一整套 intervention grid。维度包括：patch 哪个 cache segment；只换 K、只换 V、还是 K/V 一起换；是否同时复制当前 hidden state；是否使用语义相近但答案相同的 source control；是否做 decoded validation，确认被 patch 的 latent 轨迹确实对应可读出的反事实行为；以及 matched corruption，用同一区域做破坏性干预来测试 necessity。作者据此定义三个状态：active carrier，指最小且 gated 的 cache segment，在 oracle interchange 下足够，并且主要竞争解释无法匹配；carrier shift，指存在可转移效应，但活跃片段与主结论模型不同；no-call，指由于能力、支持度、decoded 行为或控制检查不过关，不能下机制判断。

从全文看，论文最重要的技术细节有三点。第一，它强调 exact source-recipient counterfactual，而不是开放式 prompt patching，这样可以避免目标答案不明确。第二，它把 sufficiency 和 necessity 分开，且 necessity 通过 matched corruption 而不是随意噪声注入来做，减少“哪里都能破坏”的伪结论。第三，它把 hidden state 与 cache object 明确区分：h_t 不是 cache 的一部分，只有显式复制时才算干预对象。这让“是当前状态在算，还是历史 value 轨迹在承载”这个问题可以被单独检验。1d:T74d,SCIT, the Suffix Cache Interchange Test, is a cache-level counterfactual intervention protocol. For a recipient problem r and a source problem s, the method first constructs an exact, verifiable counterfactual: the source supplies a changed intermediate quantity, which is then combined with the recipient’s remaining variables to define a known counterfactual answer. Both source and recipient are rolled out to a latent step t. At that patch point, a selected cache slice from the source is transplanted into the recipient, optionally together with the current hidden state, and the test checks whether the recipient moves toward the source-defined counterfactual answer.

The protocol is deliberately grid-like rather than a single patch experiment. It varies: which cache segment is patched; whether only K, only V, or both are copied; whether the hidden state is also copied; whether semantic source controls with the same answer are used; whether decoded validation confirms that the patched latent trajectory corresponds to the intended counterfactual behavior; and whether matched corruption on the same region supports necessity. Based on this battery, the paper defines three outcomes: active carrier, carrier shift, and no-call.

Three technical choices are especially important. First, the paper uses exact source-recipient counterfactuals rather than open-ended prompt patching, which keeps the target answer unambiguous. Second, it separates sufficiency from necessity, and tests necessity with matched corruption rather than arbitrary noise, reducing the risk of overcalling mechanisms.
