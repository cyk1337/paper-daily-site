First, the frozen base model is stored with mixed-precision post-training quantization: most linear layers use INT4, the output head uses INT8, and input embeddings use INT16. Since the training setup assumes FP32 activations and FP32 matmul kernels, weights are dequantized on demand: quantized weights live off-chip, are materialized into FP32 only for the current forward or backward operation, and are immediately released afterward. Second, the paper introduces a checkpointing scheme that combines selective activation rematerialization with off-chip tensor offloading. The transformer is represented as an ordered sequence of compute nodes, such as embedding, decoder blocks, and output layer. Only selected boundary activations are cached, intermediate activations are recomputed during backward, and weights or tensors are loaded and evicted as needed. This differs from standard activation checkpointing because it jointly schedules recomputation and off-chip storage. Third, the output-side memory is reduced through softmax approximation over semantically relevant token subsets and logits masking.
