9:T4e9,OrderGPT builds on Any-Order GPT, which separates the decoder-only computation graph from the left-to-right factorization. Variables have fixed identities, while causal attention follows a supplied reveal permutation. The method adds a lightweight OrderMLP that predicts continuous priorities for variables; sorting these priorities gives the generation order. The order is not found by combinatorial search. The model first observes predictions under randomly sampled reveal prefixes. Recurring attention patterns are aggregated into a reciprocal dependency graph. A Spectral Teacher applies a graph-Laplacian construction to recover a continuous axis that places strongly related variables close to each other. Directed attention is then used to orient the axis, preferring variables that are often attended as context to appear before their prediction targets. These continuous priorities supervise OrderMLP. During training, random orders are gradually replaced by the learned order, and the final order is frozen for standard autoregressive likelihood evaluation and generation. Compared with masked diffusion, the model does not average over many orders at convergence.
