9:T406,VideoFlexTok is an autoencoder-style tokenizer with (i) a 2D token organization (time × coarse-to-fine level), (ii) training that induces a prefix-to-detail hierarchy, and (iii) a generative decoder that can decode from any token count. Concretely: it operates in a video VAE latent space for efficiency; uses learnable register tokens per time step and interleaves latent frames and registers in the input sequence. A coarse-to-fine hierarchy along the register dimension is induced via nested dropout that randomly drops a suffix of register tokens, forcing good reconstructions from short prefixes. Because pure reconstruction tends to encode pixel-level detail early, it adds a semantic bias by distilling features from a pretrained self-supervised vision encoder (DINOv2), encouraging early tokens to carry semantic/geometric/motion information. The decoder is trained as a generative rectified flow model, enabling plausible, realistic video generation conditioned on any number of tokens—more tokens yield finer
