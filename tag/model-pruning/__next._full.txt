1b:T900,方法上最关键的设计是 neuron-row zeroing。作者把 Transformer 中所有线性层的输出行——包括 attention 的 Q/K/V/O 和 MLP 的 gate/up/down——统一视为可干预单元。对某一行做 masking，就是把对应权重行和 bias 直接置零，不做微调、不做恢复训练、不加推理时 hook。这样行为变化只能归因于被选中的 row，而不是额外训练步骤。

在 LM 层面，作者把所有层的所有 row 池化成一个全局排序，然后对每个 selector 做 LeRF/MoRF 扫描：
- LeRF：先删最低分的 k%，看 perplexity 是否保持低；
- MoRF：先删最高分的 k%，看 perplexity 是否显著恶化；
- 用 Gap = PPL_MoRF - PPL_LeRF 作为有效性指标。
一个 faithful selector 应该同时满足两点：它判为“不重要”的行删掉后损伤小，它判为“重要”的行删掉后损伤大。

作者比较了 7 类 selector：Random、Magnitude、Wanda、MeanActivation、LRP、IG，以及把 LRP 和 IG 做 Borda 聚合得到的 Consensus-2。这里有两个值得注意的点。其一，Wanda 被改成 row-level 聚合版本，保证和 neuron-row 干预粒度一致。其二，Consensus-2 的动机不是简单集成，而是假设 LRP 和 IG 的误差模式不同，希望通过排序聚合降低单一 attribution 的偏差。

在行为层面，作者没有直接用单条 harmful prompt 做 attribution，而是用 CAST 的 harmful/benign 配对样本构造 contrastive signal。原因很清楚：单 prompt attribution 容易把“处理这个 prompt 内容”的通用神经元也打高分，未必是 refusal-specific 单元；而 harmful-vs-benign 的差分更接近把共享语义抵消掉，只保留与拒答行为相关的子空间。然后仍然用 row zeroing 的方式“安装” refusal，并用 layer-matched random control 做对照：随机选相同层分布、相同行数的 row 置零，检验效果究竟来自 row identity 还是只是来自某些层被削弱。

作者对 faithful 的定义也很克制：不是要求唯一性或必要性，而是要求 sufficiency 和 specificity。因为 refusal 子空间本身是冗余的，多个不相交 row 集都可能足以触发同类行为，所以 selector 只要能稳定落到某个有效充分集上，就算因果上可信。1c:T8de,The central intervention is neuron-row zeroing. A neuron row is defined as one output row of a linear projection matrix in attention or MLP modules. Zeroing a row means setting that row of W and the corresponding bias entry to zero. No finetuning, no recovery, and no inference-time steering are used. This is important because any behavioral change can then be attributed to the selected rows themselves rather than to surrounding pipeline machinery.

For the LM-level audit, all rows across all transformer blocks are pooled into a single global ranking. The paper evaluates selectors with LeRF and MoRF sweeps:
- LeRF masks the lowest-ranked k% rows and checks whether damage stays small.
- MoRF masks the highest-ranked k% rows and checks whether damage becomes large.
- The main validity metric is Gap(S, k) = PPL_MoRF - PPL_LeRF.
A faithful selector should preserve performance when removing rows it calls unimportant, and sharply hurt performance when removing rows it calls important.

Seven selectors are tested: Random, Magnitude, Wanda, MeanActivation, LRP, IG, and Consensus-2, a Borda-style aggregation of LRP and IG. The aggregation is motivated by complementary failure modes: LRP propagates relevance through the network, while IG integrates gradients along a path, so agreement is not assumed to be sufficient and disagreement may itself carry signal.

For the behavior-level audit, the authors avoid single-prompt attribution because it tends to highlight prompt-processing neurons rather than refusal-specific ones. Instead they use contrastive harmful-versus-benign pairs from CAST, so shared semantic content is canceled and the remaining attribution is more tightly tied to refusal behavior. The same row-zeroing intervention is then used to install refusal on instruction-tuned models. A layer-matched random control keeps the same per-layer row counts but randomizes row identity, which isolates whether the effect comes from specific rows or merely from weakening certain depths.

The paper uses a restrained notion of faithfulness: sufficiency and specificity, not uniqueness or necessity. That is a sensible choice because the responsible subspace appears redundant, so multiple disjoint row sets may be sufficient to induce the same behavior.1d:T681,The LM-level study covers five base models: LLaMA-3.2-1B, LLaMA-3.2-3B, LLaMA-3.1-8B, Qwen3-8B, and Gemma-3-12B. Evaluation uses WikiText-2 perplexity and downstream accuracy. The headline result is that attribution-based selectors, especially LRP, IG, and Consensus-2, identify dispensable rows far more accurately than activation-, magnitude-, and random-based baselines, with gains reported at two to four orders of magnitude. Another notable finding is that the most rank-stable selector is the least causally faithful. This directly challenges the common use of rank stability as a validation proxy.

At the behavior level, refusal is installed on four instruction-tuned models and evaluated on CAST, SorryBench, OR-Bench-Hard, plus benign utility and fluency checks. Contrastive attribution can drive refusal close to saturation on categories such as hate and crime while preserving benign accuracy and language quality reasonably well. The layer-matched random control fails to reproduce the effect, which supports the claim that row identity matters more than depth allocation.

Two additional observations are especially useful. First, LRP and IG share only a small fraction of their top-ranked rows, yet both can install refusal. This suggests the underlying refusal subspace is redundant rather than uniquely localized. Second, the better attribution method flips between LLaMA and Qwen, indicating strong architecture dependence. The most informative part of the experimental section is not a single benchmark number, but the way the paper rules out alternative explanations such as rank stability, calibration artifacts, and layer-distribution effects.1e:T463,For pretraining research, the main value is conceptual and architectural. The paper reframes late-layer under-utilization as a property of residual signal routing rather than a generic optimization failure. That matters because it changes what kinds of interventions are plausible: if the issue is missing gradient diversity, rescaling gradients is not enough. This has implications for how to think about depth scaling, deep supervision, early-exit designs such as LayerSkip, and parameter allocation across depth. The second contribution is more practical: it suggests that uniform-width stacking may be a poor match to how training signal is distributed, and that tapering width with depth can improve deployment efficiency at fixed training FLOPs. The pruning result is also relevant because it uses accumulated training gradients as an online structural importance signal, avoiding expensive post-hoc analysis. This is not a recipe-changing result on the scale of a new optimizer or tokenizer, but it is a serious attempt to connect training dynamics, architecture shape, and pruning through one structural hypothesis.1f:T6f2,The technical story has three parts. First, the paper defines GFA in Pre-LN residual Transformers. Writing the network as x_{l+1} = x_l + F_l(x_l), the input gradient at layer l can be expanded as a sum over identity and downstream functional paths. The key quantity is not the full combinatorial path count, but a tractable proxy: how many distinct downstream signal channels are aggregated into a layer’s gradient. This fan-in decreases with depth. With deep supervision, the imbalance becomes even stronger because shallow layers receive additional gradient hierarchies from auxiliary heads. Second, the paper tests the hypothesis with both correlational and interventional evidence.
