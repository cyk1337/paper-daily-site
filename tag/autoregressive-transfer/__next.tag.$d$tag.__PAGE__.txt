5:T85f,方法核心是 OPDLM 和其中的 On-Policy Distillation。学生模型来自一个 ARLM checkpoint，但把 causal attention 改成 bidirectional attention，使其可以对被 mask 的任意位置做条件建模。教师模型则保留原始冻结 ARLM。关键难点在于，AR teacher 和 DLM student 的状态空间不同：teacher 习惯在完整左前缀上做 next-token prediction，student 则在部分已知、部分 mask 的序列状态上做去噪。作者的处理方式不是把 teacher 直接搬到随机 mask 状态上，而是让 student 先按自己的 diffusion 解码策略 rollout 出一条反向去噪轨迹，从中采样一个部分去噪状态 x_t，再对其中仍被 mask 的位置做蒸馏。

蒸馏目标的构造很讲究。对每个 masked 位置 i，student 输出该位置的 token 分布；teacher 则读取终态序列 x_0 中该位置之前的 causal prefix，给出 AR 条件分布，作为该位置的监督信号。也就是说，teacher 提供的是“如果按 AR 方式生成到这里，应该偏向哪些 token”，student 学的是“在当前部分去噪状态下，对这些尚未恢复的位置给出与 teacher 一致的分布”。这样做同时解决两件事：一是知识保留，因为监督直接来自原 AR 模型的 logits，而不是只靠 diffusion NELBO 重新学；二是 train-test mismatch，因为训练样本来自 student 自己的 on-policy rollout，而不是离线随机 mask。

论文还加入 rollout-length curriculum。直觉上，训练初期学生模型还不稳定，如果一开始就在很长的去噪轨迹上做 on-policy 学习，状态分布会太差，蒸馏信号也更噪。于是作者逐步增加 rollout 长度，让学生先在较短、较容易的轨迹上对齐，再扩展到更接近真实推理的状态。这个设计和序列决策里的 curriculum / DAGGER 风格很接近，但这里的监督源是冻结 AR teacher。另一个值得注意的点是，作者把 OPDLM 明确定位成 general-purpose DLM 和 task-specific DLM 两种用法：既可以把通用 AR 基座转成通用 DLM，也可以在特定任务上做更便宜的转换。6:T802,The central idea is OPDLM, trained with On-Policy Distillation. The student starts from an ARLM checkpoint whose causal attention is replaced with bidirectional attention, enabling denoising over arbitrary masked positions. The teacher is the original frozen ARLM. The main challenge is that the teacher and student do not share the same state space: the teacher predicts the next token from a causal prefix, while the student denoises partially observed sequences.

The paper resolves this by letting the student generate its own reverse denoising trajectories. At each training step, the student rolls out a trajectory under its own diffusion decoding policy, and one partially denoised state x_t is sampled from that trajectory. Distillation is then applied only to the still-masked positions in x_t. For each masked position i, the student predicts a token distribution conditioned on the partially denoised sequence, while the teacher provides target logits conditioned on the causal prefix of the terminal sequence x_0 up to position i. In effect, the teacher says what an AR model would prefer at that position, and the student learns to match that preference on the states it will actually encounter during inference.

This design addresses both mismatches at once. Knowledge retention improves because supervision comes directly from the original AR teacher rather than from relearning everything through a diffusion NELBO. Train–test mismatch is reduced because training states are on-policy student states, not uniformly random masks. The paper also introduces a rollout-length curriculum: early in training, the student is supervised on shorter and easier trajectories, and the rollout horizon is gradually increased as the model stabilizes. That choice is important for optimization, since long on-policy trajectories from an immature student would otherwise create noisy and low-quality supervision. The framework is presented both as a general-purpose AR-to-DLM conversion method and as a cheaper task-specific DLM adaptation method.7:T597,The most informative results are about efficiency and the necessity of on-policy training. On efficiency, the headline number is striking: OPDLM-8B uses only 0.066B training tokens and 4.2 × 10^18 FLOPs, while establishing a new known Pareto frontier on AIME-24. Relative to prior DLM or AR-to-DLM conversion systems such as LLaDA-8B, Dream-7B, SDAR-8B, and Fast-dLLM-v2-7B, the required training tokens are reduced by roughly 15× to 7,000×. That is not a marginal saving; it turns conversion into something much closer to heavy post-training than continued pretraining.

On task performance, the paper evaluates across general-purpose and reasoning-heavy settings, including math and coding, and also studies task-specific conversion. The emphasis is not on claiming the single best absolute score everywhere, but on maintaining strong performance under dramatically lower training cost. The key ablation compares on-policy and off-policy distillation, and on-policy consistently wins, directly supporting the train–inference mismatch argument. Additional appendices study smaller-scale settings, the effect of teacher size, multi-seed evaluation on GPQA-Diamond and AIME variants, and comparisons against standard supervised fine-tuning. The inference section also shows that the converted DLM can exploit multi-token decoding, so the method is not merely changing the training loss while keeping AR-like inference behavior.8:T527,Read it in the order of problem setup, method, efficiency evidence, and ablations.
