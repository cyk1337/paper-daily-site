a:T4b1,The method is built around the notion of consilience: a conclusion is reliable when the model implicitly recognizes multiple plausible approaches and still converges to the same answer.

Confidence signal:
- Token-level confidence is computed from the top-K distribution at each step (mean negative log-prob over top-K tokens). Sharper distributions imply higher confidence.
- Baseline VF-TTS selects the rollout with the highest average confidence over the whole sequence.

Failure analysis:
- On easy/medium problems, correct traces tend to have higher average confidence.
- On hard subsets (e.g., where the model’s accuracy is <20%), the confidence distributions for correct vs incorrect traces become inseparable or even reversed, making confidence maximization unreliable.

Consilience metric:
- Instead of maximizing confidence uniformly, it enforces temporal asymmetry: penalize high confidence early (to avoid premature commitment) while rewarding high confidence late (to ensure convergence).
- Practically, sample multiple traces, compute the consilience score for each, and select the top-ranked trace.
