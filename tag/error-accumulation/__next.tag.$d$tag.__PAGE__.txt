a:T457,The framework defines a ground-truth answer map, a hypothesis answer map, and a chain rule that maps current question-answer states to the next question. For recoverable inputs, the ground-truth trajectory can recover the final answer. Under a quasimetric loss assumption, the reasoning risk decomposes into two terms. Oracle-trajectory risk measures the hypothesis error when the trajectory is guided by the ground truth up to the final step. Trajectory-mismatch risk measures the extra error caused by the hypothesis driving the chain rule along a different trajectory. The oracle term is connected to domain adaptation: the ground-truth CoT trajectory pushes the test distribution into a target distribution, and CoT helps when that distribution is close to the training distribution. The mismatch term captures the cost of self-generated reasoning. The paper proves that without stability assumptions on the loss, the answer map, and the chain rule, this cost can be arbitrarily large.
