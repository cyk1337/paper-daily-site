At a decoding state s_t, the next-token distribution is represented by softmax logits. For a sampled token y_t with advantage A, the update from A log π(y_t|s_t) increases the sampled token logit and redistributes probability mass according to the current distribution. The paper derives a first-order approximation of the local entropy change ΔH_t after this logit perturbation. Entropy polarity is the resulting signed quantity that predicts whether the update expands or contracts entropy and by how much. The asymmetry is important: reinforcing an already high-probability continuation tends to make the distribution sharper, while entropy expansion usually requires a lower-probability sample with positive advantage or a strong correction from the surrounding distribution. Papo then uses polarity as an online control signal.
