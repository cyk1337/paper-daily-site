9:T56f,AURORA-LM fully decouples latent space construction and generative modeling to eliminate conflicting objectives between the two stages. First, a high-capacity text autoencoder is trained independently to produce full-width continuous latent sequences of the same length as the original token sequence, with no sequence downsampling. The autoencoder training objective focuses exclusively on exact text reconstruction, with no added regularization to smooth latents or make them easier to generate, ensuring latents carry all information present in the original text. Second, a diffusion model is tailored to model the distribution of these high-dimensional full-width latents, using a block-causal attention structure: sequences are split into contiguous chunks, with strict causal dependence across chunks (later chunks are generated only after previous chunks are fully determined), while diffusion-based parallel generation operates within each chunk. This design preserves the parallel throughput of diffusion models while maintaining the causal structure required for long-form text coherence, avoiding the long-range logical inconsistencies common to non-causal full-sequence diffusion.
