1) Capability as the target: define domain-level latent capabilities (six domains) and approximate each capability c_k(M) by aggregating normalized scores across multiple benchmarks in that domain, reducing single-benchmark noise.
2) SuperValid OOD synthesis: use an LLM-driven pipeline to distill capability-relevant “knowledge components” from benchmarks, then expand them into diverse, capability-aware general text. The design goal is to avoid copying benchmark surface forms, thereby removing benchmark-specific artifacts.
