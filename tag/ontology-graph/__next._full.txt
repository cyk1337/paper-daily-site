19:T4ec,CORTEX builds a three-layer heterogeneous structure called OCG.
1) High-Quality Content Layer: a standard CC WARC-to-text pipeline with an emphasis on retaining structured metadata (e.g., publication time) and adding components like temporal relevance filtering. Quality is assessed via distillation: a teacher LLM provides multi-aspect ratings; a 0.3B student is trained to reproduce the teacher’s ordering/grades at scale. The key is the Ordinal-Aware Regression (OAR) loss, treating quality as ordinal rather than pure regression or hard classification.
2) Lightweight Ontology Layer: an LLM-driven, automatically evolving hierarchical “concept chain” taxonomy. The design choice is to keep it lightweight (taxonomy-level organization) instead of building a full knowledge graph with costly entity linking and relation extraction.
3) Alignment Layer: links documents to ontology concepts with Typicality–Fidelity–Specificity (TFS) weighted edges. The intent is to support both domain extraction (fine-grained slicing) and cross-domain association analysis / neighbor-chain expansion.
