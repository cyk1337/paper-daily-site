18:T7b4,The method has two main components. The first is Causal History Routing (CHR). Streaming AR video generation proceeds block by block, and each new block depends on a historical KV cache. The authors argue that meaningful exploration should happen through alternative uses of historical context rather than through noise injection in latent space. In CHR, the sink cache is kept fixed to preserve global anchors, while the local cache is stochastically routed to create branch-specific contexts. The most recent frames remain fixed, while earlier local-memory slots are selectively varied across branches. This is intended to produce semantically different but causally grounded continuations. The key claim is that such exploration stays on-manifold because it does not alter the generator into a stochastic diffusion process.

The second component is an ODE-native surrogate policy. Prior ODE-based GRPO variants such as NeighborGRPO approximate the surrogate policy using Euclidean distances between generated samples. The authors argue that this assumes an overly uniform geometry in latent space and may not reflect the model’s actual preference structure. They instead define Trajectory Velocity Energy (TVE) in the flow-matching velocity-field space, measuring how likely the current policy is to reproduce a branch trajectory under the native ODE dynamics. A Gibbs-form surrogate policy is then built from TVE, and combined with group-relative rewards to form a reward-weighted contrastive flow-matching objective. The main design choice is clear: preference optimization is embedded directly into the model’s learned velocity field rather than imposed through external sample-space geometry or SDE transition kernels.

Methodologically, the paper is internally consistent. If the base model is a few-step distilled AR video generator with causal attention and KV caching, then both exploration and optimization are designed to exploit exactly those structures.19:T4ad,The experiments are conducted on multiple distilled AR video generators, covering both single-prompt short-video and multi-prompt long-video settings. The reported outcome is that KVPO improves human-preference alignment over reward-weighted distillation baselines, noise-driven exploration methods, and ODE-based methods using neighborhood-distance surrogates. The qualitative claim is that CHR produces branch diversity at the semantic and narrative level, while noise injection mostly changes texture or local appearance. The TVE-based surrogate is also claimed to better reflect branch quality than Euclidean-distance approximations.

3) Flow-model perspective: the method is motivated/justified through connections to flow-based generative modeling (continuous normalizing flows / flow matching), explaining why jointly learning the flow distributions yields stable guidance.
