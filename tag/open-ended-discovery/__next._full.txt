(1) Formalization: evolutionary discovery is cast as a POMDP with a hidden mechanism θ_sci; proposing a program yields a score from an evaluator. An “idea” is defined as a high-level mechanistic property of programs that causally drives score differences. The theory section argues that without identifying such ideas, programs are effectively unrelated and search degenerates toward exhaustive exploration; conversely, identifying ideas requires deliberate contrastive exploration (programs with/without an idea). (2) Idea-factor extraction: built on COAT, the system searches for high-level factors that explain score differences by training/using a pairwise comparator over archived programs—given (p_i, p_j), predict which scores higher. Factors that improve this pairwise ordering are admitted as reusable idea factors. The key design choice is pairwise ranking (difference-explaining) rather than raw score regression. (3) Idea-level control: each admitted idea factor becomes an arm in a multi-armed bandit that selects which idea to emphasize when generating new candidates.
