9:T606,There are three central design choices. First, the task is a 26-week factored POMDP with six hidden factor processes governing demand, supply, logistics, quality, and related risks. Each week the agent observes a dashboard of symptoms—inventory, in-transit orders, realized demand, freight quotes, berth waiting times, canal transit counts, supplier scorecards, and quality reports—then chooses replenishment actions. Second, the hidden dynamics are intentionally factorized and the event tape is fixed, which makes a fair reference policy tractable: an exact Bayes filter is run per factor, and a rollout policy acts on the same observation stream seen by the LLM. The key point is not that the oracle is strong, but that it is non-privileged. Third, the benchmark defines decomposed metrics instead of relying on total cost alone. A skill score places the agent between a symptom-blind base-stock floor and the fair Bayesian oracle. Detection lag measures how quickly the agent names the active hidden factor in its rationale. Knowing-doing rate measures how often the agent still incurs stockout-like action failures during stress weeks it has already diagnosed correctly. The benchmark also introduces isolated, persistent, and compound stress profiles, keeping the world model fixed while varying overlap among hidden failures.
