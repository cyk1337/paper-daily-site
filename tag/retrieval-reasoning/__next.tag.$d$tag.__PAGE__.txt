9:T49c,LOTAPO constructs self-generated process rewards with backward leave-one-turn attribution. For a completed search trajectory, each valid search turn consists of the model-generated search action and the corresponding retrieval observation. The method replaces one such turn with a fixed [DELETE] placeholder while keeping the downstream interactions intact, then measures how the current policy’s mean log-likelihood of the gold answer changes. This Answer-Likelihood Gain estimates the turn’s contribution in the full reasoning context: if removing a turn lowers the gold-answer likelihood, the turn receives positive attribution; if removal increases it, the turn may be redundant or misleading. The raw gains are noisy, so LOTAPO applies robust scaling, a bounded tanh transform, group normalization, and sign-consistency gating. The gate keeps only process advantages whose normalized direction agrees with the raw likelihood-gain direction.
