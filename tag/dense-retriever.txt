The last hidden states at these token positions are treated as latent thoughts and latent subqueries. Since these states are produced in a single forward pass, the model avoids the sequential cost of generating long intermediate text.

The central technical issue is retrieval. A latent subquery cannot be directly fed into a standard text retriever. The paper aligns the LLM-generated latent subquery with a pretrained dense retriever in embedding space. A natural-language subquery induces a similarity distribution over documents through the retriever. The latent subquery induces another distribution. Training minimizes the KL divergence between these two distributions. This avoids requiring large-scale supervised query-document pairs for intermediate subqueries, which are usually unavailable in agentic RAG training setups.
