数据设计也比较讲究。概念注入任务使用 60 个概念、10 个语义类别的层级化概念集，每个概念由 Mistral-7B-Instruct 生成 400 句描述句。这样做的好处是概念覆盖面比单一属性更广，也避免 steering 只在少数 topic 上成立。对于 steering，target sentence 是目标概念句，source sentence 从其他概念中随机采样；对于 SFT，则直接在这 400 句上训练。概念移除任务选 toxicity mitigation，但这里作者区分了 steering 和 SFT 的训练需求：steering 只需要 toxic/non-toxic 两组 continuation 来学方向；SFT 则必须构造“高毒 prompt + 低毒 continuation”的配对样本，否则模型学不到在诱导性 prompt 下仍保持无毒输出的能力。这一点说明作者对不同控制方法的训练信号需求理解得比较细。

评估上，论文的核心贡献之一是把“控制成功”和“文本质量”拆开。它用 LLM-as-judge 评估概念注入/移除成功率、fluency、instruction following，同时补充一组便宜的文本统计和 BERT/corpus-based 指标。作者特别分析了 perplexity 的失效原因：当模型通过重复、模板化短句、或异常长度分布来“迎合”目标概念时，PPL 可能并不差，但文本已经明显不自然。相比之下，type-token ratio 这类词汇多样性指标与 fluency 更一致。a:Ta0e,The work is a systematic comparison across three families of conditioning methods. First, prompting: for concept injection, the authors build ten prompt templates such as “You are a chatbot that specializes in <concept>,” and prepend them to generic generation prompts; for toxicity mitigation, they use prior anti-toxicity prompt templates. Second, LoRA-based supervised fine-tuning: low-rank adapters are trained on attention modules with cross-entropy loss over target concept sentences, using AdamW, rank-2 LoRA, dropout 0.05, learning rate 1e-4, and short training schedules chosen via a small grid search. Third, activation steering: they evaluate ITI, CAA, and Linear AcT. ITI learns layerwise biases from classifier tangents using last-token-pooled activations and intervenes on attention outputs for the last tokens. CAA averages source-target activation differences into a steering vector applied to future generated tokens. Linear AcT learns a linear transport map between source and target activation distributions using an optimal-transport objective, and applies interventions at LayerNorm layers.

The dataset design is stronger than in many steering papers. For concept injection, they use 60 concepts organized into 10 semantic categories, with 400 synthetic descriptive sentences per concept generated by Mistral-7B-Instruct. This broadens the study beyond a few hand-picked attributes. For steering, target examples are concept sentences and source examples are sampled from other concepts; for SFT, the model is directly trained on the 400 target sentences. For concept removal, they use toxicity mitigation on Real Toxicity Prompts. Importantly, they distinguish the supervision needs of steering and SFT: steering only needs toxic versus non-toxic examples to estimate a direction, whereas SFT requires prompt-continuation pairs where the prompt is toxic but the continuation is non-toxic, otherwise it does not learn to resist toxic prompts at generation time. That distinction is technically important and often glossed over.

Evaluation is the other major contribution. The paper uses an LLM-as-judge pipeline to score conditioning success, fluency, and instruction following, and complements it with cheaper textual and embedding-based metrics. A central analysis is why perplexity fails: interventions can induce repetition, templated outputs, or abnormal length distributions, all of which can leave PPL deceptively acceptable while the text is clearly degraded. Simpler measures such as type-token ratio correlate better with judged fluency.b:T849,The main empirical findings are fourfold.

First, there is a real effectiveness-fluency trade-off. Activation steering often improves concept control, but frequently at a substantial cost in fluency, grammatical naturalness, and instruction following. Methods that intervene broadly across generated tokens are especially prone to repetition and rigid phrasing. This means that evaluating only concept hit rate materially overstates the usefulness of steering.

Second, instruction tuning sharply reduces the effectiveness of activation steering. Applying the same steering methods to base and instruction-tuned variants reveals a clear drop on instruct models, while prompting and LoRA-SFT remain more robust there. This is the most informative result in the paper. It suggests that instruction tuning changes the mapping from hidden-state geometry to output behavior enough that simple linear interventions no longer transfer cleanly.

Third, the best method depends on the task type. For concept injection, direct prompting and SFT generally outperform activation steering. For concept removal, especially toxicity mitigation, steering is more competitive because it can directly push representations away from undesirable regions without learning a full conditional generation policy. Injection and removal are therefore not symmetric control problems.

Fourth, perplexity is not a stable fluency metric in this setting. The paper shows that PPL can be distorted by repetition and length effects, whereas type-token ratio aligns better with LLM-judge fluency scores. Some expensive judge-based conclusions can also be approximated by cheaper BERT-based or corpus-based metrics, which is useful for large-scale screening.

The concrete scales reported in the paper include 60 concepts with 400 sentences each for concept injection, 356 toxic and 379 non-toxic examples for toxicity steering, and 455 toxic-prompt/non-toxic-continuation pairs for toxicity SFT.
