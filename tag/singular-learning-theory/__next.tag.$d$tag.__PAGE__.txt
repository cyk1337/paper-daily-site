9:T463,The analysis uses singular learning theory. The central quantity is the real log canonical threshold, or RLCT, which replaces half the parameter dimension in the leading asymptotics of Bayesian generalization and marginal likelihood for singular models. Under a local linearization of the pretrained network, LoRA becomes a low-rank trace-regression problem. Its KL geometry is governed by a forward-backward covariance built from input activations and backpropagated sensitivities at the adapted layer. If this covariance is nondegenerate on the relevant low-rank directions, the classical reduced-rank regression RLCT is recovered. If its kernel intersects those directions, additional statistically indistinguishable parameters appear, changing the singular geometry. The paper characterizes this locally through observable tangent directions plus a residual singularity, and globally through affine linear sections of determinantal varieties.
