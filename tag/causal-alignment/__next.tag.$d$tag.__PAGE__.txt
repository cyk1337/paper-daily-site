During training, it builds counterfactual-CoT, biased-instruction, and empty-instruction data, then applies selective-loss fine-tuning mainly on answer generation. Counterfactual CoTs encourage the answer to change when the reasoning changes. Biased instructions reduce reliance on misleading instruction-level shortcuts.
