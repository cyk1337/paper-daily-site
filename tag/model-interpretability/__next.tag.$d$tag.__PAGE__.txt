5:T4d5,Define two token-level quantities.
- Contextual curvature: treat token hidden states at a given layer as a trajectory in representation space. Compute first-order differences between adjacent token states; define curvature at position k as the angle between adjacent difference vectors; then average over a backward-looking window (size 3) to get a localized contextual curvature.
- Next-token entropy: entropy of the model’s output distribution at that position.

The paper builds three evidence pillars:
1) Correlation + emergence: curvature predicts entropy in GPT-2 XL and Pythia-2.8B, and the relationship strengthens over training using Pythia checkpoints.
2) Targeted perturbations: perturbations aligned with the trajectory structure modulate entropy more effectively than nonspecific perturbations, supporting functional relevance rather than a mere correlate.
3) Auxiliary curvature objectives: adding curvature-targeting losses can alter entropy while preserving task performance, implying curvature is a controllable representational feature tied to uncertainty.

The approach is intentionally lightweight: geometry-based metrics instead of heavy probing models, plus checkpoint-based tracking and intervention
