18:T982,技术上，CaRE 的重点不是复杂算法，而是把评测协议做成一个可执行、可扩展、可审计的框架。核心实现是统一的 RemaskingSampler，围绕四个设计原则展开。其一，自动记录每次生成的 actual NFE，而不是接受论文里常见的 nominal step 作为 compute proxy。这个点很关键，因为不同 remasking 策略会重复 mask 已决定 token，导致同样 256 nominal steps 下，真实 NFE 可以从 128 到 513，相差约 4 倍。其二，默认同时报告四个指标：用 Llama-3-8B 计算的 PPL、MAUVE、Self-BLEU、Distinct-3。作者明确把“单指标最优”视为不充分证据，因为不同指标衡量的是流畅性、分布匹配和表面多样性的不同侧面。其三，把 unmask_temp 设为必填超参，并要求 sweep，而不是像以往工作那样固定一个温度后直接比较策略。其四，加入字符级英文过滤器，避免生成中混入非目标语言文本后污染 PPL/MAUVE 等统计。

协议层面，作者提出七点规范，核心精神是三件事：compute 要按 actual NFE 对齐，stochasticity 要显式 sweep，指标要成组报告而不是挑一个最有利的。实验覆盖 12 个 open-weight MDLM，参数规模从 150M 到 8B，包含 dense、MoE 和 soft-masked 变体；任务包括 OpenWebText/LM1B 上的 unconditional generation、HumanEval 代码生成，以及 GSM8K、HellaSwag、BBH 的 log-likelihood 评测。后面三类任务被作者当作 sampling-invariant sanity check，用来确认某些结论不是纯采样噪声。

论文最有信息量的技术发现来自交互分析。作者不是只做 pairwise 对比，而是用三因素 ANOVA 分析 strategy、temperature、compute 的联合作用。结果显示 temperature 是主导因素，效应量 η²=0.91；strategy × temperature 交互也显著，η²=0.47。这说明“某策略优于另一策略”如果不附带温度和 compute 条件，结论几乎没有可移植性。进一步地，作者区分了 stochastic unmasking 带来的表面多样性提升，与 high-entropy remasking 在高预算下造成的 persistent token churn。后者会让 token 持续被重新 mask / 重新决定，表面上增加变化，实际上缩窄了分布覆盖，因此 MAUVE 反而下降。这个机制解释了为什么一些论文在高温或高步数下报告 high-entropy 类策略更好，而另一些论文得到相反结论。19:T489,Its value to pretraining research is mostly infrastructural but still substantial. First, MDLMs are now close enough to autoregressive LMs that evaluation noise can distort conclusions about the paradigm itself. If remasking papers are not compute-matched and temperature-controlled, later claims about inference efficiency, training targets, or AR-vs-MDLM trade-offs rest on unstable ground. Second, the paper reframes inference improvements under real compute accounting. That matters beyond MDLMs: any non-AR generation method can look better if nominal steps are used as a loose proxy for cost. Third, the work surfaces a concrete interaction that had not been isolated before: stochastic unmasking is the main driver of diversity gains, while informed or high-entropy remasking can erase those gains at higher compute budgets through persistent token churn. That finding is immediately relevant for MDLM decoding design and may also inform training-time choices about denoising trajectories. The contribution is best understood as a standardization effort in the spirit of Clean-FID or SacreBLEU: not a new model, but a correction of the measurement layer.1a:T8d4,Technically, CaRE is a protocol and software framework centered on a unified RemaskingSampler. The design is intentionally simple, but the choices are sharp. First, it automatically tracks actual NFE per generation. This is the key correction: strategies that remask already decided tokens incur extra forward passes, so the same nominal step budget can hide a large compute gap. Second, the framework reports four default metrics together: PPL scored by Llama-3-8B, MAUVE, Self-BLEU, and Distinct-3. The paper treats single-metric wins as insufficient because these metrics capture different aspects of generation quality and diversity. Third, unmask_temp is made a required hyperparameter with sweep support, rather than an incidental setting buried in an appendix. Fourth, a character-level English filter is added to reduce contamination from off-target language outputs when computing distributional metrics.

The protocol itself is built around three principles: compare strategies at matched actual NFE, sweep stochasticity explicitly, and report a metric bundle rather than cherry-picking one score. The empirical scope is broad enough to make the standard credible: 12 open-weight MDLMs from 150M to 8B parameters, including dense, MoE, and soft-masked variants; unconditional generation on OpenWebText and LM1B; code generation on HumanEval; and log-likelihood evaluations on GSM8K, HellaSwag, and BBH as sampling-invariant sanity checks.

The most informative methodological contribution is the interaction analysis. Instead of pairwise comparisons only, the authors run a three-way ANOVA over strategy, temperature, and compute. Temperature dominates with effect size η²=0.91, and the strategy × temperature interaction is also large and significant at η²=0.47. This means a statement like “strategy A is better than strategy B” is not portable unless compute and temperature are specified. The paper then interprets the failure mode of high-entropy remasking through persistent token churn: repeated remasking keeps tokens unstable, which can preserve superficial variation while narrowing true distributional coverage. That mechanism explains why prior papers could report contradictory rankings under different temperatures and budgets.1b:T742,Several quantitative results carry most of the paper’s weight. First, the compute mismatch is large. At nominal 256 steps, actual NFE ranges from 128 for the no-remasking baseline to 513 for running_conf., roughly a 4x spread. This alone shows that many prior comparisons were not compute-fair. Second, once actual NFE is fixed at {128, 256, 512}, the none strategy dominates MAUVE at every budget, and the gap grows with more compute. So some remasking methods that looked stronger under nominal-step comparisons lose their advantage when real cost is matched. Third, temperature has an outsized effect: a single temperature change can raise MAUVE from 0.66 to 0.98, a gain of 0.32, larger than the maximum gap between any two strategies at the same temperature. That scale makes unswept temperature settings a major source of false conclusions. Fourth, at higher compute budgets, high-entropy remasking removes 0.296 MAUVE points of the diversity benefit from stochastic unmasking, with p=0.020. The paper attributes this to token churn rather than a generic randomness issue.

The ranking reversals are not limited to open-ended generation. The paper reports that strategy rankings also flip on HumanEval when actual NFE is equalized, which matters because it shows the issue extends to code generation. For GSM8K, HellaSwag, and BBH, the authors use log-likelihood scoring mainly as a sanity check to separate sampling effects from changes in the underlying conditional model. One concrete number mentioned in the paper is that under 128 deterministic steps, high_entropy reaches MAUVE 0.612 and is best in that local setting, but that advantage is not stable once compute, temperature, or metric changes. The broader conclusion is not that one strategy wins universally, but that rankings are fragile unless all three confounds are jointly controlled.1c:T5c9,Start with the introduction and the motivating figure that shows contradictory rankings across recent remasking papers. Then move to the protocol section and focus on three implementation choices: how actual NFE is defined and tracked, why unmask_temp is mandatory and swept, and why the metric bundle includes both distributional and diversity measures.
