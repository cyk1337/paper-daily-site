9:T45c,The paper presents watermarking in a common formalism. During autoregressive generation, the LLM provides a next-token distribution p_t. A watermark uses pseudorandom vectors U derived from a context hash and a private key, and applies a logits transformation f(U, p_t) to produce a watermarked distribution. Detection recovers token-level scores U_{a_t, token_t} at retained positions, deduplicates repeated seed-token pairs, and relies on those scores being i.i.d. under the null hypothesis. The key addition is a reliability definition with two criteria: soundness in expectation over the private key, and a small probability, for a fixed text distribution, of sampling a key that causes false flags. The autoresearch harness isolates the agent, lets it propose and implement schemes, and submits them to a private verification pipeline. The pipeline first rejects schemes that fail the reliability tests, then evaluates accepted schemes on detectability, robustness, and quality.
