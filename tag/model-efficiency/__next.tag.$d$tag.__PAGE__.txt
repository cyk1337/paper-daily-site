9:T52e,The proposed method, Learnable Subspace Projections, assigns an orthogonal projector to each target linear layer, or to a tied group of layers that read the same activation. For a linear layer with weight W, the method learns an orthonormal matrix U and applies P=I-UU^T in the input space, thereby removing k input directions. Layers such as Q/K/V projections, or gate/up projections in the MLP, can share one projector because they consume the same activation space. During compression, pretrained weights are frozen and only the projectors are optimized. The default objective is KL divergence between the compressed model’s output distribution and the dense model’s output distribution; an alternative variant optimizes the original training objective. The method uses an activation-space parameterization and fused QR orthonormalization to keep the constrained optimization tractable at billion-parameter scale. Initialization comes from a whitened truncation rather than plain SVD. Rank allocation is also loss-aware: each candidate projector is applied in isolation, the induced output KL is measured, and the compression budget is allocated according to KL cost per saved parameter.
