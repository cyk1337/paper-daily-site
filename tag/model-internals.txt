18:Ta28,文章的技术路线是把每一种失败模式都拆成四步：先展示最初看起来像发现的现象，再指出暴露问题的 tell，再给出修复协议，最后用独立控制验证修复是否有效。贯穿全文的主线是四条纪律：第一，用 positive-control ladder 校准仪器，确认它在目标位置真的有区分能力；第二，用 orthogonal cell 做认证，避免同一类测量自证；第三，在花算力做 patch 或大规模干预前先算 power；第四，所有“reads X”结论都必须相对模型 commit 深度来陈述。

文中一个关键背景是 decision site 的低维瓶颈。作者用 participation ratio 估计残差流协方差的有效维度，发现四个模型在控制 token 处只有约 8 到 15 维，而同层内容 token 位置仍是 33 到 40+ 的健康高维。这个事实解释了为什么 projection-fraction instrument 会失灵：在一个只有十来个有效自由度的通道里，正控方向投到自身 span 的比例可能并不比随机方向高，导致“低投影”不能说明缺失，只能说明仪器没分辨率。

六个 failure mode 里，较有方法学含金量的几个是：
1. 对 projection fraction，作者要求先做 band/below-null 检查和正控梯度，只有当正控在该位置能明显高于 null，投影读数才可解释。
2. 对 covariance-matched null，作者指出在 massive-activation family 中，匹配协方差会让几乎所有方向都显得“典型”，null 失去对比度，因此需要额外的 family-aware 控制，而不是只看协方差匹配。
3. 对 per-head OV attribution，作者发现 reordered-normalization 架构里按 head 估计的写入量会比真实 residual write 高约三倍，因此不能把 OV 路径贡献直接当作残差写入，需要用真实残差差分或更接近执行图的估计来校正。
4. 对 interchange patch，作者强调如果 outcome 已经贴近 ceiling，engage/disengage 两个方向会因为 operating point 不对称而出现 sign-chaotic；修复方式是把结论写成 depth- and operating-point-referenced，并在非饱和区间做验证。
5. 对 read-from verdict，作者要求把测量层和 commit 层分开：如果模型在更早层已经决定，晚层读到的相关性可能只是决策后的回声，而不是因果读取。

整体上，这不是提出单一新算法，而是把 interpretability instrument 当成实验仪器来做校准学。它和很多 mechanistic interpretability 文章最大的区别，是作者不急着给机制故事，而是先给失效边界和可复现 protocol。19:Tb2f,The paper is organized instrument by instrument. For each failure mode, it follows the same pattern: show the initial apparent finding, identify the tell that exposed the failure, propose a repair protocol, and certify the repair with an orthogonal control. Four procedural rules run through the whole note: calibrate against a positive-control ladder, certify with an orthogonal cell, compute statistical power before spending compute on interventions, and state every read-from claim relative to the model’s commitment depth.

A key empirical backdrop is the low-dimensional decision-site bottleneck. The authors estimate effective dimensionality with the participation ratio of the residual-stream covariance and find that the control token sits in an 8-to-15 dimensional channel across four models, while content positions at comparable layers remain much higher-rank. This observation explains one of the main instrument failures: in such a narrow channel, a projection-fraction metric can lose discriminative power exactly where the interesting directions live, so a low projection score may reflect instrument weakness rather than representational absence.

Among the six failure modes, several stand out as broadly reusable methodology:
1. For projection-based instruments, the protocol requires checking whether the positive control is actually above the null band at the target position. If the instrument cannot separate its own positive control from null, the projection number is not interpretable.
2. For covariance-matched nulls, the paper shows that in massive-activation families the null can saturate until nearly every direction looks typical. Matching covariance alone is therefore insufficient; one needs family-aware controls or additional diagnostics.
3. For per-head OV attribution, the authors show that reordered-normalization architectures can induce about a 3x overestimate relative to the true residual write. The fix is to ground attribution in actual residual differences or a closer execution-level estimator, rather than treating OV-path attribution as the write itself.
4. For interchange patching, they show that operating-point asymmetry matters. When one arm is near ceiling, engage/disengage interventions can become sign-chaotic. The remedy is to report effects relative to operating point and verify them away from saturation.
5. For read-from claims, the paper separates the read layer from the commitment layer. If the model has already committed earlier, a later-layer correlation may be a post-decision echo rather than a causal read.

The paper’s distinctive move is to treat interpretability tools as laboratory instruments that require calibration, power analysis, and certification. It is much less interested in a mechanism narrative than in the validity conditions under which such a narrative can be trusted.1a:T5bd,The most important quantitative result is the decision-site bottleneck itself. The participation ratio at the control token is reported as 14.7 for OLMo-3-7B-Instruct, 8.6 for Qwen2.5-7B, 10.2 for Llama-3.1-8B, and roughly 9.4 raw / 12.8 standardized for GPT-OSS-20B at its relevant decision token. These values are only 4% to 8% of their column-shuffle references, indicating a strong compression rather than a mild reduction in rank. By contrast, content positions at similar layers remain in the 33+ to 40+ range, supporting the claim that the bottleneck is localized to the decision site.

The paper is not benchmark-driven. Its empirical contribution is a set of quantified diagnostic failures. The headline numbers matter because they are large enough to reverse conclusions: per-head OV attribution can overshoot true residual writes by about threefold on reordered-norm models; covariance-matched nulls can saturate so badly that every direction appears typical; read-from verdicts can become artifacts when measured after commitment.
