a:T476,The OPSA (On-Policy Self-Distillation for Safety Alignment) framework is proposed, with the core of applying token-level KL supervision to the model's own on-policy sampled trajectories. First, the base model to be aligned is frozen as the teacher, and privileged contexts are used to activate the teacher's safety behavior: privileged contexts for harmful queries steer the teacher toward refusal behavior, while privileged contexts for benign queries preserve the teacher's normal response behavior. To select effective privileged contexts, the training-free Teacher Flip Rate (TFR) metric is introduced, measuring the proportion of unsafe responses converted to safe ones by a specific context. Contexts with high TFR are selected as conditional inputs for the teacher. During training, the student model (active parameter copy of the model being aligned) samples its own response trajectories, and the KL divergence between each token output and the conditional teacher output is used as loss.
