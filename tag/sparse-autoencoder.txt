第二步是几何与层深分析。作者从 Neuronpedia 上取 3.9M 个 SAE feature，覆盖 6 个基座模型和 3 个 SAE family：GPT2-Small 上的 res-jb，Gemma 系列上的 GemmaScope/JumpReLU，Llama-3.1-8B 与 DeepSeek-R1-8B 上的 LlamaScope/TopK，并额外在 Gemma-2-2B、Gemma-3-1B 上加入 community BatchTopK 以便同底模跨 family 比较。核心观察是 single-token feature 在 decoder space 中更紧密，聚类紧度约高 4.7 倍；它们高度集中在早层，GPT2-Small 中 91% 在 Layer 0，Gemma 中主要在 L0-L4；并且 L0 到 L1 有明显结构跃迁，Grassmannian alignment 只有 0.26，而后续层间通常大于 0.9。这说明 token identity 相关方向在最早层有特殊地位，之后表征更平滑地传播。

第三步是因果检验。作者做的是 zero-ablation at full layer depth：在某层把目标 SAE feature 置零，然后观察目标 token logit 的变化，并沿完整剩余网络传播，而不是只看局部重构误差。对于跨 family 检测，作者指出 activation-based detection 在 LlamaScope 上会失效，因为 TopK 到 JumpReLU 的转换改变了激活分布，于是改用 decoder-alignment detection：直接计算 feature decoder 向量与词嵌入矩阵的余弦相似度，再施加同样的 gap/purity 条件。这样可以在没有激活数据时做跨 family 对齐。关键结论是：GemmaScope 和 BatchTopK 上匹配到的 single-token feature 更像“因果锚点”，消融后目标 token 排名常显著下降；而 LlamaScope 上很多匹配 feature 呈现局部冗余，目标 token 往往能很快恢复原排名，恢复率达到 96%–98%。作者还专门检查了激活函数差异，认为不能简单把这种差异归因于 TopK vs JumpReLU，本质上是 SAE family 学到的表示分解方式不同。18:T7b4,实验规模是这篇文章比较扎实的部分。数据来自 6 个模型、3 个 SAE family，总计 3.9M feature，模型从 124M 的 GPT2-Small 到 9B 级 Gemma-2-9B，跨近两个数量级。主要结果有几组。

First, the paper defines single-token features using top-k activation statistics rather than relying on auto-generated explanations alone. For each feature, the authors compute Gap Ratio, Lexical Purity, and a Complete Word constraint over the top-20 activating tokens. They use a conservative operating point: gap >= 0.3, purity >= 0.6, and a full-word requirement. This captures only a small fraction of all features, but the choice is validated by explanation match, geometric clustering in decoder space, and downstream causal effects.

Second, the paper studies geometry and layer depth. It analyzes 3.9M SAE features from Neuronpedia across six base models and three SAE families: res-jb/GemmaScope-style JumpReLU SAEs, LlamaScope TopK SAEs, and community BatchTopK SAEs for matched-family comparisons on the same Gemma backbones. Single-token features are much tighter in decoder space, about 4.7x tighter than polysemantic features, and are heavily concentrated in early layers. In GPT2-Small, 91% of them are in layer 0; in Gemma they mostly appear in layers 0-4. The paper also reports a sharp structural transition from layer 0 to layer 1, with Grassmannian alignment around 0.26 there versus >0.9 for later adjacent layers. That suggests token-identity directions occupy a special regime near the input side of the network.

Third, the paper runs causal tests with zero-ablation at full layer depth. A selected SAE feature is zeroed at a given layer, and the effect on the target token logit is measured after propagation through the rest of the model. For cross-family detection, the authors note that activation-based detection breaks on LlamaScope because TopK-to-JumpReLU conversion changes activation statistics. They therefore introduce decoder-alignment detection: match decoder vectors directly against the token embedding matrix using cosine similarity, then apply the same token-dominance criteria. This allows cross-family comparisons without activation traces. The central result is that GemmaScope and BatchTopK token features behave like causal anchors, while many LlamaScope-matched features are locally redundant and recover their pre-ablation token rank 96-98% of the time. 2. Causal necessity: across seven model-by-SAE configurations and 208 full-layer conditions, 178 conditions show Benjamini-Hochberg-significant reductions in the target token logit after ablation. So the paper is not claiming that token features are merely geometric artifacts; many are causally necessary at the measured readout. Layer depth matters, though: early-layer ablations tend to create downstream cascades, while later-layer ablations more directly affect the output distribution.

3. Family effects exceed scale effects: at comparable 8B-9B scale, LlamaScope has 46x lower single-token feature prevalence than GemmaScope. If you want to dig deeper, two questions are worth carrying forward: whether LlamaScope redundancy comes from the training objective or from other SAE design choices, and whether the early-layer concentration of token features is tied to tokenizer statistics, embedding tying, or frequency structure in pretraining data.1c:Tab4,技术路线分成四层，而且每一层都围绕“把几何恢复和因果有效性拆开”展开。

第一层是构造完全可控的 ground-truth superposition toy model。作者从头训练一个很小的合成模型，不用预训练权重，也不用外部数据，使得真实 feature 方向、隐藏维度、稀疏度和 superposition 几何都已知。这样做的目的不是追求 realism，而是让“恢复是否正确”以及“干预是否沿真实路径生效”都能被严格定义。

第二层是复现并修正已有 SAE 几何结论。论文多 seed 复现了 Elhage 等人的 superposition phase diagram，并指出一个重要 artifact：在高 sparsity 区域，若训练步数不够，会把 under-training 误判成 superposition 结构。作者用 sparsity-aware step budgets 解决这个问题。除此之外，论文还描述了一个此前没有被充分刻画的 diffuse sharing regime：在极端 overcompleteness 下，每个 feature 都被“相当程度地表示”，但 feature dimensionality 很低，说明表示不是少数原子清晰分工，而是更弥散地共享字典容量。

第三层是复现 TopK SAE 与 L1 SAE 的比较，并把“L1 shrinkage”从口头说法变成直接测量。作者从头复现 TopK-vs-L1 Pareto，对 activation refinement 做了直接测试，量化 L1 正则导致的 shrinkage，而不是只凭经验判断。这里的关键点是：decoder recovery 看起来相近时，encoder 激活行为可能已经明显不同，这正是后面因果分歧的来源之一。

第四层是提出一套 causal validation battery，并把 inertness 细分成可操作的类型。作者对每个 correlationally recovered feature 做两类干预：ablation 和 sign-correct steering，并把影响一路传播到模型真实输出层，而不是停留在 SAE reconstruction 空间。基于这些干预，论文区分了两条轴。按成因分，有 structural inertness 和 competitive inertness。前者来自 antipodal-pair superposition geometry 与 positive-pass encoder 的结构性错配，即使 SAE 训练更好也未必消失；后者来自 TopK 竞争机制下退化字典的竞争病理，并且和数值边界不稳定性耦合。按方向分，有 read-inertness 和 write-inertness：前者表示 ablation 监测不到，后者表示 steering 无法写入有效影响。论文指出 antipodal pairs 可以把这两者完全解耦。

First, it characterizes the native discrete gauge of transformer residual streams under coordinate-preserving parameterizations. For LayerNorm, the residual chart has permutation symmetry up to a global sign. For RMSNorm with generic per-channel gain, the correct group is the hyperoctahedral group B_d = S_d ⋉ {±1}^d, meaning arbitrary coordinate permutations plus independent sign flips. The paper also clarifies an important distinction: broader orthogonal freedom can be recovered only after absorbing the RMSNorm gain into adjacent weights, but that changes the coordinate system and no longer preserves sparse coordinate identity. Second, it proposes sign-marginalized activation matching. Instead of matching coordinates by signed correlation, it matches by absolute correlation |<h_i, h'_j>| and recovers the sign afterward from the matched pair. This is the right objective because the true counterpart of a coordinate may be maximally anti-correlated under a negative gauge sign. The authors prove a sharp population-level failure mode for signed-correlation matching in a decorrelated setting: if half of the true signs are negative, the correct entries become the most negative ones in their rows, so maximizing signed correlation systematically avoids them even with unlimited probes.
