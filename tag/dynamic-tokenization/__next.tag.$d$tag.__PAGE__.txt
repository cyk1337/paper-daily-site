The image is split into horizontal strips; within each active strip, the highest-entropy location is selected as an anchor, followed by radius-based deduplication to avoid collapsing all anchors onto one hotspot. Around each retained anchor, the method extracts multi-scale spiral rings: a central foveal patch plus three outer rings with increasing radius and patch size. Out-of-bounds patches are filtered, leading to at most about 78 retained patches for most samples.
