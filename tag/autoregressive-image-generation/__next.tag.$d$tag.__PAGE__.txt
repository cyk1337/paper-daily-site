9:T49b,The method replaces patch-wise quantization with Channel-wise Vector Quantization (CVQ). Given a latent feature map Z∈R^{h×w×c}, standard VQ assigns a code to each spatial vector z(i,j,:)∈R^c. CVQ instead treats each channel as the quantization unit (conceptually a 2D map of size h×w) and maps each channel to a discrete code, producing a length-c 1D token sequence. The paper’s mechanism argument is optimization-dynamics driven: patch embeddings are highly repetitive across images and within a batch, so early training concentrates updates on a small subset of codes, leading to dead codes and collapse; channel partitioning yields more separable embeddings across images and broader code coverage.

On top of CVQ, it proposes Channel-wise Auto-Regressive (CAR) generation via “next-channel prediction”: generate tokens sequentially over channels, yielding a coarse-to-fine progression where early channels capture global structure and later channels refine details. Nested dropout is used to encourage an AR-friendly channel ordering (prefix channels remain informative, later channels add refinements), avoiding the need for hand-designed spatial token orders.a:T404,The main study trains class-conditional AR generators on ImageNet 256×256 while sweeping the backbone-to-total parameter ratio ρ from 0.17 to 0.90. Architecturally, it follows the TiTok-style 1D ViT autoencoder. Image patches are flattened and concatenated with L learnable query tokens, then passed through a causal ViT encoder. The encoder produces hidden patch embeddings and a 1D latent sequence z; only the query-token latents are kept as the image representation. Quantization uses an IBQ codebook with a straight-through estimator, and the decoder reconstructs the image from the quantized 1D tokens zq together with patch mask tokens. In addition to standard tokenizer losses such as L1/L2 reconstruction, perceptual loss, GAN loss, and quantizer regularization, EOSTok jointly trains the tokenizer with an AR generator. The paper further adds Autoregressive Prediction Reconstruction loss. APR decodes the teacher-forced predictions of the AR model back into pixel space and applies a reconstruction loss there, so errors made by the AR model are fed back to the tokenizer through the final image domain. For semantic supervision, the paper avoids directly aligning the 1D latent sequence to 2D vision-foundation-model features, because that would push the sequence back toward raster-ordered patch alignment.
