a:T404,VoiceTTA uses a pretrained zero-shot TTS model and optimizes learnable prefixes during inference. The discussed backbone is a flow-matching TTS system: a Gaussian latent is transported toward a mel-spectrogram distribution under text and speech-prompt conditioning, then converted to waveform by a vocoder. Candidate utterances are sampled with varying temperatures to create enough diversity for preference optimization.

The reward combines style and intelligibility signals. F0 coefficient-of-variation difference measures whether pitch dynamics match the reference. Energy coefficient-of-variation difference measures whether loudness dynamics and stress patterns are similar. Speaker similarity keeps the generated voice close to the prompt speaker. A Whisper-based WER reward discourages outputs that imitate style at the cost of intelligibility. GRPO is then used over a group of sampled candidates to update only the prefix parameters.
