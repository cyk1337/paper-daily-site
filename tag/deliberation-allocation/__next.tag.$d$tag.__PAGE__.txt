5:T406,The analysis decomposes deliberation into difficulty registration and deliberation allocation. Difficulty registration is measured across items: harder items should elicit longer traces or reaction times. Deliberation allocation is measured within item identity: after absorbing item difficulty, does an agent spend more time or tokens on trials it gets right or wrong? The authors use a public matched human–LRM corpus, normalize each agent’s deliberation measure on its own scale, and fit item-controlled outcome-conditioned models. Human seconds and model tokens are never compared directly. H-ARC is the primary setting, INTUIT is used as a structurally different replication, and Cortes serves as a boundary case. The model set consists of open-weight thinking LRMs with DeepSeek-V3 as a non-thinking baseline.
