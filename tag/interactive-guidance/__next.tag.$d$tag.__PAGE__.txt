a:T4d7,SocraticPO turns a single-turn RL rollout into an interactive multi-round trajectory. The student answers first; a verifier checks correctness; if wrong, a teacher reads the student attempt (and optionally side information such as reference solutions) and produces concise Socratic-style guidance (diagnosis + hint, not a full solution). The guidance is appended to the context and the student continues; this can repeat for multiple rounds. Training remains standard expected-reward policy gradient (compatible with Reinforce++/GRPO-style implementations); the teacher is not distilled and no teacher logits/distribution matching are required, enabling black-box teachers.
The key anti-exploitation mechanism is reward decay: if the student only becomes correct after r rounds of teacher intervention, the reward is reduced as a monotone, saturating function of r. This makes “solve correctly with less help, earlier” the optimized behavior and discourages policies that intentionally wait for hints.
