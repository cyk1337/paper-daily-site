5:T44c,Uni-LaViRA extends the LaViRA formulation by turning navigation into a structured translation problem with two complementary action channels:
1) Language action: emits semantic directional commands in natural language-like form.
2) Vision action: emits pixel-level targets (point/region-like grounding outputs).
The claim is that both lie on the “natural output manifold” of pretrained MLLMs, enabling reasoning-heavy control.
Two mechanisms address common online failures:
- TODO List Memory (TDM): replaces ever-growing dialogue history with an explicit, continuously rewritten checklist of subgoals (done / pending / in-progress). This enforces plan-then-act and keeps remaining goals in the most recent attention window.
- Second Chance Backtrack (SCB): when a recent decision is deemed off-instruction, the agent rolls back to the pre-error state but conditions replanning on the failed sub-trajectory, treating mistakes as informative context rather than something to discard.
Overall, it is closer to an agentic, structured-interface design around an MLLM than a learned navigation
