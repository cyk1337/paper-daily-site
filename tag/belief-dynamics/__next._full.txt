It introduces Prompted Predictive Resampling (PPR): the model generates a sequence of answers to the same question within a single prompted interaction; at each step, the predictive distribution over A is recomputed, yielding a belief trajectory. Martingale/c.i.d. coherence is then tested on this trajectory.
Empirical pattern: early steps show belief drift (martingale violations), but after enough resampling steps the belief process self-stabilizes and converges to a coherent regime.
Two practical add-ons are proposed: (i) a prompting trick to accelerate stabilization; (ii) an amortized finetuning strategy with a loss derived from enforcing c.i.d., aiming to get the stabilized distribution in fewer (ideally one) steps, improving calibration without hurting accuracy.
