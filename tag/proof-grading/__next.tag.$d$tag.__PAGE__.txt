Each instance provides a problem, a reference solution, a candidate solution, and an expert human score. The authors draw two disjoint 200-example samples: an exploratory sample with seed 42 and a held-out validation sample with seed 7; they also report results on the full 1,000-example benchmark. All judges receive the same prompt and are asked to emit one of four coarse scores, {0, 1, 6, 7}, while the parser accepts any integer from 0 to 7. The primary metric is pass-agreement at the ≥6 threshold, with precision, recall, F1, and Spearman correlation as secondary measures. The cheap judges are GPT-OSS-120B, DeepSeek-V4-Flash, and Gemma-4-31B; the frontier baselines are Claude Opus 4.7 and Gemini 3.1 Pro. The pre-specified ensemble is majority vote over the three cheap models. The models were selected not only for cost and accuracy but also for offsetting calibration biases, with Gemma tending to over-credit and DeepSeek-V4-Flash tending to under-credit.
