9:T7d7,KVAE is a family of encoder-decoder tokenizers for multimodal generative modeling. The visual side includes two causal video tokenizers: KVAE-3D with 4x8x8 compression and 16 latent channels, and KVAE-3D with 4x16x16 compression and 64 channels. The image tokenizer, KVAE-2D, uses 8x spatial compression with 32 channels. The audio tokenizer is continuous, full-band 48 kHz, with a 50 Hz latent stream and 64 channels.

The most interesting design choices appear in the video tokenizer. First, the backbone is attention-free and built around Conv3D blocks. The motivation is efficiency and length generalization: convolutional tokenizers are cheaper to train and can process arbitrarily long sequences at inference through caching, which is useful for long videos and causal generation settings. Second, the tokenizer is causal, implemented with asymmetric temporal padding, so it can support both images and videos without leaking future frames. Third, temporal and spatial downsampling/upsampling are applied sequentially rather than collapsed into a single operation. The paper contrasts this with approaches that patchify aggressively in early layers; KVAE-4x16x16 delays patching to the downsampling blocks instead. Fourth, the models use spatial RMSNorm, which allows segment-size adjustment at inference. Fifth, the encoder and decoder are intentionally asymmetric, especially in the higher-compression model where the decoder is made substantially wider. This reflects a clear hypothesis: under strong compression, it is better to keep the encoder stable and efficient while giving the decoder more capacity to reconstruct detail.

Another important point is that the paper treats diffusability as a primary tokenizer property. Reconstruction metrics alone are not enough. A tokenizer can reconstruct well yet still produce a latent space that is awkward for diffusion training. The later sections on diffusability evaluation and design ablations are therefore central to the paper’s contribution.a:T6c7,The reported evaluation spans reconstruction, downstream generation quality, diffusability analysis, and architecture ablations. KVAE-2D is evaluated similarly for images. On the audio side, the paper reports objective metrics such as PESQ and emphasizes the practical operating point of full-band 48 kHz audio with a 50 Hz latent stream and 64 channels.
