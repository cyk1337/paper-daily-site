9:T419,The study uses Tiny Recursive Models and runs the same learned transition function beyond the nominal inference budget. The models are normally evaluated at 16 recurrent steps, but the recurrence is extended unchanged to 512 steps. Instead of comparing all examples at the same absolute recurrent step, the analysis aligns each trajectory by its first exact-solve step. This separates examples into Early solvers, Late solvers, and Persistent puzzles. The paper then measures three quantities: completion time, latent-state motion between consecutive recurrent states, and local stability. Stability is evaluated through local Jacobians and controlled finite perturbations. A key distinction is made between perturbations along the model’s natural direction of motion and perturbations along the most expanding Jacobian direction. After completion, the attention model can absorb perturbations along the most expanding direction within 16 steps, while the MLP model tends to preserve them longer.
