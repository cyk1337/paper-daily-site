The central claim is that they can all be written in the same two-step pattern: first construct a generalized Bayes or Gibbs posterior from a prior/reference policy plus a utility signal; then fit a parametric model or policy to approximate that posterior or its posterior predictive. Second, SFT is a forward-KL projection onto a teacher conditional distribution, while KL-regularized RL and OPD approximate reward- or advantage-reweighted posteriors under a reference policy. If a Transformer is pretrained over the same task distribution, then in the infinite-data/infinite-capacity limit its optimal predictor is exactly that posterior predictive. The point is not merely to restate “ICL is Bayes,” but to place ICL and SFT inside the same language of target-distribution approximation.
