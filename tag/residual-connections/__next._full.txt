1b:T5af,WAV v1 is a lightweight extension of Block AttnRes. In Block AttnRes, each block stores only a summary C_b, the sum of all residual updates inside the block. WAV v1 keeps this summary and adds two zero-sum detail bases. The first is a phase basis, D_phase, defined by the signed difference between attention and MLP updates, capturing whether the block is more attention-dominant or MLP-dominant. The second is a split basis, D_split, defined by the signed difference between the first-half and second-half updates in the block, capturing coarse early-versus-late directional change. This gives each block not only a total displacement but also two cheap descriptors of its internal trajectory shape. The stabilization choices are conservative. Detail sources are initialized with a negative routing bias, beta_D = -2.0, so the model starts close to Block AttnRes and only uses details when useful. Detail tensors are RMS-matched to the corresponding block summary and clipped by a maximum scale factor rho, preventing them from dominating merely due to larger raw scale. The final prediction mixer does not read detail sources by default; they are used only in intermediate routing. The attention, MLP, embeddings, and output head remain unchanged. Computationally, the number of routing sources grows from about O(N) block summaries to about O(3N) block-basis sources, still block-level rather than layer-level, and the parameter overhead is negligible.1c:T51d,The authors use a deliberately minimal single-stream DiT to make the failure easier to attribute. VAE image tokens and text embedding tokens are concatenated into one sequence, and self-attention handles all multimodal interaction. The backbone is a Post-Norm residual chain, RMSNorm(X_l + f_l(X_l)), without AdaLN, cross-attention, or other depthwise control channels. Training uses a Rectified Flow objective between VAE latents and Gaussian noise. The analysis decomposes token states into a sequence-mean component and a centered component. Row-stochastic attention exactly preserves pure-mean states, while the centered component is propagated by a separate mixing operator that can become contractive at depth. On the backward pass, gradients to residual writers split exactly into mean-coherent and centered parts. As tokens align, the mean-coherent part can accumulate coherently with sequence length, entering an O(T) regime. Once values homogenize, attention-logit gradients are suppressed through the null space of the Softmax Jacobian, weakening Q/K learning and locking the network into collapse. MV-Split Residuals address this by applying a separate gain to the centered residual update and a leaky replacement on the trunk mean, rather than shrinking both components with the same scalar gate.1d:T61b,The method has three parts. First, the paper formalizes geometric continuity using the absolute cosine similarity between adjacent layers’ top right singular vectors v1 or top left singular vectors u1, and adds a sigma-squared-weighted all-spectrum variant to distinguish head-direction alignment from broader subspace alignment. Second, it runs controlled ablations on a 16-layer, width-256 residual MLP. The main finding is that residual connections make cumulative gradients across layers point in similar directions, so weight updates accumulate coherently across depth. But this alone is insufficient if the model retains rotational symmetry: layers can still drift independently within equivalent rotated coordinate systems. Activations and normalization matter because they break that symmetry. A particularly clean experiment introduces a nonlinear yet rotation-preserving activation and shows that it fails to produce continuity, isolating symmetry breaking rather than nonlinearity per se as the operative factor. Third, the paper maps the mechanism back to transformers. Q, K, Gate, and Up read from the residual stream and therefore show strong input-space continuity; O and Down write back to the residual stream and therefore show strong output-space continuity; V lacks a nearby symmetry-breaking interaction and exhibits much weaker continuity. The paper also distinguishes the roles of activation and LayerNorm: activation tends to concentrate continuity in the leading singular direction, while LayerNorm spreads it across multiple directions.1e:T52d,Experiments are split between toy MLPs and small transformers. The toy setup is a residual MLP with hidden size 256 and depth 16 trained on MNIST. The authors ablate residual connections, activations, normalization, and related factors, then track adjacent-layer singular-vector similarity and effective rank. The main empirical results are: residual connections are necessary because they substantially increase cross-layer similarity of cumulative gradients; standard activations and LayerNorm both break rotational symmetry but in different ways, with activations concentrating alignment in the top singular direction and LayerNorm distributing it more broadly; and a rotation-preserving nonlinear activation does not sustain continuity. The transformer experiments on WikiText-103 reproduce projection-specific continuity patterns, and the paper further reports the same structure in pretrained Llama-3.1-8B: Q/K/Gate/Up show high input-space continuity, O/Down show high output-space continuity, and V remains weak. The most informative direct ablation removes the MLP activation, dropping Gate v1 continuity from 0.66 to 0.37 with only about a 3% perplexity change. That result is important because it shows continuity is not just a side effect of overall model quality; it is tied to specific architectural mechanisms.1f:T7ee,技术上，这篇论文以定理化分析为主。第一部分给出 LayerNorm 的 rank-neutrality theorem：在非退化条件下，LN 精确保留 token 表示集合的 affine rank；在 zero-bias 情况下也保留矩阵 rank。也就是说，LN 既不制造 rank collapse，也不修复它。

第二部分分析 residual update。设 X^{(l+1)} = X^{(l)} + Δ^{(l)}。作者指出，在 generic 条件下，残差连接会阻止表示 rank 向低维塌缩，因为新层输出是在旧表示上做增量叠加，而不是完全替换。由此，Dong 结论适用于“纯 attention、无 residual、无 MLP”的特殊动力学，但不直接外推到实际 Transformer。

第三部分提出 head-channel non-identifiability theorem。多头注意力先得到各 head 输出 Y^{(h)}，再经输出投影并求和得到 MHA(X)=Σ_h Y^{(h)}W_O^{(h)}。一旦这些贡献被加总，单个 head 的贡献就无法从总和中规范地恢复出来；MLP 接收的是已经混合后的 residual stream，因此不能重新分离 head 级功能。作者还给出 recovery ambiguity dimension：每层存在 n(H-1)d_k 个自由度的恢复歧义。以 BERT Base 为例，序列级是 360,448，自 token 级是每 token 704。

第四部分讨论 rank contraction。作者认为 head specialization 会让 MHA(X) 在某些方向上收缩 rank，因此 MLP 需要扩展恰好被 heads 收缩掉的方向，这给 d_ff 的设定提供了一个比经验规则更原则化的视角。文中明确批评 d_ff=4d_model 只是工程启发式，真正相关的是任务所需的 non-linear complexity。

第五部分给出一个轻量设计 PG-OP，position-gated output projection，用来部分缓解 head-channel non-identifiability。它在 W_O 之外加入位置相关门控，参数量不到 W_O 的 1.6%，并提出可验证的经验预测。最后，作者尝试用 symmetry-breaking 框架统一解释多种 collapse 现象，把问题表述为 Transformer 不变性群下的轨道，以及哪些模块在打破哪些对称性。20:T838,The paper is primarily theorem-driven. The first result is a rank-neutrality theorem for LayerNorm: under non-degeneracy assumptions, LN preserves the affine rank of the token representation set exactly, and in the zero-bias case it also preserves matrix rank. So LN neither causes nor repairs rank collapse.

The second result studies residual updates of the form X^{(l+1)} = X^{(l)} + Δ^{(l)}. The claim is that residual connections generically obstruct collapse because the new representation is an additive perturbation of the old one rather than a full replacement.
