a:T44d,Autodata has an inner loop and an outer loop. The inner loop alternates between data creation and data analysis: an agent grounds on source material, generates examples, checks whether they are correct and challenging, measures whether they separate weak and strong models, and feeds the lessons back into the next generation pass. The outer loop meta-optimizes the data scientist agent itself, including its harness, prompts, tool use, and decision policy. The concrete implementation, Agentic Self-Instruct, uses a main orchestrator and four LLM-based subagents: a Challenger that creates examples, a Weak solver expected to fail on many generated cases, a Strong solver expected to solve them, and a Verifier/judge that evaluates both solutions and example quality. For verifiable tasks, the intended criterion is often that the strong solver majority is correct while the weak solver majority is wrong. For less verifiable tasks, the judge scores the gap using generated rubrics.
