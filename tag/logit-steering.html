OGLS-SD: On-Policy Self-Distillation with Outcome-Guided Logit Steering for LLM Reasoning

评自蒸馏的监督偏差是推理后训练的常见问题，只需看§3的logit steering校准逻辑即可
Yuxiao Yang,Xiaoyun Wang,Weitong Zhang
