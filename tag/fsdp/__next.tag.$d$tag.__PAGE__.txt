9:T484,The library is organized around explicit PyTorch model builders instead of a monolithic trainer abstraction. Transformer blocks receive pre-built submodules, while builders choose the concrete attention, feed-forward, normalization, embedding, and projection implementations. LoRA insertion, quantized projections, custom attention kernels, and architecture-specific changes are therefore localized to construction time. The same recipe can compare full fine-tuning, LoRA, QLoRA, QAT, or module replacements while holding the optimizer, data path, logging, and distributed policy fixed. On the systems side, torchtune builds on FSDP2 and DTensor, with tensor, sequence, expert, loss, and context parallelism.
