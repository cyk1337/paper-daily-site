9:T42f,Resonant Context Anchoring (RCA) explicitly decouples two roles of self-attention:
- Routing: which tokens are selected (governed by attention probabilities).
- Gain: how strongly selected information impacts the residual stream (governed by the magnitude of the Value vectors).

RCA uses raw pre-softmax attention scores (QK^T) as an instantaneous semantic-correlation (“resonance”) signal. When a query strongly correlates with certain context tokens, RCA amplifies the norms of the corresponding Value vectors via a gated nonlinearity. Crucially, it does not modify the attention probability distribution, aiming to preserve syntactic structure and general fluency while increasing the contextual subspace energy relative to parametric components injected by MLPs.

Compared to prior inference-time methods:
- Versus contrastive decoding: no multiple forward passes; negligible latency overhead.
- Versus activation/head suppression: it boosts contextual evidence instead of bluntly suppressing parametric memory, reducing the risk of harming
