9:Tb27,技术主线有三步。

第一步，把 masked diffusion 的 tau-leaping sampler 重写成 ordered random partition 的等价形式。这个重写很关键，因为原始 CTMC/Gillespie 视角更接近逐坐标事件过程，不容易直接分析“某一步并行揭示一批坐标”带来的误差；而 partition 视角把整个采样轨迹表示成坐标集合被分成若干按顺序揭示的块，factorization error 就能自然写成对这些块内条件依赖的累积。

第二步，引入 dependence density ρ(u)。按论文描述，除去一个 N-1 的比例因子，ρ(u) 可以理解为：当其余坐标以概率 u 独立地已被揭示时，任意两个未揭示坐标之间平均 conditional mutual information 的密度。这个定义很有力，因为它把目标分布 π 的依赖结构压缩成一个一维函数。随后作者给出 ε_fact 的精确积分表示，schedule 的作用只通过权重项进入，而数据分布的全部难点都被放进 ρ(u)。这一步把“采样 schedule 优化”从一个离散、分布相关、难以下手的问题，变成了一个可分析的泛函优化问题。

第三步，分析不同依赖结构下的最优 schedule 形态。
- 对局部依赖分布，例如遍历马尔可夫链或足够规则的平稳过程，ρ_N 收敛到非退化连续 profile g。在这个 regime 下，优化平滑 schedule 只能改善 ε_fact 的前导常数，不能改变 N/K 的主阶，因此线性 schedule 是 order-optimal。这个结论很重要，因为它说明对很多“局部相关”的序列数据，花很多力气调花哨 schedule，收益可能只是常数级。
- 对 exchangeable mixtures 这类由全局 latent variable 诱导依赖的分布，ρ_N 会集中在原点附近，极限 profile 退化。此时早期揭示少量坐标能快速降低后续不确定性，因此 schedule 形状会显著影响误差阶。论文给出一个很强的例子：当 K_N≈log N 时，uniform schedule 的误差是 Ω(N/log N)，而 geometric schedule 可以做到 O(log N)。这不是常数差，而是阶的变化。

First, the paper rewrites the masked-diffusion tau-leaping sampler in an equivalent ordered-random-partition form. This is a useful reformulation. The original CTMC or Gillespie view is natural for exact one-coordinate-at-a-time simulation, but it is awkward for analyzing the error caused by revealing a whole block in parallel. The partition view makes the trajectory a sequence of revealed coordinate blocks, which is exactly the right object for expressing factorization error.

Second, the paper introduces the dependence density rho(u). Up to an N-1 scaling factor, rho(u) is the average conditional mutual information between two distinct coordinates when each of the remaining coordinates is independently revealed with probability u. This is the conceptual center of the paper. It compresses the dependence structure of the target distribution into a one-dimensional function. The authors then derive an exact integral representation of the factorization error epsilon_fact in which the schedule appears only through weighting terms, while all distribution-specific structure is captured by rho. This turns schedule optimization into a tractable functional problem.

Third, the paper analyzes optimal schedules under different dependence regimes.
- For locally dependent distributions, such as ergodic Markov chains or sufficiently regular stationary processes, rho_N converges to a nondegenerate continuous profile g. In this regime, optimizing over smooth schedules can improve only the leading constant of epsilon_fact, not its N/K scaling. So the linear schedule is order-optimal.
- For exchangeable mixtures, where dependence is global and mediated by a latent variable, rho_N concentrates near the origin and the limiting profile degenerates. In this regime, early fine-grained steps matter much more, because revealing a small number of coordinates can sharply reduce uncertainty about the latent structure. Here the schedule can change the asymptotic order of the error. The paper gives a striking example: when K_N is about log N, the uniform schedule has error Omega(N / log N), while a geometric schedule achieves O(log N).

The paper also derives stationarity equations for finite-step optimizers, a large-N variational problem with a closed-form leading-order optimizer, and an asymptotic penalty induced by the randomness of block sizes in tau-leaping relative to a deterministic planner with the same limiting schedule. It further expresses total correlation and dual total correlation through rho, which helps place several existing heuristics and bounds into one framework.

An important modeling choice is what the paper does not do. It does not introduce a more expensive joint block predictor. Instead, it accepts the practical setting of one-coordinate conditional predictors and optimizes the sampler around that constraint.
