18:T563,The method has three parts. First, the authors build a controlled coordination substrate, ET-MCP, an ephemeral trace store exposed through MCP. It stores typed negative-knowledge events, especially failed_path events, and uses a simple TF-IDF ranker for retrieval. This keeps the memory mechanism intentionally simple so the comparison focuses on protocol effects rather than retrieval sophistication. Second, they implement two reader variants on top of the same store. In pull, the agent explicitly calls trace.query when peer information may help. In intercept, the framework automatically checks the store on every tool call and prepends a [PEER-WARNING] block when the current (name, arguments) matches a peer failure event. Because both variants share the same backend state, the comparison isolates who reads and when. Third, they define a paired measurement protocol around configuration equivalence rather than identity replay. They audit system prompt, tool list, sampling parameters, and message-array construction by code inspection plus a SHA-256 byte audit, but do not claim byte-identical replay. They also introduce pass_active^k, a pass@k metric restricted to tasks where the coordination store is non-empty at trial start. This separates overall deployability from the narrower question of whether coordination helps when it actually has an opportunity to help.19:T590,The main experiments use τ²-bench retail with Claude Haiku 4.5 at T=0, with two seeds of n=100 tasks per protocol. At the top-line level, pull, intercept, and no_coord all achieve trial-1 success of 0.54, and all paired sign tests fail to reject, with p=1.0. The key contrast is the clean trial-0 comparison between no_coord and intercept, which are configuration-equivalent. For seed 1, the paired outcomes are 21/11/68, giving a signed gap of +10 percentage points. For seed 2, they are 13/13/74, giving 0 points. Pooled across both seeds, the gap is +5 points with Wilson CI [-2, +12], Bonferroni-corrected p=0.711, so not significant. The largest single-seed contrast is pull vs intercept at seed 1: 27/9/64, or +18 points, corrected p=0.012. But it fails to reproduce at seed 2, where the contrast becomes 18/21/61, or -3 points. The pooled gap is only +7.5 points, with an upper Wilson bound around 15 points. The authors summarize the observed configuration-equivalent envelope for this benchmark/model/harness as [-3, +18] points, with pooled upper Wilson CI ≲15 points. They also run small transfer probes on Haiku-airline (n=30) and Sonnet-retail (n=30), showing that the floor is local and does not transfer cleanly.
