A structure theorem shows that a memoryless update is equivariant iff it takes the form Δ=H(GG^T)G, a left preconditioner determined by the Gram matrix. A transfer theorem shows that dynamics under a shared scalar preconditioner equal gradient flow under a rescaled time, so existing low-rank/minimum-nuclear-norm results for gradient flow carry over.
