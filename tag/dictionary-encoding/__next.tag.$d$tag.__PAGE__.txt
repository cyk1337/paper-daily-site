- Formal objective: produce compressed text T′ and dictionary D such that (i) lossless reconstruction is possible, (ii) token(T′+D) < token(T), and (iii) LLM analysis on (T′,D) is analytically equivalent to analysis on T.
- Pipeline:
  1) Whitespace segmentation into word units.
  2) Frequent subsequence mining under Lmax and fmin, processed from longer to shorter lengths to prioritize high-savings long patterns and manage nesting.
  3) Replacement with meta-tokens ⟨M#⟩ while preventing overlapping/ambiguous substitutions.
  4) Dictionary construction mapping meta-tokens back to original subsequences.
- Selection criterion: a token-savings optimization that accounts for dictionary overhead, ensuring compression is only applied when net token count decreases.
- Key operational assumption: the LLM can internalize the codebook from the system prompt and correctly interpret meta-tokens during downstream analysis without explicit
