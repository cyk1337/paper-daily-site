19:T66e,A statistical framework that decomposes learned representations into three components — signal, bias, and noise — is proposed to answer the core questions of PRH: 1) Signal component: The core of representation alignment is the Linear Representation Hypothesis (LRH), which states that the invariant common structure across all model representations is the sparse relationship between objects and their attributes being linearly encoded — the representation of each object is approximately equal to the linear combination of representations of all its constituent attributes, and the relationship between objects and attributes forms a sparse bipartite graph structure. By training top-k sparse autoencoders on features from various different models to extract sparse attribute features, the cross-model alignment degree of these sparse features is verified to be significantly higher than that of the original dense representations, proving that the sparse linear object-attribute structure is the common Platonic signal in PRH. 2) Bias component: Model-specific factors such as architecture and training procedures introduce implicit biases to representations, leading to inconsistent alignment results under different similarity metrics. This work proposes that such biases can be partially mitigated by statistical operations such as centering and normalization. 3) Noise component: The distribution of inference data directly affects representation alignment effect; meaningful representation alignment hardly occurs on out-of-distribution data, which is consistent with existing experience in multimodal out-of-distribution 给大语言模型插入一个控制向量（steering）是引导它输出特定内容的好方法——比如让它说话更礼貌、更专业、更简洁。但有个让人头疼的问题：同样的方法，有时候有用，有时候没用，甚至适得其反，而且尚无可靠的解释。 📐 现有解释有什么问题？ 学术界主流用"线性表示假设"（LRH）来解释 steering：模型把每个概念编码为一个方向向量，往那个方向推一把，概念就出来了。但 LRH 有个前提：独立概念之间必须能被映射成互相正交的方向。而现实是，模型要表示的概念数量远超它的维度，根本做不到全部正交。 这意味着：steering 的不稳定可能不是偶然噪声，而是有更深层的几何原因。 🥁 我们发现了什么？ 通过广泛的观测，我们怀疑概念可能不止是线性编码的。提出了柱面表示假设（CRH），把模型的概念表征想象成一个圆柱体，对于每个输入样本有： 🏹
