1b:T464,XComp combines two compression axes:
1) LP-Comp (Learnable & Progressive token compression): instead of heuristic token selection/pooling between LLM layers, the model undergoes an extra supervised “compression tuning” stage so that LLM layers learn to compress many per-frame tokens into very few (down to 1 per frame at the last layer). A progressive schedule gradually increases compression across layers/stages to prevent abrupt representational collapse at high compression ratios.
2) QC-Comp (Question-conditioned frame selection): at inference, frames are selected based on question-to-video attention scores. The authors highlight transformer positional bias in long contexts (early/late tokens receiving inflated attention), and mitigate it by splitting the long video into shorter chunks and performing relevance selection within each chunk (a localized selection scheme inspired by local attention ideas).
The base VLM pipeline remains LLaVA-like (vision encoder + projector + LLM); novelty is concentrated in learned inter-layer compression + progressive scheduling + chunked attention-based frame
