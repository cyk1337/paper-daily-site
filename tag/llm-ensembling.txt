An ensemble weight vector w defines a mixture distribution by convexly combining the model distributions; operationally, each generation can be assigned to a model sampled according to w. The target is clean risk: over the population of questions, the gold answer must be the unique mode of the infinite-generation mixture distribution. Since the p_i are unknown, the learner observes N sampled answers from each model on each of Q labeled questions. A zero-buffer ERM can overfit finite-N noise: an empirical margin may be positive even when the true clean margin is non-positive. The proposed buffered ERM counts a question as solved only when the empirical margin exceeds a positive threshold epsilon.
