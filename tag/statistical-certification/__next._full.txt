Holm’s step-down procedure controls the family-wise error rate at δ across candidates. The guarantee allows arbitrary within-unit dependence across candidates, such as correlated judge scores on the same prompt, while requiring i.i.d. evaluation units. Compared with matched-proposal Bonferroni, Holm gives a uniformly stronger rejection set on every dataset.
