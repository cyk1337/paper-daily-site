18:T61a,Training is modeled as a deterministic discrete-time dynamical system over a complete state S_t=(w_t,m_t,v_t,\tau_t), where w are parameters, m the first moment (momentum/Adam first-moment buffer), v the second moment, and \tau auxiliary deterministic state (clock/schedule).
- Forward sensitivity (transport): define q_t=\partial S_t/\partial \alpha for a source-strength parameter \alpha. The recurrence includes (a) injected source perturbations d_t (teacher-induced gradient changes) and (b) feedback through \partial G/\partial S because past perturbations alter w_t and therefore future gradients.
- Backward sensitivity (valuation): define a terminal observer O (in LLMs, a target-minus-reference candidate-string log-likelihood on a frozen prompt bank). Compute the costate p_T=(\partial O/\partial S_T)^\top and propagate it backward to obtain a per-step “future value” \lambda_t capturing how valuable a state perturbation at time t is for the final behavior.
- Exact transport–valuation identity: total behavioral change is expressed as a path integral pairing forward-propagated perturbations with backward-propagated values, making route dependence explicit.
- State surgery for causal identification: by transplanting components of the trainer state at a cut, they show the first moment m is the main carrier that continues to write source ancestry into weights after source removal. Transplanting m alone can change future trajectories while keeping current parameters/outputs unchanged; recovering terminal behavior requires (w,m) together.19:T4e9,The study evaluates Gemma-3 4B, Qwen-3.5 9B, and Gemma-3 12B on BBH, GPQA, and MMLU. It uses four misleading cue types adapted from Meek et al.: a Stanford-professor sycophancy cue, XML metadata, a grader-hacking hint, and an unethical insider tip, each pointing to an incorrect option. Cue type is varied on GPQA, while dataset transfer is tested with the Stanford cue fixed. For each scenario, degenerate rollouts are filtered, but examples are not conditioned on whether the model follows the cue or whether the uncued answer differs. The split is roughly 67/33 train/test, with about 129–165 test items per scenario. Generation uses greedy decoding with matched limits and penalties within each model. The main findings are that reliable increases in cue acknowledgment appear mostly in Gemma-3 12B and vary by dataset; however, vectors trained on one cue perform similarly to vectors trained on the evaluated cue, and vectors trained on one dataset transfer comparably to target-dataset vectors when the cue is fixed. Synthetic and cue-agnostic optimized directions perform similarly to labeled contrastive vectors. Additional analysis shows that successful steering leaves the overall rate of cue use roughly unchanged while reducing hidden cue use.1a:T6d9,实验覆盖三类模型：Mistral-7B、Qwen3-8B、Llama-3.1-8B。SOP canary 预注册为 24 个 SOP × 3 个随机种子，共 144 个 paired events per arm，并按 seed 报告。验收 guard 包括 WikiText-2 perplexity、MMLU 0-shot，以及 data-free output-fidelity probe。

关键结果很清楚。Mistral-7B 上，一个 in-regime 的 SVD build 只有 1.069× baseline perplexity，仍在常见 1.15× 接受带内，但相对原模型每个 query 多编造 +1.729 个步骤，95% CI 为 [1.076, 2.424]，三个 seed 分别是 2.06/1.90/1.85。与之匹配 perplexity 的 magnitude pruning build invention 为 -0.146，CI [-0.375, +0.035]，基本在 0 附近。Qwen3-8B 上，1.17× perplexity 的 SVD build invention 为 +0.208 [0.042, 0.375]，而 damage-matched prune 为 0.000 [0, 0]。Llama-3.1-8B 上，在 ppl 1.29× 时，SVD invention 为 +1.285 [0.743, 1.882]，而 pruning 仅 +0.069 [0.021, 0.125]，差距约 18×。

论文还给出一个很强的 dissociation：同一组 Qwen3-8B 的 probe-allocated b98 SVD 权重，在 paired fidelity test 上显著优于对照，但在 canary 上却显著失败，invention 为 +0.643 [0.171, 1.229]。也就是说，fidelity-up 和 behavior-broken 可以在同一组 tensor 上同时成立。

机制分析部分的结论也值得记住：量化在中等剂量下即便 raw perplexity 损伤更大，也未必触发 canary；但当量化被推到与失败 SVD cell 相近的 error rate，例如 1.05×，也会失败，pooled clean-slice Δ 为 +0.10 [0.029, 0.20]。作者据此认为，触发因素是误差相干性乘以错误率，而不是单纯的 perplexity 损伤。并且 onset 很早，在约 2% perplexity dose 内就能出现，说明这是分布式而非局部层的效应。1b:T746,The work has three main components. First, it builds a canary task for procedure execution. Each synthetic SOP contains 10 ordered steps plus a conditional 11th step. The prompt includes both a target SOP and a distractor SOP, and the model must enumerate the exact ordered steps under two paraphrased conditions. Evaluation includes step recall and branch correctness, but the key metric is invented_x: the number of step strings drawn from the global SOP vocabulary that do not belong to the target SOP or its own conditional branch. This isolates fabricated procedure content rather than generic recall errors.

Second, the paper compares compression operators at matched perplexity dose. It contrasts coherent low-rank truncation, implemented as per-tensor SVD truncation, with magnitude pruning, and uses quantization as an additional control. The design is important: by matching perplexity as closely as possible, the authors try to hold overall damage constant and test whether behavior still differs by operator. Their central finding is that coherent low-rank error induces procedure invention while pruning at similar perplexity often does not. This leads to the mechanism claim that the relevant axis is coherence times rate, not damage magnitude alone.

Third, the paper proposes a cheap data-free detector, a two-axis coherence screen, to predict which compressed builds will fail the canary. The exact implementation details are not fully visible from the provided text, but it is described as a fixed-threshold pre-flight check operating across architectures without labeled safety data. The authors are careful to say that the fidelity probe itself is not the main claim; the stronger point is the dissociation between fidelity and safe agentic behavior, and then the conversion of that cautionary result into a more actionable screening method.1c:T72f,The experiments cover three model families: Mistral-7B, Qwen3-8B, and Llama-3.1-8B. The SOP canary is pre-registered with 24 SOPs and 3 random seeds, giving 144 paired events per arm, with seed-level reporting. The standard acceptance guards are WikiText-2 perplexity, zero-shot MMLU, and a data-free output-fidelity probe.

The main quantitative results are sharp. On Mistral-7B, an in-regime SVD build at only 1.069× baseline perplexity, comfortably inside the common 1.15× acceptance band, produces +1.729 invented steps per query over baseline, with a 95% CI of [1.076, 2.424]. The three seeds are consistently high at 2.06, 1.90, and 1.85. A magnitude-pruned build matched to the same perplexity gives -0.146 with CI [-0.375, +0.035], effectively near zero. On Qwen3-8B, an SVD build at 1.17× perplexity yields +0.208 [0.042, 0.375], while the damage-matched prune arm is exactly 0.000 [0, 0]. On Llama-3.1-8B, at 1.29× perplexity, SVD gives +1.285 [0.743, 1.882] whereas pruning gives only +0.069 [0.021, 0.125], an 18× gap.

A particularly strong dissociation appears within a single Qwen3-8B arm. The probe-allocated b98 SVD build wins a paired output-fidelity test and still fails the canary with +0.643 invented steps [0.171, 1.229]. So fidelity-up and behavior-broken can hold on the same tensors.

The mechanism section adds an important control.
