18:T436,CurveFP is a block-scaled closed-product codebook family: 1) Quantized magnitudes are distributed across K interleaved logarithmic curves; each nonzero element stores a sign, two's-complement exponent, and curve index under a shared per-block power-of-two scale. 2) A rational radix r=2^(p/q) parameterizes the logarithmic lattice: p/q<1 contracts range and improves local resolution, while p/q>1 expands range. 3) Uniform curve spacing makes multiplication exactly closed in the code domain: sign XOR, curve-index addition, and exponent addition with carry — no lookup table, projection, or general multiplier is needed to form the product code. 4) A finite phase count qK/gcd(p,qK) is derived to explicitly map representation choices to the number of fractional-exponent classes that accumulation must combine, yielding an explicit quality-vs-complexity contract. 5) One algebra is instantiated as CurveFP8 for training (E4C3 for weights/activations, E5C2 for gradients, mirroring the range split of FP8 E4M3/E5M2) and CurveFP7 (E3C3, 12.5% narrower than FP8) for inference.19:T90b,方法很干净。作者从浮点数表示出发，写出在 mantissa 位数为 m 时，参数 wi 的表示间隔 ULP_m(wi)=2^{floor(log2|wi|)-m}。若一次梯度下降更新满足 |Δwi| < 1/2 ULP_m(wi)，按 round-to-nearest 后该坐标保持不变，于是该坐标被冻结。基于这个逐坐标条件，作者定义 frozen fraction f，即一次 step 后 bitwise 不变的参数比例，并把 f 首次达到 1/2 的时刻定义为 freeze time τ*。

更关键的是，他们把条件改写成无量纲形式 ρ_i < 2^{-φ_i}，其中 φ_i 是 log2|wi| 的小数部分，表示参数位于当前 binade 内的相对位置。若 φ_i 近似均匀分布，则 half-ULP 边界对应一个固定的一比特宽区间，其中位阈值给出 ρ* = 1/sqrt(2) ≈ 0.707，没有拟合参数。这个推导解释了为什么冻结阈值是 O(1) 常数，而不是随模型维度变尖锐的相变。

预测器的构造也很直接：先跑一条 fp64 高精度轨迹 {w_t, g_t}，然后对任意目标精度 m，在每个 step 上计算预测冻结比例 \hat f_t(m)=<1[η|g_{t,i}| < 1/2 ULP_m(w_{t,i})]>。\hat f_t 穿过 1/2 的时刻就是预测的 τ*。这里完全不需要低精度训练数据，因此如果预测成立，就说明冻结主要由算术离散化决定，而不是低精度训练反馈改变了动力学。

实验设计上，作者用了两类实现。其一是真实 bf16 硬件。其二是 mantissa-truncation emulator：所有算术在 fp64 中做，但每步后把权重 round 到 m-bit mantissa 网格。这样可以单独研究 mantissa 精度，而不混入 exponent 范围和 denormal 等因素。作者先在 m=7 上验证 emulator 与真实 bf16 一致，再扫 m∈{5,6,7,8,10,12}。这套设计的优点是把问题拆得很清楚：冻结来自权重存储精度本身，而不是某个硬件实现细节。

从全文看，论文还强调了两个细节。第一，冻结 onset 是 crossover，不会因为参数数目变大而变成无限尖锐的临界点，因为 half-ULP band 的宽度天然就是一比特。第二，在多层网络里，全局 L2 聚合的 ρ 可能失真，因为不同层尺度不同，小 bias 向量还可能长期不冻结；因此真正可靠的是逐坐标或分层统计，而不是单个全局标量。这一点对大模型监控很重要。1a:T9b4,实验分四层展开，逻辑很完整。

1. frozen-feature 线性 GD + 真实 bf16 硬件：作者先在最简单可控的设定里直接观测冻结。step 100 时 frozen fraction f=0，step 300 时 f=1.0，之后训练最后 90% 的过程参数都保持冻结。用首次完全冻结点读取阈值，得到 ρ*≈0.72。

2. mantissa emulator 扫精度与学习率：在 m=7 上，emulator 对真实 bf16 的 freeze curve 拟合 RMSE 仅 0.002，说明它是可信替身。进一步扫 m∈{5,6,7,8,10,12}，覆盖 128× 的精度跨度，以及 4× 学习率范围，得到稳定阈值 ρ*=0.71，跨设置 coefficient of variation 为 0.06。这里最关键的结果不是单点数值，而是阈值几乎不随精度和学习率变化，只是 freeze time 在大范围移动。

3. 先验预测 τ*：这是全文最有说服力的实验。作者只用高精度轨迹和目标 mantissa 位数预测冻结时间，预测与实测在 384× 的 freeze-time 范围内一致，覆盖从 11 到 4222 steps。对可训练网络的 55 个 clean cells，predicted/measured 的中位比值为 1.00，约 94.5% 的 cell 落在 15% 误差内。更重要的是，预测能跟住个体轨迹差异；某些 run 比邻近设置早两个数量级冻结，预测也能提前指出。

4. 可训练非凸网络与真实数据：
- 两层 tanh teacher-student 回归中，emulator 与真实 bf16 的 freeze curve RMSE 为 0.007，阈值约 ρ*≈0.74；跨 seed 的 within-cell CV 中位数为 0.21。说明冻结并不依赖 frozen features 或凸性。

整体上，最值得记住的数字有三个：阈值 ρ* 大致稳定在 0.71–0.74；高精度轨迹预测 freeze time 的中位比值 1.00；预测覆盖 11 到 4222 steps 的 384× 时间尺度。1b:T805,The analysis starts from floating-point spacing. For a format with m mantissa bits, the spacing at coordinate wi is ULP_m(wi)=2^{floor(log2|wi|)-m}. Under round-to-nearest, a gradient step leaves wi unchanged whenever |Δwi| < 1/2 ULP_m(wi). This gives a per-coordinate freeze condition and leads to a measurable frozen fraction f, defined as the proportion of coordinates that remain bitwise unchanged after a step. The freeze time τ* is the first step where f reaches 1/2.

The key theoretical move is to rewrite the condition in dimensionless form as ρ_i < 2^{-φ_i}, where φ_i is the fractional part of log2|wi| and indicates where the weight lies within its binade. If φ_i is approximately equidistributed, the half-ULP boundary occupies a one-bit-wide band, whose median yields a parameter-free threshold ρ*=1/sqrt(2). This explains why the onset is an O(1) crossover rather than a sharp size-dependent phase transition.

The predictor is deliberately simple. Run one fp64 trajectory {w_t, g_t}. For any target mantissa length m, compute the predicted frozen fraction at each step as \hat f_t(m)=<1[η|g_{t,i}| < 1/2 ULP_m(w_{t,i})]>. The predicted τ* is where \hat f_t crosses 1/2. Since the predictor uses no low-precision data, successful prediction would imply that the phenomenon is governed primarily by arithmetic discretization rather than by altered low-precision dynamics.

The experimental design separates arithmetic effects cleanly. Real bf16 hardware establishes that the freeze is physically present. A mantissa-truncation emulator performs all arithmetic in fp64 but rounds weights to an m-bit grid after each step, allowing controlled sweeps over mantissa precision without confounding exponent range or denormals. The paper also emphasizes an important practical nuance: in multilayer networks, a single pooled L2-based ρ can become misleading because layers live at different scales and tiny bias vectors may never freeze. Layerwise or coordinatewise statistics are therefore more informative than a single global scalar.1c:T91d,The empirical story is built in stages.

Second, the mantissa-truncation emulator is validated against real bf16 at m=7, reproducing the freeze curve with RMSE 0.002. Sweeping m in {5,6,7,8,10,12} spans a 128x precision range, and varying the learning rate spans another 4x. Across this grid, the threshold remains stable at ρ*=0.71 with coefficient of variation 0.06. The main point is that precision and learning rate shift the freeze time substantially, but not the threshold itself.

Third, the a-priori prediction of freeze time is the strongest result in the paper. Using only the high-precision trajectory and the target mantissa length, predicted and measured τ* agree across a 384x range of freeze times, from 11 to 4222 steps. On 55 clean trainable-network cells, the median predicted/measured ratio is 1.00, and about 94.5% of cells fall within 15%. The predictor also tracks trajectory-specific outliers rather than only average trends.

Fourth, the phenomenon survives beyond convex toy settings. In a two-layer trainable tanh teacher-student regression problem, the emulator matches real bf16 with RMSE 0.007 and the threshold is around ρ*≈0.74. Finally, the paper extends the observation to a 124M GPT-2 transformer with weights constrained to an 8-bit floating-point grid after each step. The full numerical details are not included in the excerpt here, but the inclusion of this experiment is important: the authors are not claiming a toy-only effect. They are arguing that the same arithmetic mechanism appears in standard Transformer training as well.
