c:T95c,技术方案由 VeriEvol 框架和 HTV-Agent 两个核心部件组成。

第一部分是 type-aware prompt evolution。输入是低难度 seed 样本 x=(I,q,a)，目标是构造更难但仍然 image-grounded 的新样本 \u007e{x}=(I,q',â,m)。作者没有采用单一通用改写器，而是按题型路由，例如 geometry、chart、OCR 等，每一类使用 family-specific evolution operators。这样做的原因很明确：视觉数学题的难度不是靠把问题写长，而是靠增加真正依赖图像结构的推理步骤。几何题可以通过引入辅助关系、角度/长度约束、多步推导来变难；图表题则可以通过跨区域比较、组合统计量、条件筛选来增加推理深度。route-specific operator 的价值在于避免生成“表面更复杂、实则可由文本先验回答”的伪难题。

第二部分是 HTV-Agent，即 hypothesis-test verifier。它不是让一个 judge 直接给分，而是采用“先提出候选解释，再主动寻找反证”的 falsification 流程。文中描述为四阶段：
1. independent solver hypotheses：多个独立求解器先给出候选答案或推理假设；
2. refutation-seeking verification：从 counter-evidence、programmatic checking、visual evidence 等多个通道尝试推翻候选答案；
3. conflict resolution by a decider：当不同通道结论冲突时，由 decider 汇总证据做裁决；
4. deterministic acceptance gate：只有满足预设接受条件的样本才进入 RL 数据池。

这里最关键的设计不是“多 agent”本身，而是 verifier 的接口化和可扩展性。作者把 difficulty scaling、answer verification、policy optimization 三者拆开：增加题目难度时，只需扩展 evolution routes；提高答案可靠性时，只需增加新的 refutation channel 或更严格的 acceptance contract；而训练端仍然消费标准 prompt-answer-reward tuple，不需要改 GRPO recipe。相比把生成、验证、训练绑成一个端到端黑盒，这种分层设计更适合规模化迭代，也更容易审计错误来源。

数据产物也分成两层：SFT 数据允许较宽松的 reasoning trace 和 evidence；RL 数据则要求更强的答案规范化和 deterministic reward-checker 保证。这个区分很合理，因为 SFT 可以容忍一定表达多样性，而 RL 的 reward 若不稳定，会直接污染梯度信号。d:Ta23,The framework has two main components: VeriEvol for prompt evolution and HTV-Agent for answer verification.

The first component is type-aware prompt evolution. Starting from a low-difficulty seed sample x=(I, q, a), the system constructs a harder but still image-grounded sample \u007etilde{x}=(I, q', â, m). Instead of using one generic rewriting model, it routes each seed by problem family—such as geometry, charts, or OCR—and applies family-specific evolution operators. This design choice is important. In visual math, difficulty should come from additional grounded reasoning steps, not from making the prompt longer or more verbose. Geometry can be made harder by introducing auxiliary relations and multi-step constraints; chart questions can be made harder by requiring cross-region comparison, aggregation, or conditional filtering. Route-specific evolution is meant to avoid pseudo-hard examples that look more complex but can still be solved from text priors.

The second component is HTV-Agent, a hypothesis-test verifier. Rather than asking a single judge to score an answer, it follows a falsification-oriented pipeline: propose candidate solutions, then actively try to refute them. The paper describes four stages: (1) independent solver hypotheses, where multiple solvers generate candidate answers or reasoning hypotheses; (2) refutation-seeking verification, which checks those candidates through counter-evidence, programmatic tools, and visual evidence channels; (3) conflict resolution by a decider when channels disagree; and (4) a deterministic acceptance gate that only admits samples satisfying predefined criteria into the RL pool.

The key idea is not merely “multi-agent verification.” It is the separation of interfaces. Difficulty scaling, answer verification, and policy optimization are decoupled. New evolution routes can be added without changing the verifier. New verification channels can be added without changing the RL optimizer. The training side still consumes standard prompt-answer-reward tuples, so existing SFT and GRPO recipes remain usable. This is a cleaner systems design than an end-to-end generator-verifier-trainer loop where all failure modes are entangled.

The paper also distinguishes between SFT and RL data products. SFT samples can tolerate more flexible reasoning traces and evidence. RL samples require stronger answer normalization and deterministic reward-checker guarantees.
