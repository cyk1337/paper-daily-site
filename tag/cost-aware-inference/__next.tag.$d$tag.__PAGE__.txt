9:T5e9,The method has three main components. First is the milestone-plan-act workflow. Instead of planning at the finest step granularity from the start, the orchestrator first decomposes a complex query into milestones. This reduces low-level execution noise and gives the policy a more stable state abstraction. Within each milestone, it builds a dependency-aware execution graph and can run independent steps in parallel across executors. Second is explicit executor profiling. Each executor comes with textual descriptions of capability and cost, so the orchestrator reasons jointly over task requirements and executor profiles rather than routing by query features alone. This differs from standard learned routers that often rely on shallow signals and fixed executor sets. Third is end-to-end RL with tree-structured rollout.
