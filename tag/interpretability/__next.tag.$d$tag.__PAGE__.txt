9:Ta82,The setup is intentionally simple enough for mechanistic analysis. The task is to take K distinct integers, followed by a SEP token, and autoregressively output them in sorted order. Most experiments train at K=16 and evaluate across lengths from 2 up to N. The model is a two-layer, single-head, causally masked transformer with no positional encodings. Removing positional encodings is not just a simplification choice; it avoids a structural barrier to length generalization and prevents the model from relying on absolute-position-specific strategies.

The central finding is that training converges to two solution classes. One is effectively single-stage: the second attention layer can be ablated with little or no effect, so most of the sorting logic is implemented without meaningful second-layer participation. The other is genuinely two-stage: both layers matter, and ablating the second layer hurts performance. This second class generalizes almost perfectly to longer sequences.

The mechanistic analysis of the two-stage solution has two main components. First is cross-layer specialization. The two layers are not redundant; they appear to divide labor, especially on cases where adjacent sorted numbers have small gaps. The paper defines the gap as the difference between consecutive sorted elements and argues that small-gap cases are relatively rare at K=16 but become increasingly common as sequence length grows. A model that does not learn to handle these cases robustly can look perfect in-distribution and still fail under length extrapolation. Second is a hinting mechanism. The first layer appears to narrow the set of plausible next tokens or provide a structured intermediate signal, and the second layer then selects among these candidates. As the number of possible candidates grows with input length, this staged procedure is more stable than a flatter one-layer-style selection rule.

The paper also studies why one algorithm emerges instead of the other. A key result is that layer-wise initialization scale can strongly bias training toward the single-stage or two-stage solution. That matters because it shows the bifurcation is not merely a post hoc interpretability story; it is tied to controllable early training dynamics. The authors also test minimum-finding and a greedy coin denomination task. Minimum-finding shows the opposite pattern, where the simpler solution generalizes better, while the coin task exhibits a broader mix of behaviors. 1) Subset intervention as a set-function
- For input x, take an activation h_x at a fixed layer/token.
- Select top-K active SAE features N_x with decoded contributions δ_i(x)=z_i(x)d_i.
- Define a fixed remainder h_x^0 = h_x - Σ_{i∈N_x} δ_i(x), which keeps everything else constant (unselected content + reconstruction residual).
- For any subset S⊆N_x, intervene with ~h_x(S)=h_x^0 + Σ_{i∈S} δ_i(x).
- For a scalar behavioral readout ψ, define g_x(S)=ψ(F(~h_x(S))). This isolates behavioral changes attributable to the retained selected contributions.

2) SAE Additivity Residuals (SAR)
- Approximate g_x using function classes that include interactions up to order r: r=1 (additive main effects), r=2 (additive + pairwise terms), etc.
- Under a subset distribution μ, take the L2(μ) projection Π_r g_x and define SAR_r as the normalized residual energy (variance not explained by order ≤r).
- Define Δ_2 = SAR_1 − SAR_2 and ρ_2 = Δ_2 / SAR_1 to quantify how much of the first-order residual is recovered by pairwise structure.

3) Why independent subset sampling matters
- With independent inclusion of each feature (a product measure μ), SAR admits an exact interaction decomposition: SAR_r equals the normalized energy of interactions above order r. This gives SAR a clean statistical meaning rather than a heuristic score.

4) Coordinate choice vs subspace: sum-preserving random rotations
- Construct orthogonal Q with Q·1=1 and rotate the K contribution vectors: D'_x = D_x Q.
- This preserves the selected subspace span(D_x) and the total contribution (sum of columns), keeping the downstream network and fixed remainder unchanged.
- Compare SAR and ordering metrics between learned SAE coordinates and these matched rotations to isolate the effect of coordinate system.
