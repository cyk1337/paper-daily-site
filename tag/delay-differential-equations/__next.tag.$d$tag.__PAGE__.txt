9:T61e,The model starts from diagonal SSMs and adds explicit delayed-state feedback. In a standard discrete SSM, the next state depends on the current state and the current input. In DSSMs, one or more delayed states, such as h_{t-τ}, are also injected into the recurrence. This gives the model a direct pathway to preserve and reuse historical information instead of relying entirely on exponentially decaying modes in the current hidden state. The paper has to solve three technical issues. First is stability: delayed feedback makes the system more delicate than standard ODE dynamics, so the authors introduce a practical parameterization and discretization that keep the recurrence stable. Second is history management: a DDE-inspired discrete model needs explicit buffers for past states and careful handling of delay indexing and initialization. Third is parallel training: standard LTI SSMs can be trained via convolution kernels and FFT, but delayed feedback changes the transfer function and creates aliasing and numerical issues in the frequency domain. The paper derives the exact transfer function for the delayed recurrence and proposes a kernel contour shift technique to suppress time-domain aliasing, preserving efficient FFT-based training. The use of diagonal parameterization is deliberate, since it keeps modes decoupled and makes both stability analysis and FFT implementation tractable. Unlike H3 or hybrid attention-SSM models, DSSMs do not add an external recall module; they modify the underlying dynamics to include explicit historical feedback.a:T477,The experiments are organized around three settings: targeted delayed-memory tasks, efficiency benchmarks, and standard sequence-modeling datasets. On efficiency, the model still supports FFT-style parallel training and linear recurrent inference, showing that delayed feedback does not collapse back into expensive attention. On standard datasets, the delayed-memory mechanism appears to transfer beyond synthetic tasks into broader sequence modeling. For foundation-model readers, the experiments mainly show that the architecture addresses a real weakness of SSMs, not that it already beats mainstream LLM backbones at scale.
