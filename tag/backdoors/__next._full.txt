To induce diversity, the authors prepend short random prefills drawn from common public corpora, then decode with temperature zero. These prefills act as varied entry points that can trigger overgeneralized finetuned behavior outside its intended setting. Second, score each completion under both the finetuned model and a reference model, which can be the pre-finetuning checkpoint or a cross-family model. Completions are ranked by perplexity-based gaps, typically favoring text that is low perplexity for the finetuned model and high perplexity for the reference model. Such text is likely to reflect newly learned behavior, memorized training strings, or emergent side effects of finetuning. The method does not require internal activations or prior knowledge of the target behavior.
