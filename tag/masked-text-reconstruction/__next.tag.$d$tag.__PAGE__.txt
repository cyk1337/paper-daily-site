9:T595,There are three main design choices. First, the task removes the QA scaffold. The input is one or more images with a local masked region, and the model must directly output the hidden text. This forces the model to perform relevance discovery, structural grounding, and content reconstruction by itself. Second, the dataset construction is intentionally human-curated rather than random masking. The masked region must be recoverable from surrounding evidence; the remaining context must provide enough signal; the sample should require visual, structural, or cross-region reasoning instead of pure language prior; and ambiguous cases are filtered by secondary review. This is important because random masking would often test guessability rather than multimodal understanding. Third, evaluation is level-aware. Since targets range from short strings to full sentences and paragraphs, the benchmark uses different criteria by difficulty/length: exact matching for short targets, semantic similarity and factual consistency for longer ones, plus an LLM-based factuality gate to better align automatic scoring with human judgment. The benchmark contains 2,771 test samples, covers single-page and multi-page inputs, 22 languages, and several source types including academic documents, webpages, charts/diagrams, and scene text.
