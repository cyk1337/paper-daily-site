18:T566,The analysis extends prior work on diffusion over mixture distributions by giving each cluster its own low-dimensional Gaussian structure. The first key step is to express the mixture score as a posterior-weighted average of cluster-wise scores. This turns denoising into a dynamical Bayesian classifier: at high noise, posterior mass is spread across clusters and the trajectory explores; once the signal-to-noise ratio crosses a threshold, the posterior concentrates and the trajectory commits to a single cluster with high probability. The threshold is shown to occur at SNR of order Theta(log(KD)/D). The second key step is to split the reverse process at this commitment time. Before commitment, the analysis handles inter-cluster ambiguity; after commitment, it studies denoising within one cluster and exploits its low intrinsic dimension. This yields KL error bounds whose dominant dimensional dependence is linear in the maximum intrinsic dimension across clusters, with only logarithmic dependence on D. Relative to prior single-manifold or purely qualitative mixture analyses, the novelty is the joint treatment of multimodal phase transition and intrinsic-dimension-sensitive KL bounds.
