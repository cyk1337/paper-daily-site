9:Tadc,方法上，作者做得比较干净，重点在于把 midtraining 的因果效应尽量从 post-training 中剥离出来。

首先是数据构建。作者从 Anthropic 2026 Constitution 中手工抽取 40 个 constitutional values，并配上定义文本。随后用 Sentence-BERT 对每个 value 做嵌入，计算其与其他 value 的平均余弦相似度，作为 centrality score。两个偏组织性的 value——helpfulness 和 principles hierarchy——因明显更外围，被排除出 curriculum unit selection，剩下 38 个 value。作者再用层次聚类把这些 value 分成四簇，并按平均 centrality 从高到低排序，得到 curriculum 条件下的教学顺序：从更核心的伦理价值，到身份/品格/福祉，再到操作安全与关系行为，最后到认识论完整性与诚实。这里的设计意图很明确：如果价值之间存在结构中心与边缘，那么先教中心、再教外围，可能更利于形成稳定表征。

其次是 2x2 因子设计。两个因素分别是 curriculum ordering 与 deliberative reasoning。也就是：是否按 centrality 导出的顺序组织内容，以及样本中是否包含显式价值推理。这样得到四个 constitutional midtraining 条件，再加一个 replay-only control。语料规模上，摘要给出总 constitutional corpus 为 394M tokens，其中 reasoning 版约 257.6M，no-reasoning 版约 136.8M。这个规模不算大到改变通用能力分布，但足以作为一个有针对性的 midtraining 注入。

第三是模型与训练位置。所有条件都从同一个 Nemotron-3-Super-120B-A12B-Base 出发，这是一个 hybrid Mamba-2/attention 的 MoE 基座模型，总参数 120B、激活参数 12B，预训练约 25T tokens。作者把 constitutional 数据插入 midtraining，而不是混在后续 SFT 里；之后再接一个 value-neutral SFT 阶段，确保如果 CMT 条件优于 control，这个优势主要来自 midtraining 内容，而不是后续监督阶段重复灌输了相同价值。

第四是评测设计。作者不仅看标准 alignment benchmark，还看 self-generated 与 established benchmark 的组合，并覆盖 alignment under pressure、value conflict resolution、blackmail、emergent misalignment 等更接近 disposition 的场景；同时在三个阶段评估：midtraining 后、SFT 后、以及 benign fine-tuning 后。这样可以区分“即时行为变化”和“能否穿过后续训练仍保留”的差异。

从方法论上看，这篇文章最重要的不是某个数据模板，而是实验隔离做得比较到位：同一 base model、同一后续 value-neutral SFT、同一 benign fine-tuning，只改变 midtraining 内容与组织方式，因此更接近在问“midtraining 到底能不能塑形”。a:T892,实验结论可以概括成三层。

第一层，constitutional midtraining 相比 replay-only control，确实带来更强的 alignment，而且这种提升不是只停留在 midtraining 刚结束时。作者在 post-midtraining、post-SFT、post-benign fine-tuning 三个阶段都做了评测，结论是优势能够延续到后续阶段，说明这不是一层很薄的行为补丁，而更像是对模型倾向的重新塑形。摘要里明确写到 gains are durable and generalizable，并且 survive post-training and benign fine-tuning。

第二层，真正驱动收益的是 content presence。也就是，只要 constitutional 内容在 midtraining 中出现，收益就已经相当显著；相比之下，curriculum ordering 和 deliberative reasoning 的额外增益更像二级效应。这个结论很重要，因为作者原本专门为这两个因素设计了结构化实验，结果却发现“有没有把价值内容放进去”比“怎么排顺序、是否显式写推理”更关键。这对数据工程的启发比对齐口号更实在。

第三层，收益具有一定 OOD 泛化，并且在压力场景下仍可观察。论文列出的评测包括 alignment under pressure、value conflict resolution、blackmail、emergent misalignment，这些都不是简单拒答率能概括的指标，而是在看模型是否形成了更稳定的价值取向。作者还开源了数据和一组 120B checkpoints，方便后续做 mechanistic analysis，这说明他们也把这项工作视为“行为倾向如何进入表征”的起点，而不只是 benchmark 提分。

从当前提供的全文片段里，缺少完整结果表，因此无法安全写出每个 benchmark 的精确分数、提升幅度、训练步数或 token replay 比例。可以确定的硬信息包括：模型是 120B total / 12B active，底座预训练约 25T tokens；constitutional corpus 总计 394M tokens，其中 reasoning 与 no-reasoning 两部分分别约 257.6M 和 136.8M；实验是 2x2 因子设计加 control，并在三个训练阶段做追踪评估。若精读原文，最该补的是各条件在不同 benchmark 上的绝对分数、方差、以及 benign fine-tuning 后保留率。b:T930,The methodology is notable for trying to isolate the causal effect of midtraining from later alignment stages.

The data pipeline starts from Anthropic’s 2026 Constitution. The authors manually extract 40 constitutional values and pair each with definitional text. They embed each value using Sentence-BERT and compute a centrality score based on mean cosine similarity to all other values. Two organizational values, helpfulness and principles hierarchy, are treated as outliers for curriculum-unit selection and removed from that part of the design, leaving 38 values. These are clustered hierarchically into four groups, ordered by average centrality. This produces a curriculum from more central ethical values toward more peripheral categories such as operational safety and epistemic integrity. The motivation is straightforward: if constitutional values have an internal structure, a central-to-peripheral curriculum may shape representations differently from an unordered presentation.

The training design is a 2x2 factorial setup with two factors: curriculum ordering and deliberative reasoning. This yields four constitutional midtraining conditions, plus a replay-only control. The abstract states that the total constitutional corpus is 394M tokens, with a reasoning-rich version of about 257.6M tokens and a no-reasoning version of about 136.8M tokens.

All conditions start from the same base model, Nemotron-3-Super-120B-A12B-Base, a hybrid Mamba-2/attention MoE model with 120B total parameters and 12B active parameters, pretrained on roughly 25T tokens. The constitutional intervention is inserted during midtraining, not folded into later SFT. After that, the authors apply a value-neutral SFT stage so that any downstream advantage can be attributed primarily to midtraining rather than repeated value injection during supervised tuning.

The evaluation setup is also carefully chosen. The paper tests not only standard alignment benchmarks but also self-generated and established suites covering alignment under pressure, value conflict resolution, blackmail, and emergent misalignment. Importantly, evaluation is performed at three stages: immediately after midtraining, after SFT, and after benign fine-tuning. That allows the paper to distinguish immediate behavioral effects from durable changes that survive later training.c:T777,The experimental findings have three main layers.

First, constitutional midtraining consistently outperforms the replay-only control, and the advantage is not limited to the checkpoint right after midtraining. The gains remain visible after value-neutral SFT and after benign fine-tuning on an unrelated task. That is the core evidence that the intervention is not merely a thin post-hoc behavior patch.

Second, the dominant factor is content presence. Once constitutional content is included in midtraining, alignment improves substantially. By comparison, curriculum ordering and explicit deliberative reasoning appear to have smaller, secondary effects. This is one of the most useful takeaways in the paper because the study was explicitly designed to test those structural factors, yet the strongest signal comes from the presence of the value-bearing content itself.

Third, the gains generalize beyond narrow in-distribution settings. The evaluation suite includes pressure scenarios, value conflicts, blackmail, and emergent misalignment, which probe behavioral disposition more deeply than simple refusal metrics. The authors also release the data and matched 120B checkpoints, making the work useful for later mechanistic studies of how alignment-relevant content changes internal representations.
