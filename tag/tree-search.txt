(1) Baseline reproduction: it reproduces part of the Lean vericoding benchmark on a cross-vendor model pool, measuring pass rates as well as reasoning-token emission, think-block frequency, and multi-block behavior. (2) Agent-loop baseline: instead of one-shot generation, the model iteratively calls tools, receives verifier/compiler feedback, and repairs its proof attempt over multiple turns. The study tracks solve rate by subset, per-turn tool-call rates, and scaling with K. (3) Search-based orchestration: the main contribution is to search not only over complete outputs but over intermediate proof states and context configurations. The state-based orchestrator branches from partial proof states and dispatches subagents to continue from different intermediate states. The context-based orchestrator searches over agent contexts, including resume strategies that restart from cleaner or reduced contexts. The final orchestrator combines state-based branching, resume, and context search.
