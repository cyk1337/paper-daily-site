Sequential training is used as the diagnostic baseline. The analysis has two levels: a model-level view that tracks capability changes across stages, and a token-level view that studies how individual training signals shape behavior and optimization. It first samples unsafe or unstable completions to identify failure trajectories. Prompt-end guidance tokens are used as a training scaffold to align a frozen reasoning model with these rewritten trajectories. Finally, answer-reasoning distillation internalizes the behavior into the model parameters, so inference does not require runtime guidance tokens. Compared with gradient-ascent suppression, GUARD does not merely push probability mass away from protected tokens. Compared with answer-only refusal optimization, it supervises both the reasoning channel and the final answer. The paper also introduces NFRS, a 0–1 score for structural stability, fluency, and absence of unsupported substitute content.1a:Tb9b,方法从 on-policy distillation 出发，但关键不是普通 teacher-student KL，而是“能力组合”的表示方式。作者对每个 specialist 构造一个 anchor pair：一个是 post-training 前的基线模型，一个是经过某种专项训练后的 specialist。对于同一个 token state，specialist 相对 anchor-before 的 log probability ratio 被视为该专项训练带来的 policy shift。这样定义有两个好处。第一，它抽取的是增量能力，而不是把 teacher 的全部分布都搬过来，因此更容易和其他 specialist 的增量叠加。第二，它不要求不同教师参数同构，只要求能在 student 生成的状态上打分。

在多能力组合时，Lightning Weave先把不同 anchor pair 的 log-ratio shift 对齐到 student 的 token states 上，再做加权求和，得到一个联合 shift。这里的设计选择很重要：作者不是分别构造多个 target 再做混合，而是先组合 shift，再构造单一目标分布。这样所有能力源在每个 token 决策点上共同作用，避免后续 target averaging 带来的不一致。

真正的方法核心是 Tilted-Target DOPD。作者指出，若直接把 DOPD 的 sampled-token surrogate 用在缓存轨迹上，会出现 stationary target 不保真的问题。原因是缓存来自旧的 behavior policy，当 student 更新后，基于缓存 action 估计的 regularizer 不再等价于当前 student 相对目标策略的 KL；即使 student 已经到达理想 shifted policy，loss 仍可能非零，梯度也不会消失，训练会继续把模型推离目标。为了解决这个问题，作者让每个缓存 shift 显式“倾斜”冻结的 behavior policy，构造出一个明确的 tilted target distribution，然后最小化当前 student 到该 target 的 KL。这样做后，至少在缓存状态上，目标是静态且可达的：当 student 匹配 target 时，loss 和梯度都应消失。文中还强调，这个构造保留了 token-level DOPD surrogate 在初始阶段的期望更新方向，因此既继承了原方法的有效起步信号，又修复了离线训练后期的目标漂移。

工程上，这个框架把昂贵的多教师在线打分转成一次性缓存。若有 K 个 anchor pair，在线 OPD 需要每条 student rollout 经过最多 2K 个 anchor 模型评分；Lightning Weave则先对缓存轨迹离线打分，之后训练只依赖 student 和缓存分数。这样多能力组合的成本主要前置到数据准备阶段，训练期更接近普通离线蒸馏。

与已有多教师蒸馏相比，这篇工作的区别不只是“多加几个 teacher”，而是把 teacher 的贡献限制为可解释的 policy shift，并为离线组合提供了一个有明确定点的目标分布。与参数空间 merge 相比，它不要求模型结构或参数对齐；与直接监督最终 specialist 相比，它更容易调节不同能力的权重，形成一条可控的 trade-off 曲线。1b:T9ae,The method starts from on-policy distillation, but the key idea is how capabilities are encoded. For each specialist, the authors form an anchor pair: a checkpoint before the relevant post-training stage and the resulting specialist after that stage. At a shared token state, the log-probability ratio between the specialist and the pre-stage anchor is treated as the policy shift learned during post-training. This is a useful abstraction because it isolates the incremental behavior change rather than copying the specialist distribution wholesale, and it does not require parameter alignment across teachers.

For multi-capability composition, Lightning Weave aligns these log-ratio shifts on student-generated token states and takes a weighted sum to form a joint shift. Importantly, it composes shifts first and constructs a single target distribution afterward, rather than building separate targets and averaging them later. That means all capability sources jointly shape each student decision at the token level.

The main technical contribution is Tilted-Target DOPD. The paper argues that directly reusing DOPD’s sampled-token surrogate on cached trajectories does not preserve the intended stationary solution. The cache was generated under an older behavior policy, and once the student moves away from that policy, the sampled-action regularizer no longer matches the KL that should be imposed relative to the current student. As a result, even if the student reaches the desired shifted policy, the cached surrogate can still produce non-zero gradients and push the model away from the solution. To fix this, each cached shift is used to explicitly tilt the frozen behavior policy into a target distribution, and training minimizes KL from the current student to that explicit target. This gives the offline objective a proper fixed point: when the student matches the target on cached states, both loss and gradient vanish. The paper also argues that this construction preserves the expected initial update direction of token-level DOPD, so it keeps the useful early-stage signal while repairing the late-stage mismatch.

The main novelty is the training recipe rather than the architecture. Stage I, Pure Distillation (PD), differs from standard KD in a simple but important way: it trains only on examples where the uncompressed RAG teacher is correct. Standard distillation would also transfer the teacher’s mistakes. PD uses the teacher only where it provides reliable supervision, giving the compressed model a strong initialization without forcing it to internalize the teacher’s failure modes.

Stage II, Hard Exploration (HE), is the key step. Reinforcement learning is applied only to queries on which the uncompressed RAG fails. This is a targeted exploration strategy. Instead of spending RL budget on the full data distribution, including easy cases already handled well by imitation, the method concentrates optimization on the subset where imitation is provably insufficient. The intended effect is not just error correction. It is to encourage the compressed model to discover computation patterns better suited to dense compressed inputs, and therefore capable of surpassing the token-based teacher.

Methodologically, this is a selective optimization recipe: use high-confidence supervision to enter a good basin, then use exploration only on high-value hard cases.
