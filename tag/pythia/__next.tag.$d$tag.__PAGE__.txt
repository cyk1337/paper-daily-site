5:T85d,方法上最关键的是“先把输出侧能解释的部分扣掉，再测中间层还能读出多少”。作者对每个 token 计算 cross-entropy loss、max softmax probability，以及某层 residual stream 的 L2 norm。然后用 OLS 回归用后两者去预测 token loss，得到 residual 误差，再把这个 residual 二值化，作为 probe 的目标。接着在冻结模型上，对各中间层训练线性 probe，并用 partial Spearman correlation ρ_partial 衡量 probe 输出与真实 token loss 的相关性，同时控制 max-softmax 和 activation norm。作者还定义了更严格的 r_OC 指标，在此基础上再控制一个基于输出层表示训练的 MLP predictor，用来隔离“输出表征已经足够恢复的部分”。

这个设计的意义在于，它不再把 probe 成功简单解释为“模型内部有额外信息”，而是明确要求这部分信息不能被输出置信度和简单输出侧读出替代。论文也反复强调，observability 只是在这个协议下的线性可读性，不等于模型内部真的在用这条信号做推理，也不等于更广义的 reasoning correctness。

实验设计分三层。第一层是方法学验证：说明如果不做 confidence control，probe 分数会被严重高估。第二层是 Pythia controlled suite 上的因果更强的比较：在同一训练 recipe 下，改变层数和头数等配置，观察 observability 是否系统性塌缩。第三层是跨家族观察性比较：Qwen、Llama、Mistral、GPT-2 等不同模型家族在相近规模下 observability 差异很大，说明 scale 不是主因。

全文最有信息量的技术点是“collapse”概念。作者发现某些架构配置在训练早期其实形成过可观测信号，但随着训练推进，这个信号被抹掉；而另一些配置则能保留或恢复它。这说明 observability 不是静态结构属性，而是架构与优化轨迹共同作用的结果。也因此，论文把 architecture determines observability 说得比较克制：在同一 recipe 内，配置是决定因素；跨 recipe 时，配置与训练动态共同决定塌缩点。6:T780,The core methodological move is residualization. For each token, the authors compute cross-entropy loss, max softmax probability, and the L2 norm of the residual stream at a given layer. They fit an OLS model predicting token loss from max-softmax and activation norm, take the residual, and binarize it to define the probe target. Linear probes are then trained on frozen hidden states from each layer. Observability is measured by partial Spearman correlation, rho_partial, between probe predictions and token loss while controlling for max-softmax and activation norm. A stricter metric, r_OC, additionally controls for a trained output-side predictor based on the final-layer representation.

This matters because the paper is not satisfied with showing that hidden states contain information. It asks whether they contain information that is not recoverable from standard output-side confidence signals. The authors are careful about scope: observability here is linear readability under a specific protocol, not a claim about reasoning correctness, semantic truth, or what the model itself uses internally.

The experimental structure has three layers. First, a measurement layer: show that probe scores are substantially inflated if confidence controls are omitted. Second, a controlled architectural layer using the Pythia suite: under a shared training recipe, vary configuration and test whether observability systematically collapses. Third, an observational cross-family layer: compare GPT-2, Pythia, Qwen, Llama, Mistral, and others to show that scale alone does not explain the effect.

The most interesting technical idea in the paper is observability collapse. Some configurations form the signal early in training but later erase it, while others preserve or recover it. That makes observability a joint product of architecture and optimization trajectory rather than a static representational property.7:T7a7,The first important quantitative result is methodological. Across 13 models from 6 families, confidence controls absorb 57.7% of the raw probe signal on average. On GPT-2 124M, the raw Spearman correlation between a linear probe and token loss is 0.549, but only 0.282 remains after controlling for max-softmax and activation norm. So without residualization, one would overstate output-independent observability by roughly a factor of two.

The strongest evidence comes from the controlled Pythia experiments. Under the same training recipe, every tested run with the (24 layers, 16 heads) configuration collapses to rho_partial around 0.10, despite a 3.5x variation in parameter count and two Pile variants. By contrast, six other configurations land between 0.21 and 0.38. The output-controlled residual r_OC also vanishes at the same collapse points, which supports the interpretation that what disappears is the output-independent hidden-state signal itself, not just a fragile probe metric.

Checkpoint dynamics are especially informative. Both the collapsing and healthy configurations show the signal at the earliest measured checkpoint, but continued training erases it in the 24L/16H class while the healthier configuration recovers or maintains it. This shifts the interpretation from “some architectures never form the signal” to “some architectures fail to preserve it under training.”

The cross-family evidence extends the pattern. At roughly 3B scale, Qwen 2.5 and Llama differ by 2.9x in observability with non-overlapping probe-seed distributions. Mistral 7B and Llama 3.1 8B also diverge despite broadly similar decoder-only shapes. The paper therefore argues that scale is not predictive; configuration is. A practical downstream result is that an observer trained on WikiText can catch a bounded subset of confidence-invisible QA errors without task-specific training, and instruction tuning does not erase observability entirely.8:T49b,Read the method section first and make sure the three quantities—observability, rho_partial, and r_OC—are clear. Without that, the architectural claims are easy to overgeneralize into broad interpretability statements that the paper is not making.

Then go to the control experiments showing why max-softmax and activation norm must be regressed out. That is the measurement foundation of the entire paper. After that, spend most of the time on the Pythia controlled suite and the checkpoint-dynamics figures. The most valuable result is not simply that one model family scores higher than another, but that a specific configuration class forms the signal early and then loses it during training.

Treat the cross-family comparisons and downstream QA transfer as external-validity checks rather than the main causal evidence. A skim should take 35–50 minutes. If reading more deeply, the main follow-up questions are why the 24L/16H class is especially prone to signal erasure, how this relates to depth/width/head-dimension and optimization choices, and
