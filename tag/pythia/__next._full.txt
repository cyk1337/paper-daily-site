18:T402,The key method is parameter-trajectory crosscoding. Instead of fitting sparse dictionaries to activations, the method fits one sparse dictionary to the readout rows of all tokens across all checkpoints. Each token has a shared sparse code, while each feature has checkpoint-specific decoder directions and gates. This keeps feature identities aligned over training and avoids the unstable matching problem that would arise from training a separate sparse autoencoder at every checkpoint. Rows are centered and scaled per checkpoint, so feature norms track structural reorganization rather than only global norm growth. The second diagnostic is readout swapping: hold hidden states fixed and decode them with readouts from other checkpoints. Together with linear probes, this separates hidden-state availability from native readout expression. The logit is treated as a dot product between hidden state and readout row, so changes in logits can be attributed to changes in the representation, the readout, or their interaction.19:T4a4,The empirical study uses public Pythia checkpoints at four model sizes, controlled LLaMA-style 70M runs, three initialization families, multiple data arms, optimizer-state records, and frozen-checkpoint interventions. The main observations are consistent measured core magnitude profiles after balancing, successful prediction of pooled-shape departure from field width on held-out runs and data arms, alignment of profiles across projections that share a channel space, and movement of q/k profiles with reassigned RoPE frequencies. Training trajectories show broad early formation followed by component-specific continuation or recession. AdamW second-moment factors align with functional channel spaces. Frozen-checkpoint edits separate an invariant reciprocal balance from a loss-sensitive relative gain: flattening the relative gain increases loss while matrix norms and the balanced core are held fixed, and the effect exceeds Gaussian controls after displacement normalization. The fitting protocol also reports that refitting W/s reproduces the scale ratio within about 0.2% and the shape within about 0.004; over k = 1.17 to 1.25, the relevant Weibull ratio varies by roughly 4%.1a:T85d,方法上最关键的是“先把输出侧能解释的部分扣掉，再测中间层还能读出多少”。作者对每个 token 计算 cross-entropy loss、max softmax probability，以及某层 residual stream 的 L2 norm。然后用 OLS 回归用后两者去预测 token loss，得到 residual 误差，再把这个 residual 二值化，作为 probe 的目标。接着在冻结模型上，对各中间层训练线性 probe，并用 partial Spearman correlation ρ_partial 衡量 probe 输出与真实 token loss 的相关性，同时控制 max-softmax 和 activation norm。作者还定义了更严格的 r_OC 指标，在此基础上再控制一个基于输出层表示训练的 MLP predictor，用来隔离“输出表征已经足够恢复的部分”。

这个设计的意义在于，它不再把 probe 成功简单解释为“模型内部有额外信息”，而是明确要求这部分信息不能被输出置信度和简单输出侧读出替代。论文也反复强调，observability 只是在这个协议下的线性可读性，不等于模型内部真的在用这条信号做推理，也不等于更广义的 reasoning correctness。

实验设计分三层。第一层是方法学验证：说明如果不做 confidence control，probe 分数会被严重高估。第二层是 Pythia controlled suite 上的因果更强的比较：在同一训练 recipe 下，改变层数和头数等配置，观察 observability 是否系统性塌缩。第三层是跨家族观察性比较：Qwen、Llama、Mistral、GPT-2 等不同模型家族在相近规模下 observability 差异很大，说明 scale 不是主因。

全文最有信息量的技术点是“collapse”概念。作者发现某些架构配置在训练早期其实形成过可观测信号，但随着训练推进，这个信号被抹掉；而另一些配置则能保留或恢复它。这说明 observability 不是静态结构属性，而是架构与优化轨迹共同作用的结果。也因此，论文把 architecture determines observability 说得比较克制：在同一 recipe 内，配置是决定因素；跨 recipe 时，配置与训练动态共同决定塌缩点。1b:T780,The core methodological move is residualization. For each token, the authors compute cross-entropy loss, max softmax probability, and the L2 norm of the residual stream at a given layer. They fit an OLS model predicting token loss from max-softmax and activation norm, take the residual, and binarize it to define the probe target. Linear probes are then trained on frozen hidden states from each layer. Observability is measured by partial Spearman correlation, rho_partial, between probe predictions and token loss while controlling for max-softmax and activation norm. A stricter metric, r_OC, additionally controls for a trained output-side predictor based on the final-layer representation.

This matters because the paper is not satisfied with showing that hidden states contain information. It asks whether they contain information that is not recoverable from standard output-side confidence signals. The authors are careful about scope: observability here is linear readability under a specific protocol, not a claim about reasoning correctness, semantic truth, or what the model itself uses internally.

The experimental structure has three layers. First, a measurement layer: show that probe scores are substantially inflated if confidence controls are omitted. Second, a controlled architectural layer using the Pythia suite: under a shared training recipe, vary configuration and test whether observability systematically collapses. Third, an observational cross-family layer: compare GPT-2, Pythia, Qwen, Llama, Mistral, and others to show that scale alone does not explain the effect.

The most interesting technical idea in the paper is observability collapse. Some configurations form the signal early in training but later erase it, while others preserve or recover it.
