The second component is SPaRTan, a training-free self-improvement loop. A model first plays self-play games, then reads its own trajectories and writes a transferable strategic playbook, and finally injects that playbook into the system prompt for future games. The method is framed as a form of in-context fine-tuning: the trajectories act as training data, and the playbook acts as the updated non-parametric state. The choice of natural-language playbooks instead of parameter updates is deliberate: it avoids the cost and instability of RL or SFT, and it makes transfer across games, roles, and even models directly testable.
