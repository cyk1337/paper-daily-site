18:T793,The analysis targets the Thinker backbone of Qwen3-Omni-30B-A3B-Instruct: 48 layers, hidden size 2048, with audio encoder features injected at audio-token positions before the text Transformer continues processing. The authors do not train probes or modify the model. They apply a logit lens to intermediate residual states, projecting hidden states through the final untied output head and measuring the rank of target tokens in vocabulary space. They use token rank rather than raw probability because logit scale drifts substantially across depth.

A key design choice is to predefine the workspace band as layers 17-38, roughly 35%-80% of depth, following prior text-model workspace studies instead of tuning the band on audio data. Readout is taken at audio-token positions, and for each answer option they report the best rank achieved by its first token across those positions and layers.

The central control isolates audio evidence from text prior. The written question and options are kept byte-identical, while only the waveform is swapped among the true clip, a mismatched clip from the same category, or silence. Because audio features are injected at positions that precede the textual question/options in the causal stream, readouts at those positions cannot simply copy the later option text. This makes a strong case that readable option-related concepts in the middle layers are driven by the waveform.

The paper then studies five aspects: hidden multi-hop concept formation, multilinguality of the workspace, differences between direct audio input and text caption input, depth-wise emergence of audio-driven signals, and layer-deletion evidence about where listening, integration, and output delivery happen. The resulting picture is a three-band organization: early sensory access, middle verbalizable workspace, and late output alignment. Errors often appear early as text-mode priors and are rarely corrected later.19:T8f2,The experiments are mainly controlled interpretability studies rather than benchmark-style evaluation. The model under study is Qwen3-Omni-30B-A3B-Instruct with a 48-layer Thinker backbone.

Five empirical findings stand out.

1. Hidden reasoning can be read without chain-of-thought. In the most striking example, for an audio clip whose verbatim transcription is essentially garbled, the middle-layer readout progresses through “water + gate,” then “scandal,” then “president,” and finally resolves to “Nixon.” This suggests that latent multi-hop semantic structure is already present before the model emits any answer.

2. The workspace is multilingual. On entirely English inputs, 38.5% of top-1 readouts are Chinese. The implication is that the intermediate workspace is not tied to the surface language of the prompt, but behaves more like a shared semantic space with multiple lexicalizations.

3. Audio preserves information that text captions lose. The authors compare direct audio input against feeding the model its own emotion-stripped caption of the same clip. The audio pathway recovers sound source, speaker role, and affective information that the text pathway does not. This supports the claim that the readable middle-layer content is not reducible to transcript text.

4. Audio-driven signals emerge at a specific depth range. The gap over text prior is near zero at input, becomes significant from roughly 12% depth onward, and is most cleanly separated from prior in the predefined workspace band (layers 17-38). The paper also argues that these middle-layer states are causally used before the final motor layers.

5. The answer is distributed, and hallucinations are born early. Layer deletion localizes “listening” to early entry layers and “delivery” to the final output layer, while no single interior layer is indispensable. A text-mode hallucination often takes over early and is usually not revised later.

The authors are careful about limitations: they use a logit lens rather than the more faithful Jacobian lens; they study one model family; the dataset slice is small and intentionally sound-dominated; and readout is sparse across audio positions. So the results should be read as mechanism evidence, not as a capability benchmark.1a:T4cb,For a quick but useful read, focus on three parts.

First, read the introduction and method carefully to understand the control logic. The most important point is how the paper separates waveform-driven internal content from prompt prior: byte-identical text, waveform-only swaps, and readout at audio-token positions that causally precede the options.

Second, inspect the qualitative case studies tied to the five findings, especially the Watergate/Nixon example, the multilingual readouts, and the audio-versus-caption comparisons. These examples carry most of the paper’s interpretability argument.

Third, read the depth analysis and layer-deletion sections. That is where the paper becomes relevant to pretraining researchers: it suggests that audio information enters a shared language-like workspace relatively early, and that hallucinations may crystallize before late layers have any chance to repair them.

A 30-45 minute skim is enough to extract the main ideas. If reading more deeply, the two questions worth pushing on are: how faithful the logit-lens readout is in the audio setting, and whether this middle-layer verbal workspace is specific to Qwen3-Omni or a broader property of multimodal language models.1b:T977,技术路线分三步，层层递进。第一步是行为层面确认 filler uplift。作者构造四类带明确中间变量的任务：1-fact addition、2-fact addition、2-hop letter position、以及完全在上下文内计算的线性方程系统。然后在问题和答案槽之间插入不同类型的内容空洞 token，如 dots、counting sequences、alphabet sequences，并比较有无 filler 时的正确率变化。这里设计得比较好的一点，是既覆盖“检索型中间量”，也覆盖“上下文内计算型中间量”，避免把现象误判为单纯 retrieval artifact。

第二步是定位机制。作者先看 attention，发现加入 filler 后，answer token 不再主要直接看 question，而是形成 question→filler→answer 的 relay 结构。这个观察本身还不够，因为 attention 不等于 computation。于是他们进一步用 logit lens 在每一层、每个 filler 位置上读 residual stream，检查哪些中间值在何时出现。结果显示，不同任务的中间变量会按时间和位置逐步浮现：例如 2-fact addition 中，A1 往往在 filler 前段较早层出现，A2 在后续位置出现，而 A1+A2 的和在靠近答案前的晚层才稳定“结晶”。这说明 filler 区域不是均匀缓存，而是有阶段性的计算轨迹。

第三步是做因果验证。作者采用 KV-cache transplant，只替换 filler token 对应的 KV 行，而不动问题和答案位置，观察答案分布是否随 donor example 改变。如果仅移植 filler 区域就能显著把答案 logits 推向 donor 的中间值或最终答案，那么 filler 中编码的内容就不是旁观信号，而是实际参与了生成。更细的一步是只移植那些 logit lens 已经能读出 addend 的位置，发现大部分效应都能保留；移植互补位置则几乎无效。这把“某些特定 filler 位置承载关键中间量”从相关性推进到了位置级因果性。

在无监督恢复部分，作者给出一个很实用的 pipeline：对所有 layer-position 做 logit lens，减去跨样本均值以抑制系统性伪影，再跨位置聚合 token 分数，把 top-50 候选交给 LLM judge 解释。这个流程不需要中间变量标签，也不训练 probe，重点是证明隐藏计算并不一定需要重监督才能恢复。与常见 probe 工作不同，这里更强调“可审计性”而不是“线性可分性”。1c:Ta01,The method proceeds in three stages. The first stage establishes the behavioral phenomenon. The authors build four task families with explicit intermediate values: 1-fact addition, 2-fact addition, 2-hop letter-position queries, and a system-of-equations task where the relevant values are computed entirely in context. They insert content-free fillers between the question and the answer slot, using dots, counting sequences, and alphabet sequences. This task design is important because it spans both retrieved intermediates and in-context computed intermediates, reducing the chance that the effect is just a retrieval artifact.

The second stage localizes the mechanism. Attention analysis shows that when filler is present, the answer token no longer relies mainly on a direct question-to-answer path. Instead, the model forms a relay structure: question to filler to answer.
