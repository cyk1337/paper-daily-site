18:T9a8,方法叫 Joint-Output On-Policy Distillation，简称 JO-OPD。核心思路是：既然同一个模型在 S2T 模式下文本回答更准，就把这个更强的文本策略当 teacher，去监督学生在 S2TS 联合生成轨迹上的文本预测；同时再加一个 preservation objective，尽量别把原有的非文本生成能力破坏掉。

具体机制分三步。

第一步，学生模型以 S2TS 模式生成 joint trajectory，里面包含文本 token、声学 token 和控制 token。训练不是在 teacher 的理想轨迹上做，而是在 student 自己生成的轨迹上做，这就是 on-policy 的关键。原因很直接：推理时模型看到的是自己生成出来、带有声学历史的上下文，如果只在 teacher forcing 轨迹上蒸馏，会出现训练-推理分布不一致。

第二步，在每个文本位置 i，构造 teacher 和 student 的不同上下文。student 看到完整的 joint history，也就是文本和声学 token 都在；teacher 则对同一段 student 前缀做 text-only projection，只保留其中的文本 token，去掉 acoustic outputs 和 control tokens。这样 teacher 给出的分布代表“如果没有被声学历史干扰，这个位置更合理的文本偏好是什么”。蒸馏时只在 text vocabulary 上比较分布，而不是让 student 去匹配 text 与 non-text 的总概率分配。这个设计很重要，因为作者要修的是文本决策，不是强行改变什么时候该说话、什么时候该出声学 token。

第三步，加入 non-text preservation。由于蒸馏目标会推动模型把概率质量更多放到正确文本上，如果不加约束，可能伤到原本的语音生成行为。为此作者用 base model 在 S2TS 模式下的非文本预测分布作为参考，对 student 的 native non-text predictions 做正则，尽量保持声学和控制 token 的生成习惯。论文还区分了 JO-OPD-Soft 和 JO-OPD-Hard：前者保留 teacher top-k 文本 token 的软分布并重归一化，后者用 one-hot 目标。按经验判断，Soft 版本更能传递相对偏好信息，尤其在开放词表回答中更稳。

这个方法和常见知识蒸馏的区别在于两点：一是 teacher 与 student 共享同一个底座，只是运行在不同输出模式；二是监督发生在 student 自己采样出的 joint trajectory 上，而不是离线标注序列上。它更像一种模式间 policy transfer，而不是传统的 teacher-student 压缩。19:T863,The method is Joint-Output On-Policy Distillation (JO-OPD). The idea is simple: if the same model is better at text answering in S2T mode, use that policy as a teacher for text decisions made during S2TS generation, while regularizing the model so that its non-text generation behavior is preserved.

The training loop works as follows.

First, the student model generates a joint S2TS trajectory containing text tokens, acoustic tokens, and control tokens. Distillation is performed on student-generated trajectories rather than teacher-forced ones. That on-policy choice matters because, at inference time, the model conditions on its own interleaved history, including acoustic outputs.

Second, at each text position, the teacher and student are given different views of the same prefix. The student conditions on the full interleaved history. The teacher conditions on a text-only projection of that same student prefix, where acoustic and control tokens are removed. This isolates what the model would prefer textually if it were not being perturbed by acoustic history. Distillation is applied only over the text vocabulary, not over the full text-vs-non-text probability mass. That design keeps the objective focused on improving text token choice rather than changing the timing or frequency of non-text emissions.

Third, JO-OPD adds a preservation objective for non-text predictions. Without it, improving text predictions could distort the model’s native speech-generation behavior. The base model’s S2TS non-text distribution is used as a reference, and the student is regularized to stay close on acoustic/control-token predictions. The paper also distinguishes JO-OPD-Soft and JO-OPD-Hard: the soft variant keeps the teacher’s top-k text-token distribution and renormalizes it, while the hard variant uses a one-hot target.

What makes this different from standard distillation is that teacher and student are the same base model run in different output modes, and supervision is aligned to the student’s own joint trajectory. It is better viewed as policy transfer across generation modes than as classic model compression.1a:T617,The first important result is the size of OMG itself. On paired evaluations of Step-Audio-2-mini and Baichuan-Audio-Instruct, the gap is large under identical speech inputs. On Spoken-MQA and speech-rendered GSM8K, OMG reaches 42.87 and 29.72 percentage points for Step-Audio-2-mini, and 12.41 and 10.39 points for Baichuan-Audio-Instruct. The paper also shows that explicit reasoning instructions on human-recorded VoiceBench-BBH further widen the gap for Step-Audio-2-mini, suggesting that the issue is not limited to arithmetic-style tasks.

The main empirical claim is that JO-OPD reduces OMG in both models. The paper carefully separates two evaluation targets: S2TS(T), the internal text in the joint trajectory, and S2TS(S), the spoken response after speech reconstruction and transcription.
