a:T570,The model is trained as a continuous diffusion SLM over log-mel filterbanks rather than over discrete codec or SSL tokens. The choice is deliberate: log-mel features are physically grounded, model-agnostic, and avoid the compression artifacts and encoder-decoder coupling introduced by learned tokenizers. The data source is SpeechCrawl, a large conversational speech corpus designed to be diverse, multi-speaker, and multilingual. The raw samples average about 30 minutes, with roughly 60% English. For controlled scaling experiments, the authors use WhisperX with Whisper large-v3 to estimate language composition, then keep samples longer than five minutes with at least 99% English speech, yielding 7 million hours of English speech. A central methodological addition is phoneme Jensen-Shannon divergence, or pJSD. Generated and real speech are converted into phoneme sequences, and the Jensen-Shannon divergence between their phoneme n-gram distributions is computed. This evaluates linguistic shape without requiring factorized AR likelihood. The scaling analysis covers validation loss, pJSD, perceptual quality metrics, and components from Meta Audiobox Aesthetics.
