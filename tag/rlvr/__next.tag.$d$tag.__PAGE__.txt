9:T5d4,RISE constructs a synthetic teacher by extrapolating the displacement between a current RLVR-updated checkpoint and a trailing anchor checkpoint. Let π_{θ_n} be the policy at iteration n, updated by RLVR to π_{θ'_{n+1}}. Choose a representation map φ that embeds a policy into a vector space where linear operations are meaningful (parameters or logits/distributions). Define the recent improvement direction as Δ = φ(π_{θ'_{n+1}}) − φ(π_{θ_n}). The self-extrapolated teacher is obtained by projecting further along this direction with scale β:

φ(teacher) = φ(π_{θ'_{n+1}}) + (β−1)·Δ, with β>1.

Two instantiations are emphasized:
- Weight-space extrapolation (φ=θ): θ_future = θ'_{n+1} + (β−1)(θ'_{n+1}−θ_n), essentially task arithmetic with an extrapolation coefficient; the teacher is an explicit model.
- Logit-space extrapolation (φ=logits / next-token distributions): extrapolate at the output distribution level (implemented via a geometric mixture / distributional extrapolation), producing token-level targets without necessarily materializing a full parameter-space teacher.

Training alternates RLVR and OPD in a recursive loop: RLVR grounds the direction (so extrapolation is not amplifying arbitrary drift), and OPD distills the extrapolated teacher into the student via token-level KL/CE. 论文先在理论上 formalize 了 coverage。设任务有二值可验证奖励，RL 初始阶段真正有用的是：对一个 prompt，模型在 k 次采样内至少出现一个 reward-bearing response 的概率，这正是 pass@k 的含义。large-k pass@k 越高，RL rollout 越容易拿到正信号。TailSFT 因而不再试图均匀提升所有监督样本的 likelihood，而是优先学习那些初始模型还不容易覆盖到的序列。

关键设计点有两个。第一，过滤是 sequence-level，而不是 token-level reweighting。因为 coverage 关心的是“完整响应是否可被采到”，不是局部 token 拟合。第二，过滤标准是相对于 initial policy，而不是相对于当前训练中的 policy。作者强调这一点并给出理论说明：若按当前策略动态过滤，训练过程会不断改变“谁算已经学会”，容易引入不稳定或错误的偏置；相对于初始策略做过滤，则更接近“保留初始模型已有覆盖、把增量学习预算投向尾部”的目标，并且能在文中的设定下给出优于标准 CE 和一些朴素过滤方案的 coverage 保证。

从算法形态看，TailSFT 是一个 drop-in SFT objective modification，不需要改模型结构，也不依赖 RL 阶段信息。实现上更像在 SFT 数据上做基于 base policy 的 hard example selection，但它不是为了提升训练时 loss 或 pass@1，而是为了提升后续 RL 可利用的探索起点。论文还提出了一个 coverage-ratio diagnostic，用 base model 和标准 SFT run 的统计量来判断 TailSFT 是否有望改善 coverage。这个诊断给了方法一个实用入口：不是所有数据集、所有任务都一定适合过滤，先看 coverage ratio 可以减少盲试。16:T85d,TailSFT is a sequence-level filtering method for supervised fine-tuning. During SFT, it removes sequences that are already sufficiently well fit by the initial policy, and concentrates updates on the under-modeled tail of the response distribution. The motivation is that standard cross-entropy keeps rewarding likelihood increases for every demonstration, including responses the model can already sample easily. Because probability mass is limited, pushing those easy responses further can reduce mass assigned to other potentially useful responses, hurting coverage.

The paper formalizes coverage in the setting of binary verifiable rewards. For a prompt x, what matters for RL initialization is the probability that at least one of k samples receives reward 1. This is exactly what pass@k measures. Large-k pass@k is therefore a proxy for how much positive learning signal RL can expect from the initialization under a realistic rollout budget.

Two design choices are central. First, filtering is done at the sequence level rather than the token level, because coverage is about sampling complete reward-bearing responses, not about local token fit. Second, filtering is defined relative to the initial policy rather than the current policy during training. This is not a cosmetic detail. The authors argue, and partially prove, that filtering relative to the initial policy better preserves useful pre-existing coverage and avoids the instability or myopia of more naive dynamic filtering schemes. Under their setup, this criterion yields better coverage guarantees than standard CE and simpler alternatives.

Operationally, TailSFT is a drop-in modification to SFT rather than a new architecture or an RL-dependent method. It resembles hard-example selection based on the base model, but the target is not better training loss or pass@1; it is better downstream RL initialization. The paper also introduces a lightweight coverage-ratio diagnostic, computed from the base model and a standard SFT run, which provides a sufficient condition for TailSFT to improve coverage. That diagnostic makes the method more actionable in practice.17:T5c1,The experiments are organized in three layers. In that setting, filtering already-fit examples improves large-k pass@k even when cross-entropy becomes worse and pass@1 does not improve. This is an important result because it shows TailSFT is not just an empirical trick on benchmark tasks; it is optimizing a genuinely different objective.

Second, the method is tested on OLMo-3 7B for math and coding. According to the reported headline numbers, TailSFT improves pass@16 by up to 16.8 absolute points on coding and 3.1 points on math.
