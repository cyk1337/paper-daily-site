9:T446,The central method is double stratification. The evaluation and the training population are both split along two axes: exposure, meaning whether the agent observed attacker-controlled injection content, and commitment, meaning whether it goes on to make an unsafe tool call that advances the attacker’s goal. The authors add a thin audit layer to AgentDojo-style evaluations and extract activations at security-relevant decision points, including after untrusted content, before tool selection, and before tool execution. They compare logistic probes on activations, classifiers over Natural Language Autoencoder verbalizations, TF-IDF text baselines, and the model’s own zero-shot verbalized forecast. The critical contrast is naive mixed unsafe-versus-rest training versus exposed-only commitment training. The mixed contrast includes unexposed safe cases together with exposed safe and exposed unsafe cases, so the easiest learned signal is exposure. The commitment contrast restricts training to exposed cases and asks whether the model will actually comply with the injected objective.a:T4a0,The experiments cover the primary Qwen AgentDojo grid, held-out suites, template transfer, and replications on Mistral-7B and OLMo-2-7B.
