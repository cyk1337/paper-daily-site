9:T5b8,The method has three layers. The first is the institutional red-teaming protocol itself: treat a deployment rule as the intervention variable, hold everything else fixed, and measure the induced safety deficit. The second is a compact parameterization of consequence-allocation rules using three auditable coordinates. Concentration (kappa) captures how sharply loss is focused on one agent versus spread across the group. Identity salience captures whether the loss bearer is explicitly named as a structural target rather than selected anonymously or randomly. Incidence captures whether the burden falls regressively on less-resourced agents, progressively on more-resourced agents, or neutrally. This turns vague rule text into a structured design space. The third layer is the benchmark instantiation, IABench-CA. It uses a three-agent volunteer’s-dilemma game: agents hold private resources, decide how much to contribute toward a common threshold, and if the threshold is missed, a consequence rule determines who pays. The benchmark spans 228 contexts, 5 canonical rules, and 7 model populations, for 33,924 games. The paper also defines an Institutional Alignment Gap against a normative cooperative reference, and uses auto-labeled reasoning traces to inspect strategic explanations.
