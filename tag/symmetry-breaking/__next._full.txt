17:T404,Starting from the established key-value memory interpretation of vanilla FFNs, the work first proves that static or per-pair query projections can be absorbed into the key matrix without increasing expressivity. To unlock additional capacity, dynamically computing per-pair queries and keys from input tokens is required, but this is parameter-prohibitive for large hidden dimensions. A rank-1 outer product approximation of each per-pair QK^T matrix recovers exactly the elementwise multiplicative form of gated MLPs. The work further proves that applying nonlinear activation to one branch (the query branch) before multiplication breaks two symmetries of the unmodified rank-1 formulation: (1) query-key exchange symmetry, broken by any nonlinear activation (only linear activations preserve it); (2) inverse-scaling symmetry, broken by non-homogeneous activations such as SiLU and GELU (ReLU preserves it for positive scaling factors due to positive homogeneity). A rank-R generalization of the formulation is also proposed.18:T61b,The method has three parts. First, the paper formalizes geometric continuity using the absolute cosine similarity between adjacent layers’ top right singular vectors v1 or top left singular vectors u1, and adds a sigma-squared-weighted all-spectrum variant to distinguish head-direction alignment from broader subspace alignment. Second, it runs controlled ablations on a 16-layer, width-256 residual MLP. The main finding is that residual connections make cumulative gradients across layers point in similar directions, so weight updates accumulate coherently across depth. But this alone is insufficient if the model retains rotational symmetry: layers can still drift independently within equivalent rotated coordinate systems. Activations and normalization matter because they break that symmetry. A particularly clean experiment introduces a nonlinear yet rotation-preserving activation and shows that it fails to produce continuity, isolating symmetry breaking rather than nonlinearity per se as the operative factor. Third, the paper maps the mechanism back to transformers. Q, K, Gate, and Up read from the residual stream and therefore show strong input-space continuity; O and Down write back to the residual stream and therefore show strong output-space continuity; V lacks a nearby symmetry-breaking interaction and exhibits much weaker continuity. The paper also distinguishes the roles of activation and LayerNorm: activation tends to concentrate continuity in the leading singular direction, while LayerNorm spreads it across multiple directions.19:T52d,Experiments are split between toy MLPs and small transformers. The toy setup is a residual MLP with hidden size 256 and depth 16 trained on MNIST. The authors ablate residual connections, activations, normalization, and related factors, then track adjacent-layer singular-vector similarity and effective rank. The main empirical results are: residual connections are necessary because they substantially increase cross-layer similarity of cumulative gradients; standard activations and LayerNorm both break rotational symmetry but in different ways, with activations concentrating alignment in the top singular direction and LayerNorm distributing it more broadly; and a rotation-preserving nonlinear activation does not sustain continuity. The most informative direct ablation removes the MLP activation, dropping Gate v1 continuity from 0.66 to 0.37 with only about a 3% perplexity change.
