The position of this margin directly predicts how easily content can be recovered via relearn attacks. KKT stationarity analysis shows the cliff arises when the retain regularizer holds the diagnostic log-odds of forget content above a floor, a condition induced by token-saturating losses whose gradients vanish once forget tokens are suppressed. Margin Calibration (MC) is a non-saturating correction: a softplus hinge loss anchored at the reference model's per-token margin, whose gradient scales with the margin gap and thus does not vanish even when forget tokens are suppressed. A KL constraint on a disjoint instruction corpus limits drift on non-forget behavior.
