9:T4b1,The method is built around the notion of consilience: a conclusion is reliable when the model implicitly recognizes multiple plausible approaches and still converges to the same answer.

Confidence signal:
- Token-level confidence is computed from the top-K distribution at each step (mean negative log-prob over top-K tokens). Sharper distributions imply higher confidence.
- Baseline VF-TTS selects the rollout with the highest average confidence over the whole sequence.

Failure analysis:
- On easy/medium problems, correct traces tend to have higher average confidence.
- On hard subsets (e.g., where the model’s accuracy is <20%), the confidence distributions for correct vs incorrect traces become inseparable or even reversed, making confidence maximization unreliable.

Consilience metric:
- Instead of maximizing confidence uniformly, it enforces temporal asymmetry: penalize high confidence early (to avoid premature commitment) while rewarding high confidence late (to ensure convergence).
- Practically, sample multiple traces, compute the consilience score for each, and select the top-ranked trace. No external verifier and no additional training are required; only logits are needed.a:T884,方法很直接，但设计上有几个关键点。RGSD 用同一个 base checkpoint 复制出 teacher 和 student。student 只看原始 prompt，按当前策略 on-policy 采样回答；teacher 冻结参数，但在输入里额外看到该 prompt 对应的 rubric，并在 student 已生成前缀的条件下，对下一个 token 给出分布。训练目标不是让 student 拟合 teacher 的整句参考答案，而是对 student 自己采样出来的轨迹做逐 token 分布蒸馏。

从全文描述看，teacher 的 rubric 输入不是机械地把 checklist 原样拼进去，而是包成与 prompt 同一轮 user message，并加一段过渡指令，要求回答满足这些要求但不要显式提 rubric。这一点很重要，因为目标不是让模型学会复述 rubric，而是把 rubric 约束转成自然回答风格。论文还做了 enrichment source 的对比：把 teacher 额外条件换成 reference response，效果不如直接给 rubric，说明结构化评价标准比单个示例更适合作为蒸馏条件。

主结果上，RGSD 与 judge-based GRPO 整体接近，且略有优势：medical 域平均提升为 +6.1 分，对比 GRPO 的 +5.9；science 域为 +4.9，对比 +4.5。这里的提升是相对各自 base model 的 rubric-based 评测增益。论文强调，RGSD 达到这些结果时不需要任何训练时 judge call，而且每个 prompt 只生成一条响应，不像 GRPO 需要多条 rollout 再评分，因此训练吞吐和工程复杂度都更好。

一个有意思的现象出现在 Qwen-2.5-7B-Instruct：RGSD 在大约只有 GRPO 一半的响应长度时就达到峰值质量。这说明 token-level distillation 不只是省 judge 成本，也可能抑制 RL 常见的 length inflation。对开放式生成来说，这个点很实际，因为很多 rubric-based RL 会通过更长、更面面俱到的回答去“刷分”。

消融里最重要的两组。第一组是 judge strength：当 GRPO 使用更强、更贵的 judge 时，在部分设置下可以超过 RGSD。这说明 RGSD 不是无条件优于 verifier-based RL，它更像是在“中等 judge 质量 + 高训练成本”区间里提供了更好的性价比。第二组是 enrichment signal：teacher 额外看到 rubric，比看到 reference response 更有效，说明 rubric 提供的是更可泛化、更可组合的约束信息，而不是单一答案模板。

Several design choices matter. First, this is on-policy distillation rather than offline imitation. The teacher does not supervise its own preferred trajectory; it provides local corrective distributions on states the student actually visits. That makes the update closer to policy improvement than standard response distillation. Second, teacher and student share the same underlying model family and initialization. The gain does not come from a stronger teacher model, but from exposing the teacher to extra task structure through the rubric. Third, the paper uses a clipped Jensen-Shannon divergence instead of a plain KL loss. The motivation is stability: early in training, teacher and student can disagree sharply, and a one-sided KL can overemphasize low-entropy teacher peaks. A symmetric JS objective with clipping reduces extreme gradients and makes the distillation signal less brittle.

The paper also pays attention to how the rubric is injected. The rubric is wrapped into the same user turn as the prompt, followed by a short transition telling the teacher to satisfy the criteria without explicitly mentioning them. This is important because the target behavior is not rubric recitation, but natural responses that implicitly satisfy the checklist. An additional ablation compares rubric conditioning against conditioning on a reference response; the rubric is stronger, which suggests that structured criteria are a better source of transferable supervision than a single exemplar.

On the main comparison, RGSD is competitive with, and slightly ahead of, judge-based GRPO on average. In medical tasks, the average gain is +6.1 points versus +5.9 for GRPO. In science, it is +4.9 versus +4.5. The paper emphasizes that RGSD reaches these numbers without any training-time judge calls and with only one generated response per prompt, whereas GRPO requires multiple rollouts plus grading. So the quality is similar, but the training loop is materially cheaper and simpler.

One notable behavioral result appears on Qwen-2.5-7B-Instruct: RGSD reaches peak quality at about half the response length of GRPO. This is more than a cost detail. It suggests that token-level distillation may reduce the length inflation often seen in RL-style optimization, where models learn to satisfy rubrics by becoming verbose.

The most informative ablations are twofold. First, judge strength matters. When GRPO uses a stronger and more expensive judge, it can outperform RGSD in some settings. Second, the enrichment source matters. Conditioning the teacher on the rubric works better than conditioning on a reference response, indicating that explicit criteria provide more compositional and generalizable guidance than a single target answer.
