a:T43d,The paper introduces PreRL and then Dual Space RL (DSRL):
- Define two optimization spaces: post-train space optimizes πθ(y|x)=P(y|x); pre-train space optimizes πθ(y)=P(y) by dropping the conditioning input during updates.
- Justify PreRL by showing strong gradient alignment between ∇ log P(y) and ∇ log P(y|x), making marginal optimization a viable surrogate that better preserves exploration.
- Analyze sample roles: PSR (reinforcing self-generated correct samples) degrades in pre-train space due to probability-mass accumulation; NSR (leveraging negative samples) is highly instructive, rapidly pruning incorrect trajectories and eliciting endogenous reasoning behaviors.
- DSRL uses a Policy Reincarnation style schedule: first run NSR-PreRL to expand the reasoning horizon / prune wrong modes, then switch to standard RL in P(y|x) for direct policy optimization.
