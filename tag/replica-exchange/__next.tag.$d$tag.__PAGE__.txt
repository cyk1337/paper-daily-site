9:T445,The authors formulate variable-length generation in a padded sequence space with an absorbing empty token, so autoregressive decoding can be viewed as a time-dependent transport from the empty sequence to complete samples. They then define a family of target distributions derived from the base LLM, including powered distributions for sharpening, product distributions for combining constraints, and tilted distributions using additional potentials. Two sampling algorithms are proposed. The Sequential Monte Carlo variant maintains a population of particles along an annealing path and uses importance weights plus resampling to avoid the degeneracy of one-shot importance sampling. The Replica Exchange variant runs chains at different temperatures or sharpness levels and swaps states across replicas, allowing high-temperature chains to explore while low-temperature chains focus on high-probability regions.
