9:T4a4,RACE has two core operations. Referential Abstraction removes identity-revealing text and replaces it with image-grounded references. These can be specific visual references, such as position, appearance, occlusion, or spatial relation, or ambiguous relational references that require searching over multiple depicted candidates and selecting the one whose retrieved evidence satisfies a condition. The construction searches all candidates, requires a unique match, rules out alternatives, and uses a question-only probe to detect identity or answer leakage. Compositional Evidence then builds questions whose answer depends on every selected target’s evidence. The targets can come from one image or from several images, and the construction requires that removing any designated contribution leaves the answer underdetermined. The data starts from real images, including social media photos, Bilibili video frames, and MovieNet imagery. For unannotated images, filtering, visual analysis, and retrieval establish candidate identities; MovieNet provides identity-image provenance.
