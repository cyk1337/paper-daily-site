5:T479,The proposed method is NOVA-KV (Non-Orthogonal Vector-quantized Attention for KV cache). The authors formulate KV compression as a rate-distortion problem where distortion is error in attention products, split into a key-side term from q·k inner products and a value-side term from attention-weighted values. Under a high-resolution assumption they derive closed-form optimal transforms: for values the optimal transform is orthogonal and recovers the covariance-based transform used heuristically in prior work such as Oscar; for keys the optimal transform is non-orthogonal and satisfies a generalized Parseval relation, meaning MSE in the transform domain equals key-query inner-product distortion in the original domain. This reduces quantizer design to a standard MSE problem. On top of that, NOVA-KV applies vector quantization to key coefficients and groups entries so that group volumes are approximately equalized; this allows equal-size codebooks to approach the variable-rate distortion bound while preserving a fixed-width layout required by serving engines.
