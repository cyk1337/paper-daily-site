5:T457,NEO-ov removes the external visual encoder, adapter, and post-hoc fusion module. Images are converted into visual tokens by a lightweight embedding module with two convolution layers and GELU: the first convolution extracts patches with stride 16, and the second aggregates local features with stride 2, producing roughly one token per 32×32 image region. The visual tokens are wrapped by <img> and </img>, concatenated with text tokens, and processed by a single backbone. The model uses Native-RoPE with separate temporal and spatial indices: text tokens carry only a temporal index, while image tokens share a temporal index within an image and keep their h/w coordinates. Attention uses a THW-decoupled design, preserving the original LLM head dimension for the temporal branch while adding H and W dimensions for spatial modeling. The T branch handles text order, cross-image relations, and cross-frame dependencies; the H/W branches encode two-dimensional structure. The Pre-Buffer and Post-LLM layers are initialized from NEO and Qwen3, and the whole system is trained as a unified autoregressive
