1a:T888,实验分三组，结论比较一致。

第一组是小型 byte-level WikiText-2 模型，4 层 Transformer，hidden width 32，训练 600 steps。结果显示，只删 backward 路径中的一部分方向，训练会变差，而且删得越多越差；但直接把 forward head 做低秩，伤害明显更大。forward 与 backward-only 的配对差值分别为：rank 16 时 +0.0808，rank 8 时 +0.1030，rank 4 时 +0.0913，15 个配对比较全部同号，95% 置信区间都排除了 0。rank-32 的 backward control 与普通反传完全一致，说明自定义 backward 实现本身没有引入额外偏差。

第二组是更大的 subword 模型，BPE-8192，6 层、width 96，约 2.26M 参数，训练 1200 steps。这里半秩 backward-only 干预带来验证交叉熵 +0.0586，95% CI 为 [+0.0167, +0.1005]，配对效应量 d_z=1.74；而低秩 forward head 带来 +0.1795，95% CI 为 [+0.1547, +0.2042]，d_z=9.00。两者直接比较，forward 比 backward-only 更差 +0.1209，95% CI 为 [+0.0848, +0.1569]，d_z=4.16。去掉用于调学习率的 seed 1 后，forward-minus-backward 的区间仍为正 [+0.0744, +0.1756]。这说明“低秩 forward 训练更差”不能被简单解释成“backward rank 不够”。

第三组实验针对不可见 logit 残差是否已被训练过程利用。作者保留 hidden gradient，只从 LM head 更新中删除这部分贡献。摘要片段没有给出完整数值，但从主结论可知，移除这部分更新是明显有害的。这个结果是全文最关键的因果证据之一：所谓 hidden 看不到的那部分梯度，并没有被训练丢弃，而是在输出层参数更新里发挥作用。

此外，作者还复查了几何测量指标与学习进展的关系，结论是这些指标能描述投影现象，但不能稳定预测哪个模型学得更快。几种额外反馈通路的尝试也没有超过调得较好的标准 backprop。整体上，证据支持一个较窄但重要的结论：删除已经能到达 hidden 的方向会伤害学习；但“词表空间中 hidden 当前不可达的巨大残差若能直接加进模型，就会提升学习”这一更强命题，并未被证明。1b:T73b,The central methodological move is a backward-only intervention. Prior work reduced the rank of the LM head in the forward pass and observed worse training. But that intervention simultaneously changes: the space of representable logits, the decoder parameterization and conditioning, the number and arrangement of trainable parameters, and the rank of the gradient returned to the hidden state. It therefore cannot identify whether the damage comes from the backward channel.

Here, the model still computes ordinary full-rank logits, and the LM head still receives its ordinary parameter update. Only the gradient sent back to the hidden state is replaced by a lower-rank version derived from the best rank-r approximation of the current head. The hidden gradient is RMS-matched to the exact gradient so that any effect is not confounded by gradient scale.

A second key piece is the explicit decomposition of the logit gradient into the component visible to the current hidden state and the orthogonal residual g_perp. The latter does not affect the current hidden state because it lies outside the column space of W, but it generally does contribute to the gradient of W itself. That means it changes the LM head, which in turn changes future predictions and future feedback subspaces. So “invisible to the current hidden state” is not the same as “discarded by training.”

The paper then runs a complementary ablation: keep the exact hidden-state gradient, but remove from the LM-head update the contribution coming from the currently invisible logit residual, while matching update magnitude. This directly tests whether that residual is functionally used by training even if it does not reach the current hidden state. The paper also critiques the SpamLang setup for conflating repeated loss terms with independent information coverage.1c:T829,There are three main experimental blocks.

First, a compact byte-level WikiText-2 model with 4 Transformer layers, hidden width 32, trained for 600 steps. Removing directions from the backward path hurts learning, and the effect grows as more directions are removed. But reducing the forward head rank hurts substantially more than changing only the backward path. The paired forward-minus-backward-only validation cross-entropy differences are +0.0808 at rank 16, +0.1030 at rank 8, and +0.0913 at rank 4. All 15 paired comparisons have the predicted sign, and the reported 95% intervals exclude zero. The rank-32 backward control exactly reproduces ordinary backprop, validating the custom backward implementation.

Second, a larger subword model with BPE-8192, 6 layers, width 96, about 2.26M parameters, trained for 1,200 steps. The same qualitative pattern holds. A half-rank backward-only intervention increases validation cross-entropy by +0.0586, with 95% CI [+0.0167, +0.1005] and paired effect size d_z = 1.74. A reduced-rank forward head causes a much larger increase of +0.1795, with 95% CI [+0.1547, +0.2042] and d_z = 9.00. The direct forward-versus-backward-only gap is +0.1209, with 95% CI [+0.0848, +0.1569] and d_z = 4.16. Excluding the learning-rate screening seed preserves the ordering, and the forward-minus-backward interval remains strictly positive.

Third, the paper tests whether the logit-space residual that does not reach the current hidden state is already being used through the LM-head update. The exact hidden gradient is preserved, but that residual contribution is removed from the head update. The paper reports that this removal is strongly harmful. Even without all numeric details in the provided excerpt, this is one of the most important causal results: the supposedly “lost” component is not lost to the training process.

The paper also finds that geometric measurements of the projection do not reliably predict learning progress, and several attempts to add separate feedback routes fail to outperform ordinary, well-tuned backpropagation.1d:T57f,Start with the section defining what “lost” means. The paper’s main argument depends on a precise distinction between a component being invisible to the current hidden state and being irrelevant to training as a whole. Then read the backward-only control carefully. The important design choices are: keep full-rank logits, keep the ordinary LM-head update, and match hidden-gradient RMS. That is the cleanest part of the causal identification.

Next, focus on the paired-seed results for the larger subword model. The absolute scale is small, but the comparison is well controlled and statistically reported with confidence intervals and paired effect sizes. Finally, read the ablation that removes the invisible component only from the LM-head update. That experiment is the strongest evidence against the claim that the orthogonal residual is simply discarded.
