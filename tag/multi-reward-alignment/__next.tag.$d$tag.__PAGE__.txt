a:T43b,The proposed method is Pareto Frontier-Guided Optimal Transport, or PG-OT. Instead of collapsing multiple rewards into a single weighted scalar and maximizing it against one global target, the method builds a prompt-specific Pareto frontier. Generated samples for the same prompt form the source distribution, while the corresponding Pareto frontier is used as the target distribution. Optimal transport then maps the source samples toward that frontier. This design respects prompt-level differences in attainable reward boundaries. The method also separates reward models by reliability. For stronger reward models, it uses an online strategy that dynamically collects and updates Pareto frontiers during training. For weaker reward models that are more prone to collapse, it uses an offline strategy that precomputes samples and extracts frontiers before optimization. The paper also introduces Joint Domination Rate and Joint Collapse Rate.
