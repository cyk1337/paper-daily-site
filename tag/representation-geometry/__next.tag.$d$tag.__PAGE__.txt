Third, they provide geometric evidence. Using fine-grained and coarse-grained probes, angular coverage analysis, and manifold visualization, they show that full alignment produces more diverse and more widely spread token features, consistent with aggressive fusion. To measure what geometry the model learns, the authors use Representational Similarity Analysis (RSA). For each toy language, they define a target similarity structure induced by latent semantic categories, expressed as a Gram matrix. They then compare the similarity matrix of learned context embeddings against both this semantic target and the ETF geometry predicted by NC. This lets them track, throughout training, whether representations are organized by latent shared factors or by label-only collapse.

They train standard decoder-only Transformers while sweeping depth and width, and they analyze trajectories rather than only endpoints. The central empirical finding is that gradient descent first discovers a semantically structured geometry organized by shared latent factors, despite purely one-hot supervision. With sufficient capacity and enough optimization time, however, this semantic geometry is eventually replaced by the symmetric ETF geometry predicted by NC. In other words, semantic structure is not evidence against NC; it is a transient phase before the NC-like terminal state.

The paper also introduces a simplified mathematical model that reproduces this “semantic alignment first, collapse later” behavior. Its value is not that it fully models real language training, but that it provides a minimal analyzable mechanism: early optimization can exploit directional signals induced by shared latent factors across examples, while prolonged optimization under one-hot labels pushes representations toward a more symmetric label-driven endpoint.

Relative to prior work, the novelty is not another proof of NC and not another argument that soft label overlap induces semantics. The distinctive contribution is to study the regime where explicit label bridges are absent and still show how semantic geometry can emerge transiently.
