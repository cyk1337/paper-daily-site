18:T55c,A three-stage pipeline that separates “verifiability” from “causal executability”.
1) Answer-aware Tutor, backward chaining: given the ground-truth answer, the Tutor constructs a backward chain of shell commands whose executions retrieve supporting evidence step by step. Backward construction makes multi-hop questions easier because each hop can be anchored to executable string/entity matches and hard-verified by command outputs.
2) Answer-blind Planner, forward causalization: at inference time the agent cannot see the answer; it must act based only on observable history (previous commands and returned snippets). The Planner generates forward reasoning traces and commands from the observable state, and the Tutor aligns them to the verified backward chain, producing trajectories that are both causally grounded and guided toward a known-good search path.
3) GRPO refinement: starting from the cold-start policy, the agent is further improved via Group Relative Policy Optimization through direct interaction with the corpus. The paper’s motivation is that naïve RL tends to collapse into degenerate behaviors, so strong initialization is required before online optimization.
