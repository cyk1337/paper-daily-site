Each case is independently reproduced, sanitized for leakage, and reviewed by distributed-systems researchers, so the emphasis is on fidelity and evaluability rather than raw scale. The evaluation runs each case under two conditions: symptom-only and context-augmented. In symptom-only, the agent receives the bug symptom and codebase, similar to standard SWE-style repair.
