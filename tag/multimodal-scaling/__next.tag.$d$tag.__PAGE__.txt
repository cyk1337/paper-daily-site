9:T445,A controlled experimental program that disentangles *format* from *content* under fixed compute/data budgets.
- Fixed model/training: ~3B dense MLLM (0.4B ViT encoder + 2.5B decoder-only Transformer; 32 layers, hidden 2560, GQA, RMSNorm, RoPE, SwiGLU, context 4096). Training uses Adam and a fixed budget of 4.33B samples (~100B tokens), keeping architecture/optimizer/schedule constant.
- Data notation: Caption0/VQA0 are original caption/VQA; Caption1 are captions generated for the same VQA image set; VQA1 are synthetic VQA pairs reconstructed from Caption1.
- Key tests:
  1) Replace VQA0 with Caption1 (same images) to remove task-form supervision while keeping semantic coverage comparable.
  2) Reconstruct VQA1 from captions to test whether VQA supervision is largely a reformatting of caption knowledge.
  3) Increase knowledge density via structured caption enrichment and cross-modal knowledge injection, then measure whether gains become more consistent with scaling.
