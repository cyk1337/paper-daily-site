c:T401,The method is a Sign-Aware Gated SAE built around a Bi-Jump-ReLU activation. It keeps the useful separation in Gated SAEs between support selection and magnitude estimation, but makes the support two-sided. A polarity-sensitive gate decides whether a latent is active on the positive or negative side. A signed-magnitude path estimates the coefficient without the usual L1 shrinkage effect. A Gated-style auxiliary reconstruction term prevents the gate from collapsing. As a result, one decoder direction can represent both poles of an anticorrelated feature pair. The paper also gives a simple parameter accounting argument: if a fraction p of features appear as anticorrelated pairs, the required width can be reduced to H(1-p/2), and the signed construction becomes parameter-efficient when p is roughly above 8/(d_in+4).
