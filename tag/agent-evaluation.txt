19:T688,The central idea is a harness-guided benchmark construction framework. Instead of starting from a fixed task set and asking models to optimize harnesses on it, the authors first induce a diverse set of candidate harnesses on auxiliary tasks, then use those harnesses to identify tasks that are genuinely responsive to harness quality. This design choice is important: if tasks are not harness-sensitive, the benchmark will mostly measure base-model strength.

The framework has three main components. First, harness-sensitive task construction: candidate tasks are retained only if performance varies meaningfully across different harnesses under a fixed policy model. Second, sensitivity-aware stratified splitting: tasks are split into validation and evaluation sets not by naive random partitioning, but by preserving distributions over sensitivity and related task characteristics, while keeping the suites disjoint. This is meant to reduce task-specific overfitting to the validation set. Third, controlled long-horizon evolution: at each iteration, the evolver sees cumulative validation-side evidence from previous harnesses, including aggregate scores and task-level outcomes, then edits the executable harness code and reruns the fixed policy model. This keeps the benchmark focused on iterative scaffold improvement under a fixed underlying model.

Methodologically, the paper is less about a new evolver algorithm and more about controlling the coupling among evolver, policy model, and task suite. A notable framing is that evolved harnesses are treated as transferable reasoning structures, closer to reusable external programs than to model-specific prompts.1a:T6e9,The main evaluation covers three domains—Search, Office, and General Agent tasks—built from five established benchmarks, with 9 frontier and open-weight models used as evolvers. Under a fixed policy model, the strongest evolvers are GPT-5.6 Sol and Claude Opus 4.8, improving over the seed harness by 16.6 and 16.1 points respectively, approaching a human-engineered baseline of 47.5. The result shows that current top models can make substantial scaffold improvements, but still leave a noticeable gap to manual engineering.

The analysis is more informative than the headline numbers. First, evolution shows clear early saturation: models discover useful structures quickly in early rounds, then often introduce harmful modifications later. That suggests current systems are better at finding obvious fixes than at maintaining and extending a working scaffold without regressions. Second, gains are strongly domain-dependent. Search benefits the most, likely because web-navigation structure can be replicated and stabilized. General tasks sometimes allow evolved harnesses to surpass manual engineering. Office tasks remain difficult because they require highly specific processing workflows that are hard to infer from limited validation feedback. Third, transfer experiments show that synthesized harnesses improve multiple policy models, including Qwen, DeepSeek, and GLM families. This supports the claim that the evolved artifacts are not merely prompt hacks tied to one base model, but more general execution structures.

Even under temperature-0 “deterministic” settings, the serving stack itself can invalidate same-model replay: FP8-served 4B controls diverge on 90–96% of forks, while AWQ-served 14B controls stay nearly identical.1d:T83e,方法核心是把 AIVAT 和 confidence sequences 结合成 AV-AIVAT。AIVAT 原本用于不完全信息博弈中的方差缩减，它利用 value function 对某些决策节点构造条件均值为零的校正项，从而在不引入偏差的前提下降低 payoff 方差。问题在于，经典 AIVAT 默认 value function 是冻结的，而实际评测中人们往往希望边评边学，让 value model 越来越准。论文提出的关键接口是 predictable / pre-action conditional-kernel interface：在每个 correction node，评测器必须在动作发生前就知道该动作在当下信息条件下的 conditional kernel，而且是否启用该 correction 也必须在动作前就固定。与此同时，时刻 t 的 value function 只能使用 1 到 t-1 局的数据，不能用当前局。这样可以保证当前样本不会“选择自己的校正项”，从而保住 conditional mean-zero 的性质。

在这个接口之上，论文给了两类 confidence sequence。第一类是 EB-CS，强调 exact finite-sample validity，但要求事先给出一个与数据无关、几乎处处成立的 corrected payoff 上界 B。作者在 Leduc 上推导并穷举验证了这样的 bound。第二类是 AsympCS，放宽为渐近有效，需要满足极限方差条件，实际中更高效。论文还给出一个理论结果：在 variance-adaptive width 和 sublinear variance-regret 条件下，past-only online learner 的停止效率在渐近上不会比 deterministic oracle-width benchmark 慢。直观上，这说明在线学 value model 只要不偷看当前局，就可以逐步逼近“知道最好 value function”的评测效率。

另一个很有价值的分析是 width floor。作者指出，方差缩减并不总会等比例转化为更早停止，因为在 bet-capped EB-CS 下，声明的 payoff bound 会引入一个确定性的宽度下界 4B log(2/α)/t。一旦这个 floor 开始主导，继续降低方差也不会明显缩短停止时间。论文把这件事整理成三阶段的 order-level design guide，这比单纯报告“方差降了多少倍”更有解释力。1e:T866,The method combines AIVAT with confidence sequences to form AV-AIVAT. AIVAT is a variance-reduction estimator for imperfect-information games. It uses a value function to construct conditional mean-zero corrections at selected decision points, reducing payoff variance without introducing bias. Classical AIVAT assumes a frozen value function, but in practice one would like the value model to improve during evaluation. The key contribution is a predictable, pre-action conditional-kernel interface. At each correction node, the evaluator must know the action’s conditional kernel given the information available immediately before the action, and the decision to enable the correction must also be fixed before the action is observed. In addition, the value function at time t may use only games 1 through t-1, never game t itself. These conditions prevent the current sample from choosing its own correction and preserve the conditional mean-zero property.

On top of this interface, the paper develops two confidence-sequence options. EB-CS provides exact finite-sample validity, but it requires a data-independent almost-sure bound on the corrected payoff. The authors derive and exhaustively verify such a bound in Leduc. AsympCS instead relies on asymptotic variance conditions and is more efficient in practice. The theory also includes a no-asymptotic-delay style result: under variance-adaptive widths and sublinear variance regret, a past-only online value learner asymptotically matches a deterministic oracle-width benchmark. Intuitively, online value learning can approach oracle stopping efficiency as long as it does not peek at the current game.

A particularly useful part of the paper is the width-floor analysis. Variance reduction does not automatically translate into proportionally earlier stopping. In the bet-capped EB-CS setting, the declared payoff bound induces a deterministic lower bound on interval width, 4B log(2/alpha) / t. Once this floor dominates, further variance reduction has little effect on stopping time. The resulting three-regime design guide is more informative than reporting variance reduction alone.1f:T688,The main empirical study uses a heads-up no-limit hold’em corpus with 71,439 paired hands across 15 LLM-agent configurations. AIVAT reduces variance by a median factor of 54 relative to raw per-hand payoffs. The more important metric is stopping time. At alpha = 0.05 and a target half-width of one big blind, paired replay shows a median raw-to-AIVAT stopping-time ratio of 74 under AsympCS. In practical terms, the same evaluation precision can be reached with roughly one seventy-fourth as many hands. Under the descriptive EB-CS execution, the ratio is only 1.37. The paper explains this large gap through the width floor: once the deterministic bound-driven floor dominates, lower variance no longer buys much earlier stopping.

For online learning, the authors test past-only value updates under two controlled profile-mismatch settings. The online learner recovers 77% to 79% of the frozen-to-oracle variance gap, which supports the claim that online improvement is possible without sacrificing validity.

The rolling-leaderboard simulation is also important. Under the null, continuously monitoring an ordinary fixed-sample interval and stopping at the first favorable look yields a 61% false-positive rate. Recomputing a confidence sequence at that same stopping time overturns essentially all such claims. When AsympCS continues under its own stopping rule, it detects injected effects of 0.1 and 0.2 big blinds in 81% and 100% of entries by 5,000 hands. The most important design choice is the three streaming scenarios. In Isolated, each benchmark forms its own stream, so experience is accumulated only within domain. In Sequential, benchmarks are concatenated in a fixed order, so experience transfers from one benchmark to the next. In Interleaved, tasks from all benchmarks are shuffled into a single mixed stream. This is not just a data-order variation. It explicitly controls two variables: the scope of experience transfer and the degree of domain mixing.

First, self-evolution is not reliably beneficial, and the streaming scenario matters a lot. Isolated is usually the most reliable because the experience distribution is narrower and less contaminated. Sequential is often worse than Interleaved, which is somewhat counterintuitive. The paper’s interpretation is that fixed-order transfer can inject strong domain-specific bias from earlier benchmarks into later ones, causing negative transfer, whereas interleaving keeps the agent exposed to multiple domains and can work better for retrieval-based experience mechanisms.

Second, gains are gated by model capability. The weakest model can show negative evolution gain, meaning self-evolution makes it worse. A mid-capability model can benefit more than a stronger one, so the effect is not monotonic in base-model strength. A reasonable reading is that the model must be strong enough to extract signal from noisy self-generated experience, but once the base model is already very capable, the marginal value of external evolution mechanisms may shrink.

Third, no single self-evolution method dominates across all models and scenarios. Context-integrated methods tend to work better in Isolated streams, while retrieval-based methods are stronger in Interleaved streams.
