18:T448,The core metric is Token Economy Score, TES(Mr, Mb, T) = [Acc(Mr,T) - Acc(Mb,T)] / [GenTok(Mr,T) / GenTok(Mb,T)]. Mr is the reasoning-enabled model, Mb is the non-reasoning baseline, and GenTok is reasoning tokens plus final answer tokens. Input tokens are deliberately excluded because they are mostly determined by the benchmark prompt and evaluation harness rather than by the model’s reasoning behavior. TES is therefore a marginal metric, unlike absolute accuracy-per-token metrics such as Per-Token Intelligence. TES greater than 1 means the accuracy gain in percentage points exceeds the generated-token multiplier; values between 0 and 1 indicate positive but weak marginal efficiency; values at or below 0 indicate that reasoning is wasteful or damaging. The paper also defines deployment-facing quantities, including Reasoning Cost Share and Deployment Cost Multiplier, to connect token behavior with cloud and on-premises cost profiles.
