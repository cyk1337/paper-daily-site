Tokens are ranked within each sample (to avoid cross-sample length/difficulty confounds): bottom 20% are “anchors” (concentrated contextual support), top 20% are “explorers” (diffuse support).
Two experiment families are used:
1) Hard-masked selective training: only selected response tokens contribute to the token-level DAPO objective, comparing full-token vs random-20% vs anchor-only vs explorer-only.
2) Soft reweighting intervention: all valid response tokens remain in the objective, but are assigned nonnegative weights w_t (advantage-normalized), with entropy-aware weighting serving as a causal validation of the diagnostic.
