For recoverable inputs, the ground-truth trajectory can recover the final answer. Under a quasimetric loss assumption, the reasoning risk decomposes into two terms. Oracle-trajectory risk measures the hypothesis error when the trajectory is guided by the ground truth up to the final step. Trajectory-mismatch risk measures the extra error caused by the hypothesis driving the chain rule along a different trajectory. The oracle term is connected to domain adaptation: the ground-truth CoT trajectory pushes the test distribution into a target distribution, and CoT helps when that distribution is close to the training distribution. The mismatch term captures the cost of self-generated reasoning.
