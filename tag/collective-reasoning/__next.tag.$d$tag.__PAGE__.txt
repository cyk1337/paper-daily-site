b:T455,AgentFugue introduces a shared reasoning hub. Each agent explores the same task independently. After an agent finishes a coherent interaction episode, the hub writes a compact note describing what was established, what was attempted, and what was ruled out. Other agents can later retrieve selected notes that are relevant to their current search state. This is meant to avoid two failure modes: with no communication, the system reduces to independent sampling; with unrestricted communication, raw trajectory noise can dominate the context and collapse exploration diversity. The hub is not a central planner. It does not assign roles or decompose the task. It acts as an external communication layer. The paper studies both homogeneous teams, where all gains must come from trajectory interaction, and heterogeneous teams, where different models or configurations may contribute complementary biases.
