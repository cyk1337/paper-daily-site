9:T4f8,The method rewrites the Gaussian kernel exp(-τ/2||q-k||²) into a query norm term, a key norm term, and an inner-product term. Softmax attention already computes normalized reductions over exp(τ q·k), so the remaining issues are removing the softmax normalization and incorporating the key-dependent Gaussian factor into the values. Two reductions are given. The prescale variant reads the log-sum-exp values produced by attention backends, multiplies the normalized output by exp(lse), and applies the query/key norm corrections. It is algebraically compact, but the exposed logits do not support the needed differentiable path through the public PyTorch API. The reweight variant avoids reading logits by augmenting queries and keys with two extra coordinates and values with an auxiliary channel. A single scaled_dot_product_attention call returns both the desired numerator and a correction term, whose ratio recovers the unnormalized Gaussian sum. This version is fully compatible with PyTorch SDPA and autograd, including flash-backed backward kernels.
