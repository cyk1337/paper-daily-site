First, trajectory reconstruction. The model is treated as a flow-matching or ODE-based generator with a learned velocity field. Given a real video encoded into the pretrained VAE latent space, the authors integrate the velocity field backward from the clean latent toward noise, producing an approximate reverse trajectory. This allows them to rerun the backbone at each time step and collect block-level hidden states and attention patterns. The appendix discusses approximation error and the conditions under which the recovered trajectory is a faithful proxy. Second, linear probing. They train lightweight probes on intermediate transformer states to predict physical plausibility and some quantitative physical variables. The point is not to maximize probe capacity, but to localize where and when physical information becomes linearly accessible. Third, causal intervention. They perform block-level noise interventions and use a probe-surprise style analysis to identify which components carry or amplify the physical signal. This goes beyond standard probing: it asks not only whether the information is readable, but also whether perturbing specific computations changes the signal and downstream generation. Compared with predictive representation learners such as V-JEPA, the model here is not trained with an explicit future-prediction objective. Compared with standard diffusion interpretability work, the analysis is anchored on real videos embedded back into the model’s trajectory rather than on arbitrary generated samples.7:T56d,The experiments are organized around a few core phenomena rather than a broad benchmark sweep. First, on real videos with known physical plausibility labels, the recovered trajectories show that physical plausibility is linearly decodable from intermediate diffusion-transformer states. The signal is not uniform across the network: it tends to peak at certain depths and noise levels, often in intermediate blocks rather than at the input latent or final layer. Second, the paper reports that some quantitative physical variables are also decodable, which suggests the model is not merely storing a coarse plausible-versus-implausible cue. Third, the strength of the signal depends on trajectory fidelity: when the reconstructed path better follows the model’s learned continuous dynamics, probe performance improves, supporting the claim that physical structure emerges from denoising dynamics. Fourth, intervention experiments show that perturbing specific blocks weakens decodability or changes generation behavior, indicating that these layers are involved in forming and transmitting the signal rather than passively correlating with it. 对比方法有四类。第一类是 probe，本质是判别给定候选答案在该层隐藏状态中是否带有真假标签。第二类是 INSIDE EigenScore，需要对同一 prompt 采样多个 completion，再从内部状态谱特征估计不确定性。第三类是 self-consistency，用 exact match plurality 或句向量相似度衡量多次采样的一致性。第四类是注意力熵，只取首层、中层、末层最后一个 answer token 的注意力分布熵做特征。论文最重要的技术判断其实在协议解释：在 paired-label 设定下，probe 看到的是“prompt + 指定候选答案”的条件表征，而采样类方法只看到 prompt 后的自由生成分歧，因此两者并不在利用同一信息。作者把很多采样法表现差的原因，归结为这种结构性失配，而不是简单说采样法无效。

从结果看，作者进一步声称 truthfulness signal 近似线性：MLP probe 相比线性 probe 提升很小，说明非线性边界不是主要收益来源。层位上，Llama 和 Mistral 的最佳层集中在 32 层模型的第 13–18 层，Qwen 集中在 28 层模型的第 19–25 层，都是中后层而非最后层。这一点比单纯 AUROC 更值得看，因为它暗示真假判别可能在语义整合后、logit 投影前最清晰。9:T411,Its value for pretraining researchers is not the hallucination application itself, but what it says about internal representations. First, 7B–8B instruction-tuned models appear to carry a fairly stable truthfulness-related signal in mid-to-late layers, and that signal is close to linearly separable. That suggests factuality-relevant information is not only reflected in final token probabilities; it is already organized in intermediate semantic states. Second, the signal remains readable after NF4 quantization, which hints that some internal semantic geometry is robust to low-bit deployment. The authors study three common open chat models—Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct, Mistral-7B-Instruct-v0.3, and Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct—loaded in 4-bit NF4 on a single 8GB GPU. They use four binary hallucination benchmarks: TruthfulQA, HaluEval-QA, FEVER, and a synthetic dataset. Each example is turned into a paired-label instance with the same prompt and two candidate answers, one truthful and one hallucinated, yielding an almost perfectly balanced classification task.

For representation extraction, they follow the SAPLMA-style protocol: concatenate prompt and candidate answer, run one forward pass, collect the embedding output plus every transformer block output, and apply last-token pooling over the answer span to get one vector per layer. On top of each layer representation, they train a linear probe and a small MLP probe.

They compare four detector families. Probes operate on the conditional representation of a provided answer. INSIDE EigenScore samples multiple completions and derives an uncertainty score from internal-state spectra. Self-consistency measures agreement across sampled generations using exact-match plurality or embedding similarity. Attention entropy uses per-head entropy summaries from early, middle, and late blocks. The most important methodological point is the protocol asymmetry: probes see the supplied candidate answer, while sampling-based methods only see the prompt and then generate freely. So under this paired-label setup, the two families do not consume the same information. The paper’s negative result for sampling methods is therefore framed as a structural mismatch, not simply as algorithmic failure.

A second notable claim is that the truthfulness signal is approximately linear. MLP probes bring little gain over linear probes, suggesting that nonlinear decision boundaries are not doing most of the work. Layerwise peaks are also consistent: blocks 13–18 out of 32 for Llama and Mistral, and blocks 19–25 out of 28 for Qwen. That mid-to-late concentration is more interesting than the headline AUROC because it hints that factuality-related separation becomes clearest after semantic integration but before the final generation-oriented layers dominate.b:T5e7,The experimental scale is modest but tidy: up to 400 items per dataset, evaluated layer by layer for 3 models × 4 datasets. The headline result is that a single-layer linear probe reaches 0.904–1.000 AUROC on held-out splits, while sampling-based detectors stay at or below 0.541 AUROC under the same protocol, essentially near chance. The authors attribute this gap to information mismatch rather than to a universal superiority of probes.

Dataset difficulty follows intuition. TruthfulQA is the hardest because the negative answers are plausible distractors. HaluEval is easier because explicit supporting knowledge is prepended. The synthetic benchmark is the cleanest true/false contrast and therefore yields the highest scores. FEVER sits in between.
