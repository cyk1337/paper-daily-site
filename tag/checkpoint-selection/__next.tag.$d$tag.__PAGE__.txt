9:T402,The paper starts from a binary-reward analysis. For GRPO with group size g and per-rollout success probability p, the expected within-group advantage variance is p(1-p)(g-1)/g. When p is close to 0 or 1, groups are likely to have identical rewards and contribute little group-relative signal. The empirical design isolates SFT depth as the main variable. The authors train ladders of SFT checkpoints with the same architecture, data, hyperparameters, and evaluation protocol, then run the same GRPO recipe from each checkpoint. The main ladder uses Qwen2.5-Coder-3B-Base; a contrastive ladder uses DeepSeek-Coder-6.7B-Base. On Qwen, deeper SFT increases pre-RL pass@1 but reduces the best post-GRPO pass@10, producing a rank inversion. On DeepSeek, the checkpoints remain in a safer regime and the GRPO rankings compress rather than invert. KL regularization and label smoothing are tested as interventions, but they do not rescue the collapsed Qwen checkpoints, indicating that the failure is upstream of these GRPO variants.a:T5b3,The Qwen2.5-Coder-3B-Base study uses five SFT depths and three seeds.
