9:T5b8,The method has two main components. The first is a formalization: red-team auditing is cast as an MDP where the state is the conversation prefix, the action is the auditor’s next user turn, the transition is the target model’s stochastic reply, and the reward is the LLM-judge score for the target behavior. This separates the search algorithm from the scoring heuristic and makes it natural to compare different search strategies over the same implicit conversation tree. The second component is the introduction of value probes as branch-ranking heuristics. The authors collect judge scores on nodes from audit rollouts, back them up with discount factor gamma = 0.9, and use those backed-up targets as supervision for a linear probe over mean-pooled activations from a single layer of the target model. During auditing, this learned value estimate is used inside a beam-search harness to rank prefixes that are likely to lead to high future judge scores. The practical appeal is that the probe is much cheaper than repeated LLM-based evaluation, and the paper further argues that probes can be trained from off-policy data and even from smaller models before auditing the target model itself.
