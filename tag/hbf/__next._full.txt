第二个组件是 buffered cache-aware scheduling。作者观察到，如果调度器只追求尽快塞满设备，很容易把 HBM 挤满，导致后续 decode 增长或跨轮复用 prefix 时不得不频繁驱逐和回载缓存。于是他们引入 admission buffer，也就是主动保留一部分 headroom，不把内存用到极限。这个策略看起来保守，但在多轮工作负载里反而能减少 cache churn、降低 HBF 写入、提高整体完成时间。这里的思路和很多训练系统里的“不要把某个资源打满”很像：局部利用率最大化不等于全局吞吐最优。

评估方法是 request-level simulator，底层 kernel latency model 用 GPU 实测拟合。模拟器支持异步多副本执行、mixed prefill/decode batching、chunked prefill、session 内顺序依赖、多层级 cache residency 跟踪，以及 dense model 和 sparse-MoE model 的不同权重/专家放置。作者关注的指标不是单请求 latency，而是 fixed workload completion time、modeled energy、cache hit、KV writes 和基于 endurance 的寿命估计。这和他们的目标一致：研究高吞吐、可容忍较高单请求延迟的工作负载，比如 synthetic data generation、异步 agent workflow、RL rollouts。

和已有工作相比，文章的区别不在于单点优化某个 kernel，而在于把容量扩展、KV 复用、调度和写寿命放到同一个分析框架里。很多 serving 论文只讨论 HBM 内的 batching 或 prefix cache；这篇文章更关心当设备侧出现一种“比 HBM 慢、但比 host 近得多”的新层级后，系统策略如何随之改变。19:T869,The method has two main components. The first is an HBM–HBF–host hierarchical storage system. The paper treats model weights, active KV state, and reusable prefix caches as distinct data objects with different access patterns, and manages their placement across HBM, HBF, host DRAM, and SSD. Newly generated KV state and reloaded prefixes enter HBM first; colder cache can be demoted to HBF or lower tiers when HBM becomes scarce. The point is not a simple cache policy but a placement strategy that accounts for each tier’s capacity and access cost. The paper also compares two hardware organizations—Sandisk’s shared-site architecture and the H3 architecture—so the storage policy is grounded in realistic hardware assumptions.

The second component is buffered cache-aware scheduling. The authors observe that aggressively filling device memory can be counterproductive in multi-turn workloads: it leaves no room for decode growth and forces frequent eviction of reusable prefixes, which then have to be reloaded or recomputed. Their solution is to reserve headroom through an admission buffer. This looks conservative locally, but improves global throughput by reducing cache churn, HBF writes, and repeated prefill. The idea is familiar from other large-scale systems: maximizing instantaneous utilization of one resource does not necessarily maximize end-to-end throughput.

Evaluation is done with a request-level simulator whose kernel latency models are fitted to GPU measurements. The simulator supports asynchronous execution across replicas, mixed prefill/decode batching, chunked prefill, session ordering constraints, and cache residency tracking across all tiers. It also models both dense and sparse-MoE serving, including weight and expert placement choices. The primary metrics are fixed-workload completion time, modeled energy, cache-hit fraction, KV writes, and endurance-based lifetime estimates. That metric choice matches the target setting: high-throughput workloads such as synthetic-data generation, asynchronous agent workflows, and RL rollouts, where per-request latency can be traded off for overall throughput.1a:T685,The headline results are straightforward. On 1× traces for Llama-3.1-405B and GLM-5.2, hybrid systems that keep weights in HBM reduce completion time by 46.0% to 56.3% and modeled energy by 14.4% to 24.1% relative to HBM-only baselines. This indicates that extra device-side capacity can be converted into better weight reuse and less repeated prefill rather than simply causing more movement overhead.

The scheduling experiment is also informative. Reserving 10% headroom with buffered cache-aware scheduling reduces completion time by 4.7% relative to unbuffered cache-aware scheduling, while extending estimated HBF write lifetime from 4.77 years to 14.82 years under the paper’s endurance model. That is a useful systems result: leaving some memory unused can improve both throughput and device longevity.

The paper also provides trace-driven analysis of where the gains come from in dense versus sparse-MoE serving, and how longer conversations change the trade-offs. From the provided content, the decomposition includes weight reuse, avoided repeated prefill, KV access cost, cross-tier data movement, and parallelism effects. In MoE serving, expert placement matters because not all weights are equally hot. In longer conversations, retained prefixes become more valuable, but KV pressure and write pressure also increase.

One caveat is important: these are simulator-based results with latency and energy models, not full production deployment measurements. The paper is therefore strongest as a characterization of when HBF is useful and what policies make it useful, rather than as a direct claim about guaranteed gains on a specific commercial system.1b:T51c,For a quick read, start with the introduction and methodology. Three assumptions drive the whole paper: the target is high throughput rather than low latency; the workloads are multi-turn and agentic; and HBF is constrained not only by read cost but also by slow writes and finite endurance. Once those are clear, the design choices make sense.
