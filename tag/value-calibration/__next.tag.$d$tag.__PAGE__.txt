a:T40d,The method, HL-Gauss PPO, replaces the scalar MSE value head with a categorical head over a discretized value support. The critic is trained with cross-entropy against smoothed HL-Gauss targets. The actor side is unchanged: the categorical distribution is decoded into a scalar expectation, which is then used in standard GAE and PPO updates. This is not a distributional policy-improvement method; classification is used only as a surrogate objective for learning a scalar critic.

The paper includes controls for one-hot and two-hot targets, a Bernoulli two-bin baseline, support choices, and smoothing. These ablations separate several possible explanations: binary classification, extra output-head capacity, target smoothing, and the value-support parameterization. The main diagnostics are calibration and raw advantage dynamics.
