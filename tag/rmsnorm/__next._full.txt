18:T698,The paper has three main technical components. First, it characterizes the native discrete gauge of transformer residual streams under coordinate-preserving parameterizations. For LayerNorm, the residual chart has permutation symmetry up to a global sign. For RMSNorm with generic per-channel gain, the correct group is the hyperoctahedral group B_d = S_d ⋉ {±1}^d, meaning arbitrary coordinate permutations plus independent sign flips. The paper also clarifies an important distinction: broader orthogonal freedom can be recovered only after absorbing the RMSNorm gain into adjacent weights, but that changes the coordinate system and no longer preserves sparse coordinate identity. Second, it proposes sign-marginalized activation matching. Instead of matching coordinates by signed correlation, it matches by absolute correlation |<h_i, h'_j>| and recovers the sign afterward from the matched pair. This is the right objective because the true counterpart of a coordinate may be maximally anti-correlated under a negative gauge sign. The authors prove a sharp population-level failure mode for signed-correlation matching in a decorrelated setting: if half of the true signs are negative, the correct entries become the most negative ones in their rows, so maximizing signed correlation systematically avoids them even with unlimited probes. Third, it introduces transport via a checkpoint atlas. Rather than matching only two endpoints, the method composes local B_d gauges saved along a training trajectory.
