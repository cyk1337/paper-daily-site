Workloads are represented as weighted buckets over (input tokens, cached-prefix tokens, output tokens), capturing real traffic mixtures rather than mean lengths. Service time is modeled with separate prefill and decode latency surfaces whose coefficients are calibrated per engine/accelerator; the design reflects prefill’s compute scaling and decode’s memory/KV scaling, with concurrency implicitly constrained by KV memory footprint.
