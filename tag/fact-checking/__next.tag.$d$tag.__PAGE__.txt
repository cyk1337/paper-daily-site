Occupation and education are sampled jointly to keep profiles plausible. The primary condition uses natural biography lines rather than explicit profile cards. The metric reads next-token probabilities after “Answer:” and renormalizes over several single-token spellings of True and False. The job gap is measured in probit space, either through claim-fixed-effect regression for randomized profiles or paired differences for minimal pairs. For OLMo-2 32B, the paper examines base, SFT, DPO, and final checkpoints. It builds a difference-of-means status direction in the residual stream and tests it with activation steering. The key mechanistic result is that post-training does not simply make occupation more salient; it changes the sign with which the model uses that information.a:T4d9,The dataset is built in three stages. The authors sample 85 questions out of 385, generate two answers per question using two randomly chosen models from a pool of eleven LLMs, and decompose the 170 responses into independently verifiable atomic claims, yielding 1,913 claims. Second, evidence collection is controlled through a predefined domain allowlist of accountable web sources, while two fact-checking domains are excluded to reduce verdict leakage. All annotators inspect the same pre-collected candidate pages and record both the URL and the supporting passage. The key idea is not just better annotation quality. It is to turn time sensitivity and answer-centrality into explicit evaluation dimensions. Third, answers to 46% of the questions can change over time, and introducing a reference time shifts the scores of some models more than others, showing that temporal conditioning is itself an axis of model behavior.
