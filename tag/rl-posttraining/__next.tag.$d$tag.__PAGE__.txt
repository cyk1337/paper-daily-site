9:T57d,StepAudio 2.5 uses an asymmetric audio-encoder–adaptor–LLM-decoder architecture. The audio encoder turns waveform-derived features into compact acoustic embeddings. A lightweight adaptor maps those embeddings into the hidden space of a decoder initialized from a text LLM. The decoder operates over a unified sequence space containing both text tokens and audio tokens, and carries most of the semantic reasoning, context tracking, instruction following, and generation burden. The report places post-training at the center. For ASR, it combines capability-specific SFT with a verifiable multi-token decoding head, exploiting the relatively deterministic structure of transcription to emit multiple tokens per decoding step. For TTS, it uses semantic-to-audio alignment, context-rich supervision, and RLHF-style preference optimization to improve controllability and expressiveness. For realtime speech interaction, it applies progressive SFT for persona and paralinguistic behavior, followed by RLHF driven by a generative reward model and explicit interaction rubrics. Compared with cascaded ASR-LM-TTS pipelines, the unified design avoids losing prosody, emotion, pauses, and other speech information at a text-only bottleneck.
