9:T48a,LRMoE uses a two-phase design. During the forward pass, it preserves all routed computations. After gating reveals the actual routed load, a controller plans retention quotas and expert placement under memory and communication constraints. During backpropagation, once incoming gradients arrive at the MoE layer, the system computes a Load Recalibration Metric for each token-expert pair. For a protected expert projection, the pairwise weight-gradient contribution is the outer product of the weighted incoming gradient and the saved projection input. LRM uses the squared norm of this contribution as a ranking score, without materializing a full per-pair gradient matrix. Within each expert quota, LRMoE keeps the highest-scoring pairs. A capacity-aware routing algorithm then assigns retained pairs to eligible expert instances. Physical compaction groups gradients and saved activations by destination and expert into dense payloads, reducing backward all-to-all traffic.
