9:T79f,The method starts with a single-level discrete wavelet transform. An image is decomposed into one low-frequency approximation band and three high-frequency detail bands. The implementation uses the Haar wavelet throughout, mainly because it is orthogonal, invertible, simple, and effectively acts like a lossless 2×2 average-pooling-plus-residual decomposition. Unlike a learned latent space, this transform introduces no reconstruction loss and requires no extra encoder training.

The core change is not architectural. The model keeps the JiT pixel-space transformer and modifies the noise schedule. The LF band is trained over the full time interval t in [0,1], while the HF bands are only active over [t*,1]. During inference, the model first denoises the coarse band alone. At time t*, HF noise is injected, the bands are recombined through the inverse wavelet transform, and the model continues with joint refinement. This preserves cross-band interaction in the later stage, but avoids spending early trajectory steps on HF content that is effectively pure noise.

Conceptually, this is a frequency-aware flow-matching schedule. Compared with standard pixel-space FM, it explicitly respects the natural frequency hierarchy of images. Compared with latent diffusion, it obtains a coarse-to-fine decomposition without a lossy VAE bottleneck. Compared with progressive multi-scale architectures, it is much simpler and cheaper to integrate into an existing transformer pipeline.

A second contribution is the evaluation protocol. The authors argue that standard FID is too forgiving at high resolution because it downsamples to 299×299 and discards much of the fine detail. They therefore add 5-crop FID for native-resolution local structure and high-frequency Fréchet Wavelet Distance for texture fidelity. 第一部分是 FP-MGM，也就是 fixed-point masked generative model。作者把原始 masked denoiser 中间的一部分 Transformer 层替换成一个共享参数的 fixed-point block。这个 block 会被重复应用若干次，直到达到某种平衡态，平衡态表示作为该层输出。这样做有两个直接效果：一是参数量下降，因为多层显式堆叠变成了同一模块反复使用；二是有效深度不再固定，可以通过 solver iteration 数控制。作者把这个思路分别实例化到文本 MDLM 和图像 MaskGIT 上，得到 FP-MDLM 和 FP-MaskGIT。

第二部分是 cross-step consistency loss，记作 L_CONS。作者认为仅仅把层改成 fixed-point 还不够，因为 masked generation 的相邻去噪步之间，输入 token 会发生离散跳变：有些位置从 mask 变成可见 token，有些位置保持 mask，有些位置保持已知 token。这会让表示空间也出现不连续变化，尤其在低预算采样时更明显。为此，论文引入一个跨步一致性损失，让较噪的 student state 在表示上对齐较干净的 teacher state。文中把它解释为一种 cross-time self-distillation。它的作用不是直接改变 sampler，而是让 denoiser 学到更平滑的时间演化，从而在较少 refinement step 或较少 block forward pass 时仍能给出更稳的 masked-token 预测。

第三部分是 three-state reuse（3SR）。固定点模型常见的提速方法是 warm-start：用上一步的 fixed-point 解作为下一步求解初值。但在 MGM 里，上一时刻的解并不是对所有 token 都同样可信，因为 token 状态变了。作者把位置分成三类：未变化且可见的 token，仍然被 mask 的 token，以及新揭示出来的 token。3SR 对这三类位置采用不同的复用强度：对未变化可见 token 几乎完全复用上一步解；对仍 mask 的位置部分复用；对新揭示 token 更依赖当前输入注入。这个设计很贴合 masked generation 的离散状态机，而不是把 DEQ 的 warm-start 机械照搬过来。

第四部分是 checkpoint conversion。作者还展示了一个实用路径：已有的 MDLM checkpoint 不必从头重训，可以通过一个较短的蒸馏阶段转成 FP-MDLM。这一点对研究和工程都重要，因为它降低了验证 fixed-point 架构的试验成本。

和已有方法相比，这篇的区别在于：它不是单纯改 reveal schedule，也不是做 token skipping 或 mask-aware sparse compute，而是把“每一步 denoiser 的深度是否应该固定”作为核心问题来处理；同时它明确指出，离散 masked generation 需要额外的跨步表示约束和状态感知复用，否则 fixed-point 结构本身并不能自动带来低预算优势。d:Ta04,The full method is called CoFRe and combines architecture, training loss, and inference-time reuse.

First, the paper introduces FP-MGMs. The middle part of the masked denoiser is replaced with a shared fixed-point block. Instead of stacking many distinct transformer layers, the model repeatedly applies the same block and uses the equilibrium representation as the output. This reduces parameters and allows the effective depth to be controlled by the number of solver iterations. The idea is instantiated for text with MDLM, producing FP-MDLM, and for images with MaskGIT, producing FP-MaskGIT.

Second, the paper adds a cross-step consistency loss, L_CONS. The motivation is that masked generation has abrupt discrete changes between neighboring denoising steps: some positions stay visible, some remain masked, and some become newly revealed. These abrupt token-state changes induce non-smooth hidden-state transitions, which are especially harmful under low sampling budgets. L_CONS aligns the representation of a noisier student state with that of a cleaner teacher state. The paper frames this as a form of cross-time self-distillation. Its role is to make the denoiser’s temporal evolution smoother and to sharpen masked-token predictions when only limited compute is available.

Third, the paper proposes three-state reuse (3SR) for warm-starting the fixed-point solver. Standard fixed-point models often initialize the next solve from the previous equilibrium. In masked generation, that previous solution is not equally trustworthy for all token positions because the token states have changed. 3SR therefore treats three token categories differently: unchanged visible tokens, still-masked tokens, and newly revealed tokens. Unchanged visible tokens reuse the previous solution strongly, still-masked tokens reuse it partially, and newly revealed tokens rely more on the current input injection. This is a masked-generation-specific adaptation of warm-starting rather than a direct import from DEQ models.

Finally, the paper shows that pretrained MDLM checkpoints can be converted into FP-MDLMs through a short distillation stage, avoiding full retraining. That makes the approach more practical for research iteration.

Relative to prior work, the key distinction is that the paper does not mainly optimize the reveal schedule or skip masked-token computation.
