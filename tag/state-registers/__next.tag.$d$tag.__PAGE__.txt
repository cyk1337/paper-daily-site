Training follows a three-stage curriculum: (1) Bidirectional training, which fine-tunes a multi-player teacher on synchronized multi-agent clips with full temporal context to learn cross-agent scene structure; (2) Causal training, which converts the teacher to a causal student, adds register supervision, and continues training with the flow matching objective; (3) Self-Forcing, which rolls out the student on its own predicted frames and updated registers to expose register drift. The architecture uses Mixture-of-Transformers (MoT), assigning separate weights to register tokens and frame tokens while maintaining joint attention, to mitigate competition between the frame generation and state update objectives.
