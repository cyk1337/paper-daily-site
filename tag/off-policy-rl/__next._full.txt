第一，on-policy 时，这个 population loss 恰好等于 KL-regularized optimality gap，也就是离正则化最优策略还有多远；这给了目标函数很干净的解释。第二，off-policy 时，不再追求无偏估计这个 population quantity，而是直接证明：在 coverage 条件下，有限组上的 raw loss 可以上界 learner 单次采样 reward 与 behavior best-of-K reward 之间的差距，外加若干显式项，包括正则项、reference-behavior mismatch、组大小和有限样本误差。这个思路和传统 off-policy 校正不同，它绕开了“先把 off-policy 目标估准，再优化”的路线，而是直接把训练时可观测的组损失和最终 reward benchmark 连起来。
论文还分析了 periodic reference refresh。固定 reference 时，Gibbs target 是固定的；如果周期性把 reference 刷新到当前 learner，目标会随轮次移动。作者证明：若每一轮都精确拟合对应的 Gibbs target，那么做 T 轮、每轮温度 β 的 refresh，等价于对初始 reference 做一次温度 β/T 的 Gibbs 更新。这个结果解释了为什么 refresh 能逐步把策略往更高 reward 区域推，而不是停留在单个固定 reference 定义的局部目标附近。对不精确更新，论文再给出有限数据下的 reward 保证，误差项由每轮 token 级 mismatch 和内层拟合误差累积控制。二值奖励和局部 reference 控制下，最终策略的单样本 reward 可以逼近初始策略的 best-of-K reward。和 AGRO 的关系也讲得比较清楚：两者共享同一个 regularized optimum，但 GGPO测的是组内 log-mean-exp contrast，AGRO测的是 adjusted reward variance，因此优化几何和有限组保证不同。19:T9f9,The method is built from KL-regularized policy optimization and its Gibbs-form dual solution. Given a reference policy and temperature, there is a Gibbs target under which the KL-adjusted reward is constant on the support of the target distribution. GGPO does not estimate this target policy explicitly, and it does not apply learner-to-behavior importance weights. Instead, it uses groups of responses sampled from the behavior policy for the same prompt, and measures how far the within-group KL-adjusted rewards are from satisfying that constancy condition.
The resulting objective minimizes a log-mean-exp contrast of KL-adjusted rewards inside each response group. This is different from GRPO-style relative advantage updates, and also different from AGRO, which minimizes the variance of the same adjusted reward. The distinction matters: GGPO and AGRO share the same regularized optimum under common support, but they induce different optimization geometry and different finite-group guarantees.
The paper makes two theoretical moves. First, in the on-policy population setting, the GGPO objective is exactly the KL-regularized optimality gap. That gives the loss a clean interpretation: it is not merely a heuristic group contrast, but a direct measure of suboptimality under KL regularization. Second, in the off-policy setting, the analysis does not try to recover an unbiased estimate of that population quantity. Instead, it proves a direct reward comparison: under bounded coverage, the raw finite-group loss controls the gap between the learner’s expected reward from a single sample and the behavior policy’s best-of-K reward, up to explicit terms for regularization, reference-behavior mismatch, group size, and finite-sample error. This is the central conceptual shift of the paper.
A second major component is periodic reference refresh. With a fixed reference, GGPO optimizes toward a single Gibbs target. Refreshing the reference to the current learner changes the target across rounds. The paper shows that if each round exactly fits its Gibbs target, then T refresh rounds at temperature beta are equivalent to one Gibbs update at temperature beta/T. This explains why refresh can compound improvement rather than merely refine a fixed target. For imperfect updates, the paper derives finite-data guarantees in terms of aggregate tokenwise mismatch and inner optimization error. Under binary rewards and local reference control, the final policy’s single-sample reward can approach the best-of-K reward of the initial policy.1a:T5ca,The empirical study is organized around three mismatch regimes: training-inference mismatch, controlled policy staleness, and offline training. Evaluation is on five mathematical reasoning benchmarks and two model sizes. The headline result stated in the paper is that GGPO outperforms both GRPO and AGRO under training-inference mismatch, and remains stable as synchronization delay increases from N=8 to N=128, whereas GRPO and AGRO degrade. At N=128, token-level TIS improves GRPO, but GGPO still performs better without relying on importance weighting.
