9:T9a1,作者先搭了一个统一基线，把不同递归推理模型放到相同数据、相同输入输出接口、相近训练设置下比较。所有任务都被表示成 padded token sequence 输入，模型直接预测完整目标，不提供中间 supervision 或 chain-of-thought。这一点很重要，因为它避免把性能差异混进不同任务接口或中间标签设计里。

数据上，除了沿用 Sudoku、Maze、ARC-AGI，还加入了两个更可控的域。Game of Life 用来控制 sequential rule application 的步数，从而测 extrapolation beyond training horizon；Arithmetic 用逆波兰表达式恢复被 mask 的运算符序列，控制组合搜索难度，并设计了 operand multiset OOD 和 target range OOD。这样做的好处是，作者不只看 in-distribution 解题率，而是专门测“学到的递归计算”能否外推到更长计算链、更陌生输入结构和更远目标范围。

方法上，论文的重点不是提出一个全新架构，而是把递归模型的关键设计轴拆开做受控消融。根据引言，核心轴包括：
1. 梯度传播长度，也就是 credit assignment 穿过多少次递归展开。作者发现不是越长越好，而是存在 interior optimum。太短学不到长期依赖，太长则优化噪声、梯度不稳或 credit diffusion 变严重。
2. batch 形成方式。相同 effective batch 下，大 physical batch 明显优于 gradient accumulation。这说明递归模型对 step-level optimizer noise、归一化统计或硬件执行形态更敏感，不能简单套用常规 Transformer 的“累积等价”直觉。
3. recurrent-state stabilization。论文强调 latent state 更新必须被控制，否则共享 block 反复作用会导致状态漂移、爆炸或进入不稳定吸引子。虽然当前片段没有完整公式，但从表述看，最终 recipe 包含 controlled state updates 和 recurrence-specific regularization，可能类似残差门控、状态插值、更新幅度约束或归一化稳定器。
4. 架构容量与层级。作者比较显式 hierarchy、更大的 recurrent block、以及额外 test-time computation，结论是收益依任务而定，不存在统一占优配置。

最终模型是一个 13.6M 参数的稳定递归推理器，结合共享 recurrent computation、受控状态更新、中等梯度传播范围和针对递归的正则化。它更像一套训练 recipe 加一组结构选择，而不是单点创新模块。a:T96b,The authors build a common training and evaluation pipeline for recursive reasoners. All tasks are cast into the same interface: a padded token sequence as input, and direct prediction of the full target, with no intermediate supervision and no chain-of-thought traces. This is an important control, because it removes confounds from task-specific interfaces or auxiliary labels.

The benchmark suite includes standard domains used by prior work—Sudoku, Maze, and ARC-AGI—and adds two more controlled domains. Game of Life lets the authors vary the number of sequential rule applications and test extrapolation beyond the training horizon. Arithmetic uses reverse-Polish expressions with masked operators, turning the task into a constrained combinatorial search problem with explicit OOD splits over operand multisets and target ranges. These additions make the study more informative than a pure in-distribution benchmark comparison.

The paper’s main technical contribution is not a brand-new architecture, but a controlled decomposition of the design space. The key axes are:
1. Gradient propagation horizon. The paper finds an interior optimum: too short and the model cannot assign credit across enough recurrent steps; too long and optimization becomes unstable or diluted.
2. Batch formation. At matched effective batch size, large physical batches outperform gradient accumulation. This suggests recursive models are unusually sensitive to optimizer noise, normalization behavior, or execution-level details.
3. Recurrent-state stabilization. Repeated application of a shared block can make latent states drift, explode, or enter unstable dynamics. The final recipe therefore includes controlled state updates and recurrence-specific regularization. The excerpt does not expose the full equations, but the mechanism is clearly about constraining how much the latent state changes at each recurrent step.
4. Capacity and hierarchy. The study compares explicit hierarchy, larger recurrent blocks, and extra test-time compute, and finds that their benefits are domain-dependent rather than universal.

The final model is a 13.6M-parameter recursive reasoner that combines shared recurrent computation, controlled latent-state updates, an intermediate gradient horizon, and recurrence-aware regularization. It is best understood as a stable optimization recipe plus a set of validated design choices.b:T78a,The evaluation spans six domains: Sudoku, Maze, Game of Life, Arithmetic, and ARC-AGI settings including ARC-AGI-1 and ARC-AGI-2/ConceptARC-related protocols. The two added domains are particularly useful. Game of Life is built from 200,000 trajectories on random binary grids up to 17×17, with tests for both unseen initial patterns and extrapolation 1, 2, 3, and 10 steps beyond the training horizon. Arithmetic uses reverse-Polish expressions with 3 to 8 operands and four operators, and evaluates OOD generalization by unseen operand multisets and by target values outside the training range.

The headline result is that the unified stable recipe yields a 13.6M model that is strongest among the evaluated recursive baselines on nearly all reported metrics, including 71.16% on Arithmetic OOD, 98.41% on Sudoku, and 59.50% pass@2 on ARC-AGI-1. Even without over-reading the absolute numbers, that is enough to show that optimization and stability choices materially change outcomes.

The ablation findings are the real substance. First, gradient horizon has a middle optimum, so neither aggressive truncation nor very long backpropagation is ideal. Second, large physical batches beat gradient accumulation at the same effective size, which matters for both systems design and reproducibility. Third, recurrent-state stabilization is necessary for reliable training. Fourth, explicit hierarchy, larger recurrent blocks, and extra test-time compute help only on some domains. Some tasks benefit from deeper iterative computation, some from more capacity, and some barely improve with additional recurrence. That is a more careful conclusion than the simple claim that more test-time compute always helps.

From a foundation-model perspective, the most useful outcome is the re-attribution of performance differences to optimization and latent-state dynamics rather than to model naming or superficial architectural packaging.c:T938,模型是一个单 block 的 Universal Transformer，每一步都对完整序列做双向注意力，序列形式为若干 memory tokens 加原始 Sudoku token 序列。块内采用 pre-norm attention + SwiGLU FFN，并加入每步 learned positional embedding、RoPE、QK normalization。作者还用了一个较少见的 DerfNorm 设计，用 erf(alpha*x+s) 取代标准归一化层，目的是减少归一化对递归动态的干扰，但全文的核心结论并不依赖这个点。

真正的技术重点有三个。第一，memory tokens 被当作可学习寄存器，而不是普通前缀 token。作者系统扫了 token 数量，发现 81-cell Sudoku 上存在明显阈值：T=0 必败，T=4 处于临界区，T=8 开始稳定成功，T=8 到 32 基本进入平台区，再增大到 T=64 则因注意力稀释而退化。这说明 memory token 的作用不是“多一点上下文更好”，而是需要足够数量来承载中间状态，但过多又会稀释对问题 token 的注意力。

第二，作者把 ACT 失败归因到 router initialization trap。标准零偏置会让初始 halting probability 约为 0.5，Graves 的正偏置建议会更高到约 0.73，这两种都会让 token 在 1 到 2 步内过早停止。作者反过来采用 deep-start 初始化，把 halting bias 设为 -3，使初始 p 约为 0.05，等价于默认先跑满 K=18 步，再让模型学会在哪些位置提前停。这个设计背后的判断很清楚：对需要深计算的任务，先深后浅比先浅后深更容易优化，因为浅策略几乎不给“深度有用”这件事留下探索空间。

第三，作者做了较细的训练动态诊断。他们记录每步 router probability、step-weight 分布、router gradient norm 和注意力质量，发现能从 shallow-halt 中逃出的 run 往往伴随 router 梯度 10 到 45 倍的尖峰；而失败 run 的 router 梯度长期低于 0.04，说明 halting 子网络几乎被冻结。这个分析把“ACT 不稳定”从经验现象推进到更可操作的诊断框架。

和已有方法相比，这篇文章不是提出新的递归架构，而是在现有 UT+ACT 框架里指出：memory state 与 halting initialization 是两个一阶因素。它也顺带说明，其他能解 Sudoku 的递归模型如 TRM、HRM 依赖的是不同机制，不能据此反推 UT 不需要显式 memory。d:T9ca,The model is a single Universal Transformer block applied recurrently up to K=18 steps. At each step, the sequence is composed of learned memory tokens followed by the Sudoku tokens, and the whole sequence attends bidirectionally. The block uses attention plus SwiGLU, with per-step learned positional embeddings, RoPE, and QK normalization. The paper also uses a normalization-free variant called DerfNorm, but that is not the main source of the reported effect.

There are three technical points that matter. First, memory tokens are treated as persistent learned registers rather than generic prefix tokens. The authors sweep the number of memory tokens and find a sharp threshold: T=0 always fails, T=4 is borderline, T=8 reliably works, T=8 to 32 forms a broad plateau, and T=64 degrades because attention gets diluted. This is a stronger claim than “more tokens help.” It suggests a minimum state budget is needed for recursive computation, while too many state slots hurt token-to-problem interaction.

Second, the paper identifies an ACT router initialization trap. With zero bias, the initial halting probability is around 0.5; with the positive bias recommended in earlier ACT work, it is even higher, around 0.73. Both settings make tokens halt after roughly 1–2 steps. The proposed fix is a deep-start initialization with bias -3, giving p about 0.05. That means the model starts by using nearly the full depth budget and only later learns where to stop early. The rationale is simple and convincing: on tasks that truly need depth, starting shallow prevents the model from ever seeing evidence that deeper computation helps.

Third, the authors instrument the training dynamics in detail. They log per-step router probabilities, step-weight distributions, router gradient norms, and attention mass. Successful runs escaping the shallow-halting regime are associated with 10x to 45x spikes in router gradient norm, while failed runs keep router gradients near zero for the entire training trajectory. This turns ACT instability from a vague empirical complaint into a more diagnosable optimization phenomenon.

Relative to prior work, the paper does not introduce a new recursive architecture. Instead, it isolates two first-order factors inside the existing UT+ACT setup: explicit memory state and halting initialization. It also makes clear that success of other recursive models on Sudoku does not imply that UT can do without memory, because those models rely on different mechanisms.e:T797,The main experiments are on 81-cell Sudoku-Extreme. The model has about 3.2M parameters, hidden size 512, 8 attention heads, vocabulary size 11, and a maximum of K=18 recurrent steps. The study systematically varies memory-token count, ACT versus fixed depth, initialization scheme, and random seed.

The central result is the memory threshold. Across all tested seeds and settings, no-memory configurations never achieve non-trivial performance. T=4 is only marginally viable. T=8 is the first reliably successful setting, and T=8 to 32 stays on a stable plateau, with a representative exact-match result of 57.4% ± 0.7%. At T=64, performance drops, which the authors attribute to attention dilution.

The ACT versus fixed-depth comparison is also informative. With memory tokens, fixed-depth reaches 53.4% ± 9.3% exact match, but with high variance. ACT improves this to 56.9% ± 0.7%, indicating much better reliability. With lambda warmup, the model reaches about 57.0% ± 1.1% while using 34% fewer ponder steps, so ACT provides both compute savings and lower variance in this setting.

For the initialization trap, the authors analyze 15 runs and show that with bias=0, all runs start with p around 0.48–0.52 and halt after about 2 steps. By roughly 3k training steps, they settle into a shallow-halting pattern with average halt around 5–7. Only rare runs escape, and those escapes coincide with large spikes in router gradient norm. Switching to bias=-3 converts previously failing configurations into successful ones and changes the learning curve from abrupt phase transitions to smoother improvement.

They also test whether this is merely a DerfNorm artifact. Under a matched configuration, DerfNorm can escape and reach 50.6% EM, while RMSNorm stays stuck at 0.0% EM with halt around 3.5.
