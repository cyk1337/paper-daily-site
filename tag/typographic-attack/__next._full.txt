18:T53f,The method is a training-free loop: stochastic concept mining → attribution-based ranking → circuit intervention.
1) Stochastic concept mining: under the linear representation hypothesis, hidden states are treated as linear mixtures of concept directions. Instead of learning a dictionary/SAE, the authors sample pseudo-concept vectors. A key design choice is to sample/search in the per-head low-dimensional subspaces induced by MHSA projections (d_head), which empirically increases “concept hit” probability and reduces entanglement.
2) Vulnerability explanation via attribution: they propose a gradient-based attribution score that combines (a) whether an attention head focuses on text patches (focus) and (b) whether the sampled concept direction aligns with lexical semantics (direction). This yields a normalized score to identify TA-vulnerable concepts and the hosting heads/modules.
3) Mechanistic intervention: once harmful circuits are identified, they apply attention reweighting or ablation of selected heads/paths at test time to suppress lexical-dominated routes and recover visual-semantic behavior.
