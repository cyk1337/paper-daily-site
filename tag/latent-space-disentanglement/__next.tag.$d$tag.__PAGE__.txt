b:T416,The proposed method, Adversarial Orthogonal Disentanglement, extracts hidden states from a selected LVLM layer at the final token position for image-question inputs. It learns a projection direction that concentrates hallucination-predictive signals, while an adversarial objective with a Gradient Reversal Layer removes those signals from the orthogonal residual subspace. The result is a learned hallucination direction with a cleaner separation from general visual semantics. At inference time, the base LVLM is not retrained. Instead, the method performs activation-steered contrastive decoding: hidden states are shifted along the learned direction to form hallucination-amplified and factual-steered representations, and the resulting logits are contrasted to penalize hallucination-prone continuations. Compared with attention pruning, it does not rely on potentially faulty attention weights.
