- Image tokenizer: trained from scratch on GPIC images, Cosmos-Tokenizer-style encoder–decoder with FSQ; 8× spatial downsampling; 256×256 becomes a 32×32 grid (1024 tokens); codebook size 64k.
- Text tokenizer: scratch-trained byte-level BPE with vocab 16,384, trained on GPIC captions plus OpenWebText for coverage (tokenizer training, not LM pretraining).
- Joint vocabulary: 16,384 text + 64,000 image + 256 special = 80,640, with shared embedding and output head.
- Sequence formats: Text-only, Image-only, Image-to-Text (I2T), and Text-to-Image (T2I). T2I uses caption prefix conditioning to generate image tokens.
- CFG training: caption dropout on a fraction of T2I examples to learn an unconditional branch; inference combines conditional/unconditional logits with guidance scale. Notably, I2T shares the same image prefix as unconditional T2I, so I2T also trains the unconditional branch.
- Objective: weighted next-token cross-entropy with separate text/image terms and an image-loss weight λ_img, normalized by non-padding tokens.
