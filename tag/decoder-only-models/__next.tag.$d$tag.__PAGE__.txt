9:Ta3d,方法核心是 TRACE：Teacher-forced Registry of Ablated Component Effects。做法不是像 activation patching 那样在 corrupted/clean 两条前向之间恢复激活，也不是训练 SAE 去解释残差特征，而是建立一个可注册的组件库，对每个组件单独做一次消融，然后在固定答案位置上看输出分布如何变化。

从全文描述看，这个框架有几个关键设计。第一，统一注册接口。它允许把不同层级的对象都视为“组件”，包括整个 transformer block、MHSA 子层、FFN 子层、单个神经元，甚至输出 logit 相关通道。这样做的意义是把不同粒度的解释对象放到同一实验协议下，而不是每种组件各用一套方法。

第二，teacher forcing。作者刻意把研究对象限制在“单个命名实体生成”，并在答案 token 位置固定上下文，避免自由生成时后续 token 误差传播污染 attribution。这样测到的是某个组件对目标 token 条件概率的直接支持，而不是对整段生成轨迹的混合影响。代价是它更适合 factual recall 场景，不适合 reasoning trace 很长的模型。

第三，行为数据集设计。作者构造了两个域：医疗药物知识和一般知识。医疗部分进一步限定到 drug 子域，选了 8 个药物实体，每个实体写 6 类线索提示，如历史、化学式、通用名、一线治疗、药物相互作用等，共 48 个 prompt。一般知识部分来自 TREC-8 QA，经改写后保证答案是单一命名实体，并筛选成与医疗域格式一致的问答。这个设计不是为了 benchmark 分数，而是为了区分“跨域都重要的通用生成组件”和“只在某一域显著的专门组件”。

第四，消融效应度量。摘要里明确说是测量 ablation 后固定答案 token 输出分布的变化。虽然截取内容里没有完整公式，但可以确定它不是只看 top-1 是否翻转，而是看目标答案概率或相关分布量的下降幅度，因此能得到连续的贡献分数。相比只做正确/错误二值统计，这种度量更细，也更适合比较不同组件类型。

Instead of activation patching between clean and corrupted runs, and instead of training a sparse autoencoder over residual activations, the framework registers model components at multiple granularities, ablates them one by one, and measures the effect on the output distribution at a fixed answer position.

Several design choices matter. First, the registry abstraction. Different objects can be treated uniformly as components: full transformer blocks, MHSA sublayers, FFN sublayers, individual neurons, and even output-logit-related units. This is the main reason the framework can compare heterogeneous component types under one protocol.

Second, teacher forcing is central. The study is intentionally restricted to prompts whose answer is a single named entity, and the answer position is evaluated with the surrounding context fixed. This avoids contamination from autoregressive error propagation and makes the score closer to direct support for the target token rather than support for an entire generated trajectory. The trade-off is obvious: the setup is well suited to factual recall, not to long reasoning traces.

Third, the behavioral dataset is purpose-built. The authors use two domains: medical knowledge and general knowledge. The medical side is narrowed to drugs, with 8 entities and 6 clue categories per entity, for 48 prompts total. The general-knowledge side is adapted from TREC-8 QA and rewritten so that each answer is a single named entity. This is not benchmark construction for accuracy; it is an experimental design for separating domain-general generation components from domain-specific ones.

Fourth, the effect metric is distributional rather than purely binary. From the abstract and method description, the framework measures how the output distribution at the fixed answer token changes after ablation. Even without the full formula in the provided excerpt, it is clear that the method is not limited to whether top-1 flips. That makes the signal more continuous and more suitable for comparing components across prompts.

Relative to prior work, the positioning is clear. Compared with activation patching, TRACE does not require clean/corrupted paired inputs and focuses on loss of support after removal rather than sufficiency after restoration. Compared with SAE-based analyses, it avoids training an auxiliary model.
