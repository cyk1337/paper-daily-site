第一组是 content control，也就是 band structure。作者把正样本定义为共享 magnitude-band pair 的样本，例如按 ⌊a/10⌋、⌊b/10⌋ 分桶，得到 100 个类，类大小与真实等价类接近。这个先验是连贯的、可学习的，也不是随机噪声；但它依赖的是阈值/幅值类特征，而任务真正可泛化的 Fourier 电路依赖周期性特征。这样就能区分两种解释：如果“只要先验有结构、可学习”就行，band prior 应该像 sibling prior 一样有帮助；如果关键在 feature family 对齐，band prior 就会像随机分区一样阻碍泛化。实验支持后者。

第二组是 supervision control，也就是 commutativity prior。这里完全不用答案标签，只把 (a,b) 和 (b,a) 当作正样本。它只利用加法交换律这个任务家族先验，因此是 label-free invariance prior。这个设计很巧，因为它不是直接告诉模型“哪些样本同类”，而是给出一个稀疏的不变性约束；在 modular addition 里，交换后的样本确实共享同一个和，所以它是对真实等价结构的稀疏采样。作者进一步指出，稀疏性可能反而是优点：它提供方向正确但优化压力更温和的结构信号，减轻前作里观察到的 weight norm inflation 副作用。

第三组是 critical-window 实验。作者只在特定 epoch 区间打开先验损失，比较早期开、晚期开、全程开等设置。结论是先验必须在早期介入，才能改变后续表示形成轨迹；如果模型已经沿着不利路径进入记忆化盆地，后加先验很难把它拉回来。这一点和很多预训练中的“早期数据/目标决定后续表征几何”很接近。

第四组是 generality sweep。作者把分析扩展到 multiplication、subtraction、parity，不同架构变体（2-layer、MLP、LayerNorm 变体），并做 norm clamp sweep，测量前作提出的 delay-law exponent 在有无先验时如何变化。这里的重点不是追求统一提升，而是检验“表征先验 + 权重范数副作用”这个解释是不是稳健。文章还加了一个 sequence-task pilot，作为边界测试，说明这套机制目前更像是对算法任务和小模型训练动力学的精细解释，而不是已经覆盖一般序列建模。18:Ta22,实验最有信息量的结果有几组。

1. feature family 决定先验是否有效。band prior 15 次里只有 1 次出现 grokking，和 random partition 的 0/20 在统计上不可区分（Fisher exact p=0.43），但与 true structure 的 22/30 显著不同（p=2.3×10^-5）。这说明“结构连贯、类别大小匹配、可学习”都不够，先验若落在错误的特征族上，效果接近噪声结构。对照地，前作里的 sibling structure 虽然组合关系不对，但仍共享同一特征族，因此 14/15 能泛化。这个对照是全文最关键的证据。

2. 标签不是必要条件。commutativity prior 在 15/15 个 run 中全部泛化，而 random partition 是 0/20，显著性很强（p=3.1×10^-10）。相对同 seed baseline，中位数加速 2.7×。更有意思的是，它的成功率还高于使用标签定义真实等价类的 true-structure prior（22/30，二者比较 p=0.038）。作者给出的解释是：commutativity prior 更稀疏，每个被覆盖 anchor 只有一个正样本，因而提供了正确方向的几何约束，但不会像密集监督那样过度推高权重范数。

3. norm side-effect 仍然存在，但减弱。文中报告 commutativity arms 的 median peak weight norm 为 103，低于 supervised true-structure prior 的 130，但仍高于 baseline 的约 57。这与前作一致：辅助对比损失一方面加快结构形成，另一方面也会推高权重范数，延缓优化；最终效果取决于两者博弈。仅看最终 held norm 不够，轨迹更重要。

4. 与 norm clamp 结合后效果最好。把 label-free commutativity prior 与前作提出的 norm clamp（||W||=45）结合，5/5 run 全部 grok，且中位数加速达到 17×；有些 seed 在 1000 epoch 以内就完成泛化。这个结果说明，先验提供方向，norm control 去掉副作用，两者是可组合的。

5. critical window。虽然给出的全文片段没有列出全部表格数字，但作者明确报告：只在训练早期施加先验仍能保留大部分收益，而只在后期施加几乎不起作用。这不是简单的“多训练一段辅助 loss 就行”，而是表示几何在早期被锁定，后期更像是在已有表征上做微调。

6. 泛化范围。作者在 multiplication、subtraction、parity，以及 2-layer、MLP、LayerNorm 变体上做了扩展，结论是“feature-family alignment + early window + norm interaction”这套解释有一定稳健性，但不是无条件普适。sequence-task pilot 被明确当作边界探测，说明作者对外推范围是克制的。19:T8f7,The setup follows the companion paper: a one-layer transformer trained on modular addition, with the main task loss plus a supervised contrastive loss applied to the final-position representation. The key contribution is not the auxiliary loss itself, but a carefully designed set of controls that isolate why a prior works.

The first control is the band-structure prior. Positive pairs are defined by sharing the same magnitude-band pair, using bucketed values of a and b. This prior is coherent, learnable, and class-balanced relative to the true task structure. But it is built from threshold/magnitude features, whereas the known generalizing circuit for modular arithmetic relies on periodic/Fourier features. This lets the authors separate two hypotheses: if coherence alone is enough, the band prior should help; if feature-family alignment is necessary, it should behave like a random partition. The experiments support the latter.

The second control is the commutativity prior. Positive pairs are only commuted inputs, (a,b) and (b,a). This uses no answer labels at all. It injects a task-family invariance that is knowable before training and, in modular addition, corresponds to a sparse sample of the true equivalence structure because commuted pairs share the same sum. The design is important because it shows that the useful signal need not be dense label supervision; a weak but correctly aligned invariance can be sufficient.

The third component is the critical-window intervention. The prior is activated only during selected epoch ranges. This tests whether the prior changes optimization globally or whether it must shape representation geometry before the model settles into a memorization-dominated trajectory. The reported result is strongly asymmetric: early intervention works, late intervention mostly does not.

The fourth component is a generality sweep across tasks, architectures, and normalization settings, including multiplication, subtraction, parity, 2-layer variants, MLPs, and LayerNorm variants. The paper also revisits the norm-clamp mechanism from the companion work and measures how the delay-law exponent changes with and without the prior. An exploratory sequence-task pilot is included as a boundary check rather than a claim of broad universality.1a:T9a6,Several results carry most of the paper’s weight.

1. Feature-family alignment, not mere coherence, determines usefulness. The band prior yields grokking in only 1 of 15 runs, statistically indistinguishable from a random partition baseline at 0 of 20 runs (Fisher exact p=0.43), and clearly different from the true-structure prior at 22 of 30 runs (p=2.3e-5). This is the cleanest evidence in the paper: a prior can be structured, learnable, and class-size matched, yet still fail if it is expressed in the wrong feature family.

2. Labels are not required. The commutativity prior generalizes in 15 of 15 runs, versus 0 of 20 for the random partition (p=3.1e-10), with a median 2.7x speedup over same-seed baselines. More interestingly, it is more reliable than the label-supervised true-structure prior itself (22 of 30; comparison p=0.038). The authors argue that sparsity is likely the reason: one positive per covered anchor gives the right geometric direction with gentler optimization pressure.

3. The norm side effect remains but is reduced. Median peak weight norm is reported as 103 for the commutativity prior, compared with 130 for the supervised true-structure prior and about 57 for the baseline. This supports the companion paper’s picture: the auxiliary prior helps representation formation but can also inflate weight norms, which delays or destabilizes generalization. The trajectory matters more than a single final norm value.

4. Combining the label-free prior with norm control gives the strongest result. With the norm clamp at ||W||=45, the commutativity prior achieves 5 of 5 successful grokking runs and a median 17x speedup, with some seeds generalizing in under 1,000 epochs. This is a strong demonstration that the useful structural signal and the harmful optimization side effect are separable and composable.

5. Critical-window behavior is clear. Even though the excerpt does not include every table, the paper states that early-only application preserves most of the benefit, while late-only application is largely ineffective. That points to path dependence in representation formation rather than a simple cumulative regularization effect.

6. Generality is partial, not unlimited. The same qualitative account appears across several arithmetic tasks and architecture variants, but the sequence-task pilot is framed as exploratory. The paper is careful not to overclaim beyond the regime where the mechanism is directly testable.1b:T540,Start with the Introduction. It lays out the four axes—content, supervision, timing, and generality—and makes clear that the paper is testing the necessity and boundary conditions of the companion paper’s causal story.

Then focus on the two central experiments: band prior and commutativity prior. The band prior explains why a structured prior can still be useless if it is built from the wrong features. The commutativity prior explains why labels are not essential if the invariance is aligned with the task. If figures are available, prioritize success-rate tables, time-to-generalization distributions, and weight-norm trajectories.

Next, read the critical-window and norm-clamp sections together. That is where the paper becomes most relevant to pretraining practice: the prior is not just accelerating optimization in a generic sense, it is shaping early representation geometry, while norm control removes a specific side effect.

For a deeper read, it helps a lot to read the companion paper alongside this one. Many design choices—same-seed pairing, censored-run handling, matched class sizes, and weight-norm-matched controls—matter because the claims are mechanistic, not just empirical.
