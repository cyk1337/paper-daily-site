1) Learnable Modulation (LM): introduces learnable factors to modulate the distribution of pretrained full-precision weights and the ternary thresholds, reducing sensitivity to ternarization and improving alignment between FP weights and ternary representations.
2) Softened Ternarization (ST): replaces hard, non-differentiable ternary mapping during optimization with a softened (differentiable) approximation to improve convergence in the ultra-low-bit regime.
