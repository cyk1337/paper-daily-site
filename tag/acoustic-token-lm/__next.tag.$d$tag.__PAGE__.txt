Second, generation is factorized into two stages in the same acoustic-token space. A backbone model generates full-track coarse tokens. A super-resolution model then fills in higher RVQ layers. The alignment objective uses causal attention to learn text/vocal ordering, while the layer-wise refinement objective uses full attention to exploit the whole audio context when reconstructing fine tokens.
