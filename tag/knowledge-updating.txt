18:T52e,ReGrad has an offline and an online phase. Offline, the system computes document-specific gradients and stores them in an indexed Gradient Bank. At inference time, a query retrieves relevant gradients. These gradients are temporarily applied to the model weights, the model generates an answer, and the adapted weights are discarded afterward. This avoids accumulating unrelated document updates into a single global parameter state.

A key point is that the method does not rely on raw next-token language-modeling gradients alone. Such gradients are optimized for reconstructing documents and may overfit surface token patterns. Downstream use requires question answering, factual extraction, and reasoning over document content. ReGrad therefore introduces a bi-level meta-learning objective: the inner step applies a document-induced gradient, while the outer objective evaluates the adapted model on downstream supervision. This trains the gradient construction and application process so that temporary adaptation improves task behavior rather than only document likelihood.

The method can be used without retrieved text, as a parameter-only adaptation mechanism. It can also be combined with in-context RAG, where retrieved documents provide textual evidence and retrieved gradients provide parameter-space adaptation.19:T439,The work first separates factual GV-gaps from computational and aesthetic variants. The main controlled setup uses synthetic facts in domains related to public discourse. For each fact, the authors construct both generative queries, where the model must produce the answer, and verification queries, where it must judge whether a proposed statement is true. They fine-tune four open-source model families at two scales each, giving eight model settings, and trace both capabilities through three phases. In acquisition, the model learns new facts. In continual learning, additional training measures whether the two capabilities decay differently. In updating, an old fact is replaced by a new one, allowing the authors to test whether the model actually switches beliefs or accepts both versions.
