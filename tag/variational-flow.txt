1a:T53d,SVFlow defines an ODE whose vector field is a variational-posterior-weighted average of conditional score functions. With a discrete latent z and likelihood p(x|z;θ), plus variational posterior q(z|x;ϕ), the flow direction at x is E_{q(z|x)}[∇_x log p(x|z)]. Intuitively, it is likelihood-ascent under uncertain discrete selection.

Mapping to Transformers:
- Geometry: because LayerNorm/RMSNorm keeps representations near constant norm, the state is modeled on a sphere S^{d-1}; updates live in the tangent space and are mapped back via a retraction.
- Discretization: forward Euler on spherical SVFlow yields a residual-style step followed by a relaxed retraction, aligning with residual + RMSNorm blocks.
- Attention: MHA is interpreted as approximating the SVFlow vector field via a von Mises–Fisher kernel-smoothed posterior; multi-head corresponds to multiple such approximations in parallel subspaces.
- FFN/MoE: FFN/MoE approximates the same vector field in a more relaxed, network-based manner, which motivates why MoE may need explicit balancing to avoid degenerate posterior/expert usage.
