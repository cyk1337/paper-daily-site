18:T5ef,DiffScore reframes evaluation as masked reconstruction. A candidate text is corrupted under varying masking rates, and a bidirectional model is asked to recover masked tokens; higher-quality text should be more confidently recoverable under diverse masking patterns.
- Backbone: Masked Large Diffusion Language Models (MDLLMs) with an absorbing mask token [M]. The forward process masks each token independently with probability t; the reverse model is a non-causal (bidirectional) Transformer predicting clean tokens at masked positions.
- DiffScore-Zero: zero-shot scoring using the MDLLM ELBO. A key detail is the 1/t reweighting term, compensating for the expected number of masked tokens (~tL) so that contributions across timesteps are comparable.
- DiffScore-FT: task-adaptive fine-tuning without gold references, intended to isolate the gain from the evaluation paradigm rather than reference-based supervision.
- Diagnostics: (1) multi-timestep quality profiles that decompose the score across masking rates (low t ≈ local fluency; high t ≈ global coherence), and (2) a bidirectional PMI-style decomposition to separate fluency vs faithfulness/coverage dimensions—argued to be structurally difficult for AR scoring due to directional coupling.
