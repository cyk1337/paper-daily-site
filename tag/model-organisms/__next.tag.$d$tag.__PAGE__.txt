b:T474,The method has two stages. First, generate many candidate completions from the finetuned model. To induce diversity, the authors prepend short random prefills drawn from common public corpora, then decode with temperature zero. These prefills act as varied entry points that can trigger overgeneralized finetuned behavior outside its intended setting. Second, score each completion under both the finetuned model and a reference model, which can be the pre-finetuning checkpoint or a cross-family model. Completions are ranked by perplexity-based gaps, typically favoring text that is low perplexity for the finetuned model and high perplexity for the reference model. Such text is likely to reflect newly learned behavior, memorized training strings, or emergent side effects of finetuning. The method does not require internal activations or prior knowledge of the target behavior. Its limitations are also clear: strongly context-gated behaviors may not be triggered by random prefills; behaviors that are unsurprising to the reference model may not produce a strong gap; and sparse leakage may be insufficient for confident attribution.c:T410,The evaluation spans a broad set of public and newly finetuned model organisms: backdoored models, sleeper agents, models trained to learn false facts through synthetic document finetuning, hidden-behavior models trained on synthetic documents or transcripts and then refined with SFT or KTO, evaluation-awareness models, reward-model sycophancy models, and models showing broad misalignment after narrow misaligned finetuning.
