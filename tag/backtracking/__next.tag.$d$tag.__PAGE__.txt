9:T46c,Pyligent implements a validated-search view of reasoning inspired by the Diligent Learner formulation. It maintains a ChainTree of partial reasoning states. The model emits one action at a time: <node> appends an intermediate step, <done> proposes a final answer, and <backtrack> returns control to an earlier node. Training has three stages. SFT-A teaches the action format and gives the model a nonzero probability of producing valid next steps. Validator-guided exploration samples continuations from selected prefixes and labels both successful continuations and failed leaves. SFT-B then trains on examples extracted from the resulting tree: success pairs, failure pairs with validator-derived backtrack targets, and optional continuation-after-trace pairs. A reverse curriculum starts exploration near the end of a gold chain and gradually moves toward the root. At inference time, the runtime validates backtrack targets and truncates the context accordingly. Traced recovery adds a compact trace after truncation, summarizing the abandoned branch and validator reason, so the model is less likely to repeat the same mistake.a:T5e4,1.
