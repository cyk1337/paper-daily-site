Second is the proxy-score construction. During decoding, the method does not compute full-precision inner products between the query and all keys. This is a meaningful design choice because attention sparsity varies widely across layers, heads, and tasks. Top-p is a better match to heterogeneous attention distributions, but only if the proxy can estimate score magnitudes reasonably well rather than merely preserving order.
