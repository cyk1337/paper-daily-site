9:T444,The paper defines a power distribution over full completions, typically of the form q_alpha(y|x) proportional to pi(y|x)^alpha. When alpha is greater than one, probability mass concentrates on sequences that the base model already assigns high likelihood. The first technical result shows that cheap token-level approximations cannot generally reproduce this sequence-level distribution unless they have information about possible suffixes. This is because the correct token-level marginal under the power distribution depends on the total powered probability of all continuations. The RL connection comes from a KL-regularized objective where the reward is the model’s own sequence log-likelihood, r_self(x,y)=log pi(y|x). The closed-form optimizer of this objective is exactly a power distribution. Rewriting the same objective yields power self-distillation, where a student amortizes the teacher distribution induced by power sampling.
