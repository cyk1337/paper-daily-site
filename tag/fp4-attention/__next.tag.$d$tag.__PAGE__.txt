9:T411,ThriftAttention is a training-free selective mixed-precision attention method. The paper starts with a first-order perturbation analysis of softmax attention. The contribution of a quantized score error is governed by the score error itself, the attention probability, and the deviation of the value vector from the final output. The attention-probability term makes errors on high-score tokens much more consequential. In implementation, Q, K, and V are quantized with the NVFP4 microscaling format on Blackwell GPUs: E2M1 FP4 values with E4M3 FP8 scales. Q and K/V are partitioned into blocks. For each query block and key block, the method computes an importance score using the dot product between their token-wise block means. The top-k key blocks for each query block are computed in FP16, while all remaining blocks are computed in FP4. The two precision paths are merged with online softmax.
