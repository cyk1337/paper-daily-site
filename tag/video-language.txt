18:T44b,Orca encodes world signals X (in this version, primarily vision and language) into a latent state S = f_θ(X), and learns forward/backward evolution of states over time. Two factors are introduced:
- z_t for unobserved/implicit dynamics (physics, object properties, scene dynamics).
- c_t for observed/explicit conditions (instructions).

Training uses two complementary paradigms:
1) Unconscious learning: self-supervised learning from continuous videos to capture dense natural transitions, implemented as predicting next-step latents (latent-level temporal prediction rather than explicit semantic supervision).
2) Conscious learning: instruction- and event-conditioned learning using language-described events and VQA supervision to model sparse but meaningful transitions tied to decisions and outcomes.

A notable design choice is evaluation via readouts: the backbone is frozen during decoder post-training, and only lightweight readout modules are trained for text/image/action outputs.
