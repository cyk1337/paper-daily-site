9:T5ef,TRACE treats rollouts as trees rather than atomic trajectories. The root is the prompt; each ReAct turn (thought–action–observation) forms a semantically meaningful node/prefix that can be revisited and branched. Budget allocation is defined over these anchors (roots and internal prefixes).

The guiding principle is mixed-reward contrast construction: allocate more budget to anchors whose descendant continuations are likely to contain both successes and failures, because such anchors can generate sibling trajectories with opposite terminal outcomes, yielding stronger contrast for credit assignment under outcome-only rewards.

The framework has two stages:
1) Global root allocation over a large prompt pool: decide which prompts get zero vs positive rollout budget, favoring prompts with non-extreme success probabilities to increase terminal reward variance.
2) Local tree expansion: given collected rollouts, score encountered prefix nodes and allocate additional continuation branches to prefixes likely to produce outcome-diverse descendants.

A shared success-probability predictor estimates conditional success probability from prefix histories and is used to approximate each anchor’s utility under the descendant-set objective. Compared to prior work that allocates only at the prompt level (selection / per-prompt rollout counts), TRACE introduces turn-level branching as a first-class budget decision, effectively performing counterfactual exploration where it is most likely to change outcomes.a:T937,技术路线由架构与目标两部分组成，而且两者是联动设计，不是简单相加。架构上，HRM-Text 采用双时间尺度递归：慢速 H 模块负责较稳定的高层语义状态，快速 L 模块负责局部迭代细化。一次前向包含两个 high-level cycle，每个 cycle 内执行三次 L 更新再执行一次 H 更新，最终用最后的 H 状态输出 token logits。这样做的意图是用重复计算换参数规模，让模型在较少参数和较少数据下仍有较深的推理/整合过程。问题在于，重复应用同一变换会把 Transformer 中 norm placement 的老问题放大。为此作者引入 MagicNorm 和 warmup deep credit assignment。根据正文描述，MagicNorm 试图兼顾 PreNorm 与 PostNorm 的优点：既约束前向激活方差，避免递归堆叠时状态发散，又尽量保留稳定的梯度通路，减少深递归下的优化困难。配套上使用 parameterless RMSNorm、SwiGLU、RoPE 和 sigmoid-gated self-attention，说明作者在尽量压缩不必要自由度，优先保训练稳定。warmup deep credit assignment 则是一个很关键但容易被忽略的训练技巧：训练初期只对最后 2 个 recurrent step 回传梯度，随后再扩展到最后 5 步，等于显式控制有效反向深度，先学短程信用分配，再逐步放开长程依赖。这和 curriculum on optimization depth 很像，是递归 LM 能否训起来的关键之一。

目标函数上，作者没有走标准 raw-text next-token，而是直接从 instruction-response pairs 做 task-completion pretraining，只在 response token 上计算 NLL，即优化 -log P(x_a | x_q)。同时配 PrefixLM mask，让 instruction 部分双向可见、response 部分保持因果生成。这里的设计选择很鲜明：既然模型最终大多用于条件生成，就不再把大量算力花在预测 prompt-like 文本、网页噪声或任务无关续写上，而是把训练信号集中到“给定条件，完成回答”这件事。和纯 encoder-decoder 不同，PrefixLM 仍保留单流生成接口；和标准 causal LM 不同，它允许 instruction 侧更充分的信息整合。论文真正有意思的地方在于，它不是只换了一个 mask，而是把数据形态、损失定义和架构归纳偏置一起改了，形成一个面向 sample efficiency 的整体 recipe。b:Ta1f,The method is a coupled redesign of architecture and objective rather than an isolated component swap. On the architecture side, HRM-Text uses dual-timescale recurrence: a slow H module maintains a relatively stable high-level semantic state, while a fast L module performs local iterative refinement. One forward pass consists of two high-level cycles; each cycle runs three L updates followed by one H update, and token logits are produced from the final H state. The intended trade-off is clear: use repeated computation instead of proportionally increasing parameter count, so the model can perform deeper integration under smaller budgets.

The hard part is optimization. Repeatedly applying the same transformation amplifies the classic normalization-placement problem already familiar from Transformers. The paper introduces MagicNorm and warmup deep credit assignment to make this viable. From the full text, MagicNorm is designed to preserve bounded forward activation variance while keeping backward optimization stable, effectively trying to capture the good parts of both PreNorm and PostNorm under recurrence. The surrounding design choices—parameterless RMSNorm, SwiGLU, RoPE, and sigmoid-gated self-attention—suggest a deliberate bias toward stability and reduced unnecessary flexibility. Warmup deep credit assignment is especially important: early in training, gradients are backpropagated only through the final two recurrent steps, and later expanded to the final five. This explicitly controls effective backward depth, allowing the model to first learn short-range credit assignment before exposing it to longer recurrent dependencies.

On the objective side, the paper abandons standard raw-text next-token pretraining. HRM-Text is trained from scratch on instruction-response pairs, with NLL computed only on the response tokens, i.e. optimizing -log P(x_a | x_q). This is paired with a PrefixLM mask: full bidirectional attention over the instruction prefix, causal generation over the response. The motivation is sharp. If the model is mostly used for conditional generation at inference time, then spending most of the budget predicting prompt-like text, web boilerplate, or task-irrelevant continuations may be wasteful. Compared with encoder-decoder training, PrefixLM preserves a single-stream generative interface; compared with standard causal LM, it allows richer integration over the conditioning context. The interesting part is that the paper changes data form, loss support, and architectural prior together into one sample-efficiency-oriented recipe.c:T6c0,The headline result is the budget comparison. The authors claim that HRM-Text, trained from scratch on only 40B unique tokens, reaches the performance neighborhood of contemporary open models such as Llama, Qwen, Gemma, OLMo, Ouro, and Huginn on most benchmarks, while using roughly 100–900× fewer training tokens and 96–432× less estimated training compute. If those numbers are supported under fair accounting, this is not a routine gain in data efficiency; it materially changes what is feasible in the low-budget regime. The wording matters, though: the claim is about entering the same performance band on many benchmarks, not uniformly surpassing those baselines.

Three experimental blocks deserve close attention. First, stability and training dynamics: ablations for MagicNorm and warmup deep credit assignment should reveal whether training diverges, becomes noisy, or converges to much worse solutions without them. Loss curves, activation/gradient statistics, and sensitivity to backprop depth are central here. Second, objective ablations: instruction-response-only training, response-only loss, and PrefixLM masking versus standard causal LM or raw-text training. This determines whether the gains come mainly from the architecture or from a more focused data/objective design. Third, compute and data efficiency comparisons against open baselines under roughly matched parameter scales or training budgets.
