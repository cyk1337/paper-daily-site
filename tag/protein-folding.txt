It applies 12 deterministic structural operators to known coordinates, producing 14,400 question-answer records. The operators cover contacts, distance comparisons, segment orientation, center proximity, local direction, chirality, multi-constraint relations, and global-summary matching. Fold2Reason freezes the base Qwen3.5-9B weights and trains LoRA parameters. The model reads a protein view with one marker per residue; coordinates are never given as input and are used only to define targets and losses. Discrete FoldingCorpus labels supervise the native language head. In parallel, a training-time workspace projects marker states to 256 dimensions, exchanges messages over local residue pairs and sampled long-range pairs, and feeds a frozen coordinate/distogram decoder.
