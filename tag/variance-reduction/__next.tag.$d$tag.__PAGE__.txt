5:T916,方法核心是 CARV。它先把 frozen-teacher gradient 的随机性拆成不同来源，再把每一类随机性的成本显式计入，最后据此设计更合适的 Monte Carlo 估计器。

第一部分是层次化 Monte Carlo。作者观察到一次样本贡献通常包含两层随机性：外层是昂贵的上游状态构造，例如 renderer / encoder / generator forward；内层是便宜的 diffusion noising 与 teacher denoising。如果每次都把两层一起重采样，会把大量预算浪费在外层。于是他们提出 amortized resampling：缓存昂贵上游结果，只对便宜的 diffusion noise 重采样。这样做保持无偏，但能在固定预算下显著增加内层样本数。论文把这种收益用 ECM（effective compute multiplier）来量化，本质是在比较“达到同等方差需要多少额外算力”。

第二部分是 timestep importance sampling。扩散 teacher 的目标里本来就有显式 timestep weight，作者不是沿用 teacher 训练时继承下来的时间分布，而是针对下游梯度估计重新选 proposal q(t)。理论上最优 proposal 与条件二阶矩有关，实际不可得，于是他们用任务相关的可计算 proxy 来近似，并给出一个很直接的 drop-in 选择 q∝p·w。这里的关键判断是：下游要估计的是 teacher-induced gradient，不是 teacher training loss，所以最优采样分布应围绕梯度贡献而不是训练损失本身。

第三部分是 stratified inverse-CDF sampling。单纯 importance sampling 仍会受有限样本波动影响，尤其在 timestep 贡献高度不均时。作者把 stratification 和 importance sampling 结合，用 inverse-CDF 构造分层采样，使每个 batch 对时间轴覆盖更均匀，同时保留重要区域的高采样密度。文中把它描述为在无偏分配里接近最优的方案。

第四部分是 compute-aware variance accounting。论文没有停在“某个 trick 有效”，而是系统测量不同随机源、不同成本项、不同采样策略对总方差的贡献。这个框架的价值在于，它解释了为什么某些直觉上常见的做法收益有限：如果主导方差的是 timestep 和 noise，而主导成本的是 renderer/encoder，那么继续增加完全独立样本并不划算，应该优先做分层与重用。6:T9bd,The central idea is CARV, a compute-aware variance-accounting framework for gradients induced by frozen diffusion teachers. The framework decomposes randomness sources, attaches explicit cost to each source, and then derives estimator choices that improve variance per unit compute.

The first component is hierarchical Monte Carlo with amortized resampling. A typical sample contribution contains an expensive outer random component, such as rendering, encoding, or generator forward computation, and a cheaper inner random component from diffusion noising and denoising. Resampling both layers independently wastes budget on the expensive outer layer. CARV instead caches the expensive upstream computation and resamples only the cheap diffusion noise. This keeps the estimator unbiased while increasing the number of effective inner samples under the same budget. The paper quantifies the gain using ECM, the effective compute multiplier.

The second component is timestep importance sampling. Rather than inheriting the teacher’s original timestep distribution, the paper chooses a proposal distribution q(t) tailored to the downstream gradient estimator. The theoretical optimum depends on a conditional second moment and is not directly available, so the authors use tractable proxies. A simple drop-in proposal q proportional to p times the explicit teacher weight already gives measurable gains. The important conceptual point is that the right proposal should target downstream gradient contribution, not the original diffusion training loss.

The third component is stratified inverse-CDF sampling over timesteps. Importance sampling alone still suffers from finite-sample fluctuation, especially when contribution mass is concentrated in a narrow timestep region. The paper combines stratification with inverse-CDF construction so that each batch covers the timestep axis more evenly while still allocating more samples to important regions. The authors argue this is close to optimal among unbiased allocations.

The fourth component is the variance-accounting framework itself. Instead of presenting isolated tricks, the paper measures how different randomness sources and cost terms contribute to total variance. This explains why some common heuristics underperform: if variance is dominated by timestep and noise randomness while cost is dominated by rendering or encoding, then fully independent samples are a poor use of budget compared with reuse plus structured sampling.7:T6ef,The experiments cover three settings: diffusion-teacher-guided text-to-3D optimization, one-step distillation, and data attribution.

The headline result is a roughly 2–3× effective compute multiplier in text-to-3D distillation and attribution. In practical terms, CARV reaches comparable variance at about one-third to one-half of the original cost. This is the main empirical claim of the paper and the most important number to retain.

For timestep sampling, the explicit-weight importance proposal improves variance by about 1.2× over uniform timestep sampling at equal per-iteration cost. The paper also includes a toy analysis showing that the oracle proposal is variance-equivalent to roughly 3–4× uniform compute, while a Gaussian-around-the-peak approximation is worth about 2×. This indicates that timestep allocation alone has meaningful headroom.

Hierarchical resampling is presented as the dominant source of gain. Reusing expensive upstream computation and resampling only cheap diffusion noise drives most of the ECM improvement. That is an important practical takeaway: the main benefit does not come from an elaborate control-variate construction, but from exploiting the nested cost structure correctly.

A notable negative result appears in one-step distillation: variance-per-compute improves, but downstream FID does not. This is informative rather than disappointing. It suggests that better gradient estimation does not automatically translate into better end metrics when other error sources dominate or when the training objective is only weakly aligned with FID.

Overall, the experimental design is consistent with the paper’s goal. The focus is on estimator quality and variance-per-compute, not on forcing every task metric to improve.8:T4d2,Start with the problem setup and the figures that separate the two randomness layers and their costs. Without that, the method can look like a generic “sample more noise” trick, which misses the point.

Then read the variance-accounting section, especially the definition of ECM and the associated measurements. That is the conceptual center of the paper. The method only makes full sense once the evaluation target is variance under a compute budget rather than raw iteration count.

After that, focus on the timestep proposal and stratified inverse-CDF construction, together with the corresponding figures and tables. Those sections show that the method is not heuristic schedule tuning; it is a principled combination of importance sampling, stratification, and the weighting structure already present in diffusion objectives.

For a deeper read, spend time on the appendix derivations for the oracle proposal, practical approximations, and unbiased allocation.
