1) Conceptual layer (CPC): transformer computation is framed as iterative refinement of representational fragments under a global coherence functional C (explicitly treated as an interpretive hypothesis, not an explicit training objective). Components can instantiate four operator roles:
- Alignment: propose candidate relations between context fragments.
- Unification: integrate mutually supporting evidence into a consistent interpretation.
- Suppression: downweight incompatible alternatives / resolve conflicts.
- Routing: carry selected information forward to the prediction site.
Roles can be distributed across components; components can be multi-role.
2) Operational layer: define attention-head role “signatures” in weight space and corresponding activation-level role measures, then test whether weight signatures predict held-out activation measures.
