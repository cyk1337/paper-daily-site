18:T5c5,The method has three layers. First, the paper analyzes non-orthogonality from both tensor approximation and dot-product error perspectives. The key point is that the error of low-rank approximation composed with quantization is not just the sum of two independent errors; cross terms appear, so the composition cannot be treated as harmless stacking. Second, it studies ordering and derives that low-rank decomposition should be applied before quantization. The intuition is sensible: if quantization is applied first, the subsequent low-rank fit is performed on an already distorted matrix, so the principal subspace estimate itself is biased by quantization noise. If low-rank decomposition comes first, the dominant structure is extracted before discretization, and the later quantization acts on a more compact and regularized representation. Third, the paper introduces DAM, a Diagonal Adhesive Method, motivated by activation outliers. After low-rank decomposition, representation statistics can shift so that a few large-magnitude channels dominate block-wise quantization scales, reducing effective resolution for the majority of values. DAM keeps a diagonal-form corrective component to preserve scale information that is poorly captured by the joint low-rank-plus-low-bit approximation.
