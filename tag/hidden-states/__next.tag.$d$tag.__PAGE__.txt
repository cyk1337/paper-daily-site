对比方法有四类。第一类是 probe，本质是判别给定候选答案在该层隐藏状态中是否带有真假标签。第二类是 INSIDE EigenScore，需要对同一 prompt 采样多个 completion，再从内部状态谱特征估计不确定性。第三类是 self-consistency，用 exact match plurality 或句向量相似度衡量多次采样的一致性。第四类是注意力熵，只取首层、中层、末层最后一个 answer token 的注意力分布熵做特征。论文最重要的技术判断其实在协议解释：在 paired-label 设定下，probe 看到的是“prompt + 指定候选答案”的条件表征，而采样类方法只看到 prompt 后的自由生成分歧，因此两者并不在利用同一信息。作者把很多采样法表现差的原因，归结为这种结构性失配，而不是简单说采样法无效。

They use four binary hallucination benchmarks: TruthfulQA, HaluEval-QA, FEVER, and a synthetic dataset. Each example is turned into a paired-label instance with the same prompt and two candidate answers, one truthful and one hallucinated, yielding an almost perfectly balanced classification task.

For representation extraction, they follow the SAPLMA-style protocol: concatenate prompt and candidate answer, run one forward pass, collect the embedding output plus every transformer block output, and apply last-token pooling over the answer span to get one vector per layer. On top of each layer representation, they train a linear probe and a small MLP probe.

They compare four detector families. Probes operate on the conditional representation of a provided answer. INSIDE EigenScore samples multiple completions and derives an uncertainty score from internal-state spectra. Self-consistency measures agreement across sampled generations using exact-match plurality or embedding similarity. Attention entropy uses per-head entropy summaries from early, middle, and late blocks. The most important methodological point is the protocol asymmetry: probes see the supplied candidate answer, while sampling-based methods only see the prompt and then generate freely. So under this paired-label setup, the two families do not consume the same information. The paper’s negative result for sampling methods is therefore framed as a structural mismatch, not simply as algorithmic failure.

A second notable claim is that the truthfulness signal is approximately linear. MLP probes bring little gain over linear probes, suggesting that nonlinear decision boundaries are not doing most of the work. Layerwise peaks are also consistent: blocks 13–18 out of 32 for Llama and Mistral, and blocks 19–25 out of 28 for Qwen. That mid-to-late concentration is more interesting than the headline AUROC because it hints that factuality-related separation becomes clearest after semantic integration but before the final generation-oriented layers dominate.d:T5e7,The experimental scale is modest but tidy: up to 400 items per dataset, evaluated layer by layer for 3 models × 4 datasets. The authors attribute this gap to information mismatch rather than to a universal superiority of probes.

Dataset difficulty follows intuition. TruthfulQA is the hardest because the negative answers are plausible distractors. HaluEval is easier because explicit supporting knowledge is prepended. This is not a paper that changes the pretraining recipe tomorrow, but it is a solid step in measurement and mechanism analysis.10:T793,The key move is to define commitment directly from the model’s conditional distribution rather than from an auxiliary classifier. For a frozen decoder-only LM at state ξ, each candidate answer a is associated with a set of verbalizers Σ(a). The model scores full verbalizer continuations with autoregressive log-probability, marginalizes over verbalizers, and obtains answer-level scores. In the binary case, this collapses to an exact log-odds code, δ(ξ)=Sθ(yes|ξ)-Sθ(no|ξ). All temporal notions are then defined from δt. Answer onset is the first output position where a parser can uniquely determine the answer. Commitment time is the earliest pre-onset position where the induced winner matches the eventual answer with sufficient margin and never flips afterward. Lead is the token distance between commitment and onset. Several design choices are important. First, they use total continuation log-probability rather than length-normalized scores because the object of interest is probability mass assigned to finite answer continuations; length normalization would define a different target. Second, they deliberately use delayed-verdict templates so that answer visibility is separated from template progress, making onset detection reliable and exact scoring feasible. Third, probes are introduced only after the target is defined. Compact hidden-state summaries are used to linearly recover δ, not to define commitment. Fourth, the paper attempts to separate commitment from realization progress via low-dimensional factors, and validates that separation with controls, transfer tests, bootstrap analyses, and multi-seed evaluation. Fifth, it adds quality-gated templates, online calibrated rules, and activation steering to test robustness, online predictability, and local causal sensitivity.
