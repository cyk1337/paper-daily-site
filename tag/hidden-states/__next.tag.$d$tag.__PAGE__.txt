9:T469,LatentSift replaces the first-stage LLM-based execution-free verifier in a hybrid SWE verification pipeline. The standard pipeline scores all K trajectories with an EF verifier, keeps a subset, then applies regression tests, LLM-generated tests, and a final EF selection step. LatentSift instead collects hidden states while the policy is generating candidates, adding no extra LLM tokens. It represents each trajectory through three channels: reasoning states, environment-observation states, and function-call states. During training, states from successful and unsuccessful trajectories are stored in positive and negative banks. At inference time, candidate states are compared with these banks, step-level distance margins are aggregated, and channel-level ranks are fused conservatively so that a candidate must look good across the behavioral channels. A separate linear scorer trained on policy hidden states produces a learned trajectory score. The final filtering score combines normalized ranks from the distance branch and the learned scorer, then retains half of the candidates for the remaining verification stages.a:T4c4,The authors build a balanced three-class dataset with N=750 examples: 250 from LogiQA 2.0 for deductive reasoning, 250 from ARC-Challenge for inductive reasoning, and 250 from αNLI for abductive reasoning. They evaluate Qwen3-14B, a 40-layer decoder-only model with hidden size 5120, loaded in bf16. Prompts are standardized, decoding is greedy with a 2048-token budget, and thinking mode is disabled; <think> blocks are stripped to avoid confounding hidden-state geometry with output-style differences.

For each layer, the representation is the hidden state at the last input token. A linear logistic-regression probe with L2 regularization predicts the reasoning-mode label. The best layer is then analyzed geometrically using intrinsic dimensionality, local curvature, inter-class separation, and convex-hull contamination. The key contribution is the deconfounding pipeline: compare reasoning-mode probing with source-identity probing, test whether option count alone predicts labels, restrict to four-choice tasks, and residualize hidden states with respect to source identity, option count, and response length before re-probing. The paper also runs causal steering experiments with 20 random-direction controls.b:T824,方法设计比较朴素，但协议控制得较清楚。作者选了三类常见开源 chat model：Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct、Mistral-7B-Instruct-v0.3、Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct，统一以 4-bit NF4 量化加载，在单张 8GB GPU 上运行。数据上使用四个二分类幻觉基准：TruthfulQA、HaluEval-QA、FEVER，以及一个合成数据集。每个样本都构造成同一 prompt 下的 truthful answer 与 hallucinated answer 配对，类别接近 50:50 平衡。核心表征提取方式沿用 SAPLMA 风格：把 prompt 与候选 answer 拼接，做一次前向，抽取 embedding 输出和每个 transformer block 输出，对 answer span 做 last-token pooling，得到每层一个 D 维向量；然后在每一层分别训练线性 probe 和两层 MLP probe。

对比方法有四类。第一类是 probe，本质是判别给定候选答案在该层隐藏状态中是否带有真假标签。第二类是 INSIDE EigenScore，需要对同一 prompt 采样多个 completion，再从内部状态谱特征估计不确定性。第三类是 self-consistency，用 exact match plurality 或句向量相似度衡量多次采样的一致性。第四类是注意力熵，只取首层、中层、末层最后一个 answer token 的注意力分布熵做特征。论文最重要的技术判断其实在协议解释：在 paired-label 设定下，probe 看到的是“prompt + 指定候选答案”的条件表征，而采样类方法只看到 prompt 后的自由生成分歧，因此两者并不在利用同一信息。作者把很多采样法表现差的原因，归结为这种结构性失配，而不是简单说采样法无效。

从结果看，作者进一步声称 truthfulness signal 近似线性：MLP probe 相比线性 probe 提升很小，说明非线性边界不是主要收益来源。层位上，Llama 和 Mistral 的最佳层集中在 32 层模型的第 13–18 层，Qwen 集中在 28 层模型的第 19–25 层，都是中后层而非最后层。这一点比单纯 AUROC 更值得看，因为它暗示真假判别可能在语义整合后、logit 投影前最清晰。c:T411,Its value for pretraining researchers is not the hallucination application itself, but what it says about internal representations. First, 7B–8B instruction-tuned models appear to carry a fairly stable truthfulness-related signal in mid-to-late layers, and that signal is close to linearly separable. That suggests factuality-relevant information is not only reflected in final token probabilities; it is already organized in intermediate semantic states. Second, the signal remains readable after NF4 quantization, which hints that some internal semantic geometry is robust to low-bit deployment. For pretraining and training-dynamics work, this raises useful questions: whether the signal is mostly inherited from pretraining or sharpened by instruction tuning, why it peaks in middle/late layers rather than the top layer, and whether such layerwise signals could be fed back into training objectives, data filtering, or abstention mechanisms. This is better read as a mechanistic/evaluation clarification than as a new training recipe.d:T8d4,The setup is simple but carefully controlled. The authors study three common open chat models—Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct, Mistral-7B-Instruct-v0.3, and Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct—loaded in 4-bit NF4 on a single 8GB GPU. They use four binary hallucination benchmarks: TruthfulQA, HaluEval-QA, FEVER, and a synthetic dataset. Each example is turned into a paired-label instance with the same prompt and two candidate answers, one truthful and one hallucinated, yielding an almost perfectly balanced classification task.

For representation extraction, they follow the SAPLMA-style protocol: concatenate prompt and candidate answer, run one forward pass, collect the embedding output plus every transformer block output, and apply last-token pooling over the answer span to get one vector per layer. On top of each layer representation, they train a linear probe and a small MLP probe.

They compare four detector families. Probes operate on the conditional representation of a provided answer. INSIDE EigenScore samples multiple completions and derives an uncertainty score from internal-state spectra. Self-consistency measures agreement across sampled generations using exact-match plurality or embedding similarity. Attention entropy uses per-head entropy summaries from early, middle, and late blocks. The most important methodological point is the protocol asymmetry: probes see the supplied candidate answer, while sampling-based methods only see the prompt and then generate freely. So under this paired-label setup, the two families do not consume the same information. The paper’s negative result for sampling methods is therefore framed as a structural mismatch, not simply as algorithmic failure.

A second notable claim is that the truthfulness signal is approximately linear. MLP probes bring little gain over linear probes, suggesting that nonlinear decision boundaries are not doing most of the work. Layerwise peaks are also consistent: blocks 13–18 out of 32 for Llama and Mistral, and blocks 19–25 out of 28 for Qwen. That mid-to-late concentration is more interesting than the headline AUROC because it hints that factuality-related separation becomes clearest after semantic integration but before the final generation-oriented layers dominate.e:T5e7,The experimental scale is modest but tidy: up to 400 items per dataset, evaluated layer by layer for 3 models × 4 datasets. The headline result is that a single-layer linear probe reaches 0.904–1.000 AUROC on held-out splits, while sampling-based detectors stay at or below 0.541 AUROC under the same protocol, essentially near chance. The authors attribute this gap to information mismatch rather than to a universal superiority of probes.

Dataset difficulty follows intuition. TruthfulQA is the hardest because the negative answers are plausible distractors. HaluEval is easier because explicit supporting knowledge is prepended. The synthetic benchmark is the cleanest true/false contrast and therefore yields the highest scores. FEVER sits in between. Ablations show that linear and MLP probes perform similarly, supporting the “linearly decodable” claim.
