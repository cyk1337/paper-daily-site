9:T5d4,RISE constructs a synthetic teacher by extrapolating the displacement between a current RLVR-updated checkpoint and a trailing anchor checkpoint. Let π_{θ_n} be the policy at iteration n, updated by RLVR to π_{θ'_{n+1}}. Choose a representation map φ that embeds a policy into a vector space where linear operations are meaningful (parameters or logits/distributions). Define the recent improvement direction as Δ = φ(π_{θ'_{n+1}}) − φ(π_{θ_n}). The self-extrapolated teacher is obtained by projecting further along this direction with scale β:

φ(teacher) = φ(π_{θ'_{n+1}}) + (β−1)·Δ, with β>1.

Two instantiations are emphasized:
- Weight-space extrapolation (φ=θ): θ_future = θ'_{n+1} + (β−1)(θ'_{n+1}−θ_n), essentially task arithmetic with an extrapolation coefficient; the teacher is an explicit model.
- Logit-space extrapolation (φ=logits / next-token distributions): extrapolate at the output distribution level (implemented via a geometric mixture / distributional extrapolation), producing token-level targets without necessarily materializing a full parameter-space teacher.

Training alternates RLVR and OPD in a recursive loop: RLVR grounds the direction (so extrapolation is not amplifying arbitrary drift), and OPD distills the extrapolated teacher into the student via token-level KL/CE.
