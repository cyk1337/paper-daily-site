The theoretical analysis focuses on the first unresolved denoising state. When a correct page and a conflicting page each support a different coherent answer, the correct latent structure requires multiple answer positions to share one source choice. A position-factorized proposal breaks this dependence and allocates probability mass to cross-source answers; the unsupported mass grows as the conflict spans more answer positions.
