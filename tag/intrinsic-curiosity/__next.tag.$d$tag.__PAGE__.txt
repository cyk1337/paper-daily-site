5:T511,The setup is a Bayes-Adaptive MDP. Each episode samples a latent environment parameter θ from a prior, and transitions are Markovian given θ. There is no external task reward. The policy should choose actions that produce trajectories informative about θ. The ideal intrinsic reward is Bayesian information gain, the mutual information between the next observation and θ conditioned on the history. Exact BIG is hard because it requires explicit Bayesian inference over environment dynamics. The paper instead assumes a pretrained and frozen sequence model ρ trained on trajectories from the prior, with uniform actions, to output the Bayesian posterior predictive for the next state. Under this view, ρ is an in-context learner that performs fast posterior prediction from its context. The paper then studies which intrinsic rewards can be computed from prediction errors or context manipulations of ρ. It proves negative results for general MDPs: prediction-based rewards do not generally equal BIG, and classic learning progress cannot be implemented simply by replacing model updates with ICL. It also proves positive results for Bayesian Experimental Design regimes, where several reward constructions become ICL-approximable and a suitable long-horizon reward can converge toward
