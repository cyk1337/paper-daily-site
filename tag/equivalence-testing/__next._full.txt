19:T8c4,方法核心是 Typical-Acceptance Invariance Screen, TAIS。它不是提出新的 speculative decoding 算法，而是定义一套行为等价性判定流程。具体有三层：
1) 配对输出比较：同一条 prompt，分别跑 target-only 和 speculative stack，直接比较输出是否 byte-identical。对 T=0 来说，理论上 Leviathan 等人的分布保持性质会退化成逐 token 一致，除去数值非结合性等实现细节；作者把这一点当作实践验证对象，而不是新理论。
2) 统计等价检验：对安全任务的通过率/拒答率差异做 TOST，等价区间设为 ±3 个百分点。也就是说，不要求“完全无差异”，而是要求差异小到落在预先声明的可接受范围内。
3) 效应量约束：逐任务计算 Cohen’s h，并要求最大 |h| < 0.1。这个阈值由扩展实验校准，位置在观测到的 null-consistent 对比之上、传统 trivial-effect floor 之下。

实验设计是五阶段因子化设计。轴包括：接受机制（strict rejection sampling vs probabilistic typical acceptance）、模型对（三组 target-draft 配对，来自 Llama 和 Qwen）、speculation length N∈{1,3,5,8,12}。Phase 1 先跑 5 个独立模型建立基线；Phase 2/3 固定模型对和 prompt，只改变接受机制；Phase 4 扫 speculation length 看是否存在剂量效应；后续扩展再加入更激进的 draft 扰动。

扩展部分是这篇工作的关键。作者没有只测“正常 draft”，而是加入了几种可能引发担忧的条件：
- DPO-adversarial draft：用 flipped Anthropic/hh-rlhf preference 训练出的对抗性 draft，目的是故意扭曲对齐画像；
- GPTQ-4bit draft：测试量化 draft 是否通过 acceptance path 引入偏差；
- 两个随机种子复现；
- fp16 与 bf16 accumulator dtype 切换；
- 70B target + 8B draft 的单臂 scale probe，用来检查生产规模下 refusal 范围是否异常。

从机制上看，这篇工作的隐含判断是：在 greedy decoding 下，只要 target verification 真正逐步约束最终输出，draft 的安全画像不应穿透 acceptance step。作者用对抗 draft 和量化 draft 去压这个假设，结果仍未观察到超出检测阈值的偏差。1a:T820,The main contribution is Typical-Acceptance Invariance Screen (TAIS), a behavioral-equivalence protocol rather than a new decoding algorithm. It has three layers.

First, paired output comparison: for each prompt, run both target-only decoding and the speculative stack, then compare outputs directly, including byte identity. At T=0, the distribution-preservation guarantee from Leviathan et al. should collapse to token-level equality modulo implementation-level numerical issues; the paper treats this as an empirical validation target.

Second, equivalence testing: for safety outcomes such as refusal/pass rates, apply TOST with a predeclared equivalence band of ±3 percentage points. The goal is not to prove exact equality, but to show that any difference is practically negligible within a stated margin.

Third, effect-size control: compute per-task Cohen’s h and require max |h| < 0.1. The threshold is calibrated from the expansion study, placed above the loudest null-consistent contrast and below the conventional trivial-effect floor.

The experimental design is a five-phase factorial setup over three axes: acceptance method (strict rejection sampling vs probabilistic typical acceptance), model pair (three target-draft pairs from Llama and Qwen families), and speculation length N in {1, 3, 5, 8, 12}. Phase 1 establishes standalone baselines for five models. Phases 2 and 3 vary only the acceptance method while holding prompts and model pairs fixed. Phase 4 sweeps speculation length to test dose-response behavior.

The most useful part is the stress testing. The paper does not stop at standard drafts. It adds a DPO-adversarial draft trained on flipped Anthropic/hh-rlhf preferences, a GPTQ-4bit draft, two random seeds, fp16 vs bf16 accumulation, and a 70B target with an 8B draft as a one-arm scale probe. The underlying hypothesis is that under greedy decoding, if target verification is functioning as intended, draft-side alignment differences should not survive the acceptance step. The experiments are designed to pressure-test exactly that claim.1b:T5ab,The confirmatory core contains 16,783 paired samples. The matched expansion adds 44,066 samples, plus a separate 4,006-sample 70B scale probe, for a total near 65k. The paper also mentions a 48,072-sample expansion in one place; that larger number appears to include the unmatched scale probe, so readers should keep the bookkeeping distinction in mind.

Main findings:
- Across matched TAIS probes, every well-conditioned per-task contrast falls within the ±3pp equivalence margin. In the core ledger, 25 of 27 cells pass; the remaining two are not substantive failures but ceiling-region Wald-CI edge cases where p_target = p_spec.
- The largest observed Cohen’s h on matched AdvBench refusal contrasts is only 0.024, far below the 0.1 cutoff.
- Neither the DPO-adversarial draft nor the GPTQ-4bit draft produces detectable safety leakage after target verification.
- The conclusion is stable across two seeds and across fp16/bf16 accumulation.
- The 70B production-scale probe stays within the expected refusal range, but because it lacks a matched 70B target-only arm, it is correctly reported as a scale sanity check rather than part of the main equivalence claim.
