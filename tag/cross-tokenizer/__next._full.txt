For each teacher token, BPM takes its byte string and finds the longest student token whose byte string is a prefix of that teacher token’s bytes. The teacher token’s probability is assigned to that student token, and probabilities mapped to the same student token are aggregated. Probability mass that cannot be matched is assigned to an explicit residual category, which is required to preserve total mass. When byte prefixes do not span multiple teacher tokens, the transformed target exactly recovers the teacher’s byte-prefix marginal. At spanning positions, the method uses a mass-preserving chain-factorized lower bound. Training remains on-policy: the student generates trajectories, the teacher is queried at the corresponding byte-aligned states, and the student minimizes a generalized Jensen-Shannon divergence to the transformed target.
