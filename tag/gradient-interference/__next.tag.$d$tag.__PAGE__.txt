9:T413,FOGO combines two mechanisms. First, it spectrally orthogonalizes the momentum matrix: for matrix-valued parameters, it approximates the SVD of the momentum B_t and uses the polar factor U_t V_t^T as the update, flattening the singular spectrum so that a few dominant modes do not monopolize optimization. This inherits from Muon's matrix orthogonalization, but FOGO ties it to a unified forgetting interpretation. Second, it maintains a directional memory via random projection: high-dimensional gradients are projected with a Rademacher matrix into a low-dimensional space where pairwise distances are approximately preserved by the Johnson-Lindenstrauss lemma; representative past directions are stored as centroids in a compact codebook, and new updates are corrected by lightweight orthogonalization in projected space before a proximal step maps them back to parameter space.
