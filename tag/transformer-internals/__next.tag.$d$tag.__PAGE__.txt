a:T5a7,The proposed framework is computation-conditioned credit transport, or CCT. Standard GAE can be written as a stationary upper-triangular transport kernel whose weights depend only on temporal distance. Group-relative methods often assign a response-level statistic to all valid response tokens, giving a rank-one broadcast structure. CCT instead extracts a detached statistic from the behavior policy’s internal computation at each prefix and maps it to a bounded retention gate κ_t. The sensitivity of an earlier advantage A_t to a later residual δ_u is then the product of gates along the intervening path. This makes the transport kernel nonstationary and trajectory-specific while keeping the task reward and outer objective unchanged. The instantiated algorithm, CompPO, uses native attention concentration as a cheap structural proxy. The gate enters a computation-conditioned bootstrap and a path-dependent generalized-advantage trace, called Comp-GAE. A transport-aligned critic pools value-relevant history from the actor’s hidden states and attention, using the same gate to connect local and routed-history value estimates. The outer objective remains clipped PPO, with the same verifier, rollout format, and reward.
