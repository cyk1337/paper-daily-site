另一个重要选择是只在中间层 residual stream 上训练，而不是 MLP 激活或更深层表示。原因有三：残差流维度较小，训练和推理更便宜；残差流是各子模块共享的通信通道，更可能承载通用语义特征；中间层通常兼具局部语义和全局抽象，既不太浅也不太任务化。这个选择很合理，也意味着论文结论主要适用于“中层残差表示可解释”，不应直接外推到所有层和所有子空间。

The conceptual starting point is the linear representation hypothesis together with the superposition hypothesis: meaningful concepts correspond to directions in activation space, and high-dimensional networks can pack many more features than dimensions by using approximately orthogonal directions. SAEs are used as a sparse dictionary-learning approximation to recover those directions.

The authors collect activations from the middle-layer residual stream of Claude 3 Sonnet and apply scalar normalization so that the average squared L2 norm matches the residual dimension D. The SAE itself is standard: an encoder maps D-dimensional activations into an F-dimensional overcomplete feature space, followed by ReLU to produce nonnegative sparse feature activations; a decoder linearly reconstructs the original activation. Training minimizes reconstruction error plus an L1 sparsity penalty. A crucial detail is that the L1 term is weighted by the norm of each decoder column. This stabilizes the interpretation of unit-normalized decoder vectors as feature directions and prevents a degenerate rescaling trick where the model shrinks activations and inflates decoder norms without changing the reconstruction. That detail is central to making feature activations interpretable.
