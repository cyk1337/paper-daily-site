19:T446,PredicateLongBench frames tasks as constraint satisfaction over a long token sequence: given a long context consisting of space-separated words, the model must output the longest contiguous subsequence that satisfies a set of predicates.

Formally, for a predicate class H and predicates f1..fj∈H, find the maximum k such that there exists a contiguous subsequence w=(w1..wk) with fi(w)=1 for all i.

The design emphasizes controllable difficulty:
- Predicate choice controls dependency structure and computational demands (e.g., unary predicates resemble filtering/retrieval; richer predicates induce cross-position relations and more global reasoning).
- Distractor design is treated as a first-class variable; they study how structured distractors change performance.
- A “search space” axis increases difficulty even under fixed token budgets by expanding the combinatorial space of candidates.
