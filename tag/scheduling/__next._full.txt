18:Tb5f,技术方案可以分成四部分看。

第一部分是理论分析。作者先分析中间状态 s = αe_i + βz 的解码竞争关系。若用欧氏距离，候选 token 的得分会分解成一个随信号缩小而衰减的对齐项，以及一个与当前状态无关的静态范数项。问题就在这里：当 α 变小，即便 β=0、状态方向仍完全对准源 token，低范数 token 也可能因为静态范数优势而“反超”。这不是语义替换，而是度量退化。于是作者转到方向空间，用 cosine / directional ranking 来定义竞争，得到 pairwise crossing 的阈值条件：候选是否超过源 token，取决于有效噪声是否大于两者的语义分离。这个阈值理论把 token identity metric 从经验统计量还原成了大量 pairwise crossing 的聚合结果。

第二部分是 noising geometry。既然问题来自 norm decay，作者就不再沿普通连续高斯轨迹做 corruption，而是设计了 spherical noising。做法是把状态看成沿球面 geodesic 向一个 mask-biased endpoint 插值。这样可以尽量保持向量范数稳定，把竞争关系限制在方向空间里发生，避免欧氏几何下的伪竞争。这里的关键不是“球面”这个词本身，而是它服务于一个更明确的目标：让 corruption 过程和 token competition 的真实判据一致。

第三部分是时间调度，也就是 Semantic Retention Scheduling。Competition Threshold Theory 说明，大量 identity crossing 只会在接近高噪声端才发生。若仍用常规时间参数化，训练的大部分步数都浪费在身份几乎不变的区间，真正困难的身份转移区间反而预算很少。作者因此按 logarithmic rank decay 重新分配时间步，把更多 denoising budget 放到 identity 快速变化的区间。这不是简单的 loss-aware resampling，而是直接围绕 token rank / identity decay 设计 schedule。

第四部分是模型实例化，即 LLaCO。它基于预训练 backbone 适配到连续表示空间，包含 embedding directional adapter 和 timestep-conditioned modulation。前者负责把离散 token embedding 映射到适合方向竞争的连续空间，后者让 backbone 在不同噪声阶段采用不同调制。推理时，模型做 sequential blockwise denoising，并沿 spherical geodesic 逐步把连续语义轨迹翻译回离散 token。这里仍保留 blockwise 生成而不是一次性全序列并行，说明作者在生成质量与并行性之间做了折中：先把连续扩散的语义轨迹建稳，再逐步释放并行生成潜力。

和已有 continuous diffusion LM 相比，区别主要有三点：一是从经验 identity metric 转向解析阈值理论；二是从普通欧氏 corruption 转向方向一致的球面 noising；三是 schedule 不再围绕时间均匀性或损失均匀性，而是围绕语义身份保留来分配训练预算。19:T8b3,全文给出的核心实验结论是：LLaCO 在大规模实例化下显著优于此前连续扩散基线，并且已经接近同规模离散扩散模型。模型规模方面，作者明确实例化了一个 8B 模型，这是这篇工作的一个重要信号，因为很多连续扩散 LM 论文停留在中小规模验证，难以判断方法是否能撑到真正的 LLM 区间。

First, the theory. For an intermediate state s = αe_i + βz, Euclidean decoding can be decomposed into an alignment term that shrinks with the signal and a static norm penalty that does not depend on the current state. This asymmetry creates a degeneracy: as α decreases, a lower-norm token may overtake the source even at zero noise. That is not semantic replacement; it is a metric artifact. The paper therefore switches to directional competition, effectively a cosine-based ranking view, and derives pairwise crossing thresholds. A candidate overtakes the source only when the effective directional noise exceeds their semantic separation. Existing token identity metrics are then reinterpreted as statistical aggregates of many such pairwise crossings.

Second, the noising geometry. Since the pathology comes from norm decay, the paper replaces standard continuous corruption with spherical noising. The trajectory is formulated as a geodesic interpolation toward a mask-biased endpoint, so vector norms are preserved more faithfully and competition happens in directional space rather than being distorted by Euclidean norm effects. This is an important design choice: the corruption process is made consistent with the competition criterion.

Third, the schedule. Competition Threshold Theory implies that in high dimensions, most meaningful identity transitions happen only at very high noise levels. A standard time parameterization therefore wastes much of training on regions where token identity barely changes, while under-allocating budget to the narrow transition zone. The proposed Semantic Retention Scheduling reallocates timesteps according to logarithmic rank decay, explicitly concentrating optimization where identity transitions occur.

Fourth, the model instantiation, LLaCO. A pretrained backbone is adapted to continuous representations using an embedding directional adapter and timestep-conditioned modulation. At inference time, generation proceeds through sequential blockwise denoising along spherical geodesics, translating continuous semantic trajectories back into discrete tokens. Compared with earlier continuous diffusion LMs, the novelty is not just a new schedule. It is the combination of a directional theory of token competition, a norm-preserving corruption path, and a schedule aligned to semantic retention rather than raw time.1b:T869,The main empirical message is that LLaCO scales beyond toy settings and performs substantially better than prior continuous diffusion baselines, while becoming competitive with discrete diffusion models at similar scale. The most useful way to read the experiments is as a three-level validation stack. First, theory validation: the paper compares Euclidean decoding with directional ranking and shows that Euclidean distance produces premature overtaking, including near-zero-noise artifacts, whereas directional competition better matches actual identity decay.
