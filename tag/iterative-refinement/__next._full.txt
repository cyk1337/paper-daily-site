18:T49f,PONDER frames sequential masked generation as an MDP.
- State: the current partially filled canvas x^(t) (visible patches as tokens, missing patches as MASK).
- Frozen generator: a backbone produces per-patch features from x^(t); a diffusion/continuous head fills selected patches.
- Policy actions: choose a set of patches to unmask and generate next; optionally choose previously generated patches to re-mask so they will be regenerated later (“rethinking”). This explicitly models revision rather than hoping later steps fix early mistakes.
- Training: keep the generator fixed; train only the policy with RL using a terminal reward computed on the final sample. Crucially, the method does not need token probabilities or exact log-likelihoods from the generator, which is what makes it applicable beyond discrete masked LMs.
- FIDgrad reward: approximate each sample’s marginal contribution to FID by linearizing the FID objective in Inception-feature mean/covariance statistics, yielding an image-level scalar reward usable by policy gradients.
