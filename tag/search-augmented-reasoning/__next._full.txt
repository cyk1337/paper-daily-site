19:T4b0,Search-E1 alternates two stages:
1) GRPO stage: sample a group of trajectories per question, score them by final-answer exact match, and run standard GRPO updates. This yields sparse supervision because the advantage is trajectory-level.
2) OFSD (Offline Self-Distillation) stage: after GRPO converges, resample rollouts on training questions to build a pool. For each question, mine a (reference, student) pair from sibling trajectories—reference is typically correct and more efficient (e.g., fewer redundant steps), student is worse. Then construct two conditioning contexts so the same policy acts as both teacher and student: under a privileged context that exposes the reference, the model defines a teacher distribution; under the student context, it defines the student distribution. Training minimizes token-level forward KL (teacher || student), with pointwise clipping to prevent a small number of tokens from dominating gradients.
