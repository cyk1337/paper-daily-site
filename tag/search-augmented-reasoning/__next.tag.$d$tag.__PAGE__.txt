第二阶段是 RL refinement。蒸馏后的学生再用 RL 继续优化，目标不是复现教师，而是利用蒸馏后更高熵、更丰富的行为分布作为初始化，进入 RL 单独训练难以到达的解。论文的一个核心判断是：RL from scratch 在 search-augmented reasoning 上容易陷入差的局部策略，比如低质量查询、无效多轮搜索、推理分解不足；而 OPD 先把策略空间“撑开”，再让 RL 用结果奖励做任务对齐。

理论部分主要比较了 forward-KL 与 reverse-KL surrogate 的梯度性质。作者给出 forward-KL 下二阶矩有界的论证，因为 clipped ratio 被限制在 [epsilon, R_max]；而 reverse-KL 的有效权重 c_t=pi_student/pi_teacher-1 无界，且与学生采样概率正相关，所以高方差项更常被采到。经验上他们也观察到 reverse-KL 更不稳。另一个经验发现是，forward-KL 蒸馏后策略熵更高、搜索轮数更少，说明它不是简单把学生收缩到教师模式，而是在保留多样性的同时改善行为质量。这一点和很多人对 KL 蒸馏“会不会过度模仿”的直觉不同。a:T828,The method has two stages. Stage one is on-policy distillation, but not in the usual offline sense. The student interacts with a live search engine and generates full trajectories containing reasoning tokens, query tokens, and answer tokens. A frozen general-purpose instruct model serves as the teacher and scores the student’s own trajectories token by token. Training uses a per-position forward KL objective.

The key implementation detail is the clipped importance ratio r_t = pi_teacher / pi_student, detached from gradients and clipped into a bounded interval. This gives the practical forward-KL gradient used in training. Tokens that the teacher strongly prefers but the student underestimates receive amplified updates, up to a cap. The paper argues that this acts like token-level adaptive reward shaping. In particular, query tokens benefit because the teacher tends to assign higher probability to more specific, retrievable, entity-grounded queries than to weak query formulations such as copying the original question.

Stage two is RL refinement. After distillation, the student is further optimized with RL. The claim is not that distillation solves the task by itself, but that it expands the student’s behavioral support and gives RL a much better initialization. RL from scratch often struggles to discover good search decomposition, evidence integration, and efficient querying. Distillation injects these local behaviors first; RL then optimizes task reward on top of that richer policy.

The theory section contrasts forward KL with reverse-KL-style surrogates. With clipped forward KL, the effective per-token weight is bounded, so the gradient second moment is controlled. In reverse KL, the effective weight can be unbounded and is positively correlated with the student’s own sampling probability, which makes high-variance terms appear more often. Empirically, the paper reports that forward KL is more stable, increases policy entropy, and reduces search turns. That combination is important: the student does not simply collapse into teacher imitation.b:T601,The main result is a mean EM of 0.4402 across seven knowledge-intensive QA benchmarks using a 3B student model. This exceeds all reported 3B RL baselines; the prior best 3B baseline cited in the paper is GiGPO-Instruct at 0.421. The gains are especially strong on multi-hop datasets, which is where the method is most convincing: compared with the best 3B baseline AutoRefine-Base, the model improves by 13.1% on HotpotQA and 8.5% on 2WikiMultihopQA.

The ablations worth noting are threefold. First, a frozen off-the-shelf teacher works without task-specific teacher fine-tuning, supporting the paper’s central claim that the teacher mainly provides local behavioral guidance rather than a task-optimal ceiling. Second, forward KL is more stable than reverse-KL variants, and clipping further improves training robustness. Third, the two-stage pipeline matters: OPD plus RL beats RL-only and also beats distillation-only, which supports the division of labor between policy shaping and reward optimization.

The paper also reports two behavioral effects: higher policy entropy after distillation and fewer search turns. These are useful diagnostics. Higher entropy suggests broader behavioral support rather than narrow imitation, while fewer search turns suggest better query quality and evidence use rather than brute-force retrieval.
