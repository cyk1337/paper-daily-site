- 低深度：所有模型在 depth 1 均 ≥96%。
- depth 5：GPT-4o 与 Claude Sonnet 4 在所有组别均 ≥96%；Llama 有明显 novelty gap。

  * depth3：开始进入长 CoT，TARN 平均 321 tokens、DREM 272 tokens，准确率均跃升到 98%。
- 处理成本差异：depth 5 时 GPT-4o 对 Group A 平均 141 tokens，对 Group B 平均 343 tokens（2.4×），在准确率相当情况下显示“新算子需要更重的显式处理”。

- Benchmark: Boolean operator evaluation where each operator is defined by an explicit truth table, but operator names vary in familiarity.
  * Group A: standard names (AND/OR/XOR/IF-THEN).
  * Group B: unfamiliar names mapped to non-trivial Boolean functions (e.g., A∧¬B, NOR, converse implication, XNOR), chosen to span structural properties like symmetry and sparsity.
  * Group C (Trojan): ZENT has the exact XOR truth table under a novel name; this isolates name familiarity from operator logic.
- Task: left-associated homogeneous chains at depths 1–10, plus mixed-operator chains.
- Behavioral proxy: completion length is used to infer whether the model is doing terse retrieval vs. explicit multi-step reasoning.
- Intervention: Explicit Truth-Table Tracing (ETT) forces step-by-step truth-table lookup to test whether failures are due to missing strategy vs. deeper inconsistencies.
- Localization: a self-correction probe on dissociation cases is used to argue the error is concentrated at the final autoregressive answer declaration rather than in the intermediate reasoning steps.
