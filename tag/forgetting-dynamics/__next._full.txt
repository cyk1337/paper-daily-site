19:T544,A factorial experimental design over three language roles:
- Setup: fine-tune to inject knowledge in language L_FT, unlearn a subset in language L_unl, and query the supposedly forgotten knowledge in language L_Q. Enumerate permutations of (L_FT, L_unl, L_Q) across five languages varying in script/family and pretraining coverage.
- Transfer measurement: quantify forgetting effectiveness when querying in languages different from the unlearning language; analyze patterns by linguistic relatedness and dominance.
- Cross-lingual prompting: ask in L_Q but allow decoding in a different language; measure recovery gains and relate them to unlearning-transfer strength.
- Mechanistic analysis: layer-wise comparison of hidden representations pre/post unlearning across languages; finding that cross-lingual alignment is largely preserved in early/mid layers and altered more in later layers.
- Unlearning directions (steering vectors): extract explicit directions from representational shifts; steering along them restores knowledge across languages without re-training on the forgotten data, supporting a language-agnostic suppression signal.
