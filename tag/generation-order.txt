18:T44e,HMDLM augments masked discrete diffusion with a discrete hierarchy variable h_i for each position.
- Forward diffusion: not only corrupts tokens x toward [MASK], but also diffuses hierarchy states toward an unknown/masked state, so the reverse process must recover structure.
- Reverse denoising is factorized into two stages:
  1) Predict hierarchy for masked positions: p(h | x_t).
  2) Predict tokens conditioned on hierarchy: p(x_0 | x_t, h).
  This encourages the model to infer “which level this position belongs to” before committing to token identities.
- Level-specific schedules: each hierarchy level has its own noise/unmasking schedule, implementing “resolve higher-level positions earlier” at the process level rather than via ad-hoc decoding.
- Training: derives a hierarchy-aware negative ELBO (NELBO) for the joint diffusion over tokens and hierarchy.

During training, random orders are gradually replaced by the learned order, and the final order is frozen for standard autoregressive likelihood evaluation and generation.
