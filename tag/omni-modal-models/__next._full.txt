For each layer and each phase, the method records whether the original router selected an expert, the pre-top-k router softmax probability, and the expert’s ungated output. The selector then optimizes a phase-weighted regularized log-determinant objective over a single retained expert set. The log-det term favors feature volume, so it rewards both high-energy experts and non-redundant experts. Greedy selection is implemented with Schur residuals and Cholesky updates.
