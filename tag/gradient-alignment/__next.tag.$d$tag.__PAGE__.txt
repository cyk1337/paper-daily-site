9:T41c,The method, GraP, frames loss-weight tuning as bilevel optimization. The inner problem updates the model with a weighted sum of pretraining losses. The outer problem measures downstream loss on a labeled subset. For a one-step SGD unroll, the hypergradient for a weight w_k is proportional to the inner product between the downstream gradient and the gradient of the k-th pretraining loss. This gives a direct gradient-alignment signal. Computing full-parameter gradient inner products for every loss would be expensive, so GraP computes the alignment in the shared representation space instead. Each pretraining head and the downstream head produce gradients with respect to the backbone representation z, and these gradients are used to update the loss weights. The paper also argues that normalization should constrain the norm of the resulting update vector, not merely the weight vector, because losses can have very different gradient scales.
