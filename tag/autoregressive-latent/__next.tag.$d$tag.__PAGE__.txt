a:T509,The system is built on a 15B video diffusion transformer and generates 24-fps video at 540p and 720p. Instead of producing a full long video in one pass, it generates short latent chunks autoregressively. Each chunk is conditioned on camera trajectories and switchable text prompts. The central mechanism is a bounded visual context composed of a global scene anchor, compressed recent history, geometry-aligned spatial memory, and recent-frame conditioning. Temporal memory maintains short-range dynamics and frame continuity. Spatial memory stores past observations and reprojects them into the current target view when the camera revisits a region. This keeps per-chunk compute approximately constant as rollout length grows. Training has three stages: adapt a general bidirectional video prior to world-modeling data; convert it into an autoregressive generator with camera control and spatiotemporal memory; then train with corrupted histories and replay prediction residuals collected from the model’s own rollouts. For inference, the paper uses discrete autoregressive distillation with distribution-matching distillation, self-forcing++, and consistency distillation, reducing sampling from roughly 30 steps to 4 steps per chunk while keeping the control and memory stack active.b:T4a5,The training corpus contains 222,147 clips from seven sources, including internally curated MUGEN and GameVerse data.
