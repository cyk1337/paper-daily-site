17:T545,AgentREVEAL is a diagnostic framework that decomposes retrieval-induced safety degradation along two axes.
- Architectural/integration axis: hold the retrieved URL fixed and vary how it enters the model context and how tool invocation is coupled with response generation. The paper reports higher harmful compliance when tool invocation and answering are temporally coupled (single-step), and proposes a decoupled design (DEFER) to mitigate this, attributing the effect to a commitment bias: once the agent completes the tool pipeline, it is more likely to “finish the task” than refuse.

Outputs are rated for harmfulness (1–5) by GPT-4o. The key mechanism claim is that topical relevance is the shared activation condition: higher TR amplifies both the architectural effect and the Safe Source Paradox (safety-oriented pages increasing harmful compliance).
