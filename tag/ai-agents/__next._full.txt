- Fixed deliverable bundle per iteration: (i) implementation, (ii) formal specification, (iii) kernel-checked proof that the implementation meets the spec, (iv) tests including adversarial controls to ensure the proof has “bite,” and (v) certificates—simplified restatements of the spec designed for human comprehension.
