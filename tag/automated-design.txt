18:T467,AgentFactory formulates an agentic system \(s\) as a pair drawn from a joint space \(\mathcal{S} = \mathcal{M} \times \mathcal{W}\):
- Model space \(\mathcal{M}\): includes selecting a foundation model from an available set, plus optional instruction fine-tuning choices—dataset \(\mathcal{D}\), hyperparameters \(\mathcal{H}\), and tuning method \(\phi\) (LoRA/QLoRA/full FT). For closed models, fine-tuning is disabled (empty \(d,h\)).
- Workflow space \(\mathcal{W}\): can be graphs, neural workflow representations, or executable code.
- Multi-objective evaluation: a vector of metrics \(\mathbf{g}(s_i)\) computed by evaluation functions \(\mathbf{G} = \{G_1,\dots,G_p\}\) (accuracy, cost, latency, etc.).
- Optimization: because the space is non-differentiable and partially observable, the framework uses an “LLM optimizer” to propose iterative modifications based on the history (trajectory) of candidate systems and their metric vectors.
