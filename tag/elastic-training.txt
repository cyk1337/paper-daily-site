17:T456,DynaTrain’s key idea is the Virtual Parameter Space (VPS): represent parameters/gradients/optimizer states as coordinates in an unsharded global logical space, with partition metadata. Any concrete parallelism layout becomes a deterministic projection from VPS to each rank’s local sub-region. Switching layouts is then reduced to computing geometric intersections between source and destination sub-VPS regions, avoiding a combinatorial set of bespoke conversion rules.
- State Routing Planner: computes per-rank send/recv/retain sets purely in VPS coordinates.
- State Transition Engine: lowers the plan into an optimized, memory-aware execution pipeline—packing fragments into contiguous buffers, promoting P2P to collectives when possible, and enforcing a provably deadlock-free schedule.
- Elastic Device Manager: decouples logical strategies from physical process groups and overlaps new-world communicator initialization with ongoing training to hide topology-reconfiguration latency.
