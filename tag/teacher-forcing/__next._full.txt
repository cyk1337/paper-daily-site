17:T523,The method has a training component and an inference component. On the training side, Multi-block Teacher Forcing concatenates a clean prefix with a bounded group of consecutive noisy blocks, instead of corrupting only the current block. Noisy blocks inside the group can attend to one another through a Group-Aware Dual-Stream Mask, while preserving block-causal generation constraints. The dual-stream design separates the prediction view from the conditioning content view, so the model can use neighboring noisy blocks without leaking the target token. MultiTF also uses a more aggressive and randomized noise scheduler inside each group, creating heterogeneous slot-wise noise patterns similar to inference: front slots may be nearly solved while later slots remain heavily masked. Blocks are partitioned into groups with varying sizes so the model sees different running-set sizes and relative slot positions. On the inference side, the Block Buffer keeps a fixed number of physical block slots. Future blocks enter by activating idle slots, and completed front blocks are committed to the KV cache before their slots are reused. This keeps input shape static, supports CUDA Graph replay, preserves prefix caching, and turns higher TPF into wall-clock throughput rather than only a nominal parallelism metric.18:T756,The method unifies two complementary views. At the objective level, consistency models are treated as forward-divergence-like, trajectory-preserving, and mode-covering, whereas DMD is treated as reverse-divergence-like and directly aligned with the student-generated distribution. At the causal training level, teacher-forcing plays the role of stable offline supervision on teacher trajectories, while self-forcing plays the role of on-policy refinement under autoregressive rollouts.

Causal-rCM combines them as teacher-forcing CM plus self-forcing DMD. In the first part, the student learns few-step causal distillation on offline causal contexts and teacher trajectories, which gives a stable initialization under correct histories. In the second part, the student is optimized on its own rollouts so that the distilled few-step sampler better matches inference-time behavior. The paper argues that this complementarity can be realized either jointly or in a staged forward-to-reverse schedule.

A useful part of the paper is its systematic treatment of initialization for self-forcing DMD. It compares several existing choices, including ODE-pair regression, diffusion-forcing-style causal adaptation, and hybrid TF/DF initialization, against TF-CM initialization. The claimed takeaway is that reverse/on-policy optimization alone is too brittle when coverage is poor, while a forward/offline consistency stage provides a much better basin for subsequent DMD refinement.

The engineering layer is also nontrivial. The authors implement teacher-forcing continuous-time consistency models, including sCM and MeanFlow variants, in an autoregressive causal-mask setting. The custom-mask FlashAttention-2 JVP kernel is the key systems piece that makes this practical. That is likely the most transferable part for researchers working on alternative language-modeling paradigms.19:T49f,From the provided full-text portion, three experimental claims are clear. First, training efficiency: teacher-forcing continuous-time CM converges about 10x faster than discrete-time CM. This is the most concrete quantitative result visible in the available text. Second, the paper performs a systematic study of initialization strategies for self-forcing DMD and argues that TF-CM initialization is more stable and more suitable as a unified recipe. Third, the overall method is presented as a leading open recipe for AR diffusion distillation in streaming video generation and interactive world models.
