Two stabilizers are central. Positive-advantage gating activates or strengthens the distillation signal only when the verifier-side advantage is positive, reducing the chance that the teacher reinforces bad trajectories. A warmup-decay schedule for the distillation coefficient gives dense guidance early, then gradually hands control back to the verifier objective later, limiting teacher-induced ceiling effects.
