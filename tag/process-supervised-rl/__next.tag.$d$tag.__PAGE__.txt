9:T76e,The method has three stages. Stage one is MOSAIC trajectory synthesis. Starting from extended-thinking traces, the authors convert linear text reasoning into interleaved trajectories composed of deliberate thinking, action, and observation. Actions can be intermediate code snippets or final answers. The important part is not merely inserting tool-call markers. MOSAIC injects stochastic adversarial perturbations so that originally smooth trajectories become failure-prone and require recovery. In effect, the synthesized data contains replanning, self-correction, and strategic adaptation instead of only idealized successful runs.

Stage two is behavioral initialization through cold-start SFT. This stage teaches the protocol of alternating thought, action, and observation. Its role is mainly to align the model to the interaction format and basic behavior pattern before RL, reducing the search burden in a large action space.

Stage three is process-supervised RL. Built on GRPO, the paper introduces an Action-Centric Process Reward that provides dense turn-level supervision rather than only final-answer reward. From the method description, the reward is intended to score not just whether the final answer is correct, but whether intermediate deliberation is sensible, tool invocation is precise, and the model reacts appropriately to new observations. Compared with outcome-only RL, this is a better fit for multi-step tool use because many failures are caused by early bad actions, and sparse terminal rewards make credit assignment too weak.

The central claim is interleaved deliberation: effective tool use requires local planning and self-correction at every think–act–observe turn, not a one-shot plan followed by blind execution. Still, the likely experimental structure is clear. First, there should be headline comparisons against SFT-only baselines, outcome-reward RL baselines, and probably standard ReAct-style tool-use methods. Third, there should be efficiency analyses: whether the gains come from better decision quality at key steps or simply from spending many more reasoning tokens.
