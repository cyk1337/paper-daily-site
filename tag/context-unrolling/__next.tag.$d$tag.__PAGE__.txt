There are four notable ingredients. First, the training data is not limited to understanding and generation pairs; it also includes reasoning-oriented multimodal content such as long-form descriptions, dense attributes, spatial grounding, depth maps, and camera transformations. This is meant to expose the model to trajectories where intermediate structure is produced before the final answer. Second, the model introduces a hidden reasoning space, a dedicated latent space intended to hold intermediate states that may not be best expressed directly as natural language or pixels. This differs from plain chain-of-thought because it aims to provide a modality-agnostic internal interface that can still interact with other modalities. Third, inference is framed as iterative context construction: atomic primitives such as describe, predict pose, roll out visual tokens, synthesize a novel view, and estimate depth are treated as read-write operations over a shared context. The model progressively expands context and then decodes conditioned on that richer workspace. Fourth, the architecture is a unified MoE model with 3B active parameters and any-to-any multimodal capability.
