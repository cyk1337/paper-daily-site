9:T481,Key observation: lower entropy of early-layer image–text attention correlates with better grounding coverage and higher VQA accuracy.
1) Image–text entropy: from layer k, obtain visual tokens and text tokens; compute a prompt-relevant distribution via temperature-scaled softmax over CLIP similarities, then compute Shannon entropy H_S = -\sum p\log(p+\epsilon). High entropy indicates dispersed attention, consistent with fragmented or noisy prompt-relevant regions.
2) ECS (Entropy-based Cropping Strategy, inference): evaluate a small set of candidate crops/aspect ratios; compute H_S using very early layers (k=1 in ECS) and pick the crop with minimal entropy; combine the selected crop with the original image as the final input.
3) ERT (Entropy Regularization Training, fine-tuning): add entropy as a regularizer to the standard next-token loss: L = L_NTP + \alpha H_S (k=2 in ERT). The paper argues this sharpens attention on relevant tokens (p_i > 1/e) while suppressing irrelevant ones (p_i < 1/e), loosely analogous to negative-sample suppression in contrastive learning.
