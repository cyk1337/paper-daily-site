(1) Gradient-boundary verification: the provider records weight and gradient-related evidence at intervals, and the owner locally replays a small sample of steps on potentially heterogeneous hardware. Observed gradient deviations are compared against an empirically calibrated percentile boundary derived from short calibration runs, so benign cross-device drift is tolerated while real deviations are flagged.
