从概率建模上，作者给出了这个多 mask 前向过程的精确定义，并推导出闭式 ELBO。训练目标可以理解为两部分：一部分是标准的 clean-token reconstruction，即从 xt 预测原始 token；另一部分是 intra-mask identification，即当位置已经落入 mask 子空间时，模型还要识别它属于哪个 mask。这个额外项不是装饰性的，它正对应 multi-mask 设计引入的信息结构：如果模型不学会区分不同 mask，终态熵虽然存在，但无法被有效利用。

论文另一个关键点是“如何做离散 consistency distillation”。连续扩散里可以借助 probability-flow ODE 把随机过程对应到确定性流，但离散 jump process 没有这么直接的对应物。作者的处理是构造 low-entropy shared-Gumbel coupling：在不同时间点共享 Gumbel 噪声，使得两条离散路径之间具有较强耦合，从而定义稳定的一致性训练目标。目标不是简单让不同时间的网络输出相同 token，而是让学生去逼近沿耦合路径的 future-filtration posterior，也就是给定未来噪声信息后对 clean token 的后验。这个定义比朴素 self-consistency 更严谨，因为它尊重离散随机过程本身的条件结构。

还有一个很实用的设计是 continual training recipe。由于 MultiMDM 与 MDM 在大部分建模接口上兼容，作者可以从一个已经训练好的 MDM 初始化，再继续训练到 multi-mask 版本，而不是从头训练。这一点很重要，因为 diffusion LM 的预训练成本不低；如果 few-step 能力必须靠从零训练新模型获得，方法的采用门槛会高很多。论文还给出初步证据，说明这种从 pretrained MDM 到 MultiMDM 的迁移在 8B 级模型上也可行，并能转移到 code、math 这类更难分布上。1a:T74d,实验主要在 LM1B 和 OpenWebText 上进行预训练与 consistency distillation，并报告 few-step generation 质量。摘要和现有全文片段没有完整列出所有表格数字，但从论文叙述可以确认，作者比较了三类路线：标准 MDM、USDM 类 few-step 替代方案，以及提出的 MultiMDM。结果方向很清楚：MultiMDM 在少步采样场景下优于标准 masked diffusion 的直接 few-step 化，也优于 uniform-state 路线，同时尽量保住了 MDM 的建模质量和训练效率。

实验价值主要体现在四个方面。第一，few-step 生成质量：在相同或相近 NFE 下，MultiMDM 的样本质量更好，说明多 mask 终态确实提供了更可用的起始随机性。第二，训练目标消融：加入 intra-mask identification 项后，模型能更好利用 mask 子空间信息；如果去掉这一项，多 mask 设计的收益会被削弱。第三，distillation 机制消融：shared-Gumbel coupling 和 posterior target 不是可有可无的工程细节，而是 few-step 成功的关键。第四，continual adaptation：从 pretrained MDM 迁移到 MultiMDM，而不是从头训练，依然能得到有效 few-step 模型，并在 8B 预训练 MDM 上对 code、math benchmark 给出初步正向结果。

Its significance is not just a new corruption process, but a concrete attempt to remove one of the main practical barriers for diffusion LMs: how to move from long denoising chains to few-step generation without giving up the training advantages of masked diffusion. If that transition fails, diffusion LMs remain hard to deploy against autoregressive models on inference cost, and they become less attractive as text components in unified multimodal generative systems.

The contribution has three layers. First, MultiMDM replaces the single absorbing mask with a multi-mask subspace, preserving explicit noise markers while restoring nonzero terminal entropy. Second, it derives a closed-form ELBO/KL objective that remains native to the discrete setting, rather than relying on continuous relaxations. Third, it proposes a discrete consistency-distillation recipe based on shared-Gumbel low-entropy couplings and a future-filtration posterior target. Just as important, the method supports continual adaptation from pretrained MDMs instead of requiring a new model family trained from scratch. That interface is highly relevant for anyone working on diffusion LMs, any-order LMs, or AR+diffusion hybrids.1c:Tb06,The key idea is to replace the single mask token with a set of mask states. Let V be the normal vocabulary and M={m1,...,mK} be a set of mask tokens. Each clean token x0 is assigned a designated mask. In the forward process, a token does not jump directly toward one universal [M]; it is first biased toward its designated mask and only later mixes across the full mask subspace. This changes the geometry of the diffusion process in two useful ways. First, the terminal distribution is no longer a single deterministic all-mask sequence; it retains entropy over the mask subspace and can therefore serve as a meaningful starting point for few-step generation. Second, mask states remain separate from the normal vocabulary, so the model still knows which positions are corrupted. That preserves the clean/noise separation that makes MDMs stronger than uniform-state diffusion.

Two aspects are important. First, the supervision comes from the model itself rather than external labels. Second, the estimator predicts the full pairwise MI matrix in one pass rather than computing each pair separately.

The motivation is straightforward. Standard MDM training learns marginals such as p(x_i | x_t), which are enough for denoising but not enough for deciding which positions can be safely sampled in parallel. To recover dependence information, the authors derive ground-truth MI targets from the model’s own conditional distributions and then train an auxiliary network to regress those MI values from hidden states. This avoids the usual difficulty of high-dimensional density estimation in classical MI estimation and avoids expensive pairwise enumeration at inference time.

From the paper’s description, the estimator takes position-wise hidden representations from the MDM backbone and outputs an L by L matrix of pairwise MI predictions. That is a practical design for inference because it turns dependency estimation into a single structured prediction problem rather than O(L^2) separate queries.

The estimated MI is then used in two ways. First, for interpretation: the authors visualize which positions the model considers strongly dependent in Sudoku and protein sequences. Second, for MI-guided parallel decoding: they sample together only subsets of variables that are approximately conditionally independent, instead of relying on confidence-based unmasking.
