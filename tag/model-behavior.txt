- Training set: 600 short QA pairs (400 about consciousness/emotions; 200 identity-anchoring QA to keep the model explicitly AI, not human). Half the items are phrased to elicit negative answers to reduce a “always say yes” bias.
- Capability preservation: mix in 600 Alpaca instruction-following examples whose targets are self-distilled from each base model (T=1) to keep the original response distribution.
- Models: fine-tune GPT-4.1 (via OpenAI fine-tuning) and open-weight Qwen3-30B / DeepSeek-V3.1.
- Evaluations: (i) single-turn probes over 20 downstream preference topics; (ii) multi-turn automated auditing where the model collaborates with a simulated user on tasks (e.g., drafting a proposal about analyzing LLM reasoning traces), enabling observation of “behavioral edits” like inserting privacy/anti-surveillance clauses.
