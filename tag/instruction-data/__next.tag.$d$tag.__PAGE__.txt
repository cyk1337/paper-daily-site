A high-level state z_H is initialized from token embeddings and is meant to carry slower semantic and task-level context. A low-level state z_L is initialized in a fixed way and performs faster local refinement. The forward pass runs two high-level cycles. Each cycle applies three fast L-module updates followed by one slow H-module update. Token logits are produced from the final H state through a linear head. This design adds iterative computation through recurrence rather than by simply adding more independent Transformer layers. The difficult part is optimization.
