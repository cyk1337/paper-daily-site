第一是 exactness。算法仍然保证最终输出分布与直接从 target model 自回归采样完全一致，不是近似采样。也就是说，加速不以分布偏移为代价。

第二是 drafter invariance。论文强调其 list-coupling without communication 结构，使得接受机制对具体 drafter 的某些实现细节不敏感。这个性质在 watermark 场景尤其重要，因为如果水印嵌入方式和某个特定 drafter 强绑定，系统会更脆弱，也更难分析检测统计量。

第三是 watermarkability。作者不是在 speculative sampling 之后再叠加水印，而是直接利用 Poisson process 中的 keyed randomness 嵌入可恢复统计信号。论文声称该水印是 unbiased 的，即不会改变目标分布；同时水印强度不会随着采样效率提升而同步恶化。这里的含义是：在他们的构造下，增加 multi-draft 带来的接受率收益，不必像已有 no-go 结果那样直接拿水印强度去交换。

第四是理论比较对象。论文把 Rowan et al. 2025 作为主要 baseline，并声称在理论上证明了更高 acceptance rate。结合引言表述，作者并没有说自己绕开了 Hu and Huang 2024 的不可能性结论，而是明确指出那个结论对固定 draft 数仍成立；他们做的是沿着 draft 数这个额外自由度改善 frontier。这一点很重要，说明方法创新来自问题重参数化，而不是对原结论的反驳。

Here, the algorithm generates and verifies multiple draft continuations and uses Poisson processes to build an exact list coupling. The novelty is not merely having multiple drafts; it is that Poissonization gives a coupling structure with several desirable properties.

First, exactness. The final output distribution is exactly the same as direct autoregressive sampling from the target model. This is not an approximation scheme.

Second, drafter invariance. The paper emphasizes a list-coupling-without-communication construction, which makes the acceptance mechanism less tied to a particular drafter implementation. That matters even more under watermarking, because a watermark that depends too strongly on one drafter can be brittle and harder to analyze statistically.

Third, natural watermarkability. The watermark is not bolted on after speculative decoding. Instead, keyed randomness is embedded directly into the Poisson-process sampling procedure, producing a recoverable statistical signal while keeping the target law unbiased. The claimed benefit is that watermark strength does not deteriorate simply because sampling efficiency improves. In other words, the efficiency gain from multi-draft verification does not have to be paid for by weakening the watermark in the same way as in earlier impossibility-style results.

Fourth, the theoretical comparison point is Rowan et al. 2025. The authors claim a provably higher acceptance rate than that method. Importantly, they do not claim to invalidate the no-go theorem of Hu and Huang 2024. They explicitly state that the trade-off still applies for a fixed number of drafts. Their contribution is to improve the frontier by introducing the number of drafts as an additional degree of freedom.

Mechanistically, the Poisson process plays two roles: it provides an analyzable random mechanism for candidate generation and exact coupling, and it also provides a natural source of keyed randomness for watermarking.
