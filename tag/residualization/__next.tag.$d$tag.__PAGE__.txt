9:T893,方法上，这篇工作几乎没有把重点放在“发明一个更强的残差化器”上，而是建立了一套证据框架。作者固定了 measurement setup：需要一个原始分数、若干 surface features，以及一个与这些表面特征来源分离的 construct measurement。然后用同一个 residualization operator，在三种证据情境下分别检验它能支持什么主张。

第一种是 designed intervention。代码审计场景里，作者利用 unit-test correctness 作为外部构念标签，并只通过 comments/docstrings 改写来操纵格式通道，保证语义和正确性不变。这样就能直接测“格式效应是否被 attenuate”。这里 residualization 的角色不是生成新 gold score，而是作为审计探针：如果调整后，镜像格式条件下的偏好差距缩小，同时 correctness margin 基本不变，那么可以说它削弱了该格式通道。

第二种是 observational evidence。这里没有保标签干预，只能根据 surface-only predictor 的误差来定义某个 slice，再看 residualized score 在这个 slice 上是否与外部标注更一致。作者强调，这种设计只能支持 slice-restricted alignment gain，不能外推到总体有效性。原因很直接：slice 本身就是按某个模型的错误模式定义的，调整后在 slice 内变好，不代表总体更好，也不代表构念更纯。

第三种是 construct–artifact entanglement。作者构造并分析了构念与表面特征相关的情形，说明 decorrelation、loading 提升、slice gain 都不足以识别 validity restoration。换句话说，如果“好答案通常更长、更有注释、更像某种风格”本身就是任务分布的一部分，那么把这部分减掉，可能连有用信号一起减掉。

全文还有一个重要点：作者把 residualization 明确定位为 audit perturbation，而不是 repaired score。这一点和很多长度控制 win rate、judge debiasing 实践不同。作者还给出一套 reporting protocol，并允许 explicit refusal：证据不够时，就不声称分数被修复。这种写法比常见的“做了控制变量所以更公平”要严格得多。a:T7d0,The paper is organized around an evidence framework rather than a new estimator. The setup fixes three ingredients: an original score, a set of surface features, and a provenance-separated construct measurement. The same residualization operator is then examined under three evidential regimes.

First, in the designed-intervention regime, the authors use a code audit where unit-test execution provides correctness labels and only comments/docstrings manipulate the formatting channel. Because the intervention preserves correctness while changing format, attenuation of the format effect becomes directly measurable. Here residualization is used as an audit perturbation: if mirrored formatting conditions move closer after adjustment while correctness margins remain stable, one can claim attenuation of that specific surface channel.

Second, in the observational regime, no such intervention exists. The authors define slices using the errors of a surface-only predictor and test whether the residualized score aligns better with external annotations on that slice. The key point is that this supports only a slice-restricted alignment claim. Since the slice is itself induced by a model of surface effects, improvement inside the slice does not identify broader validity or better population-level measurement.

Third, the paper studies construct–artifact entanglement. When the target construct is genuinely correlated with the artifact, subtracting the predictable surface component can remove construct-relevant signal together with nuisance variation. In that case, lower correlation with artifacts, better loading diagnostics, or even slice gains do not identify validity restoration.

A central conceptual move is to treat residualization as an audit tool rather than a repaired score. That framing is stricter than common practice in length-controlled win rates or judge debiasing, and it leads to a reporting protocol with an explicit refusal option when the evidence is insufficient.b:T6a5,The most concrete experiment is the code preference audit. On MBPP-style pairs containing a terse correct solution and a commented buggy solution, a public preference reward model selects the correct answer only 0.507 of the time, essentially chance. When the verbosity–correctness pairing is reversed, the same model selects the correct answer 0.615 of the time, showing a substantial format effect. Under the designed intervention, residualization attenuates the format effect by about 0.12 on both correct and buggy code, shrinking the mirrored-preference gap from 0.108 to 0.037 while leaving correctness margins largely unchanged. Length-only corrections fail to close that gap, indicating that the issue is not reducible to raw length alone.

The paper also stresses the limits of this positive result. Across three held-out edit templates, the same operator overshoots one template, attenuates another, and amplifies a third. So even in a controlled setting, the effect of residualization is template- and channel-dependent.

In the observational analyses, held-out and disjoint-annotator tests support only a slice-level alignment gain. Every metric outside the declared slice declines after adjustment, including full-population alignment, the observable flagged subgroup, and within-question ranking. This is one of the strongest empirical points in the paper.

The construct–artifact entanglement section shows an even sharper failure mode: measured loading can improve while the adjusted judge becomes less useful.
