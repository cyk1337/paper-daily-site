9:T469,1) Diagnostic metric: Proposes dimension-corrected Principal-Subspace Overlap. For each pretrained linear weight, SVD is performed to extract top-k left and right singular vectors as principal subspaces. The stepwise realized update (ΔW, including effects of optimizer, learning rate, and momentum) is decomposed into four orthogonal blocks, and the expected overlap under an isotropic null hypothesis is subtracted to obtain excess overlap. 2) Optimization method: Proposes GCPO (Geometrically Constrained Policy Optimization). During each rollout iteration, a hard bilateral orthogonal constraint is applied to the effective update of each adapted layer—forcing updates to lie in the bilateral orthogonal complement of the selected pretrained principal subspaces. This guarantees that the layer's response to inputs in the pretrained principal input subspace remains completely unchanged, while retaining a high-dimensional complement space for task adaptation.
