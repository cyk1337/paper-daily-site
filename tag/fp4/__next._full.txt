1a:T405,Mix-Quant applies NVFP4 weight-and-activation quantization only during prefilling, while keeping decoding in BF16. The rationale is straightforward but well argued. Prefill processes a fixed context in parallel and is compute-bound, so low-bit matmuls can directly reduce latency. Decode is autoregressive and more sensitive because each generated token becomes future input; quantization error therefore propagates across the trajectory. The paper also argues that agentic contexts contain substantial redundancy—conversation history, tool outputs, retrieved passages, environment state—so prefill can tolerate more approximation. Compared with weight-only INT4 PTQ, this approach can accelerate compute-heavy prefill rather than only memory-bound decode. Compared with full-path low-bit quantization, it avoids error accumulation in generation.
