Direct pixel-space unified modeling is harder than latent-space modeling, and the model can otherwise overfit to low-level local statistics instead of learning robust visual semantics. The masking mechanism introduces a feature-learning bias that encourages the model to recover or model semantically meaningful visual structure under partial observation. In effect, it injects a masked-visual-modeling style signal into unified pixel-space pretraining, improving stability and representation quality.

Conceptually, Tuna-2 differs from the dominant “strong vision encoder plus LLM” recipe. It is closer to a monolithic decoder-only multimodal model where perception is not outsourced to a separately pretrained encoder. Compared with VAE-latent generation, it avoids the information bottleneck and the mismatch between understanding and generation objectives.
