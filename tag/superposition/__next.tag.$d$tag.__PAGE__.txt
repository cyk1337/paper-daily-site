5:Tab4,技术路线分成四层，而且每一层都围绕“把几何恢复和因果有效性拆开”展开。

第一层是构造完全可控的 ground-truth superposition toy model。作者从头训练一个很小的合成模型，不用预训练权重，也不用外部数据，使得真实 feature 方向、隐藏维度、稀疏度和 superposition 几何都已知。这样做的目的不是追求 realism，而是让“恢复是否正确”以及“干预是否沿真实路径生效”都能被严格定义。

第二层是复现并修正已有 SAE 几何结论。论文多 seed 复现了 Elhage 等人的 superposition phase diagram，并指出一个重要 artifact：在高 sparsity 区域，若训练步数不够，会把 under-training 误判成 superposition 结构。作者用 sparsity-aware step budgets 解决这个问题。除此之外，论文还描述了一个此前没有被充分刻画的 diffuse sharing regime：在极端 overcompleteness 下，每个 feature 都被“相当程度地表示”，但 feature dimensionality 很低，说明表示不是少数原子清晰分工，而是更弥散地共享字典容量。

第三层是复现 TopK SAE 与 L1 SAE 的比较，并把“L1 shrinkage”从口头说法变成直接测量。作者从头复现 TopK-vs-L1 Pareto，对 activation refinement 做了直接测试，量化 L1 正则导致的 shrinkage，而不是只凭经验判断。这里的关键点是：decoder recovery 看起来相近时，encoder 激活行为可能已经明显不同，这正是后面因果分歧的来源之一。

第四层是提出一套 causal validation battery，并把 inertness 细分成可操作的类型。作者对每个 correlationally recovered feature 做两类干预：ablation 和 sign-correct steering，并把影响一路传播到模型真实输出层，而不是停留在 SAE reconstruction 空间。基于这些干预，论文区分了两条轴。按成因分，有 structural inertness 和 competitive inertness。前者来自 antipodal-pair superposition geometry 与 positive-pass encoder 的结构性错配，即使 SAE 训练更好也未必消失；后者来自 TopK 竞争机制下退化字典的竞争病理，并且和数值边界不稳定性耦合。按方向分，有 read-inertness 和 write-inertness：前者表示 ablation 监测不到，后者表示 steering 无法写入有效影响。论文指出 antipodal pairs 可以把这两者完全解耦。

此外，作者还把这套审计流程封装成 sae-causal-audit 工具，接口只要求对象能 encode、decode 并暴露 dictionary，因此理论上可审计任意 SAE 实现，包括 sae_lens.SAE。工具设计里还专门处理了 sign-handling bug 这一类常见错误，使其在接口层面不易发生。6:T9d8,实验部分信息量很高，且不少结论都带具体数字。

第一，superposition phase diagram 的复现不是单次跑通，而是多 seed 统计。作者发现高 sparsity 区域存在收敛伪象：训练不够时会看起来像某种 superposition 几何，实际只是没收敛。这个问题通过 sparsity-aware step budget 解决。论文还报告了 diffuse sharing regime 的量化特征：在极端 overcompleteness 下，每个 feature 的表示强度大致为 ||W_i||^2 ≈ 1.40，而 feature dimensionality 约为 D_i ≈ 0.08，说明 feature 普遍被表示，但每个 feature 占用的“维度份额”很小。

第二，TopK-vs-L1 复现部分，作者不仅比较 Pareto，还直接测 activation refinement，验证 L1 shrinkage 的存在。虽然当前给出的正文片段没有完整表格数字，但结论很明确：仅看 decoder 对齐会高估 L1 SAE 的恢复质量，因为 encoder 激活被 shrinkage 扭曲后，因果效应会进一步偏离。

第三，也是最关键的，因果验证结果显示 correlational recovery 和 causal efficacy 可以明显分离。在一个 degraded SAE 上，22 个几何匹配到的 feature 里有 17 个是 causally inert；在一个 well-trained SAE 上，同样是 22 个匹配里只有 2 个 inert。更强的一点是，inert match 的 cosine 可以高到 0.9998，说明“几何几乎完美”并不能保证“因果上有效”。这基本击穿了只用 cosine recovery 做结论的做法。

第四，真实模型小规模审计。作者拿一个已发布的 production SAE，gpt2-small-res-jb，对 83 个手工编写、跨多个语义域的 concept 做审计，恢复出 7 个 correlationally matched feature，其中 1 个，也就是 14%，是 causally inert。样本不大，但方向上和 toy setting 一致。论文还发现一个意外现象：少数 decoder atoms 会反复成为几十个语义上互不相关 concept 的最近邻匹配。作者用三组独立构造、规模和多样性逐步增加的 concept batch 复现了这个 atom collision 现象，并用受控负例排除了 prompt template artifact 的解释。

第五，复现性实验。作者在 CI 调试中说明了为什么浮点训练跨平台做不到 byte-exact reproducibility，并提出两层保证：固定环境内做 SHA-256 级别的字节一致；跨环境则要求 semantic equality，数值容差 rtol=1e-4，边界敏感的整数计数允许 ±1。这个部分不是主结果，但对做 SAE 或小模型复现实验的人很实用。7:Taa1,The technical program has four layers, all built around separating geometric recovery from causal validity.

First, the authors construct a fully controlled ground-truth superposition model. It is a small synthetic model trained from scratch, with no pretrained weights and no external data. The point is not realism but identifiability: true feature directions, sparsity, hidden dimensionality, and superposition geometry are all known, so both recovery and intervention can be evaluated rigorously.

Second, they reproduce and refine prior geometric results. The paper reproduces the Elhage et al. superposition phase diagram with multi-seed statistics and identifies an important artifact: at high sparsity, insufficient training can masquerade as superposition structure. This is corrected with sparsity-aware step budgets. The paper also describes a less-discussed diffuse sharing regime at extreme overcompleteness, where every feature is substantially represented while each feature occupies only a small effective dimensional share.

Third, the paper reproduces the TopK-vs-L1 SAE comparison and turns the usual claim about L1 shrinkage into a direct measurement. Instead of asserting that L1 regularization distorts activations, the authors run an activation-refinement test that quantifies the shrinkage. This is important because similar-looking decoder recovery can coexist with very different encoder behavior, which later shows up as causal mismatch.

Fourth, the paper introduces a causal validation battery and a taxonomy of inertness. For every correlationally recovered feature, the authors run ablation and sign-correct steering interventions and propagate the effect through the model’s actual output stage rather than stopping at the SAE reconstruction space. This leads to two orthogonal taxonomies. By cause, there is structural inertness and competitive inertness. Structural inertness arises systematically from antipodal-pair superposition geometry combined with a positive-pass encoder, and better SAE training does not necessarily remove it. Competitive inertness is a TopK competition pathology in degraded dictionaries and is tied to numerical boundary instability. By direction, there is read-inertness and write-inertness: ablation can fail to reveal a feature even when steering can affect it, and steering can fail even when ablation suggests relevance. Antipodal pairs can fully dissociate these two notions.

The work is also packaged into a model-agnostic auditing tool, sae-causal-audit. Any object that can encode, decode, and expose its dictionary can be audited, including sae_lens.SAE. The interface is designed to make a documented class of sign-handling bugs hard to express.8:T9ab,The empirical section is unusually rich and several results are numerically sharp.

First, the superposition phase diagram is reproduced with multi-seed statistics rather than a single run. The paper shows that high-sparsity regions suffer from a convergence artifact: under-training can look like a geometric phase transition. This is fixed with sparsity-aware training budgets. The newly described diffuse sharing regime is quantified by values such as ||W_i||^2 ≈ 1.40 and feature dimensionality D_i ≈ 0.08 under extreme overcompleteness, indicating that features are broadly represented but each occupies only a small dimensional share.

Second, in the TopK-vs-L1 reproduction, the paper directly measures activation refinement and confirms L1 shrinkage rather than treating it as folklore. The excerpt provided here does not include the full Pareto tables, but the qualitative conclusion is clear: decoder alignment alone overstates recovery quality when encoder activations are distorted by shrinkage.

Third, and most importantly, causal validation reveals a strong gap between correlational recovery and causal efficacy. In a degraded SAE, 17 of 22 matched features are causally inert. In a well-trained SAE, only 2 of 22 matched features are inert. More strikingly, inert matches can have cosine similarity as high as 0.9998. This is the paper’s strongest result: near-perfect geometric recovery does not guarantee causal usefulness.

Fourth, the authors run a first small-scale audit on a real published SAE, gpt2-small-res-jb. Against 83 hand-authored concepts spanning unrelated semantic domains, they recover 7 correlational matches, of which 1, or 14%, is causally inert. The sample is small, but the direction is consistent with the toy setting. They also report an unexpected atom-collision phenomenon: a small number of decoder atoms repeatedly appear as nearest matches for dozens of semantically unrelated concepts. This is replicated across three independently constructed concept batches of increasing size and diversity, and a controlled negative case argues against a prompt-template artifact.

Fifth, the reproducibility section explains why byte-exact reproducibility across platforms is unavailable for floating-point training workloads. The paper proposes a two-tier guarantee: SHA-256 byte exactness within one pinned environment, and semantic equality elsewhere with rtol = 1e-4 and boundary-sensitive integer counts allowed to vary by ±1.9:T755,This is worth a careful read. Start with the central claim and keep it in mind throughout: decoder geometry and causal validity are different empirical statements. Once that is clear, the paper’s structure becomes straightforward.

On a first pass, read the Introduction and the contribution list to see that this is not just another toy SAE reproduction. Then jump directly to the causal validation section, especially the ablation and sign-correct steering protocol. Focus on how interventions are propagated to the model’s actual output layer and how inertness is operationally defined. That is the conceptual center of the paper.

Next, go back to the superposition phase-diagram reproduction. Pay attention to how the high-sparsity convergence artifact is diagnosed, how training budgets are adjusted with sparsity, and what characterizes the diffuse sharing regime. This helps explain why geometrically recovered features can still fail at the encoder or competition stage.

Then read the TopK-vs-L1 reproduction and the activation-refinement test. The key question there is not simply which SAE reconstructs better, but which one recovers features that remain causally actionable. If you usually look only at reconstruction, sparsity, or cosine metrics, this section fills in the missing encoder side.

After that, read the taxonomy and tooling sections. Structural vs competitive inertness and read- vs write-inertness are useful concepts because they turn intervention failure into something diagnosable. Finish with the real-model audit on gpt2-small-res-jb and the atom-collision finding to judge whether the toy conclusions already have echoes in practical SAEs.
