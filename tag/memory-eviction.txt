1b:T4ec,1) Estimation framing: each cached item x has latent utility u(x), roughly how much keeping it reduces future loss; an eviction policy is an estimator û(x).
2) Commit lag H as the key degree of freedom:
- H=0 covers existing approaches that decide at arrival: filtering (past-only signals like recency or accumulated attention) and prediction (learned guesses about future reuse).
- H→∞ corresponds to Belady’s offline optimum.
3) Fixed-lag smoothing: delay the keep/evict commitment by H steps so near-future evidence of reuse is observed rather than predicted, trading off buffer memory and latency.

5) Resulting policy: RMM, a training-free eviction rule that strictly generalizes H2O; additionally, a small model is trained to predict an ordering that is then measured/confirmed under the lag.
