9:T579,The approach combines architecture, data, and training-objective design. First, the models target f16 and f32 spatial compression. An image is mapped to a latent tensor of shape H/f × W/f × C. Compared with f8, f16 reduces latent sequence length by about 4× and the quadratic attention term by about 16×; f32 reduces sequence length by about 16× and the quadratic attention term by about 256×. Second, the VAE expands the latent channel dimension to relieve the information bottleneck and introduces Global Skip Connections from pixels to latents, aiming to preserve small details such as characters, strokes, lines, and layout cues. Third, the training data is scaled to billions of images, with a dedicated document corpus covering papers, posters, slides, web pages, and related text-rich sources. A synthetic rendering pipeline supplies dense character-level supervision for document reconstruction. Fourth, the latent space is regularized through semantic alignment with intermediate DINOv2 features. The training uses a staged alignment schedule: stronger semantic organization early on, then a more balanced optimization between reconstruction and generation compatibility. The backbone is asymmetric and attention-free, with a lightweight encoder to reduce encoding overhead during diffusion training and an attention-free design to keep throughput high for ultra-high-resolution inputs.a:T475,The evaluation is organized around reconstruction fidelity and latent diffusability.
