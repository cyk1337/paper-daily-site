Near-parallel means that examples remain aligned by source identifier and task intent, while target-language content may be rewritten when literal translation would invalidate the task. The pipeline treats each record as a structured instruction-following instance. A sample-level policy first routes the example to either field-wise machine translation or task-level localization. For examples that depend on linguistic form, such as grammatical errors, rhyme, alliteration, word length, acronym formation, or phonological constraints, the system reconstructs a task-equivalent target-language instance.
