a:T417,VRPO reframes representation alignment as reinforcement-style optimization. The diffusion model is treated as a policy optimized by gradient ascent on a composite reward, replacing REPA’s static similarity loss.
- Reward components:
  (1) Fidelity reward: perceptual quality signals (the paper cites metrics like FID/LPIPS).
  (2) Semantic reward: alignment to pretrained visual embeddings (e.g., DINOv2 feature similarity).
  (3) Stability reward: encourages consistency across denoising timesteps, motivated by REG-like temporal/semantic consistency constraints.
- The practical design goal is “no extra networks, no architecture changes,” keeping compatibility with DiT/SiT. Complexity is pushed into reward construction and adaptive balancing rather than adding teacher branches or new modules.
- The claimed mechanism is adaptive weighting: stronger semantic guidance when under-aligned early, then naturally relaxing as alignment improves, reducing the typical fidelity–alignment tug-of-war
