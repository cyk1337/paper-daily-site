18:T7dc,The setup is deliberately controlled. Each document is a sequence of assignment statements of the form [ent] [attr] [val] [SEP], followed by a query [QUERY] [ent] [attr] [ARROW]; the target is the value currently bound to that slot. Bindings are resampled per document from disjoint vocabularies, and the stream is infinite, so the task cannot be solved by memorizing weights. For the queried slot, an old value v_old is written and then repeated R_old times, later replaced by a new value v_new written once, and the query appears ΔD statements after the final update. Because the current value appears exactly once while the superseded value appears R_old times, “most recent” and “rarest” always select the same answer on training data.

To read out the mechanism, the authors introduce a minimal causal edit: they invert the multiplicities of the competing values while holding the ground truth, total token count, answer position, and all other tokens fixed. They then measure the signed change in answer log-odds, Δ. Positive Δ indicates sensitivity consistent with rarity; negative Δ indicates that repetition boosts the value that should be suppressed. This is stronger than a correlational probe because it directly tests causal sensitivity to one statistic while preserving task semantics.

A second technical contribution is the analysis of an early training shortcut. Before a retrieval circuit forms, models learn to copy a value from a fixed token offset near the end of the document. That strategy depends on layout regularities rather than slot matching plus temporal retrieval. Its accuracy ceiling is predicted by 1/|supp(ΔD)|, and the paper shows the models nearly saturate that ceiling when the support is narrow. Applying the intervention during this phase yields strongly negative Δ, which would look like a clean frequency-sensitive regime if one ignored training stage. The paper uses this to argue that mechanism readouts must be interpreted jointly with training dynamics.19:T613,The main experiments use 26M-parameter transformers, with 75 total runs across redundancy settings. Several findings stand out. First, every analyzed cell reaches held-in accuracy of at least 0.999, including strata where the trivial heuristic “the last update is the answer” fails. So standard evaluation cannot distinguish opposite internal dependencies on repetition count. Second, under the causal edit, the same nominal configuration can separate sharply across seeds: in 13 of 25 cells, the sign fraction differs by more than 0.3 across three seeds, with the largest difference reaching 0.879, while the document-level standard error is only 0.025. That gap is too large to attribute to sampling noise. Third, within a single run, post-formation checkpoints can still vary substantially, with within-run standard deviation up to 0.303; doubling the training schedule also moves cells that the data distribution does not distinguish in advance. By contrast, the timing of retrieval formation remains stable across these comparisons. Fourth, a control edit on a non-queried slot changes the same number of tokens but stays within 0.015 nats, supporting the specificity of the intervention. Fifth, when pooled across seeds, sign fraction increases monotonically with redundancy level through at least R_old = 12, showing that redundancy changes the tendency of the readout without uniquely fixing the mechanism. The paper also explains why R_old = 1 and 2 are excluded and why 16 is the structural upper bound under the document layout constraints.1a:T48d,Start with the setup and the exact equivalence construction. The key point is not that one rule beats another, but that the objective is made exactly indifferent between them. Then focus on the minimal causal edit and the interpretation of the signed log-odds change Δ; that is the central measurement device. After that, read the section on the pre-retrieval fixed-offset copying phase. It is the part that prevents an overly simple reading of the intervention results and gives the paper its strongest training-dynamics insight.

If reading selectively, prioritize: the task generator, the main figure contrasting held-in accuracy with intervention-based separation, the seed/checkpoint/schedule instability evidence, and the control edit. That is enough to get the main message in about 30–45 minutes. For deeper study, the appendices on other candidate rules and the discussion of why timing is stable while mechanism identity is not are worth attention. WildGuard-7B and GuardReasoner, whose training datasets exhibit the audited imbalance, show the strongest vulnerability. LlamaGuard3 and Qwen3Guard also show the shortcut despite undisclosed training distributions. Aegis2-derived guards are much less affected, plausibly because Aegis2 augments safe responses to harmful prompts with more diverse refusals and redirections. The key experimental factorization is a 2×2: recurrent vs non-recurrent computation, and local sequential glimpses vs global full-image input. The main claim is mechanistic: recurrence alone is insufficient—if a recurrent model still receives a global view, it can learn perceptual shortcuts that do not extrapolate. A practical nuance is that glimpse size/resolution acts like an information bottleneck: too large leaks global cues; too small starves the model and destabilizes learning.1d:T9fa,方法叫 TaNOS，由三个部件组成，而且三者各自对应一个失效来源。

第一是 operation sketches。作者不让模型从自然语言问题里自己完整恢复运算模板，而是显式提供一个最小化的运算草图，类似把“同比变化”“差值”“比例”“聚合后再比较”这类计算骨架抽出来，用抽象变量和操作符表示。关键点不在于给模型更多信息，而在于把本来会消耗容量的低层算术模板识别外显化，让模型把注意力放到表中实体定位、行列关系、条件筛选和变量绑定上。论文的立场很明确：这些草图在测试时并非额外 oracle，因为问题本身通常已经隐含了这种结构，只是作者把它显式化，减少模型在 trivial operation inference 上的浪费。

第二是 header anonymization。作者不是做全局统一替换，而是实例级、双射式的 header 映射。每张表的列名被映射成唯一占位 token，既保留同一表内的引用一致性，又切断跨样本的词汇共现统计。这样做比简单删除 header 更稳，因为结构关系还在；也比固定匿名词表更强，因为模型难以在不同样本间重新建立稳定的“某个匿名 token 常对应某类运算”的伪规律。这个设计很像在预训练里故意破坏 spurious lexical anchor，但保留局部结构可解性。

第三是 self-supervised learning，而且是 program-first 而不是 question-first。作者先从表中构造可执行程序，得到确定答案，再让 LLM 把程序和答案表达成自然语言问题。这样生成链路的约束方向与常见做法相反：不是先写问题再猜程序，而是先保证程序正确、答案可执行，再生成语言表述。好处是逻辑一致性高，训练样本可自动验证，减少“问题像真的、程序是错的”这类噪声。结合 operation sketches，模型在训练时看到的是表、匿名 header、问题、草图以及对应程序/答案，从而把监督集中到结构映射而不是词面联想。

从方法差异看，TaNOS 不是单纯的数据增强，也不是普通 CoT 提示。它更接近一种面向结构推理的中间表示工程：用 sketch 约束推理空间，用 anonymization 改变可利用统计规律，用 program-first SSL 提高监督纯度。和只做 SFT 的基线相比，核心区别是训练信号被重新组织了；和直接合成更多 QA 数据相比，核心区别是作者强调 correctness-guaranteed 的可执行监督，而不是规模优先。1e:T831,实验围绕金融表格数值推理展开，主模型是 8B instruction-tuned LLM。论文给出的主结果有几个点。

一是全监督场景下，TaNOS 在 Financial TableQA benchmark 上达到 85.38% execution accuracy，明显高于 fully supervised baseline 的 73.97%。这个提升幅度已经说明它不是只靠多一点数据或 prompt 技巧，而是训练信号组织方式确实改变了泛化行为。

二是低资源场景下，只用 10% 标注数据，TaNOS 仍有 80.13% accuracy，依然超过全监督基线和文中比较的 API 模型。这个结果支持作者关于 SSL 能缓解逻辑监督稀缺的判断，也说明中间训练带来的收益不只是“有更多样本”，而是样本质量和结构偏置更合适。

三是跨域迁移。作者在多个金融数据集之间做 train-on-one, test-on-another 的迁移评估。引言里给了一个典型例子：在 FinQA 上训练的模型，域内 program accuracy 为 68.72%，迁移到 MultiHiertt 降到 43.74%，到 NumReason-500 降到 39.66%。TaNOS 的目标就是缩小这种 gap。论文声称在 domain-shift 实验中，TaNOS 的跨域性能差距只有约 2 个百分点，而普通 SFT 超过 10 个百分点，说明 header 依赖和模板记忆被明显削弱。

四是消融。虽然给出的全文片段没有完整表格，但从方法设计和文中叙述可以看出，三个组件是互补关系：operation sketches 主要改善复杂结构推理和数据效率；header anonymization 主要改善 header-shift 鲁棒性；SSL 主要带来低资源收益和更稳定的迁移。值得注意的是，这类方法若只有 anonymization 而没有 sketch，模型可能因为语义锚点减少而更难学；若只有 sketch 而没有 anonymization，模型仍可能把草图和原始 header 词汇绑定起来。作者强调的是组合效果，而不是单点技巧。

First, operation sketches. Instead of forcing the model to infer the full computational template from the natural-language question, the method provides a minimal symbolic sketch of the operation structure, such as ratio, difference, percentage change, or simple compositions of these. The point is not to inject heavy supervision, but to externalize low-level arithmetic structure so the model can spend more capacity on table grounding: row/column selection, entity binding, conditional filtering, and relational interpretation. The paper explicitly argues that these sketches are not unrealistic test-time oracles, because the question already implies the operation pattern; the sketch simply makes that structure explicit.

Second, header anonymization. This is not a global replacement scheme. It is an instance-level bijective mapping applied separately to each table. The design preserves within-table referential consistency while breaking cross-example lexical statistics. That matters: removing headers entirely would destroy useful structure, while using a fixed anonymization vocabulary could still let the model relearn stable pseudo-correlations. The per-instance mapping is a stronger way to suppress lexical memorization while keeping the task solvable through structure.

Third, self-supervised learning via a program-first pipeline. The authors reverse the usual generation order. They first construct executable programs over tables, obtain verified answers, and only then use an LLM to verbalize them into natural-language questions. This is a key design choice. In many synthetic pipelines, question-first generation leads to semantically plausible but logically inconsistent program annotations. Program-first generation keeps the supervision executable and correctness-guaranteed, which raises the purity of the training signal. During training, the model sees tables, anonymized headers, questions, sketches, and corresponding programs/answers, encouraging alignment between language and structure rather than language and lexical shortcuts.

Relative to prior work, TaNOS is not just data augmentation and not just prompting. It is a representation-and-objective redesign for structural reasoning.
