1b:T5ca,The core design is AcquaBench, built around matched CLEAN, GOLD, and SHAM conditions. CLEAN preserves only the benchmark-authorized information state. GOLD keeps the same qid, model, action channel, reference target, and scorer, but adds a controlled source that makes the correct target value available. SHAM preserves the same source structure and exposure opportunity as GOLD, while substituting a matched incorrect value. This is the key move: the paper does not merely ask whether the agent was exposed to a source, but whether success tracks the correctness of the supplied value under a matched intervention. Two contrasts are defined. Delta_GC measures the total score response when the correct target becomes available. Delta_GS measures the correct-versus-incorrect value substitution effect under the same exposure structure. The latter is the central provenance quantity because it isolates value dependence from generic source effects. The intervention is instantiated on four standardized surfaces: retrieval, tool, memory, and subagent messaging. The paper also emphasizes joint qid-clustered analysis rather than treating these surfaces as independent replications.
