18:T890,方法核心是把 token ordering 写成一个带终局奖励的马尔可夫过程控制问题，然后用 Doob h-transform 去构造一个 reward-tilted 的 reveal law。直观上，host DLM 给出的是一个 proposal distribution，告诉你在当前 partially revealed state 下，哪些 token 位置/内容更“自然”或更“有把握”；DPRM 在这个 proposal 上再乘一个与未来终局奖励相关的 h 函数，相当于把原始 reveal 过程重加权，让那些虽然当前置信度未必最高、但更可能导向高终局回报的 reveal 动作获得更高概率。

论文没有停在理论最优策略，因为 exact conditional expectation 在每一步、每个候选 token 上都算一遍，代价太高。实际实现分三层近似。第一层是 stagewise Soft-BoN approximation，可以理解为在每个 reveal stage 上用 soft best-of-N 的方式近似 reward-tilted 选择，而不是直接求全局最优控制。作者给出 terminal KL 上 O(1/N) 的收敛结论，说明采样数增加时会逼近精确 DPRM。第二层是 online bucketized controller：把候选 token 或候选动作按某种统计量分桶，在线估计 bucket-level reward，逐步替代纯 confidence score。这样做的原因是单 token 粒度的 reward estimate 方差太大、更新太慢，而 bucket 化能在样本效率和分辨率之间做折中。第三层是 warmup-to-guidance 的调度：一开始仍以 confidence-driven ordering 为主，因为早期 reward estimate 不可靠；随着在线估计稳定，再逐步切到 reward-aware ordering。这个设计很像从 exploitation 过渡到 informed exploration，但它不是通用 RL 控制器，而是专门嵌在 DLM reveal 过程里的轻量策略层。

和已有方法相比，差异点很清楚。随机 masking 不利用状态信息；confidence ordering 只看局部 posterior / certainty；teacher-forced progressive masking 虽然在 train-test alignment 上更进一步，但实际 policy 仍偏 confidence heuristic。DPRM 的新意在于：它把 ordering policy 显式写成“proposal × future reward tilt”，并给出可计算近似，而不是继续在 confidence 上做启发式修补。19:T7ec,实验覆盖 7 个场景，作者刻意保持 host model、训练目标和数据流程尽量不变，只替换 ordering controller，这使结果更容易归因到 policy 本身。自然语言方向，DPRM-PUMA 在 GSM8K validation mean 上从 29.34 提到 34.27，提升 16.8%；DMPO-DPRM 在 MATH Hard 上从 44.3 到 47.9，提升 8.1%，在 Countdown Hard 上从 29.6 到 33.4，提升 12.8%；DPRM-Prism 在 GSM8K voted accuracy 上从 82.41 到 83.85，增加 1.44 个点。这里可以看出一个特点：在 reasoning / test-time scaling 场景里，ordering policy 的收益不是只体现在 token-level reconstruction，而是会传导到最终答案正确率。

科学序列建模方向的结果更能说明“顺序是建模假设的一部分”。蛋白扩散建模里，DPLM ordering variant 把 forward-folding RMSD 从 35.47 降到 29.43，下降 17.0%；TM-score 从 0.3071 提到 0.3321，提升 8.1%。单细胞扩散里，DPRM-DCM 把 token recovery 从 63.97% 提到 75.92%，zero-expression accuracy 从 78.39% 提到 99.90%。分子生成里，DPRM-GenMol 把 linker validity 从 0.142 提到 0.429，scaffold quality 从 0.429 提到 0.712。DNA reward optimization 里，DPRM-SDPO 提高 HepG2 分数，同时比 confidence-only progressive ordering 更好地保留 ATAC 和 k-mer 质量。作者强调的不是所有指标都单调上涨，而是 ordering policy 改变了性能前沿和 trade-off 形状。

从消融角度，最值得注意的是 warmup、bucketization、reward estimate reliability 这几项。论文的论证链条是：纯 reward guidance 早期不稳，纯 confidence 后期近视，因此需要一个从 confidence 到 reward-aware 的渐进切换。理论部分用 empirical-Bernstein rate 解释在线 bucketized score 对 exact DPRM score 的跟踪误差来源，包括 bucket coarsening bias、warmup bias 和 nonstationarity。也就是说，作者并不把近似误差藏起来，而是明确指出性能取决于 reward estimate 的方差控制和状态分布漂移。1a:T72b,The method formulates token ordering as a sequential control problem over partially revealed states, with a terminal reward defined on the completed generation. The exact controller is derived via a Doob h-transform: starting from the host model's proposal distribution over reveal actions, DPRM reweights that proposal by an h-function tied to expected future reward. Intuitively, the policy no longer asks only which token is currently most confident, but which reveal action is most promising for the final outcome.

Because the exact conditional expectation is too expensive to compute for every candidate at every step, the paper introduces a practical approximation stack. First, it uses a stagewise Soft-BoN approximation, effectively a soft best-of-N selection at each reveal stage. The authors prove O(1/N) convergence in terminal KL to the exact DPRM target, giving a clean approximation guarantee as the sample budget grows. Second, they introduce an online bucketized controller. Candidate actions are grouped into buckets, and reward estimates are accumulated at the bucket level rather than per action. This reduces variance and improves sample efficiency, at the cost of some coarsening bias. Third, the controller uses a warmup schedule: it begins with confidence-driven ordering when reward estimates are unreliable, then gradually shifts toward reward-aware guidance as online estimates stabilize.

This differs from prior approaches in a precise way. Random masking ignores state-dependent control. Confidence-based policies rely on local certainty. Teacher-forced progressive masking improves train-test alignment but still uses confidence-like heuristics in practice. DPRM instead makes the policy explicitly equal to proposal times future-reward tilt, then builds a computable approximation around that principle.1b:T6d7,The evaluation spans seven settings, and the experimental design is careful: the host model, objective, and data pipeline are kept as fixed as possible, while only the ordering controller is changed. In natural language tasks, DPRM-PUMA improves GSM8K validation mean from 29.34 to 34.27 (+16.8%). DMPO-DPRM improves MATH Hard from 44.3 to 47.9 (+8.1%) and Countdown Hard from 29.6 to 33.4 (+12.8%). DPRM-Prism improves GSM8K voted accuracy from 82.41 to 83.85 (+1.44 points). These results suggest that ordering affects not just token reconstruction quality but downstream reasoning accuracy.

The scientific-domain results are even more revealing. In protein diffusion, ordering variants reduce forward-folding RMSD from 35.47 to 29.43 (-17.0%) and improve TM-score from 0.3071 to 0.3321 (+8.1%). In single-cell diffusion, DPRM-DCM improves token recovery from 63.97% to 75.92% and zero-expression accuracy from 78.39% to 99.90%. In molecular generation, DPRM-GenMol raises linker validity from 0.142 to 0.429 and scaffold quality from 0.429 to 0.712.
