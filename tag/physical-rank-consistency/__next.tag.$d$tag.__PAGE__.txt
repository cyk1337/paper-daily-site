9:T846,方法分两部分：先定义该保留什么，再设计训练目标去保留它。第一部分是 PRC，physical rank consistency。作者先定义动作 chunk 之间的物理距离，距离由平移、旋转和 gripper 状态共同构成；然后对每个原始动作 chunk，考察其在原始空间中的近邻动作，比较这些近邻到该动作的距离排序在重建后是否仍然保持。这里有两个设计点比较关键。其一，PRC 在 decoded action space 上算，而不是在 tokenizer 内部 latent 或 token space 上算，这样不同词表大小、不同序列长度、不同 decoder 架构之间才可比。其二，它关注的是 local rank 而不是绝对距离，因此更贴近“细微调整是否还在”的问题。

第二部分是 ActionPiece 本身。整体架构是 Transformer tokenizer 加 residual vector quantization。训练时除了常规 reconstruction loss，还加入两个关系保持目标。PRP，physical rank preservation，用原始动作间的 near-far 关系监督 encoder feature 距离和 quantized feature 距离的排序，让表示空间学到与物理动作差异一致的局部结构。QR，quantization regularization，则把同样的排序约束施加到 codeword assignment distribution 的散度上，也就是不只要求连续特征空间保留关系，还要求映射到离散词表时，这种关系不要被量化过程破坏。这个设计比只在 encoder latent 上做 metric learning 更完整，因为 VQ tokenizer 的误差很大一部分就出在 assignment 阶段。最终 encoder、quantizer、decoder 联合训练；训练好后冻结 tokenizer，encoder+quantizer 产生离散动作 token 作为 AR policy 的监督目标，decoder 把预测 token 还原成动作 chunk。

和已有动作 tokenizer 相比，差异主要有三点。第一，评价标准从 MSE 扩展到关系保真。第二，监督信号不是只约束重建端，而是同时约束表示学习和 codeword 分配。第三，优化目标明确围绕“执行时需要保留的局部物理差异”来设计，而不是把压缩问题当成一般信号重建。a:T835,The method has two layers: define the right fidelity target, then optimize the tokenizer to preserve it. The fidelity target is Physical Rank Consistency (PRC). The authors define a physical distance between action chunks using translation, rotation, and gripper state. For each original action chunk, they examine its local neighbors and compare the ranking of neighbor distances before and after reconstruction. Two design choices matter. First, PRC is computed in decoded action space rather than latent or token space, which makes comparisons meaningful across vocabularies, token lengths, and decoder architectures. Second, it focuses on local rank preservation rather than exact distances, which better captures whether subtle contextual adjustments remain distinguishable.

ActionPiece itself is built on a Transformer tokenizer with residual vector quantization. Training uses standard reconstruction loss plus two relational objectives. Physical Rank Preservation (PRP) uses near-far supervision derived from original physical distances to enforce consistent ordering in both encoder-feature distances and quantized-feature distances. This shapes the continuous representation space to reflect physically meaningful local structure. Quantization Regularization (QR) pushes the same ordering signal into the divergence between codeword assignment distributions, so the discrete mapping stage does not destroy the structure learned by the encoder. This is an important distinction from metric-learning-only approaches: in VQ systems, a large fraction of the damage happens during assignment, not only in the continuous embedding space. After joint training, the tokenizer is frozen; the encoder and quantizer provide discrete policy targets, and the decoder maps predicted tokens back to executable action chunks.

Relative to prior action tokenizers, the novelty is threefold: evaluation moves beyond MSE, supervision is applied to both representation learning and codeword assignment, and the optimization target is explicitly tied to preserving execution-relevant local physical differences.b:T66f,The policy side is controlled with a shared Qwen3-VL-4B autoregressive setup, so the comparison is intended to isolate tokenizer effects. On the main benchmarks, ActionPiece reaches 94.8% success on LIBERO and 68.8% on unseen LIBERO-Plus, outperforming the strongest baseline by 1.1 and 4.5 points respectively. The larger gain on LIBERO-Plus suggests that preserving local relational structure helps more in generalization-heavy settings. On SimplerEnv and VLA-Arena, which stress real-to-sim transfer and harder control conditions, ActionPiece achieves aggregate success rates of 71.9% and 51.5%, the best among the methods evaluated in the paper.

The ablations are more informative than the headline numbers. PRP and QR each help, but the joint model gives the best decoded PRC and the best downstream policy success, which supports the paper’s central claim that relational fidelity must be maintained both before and during quantization. Another important result is the comparison between reconstruction fidelity, PRC, and execution success across tokenizers. Low reconstruction error does not reliably imply better policy performance, while PRC tracks downstream execution more consistently. That is the key empirical takeaway: optimizing the wrong tokenizer metric can systematically bias the whole policy pipeline.
