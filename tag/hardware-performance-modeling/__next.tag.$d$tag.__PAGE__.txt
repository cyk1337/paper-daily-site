9:T4fa,PerfReasoning has two coupled tasks:
1) Reasoning Q&A: each item compares two mappings. The benchmark uses three prompt formulations (agree with a true statement, agree with its negation, and direct comparison). The reported score is the minimum accuracy across the three to control agreement bias. Each pair varies exactly one mapping axis (tile size, loop order, tensor keep/bypass) and asks about off-chip traffic or buffer capacity.
2) Model construction: the model must generate a Python analytical performance model that reads workload/architecture/mapping YAML specs and outputs total and per-tensor backing-store accesses and buffer capacity. The generated program is executed in isolation on hidden cases and compared against Timeloop-derived labels. Passing requires both access and capacity predictions to match within 1e-6 relative error; the benchmark also reports Q-error and pairwise preference accuracy (whether the program ranks two mappings correctly).
Dataset/setup: 216 valid configurations (108 mapping pairs) over matmul, batched matmul, and conv; a fixed two-level hierarchy MainMemory→Buffer→MACC; labels span >7 orders of magnitude and include all-kept and bypass cases.
