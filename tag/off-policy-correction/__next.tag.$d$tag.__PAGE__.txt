b:T40d,The paper separates the total importance ratio into two factors. The first factor repairs training-inference discrepancy by comparing the training-side and inference-side distributions at the same old policy version. The second factor corrects policy staleness by comparing the current training policy with the old training-side policy. These two factors should be controlled differently: discrepancy repair is naturally handled through masking, filtering, or down-weighting inconsistent tokens, while PPO clipping should apply to the staleness ratio with its advantage-sign-dependent boundary. The missing-old-logit problem removes the denominator needed for the staleness ratio. The paper also analyzes interpolation-based proxy references and argues that, under common constructions, they mostly reparameterize the effective clipping interval rather than recover the missing old policy.
