Annotators are heterogeneous in utilities (hence in V) but share a common, unknown boundary b (decision threshold).

Key observation: marginalizing out response time reduces DDM to Bradley–Terry, so choice-only learning discards intensity information. Given b, the authors derive an unbiased estimator of the population mean drift μ=E[V] as a weighted average of response times with closed-form weights w_b(·).
