从机制上看，论文先保留 diffusion sampler 的 predictor 部分，用它把样本从高噪声状态逐步带到目标分布附近。然后在每个阶段或若干阶段插入 Gibbs-based corrector，对离散坐标做条件更新。关键观察是，在 uniform-rate 模型里，score estimator 已经编码了局部条件结构，足以恢复 Gibbs 更新所需的相对后验量，因此不必像一些 predictor-corrector 方法那样再训练额外的能量函数、校正器或辅助网络。

论文的理论部分给出一个比较完整的误差分解框架。总误差被拆成三部分：初始化误差、corrector 的 mixing 误差、以及 score estimation 误差。作者的论证重点不只是“Gibbs 会混得更快”，而是 diffusion predictor 提供了一个 warm start，使得 corrector 不需要从远离目标分布的位置开始混合；同时，这个 warm start 还能减轻 score 估计误差在后续步骤中的放大。基于这个结构，论文给出 GADD 的整体采样复杂度为 O(polylog(ε^{-1}))。这点是全文最核心的理论主张。

与已有方法相比，差异主要有三层。第一，和 exact simulation、τ-leaping、Euler/Tweedie 这类数值离散化方法不同，GADD 不是单纯提高 reverse process 的时间离散精度，而是显式引入 MCMC corrector。第二，和 masked diffusion 上依赖吸收态结构的快速采样不同，GADD 面向的是没有单调 unmasking 结构的 uniform-rate 模型。第三，和需要额外训练 corrector 的方法不同，它强调“零额外训练”，只复用标准 score network。这个设计对大模型尤其重要，因为如果加速方法本身还要再训练一套网络，实际采用门槛会很高。18:T7ab,The proposed method is Gibbs-Accelerated Discrete Diffusion (GADD), a predictor-corrector sampler tailored to uniform-rate discrete diffusion models. Its defining feature is that the corrector requires no additional training. Instead, it uses the structure of the already-trained concrete score function to construct the posterior quantities needed for Gibbs updates.

Operationally, GADD keeps a diffusion-style predictor that moves the sample from a high-noise state toward the target distribution. It then inserts a Gibbs-based corrector that updates discrete coordinates conditionally. The key observation is that the score estimator already contains enough local information to recover the relative posterior likelihoods needed for Gibbs sampling. This avoids the need for separately trained energy models, auxiliary correctors, or learned refinement networks.

A major part of the paper is the error-analysis framework. The total error is decomposed into initialization error, mixing error from the corrector, and score-estimation error. The argument is not simply that Gibbs mixes faster in isolation. Rather, the diffusion process provides a warm start, so the corrector does not need to mix from a poor initialization; at the same time, this warm start also reduces the impact of score-estimation error. Under this structure, the paper proves an overall sampling complexity of O(polylog(ε^{-1})). That is the central theoretical claim.

Relative to prior work, the differences are clear. Compared with exact simulation, τ-leaping, Euler, or Tweedie-style discretizations, GADD is not just a better numerical integrator for the reverse process; it explicitly introduces an MCMC corrector. Compared with fast samplers for masked diffusion, it does not rely on absorbing-state structure. Compared with methods that train additional correctors, it is training-free beyond the standard score model, which is an important practical property for large models.19:T681,The experiments cover synthetic distributions, zero-shot unconditional text sampling, and zero-shot conditional music generation.
