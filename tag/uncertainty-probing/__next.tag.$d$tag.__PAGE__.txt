9:T473,The key device is an exact label null. The authors keep real output-routing pairs from trained vision transformers, but resample correctness labels from a frozen logistic generator that depends only on model confidence and is fitted on disjoint data. Under this construction, routing has no extra information about correctness given the output view. They formalize the target quantity using strictly proper losses: under log loss, the population gain corresponds to conditional mutual information I(T;R|O); under squared loss, it corresponds to a squared conditional-probability gap. The paper then contrasts this ideal quantity with empirical gains from restricted probes. It evaluates four output views, from a single transformed top-class confidence to sorted probabilities and sorted logits. A central audit isolates checkpoint selection: the same MLP training trajectory is read either at the checkpoint selected by validation accuracy or by validation log loss.
