The benchmark spans 6 languages (EN/DE/ES/FR/HI/ZH), 4 conflict domains, and 3 hierarchy settings (System>>User, System>>Tool, User>>Tool). Each instance has a Reference condition (only the higher-priority instruction) and a Conflict condition (an explicit contradictory lower-priority instruction); the performance drop from Reference to Conflict quantifies hierarchy breakage.
