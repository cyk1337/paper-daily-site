第二步，把 residual stream 投到 SAE 概念基上。作者不是直接在原始高维残差向量上搜索 patch，而是借助 sparse autoencoder 学到的稀疏概念方向，把每个 token、每一层的表示分解成一组可解释 feature / concept。这样做有两个目的：一是把搜索空间从“任意向量替换”收缩到“沿概念方向替换”，更容易得到稀疏解释；二是让最后得到的干预具备一定语义可读性。

第四步，迭代式最小干预搜索。LOCA 不一次性 patch 大块表示，而是每轮选择当前最有用的 token-concept 对进行 patch，然后重新评估后续候选，直到模型从 jailbreak 成功转为拒答，或达到预算上限。这个逐步搜索比一次性 top-k patch 更接近“最小充分干预集”的定义，也能减少冗余 patch。

First, it moves from global linear explanations to sparse, sample-specific causal explanations. That is useful for understanding what alignment fine-tuning actually changes internally. Second, it makes a methodological case for activation patching over activation steering in this setting: patching stays closer to in-distribution activations and supports token-level causal localization. Step 1 is token matching across structurally different prompts. Standard activation patching works best when source and target sequences are aligned. Here, the original refused prompt x_o and the successful jailbreak x_j can differ in wording, length, and template structure. LOCA first constructs correspondences between tokens in x_j and x_o so that patching can be applied even when the prompts are not isomorphic.

Step 2 is to move from raw residual activations to a sparse concept basis using sparse autoencoders. Instead of searching over arbitrary residual dimensions, LOCA decomposes token representations into SAE features or concept directions. This reduces the search space and makes the resulting interventions more interpretable. The patching is therefore not just token- and layer-specific, but also concept-specific.

Step 3 is candidate ranking via a first-order approximation. Exhaustively testing every layer-token-concept intervention would be too expensive. LOCA estimates the marginal effect of each candidate patch on the refusal objective using a first-order approximation, then ranks candidates by expected usefulness. This serves as an efficient causal screening mechanism.

Step 4 is iterative minimal intervention search. Rather than applying a large batch of patches at once, LOCA greedily applies the most promising token-concept patch, reevaluates the remaining candidates, and repeats until the jailbreak is converted back into a refusal or a budget limit is reached. This makes the method closer to finding a minimal sufficient intervention set rather than a broad correlated region.

A key design choice is the preference for activation patching over activation steering.
