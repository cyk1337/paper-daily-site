The new component is domain-aware distillation. For an input from a known domain, the loss from the matching teacher is upweighted, while losses from non-matching teachers are kept active with smaller weights. A scalar alpha controls the ratio between matched and mismatched teachers. This avoids two extremes: uniform teacher averaging, which ignores teacher specialization, and hard domain routing, which discards useful cross-domain structure.
