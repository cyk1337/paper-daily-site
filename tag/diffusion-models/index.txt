18:T4a2,Doob Policy Optimization (DoobPO) is a two-stage, likelihood-free framework:

1) Policy-improvement stage (model-agnostic): compute a target policy ratio / terminal tilt representing the desired improvement using a chosen update rule. The framework supports multiple updates (regularized Policy Mirror Descent, SAC, PPO, GRPO) without requiring exact likelihoods of the underlying generative policy.

2) Generative distillation stage (model-specific but unified): characterize the ratio-tilted target process using Doob’s h-transform, then learn the corresponding diffusion drift or flow velocity via Doob’s Drift Matching. This transfers the improvement into the generative policy.

For tractability, the paper introduces conditional drift matching and proves its gradient is proportional to the gradient of the ideal Doob drift matching objective for both diffusion and flow policies (Theorems 3.1 and 3.2).
