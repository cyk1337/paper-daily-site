Two design ideas carry most of the technical weight. The first is Logical Spaces. LeanGRPO-Reweight is more subtle. During rollout, each selected-timestep graph is immediately consumed through a provisional backward pass, producing a provisional gradient and allowing saved activations to be released right away. This shifts the memory burden from retained activations to stored provisional gradients.
