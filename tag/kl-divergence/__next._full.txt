18:T7d8,论文先做了一件很重要的事：把 SFT、forward KL distillation、reverse KL distillation 放到统一的重加权对数似然框架里。直观上，标准 SFT 对每个位置只强化 ground-truth token；FKLD 用 teacher 全分布给 dense supervision，鼓励 student 覆盖 teacher 支持集；RKLD 更像让 student 对自己高概率区域负责，倾向收缩到少数高质量模式。作者把这些目标改写成 token-level 权重作用下的 log-likelihood，从而把“散度最小化”转成“对哪些 token 加强、对哪些 token 抑制”的问题。

在这个视角下，作者提出 Hybrid Policy Distillation。核心机制有两层。第一层是 forward / reverse KL 的混合。作者使用 negative K1 estimator 构造 token 级重权信号。对离线 expert token，如果权重为正，表示 student 低估了该 token，需要按 forward-KL 风格提升其概率；如果权重为负，则说明 student 对某些 token 过度自信，需要抑制。第二层是 off-policy 与轻量 on-policy 的混合。除了离线数据中的 expert token，方法还会从 student 进行近似、轻量的 on-policy 采样。对 student 采样出的 token，同样计算抑制信号，惩罚 student 在自己 rollout 中的过估计行为，并把被压下来的概率质量重新分配给离线 expert token。这样做的目的，是在不进行昂贵完整 OPD 的情况下，让训练看到一部分“学生真实会生成什么”，从而缓解纯离线蒸馏的行为偏移。

First, it offers a unified token-level reweighted log-likelihood view of several distillation objectives. That is useful because it explains what these objectives are actually doing to the gradient, instead of treating them as disconnected recipes. Second, it targets a central issue in teacher-student transfer: a capacity-limited student cannot faithfully represent a highly multimodal teacher distribution. The proposed hybrid objective is an attempt to navigate between excessive mode coverage and brittle mode seeking. For researchers working on model compression, teacher-student transfer, or the interface between pretraining and post-training, the conceptual framing is arguably as valuable as the specific algorithm. The contribution is not foundational in the way a new pretraining objective would be, but it is a fairly complete treatment of an important distillation design space.1a:T883,The paper first reframes SFT, forward-KL distillation, and reverse-KL distillation under a unified reweighted log-likelihood perspective. Intuitively, standard SFT only reinforces the ground-truth token at each step. Forward KL uses the teacher's full predictive distribution to provide dense supervision and encourages the student to cover the teacher's support. Reverse KL puts more pressure on the regions where the student already places mass, making it more mode-seeking. By rewriting these objectives as token-level weighted likelihood terms, the authors turn divergence minimization into a question of which tokens should be upweighted and which should be suppressed.

Based on this view, they propose Hybrid Policy Distillation (HPD). The method combines two kinds of hybridization. The first is forward/reverse KL hybridization. HPD uses a negative K1 estimator to construct token-level reweighting signals. For offline expert tokens, a positive signal means the student underestimates that token and should be pushed upward in a forward-KL-like manner. Negative values are used to suppress overestimation. The second is off-policy/on-policy hybridization. In addition to offline expert data, HPD draws lightweight approximate on-policy samples from the student. For these student-sampled tokens, the method also computes suppressive signals, penalizing tokens that the student overproduces in its own rollouts, while redistributing probability mass toward offline expert tokens. This gives the student some exposure to its own inference-time behavior without paying the full cost of standard on-policy distillation.

Two design choices matter here. First, the method does not optimize exact vocabulary-level KL in the most direct way. Instead, it compresses teacher information into token-level rewards or weights and keeps the efficient one-hot training path. That is a deliberate trade-off between dense teacher supervision and throughput. Second, the on-policy component is intentionally lightweight and approximate rather than a full RL-style rollout-and-score pipeline, which keeps the method closer to practical distillation than to expensive policy optimization.1b:T5b0,The evaluation covers long-generation math reasoning and short-generation dialogue and code tasks. That choice is sensible: math exposes instability and compounding errors over long horizons, while dialogue and code test whether the method also works in more standard instruction-following settings. The paper reports that HPD improves optimization stability, computational efficiency, and final performance, and that it consistently matches or outperforms a standard SFT plus on-policy distillation pipeline at lower cost. It also serves as a stronger initialization for subsequent OPD, yielding further gains.
