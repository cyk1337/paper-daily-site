5:T411,The work separates success into (i) Lean validity (compilation/type correctness) and (ii) semantic faithfulness to the original natural-language theorem. Since Lean cannot decide semantic equivalence, they introduce a calibrated semantic filter: multiple independent LLM judges score whether the generated Lean declaration preserves meaning; results are reported under a strict consensus rule, and a human expert audit calibrates how conservative/precise the consensus labels are.

Beyond measurement, they run a factorial ablation over three intervention axes:
- Parametric drafting: the base model producing an initial Lean statement.
- Mathlib/context grounding: retrieving/providing relevant definitions and library context.
- Lean elaboration feedback: iterative repair using elaborator/compiler feedback.
