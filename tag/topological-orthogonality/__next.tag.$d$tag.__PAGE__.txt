9:T59d,The method, MUSE, is built around what the paper calls Topological Orthogonality. The central hypothesis is that semantic and structural objectives can be projected into approximately orthogonal parameter domains, reducing destructive interference. Concretely, the model assigns content primarily to token feature values and geometry/relations primarily to the topology induced by self-attention. This is implemented with a Synergistic Block that splits supervision into two pathways. Active Semantic Anchoring uses semantic supervision to update feature values so the latent space carries concept-level information more stably. Structural Topology Alignment uses structural supervision to shape attention patterns or attention-induced relational topology, so reconstruction preserves spatial organization rather than memorizing texture. The paper also frames the problem geometrically: an ideal latent should satisfy semantic invariance on one manifold and structural equivariance on another, and MUSE inserts an explicit structural bridge to avoid direct conflict. Compared with methods such as UniTok or UniLIP that mainly balance multiple losses in one shared space, MUSE emphasizes parameter-level role separation rather than only loss weighting. The claimed gradient analysis, showing that the two objectives naturally fit different parameter domains, is the part that most clearly distinguishes it from generic multitask training.a:T4b5,From the provided full-text excerpt, the most important results are threefold. Second, a particularly interesting result is that its linear probing score exceeds that of its teacher backbone, 85.2% versus 82.5%. If the setup is sound, this means reconstruction did not dilute semantics; structural alignment actually improved representation quality. That is more informative than simply showing coexistence of two capabilities. Third, the paper claims that plugging MUSE into unified multimodal models improves generation and editing without harming perception, indicating that tokenizer gains transfer upward into full UMMs rather than staying local to encoder evaluation.
