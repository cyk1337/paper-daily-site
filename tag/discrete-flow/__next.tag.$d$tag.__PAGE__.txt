9:T835,技术方案分两层。第一层是 selective absorption 机制。作者给每个位置加一个二值 absorption indicator；一旦某个位置被 absorb，它的 outgoing transition rate 置零，不再参与后续 CTMC 演化。这样就把原来“所有位置持续更新”的 uniform discrete flow，改成“部分位置冻结、其余位置继续流动”的混合过程。关键约束是：对仍然 active 的位置，保持原来的 uniform-flow velocity，不去改训练目标，也不重写 denoiser。

第二层是如何决定吸收顺序。随机吸收显然不行，因为一旦把错 token 固定住，就失去后续纠错机会。作者提出 LEDFlow，用 predictive entropy 做局部可靠性估计：每一步在给定 absorption budget 下，选当前熵最低的 active positions，把它们按当前最可能 token 固定下来；其余位置继续按 kinetic-optimal velocity 更新。下一步再重新计算熵，因此顺序是自适应的，而不是预先排好的静态 order。

理论部分的重点不是证明整个 sampler 全局最优，而是分析“不可逆吸收”引入的误差。作者把 stepwise absorption error 分解成两个 KL 项：一个对应被同时吸收位置之间的 joint dependence 丢失，另一个对应在已吸收上下文条件下的 conditional prediction error。随后在 oracle-reachable states 和 entropy-error regularity 的假设下，证明在固定吸收预算时，选择最低熵位置可以最小化 conditional error 上界。这个论证的意义在于解释为什么局部熵是合理信号，而不是仅凭经验有效。

作者还专门讨论了 global entropy lookahead。直觉上，往前看几步似乎能做更优决策，但论文指出在 denoiser 不完美时，未来状态估计误差会层层累积，decision error bound 会随 lookahead window 增长。也就是说，复杂的全局规划未必比局部熵更稳，反而更贵、更脆弱。这一点和很多生成式推理系统里的经验是相通的：如果模型本身有偏差，过深的 rollout 可能放大而不是消除误差。a:T89f,The method has two layers. The first is selective absorption. Each position is assigned a binary absorption indicator; once a position is absorbed, its outgoing transition rates are set to zero, so it no longer evolves under the CTMC. This changes the original all-positions-update regime of uniform discrete flow into a mixed process where some positions are frozen and others continue to move. A key design choice is that the velocity on active positions is left unchanged, so the method does not alter the trained denoiser or the underlying flow objective.

The second layer is the absorption policy. Randomly freezing positions is risky because an incorrect absorbed token cannot be corrected later. The proposed sampler, LEDFlow, uses predictive entropy as a local reliability signal. At each step, under a fixed absorption budget, it selects the active positions with the lowest entropy and absorbs them using their current most likely values. The remaining positions continue evolving under the kinetic-optimal velocity. Entropy is recomputed at every step, so the generation order is adaptive rather than predetermined.

The theory is framed around the error introduced by irreversible absorption. The authors decompose the stepwise absorption error into two KL terms: one for the loss of joint dependence among simultaneously absorbed positions, and one for conditional prediction error under the context created by previously absorbed values. Under assumptions on oracle-reachable states and entropy-error regularity, they show that, with a fixed absorption budget, choosing the lowest-entropy positions minimizes an upper bound on the conditional error term. This does not prove global optimality, but it gives a principled reason why local entropy is the right signal.

The paper also analyzes global entropy lookahead. In principle, evaluating future successor states could yield better decisions. In practice, with an imperfect denoiser, future-state estimation errors accumulate, and the decision-error bound grows with the lookahead window. The implication is important: more global planning is not necessarily more reliable, and can be both slower and less robust than a local entropy rule.b:T61d,The experiments span three task groups: structured reasoning, multimodal understanding, and text-to-image generation. The reasoning results are the most informative because they expose the exact failure mode the method is designed to fix.

In the Sudoku trajectory diagnostic, the native kinetic-optimal sampler produces 9.4% correct-to-wrong reversions, while LEDFlow reduces this to 2.6%. The fraction of token changes occurring after t = 0.75 also drops from 46% to 15%, which is a strong mechanistic result: the method is not just improving final accuracy, it is specifically suppressing unstable late revisions.

On Nikoli Sudoku, LEDFlow reaches 0.845 solve accuracy and achieves the best average rank across nine reasoning tasks. The pattern matters more than the single number: gains are largest on tasks with strong structural dependence, where late token flips are especially costly.

For text-to-image generation, LEDFlow achieves the best GenEval overall score among decode-time samplers, with a 2.8% absolute improvement over the native sampler. For multimodal understanding, it improves over the native sampler on all six reported benchmarks. The ablations are also useful. Entropy-guided absorption outperforms alternative absorption orders, supporting the central design choice. For lookahead, mean absolute score error rises from 0.17 with a single lookahead term to 1.76 under full lookahead, while longer lookahead can cost about 8x more inference time without meaningful gains.
