18:T477,SparseBalance combines coarse-grained data reorganization with fine-grained runtime control:
1) SAB (Sparsity-Aware Batching):
- Lightweight sparsity estimation to approximate how expensive a sample will be under sparse attention.
- Latency-based packing: instead of packing by token count, it packs by predicted execution latency.
- A latency prediction module built via offline profiling that maps (sequence length, sparsity) to real runtime, used by both SAB and DST.
2) DST (workload-aware Dynamic Sparsity Tuning):
- Runtime, layer-level bidirectional sparsity adjustment per microbatch: reduce attention budget for bottleneck microbatches to eliminate stragglers; increase budget for non-bottleneck microbatches to fill PP bubbles and improve accuracy.
- Anchor-guided thresholding to decide adjustment direction and bound step size, aiming to keep training stable while exploring the accuracy–efficiency trade-off.
3) System framing: explicitly targets PP bubble reduction and DP barrier straggler mitigation, and argues length heterogeneity propagates across parallelism dimensions; DST aims to equalize per-layer
