18:T5c5,CURA is a read-only external monitor. It observes harness-visible telemetry rather than hidden states, logits, or internal traces. The monitored system is a three-stage pipeline: a read-only feasibility gate, a planner, and a GUI executor. CURA converts the trajectory into a sequential risk process built from four families of task-agnostic signals: execution effort, reasoning semantics, execution physiology, and visual dynamics. Intuitively, these capture how much work the agent is doing, what kind of reasoning it is expressing, whether its action rhythm looks unhealthy, and whether the screen is changing in ways consistent with progress. The online detector is based on CUSUM, which accumulates evidence over time instead of making a one-shot judgment. Thresholds are calibrated with Learn-then-Test using successful trajectories only, yielding finite-sample false-alarm guarantees without requiring failure labels. This design choice is important: the goal is not to maximize retrospective AUROC, but to detect failure early under a controlled false-alarm budget. The paper also studies where signal enters the pipeline: some risk is measurable before the first action, more appears at the planning stage, but fast quiet failures and belief-level errors remain difficult for purely external monitoring.
