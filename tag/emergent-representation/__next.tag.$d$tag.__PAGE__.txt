5:T602,Setup:
- Create a hidden total order over N entities.
- Train a small Transformer only on adjacent comparisons (i, i+1).
- Evaluate on held-out non-adjacent pairs (i, j) with larger rank distance.

Key empirical findings:
- When OOD generalization emerges, entity embeddings collapse onto an approximately 1D manifold; the first principal component recovers the true rank ordering with near-perfect fidelity.
- Even at near-ceiling accuracy, confidence / separation increases with rank distance, matching the symbolic distance effect.

Core theoretical argument:
- Idealize the comparator as a fixed linear readout direction w, producing scalar scores s_i = w·h_i, and predicting by the sign of s_j - s_i.
- Training on adjacent pairs imposes margin constraints s_{i+1} - s_i ≥ γ.
- With L2 regularization (weight decay), learning selects the minimum-norm feasible solution.
- This yields: (1) collinearity—any component orthogonal to w is useless for satisfying constraints but increases norm, so it is driven to zero; (2) (near) equal spacing / anchoring structure—constraints become tight, producing an ordered, roughly evenly spaced 1D embedding geometry, with additional non-uniformities linked to classic end-anchor effects.

Differentiator vs prior work:
- It does not merely observe linear structure; it derives the 1D collapse as the optimizer’s implicit bias under local constraints, and connects behavioral distance effects to geometric margins.
