This upper latent is meant to capture a coarser temporal abstraction over multiple tokens. The model jointly learns four parameterized components: the standard token output head p_theta, a lower-level dynamics model p_psi for NextLat-style transition consistency, an upper-level dynamics model p_phi that predicts k steps ahead in the abstract latent space, and a combined head p_rho that predicts the next token from both lower and upper latents. A subtle but important implementation detail is the stop-gradient placement. In the combined-NTP path, the upper latent is computed from stop-grad lower states before SWA. This prevents the combined-head cross-entropy from backpropagating into h_t through the SWA window. The authors explicitly argue that otherwise the lower token-level representation would receive overlapping and competing supervision from standard NTP, lower-level transition consistency, and the combined head.
