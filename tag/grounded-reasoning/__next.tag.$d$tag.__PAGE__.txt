9:T4ec,GDP.pdf contains 100 question–document pairs spanning Finance, Healthcare, Legal, STEM/Research, Engineering, Construction, Manufacturing/Supply Chain, Insurance, Real Estate, and Human Resources. The construction pipeline is: (1) domain experts contribute questions from their actual workflows together with source PDFs; (2) several frontier multimodal models attempt each candidate, and an item is retained only if at least two models make a substantive failure (materially wrong answer, missed decisive evidence, or fabricated claim); (3) tasks answerable from general prior knowledge without reading the PDF are rejected; (4) each item is tagged against an 11-axis capability taxonomy organized into three tiers: foundational extraction and grounding, structural and multimodal comprehension, and advanced reasoning. It uses lightweight prefill-time attention statistics to predict relay layers, applies task-aware scheduling to expand or contract visual relay allocation, and anchors selected visual support after handoff so that decoding remains grounded.
