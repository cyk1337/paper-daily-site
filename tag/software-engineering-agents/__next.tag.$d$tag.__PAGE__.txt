9:T469,LatentSift replaces the first-stage LLM-based execution-free verifier in a hybrid SWE verification pipeline. The standard pipeline scores all K trajectories with an EF verifier, keeps a subset, then applies regression tests, LLM-generated tests, and a final EF selection step. LatentSift instead collects hidden states while the policy is generating candidates, adding no extra LLM tokens. It represents each trajectory through three channels: reasoning states, environment-observation states, and function-call states. During training, states from successful and unsuccessful trajectories are stored in positive and negative banks. At inference time, candidate states are compared with these banks, step-level distance margins are aggregated, and channel-level ranks are fused conservatively so that a candidate must look good across the behavioral channels. A separate linear scorer trained on policy hidden states produces a learned trajectory score.
