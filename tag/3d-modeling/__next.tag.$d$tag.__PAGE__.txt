9:T447,3DCodeBench is built around benchmark construction and protocol design:
- Data: extracted procedural factories from Infinigen and converted (via an agentic curation pipeline plus human verification) into 26K triplets of text/image prompts ↔ standalone procedural code ↔ rendered/constructed 3D objects, spanning 212 categories. The benchmark emphasizes real complexity (some classes average 400+ lines of code).
- Agentic evaluation: beyond single-shot prompting, models are evaluated in controlled multi-turn settings with execution-error feedback, retries, visual self-critique, and API documentation augmentation—closer to real Blender workflows.
- Metrics: executability, perceptual view similarity (e.g., SigLIP-2, DINOv3 embeddings), and 3D geometric alignment (e.g., Chamfer distance, Uni3D).
- Human eval: 3DCodeArena collects pairwise preferences and produces Elo rankings to capture perceptual/aesthetic quality that automated metrics miss.
