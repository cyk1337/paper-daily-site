9:T42c,The paper formalizes strong-to-weak scaffolding with a builder model and a fixed target model. For each benchmark, the builder receives a small validation split, 5% of the data, and must construct a scaffold that transfers to a hidden test split. The scaffold may include task routing, prompt templates, deterministic solvers, few-shot examples, verification passes, format enforcement, decomposition, or tools. The target model is not trained. This isolates transfer through inference-time structure rather than transfer through parameters. The empirical domain is Theory-of-Mind reasoning, where models must track nested beliefs, perspective changes, hidden information, and Bayesian goal inference. These tasks are difficult for small models but also expose structure that a scaffold can exploit. The analysis covers effect size, stability across independent scaffolds, validation usage, scaffold mechanisms, platform effects, dependence on the target model, builder reasoning effort, mechanisms associated with gains, cognitive-load reduction, and residual errors.a:T4a9,The reported gains are large for the weaker target. The best scaffold raises GPT-5.4-mini from 0.49 to 0.91 macro-average accuracy, a 0.42 absolute improvement, and every builder configuration yields positive net uplift. The gains are not mainly explained by more validation search, more sampling, or simply eliciting longer target-model reasoning. They are attributed to externalizing structure, deterministic offloading, and strict format control. The mechanism differs by benchmark. On BigToM, the best scaffolds discover that parts of the task can be reduced to structured rules, making the benchmark close to a set of compilable procedures. On other Theory-of-Mind benchmarks, the gains are described as reducing the target model’s reasoning burden rather than exploiting an obvious shortcut. Remaining failures concentrate in the hardest cases, especially higher-order Hi-ToM examples with recursion depth at least two and Bayesian goal-inference subtypes. Builder reasoning effort improves scaffold quality monotonically across effort tiers. Platform effects are smaller than builder-model effects.
