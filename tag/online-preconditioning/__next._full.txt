Since the Delta update corresponds to gradient descent on 1/2 ||S k_t - v_t||^2, the new method right-preconditions the gradient. The key algebraic result is that this right-preconditioning is exactly equivalent to scaling the write-side key feature by feature: \tilde{k}_t = d_t ⊙ k_t. The APF variant adds learned token-wise and head-wise forgetting for the preconditioner only, which helps under non-stationary contexts without decaying the main memory state.
