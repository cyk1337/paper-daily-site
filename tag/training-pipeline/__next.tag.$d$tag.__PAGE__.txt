9:T404,Boundary-Calibrated Intervention Transfer, or BCIT, stores each update as a state-bound record: the source context, the observed source evidence, prespecified applicability conditions and named hard conflicts, and provenance links for the model, data, configuration, and evaluation artifacts. The decision is made for a pair consisting of one candidate and the current context. A non-compensable conflict leads to rejection. Insufficient or unresolved evidence triggers a bounded validation run from the current parent. Only candidates satisfying a frozen authorization rule receive the full training budget. A fully trained child is still subject to a separate promote-or-rollback rule. The system may generate a small number of new atomic proposals when progress stalls, but those proposals have no fabricated source evidence and cannot skip validation.
