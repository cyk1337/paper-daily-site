9:T457,The work impacts pretraining research on three levels. First, it extends the interleaved reasoning paradigm initiated by o3 from pure-text and visual-only tools to the MLLM + Python code invocation scenario for numerical computation, filling the capability gap of non-visual tools in multimodal agentic RL and providing a reproducible training recipe for multimodal foundations to move from perception-reasoning to a perception-computation-reasoning closed loop. Second, the combined recipe of cold-start data construction, dual-track data filtering, and group-constrained GRPO directly addresses two engineering pain points: the difficulty of activating tool use in small models, and the long-term instability of agentic RL. The unexpected finding that adaptive tool use actually stabilizes training offers practical reference for analyzing training dynamics in RL pretrain. Third, the entire pipeline is open-source implementable and does not depend on proprietary capabilities of closed-source models, providing direct engineering guidance for the community to reproduce o3-style multimodal reasoning models.a:T4ff,The AIR framework has two training stages: cold-start SFT and interleaved reasoning RL, supported by three technical lines. First, two-stage cold-start data construction: an MLLM first generates pure-text CoT reasoning traces, then an LLM rewrites the CoT into interleaved reasoning format with inserted code calls, followed by verification of code execution integrity and answer correctness. This decoupled generation avoids the insufficient reasoning depth and low efficiency of one-step synthesis in multimodal scenarios. Second, dual-track data filtering: the Self-Sampled strategy performs multiple rollouts on the SFT model and uses Pass@k as the admission threshold for candidate samples, filtering noise through multi-turn consensus of the target model itself; the Prior-Filtered strategy acts as a complement, using a strong teacher model as an external quality gate to further remove low-quality interleaved reasoning samples. Third, group-constrained GRPO reward mechanism: group constraints are applied to tool-use behavior during RL, guiding the model to adaptively judge whether tool invocation is needed and choose the right timing. This constraint also suppresses training fluctuations when tool-use rate increases, mitigating long-term instability in agentic RL.b:T400,Experiments focus on multimodal complex numerical computation and mathematical reasoning tasks. In the cold-start stage, two-stage data construction delivers clear improvements over one-step synthesis in code executability and final answer accuracy. Dual-track filtering, compared with no filtering or single-track filtering, significantly reduces noise in SFT data and leads to faster convergence in subsequent RL. In the RL stage, group-constrained GRPO outperforms vanilla GRPO in two aspects: adaptive tool-use rate is more reasonable, without extreme over-use or non-use, and accuracy on problems requiring computation improves notably; training curves are smoother, with no reward oscillation or model collapse under high tool-use settings, which is common in vanilla GRPO.
