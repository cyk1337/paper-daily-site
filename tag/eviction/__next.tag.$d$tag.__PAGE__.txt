在 attention level，上述 certificate 的经验覆盖率达到 0.97，达到目标覆盖；certificate 与真实误差的相关性超过 0.94；同时没有带来精度损失。作者还专门测了 deterministic eviction 的自诊断信号失效：在真实任务上，类似 H2O、SnapKV 风格 eviction 的 retained-attention entropy 对 eviction-induced failure 的 AUC 约为 0.50，基本等于随机；一些其它在线信号如 margin 也接近 chance，evicted score mass 最多到 0.73，而随机化 certificate 可到 0.77–0.86。

在 synthetic long-context suites 上，certificate 能从 attention 误差转移到 task failure：16/16 个 model-task cell 都是正向 AUC，平均 0.836。这说明在压缩伤害主导的环境里，证书确实能反映任务层面的失败风险。

但真实工作负载的结果更克制，也更有信息量。作者明确承认 7 条预注册 claim 里有 3 条失败。原因是 LongBench 这类 question-aware eviction 在 25%–50% budget、6k 和 16k context 下本来就几乎不伤质量，所以总体 failure 更多来自任务本身难度，而不是 cache 压缩。于是 mean output log-probability 这种免费 baseline 在“预测答案会不会错”上反而优于 certificate；certificate-gated 从 25% 升到 50% budget 的 escalation 也没有收益。真正成立的是 attribution：在失败样本中，certificate 区分 eviction-induced failure 和 inherent failure 的 AUC 分别约 0.75 和 0.73，而 output confidence 在这个任务上接近或低于随机。进一步地，certificate-gated recomputation 在同等算力下优于 random gating 和 confidence gating；后者甚至可能低于随机，因为它把重算预算浪费在模型本来就答不出的样本上。整套证据链大约花了 70 GPU-hours，单 shard 在 H100/H200 上可跑。6:T70f,The work has two layers. First, an impossibility result. In a single-head, single-step attention setup, the authors formalize the online sigma-field available to the serving system: retained KV pairs, query history, scores, and any statistics computed from them. They prove that no estimator measurable with respect to this information can consistently estimate the true eviction-induced attention-output error under deterministic top-k-style selection. The proof is constructive: keep the retained state fixed, alter the evicted values, and the true error can change arbitrarily while every observable quantity remains unchanged.

Second, they restore identifiability via randomized design. The proposed mechanism is certainty-plus-Poisson eviction: high-score items are kept deterministically, while the tail is sampled with known inclusion probabilities π_i. Once the sampling design is known, design-based survey inference becomes available. The key implementation trick is elegant: Hájek correction is realized inside attention by adding log(1/π_i) to the logits of retained tail tokens, which performs inverse-probability reweighting through the softmax normalization. They then derive a retained-set variance estimator of Sen–Yates–Grundy type for the linearized error, and wrap it with an empirical-Bernstein radius to obtain a per-step error certificate. An e-process extension is also discussed to move toward time-uniform validity over the autoregressive trajectory.

Two aspects stand out. It is training-free and lightweight: no new parameters, no retraining, and only scalar work over the sampled tail. And its target is attribution rather than generic failure prediction: it aims to tell whether cache compression caused the damage, not whether the answer will be wrong for any reason.7:T84e,The empirical study is unusually disciplined: the authors pre-register seven falsifiable claims and report the failures openly.

At the attention level, the certificate achieves about 0.97 empirical coverage, certificate-error correlation exceeds 0.94, and there is no accuracy penalty relative to deterministic top-k baselines. They also directly measure the silent-failure phenomenon: retained-attention entropy for H2O- and SnapKV-style deterministic eviction gives AUC around 0.50 for predicting eviction-induced failures, essentially chance. Other online self-signals such as margin behave similarly; evicted score mass reaches at most 0.73, while the randomized certificate reaches roughly 0.77–0.86 on the same attribution-oriented yardstick.

On synthetic long-context tasks, the signal transfers to task level: all 16 model-task cells show positive certificate-failure AUC, with mean 0.836. This is the regime where compression damage is a dominant source of failure, so the certificate remains informative.

The real-workload results are more nuanced and more useful. Three of the seven pre-registered claims fail. On LongBench-style question-aware eviction at 25%–50% budgets and 6k/16k contexts, eviction is nearly free, so overall task failure is dominated by intrinsic task difficulty rather than cache damage. In that regime, mean output log-probability, a free baseline, beats the certificate at predicting whether the final answer is wrong. Certificate-gated escalation from 25% to 50% budget also does not help. What survives is attribution: among failed examples, the certificate separates eviction-induced from inherent failures with AUC about 0.75 and 0.73 at the two scales, whereas output confidence is at or below chance because it conflates task difficulty with cache damage. In recomputation scheduling, certificate-gated reruns outperform random and confidence-based gating at matched compute; confidence gating can even underperform random because it spends budget on examples the model would fail anyway. The full evidence chain reportedly costs about 70 GPU-hours on H100/H200-class hardware.8:T45c,For a skim, focus on three things. First, read the introduction and the theorem section to understand why deterministic eviction error is not merely hard to estimate but unidentifiable. That is the central idea. Second, inspect how the Hájek correction becomes a simple logit offset; this is the bridge from survey sampling theory to an implementable attention-layer mechanism. Third, read the figures and real-workload sections that separate prediction from attribution. The paper is strongest when it shows that output confidence is better for generic failure prediction, while the certificate is uniquely useful for diagnosing compression-induced damage.

If time is limited, the introduction, Section 3, the attention-level experiments, and the recomputation scheduling results are enough to capture the main contribution in 30–45 minutes.
