b:T51d,Semidirect Fourier Delta Attention extends KDA by inserting a block-rotational control operator before the delta correction. In real coordinates, the control is a direct sum of aligned 2×2 rotations combined with positive decay. Under the identification R^{2m} with C^m, this becomes a complex diagonal phase-decay matrix Λ_t. Each transition keeps the form A_t = Λ_t - u_t w_t^*, a phase-decay operator plus a rank-one delta correction. KDA is recovered by setting the phase to zero. The important design choice is to avoid dense control matrices; a dense phase operator would turn the chunk product into dense d×d multiplication and remove the computational advantage. The main technical result is a constructive left-to-right chunk-WY theorem for products P_t = A_t A_{t-1} ... A_1. The factors Γ_t, Y_t, M_t, and W_t are updated explicitly, and rank growth is confined to a fixed chunk. The paper also gives an exact affine chunk transfer, including an additive write summary, plus complex-to-real implementation details, formal cost bounds, a stability lemma, and a rank-growth discipline.
