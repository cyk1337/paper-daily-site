It compares several proposed notions of latent diffusability: semantic separability, spatial structure, smoothness, manifold continuity, uniformity, and affine equivariance. Second, it introduces Velocity Irreducible Variance, or VIV. In Flow Matching, a noise sample x0 and a data sample x1 are interpolated into xt, and the model predicts the velocity v=x1-x0.
