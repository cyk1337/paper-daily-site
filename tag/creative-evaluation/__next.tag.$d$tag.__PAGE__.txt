a:T57a,The core contribution is benchmark design. The paper emphasizes three design choices. First, the taxonomy is built top-down from professional creative workflows rather than as a flat checklist of attributes. It covers five major axes: Real-world Fidelity, Creative Generation, Quality, Aesthetics, and Text-Image Alignment, and further breaks them into creator-relevant subdimensions such as World Knowledge, Text Rendering, and Visual Storytelling. Second, the benchmark consists of 1,000 expert-crafted structured prompts, curated through filtering and stratified sampling for broad but balanced coverage. The structured-prompt aspect is important: prompts are intentionally designed around evaluable capabilities, which helps reduce entanglement across dimensions. Third, the paper takes a clear stance against over-reliance on a single MLLM judge. The introduction explicitly criticizes recent pipelines that use outputs from models like Gemini as supervision, arguing that this bakes the judge’s systematic bias into downstream reward models and benchmark scores. In response, Qwen-Image-Bench is co-developed with professional artists and tries to anchor evaluation in expert human standards rather than model-to-model scoring.
