Each RVQ layer quantizes the residual left by earlier layers, so lower layers encode coarse acoustic content and deeper layers add progressively finer detail. The design trades a deeper and potentially higher-fidelity discrete representation for a harder tokenizer training problem and a more expensive LM target. Second, generation is factorized into two stages in the same acoustic-token space. A backbone model generates full-track coarse tokens. A super-resolution model then fills in higher RVQ layers. Third, the paper introduces hybrid-attention training for the super-resolution model. The alignment objective uses causal attention to learn text/vocal ordering, while the layer-wise refinement objective uses full attention to exploit the whole audio context when reconstructing fine tokens.
