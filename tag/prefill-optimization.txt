论文还单独讨论了 selected-page decode semantics，并指出在低 batch 场景下，主瓶颈不是 selected-page attention arithmetic，而是 planning lifecycle。这个观察很重要，因为它解释了为什么很多稀疏 decode 方案在 paper benchmark 上很快，落到真实 serving 却收益有限：算子省下来的时间被 page selection、mask planning、runtime orchestration 吃掉了。

从当前给出的正文片段看，底座是 Qwen3-0.6B 几何配置。若全文有 perplexity 或 downstream LM 指标，阅读时要重点看 BF1 retrofit 后相对 dense-continued-training 的差距是否稳定很小，以及这种差距是否随上下文长度扩大。如果长上下文收益建立在明显牺牲短上下文 LM 质量之上，那方法的适用面会窄很多。19:T723,BF1 is a deterministic, block-aligned causal sparse-attention pattern. The sequence is partitioned into blocks of width b, with the reported public configuration using b = 64. For a query block j, the route includes four components: the causal self block, two exact local predecessor blocks, the first global block, and historical predecessor blocks at dyadic distances, i.e. j - 2^r starting from an offset of four blocks. In short, it is local + global-first + dyadic history.

The paper is explicit that this route is related to earlier log-sparse and dilated designs such as LogSparse Transformer and LongNet. The claimed novelty lies elsewhere: pretrained-model retrofit, matched topology-control experiments, and correctness-gated Blackwell measurements. The correctness-gated part is important. The authors do not only benchmark a fast path; they verify partial blocks, backward gradients, dense-path equivalence, future-token isolation, and opposite-order benchmarks up to 32K, which makes the systems claims more credible.

With fixed block width, each converted layer uses O(n log n) selected token interactions and has O(log n) graph communication depth. The communication-depth argument is the structural reason BF1 should beat pure local sliding windows: long-range information can propagate in logarithmically many sparse hops rather than linearly many local layers. Compared with static random nonlocal graphs, BF1 is deterministic and easier to optimize at the kernel level.

Another sensible design choice is that the paper does not replace all attention layers in the model and call it done. It reports an actual eight-layer retrofit separately from an all-layer timing-only ceiling. That makes the results more realistic for pretrained models, where replacing every layer with a sparse operator can be risky.1a:T74e,The strongest evidence is on the systems side. On an NVIDIA RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell GPU, the optimized BF16 implementation reportedly crosses dense attention around 4K context length. At 32K, the per-layer attention operator reaches 10.91× speedup. More importantly, the actual eight-layer retrofit reduces warm whole-model TTFT by 15.3% at 32K. That number is much smaller than the per-layer speedup, but it is also much more informative because it includes the rest of the model and runtime overheads.

For language modeling, the paper runs three matched Stage A training comparisons: dense continued training, equal-pair-count local sliding, and equal-pair-count static-random nonlocal graphs. BF1 ranks first in all three matched runs. The key point is not the ranking alone. The controls are designed to keep the sparse budget comparable, so the advantage is more plausibly due to topology rather than simply attending to more pairs. In other words, the paper tries to answer whether a dyadic-plus-first-block graph is a better sparse inductive bias for next-token LM than local-only or random sparse graphs under similar budget.

The decode analysis is also useful. The paper reports selected-page decode semantics and argues that, at low batch, the dominant unresolved cost is planning lifecycle rather than selected-page attention arithmetic. That observation explains why sparse decode methods often look strong in operator benchmarks but deliver limited gains in real serving stacks.

From the provided text, the tested geometry is based on Qwen3-0.6B. When reading the full paper, the most important thing to check is how much LM quality is lost relative to dense continued training, and whether that gap stays small across context lengths. If long-context efficiency comes with a noticeable short-context LM penalty, the deployment envelope becomes narrower.1b:T43b,A good skim order is: operator definition, systems measurements, then matched training controls.
