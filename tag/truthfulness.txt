18:T4be,They introduce CAPE (Capability Coupling Analysis of Phase Emergence). The key metric is *local coupling* within a family: γ12(N)=ΔTruthfulQA/ΔHellaSwag between adjacent sizes, i.e., a derivative of one capability with respect to another rather than with respect to N. They distinguish this from *population coupling* r(B1,B2), a Pearson correlation across a panel of models.
Empirically, γ12 is negative at small scale and crosses zero at a recipe-dependent critical size Nc, then grows roughly linearly with log10(N/Nc): γ12(N,D)=γ0(D)·log10(N/Nc(D)), with both slope and Nc depending on the training recipe D.
They argue Nc is independently shiftable by (i) data curation (Qwen generations), (ii) architecture/distillation/post-training (Gemma-4 at 4B showing “large-model-like” coupling), and (iii) width at fixed parameter count (width-normalized coupling being more consistently positive).
For mechanism, they report little evidence of “competing attention heads” in most models and suggest the bottleneck is closer to the output projection/readout than internal attention competition.
