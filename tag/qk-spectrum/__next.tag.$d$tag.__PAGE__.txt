第二步是 identify：作者设计了 controlled fault-source moves 和 always-on module-routed gradient correction。关键点是 source mechanism 仍然开启，只改变哪条模块路径收到 clean fp32 gradient。这样测的是“阻断通道”，不是“修复源头”。结果显示，来自 attention 内部和 attention 外部的误差都会汇聚到同一个 dominant QK channel；纠正 QK route 可以稳定训练，纠正 MLP route 不行。这给出了 source-channel dissociation：fault source is not failure channel。

第三步是 explain and contain：作者分析进入 QK channel 的条件，指出 temporal sign-coherence 是关键，即每个 head 的 error sign 在 optimizer steps 间持续一致。它不同于 per-tile scalar mean 或误差总能量。论文还用 equal-energy off-target control 和移除每个 run 自身 dominant QK weight directions 的实验，说明 QK channel 是早期 runaway 的驱动因素，不只是 collapse 的观测指标。最后提出 QK-Guard：平时不改变模型；在线监控 attention-logit saturation；当检测到 runaway 正在发展时，在 QK locus 启用 parameter-free per-head QK normalization。它不回滚训练，也不修复原始 fault，而是阻断下游通道。对照实验显示，同一触发时刻做 softmax-local action 或 learning-rate cut 不能救回训练，说明 action locus 比“是否响应及时”更关键。6:T6ee,实验围绕 GPT-2 small 在 OpenWebText 上的 bf16 训练 collapse 展开。作者报告 baseline 在观察窗口内各 seed 都 collapse，而 fp32 accumulation 相关条件没有观察到 collapse：包括 accumulator → fp32 的两个 seed、fp32/fp32 carry 的两个 seed，以及一个手动停止时仍健康的长 run，记录到 103400 logged steps。这里作者没有把它当 collapse-rate 估计，而是作为 sufficiency counterexample：在原 baseline 必崩的窗口内，单因素 fp32 accumulation 可以阻断该 fault。

定位实验区分了 streaming carry 的两类路径。running output O 在 backward 中通过 D-term 影响 score gradient，进而影响 dQ 和 dK，但不直接影响 dV；statistics carry 通过 saved log normalizer 重标定 recomputed probabilities P，会影响 dQ、dK 和 dV。论文借此解释为什么某些 carry precision 会表现为 QK 相关错误，而不是平均地污染所有 attention 参数。

识别实验把 fault 移动到 attention 外部，仍观察到同样的 QK spectral runaway；然后用 module-routed clean gradient 做通道阻断。结果是 QK route correction 能稳定训练，MLP route correction 不能。这是最关键的因果证据。

First, the authors reproduce a GPT-2-class collapse in a PyTorch implementation of FlashAttention-2's tiled online-softmax recurrence. This implementation exposes the running output O and the softmax statistics carry, which allows controlled precision edits. The decisive intervention is fp32 accumulation in the streaming accumulator: it prevents the reproduced collapse while preserving the tiled computation. This rules out several simpler explanations, including bf16 operands, tiling itself, the FlashAttention family as a whole, or a single-scale artifact.

Second, the authors introduce an identification assay based on controlled fault-source moves and always-on module-routed gradient correction. The source mechanism remains enabled, while clean fp32 gradients are routed through selected modules. This tests channel blocking rather than source repair. The result is the central source-channel dissociation: faults placed inside attention and faults placed outside attention both drive the same dominant QK channel; QK-route correction stabilizes training, while MLP-route correction does not.

Third, the paper explains how errors enter the QK channel. The distinguishing property is temporal sign-coherence: the error sign for a head persists across optimizer steps. This is separated from per-tile scalar mean and total error magnitude. The authors also test causality with equal-energy off-target controls and by removing each run's own dominant QK weight directions, showing that the QK channel drives the early runaway rather than merely tracking it.

Finally, the paper proposes QK-Guard, a dormant containment controller. It monitors attention-logit saturation and, once a runaway is developing, applies parameter-free per-head QK normalization at the QK locus. It does not repair the underlying fault or rewind the run. At the same trigger point, a softmax-local intervention and a learning-rate cut fail, indicating that the rescue depends on where the action is applied.8:T5ef,The empirical base is a bf16 GPT-2 small training setup on OpenWebText. The baseline collapses in every seed within the observed window. In contrast, fp32-accumulation variants do not collapse in the reported runs: two seeds for the accumulator-to-fp32 arm, two seeds for the fp32/fp32 carry condition, and one long run that was manually stopped while still healthy at 103400 logged steps. The paper treats these as sufficiency counterexamples, not as collapse-rate estimates.

The accumulator analysis separates the backward paths of the streaming carries. The running output O re-enters backward through the D-term, perturbing the score gradient and therefore dQ and dK, but not dV. The statistics carry affects recomputed probabilities through the saved log normalizer and can affect dQ, dK, and dV. This explains why a carry-precision fault can show up as a QK-driven instability rather than as uniform attention-parameter noise.

The channel-identification experiments move the fault outside attention and still observe the same QK spectral runaway. With module-routed clean gradients, QK correction blocks the collapse while MLP correction does not. The mechanism experiments manipulate error-sign sequences and show that temporal sign-coherence, rather than scalar mean or aggregate energy, predicts entry into the QK channel. Section 3 is the key mechanism section. The module-routed gradient correction assay is the central experiment: the fault remains active, but only one route receives a clean gradient. This is what makes the QK-channel conclusion stronger than a standard kernel fix.

Section 4 is worth reading for the temporal sign-coherence manipulations and the causal controls around dominant QK directions. These experiments explain why some low-precision errors become dangerous and why QK spectral growth should be treated as a driver, not only as a symptom.
