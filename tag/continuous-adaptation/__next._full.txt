A runtime copy is available immediately from the next step within the same episode. A candidate copy is stored but cannot guide future episodes until it is validated. During validation, compatible hidden candidates register predictions before the agent acts; later observations provide evidence for or against the predicted effect. The source episode is excluded, and each later episode contributes at most one conclusive verdict per item. Candidates with enough support and predictive precision are promoted to verified memory.
