a:T569,The method is State Transition Pretraining, or STP. Training data consists of step-level tuples (s_t, a_t, s_{t+1}), which can be extracted from existing trajectories or collected automatically from interactive environments. STP jointly optimizes two objectives. Forward dynamics predicts the next GUI state from the current state and action, effectively an image-text-to-image task. This is meant to build an internal model of how GUI states change under actions such as click, type, or scroll. Inverse dynamics predicts the action from consecutive screenshots, an image-image-to-text task. This forces the model to identify what changed, where the interaction occurred, and which visual details are action-relevant. A notable design choice is the use of a unified multimodal model rather than separate modules for perception, action classification, and state prediction. That allows both objectives to share a common tokenized interface and makes the pretrained model easier to transfer into downstream trajectory fine-tuning. The paper positions STP as initialization rather than replacement: it removes some of the burden from trajectory SFT by learning local structure first. The ablations suggest that forward and inverse dynamics provide complementary signals, and that the gains are not sensitive to whether transitions are sampled as full trajectories or as independent steps.b:T40f,The main results cover both desktop and mobile GUI benchmarks. On AgentNetBench, under matched trajectory fine-tuning settings, STP improves average success rate by 2.3% to 6.2% and coordinate success rate by 1.6% to 6.8%, depending on the fine-tuning split. On AndroidControl, it improves step success rate by 0.9% and coordinate prediction accuracy by 1.3%. On GUIOdyssey, it improves step success rate by 0.6% and coordinate prediction accuracy by 1.6%. The paper also provides optimization evidence: the left panel of Figure 1 shows better cross-entropy behavior during downstream trajectory fine-tuning when initialized with STP. The scalability results are important. With fine-tuning data fixed, increasing transition-pretraining data steadily improves downstream performance. With STP fixed, scaling trajectory fine-tuning data from 2K to 18K still leaves STP ahead of the fine-tune-only baseline.
