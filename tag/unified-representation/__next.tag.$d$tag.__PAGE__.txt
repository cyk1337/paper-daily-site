Instead of animating a face after waveform synthesis, the authors adapt the speech refined head with LoRA and turn it into a Joint Refined Head. Each 5 Hz LLM output is expanded into five conditioning vectors. The JRH autoregressively processes these vectors together with the embedding of the previous speech token, producing five hidden states for the current chunk. These states form the unified representation H_t. Two streaming branches consume H_t: the speech branch uses a frozen CosyVoice 3 speech decoder and vocoder to synthesize audio, while the motion branch uses an autoregressive Motion Decoder to generate continuous facial-motion chunks, which are rendered with a reference portrait.
