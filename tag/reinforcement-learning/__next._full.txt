The system also uses failure-driven synthesis, where failed trajectories are diagnosed and converted into targeted new tasks.19:T6fd,ReasonUni is built on a unified multimodal model with a shared discrete token space, instantiated in the paper with an X-Omni-style pure AR architecture. Images are quantized into visual tokens by a visual tokenizer, text remains language tokens, and visual inputs and outputs share the same semantic codebook. This gives one backbone two conditional policies: understanding p(theta)(T|V) and generation p(theta)(V|T). The paper’s critique of prior reconstruction methods is that they mostly supervise the model to describe what is already present. That encourages copying and preservation, but does not force explicit reasoning about intended edits, structural changes, or invariances. ReasonUni changes the training problem into reasoning-driven editing. Given a source image, an edit instruction, and a target image, the model first generates a textual description of the desired target state, then generates the target image conditioned on that description. The intermediate text is therefore not just a caption; it is a reasoning-bearing representation of the post-edit world state. To jointly optimize both stages, the paper introduces TreeGRPO. For the same input, the model samples multiple reasoning paths, each treated as a branch. Under each branch, it samples multiple generated images as leaves. Rewards are then used at two levels: leaf rewards are aggregated to produce a more reliable branch-level signal for the understanding module, and within-branch normalization guides the generation module under a fixed target-state description. The key idea is structured credit assignment. A reasoning path is judged by the distribution of images it induces, which reduces the noise of single-sample feedback and lets image-level rewards shape the intermediate reasoning process.1a:T4dd,From the provided full-text sections, the paper reports consistent gains on both understanding and generation benchmarks relative to UAE, X-Omni, and related baselines. On the understanding side, it improves both perception and reasoning on MMBench; the logical-reasoning subset gains 6.77 points over X-Omni, whereas UAE gains 3.38 on the same subset. That is an important result because it suggests the benefit is not merely better visual-detail reconstruction.
