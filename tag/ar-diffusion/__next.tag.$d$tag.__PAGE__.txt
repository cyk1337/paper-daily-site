9:T734,The system, DuetGen, combines a Qwen3.5-2B autoregressive planner with a 4B single-stream DiT through what the authors call DeepFusion. Given a prompt, the planner emits a bbox-content plan, where each region contains four coordinates and a text string. Instead of feeding only the final serialized plan to the renderer, the method extracts the planner’s final-layer hidden states for both prompt tokens and plan tokens, then maps them through a lightweight projector into condition tokens for the DiT. This is the key design choice: the renderer conditions on structured continuous states, not just discrete layout symbols. Training uses three coordinated objectives. First, the planner is trained with the standard autoregressive next-token loss over the serialized plan. Second, the DiT is trained with a text-region-weighted flow-matching loss. Spatial locations inside rasterized reference text boxes receive a larger weight gamma, so the denoising objective focuses more strongly on text-bearing regions, where fidelity is hardest and most important. Third, the model adds auxiliary coordinate supervision: hidden states corresponding to each coordinate span are mean-pooled and regressed to the normalized bounding box. This head is used only during training, with the purpose of making geometric information more explicit in the planner states that later condition the DiT. At inference time, the model further applies Phase-Aware Attention Modulation, or PAM. PAM selectively strengthens attention from image patches inside a planned region to the matched bbox and content states, improving region-specific execution when multiple text regions or long strings compete for attention. The contribution is therefore not a simple pipeline combination, but a three-level coupling across representation, objective, and attention execution.a:T481,From the provided full-content excerpt, the setup uses a 2B planner and a 4B DiT, with the DiT first pretrained from random initialization for general image generation before joint planning-rendering training.
