9:T4c0,Self-Indexing Attention builds a shared transform-domain sign-magnitude representation. A structured orthogonal transform (motivated by Hadamard-style transforms with random sign flips, LSH intuition, and transform-domain KV compression such as QJL/TurboQuant) is applied to Q/K/V. The per-coordinate sign of transformed keys is stored as a 1-bit, token-level index. Query–key similarity is approximated via sign agreement (a proxy for angular similarity).

Because sign bits are part of the compressed key payload, the same representation supports retrieval and compression without separate index metadata.

To make it work across inference stages:
- Prefill: group queries to amortize selection cost. Instead of average pooling (which can cancel signs), use absmax-sign pooling: for each coordinate, take the sign of the query with the largest absolute value, preserving evidence important to any query in the group.
- Decode: perform packed 1-bit sign-agreement scoring for fast candidate selection, then apply norm-weighted agreement to reintroduce magnitude information lost in sign-only matching.
