9:T426,The method represents a video as B latent chunks. Each chunk follows K noise levels and is denoised from x_b^K to x_b^0. A denoising step consists of a network prediction F_theta followed by a sampler update S_j. The key abstraction is a context-selection policy Phi_b(j,r), which chooses the noise state of historical chunk b-r when current chunk b is at denoising step j. This policy is tied to the history window W, attention masks, cached representations, sampler behavior, execution scheduling, and training-state sources. Three representative policies are studied: clean history uses clean endpoints from past chunks; same-level history uses past chunks at the same denoising level as the current chunk; progressive history makes more distant history cleaner. Compared with Self Forcing, which mainly addresses the source mismatch between training and inference histories, this work focuses on the denoising state of the historical context. Compared with pure streaming execution optimizations, it analyzes the modeling choice and execution graph together.a:T425,Self-Correcting RAG is a two-stage unified framework: pre-generation context optimization + inference-time search-based generation.
(1) MMKP Context Selector (input side): context selection is formalized as a Multi-dimensional Multiple-Choice Knapsack Problem (MMKP). Retrieved chunks U are semantically clustered into groups G_i using embedding cosine similarity (within-group redundancy). A multiple-choice constraint selects at most one chunk per group, maximizing fused utility (information density) under multi-dimensional capacity constraints (at least token budget plus redundancy penalty). The formulation is NP-hard (reduction proof in the appendix).
(2) NLI-Guided MCTS Generator (output side): generation is treated as path planning with Monte Carlo Tree Search rather than greedy decoding.
