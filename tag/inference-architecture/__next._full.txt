1b:T48b,Dual-Flow Transformer uses an asymmetric shared-KV design. The primary flow is a full standard causal Transformer that processes the entire prompt independently and produces the only persistent KV cache, with no dependency on the auxiliary flow. The auxiliary flow only reads the primary flow's states and KV cache, never writes persistent state, and never feeds back into the primary flow. The two flows share all attention, MLP, and output matrices, with only separate token embeddings and lightweight learned coupling vectors. The auxiliary flow is not evaluated across the full prompt during prefill — it only runs at the final prompt token position and each subsequent generated token, so prefill compute cost and KV cache size are nearly identical to a standard Transformer. Training uses a mixture likelihood over the two flows' next-token distributions as the supervision signal.
