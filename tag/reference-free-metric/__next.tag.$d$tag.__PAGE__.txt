5:T908,方法核心是 RAFS，Reasoning–Answer Faithfulness Score，一个 0–100 的实例级分数。它试图估计的不是“答案对不对”，而是“这条 reasoning trace 是否局部可信、是否真正推出了这个答案、在重采样和反事实干预下是否稳定”。作者把这个目标拆成几类可观测信号，再做集成。

第一部分是 step validity。把自然语言推理链切成 atomic steps，用 verifier g 对每一步在给定题目和前缀推理下是否成立打分。这里 verifier 可以是 LLM critic、确定性算术检查、矛盾检测器等的组合。为了既惩罚单个致命错误又保持可微和平滑，作者不是简单取平均，而是把几何均值和最小值混合，得到一个对“最早致命错误”敏感的局部可信度分数，并返回 earliest suspect step。

第二部分是 reasoning-to-answer entailment。即使每一步看起来都顺，最终答案也可能不是由整条链推出，而是后验拼接出来的。于是作者估计 e = P_g(R ⊧ A | x)，衡量完整推理链是否蕴含最终答案。这个设计是为了压低 post-hoc rationalization 的分数。

第三部分是 answer consensus 与 conditional reasoning consistency。作者通过重采样多条解题轨迹，观察答案是否收敛、在给定同一题目时 reasoning 与 answer 的条件一致性如何。这样做是为了区分“偶然答对一次”和“稳定地沿相近推理得到同一结论”。

第四部分是 targeted counterfactual sensitivity。作者认为仅靠表面 entailment 仍可能被“因果惰性”的漂亮解释骗过，所以会对关键步骤做定向干预，检查答案是否按预期方向变化。如果改动一个枢纽步骤后答案几乎不动，说明原始解释可能只是装饰性的，不是真正驱动答案的因果链。这个部分是 RAFS 最有辨识度的设计，因为它试图把 explanation plausibility 和 causal relevance 区分开。

最后，RAFS 输出不仅有总分，还包括 failure type、最早可疑步骤和 abstention decision。论文明确强调它不是 reasoning truth oracle：如果模型在多次采样中稳定重复同一个误解，RAFS 仍可能给出较高一致性分数。因此它测的是 observable faithfulness，而不是绝对正确性。6:T938,The main proposal is RAFS, the Reasoning–Answer Faithfulness Score, a 0–100 instance-level score. It does not try to estimate whether the answer is correct in an absolute sense. Instead, it estimates whether the observed reasoning trace is locally credible, whether it entails the final answer, and whether that reasoning–answer relation is stable under resampling and targeted interventions.

The first component is step validity. The chain of thought is segmented into atomic steps, and a verifier g scores whether each step follows from the problem and the preceding steps. The verifier can combine an LLM critic, deterministic arithmetic checks, contradiction detection, and related tools. Rather than averaging step scores, the paper uses a mixture of geometric mean and minimum so that a single fatal step is penalized strongly while the score remains smooth. The earliest low-confidence step is also returned as the suspect step.

The second component is reasoning-to-answer entailment. Even if each step looks plausible, the final answer may still be post-hoc or only loosely connected to the chain. The paper therefore estimates e = P_g(R entails A | x), which is meant to reduce the score of polished but unsupported explanations.

The third component is answer consensus and conditional reasoning consistency under resampling. Multiple sampled solutions are used to test whether the answer is stable and whether similar reasoning patterns lead to the same conclusion. This helps distinguish one-off lucky hits from stable reasoning behavior.

The fourth and most distinctive component is targeted counterfactual sensitivity. The authors argue that entailment alone is not enough because a model may produce explanations that sound coherent but are causally inert. They therefore perturb pivotal reasoning steps and check whether the final answer changes in the expected direction. If the answer remains unchanged after a meaningful intervention, the original explanation is treated as less faithful.

RAFS outputs not only a score but also a failure type, the earliest suspect step, and an abstention decision. The paper is careful to state that RAFS is not a truth oracle. If a model consistently repeats the same misconception across samples, the score can still be high. So the target is observable faithfulness, not guaranteed correctness.7:T596,The paper is framed as a methodology paper, and an important detail is that the GSM8K/MATH confirmatory study is explicitly described as preregistered and results-blind. That means the emphasis is on the evaluation design, annotation protocol, intervention rules, calibration choices, and falsifiability of the setup, rather than on a finalized leaderboard-style empirical claim.

What is clear from the full text is the evaluation structure. First, the paper distinguishes four outcome types: reasoning-correct / answer-correct, reasoning-incorrect / answer-incorrect, reasoning-incorrect / answer-correct, and reasoning-correct / answer-incorrect. Second, the authors include a feasibility pilot only to validate pipeline execution, intervention coverage, and cost, but not to tune confirmatory hypotheses. Third, the intended evaluation includes detection of silent failures, localization of the earliest suspect step, and practical abstention or regeneration decisions.
