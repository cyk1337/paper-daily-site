Each rationale therefore commits to a distribution u(r). The objective scores that per-rationale distribution using a shifted negated Brier score, with log score behaving similarly in the experiments. The central identity is J(E_r u)=E_r J(u)+Var_r(u) for the Brier score. Scoring the mixture of multiple rationales therefore equals the average per-rationale score plus a disagreement bonus. Per-rationale scoring avoids this and has the calibrated optimum u(r)=q(x) for every rationale, so even a single sampled trajectory can return a calibrated probability. The practical recipe is to calibrate first, then reinforce: train the readout distribution first, then reinforce reasoning with the proper score as reward while anchoring the policy with KL to the calibrated model.
