b:T489,The paper combines a formal-language/logic characterization with empirical validation.
- Starting point: prior work (Li & Cotterell, 2025) links fixed-precision Transformer LMs with global attention to a specific fragment of linear temporal logic (LTL) with a single past operator.
- Main theoretical step: prove that adding local attention corresponds to adding a new temporal operator, enabling recognition of a strictly larger family of regular languages, notably locally testable languages (closely related to n-gram-like local pattern properties).
- Complementarity result: show that using only local attention makes the computation depend on a fixed-length suffix, reducing expressivity to definite languages; hence local is not a drop-in replacement for global.
- Positional encoding check: evaluate sinusoidal (SiPE) and rotary (RoPE) encodings and argue they do not compensate for the absence of local attention.
