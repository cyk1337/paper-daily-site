9:T4ba,OPSRD uses two contexts for the same base model. The student samples an on-policy reasoning trajectory under an ordinary role-free prompt. A frozen teacher, with the student adapter disabled, receives an expert role prompt and scores the exact same student prefixes. The teacher does not need to generate a complete solution; it supplies next-token distributions at the decision points the student actually visits. Only a low-rank adapter on the student is updated.

The method selects the highest-entropy half of student positions by default, with ρ=0.5, so supervision focuses on uncertain choices rather than syntax or already-determined continuations. Distributions for distillation use temperature η=1.1. Sampling uses top-p=0.95 and top-k=20, while the loss keeps the full vocabulary. The main objective is teacher-weighted forward KL with per-token contribution clipping. The motivation is that reverse KL gives weak correction when the student assigns near-zero probability to alternatives supported by the teacher, whereas forward KL can raise those underestimated alternatives.
