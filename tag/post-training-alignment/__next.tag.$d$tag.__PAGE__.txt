9:T407,BALTO extracts checkable factual claims from generated responses, verifies them against provided evidence, and maps the verification results back to token-level signals. Its main mechanism is balanced token-level policy optimization. Hallucinated tokens receive negative advantages, faithful tokens receive positive compensatory advantages, and neutral tokens are excluded from the update. The total positive and negative faithfulness-driven signals are balanced within each response.

This makes the update act like a redistribution of probability mass: lower the probability of unsupported spans and raise the probability of supported spans, instead of suppressing the entire answer. The design directly addresses two weaknesses of response-level RL. First, a local factual error can contaminate gradients for all tokens. Second, a model can reduce penalties by becoming shorter or less informative.
