a:T41e,POETS relies on the duality between KL-regularized optimal policies and rewards. Given a reference policy, the optimal policy implicitly defines a reward through the log-ratio between the optimized policy and the reference policy, up to scaling and normalization terms. The method trains an ensemble of policies whose implicit rewards match online observed data. Diversity is induced with online Poisson bootstrapping: each incoming sample receives an independent Poisson(1) weight for each ensemble member, which approximates bootstrap resampling while remaining compatible with sequential data collection and replay buffers. Action selection can then sample an ensemble member, giving a Thompson-sampling-like exploration rule. To make this feasible for LLMs, POETS uses a Trunk & Branch architecture: the backbone is shared and evaluated once, while independent LoRA branches carry the ensemble-specific variation.
