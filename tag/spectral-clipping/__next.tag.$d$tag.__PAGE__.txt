9:T453,Muon flattens the singular spectrum of the momentum matrix, producing an update whose singular values are roughly all one. Musec replaces this with spectral clipping: only singular values above a threshold are clipped, while the skewed spectrum of the original momentum is preserved. The update therefore avoids injecting excessive magnitude into weak momentum directions. The paper also gives Musec a constrained optimization interpretation: it solves a Frobenius-penalized quadratic subproblem under a spectral-norm constraint, complementing prior interpretations of Muon as unconstrained steepest descent. For practical training, the authors introduce Soft Musec, which replaces hard clipping with a smooth saturation function and approximates the resulting matrix transform using coupled Newton-Schulz iterations, avoiding explicit SVD. The theory covers stochastic nonconvex nonsmooth objectives under weak convexity assumptions and proves convergence to a Goldstein stationary point, addressing recent results showing that exact-polar Muon may fail to converge in general nonsmooth nonconvex settings.a:T438,For a Muon matrix B_t with compact SVD B_t=UΣV^T, polar Muon uses the canonical partial polar factor UV^T, replacing every nonzero singular value by one. The paper also derives a polar/absolute-value formulation and explains why eigenvalues near the clipping threshold are numerically delicate.
