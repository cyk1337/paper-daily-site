18:T4eb,BrowserForge has four main components. First, open-web URL sourcing and cleaning: candidate URLs are drawn from Common Crawl and filtered into a large pool of real, reachable, publicly accessible websites. This replaces fixed website whitelists with the open web itself as the source of diversity. Second, parallel browser sandbox orchestration: a cluster manager schedules hundreds of browser sandboxes through a shared work queue, turning web interaction collection into a high-throughput process. Third, a Proposer–Solver synthesis loop: a Proposer agent inspects a rendered page and proposes an executable task, and a Solver agent attempts the task and records the trajectory. Structured page information such as the accessibility tree is used only during synthesis as an auxiliary signal; it is not part of the deployed agent’s inference input. Fourth, verification and cleaning: rule-based and model-based filters remove invalid or low-quality trajectories, and the surviving reasoning traces are rewritten into a unified chain-of-thought format. The design choice is deliberate: use structure when generating and validating data, but keep the final policy interface purely visual to avoid HTML/a11y brittleness and token overhead at inference time.19:T698,DART-SD has three main components. First, it builds an Interaction-State Transition Graph from teacher rollouts. Nodes are not single actions; they are cumulative interaction states, defined by the task-relevant information acquired so far. To make this possible, the method introduces information atom abstraction: tool outputs are canonicalized, semantically equivalent responses are mapped to the same atom, and non-informative outputs such as status-only or empty responses are collapsed away. This lets trajectories with different action orders but the same acquired information land on the same node, which naturally captures the diamond topology induced by order-independent sub-goals. Second, for a failed student rollout, the method performs success-reachable projection and identifies the Critical Topological Breakpoint. Instead of cutting by timestep, it asks whether each cumulative student state still lies in the region from which success is reachable in the empirical graph. The CTB is the point where the rollout leaves that success-supported region. Third, starting from a recovery anchor near the CTB, the method retrieves a success-supported continuation from the ISTG and applies localized supervision only to the recovery segment, while preserving the valid prefix. Training is progressive self-distillation: the student rolls out, exposes its current capability boundary, receives CTB-guided local correction, and gradually extends the length of valid interaction prefixes. Compared with vanilla SFT, supervision is localized. Compared with RL, the method does not rely on sparse reward propagation; it uses explicit graph structure extracted from teacher behavior.1a:T47e,The study evaluates on five benchmarks and two model scales. Instead of sampling N independent full trajectories per prompt, BPO samples one backbone trajectory, chooses branch points along it, restores the sandbox at those states, expands multiple sibling continuations, and computes advantages using a sibling baseline within each branch family.

The pipeline has five steps. (1) Roll out one backbone trajectory under the current policy. (2) Select M branch points on the backbone. The paper uses an entropy-based scheduler rather than uniform sampling: branching budget is allocated to steps where the policy is most uncertain. This is sensible because branching at already-certain decisions adds little information, while high-entropy steps are more likely to be decision bottlenecks. (3) At each selected branch state, snapshot/restore is used to fork K alternative actions or continuations. (4) Each fork is rolled out to termination to obtain a final reward. (5) For policy updates, the advantage of one sibling rollout is computed against the mean return of its siblings under the same parent state, yielding a tree-structured sibling baseline, and then plugged into a PPO-style clipped objective.
