c:T4c5,The method has two stages: structural compression followed by capability recovery. The structural stage uses Iterative Puzzle, a sequential version of the Puzzle framework. Instead of compressing the model in one large step, it alternates hardware-aware structural compression with short knowledge-distillation recovery phases. This allows each compression step to account for representation drift and inter-layer dependencies introduced by earlier steps. The compressed dimensions include heterogeneous channel pruning of routed MoE experts, heterogeneous reduction of active experts, and pruning of the SSM state size in Mamba layers. The main design choice is non-uniform capacity allocation across depth: sensitive regions keep more capacity, while more redundant regions are compressed harder. The recovery stage combines medium- and long-context knowledge distillation, RL post-training for software-engineering and agentic reasoning tasks, mixed FP8/NVFP4 inference, and a Multi-Token Prediction head for speculative decoding.
