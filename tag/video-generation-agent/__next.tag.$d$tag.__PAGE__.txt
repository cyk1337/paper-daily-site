9:T594,VideoGen-Agent uses a shared multimodal policy in a reasoning-action-observation loop. At each step, the policy conditions on the prompt, previous actions, and tool observations, then selects a tool and specifies its arguments. The six task categories are Procedural Knowledge, Single-Entity Identity, Multi-Entity Identity, Physics Simulation, Compositional Scene, and Multi-Shot. Each category has a default tool subset and workflow: procedural prompts use text retrieval for steps and motion details; identity prompts use visual retrieval and image-conditioned or multi-reference generation; physics prompts invoke simulators and pass motion conditions to a motion-conditioned generator; compositional prompts generate candidates and use verification tools such as detection and depth estimation for regeneration; multi-shot prompts generate phases sequentially and use the last frame of each segment to preserve continuity. Training has two stages. First, the policy is supervised on teacher-distilled tool-use trajectories, with the paper mentioning Claude Opus 4.7 as part of the trajectory construction process. Second, the policy is optimized with multitask agentic RL. A hybrid reward scores both tool use and generated video quality, while task advantage normalization balances learning signals across the six categories.
