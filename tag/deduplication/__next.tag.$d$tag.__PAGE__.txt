9:T42f,The framework explicitly decouples duplicate detection from copy retention.
Detection (scalable and shard-invariant): documents are segmented at natural boundaries; each segment is normalized (formatting/casing/whitespace etc., per the paper’s rules) and exact-hashed; distributed key–value aggregation computes global occurrence counts for each hash, ensuring cross-shard visibility.
Retention (explicit policy): instead of keep-one/keep-k, the method defines a retention function k=f(global_frequency, segment_length). High-frequency segments (likely boilerplate/templates) are aggressively compressed; longer repeated spans get smaller budgets as they more often reflect direct copying or templated reuse; low-frequency repeats are treated conservatively to avoid deleting legitimate quotations or mild reuse. A coherence-preserving deletion step reduces fragmentation when removing repeated spans.

The main design has three components. First, dual-track cleaning combines domain-aware heuristic rules with model-driven document understanding. Instead of applying uniform thresholds, the rules are calibrated per domain so that math, code, and other structurally unusual but useful content are not removed simply because they violate generic web-text statistics. A data understanding model annotates documents with multi-aspect writing quality, content value, and domain tags. Second, hybrid deduplication combines n-gram-style filtering with semantic deduplication. The paper highlights two hard cases: templatized clusters, where pages share a structural skeleton but differ in local parameters, and redundant crawling cases that fall just below an LSH threshold. CuraWeb uses a soft semantic deduplication strategy based on weighted voting over multiple similarity edges. A document is removed only when its accumulated penalty exceeds a threshold, which reduces over-filtering compared with hard similarity cutoffs. Third, multi-objective sampling combines writing quality, content value, and diversity. Writing quality is aggregated with a harmonic mean to penalize severe defects on any single dimension. Content value is summed linearly to reward expertise and information density.
