第二段是两级蒸馏。先做 layer-wise hidden alignment：每个 KDA 层对齐对应 teacher hidden state，teacher 冻结，误差跨层 detach，只训练 KDA 参数 193.6M，学习率 1e-4 到 1e-5，batch 为 8192 tokens/step。这个阶段把 CE 从 9.48 拉到 4.125，但 C-Eval 仍只有 25.5%。随后做 end-to-end forward KL distillation，在预训练文本上最小化 teacher-student next-token 分布的 forward KL，不混 CE；训练范围扩大到 KDA + 全部 MLP + LayerNorm，共 457.9M 参数，embedding、lm_head 和保留的 7 个 attention 层冻结。为适配单卡显存，作者实现了 chunked fp32 KL，避免物化全量 logits。7000 steps、总计 57.3M tokens 后，valid CE 到 2.9385，teacher 为 2.8108，gap 仅 +0.128 nats，valid KL 0.160，但 C-Eval 只有 28.8%。

第三段是诊断与修复。诊断上，作者构造了 161 道来自训练语料、与 benchmark 零重叠的四选一题，每题做四次 cyclic option rotation，内容不变，只旋转 A/B/C/D 标签。若模型跟随内容，正确标签应随 rotation 改变；若只是知识丢失，预测应更接近均匀随机。实际结果是学生模型 81.06% 时间选 A，161 题中有 106 题在四次 rotation 下都保持同一标签，且正确选项平均 margin 为 -0.117 nats。这说明它不是单纯不会，而是输出接口塌缩到了标签偏置。修复上，作者加入一个 1000-step 的 Stage 3b，用 completion-only KL 在 teacher 验证过的 MCQ permutations、诗歌、翻译 QA 上做格式修复。这个阶段不追求全面分布拟合，而是专门修补“问题-选项-答案”接口。之后再做 persona alignment，包括 SFT、identity booster 和一轮 on-policy DPO，并用 15 个 benchmark guard points 监控遗忘。19:Tb2e,The method has three stages. Stage 1 is architecture conversion. From Qwen3-0.6B-Base, every fourth layer remains native GQA, leaving 7 full-attention layers and replacing the other 21 with KDA. The KDA stack uses 16 heads, head dimension 128, causal short convolution with k=4, L2-normalized q/k, low-rank forget gates, beta write gates, gated RMSNorm, and NoPE. This yields a hybrid model rather than a pure linear-attention model: 75% of layers use O(1) recurrent state, while the retained GQA layers still have linearly growing KV cache. A notable engineering point is gate initialization. The paper shows that near-total retention destabilizes the recurrent state, while excessive forgetting performs worse than random; their adopted g6 initialization gives a median forget half-life of about 6.3 tokens.

Stage 2 is two-step distillation. The first step is layer-wise hidden-state alignment: each KDA layer is trained against the corresponding teacher hidden state, with the teacher frozen and errors detached across layers. Only KDA parameters are updated in this phase, 193.6M trainable parameters total, at 8,192 tokens per step. This reduces CE from 9.48 to about 4.13, but C-Eval stays at chance. The second step is end-to-end forward-KL distillation on pretraining text. The objective is pure forward KL between teacher and student next-token distributions, without mixing in CE. Trainable parameters now include KDA, all MLPs, and all LayerNorms, 457.9M in total, while embeddings, tied lm_head, and the 7 retained attention layers remain frozen. To fit a single 32GB GPU budget, they implement chunked fp32 KL instead of materializing full logits. After 7,000 steps over 57.3M tokens, validation CE reaches 2.9385 versus the teacher’s 2.8108, a gap of only +0.128 nats, with validation KL 0.160, yet C-Eval is still only 28.8%.

Stage 3 is diagnosis and repair. The diagnostic is a four-permutation multiple-choice test: 161 clean four-option questions are constructed from the training corpus with zero benchmark overlap, and each question is evaluated under four cyclic rotations of option labels. A content-following model should track the correct option as labels rotate; a model that simply forgot should fail roughly uniformly. Instead, the converted model predicts “A” 81.06% of the time, and 106 of 161 questions keep the same predicted label under all four rotations. The average margin on the correct option is negative, -0.117 nats. This points to label-interface collapse rather than uniform forgetting. The repair stage then runs 1,000 steps of completion-only KL on teacher-verified MCQ permutations, poetry, and translation QA, explicitly targeting the question-option-answer interface. The paper also compares extreme forgetting and extreme retention, showing that one collapses performance below random while the other destabilizes the recurrent state. Second, layer-wise hidden alignment reduces CE from 9.48 to roughly 4.13, but C-Eval remains around the 25% random baseline, demonstrating that hidden-trajectory matching is insufficient for restoring decision behavior. Third, end-to-end KL distillation over 7,000 steps and 57.3M tokens yields validation CE 2.9385 versus teacher 2.8108, only +0.128 nats apart, with validation KL 0.160, yet C-Eval reaches only 28.8% compared with the teacher’s 50.6%.
