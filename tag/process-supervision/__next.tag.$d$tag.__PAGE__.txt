The dataset consists of 237 step-numbered solutions to 79 Humanity’s Last Exam physics questions, generated by three frontier models, with no synthetic error insertion. Each trace is labeled for two targets: final-answer correctness and the location of the first false step. The reference standard combines physicist annotations, an independent LLM debate, and source-masked adjudication, which is a sensible way to reduce single-annotator bias on difficult technical derivations.

The key experimental device is an information ladder that varies only the epistemic status of the answer shown to the monitor: blind, unverified answer, certified answer, and answer revealed only after a blind commitment. There is also a reconsideration control and a stepwise online condition. This lets the authors separate error detection from first-error localization, and further split traces into wrong-answer cases versus correct-answer-but-flawed cases. That decomposition is the core contribution. It turns a vague question about “monitoring reasoning” into a cleaner measurement of whether the monitor is validating the argument or merely using the answer as a shortcut.

Another good choice is that the same trace is evaluated under different monitoring conditions. 这比直接做 token-level process reward 更适合搜索任务，因为搜索任务的有效中间单位不是 token，也不一定是整句 reasoning，而是“找到并确认了哪条证据”。作者因此把 credit assignment 的对象从 action surface form 转成 evidence semantics。和纯 outcome-based GRPO 相比，FactAgent 仍然利用 group rollouts，但不再让整条轨迹共享同一个粗粒度 advantage，而是通过事实聚类和后验估计，把奖励分配到真正有贡献的中间步骤上。

从设计取舍看，这个方法避免了额外训练 reward model，也不依赖人工标注每一步是否正确，代价则是需要可靠的事实抽取与聚类。如果抽取质量差，后验 utility 估计会被噪声污染。全文也承认这一点，因此 fact store 既是方法优势，也是误差传播的主要入口。c:T89d,FactAgent has two main components: a fact-centric state representation and a Bayesian utility estimation scheme for dense process supervision.

On the agent side, the paper departs from standard ReAct-style agents that keep only raw text history. The state explicitly includes a fact store. After a search action returns an observation, the agent can take an assert action that extracts structured facts from the observation and writes them into the store. The state is therefore represented as the question, the current observation, and the accumulated fact set. These facts are treated as discrete semantic evidence units rather than plain text snippets. This is important because the method assigns credit at the level of evidence semantics, not at the level of surface-form actions.

On the reward side, the paper estimates fact utility from grouped rollouts. Semantically equivalent facts are first clustered so that the same evidence is not split across multiple textual variants. Then, using final outcomes from a group of trajectories, the method performs Bayesian estimation of the posterior utility of each fact cluster. Intuitively, if a fact cluster appears disproportionately often in successful trajectories, its posterior utility increases. These fact-level utilities are then converted into dense step-level rewards for policy optimization.

This differs from standard outcome-based GRPO, where all steps in a trajectory inherit the same coarse trajectory-level signal. Here, group rollouts are still used, but the reward is redistributed through an explicit evidence layer. That makes the method better matched to search tasks, where the meaningful intermediate unit is often not a token or a reasoning sentence, but a retrieved and validated piece of evidence.

The design also avoids training an external reward model and does not require human annotation of intermediate steps. The trade-off is that the whole pipeline depends on the quality of fact extraction and fact clustering. If those are noisy, the posterior utility estimates can be corrupted.
