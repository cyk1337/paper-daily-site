A good design choice is that the free-text description is constrained to acoustic facts only. Interpretive emotion words are banned, so the task does not collapse into keyword matching.

Construction proceeds through annotation, evidence authoring and hardening, question generation, and speech synthesis. A small number of evidence sessions are embedded into roughly 100k-token conversation histories together with topic-screened filler sessions and emotive but answer-free distractors. Needle positions are stratified over early, middle, and late parts of the history, and multi-evidence arcs are distributed across sessions.
