9:T461,RSM-full is an online clustered-memory pipeline with two main design choices:
1) Cosine-gated max-member merge write rule: maintain an L2-normalized centroid μ_m per atom/cluster. A new chunk embedding h merges into an atom if cosine(h, μ_m) ≥ τ OR cosine(h, any member in atom) ≥ τ; otherwise create a new atom. The max-member condition makes merging more permissive than pure centroid thresholding, aiming to reduce fragmentation and mis-clustering under streaming drift.
- τ calibration: per embedding space, set τ ≈ Q0.70 of pairwise cosine similarities using ≤50 unlabeled validation examples.
- Write complexity: O(Md) per chunk.
2) Atom-aware grouped context packer: retrieve top-K atoms, rank members within each atom, and pack retrieved chunks grouped by atom (with headers and temporal ordering) rather than a flat relevance-sorted concatenation. The intent is to preserve local coherence and reduce attention interference from interleaving unrelated snippets.
