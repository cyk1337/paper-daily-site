Tokens are nodes, edges go from earlier to later tokens, and edge capacities come from attention weights aggregated across heads and layers. A naive propagation over this graph fails because many branches never contribute to the answer, and long paths or large fan-out dilute the influence of early but decisive tokens. The resulting token throughput identifies the dominant prompt-to-answer backbone, with high-throughput tokens interpreted as transit hubs or aggregation checkpoints. These throughput scores are then used in RLVR for token-level reward shaping and loss reweighting, amplifying updates on structurally important tokens and suppressing filler.
