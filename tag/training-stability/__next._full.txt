First, the authors reproduce a GPT-2-class collapse in a PyTorch implementation of FlashAttention-2's tiled online-softmax recurrence. This implementation exposes the running output O and the softmax statistics carry, which allows controlled precision edits. The decisive intervention is fp32 accumulation in the streaming accumulator: it prevents the reproduced collapse while preserving the tiled computation.
