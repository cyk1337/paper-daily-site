18:T4ab,The method is called Declarative Attention (DA). The model is prompted to emit parseable control tags inside its chain-of-thought, declaring the attention scope it needs at each phase of generation. An inference-side state machine reads these tags online and constructs the attention mask dynamically. DA defines three modes. <global> allows access to the full context and is used when the model needs to survey or search broadly. <focus> commits the model to a specific region of the context, restricting attention to that region. <local> limits attention to recent generated tokens, avoiding long-context reads altogether. This is the key departure from prior sparse-attention methods: the selection signal comes from the model’s own generated reasoning trace, not from hidden-state proxy scorers, recency heuristics, historical attention statistics, or an auxiliary scan over the cache. The paper also includes a practical implementation in vLLM using block-aligned, in-place KV-cache masking compatible with FlashAttention. Another notable point is that the protocol works zero-shot on off-the-shelf models, without task-specific fine-tuning or hand-designed partitions for each benchmark.19:T4cc,The evaluation covers 15 long-context tasks, with main results reported on Gemma-4-31B and Qwen-3.6-27B. The central finding is a favorable cost-accuracy trade-off. DA reduces average decoding attention cost by 52.0% on Gemma-4-31B with only a 1.27 percentage-point accuracy drop, and by 31.1% on Qwen-3.6-27B with a 2.75-point drop. The paper also shows positive scaling behavior: as model size increases from 4B to 31B, the accuracy gap between DA and full attention narrows, suggesting that self-declared attention depends on model capability. Absolute savings grow with context length, reaching up to 21 million attended tokens saved per response. The ablations are important. Relative to a maskless ablation, the DA-induced dynamic mask cuts attended tokens by as much as 71.1%, indicating that the savings are driven by the masking mechanism itself rather than by prompt formatting alone. On the systems side, a roofline-style analysis estimates decode wall-clock cost reductions to 0.71× of vanilla for Gemma-4-31B and 0.77× for Qwen-3.6-27B on a well-optimized serving stack. 第一步，使用 block-diffusion head 对一个长度为 B 的未来块同时建模。这里的关键不是把整个模型改成 diffusion LM，而是把 diffusion 式并行去噪/填充能力用作 speculative drafting head：给定当前已提交前缀和目标模型已经融合好的 vision-language hidden states，head 在一次前向里输出整个 block 各位置的分布。这样 depth 不再对应 k 次 sequential draft pass，而是一次 draft pass 就覆盖 k 个未来位置。

第二步，从整块分布中构造宽候选树。作者强调，在 one-pass drafting 的设定下，宽度比深度更便宜，因为整块已经算出来了，额外扩展多个高概率前缀主要把成本放在 verify 阶段，而不是重复 draft 阶段。于是 GLANCE 不是像传统 AR drafter 那样靠多轮逐步展开树，而是直接从 block 位置分布中提取高分前缀，形成 wide candidate tree。

第三步，用一次 target pass 验证整棵树，并证明最终提交路径与目标模型 greedy decoding 完全一致。这里的 lossless 条件很严格：不是近似一致，而是 bitwise equality with greedy decoding。作者还做了运行时 gating，不是假设等价，而是在每轮验证中检查提交路径是否满足精确一致条件。

论文还有一个很好的机制分析。作者提出 accepted block length 主要由 target 的 next-token entropy 决定，并在多个任务上拟合出统一规律。随后用 mismatched-image probe 证明图像的作用是通过降低条件熵进入系统：把图像换错，acceptance length 就按熵上升而下降；把 fused visual state 换成 text-only LLM 的状态，损失最大恰好出现在 grounded、低熵、原本最适合 speculative decoding 的任务上。这比“视觉信息有帮助”更具体，也更可操作。1c:T7f3,The method, GLANCE, replaces the usual small autoregressive drafter with a one-pass block drafter attached to the frozen target VLM’s hidden states. The design has three main pieces.

First, a block-diffusion head predicts a whole future block of length B in parallel. The key point is that the target model itself remains unchanged and autoregressive; the diffusion-style component is only the drafting head. Given the committed prefix and the target’s already fused vision-language state, the head outputs token distributions for all positions in the block in a single forward pass. This removes the usual depth cost of k sequential draft passes for k drafted tokens.

Second, GLANCE constructs a wide candidate tree from the block-level distributions. In this setup, width becomes the cheap axis because the block has already been drafted in one shot. Expanding multiple high-probability prefixes does not require repeated draft passes; most of the extra cost is pushed into verification. This is a major departure from standard autoregressive drafters, where both depth and width are expensive because they are built sequentially.

Third, the target verifies the whole tree in one pass, and the paper proves that the committed path is exactly identical to the target model’s own greedy decoding. The lossless claim is therefore strict: not approximate quality preservation, but exact equality to greedy output. The authors also add runtime gating so that equality is checked rather than assumed.

A particularly strong part of the paper is the mechanism analysis. The authors argue that accepted block length is governed by the target model’s next-token entropy, and they fit a single law across tasks. They then use a mismatched-image probe to show that the image helps through this entropy channel: replacing the image with a mismatched one raises uncertainty and shortens accepted blocks; replacing fused visual state with text-only hidden state hurts most on grounded tasks where acceptance should otherwise be longest.1d:T5ea,Three result groups matter most. First, end-to-end speedup: on grounded workloads such as document QA, infographic QA, and chart QA, GLANCE reaches up to 2.93× speedup over plain autoregressive decoding while preserving exact greedy outputs. One practical detail stands out: GLANCE uses one draft pass per round, whereas the production-style VLM EAGLE-3 head requires eight. Second, comparison against a strong baseline under matched training conditions: trained on the same corpus and schedule as an EAGLE-3 head, GLANCE achieves 2.7× longer accepted blocks. That suggests the gain comes from the one-pass block-drafting structure rather than from extra training budget. Third, mechanism validation: accepted length follows a common relationship with next-token entropy across tasks; grounded tasks sit at the low-entropy end and therefore accept the longest blocks, while open-ended description sits at the high-entropy end and accepts the shortest.

整体上，CacheBridge 不是提出新的在线推理协议，而是在“离线拟合、在线仿射应用”这个框架内，把支持结构、目标函数和实现路径都改得更贴近真实 continuation quality。20:T9b6,CacheBridge combines three components and keeps the online deployment interface unchanged: an offline-fitted affine mapper that can be applied directly at handoff time.

The first component is Head-Local. Full-Head Mapping regresses each target KV head on the concatenation of all source KV heads from selected source layers. CacheBridge argues that this assumes an overly dense correspondence between models. Instead, each target head is restricted to a single architecture-indexed source-head block, while still allowing cross-layer aggregation. This reduces fan-in dramatically and cuts the coefficient count by the number of source heads, 8× in the reported setup. More importantly, it biases the mapping toward a subspace that better matches architectural locality, reducing harmful cross-head coupling when the source and target architectures differ.

The second component is Attn-Repair. The paper points out that held-out R² only measures coordinate-wise cache reconstruction, while continuation quality depends on how those errors propagate through the target model’s queries and attention computation. Two mappings with similar cache fit can therefore produce very different continuation drift. Attn-Repair reweights residuals according to their first-order effect on the receiver’s attention output. In other words, it emphasizes errors that actually matter for downstream attention behavior rather than treating all cache coordinates equally. An effective-sample-size floor prevents the weighting from collapsing onto a tiny subset of examples, while preserving the same online affine form.

The third component is Fused-Fit. Because different target layers may choose different source-layer supports, top-k support selection creates fragmented gather patterns. A generic fitter repeatedly materializes scattered tensors, centers them, and applies weights, so memory traffic becomes a major cost. Fused-Fit introduces a fused GPU kernel that constructs the weighted sufficient statistics directly in bounded panels for head-batched matrix multiplication. It does not change the ridge objective, the solver, or the deployed mapper artifact; it simply makes offline construction structure-aware and much more efficient.

First, the method operates on keys rather than values or hidden states, because eviction affects what can later be retrieved through attention, and keys are the retrieval addresses. The more interesting systems idea is to use TwinKV as a repair pass rather than a standalone eviction policy. Given any existing method’s retained and evicted sets under a fixed budget, TwinKV looks for two types of mismatch: an evicted token with no surviving near-duplicate, and a retained token whose information is already duplicated among retained tokens. It then swaps them while keeping the budget unchanged. This is a low-intrusion design: it does not need the wrapped method’s internal scores, only its final keep set. On mass coverage, the authors argue that threshold counts are the wrong primitive.
