a:T5ea,Two complementary solutions are proposed, each sidestepping positional uncertainty through a distinct mechanism and producing a different form of any-order inference:
1. Insertion-based masked diffusion (FlexMDM): Adds token insertion capability to standard MDMs, breaking the fixed-canvas constraint. Tokens are no longer committed to a fixed final position during unmasking, as later insertions can shift existing tokens, reducing the positional commitment burden. This approach yields structural any-order inference: generation alternates across different nodes of the parsed program tree, rather than completing one contiguous region at a time. It is implemented by fine-tuning Dream-Coder 7B, a general-purpose code generation MDM, to support insertion operations.
2. Latent-space masked diffusion (LatentMDM): The core modeling contribution. It moves masked diffusion from discrete token space to a continuous latent space, shifting decision granularity from individual token positions to masked latent segments, elevating the prediction interface to a coarser semantic granularity and eliminating token-level positional uncertainty at the root. This approach yields semantic any-order inference: the model searches over semantic generation orders in latent space, completely unconstrained by left-to-right token order. 第一层是构造 sandbox。作者把图上的随机游走序列当作离散生成任务：节点 ID 是 token，长度为 L 的 walk 是训练样本，模型只从样本学习，从不显式看到图 G 或转移核。评测时则利用图结构做两类精确指标：一是 coherence / validity，检查相邻 token 是否真有边相连；二是 transition TV distance，从生成样本估计转移核，与真实随机游走核比较分布保真度。这个设计的好处是，latent structure 对模型是隐藏的，对研究者却是可控的，因此可以系统改变图密度、瓶颈、端点约束、Markov 阶数，观察采样器行为如何变化。

第二层是理论分析。论文先证明：在 perfect conditionals 下，单坐标 sequential unmasking 是 order-invariant 且精确的，因为它等价于链式分解；但 parallel unmasking 不再有这个性质，因为它把真实 block conditional 近似成多个单点条件分布的乘积，只有在这些坐标条件独立时才精确。作者构造了简单 DAG / graph 例子，证明 lowest-entropy 两两并行揭示有时优于 random，有时反而更差，因此任何只依赖局部 uncertainty score 的策略都不可能 uniformly optimal。关键决定因素不是 token 自身熵，而是 reveal set 内部以及与已知上下文之间的条件分离结构。

第三层是提出 bisection-style sampler。直觉来自 Markov separation：对一阶随机游走，如果先揭示中间位置，它会把左右两段在条件上分开；对 k 阶 Markov walk，则需要揭示一个大小匹配阶数的 separator block。算法上就是先揭中间 separator，再对左右子区间递归并行处理。作者证明，在 perfect conditionals 下，这个 coarse-to-fine schedule 对无条件随机游走和端点条件随机游走都可以做到精确采样；对 order-k 情况，NFE 复杂度是 O(k log(L/k))，并行深度显著低于逐点采样。

论文最后把这个思路迁移到真实文本 MDM。虽然语言不满足显式 Markov 图结构，作者仍然把“先揭能分隔依赖的锚点，再递归细化”当作 schedule 设计原则，在预训练 OpenWebText masked diffusion 模型上测试。这里的意义不在于证明语言也满足同样定理，而在于说明 graph sandbox 学到的 sampler inductive bias 可以转移到自然语言生成。13:T8f8,The paper has three technical layers.

First, it builds a verifiable sandbox. Training data are random walks on a hidden graph. Tokens are node IDs, and the model only sees sampled sequences, never the graph or transition kernel explicitly. At evaluation time, the graph provides exact structure-aware metrics: coherence or validity, meaning whether every adjacent token pair is a legal edge, and transition total variation distance, obtained by estimating the Markov kernel from generated walks and comparing it to the true one. This gives a Sudoku-like setup where latent constraints are hidden during training but exactly testable afterward.

Second, it develops theory for sampler separations. Under perfect conditionals, sequential one-coordinate unmasking is exact and order-invariant because it follows the chain rule. Parallel unmasking is different: it approximates a block conditional by a product of coordinate-wise conditionals, which is exact only when the chosen coordinates are conditionally independent given the current context. The authors construct simple graph and DAG examples showing that lowest-entropy two-at-a-time unmasking can beat random parallel unmasking on some structures and lose on others. This rules out any claim that a purely local uncertainty score is uniformly optimal. The decisive factor is dependence structure, not uncertainty alone.

Third, the paper proposes bisection-style samplers. The idea comes from Markov separation. For first-order walks, revealing a middle position can separate the left and right halves conditionally; for order-k walks, one needs a separator block of size matched to k. The sampler reveals the middle separator, then recurses on the two sides in parallel. Under perfect conditionals, the authors prove exactness for both unconditional and endpoint-conditioned walks. For order-k Markov walks, the number of function evaluations scales as O(k log(L/k)), giving logarithmic parallel depth rather than linear sequential depth.

They then transfer the scheduling idea to a pretrained masked language model on OpenWebText. The claim is not that language literally satisfies the same graph theorem, but that the graph sandbox reveals a useful design principle: reveal dependency-separating anchors first, then refine recursively.14:T7d2,The experiments are split between graph walks and natural language generation.

In the graph sandbox, the paper compares sequential decoding, random parallel unmasking, entropy-guided parallel policies, and bisection-style samplers. The main empirical pattern is that sampler rankings flip with graph structure. On trees and strongly coupled path-like graphs, entropy-guided parallel updates often commit too early to correlated choices, hurting coherence and transition fidelity. When extra Erdős–Rényi edges are added, creating many alternative routes, low-entropy anchors can become genuinely useful. This directly supports the theoretical claim that parallel decoding quality is governed by conditional dependence rather than uncertainty alone.

For endpoint-conditioned walks and higher-order walks, bisection-style schedules are especially effective because the separator block aligns with the underlying structure. The theoretical complexity is O(k log(L/k)) NFEs for order-k walks, and empirically these samplers achieve better validity and lower transition TV distance at similar or lower inference cost. The important point is not a single headline number, but the consistency across graph families and constraint settings.

The language experiments use a pretrained masked diffusion model on OpenWebText. There, bisection-style schedules improve the non-autoregressive speed-quality tradeoff over standard parallel schedules across MAUVE, generative perplexity, entropy, and repetition. The paper’s emphasis is not absolute quality against autoregressive baselines, but reduced quality loss at a fixed or lower number of denoiser calls.

From a pretraining perspective, two experimental aspects are especially informative. Higher-order walk results show that separator size must match the effective memory order of the target process. Endpoint-conditioned walks act like globally constrained infilling tasks and make the failure mode of naive parallel factorization much easier to see.15:T6e4,The method has two main components. The first is Reflective Masking (RM). The key idea is that remasking is no longer a fixed heuristic based only on low confidence in still-masked positions; already denoised tokens can be actively reconsidered.
