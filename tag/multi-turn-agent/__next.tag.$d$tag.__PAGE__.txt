9:T409,The paper formalizes an agent episode as a sequence of model actions and environment observations, with a binary outcome reward assigned at the end. It builds on GRPO, where multiple trajectories are sampled per task and group-relative advantages are computed from their rewards. The criticized OPSD setup conditions the same model on privileged context to obtain a stronger teacher distribution, then asks the ordinary student view to match it. This creates an irreducible information mismatch. Privileged Self-Practice changes where the privileged information is used. If the model’s rollouts on a task mostly fail, an analyzer model writes a short task-specific instruction. The system then resamples a guided group with that instruction in context. Guided and unguided groups are scored under their own sampling contexts, and advantages are computed within each group. The same GRPO objective is used; there is no loss term comparing the privileged and non-privileged views.
