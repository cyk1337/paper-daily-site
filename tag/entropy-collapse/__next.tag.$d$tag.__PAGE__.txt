This decomposition reveals three orthogonal design axes: the difficulty axis (whether gradient mass concentrates on easy or hard prompts), the sign axis (whether positive and negative masses are balanced, i.e., E[A]=0), and the scale axis (overall gradient magnitude, equivalent to implicit learning rate scaling). Three core trade-offs are formalized: 1) Amplifying correct trajectories collapses entropy, as clustered correct solutions push the policy into a narrow distribution; 2) Amplifying incorrect trajectories induces rank-1 update collapse, as diverse uncorrelated failures converge updates to a single suppression direction, blocking multi-dimensional learning; 3) Focusing on hard prompts increases update informativeness at the cost of higher variance.
