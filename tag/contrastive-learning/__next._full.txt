1b:T875,模型侧并不复杂，重点在实验设计。底座是一个 2B 参数的 frozen vision-language embedding model，只训练音频 connector 和每层 decoder 上的 modality-gated deep adapters；非音频输入时 gate 关闭，因此文本、图像、视频路径不被改动。嵌入是 Matryoshka-nested，默认读出 1024 维。预训练总语料约 888.8K clips，新增的 lexical-speech round 包含约 150K spoken-word clips、70K read-speech clips 和 audiobook 数据。训练中使用 frozen-text memory bank、false-negative masking 和 soft labels。

真正的创新在于作者如何排除其他解释，并把“判别轴”这个说法做成可检验机制。第一步，先观察 lexical speech round 带来的能力交换：关键词识别大涨，情感识别下滑。第二步，用一个 120 倍更小、但在设计上控制 lexical content 的 prosody corpus（Crema-D）做微调，证明情感能力可以快速恢复，因此不是容量不够。第三步，构造 matched-exposure negative control：用 29,428 条显式提到 emotion 的 mined clips，在相同 recipe 和 exposure 下微调，结果情感几乎不动，说明“caption 里写了情感”并不等于模型会学到情感。第四步，再排除 representational truncation 和 general degradation：前者只能解释一小部分差距，后者通过九任务板证明并不存在普遍退化。最后，作者提出 separability statistic，从语料本身估计某属性是否是区分负样本所必需的轴，并在同一批音频上做结构化干预：只改变语料组织方式，让 emotion 成为必须依赖的判别维度，情感能力就恢复；如果只是提高 caption 相似度而不改变判别需求，则恢复不了。这一步把相关性推进到了因果层面的证据。

和常见做法相比，这篇文章不是再发明一个 loss，也不是改模型容量，而是把预训练数据的“结构”拆成了更细的机制变量：同样的音频、同样的 exposure、甚至 caption 明确写出目标属性，都不足以保证属性进入表示；关键是 batch 内负样本能否在不看该属性的情况下被分开。1c:T735,最关键的结果有四组。第一组，加入 lexical-speech round 后，零样本 keyword spotting 提升 76 个点，而 speech emotion recognition 下降 14 个点。作者用的是 MAEB audio embedding benchmark / mteb 框架下的 SpeechCommands（35-way）和 Ravdess，评测方式都是把音频和类别文本映射到共享空间后做匹配。

第二组，容量假设被直接否掉。用只有 7,442 clips 的 Crema-D，从同一个 post-speech checkpoint 出发、固定 fine-tune recipe（batch 128，peak lr 3e-5，无 grad accumulation，关闭 false-negative masking 和 soft labels），只训 150 steps，emotion 从 0.211 恢复到 0.508，超过 pre-speech 的 0.348；同时 keyword 只降到 0.845，仍远高于 pre-speech 水平。训到 400 steps 时 trade-off 继续恶化，说明短 schedule 反而是更好的 operating point。作者还给了一个很直观的“交换率”：150 steps 左右大约是每损失 1 个 keyword 点，换回 6 个 emotion 点；150 到 400 steps 之间这个比率降到约 2。

第三组，数据量假设也被否掉。用 29,428 条 mined clips，caption 明确命名 emotion，且 exposure 匹配，结果 emotion 变化只有 -0.0007，几乎为零。这说明“更多带标签的数据”在这里没有触发目标属性学习。

第四组，机制验证。representational truncation 最多只能恢复约 18% 的差距；九任务板没有显示普遍性退化，因此情感下降不是被其他损伤掩盖。更重要的是，在同一音频上重组语料，让 emotion 成为 required discriminative axis 后，三种随机种子平均恢复 +0.089；而单纯提高 in-batch caption similarity 并不能带来同样效果。这个对照很关键，因为它把“相似文本更利于学情感”与“必须依赖情感才能区分负样本”区分开了。1d:T8d4,The model architecture is relatively standard; the novelty is in the experimental logic. The system starts from a frozen 2B vision-language embedding model, then trains only an audio connector—a query-based resampler—and modality-gated deep adapters attached to each decoder layer. The gate is closed for non-audio inputs, so text, image, and video pathways remain unchanged. The embedding is Matryoshka-nested with a default 1024-dimensional readout. Total pretraining data covers about 888.8K clips, and the added lexical-speech round contributes roughly 150K spoken-word clips, 70K read-speech clips, plus audiobook data. Pretraining uses a frozen-text memory bank, false-negative masking, and soft labels.

The paper’s real contribution is how it rules out alternative explanations and turns the “discriminative axis” idea into a testable mechanism. First, it establishes the capability trade-off induced by lexical speech: keyword recognition rises sharply while emotion recognition drops. Second, it uses a much smaller prosody-controlled corpus, Crema-D, to show that emotion can be recovered quickly, so the issue is not representational capacity. Third, it constructs a matched-exposure negative control using 29,428 mined clips whose captions explicitly name emotions; this produces essentially no emotion recovery, showing that explicit textual mention is not sufficient. Fourth, it rules out representational truncation and broad collateral degradation: truncation explains only a small fraction of the gap, and a nine-task board shows no general collapse. Finally, the authors define a separability statistic computed from the corpus itself to estimate whether an attribute is required for negative separation, and they validate the mechanism causally by restructuring the same audio so that emotion becomes the required discriminative axis. Emotion performance recovers under this intervention, whereas merely increasing in-batch caption similarity does not.

Compared with typical work, this is not a new loss or a larger model. It is a careful decomposition of “data quality” into a more precise variable: whether the training corpus structurally forces the model to rely on the target attribute during contrastive discrimination.1e:T843,There are four key result blocks. First, after adding the lexical-speech round, zero-shot keyword spotting improves by 76 points, while speech-emotion recognition drops by 14 points. Evaluation is done on SpeechCommands (35-way keyword spotting) and Ravdess from the MAEB audio embedding benchmark in the MTEB framework, using shared-space matching between audio embeddings and class-text embeddings.

Second, the capacity explanation is directly rejected.
