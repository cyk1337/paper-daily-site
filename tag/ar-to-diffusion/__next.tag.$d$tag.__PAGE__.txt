9:T4d9,LUDI changes the UDLM recipe in two places. First, it introduces a less uniform loss. The usual KL objective for UDLM matches the reverse transition to the exact posterior, but part of the objective encourages the prediction distribution to remain too uniform. With a large vocabulary, this weakens the clean-token signal and hurts few-step reverse generation. The authors simplify and approximate the original objective, decompose it into three terms, and remove the term that pushes predictions toward uniformity. The resulting loss drives each reverse transition more directly toward the clean token. Second, LUDI adds per-token time embeddings. A global diffusion time does not tell the model which individual positions are likely corrupted. Token-level time embeddings provide corruption hints for each position, letting the model identify reliable condition tokens and uncertain target tokens. This enables confidence-based sampling similar in spirit to masked diffusion models: generate easy or high-confidence tokens first, then use them as context for harder positions. Unlike MDLMs, LUDI keeps the uniform vocabulary prior rather than a mask-token prior.
