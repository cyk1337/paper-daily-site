18:T526,The authors instantiate a canonical-looking audit pipeline with three ingredients. First, they LoRA-memorize 60 fictional author biographies into DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-7B, using a reasoning format where the model restates the target biography in the <think> segment and then gives an answer. Second, they apply NPO unlearning with the forgetting loss masked to the answer span only; no forgetting gradient directly touches the biography template inside the trace. Third, they evaluate generations by splitting on </think> and performing substring matching separately in the trace and the answer. This setup structurally favors a positive bypass gap because the trace template is left largely untouched while the answer span is optimized away. The key technical move is to add fixed-weight probes. One probe swaps the model-generated trace for short non-canary prefills at inference time and checks whether answer continuation remains invariant. Another uses teacher-forced continuation scoring under the same six-token canary head, avoiding greedy decoding and parser dependence. The canary head is important: the metric is a head-conditioned continuation score, not a free-recall measure. A second model, DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Llama-8B, is used to show that formatting drift alone can invert the parser-split metric.19:T5fc,The main results form a compact measurement story. On Qwen-7B, after LoRA memorization, out_acc is 1.00. After NPO, answer-side out_acc falls to 0.60, while trace-side thk_leak remains 0.83, giving a parser-split gap of Δ=+0.23. At first glance this looks like a thinking-channel bypass. However, in 86% of those bypass cases, the answer span is merely a verbatim copy of the question prefix with no real continuation, while the trace contains the trained biography. A fixed-weight prefill swap then changes the answer rate substantially: a short non-canary prefill lowers out_acc from 0.60 to 0.37; bio-style and meta-style prefills land at similar rates; an empty prefill lowers it to 0.20. On a second seed, the bypass gap shrinks rather than vanishes, and the prefill effect reverses direction, bringing the answer rate close to ceiling. Teacher-forced scoring tells a different story from greedy substring matching: after NPO, top-1 continuation match under the same six-token canary head stays between 0.90 and 1.00 across prefills. On Llama-8B, the parser-split metric flips to -0.92 at K=1600, while out_acc remains 1.00 and teacher-forced top-1 is at least 0.998. The cause is format drift: in at least 60% of probes, the biography template moves outside the <think> tags, leaving the parser with an empty trace.
