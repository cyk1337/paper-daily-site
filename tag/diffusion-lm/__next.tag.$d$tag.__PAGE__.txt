第二层是如何决定吸收顺序。随机吸收显然不行，因为一旦把错 token 固定住，就失去后续纠错机会。作者提出 LEDFlow，用 predictive entropy 做局部可靠性估计：每一步在给定 absorption budget 下，选当前熵最低的 active positions，把它们按当前最可能 token 固定下来；其余位置继续按 kinetic-optimal velocity 更新。下一步再重新计算熵，因此顺序是自适应的，而不是预先排好的静态 order。

理论部分的重点不是证明整个 sampler 全局最优，而是分析“不可逆吸收”引入的误差。作者把 stepwise absorption error 分解成两个 KL 项：一个对应被同时吸收位置之间的 joint dependence 丢失，另一个对应在已吸收上下文条件下的 conditional prediction error。随后在 oracle-reachable states 和 entropy-error regularity 的假设下，证明在固定吸收预算时，选择最低熵位置可以最小化 conditional error 上界。这个论证的意义在于解释为什么局部熵是合理信号，而不是仅凭经验有效。

The first is selective absorption. Each position is assigned a binary absorption indicator; once a position is absorbed, its outgoing transition rates are set to zero, so it no longer evolves under the CTMC. This changes the original all-positions-update regime of uniform discrete flow into a mixed process where some positions are frozen and others continue to move. A key design choice is that the velocity on active positions is left unchanged, so the method does not alter the trained denoiser or the underlying flow objective.

The second layer is the absorption policy. Randomly freezing positions is risky because an incorrect absorbed token cannot be corrected later. The proposed sampler, LEDFlow, uses predictive entropy as a local reliability signal. At each step, under a fixed absorption budget, it selects the active positions with the lowest entropy and absorbs them using their current most likely values. The remaining positions continue evolving under the kinetic-optimal velocity. Entropy is recomputed at every step, so the generation order is adaptive rather than predetermined.

The theory is framed around the error introduced by irreversible absorption. The authors decompose the stepwise absorption error into two KL terms: one for the loss of joint dependence among simultaneously absorbed positions, and one for conditional prediction error under the context created by previously absorbed values. Under assumptions on oracle-reachable states and entropy-error regularity, they show that, with a fixed absorption budget, choosing the lowest-entropy positions minimizes an upper bound on the conditional error term. This does not prove global optimality, but it gives a principled reason why local entropy is the right signal.

The paper also analyzes global entropy lookahead. In principle, evaluating future successor states could yield better decisions. In practice, with an imperfect denoiser, future-state estimation errors accumulate, and the decision-error bound grows with the lookahead window. For a deeper pass, go into the appendix for the exactness proof of the sampler, complexity analysis, and the details of augmenting existing AR models.25:T4e7,The method, VIG-Sampler, is built on two empirical observations. First, candidate tokens that place stronger attention on image tokens tend to be both better grounded in the visual input and more useful for predicting the remaining masked tokens. Based on this, the sampler uses token-level image attention as the ordering score, prioritizing visually informative tokens over high-confidence but semantically weak ones. Second, when multiple tokens are decoded in parallel, their individual information gains do not add up cleanly because some of them are redundant. The paper argues that this redundancy is reflected in similar token-to-image attention distributions. VIG-Sampler therefore adds a similarity-based penalty during subset selection: if a candidate attends to the image in a way that is too similar to already selected tokens, its priority is reduced. This gives a simple two-part design: image-attention-based ranking for grounding, plus attention-similarity penalty for complementary information. Compared with certainty-based samplers, it avoids locking in punctuation, copulas, or other frequent low-content tokens too early. Compared with text-only info-gain samplers, it explicitly injects visual evidence into the decoding trajectory.26:T76c,The main idea is affix-oriented cache reuse. Instead of restricting reuse to shared prefixes, the paper generalizes it to any shared contiguous span, or affix, which may appear at the beginning, middle, or end of the context. This is a natural systems opportunity created by bidirectional attention: unlike AR models, DLLMs are not inherently prefix-centric.

The key challenge is deciding which affix-token KV states become meaningfully stale as the masked generation tokens evolve. ACache introduces Anchor Tokens for this purpose. It measures the cross-attention importance from masked generation tokens to affix tokens, identifies a small request-specific subset of influential affix tokens, and recomputes KV states only for those anchors. The remaining affix-token cache is reused. In effect, the method turns a coarse full-span refresh decision into a sparse token-level refresh decision.

Implementation-wise, ACache is built on top of Fast-dLLM. It replaces periodic full-cache recomputation with selective anchor-token recomputation, so it is an incremental but targeted improvement over existing approximate caching frameworks rather than a completely new inference stack. The authors also implement a shared-prefix prototype in Nano-vLLM to test whether the gains survive under a more realistic serving environment with paged memory management and continuous batching.

Relative to prior work, the distinction is clear. dLLM-Cache uses asymmetric prompt/response refresh and feature-similarity-based partial recomputation for response tokens. dKV-Cache focuses more on decoded-token reuse with delayed block recomputation. Fast-dLLM improves the overall inference framework but still relies on periodic refresh. ACache is novel in treating shared context itself as the optimization target and shrinking the recomputation unit from an entire shared span to a request-specific token subset.27:T504,The main results operate at three levels. First, quality recovery: compared with direct affix-cache reuse, which causes noticeable degradation, ACache recovers most of the lost accuracy across multiple benchmarks while recomputing only around 20% of affix tokens. That number matters because it suggests the stale-cache problem is sparse enough to be exploitable. Second, system gains: in the Nano-vLLM shared-prefix prototype, ACache reduces recomputation latency by up to 55.7% and improves end-to-end throughput by up to 1.68x. This indicates that the benefit is not just a FLOP-level argument but can survive a realistic serving stack. Third, scope: the method applies to shared spans beyond prefixes, which is practically relevant because reusable text in production often appears as retrieved snippets, tool outputs, templates, or summarized dialogue history rather than only as a left prefix.

For a researcher, the most informative part is likely the ablation structure rather than any single headline number: how stable the anchor-selection rule is, how quality changes as the recomputation ratio varies, whether affix position matters, and how stale-cache error accumulates across denoising steps. If the paper includes those plots, they are the ones worth studying closely.28:T7ab,方法分成两部分：TDES 和 SABA。

TDES（Target-Distilled Edge Scoring）解决父子错配。现有 DDTree 的边选择近似是 parent-agnostic 的：child token 在不同 parent 下贡献相同，只由该位置的 token probability 决定。GRAFT 改成显式给 edge 打分。做法是从 target model 的验证轨迹里蒸馏一个轻量 edge scorer，输入是相邻位置候选节点及其上下文特征，输出该 parent-child transition 被 target 接受的兼容性分数。然后树构建时不再只按各位置 token 概率乘积选路径，而是结合 edge score 进行 parent-conditional 组装。这样保留了 DLM 一次前向产出候选节点的优点，又补上了“节点之间是否能连成 target 偏好的路径”这一层信息。

从机制上看，这一步很像把 target 的局部条件分布偏好压缩成一个便宜的结构预测器。它不是重新跑 target，也不是让 drafter 变成 AR，而是在 assembly 阶段补一个 learned compatibility model。相比直接增大候选集，这种做法更有效，因为问题不是候选 token 不在，而是树结构把它们接错了。

SABA（State-Aware Budget Allocation）解决固定预算失配。作者把每轮 throughput 看成 accepted gain 与 verification overhead 的函数，学习一个按当前 decoding state 选择 tree budget 的策略。直觉上，easy round 本来 acceptance 就长，再加节点收益很快饱和；hard round 适度扩树更值钱，但也不是越大越好。SABA 用当前轮的状态特征预测不同 budget 下的收益，选 throughput 最优预算。这里的关键不是复杂控制器，而是把“树大小”从静态超参变成在线决策变量。

整体上，GRAFT 没有改 DLM 本身，也没有改 target verification 机制，而是专门优化 DLM-based tree assembly 这一层。这种切分是合理的，因为瓶颈正出在 one-pass future distributions 到 executable tree 的映射。29:T781,GRAFT has two components: TDES and SABA.

TDES, or Target-Distilled Edge Scoring, addresses parent-child mismatch. DDTree is effectively parent-agnostic: a child token contributes the same score regardless of which parent path it extends, because scoring is driven by per-position token probabilities. GRAFT instead learns an explicit edge scorer distilled from target-model traces. The scorer takes candidate parent-child pairs from adjacent positions, together with contextual features, and predicts how compatible that transition is with the target model. Tree assembly then uses these edge scores, rather than only the product of marginal token probabilities, to select target-compatible paths under a node budget.

Mechanistically, this is a lightweight structural predictor that compresses local target preferences into a cheap model. It does not rerun the target and does not force the DLM drafter into autoregressive expansion. It patches the missing conditional dependency at the tree-assembly stage. That is the right intervention point, because the failure mode is not merely missing candidate tokens; it is incorrect attachment among already available candidates.

SABA, or State-Aware Budget Allocation, addresses budget misallocation. The paper models per-round throughput as a trade-off between draft gain and verification cost, then chooses the budget conditioned on the current decoding state. Easy rounds already have long accepted prefixes, so larger trees mostly add cost. Hard rounds benefit more from extra nodes, but only up to a saturation point. SABA predicts the utility of different budgets from state features and selects the throughput-optimal one online.

Overall, GRAFT leaves the DLM and the target verification mechanism unchanged. It focuses specifically on the mapping from one-pass future distributions to an executable draft tree. That decomposition is sensible and technically clean.2a:T4a2,The main empirical claim is consistent end-to-end throughput improvement with very small overhead. The paper reports 2.13x to 6.36x throughput gains over baselines across models and datasets, while adding less than 0.5 ms overhead per decoding round. That matters because it suggests the method is not trading away the cost structure of speculative decoding.

The ablations are more informative than the headline numbers. TDES improves MAT, indicating that parent-child compatibility is a real bottleneck for DLM-based tree construction. The paper shows both examples and aggregate evidence that target-compatible tokens are often already present among candidates, but become unusable because they are connected to the wrong parent. SABA shows that a fixed budget is not robust: the throughput-optimal tree size varies substantially across rounds. Dynamic allocation preserves most of the acceptance gain while avoiding unnecessary verification cost.

From a systems perspective, the strongest result is not a single benchmark number but the combination of stable gains across tasks and low added latency. That makes the method more credible as a practical layer on top of DLM drafters.2b:T455,This is a skim paper, but the introduction, method section, and ablations are worth reading carefully.
