b:T419,The proposed method, SQSD, assigns a continuous safety-degradation risk score to each training example. The method first defines two reference directions in parameter space: a safety direction and a danger direction. Following the task-vector idea, both are constructed as displacements from the same base model. The safety direction is obtained from a DPO-aligned model trained on PKU-SafeRLHF-10K, while the danger direction is obtained from a model fine-tuned toward harmful behavior using datasets such as Aegis and BeaverTails. For each training example, the induced parameter update is projected onto these two directions. A sample is considered riskier if its update aligns more with the danger direction and less with the safety direction. This differs from perturbation-magnitude analysis: two updates with the same norm can have different behavioral consequences if their directions differ.
