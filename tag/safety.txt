19:T516,The authors first treat RIM as a conditional failure mode rather than a universal outcome. They extract two activation-space directions from the base model: a reasoning direction that separates correct from incorrect reasoning, and a safety direction that separates refusal from compliance. In Qwen2.5-Instruct 3B and 7B, where RIM appears, these directions have consistently negative cosine similarity in mid-to-deep layers, suggesting that reasoning-improving updates move representations away from the safety-refusal direction. The authors then use CKA distance ratios to compare representation changes on harmful versus harmless inputs after fine-tuning, locating a compact set of safety-decision layers. Linear probes provide an important check: after fine-tuning, the model can still recognize harmful requests, but its refusal behavior collapses. Based on this diagnosis, the paper proposes the Safety-Direction Penalty, which penalizes squared hidden-state displacement along the learned safety direction during reasoning SFT. The penalty is first applied to the localized safety-decision layers; if compensatory drift appears in unpenalized layers, the same diagnostics are used to expand the scope. The method uses no safety training data, no reference policy, and adds no inference overhead.1a:T723,The central proposal is Knowledge Triage. The authors derive a five-type knowledge model from 396,934 agent artifacts and argue that this is the coarsest partition that still supports distinct safety guarantees. The exact labels are written as {C, P, B, F, E}; the important point is that each type tolerates a different kind of distortion. Constraints require wording-level fidelity, procedures allow behavior-preserving rewrites, and episodic logs can be aggressively summarized or dropped.

The knowledge base is formalized as K=(I, τ, T, π, σ), where I is the item set, τ is the type classifier, T is a topic tree, π maps items to topic leaves, and σ defines the scope of constraint items. This lets the paper unify three context-management operations under one typed budget-allocation view:
1. TypeCompact: in-place budget reduction with type-specific fidelity policies instead of uniform summarization.
2. TypeDecompose: splitting oversized topics before compression, so non-compressible constraints are not mixed with compressible background material.
3. TypeRetrieve: retrieval from external memory that is aware of item type, not just semantic relevance.

Two supporting components are added. A post-compaction guard verifier checks whether critical guards remain enforceable after rewriting. A SafetyMargin classifier scores items by counterfactual safety impact rather than surface form, which is more meaningful than keyword-based rule detection.

A strong point of the paper is that it does not stop at empirical anecdotes. It states per-operator safety requirements and argues that type-blind strategies cannot satisfy them across compaction, decomposition, and retrieval. The claim is therefore structural: one fidelity policy cannot serve heterogeneous knowledge objects with incompatible loss tolerances.1b:T5d4,The empirical section has two layers. First, it measures the failure mode directly. On 20 production agent configurations, Claude Code’s /compact prompt on Sonnet 4.6 preserves only 53% of safety rules after one compaction round and 10% after five rounds. The introduction also cites about 50% safety-constraint retention under hierarchical truncation on 50 real agent configurations. This rapid decay is the Compaction Cliff. The paper further reports that the same pattern appears across four LLM compactor families and the strongest non-LLM compressor they tested, which supports the claim that the issue is structural rather than model-specific.

Second, it validates the proposed framework. The authors build AgentArtifactCorpus with 396,934 artifacts from 54,628 public GitHub repositories and provide 2,000 type annotations. Their five-type taxonomy covers 97% of real configuration content, which is an important practical check. End-to-end validation on five public corpora shows that typed triage, together with the guard verifier and SafetyMargin classifier, preserves safety constraints better than uniform compaction without giving up too much budget efficiency. The excerpt provided here does not include the full result tables, so the exact gains beyond the headline recall numbers cannot be reproduced faithfully. Still, the main empirical signal is clear: the benefit is concentrated in constraint retention and multi-round robustness, not in generic summarization quality.1c:T47b,For a quick read, start with the introduction and the figure that plots the Compaction Cliff. That section tells you whether the paper’s core diagnosis is convincing. Then read the typed knowledge model in Section 3. The key is not the notation itself, but why the authors insist that compaction, decomposition, and retrieval are one budget-management problem with different safety constraints by type. After that, check the taxonomy coverage analysis and the table defining the five types. This is where the practical plausibility of the framework stands or falls. Finally, inspect the guard verifier and SafetyMargin classifier to judge whether they are narrow engineering heuristics or reusable abstractions.

If you only have half an hour, read the introduction, the type table, the main cliff figure, and the primary experiment table. If you want to go deeper, focus on three questions: whether the taxonomy transfers beyond the artifact distribution they collected, how the counterfactual safety labels are constructed, and whether the typed-memory idea can be moved upstream into training objectives rather than remaining a runtime patch.1d:Tadc,方法上，作者做得比较干净，重点在于把 midtraining 的因果效应尽量从 post-training 中剥离出来。

首先是数据构建。作者从 Anthropic 2026 Constitution 中手工抽取 40 个 constitutional values，并配上定义文本。随后用 Sentence-BERT 对每个 value 做嵌入，计算其与其他 value 的平均余弦相似度，作为 centrality score。两个偏组织性的 value——helpfulness 和 principles hierarchy——因明显更外围，被排除出 curriculum unit selection，剩下 38 个 value。作者再用层次聚类把这些 value 分成四簇，并按平均 centrality 从高到低排序，得到 curriculum 条件下的教学顺序：从更核心的伦理价值，到身份/品格/福祉，再到操作安全与关系行为，最后到认识论完整性与诚实。这里的设计意图很明确：如果价值之间存在结构中心与边缘，那么先教中心、再教外围，可能更利于形成稳定表征。

其次是 2x2 因子设计。两个因素分别是 curriculum ordering 与 deliberative reasoning。也就是：是否按 centrality 导出的顺序组织内容，以及样本中是否包含显式价值推理。这样得到四个 constitutional midtraining 条件，再加一个 replay-only control。语料规模上，摘要给出总 constitutional corpus 为 394M tokens，其中 reasoning 版约 257.6M，no-reasoning 版约 136.8M。这个规模不算大到改变通用能力分布，但足以作为一个有针对性的 midtraining 注入。

第三是模型与训练位置。所有条件都从同一个 Nemotron-3-Super-120B-A12B-Base 出发，这是一个 hybrid Mamba-2/attention 的 MoE 基座模型，总参数 120B、激活参数 12B，预训练约 25T tokens。作者把 constitutional 数据插入 midtraining，而不是混在后续 SFT 里；之后再接一个 value-neutral SFT 阶段，确保如果 CMT 条件优于 control，这个优势主要来自 midtraining 内容，而不是后续监督阶段重复灌输了相同价值。

第四是评测设计。作者不仅看标准 alignment benchmark，还看 self-generated 与 established benchmark 的组合，并覆盖 alignment under pressure、value conflict resolution、blackmail、emergent misalignment 等更接近 disposition 的场景；同时在三个阶段评估：midtraining 后、SFT 后、以及 benign fine-tuning 后。这样可以区分“即时行为变化”和“能否穿过后续训练仍保留”的差异。
