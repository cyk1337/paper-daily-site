9:T426,The paper formulates response generation as an undiscounted episodic MDP: the prompt is the initial state, tokens are actions, transitions append tokens deterministically, and the scalar response reward is assigned at EOS. It then derives policy-gradient estimators under samples drawn from a behavior or inference policy. A key distinction is made between sequence-level importance sampling, which uses the product ratio over the whole response, and token-level importance sampling, which applies local ratios per decoding step; these estimate different quantities. The main technical point is that, without ratio regularization, the weighted score has zero expectation, so constant reward shifts vanish. After clipping, truncation, or masking the ratio, this zero-mean property generally fails. The proposed fix is to use the covariance between the reward and the regularized importance-weighted score as the estimand, equivalently centering the score term as well as the reward.
