9:T467,The baseline objective interpolates standard next-token cross-entropy with a KL term between temperature-scaled teacher and student next-token distributions. The paper studies both forward KL and reverse KL, varying teacher size and the interpolation coefficient. The central analysis uses teacher predictive entropy as a diagnostic. Low-entropy teacher predictions tend to appear on procedural or highly constrained tokens, where the teacher distribution is sharp and informative. High-entropy predictions are common on unstructured web text and factual continuations with many plausible next tokens. In high-entropy regions, KD reduces the relative force of the one-hot ground-truth token compared with NTP, so it can slow the learning of facts that the student has not yet acquired. Switch Distillation follows directly from this analysis: use KD on low-entropy tokens and fall back to NTP on high-entropy tokens. The routing signal comes from the same teacher logits already needed for KD, so the method adds little overhead and differs from global fixes such as tuning alpha, changing KL direction, or scaling the teacher.a:T466,The controlled experiments use the OLMo-2 ecosystem, with 1B students and larger 7B/13B teachers, including instruction-tuned teachers. This pattern is robust across teacher sizes, KL directions, and interpolation coefficients; no conventional KD objective Pareto-dominates NTP in mid-training.
