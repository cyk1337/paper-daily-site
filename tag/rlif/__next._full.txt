- Answer-level consensus reward: sample G completions per prompt, extract final answers, cluster by equivalence, and reward each completion by its cluster size (cluster voting). This favors reproducible answers rather than one-off high-confidence generations.
- Completion-level self-certainty reward: token-wise confidence/low-entropy scoring to encourage decisiveness during generation.
- GDPO normalization: normalize advantages induced by each reward channel independently within the group, then combine them with weights. This prevents one reward from dominating due to scale/variance.
