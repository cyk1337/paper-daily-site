9:T4fa,PAPO has two main components.

Step-Aware Process Rewards, or SPR, avoids broadcasting the final outcome reward to all intermediate denoising steps. Instead, it computes an immediate reward for each intermediate step based on a single denoising-step prediction, then combines this signal with the final outcome reward. This gives the optimizer a more step-specific learning signal. The design matters because one diffusion denoising step can update many masked positions in parallel, so token-level credit cannot be inherited from AR generation.

Entropy-Guided Historical Re-enactment, or EHR, replaces artificial randomly masked states with real historical states captured during natural rollouts. During training, the method re-enacts these states and samples them according to model uncertainty, using entropy to focus updates on high-information moments. This avoids spending gradients on already settled decisions and keeps the training states faithful to the model’s actual generation process.
