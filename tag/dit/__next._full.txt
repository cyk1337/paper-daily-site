19:T892,方法上，作者把生成过程写成两阶段：先由 MLLM 根据文本提示生成中间表示 D，再由 DiT 在文本和 D 的条件下生成视频。论文的核心不是提出一个单点模块，而是系统比较三类设计。

第一，中间表示 D 的形式。作者重点考察显式 visual-semantic tokens，而不是自然语言重写或隐式 latent query。显式 token 又分连续特征和离散语义 token。离散化部分比较了 EMA-based tokenizer 与 embedding-based tokenizer，并考察是否加入额外监督，如 MLLM understanding loss 或 pixel reconstruction loss。结论是 EMA 码本更稳，因为它保留原始特征尺度，不依赖梯度更新 codebook，也不需要额外辅助目标；而层次化 token 结构虽然能编码从浅层感知到深层语义的多级信息，但在视频生成里带来的收益有限。

第二，MLLM 如何生成这些 token。作者比较 causal attention 与 full attention。full attention 允许所有视觉 token 双向交互，理论上更利于整体表示；但实验显示 causal attention 更适合语义 token 的时序生成，因为它与 next-token 预测一致，也更贴合视频事件按时间展开的结构。这里的关键判断不是“因果注意力更强”，而是中间层 token 的任务是规划时序语义，而不是重建静态视觉细节，因此生成式约束反而更合适。

第三，DiT 如何融合 MLLM 输出。作者比较两条路线：一条是 query/connector 式 latent bridging，即把 MLLM 输出压成隐式条件；另一条是显式 visual token conditioning，把生成出的语义 token 直接注入 DiT。显式条件又比较 self-attention 与 cross-attention 注入方式。结论是多层 cross-attention 最有效，因为它让语义 token 在去噪全过程持续提供结构约束，而不是只在某个浅层阶段提供弱提示。

基于这些结论，作者提出 BiVidGen：使用 EMA tokenizer 产生离散语义 token，MLLM 以 causal attention 自回归生成这些 token，DiT 通过显式多层 cross-attention 消费这些 token 进行扩散渲染。这个设计的重点是把 MLLM 从“编码器”变成“规划器”。1a:T8f4,The generation process is factorized into two stages: an MLLM first produces an intermediate representation D from the text prompt, and a DiT then generates the video conditioned on both the prompt and D. The contribution is not a single module, but a systematic comparison along three design axes.

First, the form of D. The paper focuses on explicit visual-semantic tokens rather than prompt rewriting in natural language or implicit latent queries. Within explicit tokens, it compares continuous visual features against discrete semantic tokens. For discretization, the authors study EMA-based tokenizers and embedding-based tokenizers, with and without auxiliary objectives such as MLLM understanding loss or pixel reconstruction loss. Their conclusion is that EMA-based tokenization is the most stable choice because it preserves feature scale, avoids gradient-updated codebooks, and does not require extra supervision. They also test hierarchical token structures inspired by recent VLM designs, but find limited gains for video generation.

Second, how the MLLM should generate those tokens. The paper compares causal attention and full attention over visual tokens. Full attention allows bidirectional interaction and may help holistic representation learning, but causal attention works better in practice for semantic-token generation. The reason is structural: these tokens are used for temporal planning, and autoregressive generation matches the sequential nature of video events.

Third, how the DiT should use the MLLM output. The authors compare latent bridging through learnable queries and connector modules against explicit visual-token conditioning. For explicit conditioning, they test different injection mechanisms, including self-attention and cross-attention. Multi-layer cross-attention performs best, suggesting that semantic tokens are most useful when they provide persistent structural guidance throughout denoising rather than a weak one-shot hint.

These findings are combined into BiVidGen: an EMA-based tokenizer produces discrete semantic tokens, the MLLM generates them autoregressively with causal attention, and the DiT consumes them through explicit multi-layer cross-attention. The key shift is to use the MLLM as a planner rather than just an encoder.1b:T5d2,The main strength of the experiments is the systematic ablation, not a single headline number. On VBench-Long, BiVidGen outperforms the compared design variants, and the qualitative examples show better temporal consistency, more coherent causal transitions, and stronger semantic alignment.

The main empirical findings are:
1. Explicit visual tokens work better than natural-language expansion or implicit latent bridging, likely because they provide a more direct and structured conditioning signal to the DiT.
2. EMA-based tokenization is more stable and more effective than embedding-based codebooks and variants with extra supervision.
3. Hierarchical token structures bring limited benefit, suggesting that the current bottleneck is high-level semantic planning and temporal organization rather than packing more multi-level visual detail into the interface.
4. Causal attention outperforms full attention for semantic-token generation, producing more structured temporal plans and better downstream video quality.
5. Explicit token conditioning is better than query-based latent connectors, and within explicit conditioning, multi-layer cross-attention is the strongest fusion strategy.
