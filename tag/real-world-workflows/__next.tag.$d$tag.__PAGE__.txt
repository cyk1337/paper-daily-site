5:T4e3,ALE’s contribution is in benchmark construction and verification protocol:
- Admission criteria: representativeness (real software stacks used by professionals), complexity (end-to-end workflows vs. single actions), and verifiability (deterministic checks or artifact-tied rubrics).
- Data sourcing: 250+ domain experts contribute workflows they have actually completed; tasks go through multi-stage QC (expert review → engineer dry-runs → committee peer review). The explicit trade-off is higher curation cost for higher distributional realism.
- Coverage: 13 industry clusters and 55 subfields, anchored to O*NET/SOC 2018; prior benchmarks are mapped onto this taxonomy to compare coverage.
- Target system: Generalist Computer-Use Agents that unify GUI perception, CLI/code execution, file manipulation, tool use, and long-horizon planning in one action loop.
- Verification: deliverable-based or milestone-based structured checks against expert references/rubrics, aiming to make heterogeneous outputs automatically scorable.
