A denoising step consists of a network prediction F_theta followed by a sampler update S_j. The key abstraction is a context-selection policy Phi_b(j,r), which chooses the noise state of historical chunk b-r when current chunk b is at denoising step j. This policy is tied to the history window W, attention masks, cached representations, sampler behavior, execution scheduling, and training-state sources. Three representative policies are studied: clean history uses clean endpoints from past chunks; same-level history uses past chunks at the same denoising level as the current chunk; progressive history makes more distant history cleaner.
