1) Agentic Seeding: instead of teaching tool use post-hoc, the authors introduce a dedicated stage after pretraining that densely trains on long-horizon reasoning and ReAct-like trajectories (plan→act/tool-call→observe→update→answer). 2) Trajectory-augmented VQA data + SFT/RL pipeline: they formalize actions (tool invocation or final answer), observations (URLs/titles/snippets returned by tools), and interaction history, then perform SFT to learn protocols and multi-step reasoning formats, followed by RL to improve long-horizon success.
3) V-Fold: an adaptive history folding mechanism to combat attention dilution. Recent turns remain as high-fidelity text in a sliding window; older history is rendered into the visual modality so the model can still “see” raw evidence via vision-language alignment without paying large text-token costs.
