19:T4db,The paper defines Golden Negative Trajectories as expert-generated reasoning traces that end in incorrect answers. Its central empirical finding is the Reflection Advantage: on hard problems, a target model can solve more effectively when asked to reflect on a flawed expert trajectory than when solving directly from scratch. A process reward model is used to profile trajectory quality at the step level. Compared with failures from the target model or a weaker model, GNTs maintain higher process rewards and longer valid prefixes before a localized error. Causal interventions on trajectory components suggest that both the valid prefix and the error region contribute to the benefit. ReflectRL keeps the underlying training objective unchanged and modifies rollout construction. In RLVR, it samples both reflective and direct reasoning rollouts, scores them with the same verifier, and applies the usual policy update. In OPD, it uses GNTs as privileged teacher-side context while keeping the student aligned to direct reasoning. A Reflective-to-Direct Policy Transition gradually shifts the rollout distribution from reflective reasoning to direct reasoning, so the trained model does not require expert failure traces at inference time.1a:T535,1) MAD-OPD: replace a single teacher with a deliberative committee. The debate transcript becomes privileged context c for the teachers, yielding debate-conditioned token distributions. Teachers’ contributions are aggregated with post-debate confidence weights, producing the supervision signal for the student.
2) OPAD: an agentic OPD pipeline that explicitly incorporates step-level environment interaction/sampling to make OPD workable in multi-step tool-use settings, where distribution shift and compounding errors otherwise destabilize training.
