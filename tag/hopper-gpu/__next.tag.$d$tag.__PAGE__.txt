9:T6a9,The design has a single-GPU part and a distributed part. On a single GPU, FlashMask-3 builds on the column-wise sparse representation from FlashMask. The mask is indexed by key position, while each key stores query-axis intervals through four boundary vectors, commonly denoted LTS, LTE, UTS, and UTE for lower- and upper-triangular regions. This avoids constructing an n by n mask and gives the kernel a compact representation of valid query-key tiles. The new kernel integrates this representation into FlashAttention-3’s Hopper execution model. It uses three-role warp specialization: a KV Index Generator scans the boundary vectors and produces the relevant tile or KV indices, a KV Loader issues asynchronous TMA loads, and MMA Consumers run WGMMA compute. Mask handling is moved out of the main compute loop into a producer-like stage, which reduces branch overhead and keeps the asynchronous pipeline full. A preemptive persistent-tile scheduler is added to reduce SM-level imbalance under irregular sparse masks. For distributed context parallelism, the key observation is that the mask is keyed by K positions but stores Q-axis intervals. Under any multi-chunk CP assignment, a rank-local mask can be obtained by elementwise clipping, shifting, and max operations over the boundary vectors. No cross-rank mask exchange is needed, and the resulting local representation can be passed to the same kernel. The distributed system then adds a sparsity-aware hierarchical load balancer, HCC-IPO, topology-aware hierarchical routing for forward all-gather, mask-driven communication pruning, and a backward reduce-scatter path overlapped with attention recomputation using bitmask-AMO synchronization.a:T4ca,The evaluation covers microbenchmarks, context-parallel scaling, and end-to-end training. On a single H100, across 12 structured masks and sequence lengths from 4K to 128K, FlashMask-3 improves achieved TFLOPs over FlashMask by 45% to 141%, over FlexAttention by 34% to 71%, and over MagiAttention by 2% to 44%. These gains indicate that the improvement is not only from compact mask representation, but also from aligning mask-aware scheduling with Hopper’s TMA/WGMMA pipeline. In distributed context-parallel experiments with CP degrees from 4 to 32 and 8K tokens per device, it reaches 31% to 75% compute-communication overlap efficiency. Throughput improves by 1.80× to 3.71× over Megatron-Core CP ring, by 1.71× to 2.92× over Megatron-Core CP all-gather, and by 1.09× to 1.46× over MagiAttention.
