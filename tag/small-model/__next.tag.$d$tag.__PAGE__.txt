It uses a hybrid attention layout similar to recent compact models, interleaving linear attention and global attention at a 3:1 ratio. For the linear layers, it adopts Gated DeltaNet rather than a heavier alternative such as KDA, prioritizing simpler recurrent inference on edge hardware. Each GDN layer uses a short causal convolution for local token mixing and L2-normalizes queries and keys for training stability. The decoding design, X-MTP, is a shared-KV multi-token prediction module that avoids per-depth KV-cache replay in conventional MTP. A lightweight verification head supports adaptive drafting with lower rollback overhead. The paper also studies IronLLM-0.6B-Light, which removes RMSNorm via DyT and uses simplified components such as learnable upper-bounded ReLUx and data-independent gating. Stage 1 is agentic SFT to establish basic search-browse-answer behavior. The key is not simply fine-tuning on available traces, but aggressively cleaning them and resampling toward long-horizon trajectories. The paper argues that small models are disproportionately harmed by noisy supervision, so it filters malformed outputs, inconsistent tool traces, and poor reasoning patterns. It also increases the training weight of long trajectories so that the model learns the behaviors that actually matter for deep research: multi-step search, evidence gathering, and synthesis, rather than overfitting to short easy examples. Stage 2 is agentic RL. Instead of optimizing only a sparse final-answer reward at the trajectory level, the paper builds on IGPO and introduces turn-level rewards tailored to long-horizon research tasks. Each step is rewarded for information gain—whether the action and reasoning move the agent toward better evidence acquisition—and penalized for format violations to stabilize behavior. This is the central design choice: the method does not treat the task as a single terminal success/failure event, but explicitly rewards intermediate progress in evidence collection. That improves credit assignment and allows partially useful trajectories to contribute learning signal even when the final answer is not fully correct.
