The paper argues that small models are disproportionately harmed by noisy supervision, so it filters malformed outputs, inconsistent tool traces, and poor reasoning patterns. It also increases the training weight of long trajectories so that the model learns the behaviors that actually matter for deep research: multi-step search, evidence gathering, and synthesis, rather than overfitting to short easy examples.
