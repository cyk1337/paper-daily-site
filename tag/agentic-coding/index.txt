18:T78e,Architecture and stabilization stack:
- Backbone: Transformer with sparse MoE layers; hybrid attention interleaving GQA and Sliding Window Attention.
- Attention stabilization: query-aware sink token to absorb excess attention mass; QKNorm to control query/key scales plus parameter clipping; Partial RoPE applied to only a subset of head dimensions.
- MoE practicality: the first layer uses a dense MLP instead of MoE routing to reduce early-layer expert load imbalance.
Normalization / depth utilization:
- SplitNorm: normalize the residual stream entering attention (RMSNorm) while keeping the residual stream entering the FFN unnormalized, aiming to combine Pre-Norm stability with better utilization of deep layers (mitigating residual variance accumulation that can drown later updates).
- “Stable SplitNorm” adds extra normalization on attention outputs and other scale controls (the provided excerpt truncates mid-description, but the intent is to prevent large-model instabilities).
Optimization and scaling:
- Muon optimizer + μP parameterization to improve optimization efficiency and enable hyperparameter (especially LR) transfer across scales.
Training pipeline:
- Two-stage pretraining curriculum: stage 2 increases coding-data mixture to strengthen coding/agentic foundations without starting from a heavily code-skewed distribution.
- Mid-training: expand data mixture across domains and formats; progressively extend context length.
- Post-training: SFT for basic CLI interaction; RL with task-level feedback; MOPD (multi-teacher on-policy distillation) to distill complementary skills from multiple frozen experts trained under different scaffolds; model merging to combine experts while mitigating update conflicts.
