b:T498,Assuming a fixed BPE vocabulary, the method leverages the fact that many tokens can be split into equivalent subtokens along the BPE merge path.
- CANON: standard training/testing with canonical tokenization.
- STOCHASTOK: randomly split tokens into equivalent subtokens with some probability; simple but distributionally biased over the space of valid tokenizations.
- STOCHASTOK-UNI: condition on a per-token split count and sample uniformly among valid tokenizations under that constraint, reducing bias and increasing support.
- UNIFORM-K: condition on a global total number of splits K and sample uniformly, giving explicit control over perturbation strength.
They evaluate stochastic tokenization injected at different stages (pretraining, supervised fine-tuning, and in-context usage) and under two threat models: random non-canonical tokenizations vs worst-case adversarial tokenizations.
