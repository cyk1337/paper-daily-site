a:T4e1,UltraX uses function-calling refinement instead of end-to-end text regeneration. The lightweight refiner emits edit operations, not the full rewritten document. Its operation space includes deletion, modification, and insertion, addressing the incomplete edit spaces in earlier function-calling refiners such as ProX and RefineX. Supervision is built through a multi-stage pipeline. Dataset-adaptive prompt optimization first guides an expert LLM to produce higher-quality refined texts. Line Alignment Mapping then converts original-refined text pairs into executable program supervision. Dynamic Context Replacement handles context changes caused by local edits and reduces interference between consecutive operations. Low-confidence examples are filtered, and ratio-controlled sampling balances different operation combinations. At inference time, UltraX uses sliding-window prediction for long documents, global operation aggregation to merge window-level outputs, and post-processing to normalize, validate, and execute the predicted programs. Compared with full-text rewriting, the programmatic route reduces output tokens and makes chunk recombination easier to control.
