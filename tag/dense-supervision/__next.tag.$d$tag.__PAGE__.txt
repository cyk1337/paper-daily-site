9:T588,For each unlabeled prompt, the current policy samples N candidate solutions. Final answers are extracted, the majority answer is identified, and one rollout that reaches that answer is selected as the consensus solution. A frozen snapshot of the model serves as teacher and is conditioned on the prompt plus this consensus solution, giving it privileged information unavailable to the student. The student only sees the original prompt and is trained on its own sampled tokens. The loss is a full-vocabulary Jensen–Shannon divergence between teacher and student next-token distributions on those student tokens. Several design choices matter. The teacher is stronger only through conditioning, not through parameter updates, which keeps optimization stable compared with on-policy RL. Distillation happens on the student’s own trajectory rather than forcing exact imitation of the teacher chain, reducing exposure mismatch. JSD is used instead of a one-sided KL to make matching less brittle when the teacher distribution is sharp or when consensus is imperfect. The whole pipeline reuses a single generation pass to obtain both consensus and the distillation substrate, and training runs for one epoch. The oracle variant, where the teacher is conditioned on gold-verified solutions, is especially informative because it quantifies how much of true privileged context can be recovered from self-consensus alone.a:T6d7,The evaluation has two regimes. In the transductive setting, training is done directly on the unlabeled prompts of the test benchmark and evaluation is on the same prompts, matching the common test-time RL setup. In the inductive setting, training uses one unlabeled prompt set and evaluation is on disjoint, often harder benchmarks. The headline result is that Canon is the strongest label-free method among those compared on the accuracy-versus-compute frontier. The paper reports up to about +12 pass@1 points on competition math and graduate-level science benchmarks in the transductive setting, with roughly one hour of GPU training, whereas RL-based label-free alternatives require around 6–10 hours. Relative to the oracle gold-conditioned teacher, Canon trails by only about 0.5 points on AMC, 3 points on AIME 2024, and 8 points on GPQA, suggesting that self-consensus captures a substantial fraction of the value of gold privileged context. The gains are not merely probability reallocation onto already-known answers: after training, the model solves some problems it had never solved in 32 attempts before training, and majority voting itself becomes more accurate on unsaturated benchmarks. The per-prompt analysis is one of the most useful parts: gains are largest when the base model’s majority answer is correct but uncertain, near zero when the task is already saturated, and negative when the majority is confidently wrong. These observation tokens are not sampled by the policy, but the model already computes logits for them under teacher forcing over the full transcript, so their negative log-likelihood can be gathered from the same forward pass.

The design has three important properties. First, it is on-policy: the environment targets are generated in response to the current policy’s own actions. As the policy changes and visits new terminal states, the prediction targets also change. Second, it converts failed rollouts into useful training data. A failed command may still reveal which file was missing, which test failed, or which assumption about the container was wrong. Third, it encourages the policy network itself to model terminal dynamics instead of attaching a separate world model.
