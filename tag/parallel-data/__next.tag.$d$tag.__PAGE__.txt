9:T417,The paper compares standard pretraining, SPT, with parallel pretraining, PPT. In SPT, source documents and their translations are sampled as independent examples. In PPT, each document is concatenated with its translation inside the same 2,048-token training context. Each segment is prefixed with a language label such as English: or French:, and the language order is alternated so that both translation directions receive cross-lingual context. The objective remains ordinary autoregressive next-token prediction, with loss on both languages and no additional contrastive or translation-specific objective. The key mechanism is that when predicting the later segment, the model can attend to a semantically corresponding document in another language. The paper then evaluates two-stage schedules. PPT→SPT first builds cross-lingual use of parallel context and then adapts the model back to independently sampled documents.
