c:T409,The proposed method, Mansu, stands for Mechanistic-Aligned Null-Space Unlearning. It has three components. First, EAP-IG circuit attribution identifies a sparse subgraph that causally supports the forget-set answers, avoiding uniform updates across the whole model. Second, updates are restricted to this circuit and projected into the null space of the retain-set Fisher information, aiming to reduce forget-set performance while bounding damage to retained capabilities. Third, a magnitude floor tied to the NF4 bin width is applied to cumulative updates, so that the relevant weight changes survive 4-bit quantization by construction. The paper also introduces Circuit Attribution Divergence as a post-hoc mechanistic check for whether the knowledge circuit has collapsed rather than being bypassed. The key difference from gradient ascent, preference-optimization approaches, and global null-space methods is not just a new objective; it is the combination of causal localization and an explicit quantization-survival constraint.d:T546,Experiments cover Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct, Qwen-3-8B, WMDP-bio and other hazardous-knowledge domains, plus MUSE open-ended memorization.
