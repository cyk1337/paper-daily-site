Its central empirical finding is the Reflection Advantage: on hard problems, a target model can solve more effectively when asked to reflect on a flawed expert trajectory than when solving directly from scratch. A process reward model is used to profile trajectory quality at the step level. Compared with failures from the target model or a weaker model, GNTs maintain higher process rewards and longer valid prefixes before a localized error. Causal interventions on trajectory components suggest that both the valid prefix and the error region contribute to the benefit. ReflectRL keeps the underlying training objective unchanged and modifies rollout construction. In RLVR, it samples both reflective and direct reasoning rollouts, scores them with the same verifier, and applies the usual policy update. In OPD, it uses GNTs as privileged teacher-side context while keeping the student aligned to direct reasoning.
