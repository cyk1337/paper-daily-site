9:T524,The paper introduces a reproducible benchmark on fixed 19×21 ASCII gridworlds. Unobserved cells are marked as “?”, and each move reveals only a local 5×5 window around the agent; the agent’s coordinates are always known (oracle localization), so uncertainty is purely in the map. Interaction is strictly myopic: at time t the model receives the current map state plus the immediately preceding state, and must output exactly one action from a constrained schema {UP, DOWN, LEFT, RIGHT}. The simulator executes the action, updates the revealed map, and returns the new state with simple feedback for invalid moves. Tool use, code execution, and program synthesis are disallowed to isolate planning-like behavior and minimal memory. Nine contemporary LLMs (open/proprietary; dense/MoE; instruction- vs reasoning-tuned) are evaluated under zero-shot and n-shot prompting on two tasks (Exploration and obstacle-aware Navigation) across three layouts of increasing difficulty, using engineering-oriented metrics (success and efficiency) plus qualitative case studies of behaviors and failure modes.
