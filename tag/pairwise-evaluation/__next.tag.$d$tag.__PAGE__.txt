Each raw judging protocol is represented as an expert signal and decomposed into a signed preference and a candidate-invariant reliability feature. The expert bank contains nine protocols: bidirectional direct judging, self-consistency, chain-of-thought, rubric-based judging, score-then-choose, PRePair, PriDe, CalibraEval, and an internal deliberate A-versus-B expert. Experts may be run under both display orders, and probabilities are mapped back to the canonical candidate. BAER estimates each expert’s average preference probability and order instability, then builds raw and reliability-shrunk log-odds; unstable experts are downweighted.
