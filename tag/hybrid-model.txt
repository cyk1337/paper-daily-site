That matters because many alternatives to AR become difficult to integrate into existing training and serving stacks. Second, Zarya explicitly decouples training configuration from inference mode. A trained model can be used either with MDM-style sampling or with slotted speculative decoding through an inference flag, which is more flexible than methods whose training setup hard-codes the decoding regime. Small slots keep the task close to AR and easier to optimize; larger slots force broader reconstruction and more parallel behavior. At this stage, the contribution looks more like a promising modeling recipe than a definitive replacement for standard large-scale AR pretraining.1a:T84c,The method has four main components. First, the backbone remains a causal Qwen3 model. The authors do not switch to bidirectional attention. This preserves KV-cache compatibility and leverages the optimization stability of standard AR models, but it also means the diffusion objective cannot be a fully bidirectional masked denoising objective. Instead, Zarya uses a causal masked-reconstruction objective. Second, the target sequence is partitioned into contiguous fixed-length slots. For each example, a mask ratio p_mask is sampled, and a subset of slots is fully masked. Visible slots contribute an autoregressive next-token loss, while masked slots are replaced by a special <mdm_mask> token and contribute a reconstruction loss. The model therefore learns sequential prediction and context-conditioned parallel recovery within the same forward pass. Third, the input is reordered so that clean slots come before masked slots, and both groups may be permuted internally. This is a key design choice: under causal attention, masked positions can still attend to the visible prefix, which gives a denoising-like conditional structure without breaking causal masking or KV-cache reuse. Fourth, slot size is increased over training, for example from 2 to 64. Early training stays close to local AR-like prediction; later training requires reconstruction of larger spans and encourages coarser-grained parallel generation. The paper also introduces ordered_sampling, where token-level masking importance increases from left to right within a slot, combined with 1/p_mask normalization so the model emphasizes getting early slot tokens right. That makes sense because early tokens anchor the rest of the span. Additional knobs such as grouped_noise, max_span_length, and noise_sorting support prefix completion, fill-in-the-prefix, fill-in-the-middle, and random-span masking. Relative to BD3-LMs, Zarya does not commit to a fixed block size. Relative to Eso-LMs, it is not only a loss-level fusion; it also redesigns sample structure and curriculum.
