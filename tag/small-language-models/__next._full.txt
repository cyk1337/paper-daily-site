A multi-turn agent framework is mentioned as a released extension, but the reported evaluation is mainly single-turn. The stabilization stack has three main pieces. First, a merge-and-reinitialize procedure is used to avoid silent LoRA freezing in PEFT/TRL pipelines. Second, PPO training uses float32 to avoid bfloat16 overflow in probability ratios, especially below 200M parameters. Third, reward whitening, KL-related safeguards, and weight rollback are used to prevent reward-driven policy collapse.
