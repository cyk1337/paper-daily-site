For examples to be cleaned, it selects a subset of processing stages and generates example-specific instructions for LLM rewriting stages. The stages include noise pruning for line-level boilerplate and ads, surface rectification for formatting and grammar repairs, and pedagogical augmentation for math, Wikipedia-like, or reasoning-heavy content. Training uses a teacher LLM to propose initial plans, executes those plans with the actual tool models, and then evolves the plans using execution feedback. Stages that make minimal changes or harm the original content are removed; instructions are refined when the output loses information, introduces factual errors, or leaves residual noise. This produces 300K training pairs for fine-tuning the orchestrator.
