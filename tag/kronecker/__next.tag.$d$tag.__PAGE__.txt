9:T622,The paper proceeds in three steps. First, it formulates two-sided GPTQ under a Kronecker-factored quadratic geometry. Standard GPTQ uses only the input-side covariance, corresponding to a Hessian of the form E[xx^T]⊗I. The two-sided extension allows a general A⊗B, which means quantization is guided by both input and output geometry and effectively minimizes ||(B^{1/2})^T(V-W)A^{1/2}||_F^2. Second, the authors extend the high-rate waterfilling view from prior work to this two-sided setting, yielding WaterKron. Instead of assigning uniform precision to all weights, WaterKron allocates row- and column-dependent scales and combines them with entropy coding. Under high-rate, Gaussian, and scalar-grid assumptions, the resulting allocation remains within an asymptotic gap of about 0.25 bits from the information-theoretic limit for the Kronecker distortion. Third, and most importantly, the paper analyzes WaterKron under the true full-Hessian geometry. This yields an explicit mismatch factor Φ that quantifies the multiplicative distortion penalty introduced by replacing the full Hessian with a Kronecker approximation. Choosing A and B is then cast as minimizing Φ. That optimization reduces to a Gaussian covariance-fitting problem and naturally recovers classical flip-flop updates, which motivates the proposed FlipFlop Hessian. The key distinction from prior Hessian choices in the quantization literature is that the approximation is selected to minimize distortion in the true geometry, not merely because it is easy to compute or empirically decent.3:["$","main",null,{"className":"flex-1 overflow-y-auto","children":["$","div",null,{"className":"mx-auto max-w-4xl px-4 py-8 sm:px-6 xl:max-w-6xl","children":[["$","nav",null,{"className":"mb-4 text-xs text-muted-foreground","children":[["$","$L7",null,{"href":"/","className":"hover:text-foreground","children":"Home"}],["$","span",null,{"className":"mx-1.5","children":"/"}],["$","span",null,{"className":"text-foreground","children":["tag · #","kronecker"]}]]}],["$","$L8",null,{"tag":"kronecker","initialRows":[{"paper":{"id":"2609.14706","title":"WaterKron and FlipFlop Hessian: Information-Theoretically Grounded Quantization with Kronecker-factored Hessians","abstract":"How should a Kronecker-factored Hessian approximation be chosen for post-training quantization?
