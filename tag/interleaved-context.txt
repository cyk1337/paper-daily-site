18:T565,The paper is a benchmark construction effort rather than a new model. TIC-Bench organizes evaluation into three dimensions and eight task types. Logical Association includes Linear, Cyclic, and Convergent structures, covering single-chain tracking, revisiting earlier scenes, and merging multiple reasoning branches. Temporal Association includes Sequential, Retrospective, and Parallel settings, testing forward event tracking, backward evidence retrieval, and coordination across concurrent storylines. Spatial Association includes Map and Photo reasoning, where models must reconstruct relative positions from fragmented visual patches and interleaved textual descriptions. This design is useful for two reasons. It does not merely increase the number of images; it explicitly controls reasoning structure, which helps separate perceptual difficulty from evidence-organization difficulty. And it reduces the chance of answering from parametric knowledge alone, forcing stronger dependence on local contextual bindings. The paper also includes error analysis, highlighting abstraction errors and reasoning errors as dominant failure modes. This matters because it means a model may recognize local visual content correctly and still fail by losing entity mappings, confusing character states, or failing to combine distributed clues into a final answer.
