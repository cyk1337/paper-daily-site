9:T5b9,GaugeQuant learns orthogonal rotations at transformer boundaries so that activation mass is spread more evenly across dimensions. The paper identifies usable symmetries in attention and MLP blocks. Value vectors in attention admit an SO(d_k) rotation, with corresponding transformations of W_V and W_O preserving the exact output; under GQA, each KV head group shares its rotation. Query/key rotations are constrained by RoPE: they must commute with the RoPE matrix, reducing the available symmetry to U(1)^{d_k/2}, which the paper does not exploit. In the SwiGLU MLP, the down-projection boundary also admits an invariance, but the rotation cannot be fully absorbed upstream because SiLU and the element-wise product do not commute with a general dense rotation. As a result, H·R remains a runtime operation while R^T can be fused into W_down. Instead of simulating quantization, the method adds a LogSumExp penalty on rotated boundary activations. LogSumExp acts as a smooth dense proxy for the infinity norm, which matters because the uniform quantization step size is driven by the largest outlier. A stop-gradient operator ensures that this auxiliary term updates only the rotation matrices, while the base model parameters are still trained only by cross-entropy.
