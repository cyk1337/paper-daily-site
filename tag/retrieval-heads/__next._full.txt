18:T928,核心做法分两段：
1) 统一的 retrieval-head 检测与跨模态对齐分析。
- 定义：对每个样本 i，有问题 token 集 Qi、证据 token 集 Gi。对每层 l、每头 h，计算从问题位置 q 到上下文位置 t 的注意力 a_lh^(i)(q,t)。
- 校准：注意力里有位置偏好/内容偏好等与“问题”无关的成分。沿用 MMRetHead 的思路，用同一上下文但把问题替换成“无信息问题”得到 u_lh^(i,∅)，用两者差异构造 calibrated retrieval score s_lh（文中给出公式），以减少“头本来就爱看某些位置/模态 token”的伪相关。
- 排序：按 s_lh 给所有头排序，默认取 top 5% 作为 retrieval heads，并在不同任务/设置下比较稀疏性与重叠度。
- 数据设计：为了检验“模态变化但证据内容不变”，构造 AV-NIAH 成对样本：同一段背景视频中插入同一个 6 位数字作为答案，插入方式要么是音频里合成语音读出（视频不变），要么是画面中央 banner 显示 1 秒（音频不变）；问题固定为“secret number”。证据 span 通过时间段映射到对应 audio/video tokens。这样能把“内容”控制住，主要变化来自证据模态。
- 检查点跨度：分析 18 个 checkpoint，覆盖语言、图文、统一、omni-modal 模型，用来观察 retrieval heads 是否在适配新模态时被复用/保留。

2) 因果验证与基于 retrieval-head 的可训练改进。
- 因果验证：对检测到的 retrieval heads 做 targeted ablation，观察音视频检索性能下降；进一步只 ablate 跨模态共享的那一小撮头（文中称少于全部 heads 的 2%），也会显著伤害更广泛的多模态推理任务，支持“共享头是关键电路而非任务偶然”。

1) Unified retrieval-head detection with calibration.
- For each example with question tokens Q and annotated evidence tokens G, compute per-head attention from question positions to context positions.
- To remove question-independent biases (positional preferences, modality-token priors), recompute attention using an uninformative/empty question on the same context, and define a calibrated retrieval score (following MMRetHead). Heads are ranked by this score; the top 5% are treated as retrieval heads by default.

2) Controlled cross-modality setup (AV-NIAH).
- Construct paired audio-visual inputs where the evidence content is identical but presented either as synthesized speech (audio condition) or as on-screen text in a banner (visual condition), while keeping background video, insertion depth, question, and answer fixed. Map the evidence time span to the corresponding audio/video token span.
- Analyze 18 checkpoints spanning language, vision-language, unified, and omni-modal models to study head reuse and preservation during modality expansion.

3) Causal tests and optimization.
- Perform targeted head ablations to test necessity for audio-visual retrieval; additionally ablate only the subset of heads shared across modalities (reported as <2% of all heads) to test whether a compact shared circuit supports broader multimodal reasoning.
- For multimodal document retrieval (MMDocIR), select retrieval heads on document-related tasks, aggregate their question→page attention into a page score, and train it with a ranking objective to prefer relevant pages over distractors.
