9:T571,LingBot-VA 2.0 has two central pretraining components. The first is a semantic visual-action tokenizer. Instead of optimizing only VAE-style reconstruction, it aligns visual latents with a frozen visual foundation model and learns latent actions through self-supervision, placing world states and actions in a shared latent space. This lets unlabeled web video provide action-relevant supervision. The second component is a causal diffusion transformer. Conditioned on language instructions via cross-attention to a frozen pretrained language model, it predicts future visual latents and the latent actions connecting them. The causal next-latent objective is chosen instead of retrofitting a bidirectional video generator, because robot control unfolds forward in time. The backbone uses sparse MoE feed-forward layers to keep capacity while reducing active compute. The training objective adds multi-chunk prediction, supervising several future chunks at once so that the model learns trajectory-level dynamics rather than copying adjacent-frame appearance. For deployment, Foresight Reasoning runs future-latent prediction asynchronously with action execution and re-grounds predictions on the latest real observation through a learned forward-dynamics step. The system also uses few-step consistency distillation, quantized execution, and a hierarchical VLM planner for long-horizon tasks.a:T888,The core module is a causal resonant field mixer. Each head is parameterized as a damped resonator, with a kernel defined over lag t using exponential decay and oscillatory response. The main parameters are alpha for damping, omega for angular frequency, and phi for phase. In implementation, unconstrained parameters are mapped into valid ranges with softplus/sigmoid transforms, and heads are initialized with log-spaced half-lives and frequencies so different channels start with different timescales.

The input is projected through grouped linear layers into a drive tensor and a gate tensor. The drive is processed by the resonant mixer, while the gate is applied after mixing to provide content-dependent modulation without constructing query-key similarity matrices. During training and prefill, each head performs causal convolution with its resonant kernel, implemented with FFT for O(T log T) complexity. During autoregressive decoding, the model does not maintain a growing KV cache. Instead, each head keeps a fixed-size complex recurrent state, stored as paired real tensors for numerical stability. Each new token updates that state according to the same damped resonant dynamics, and the output is read from the updated state.

Two auxiliary components are important. A local lexical path preserves short-range token detail, which the resonant field alone may blur. An optional cross-head coupling matrix allows information exchange across resonant channels, reducing the expressivity loss from fully independent heads. The block is then combined with a standard feed-forward layer, so the overall architecture still resembles a modern LM block.

Relative to SSM-style models such as S4 or Mamba, the paper emphasizes explicit resonant kernels and frequency-selective dynamics rather than a more general state-space formulation. Relative to linear attention, it removes token-token similarity entirely and relies on learned causal kernels plus gating. That gives efficiency, but it also raises the usual concern: content-based addressing is weaker than explicit matching, so long-range retrieval may depend heavily on how much the gate and local branch can compensate.b:T55e,The empirical message is modest but clear: at very small scale, ResonatorLM shows strong long-context decoding efficiency and slightly better language-modeling quality than a matched Transformer baseline. At 32K tokens, the paper reports a 6.47x decoding speedup over the baseline Transformer, while also improving perplexity and accuracy. The most useful missing analyses would be the contribution of the local lexical path, the effect of cross-head coupling, sensitivity to frequency/half-life initialization, and numerical consistency between FFT prefill and recurrent decode.
