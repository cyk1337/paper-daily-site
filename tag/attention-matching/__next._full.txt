Numerical prediction is handled by discretizing a range into bins. Point prediction is handled by discretizing the image into regions. The model encodes the observation, question, and candidate descriptions, then uses the final attention layer’s pretrained query and key projections to score candidates, followed by a softmax. No extra prediction head or trainable readout parameters are added. Multi-point prediction is handled by conditional tree expansion using the same trained point predictor.
