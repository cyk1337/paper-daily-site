18:T929,方法核心是一个 iterative prune-heal-backtrack 流程。模型被表示为 residual-stream writers/readers 构成的有向图：writer 包括 token embedding、每个 attention head 的 residual contribution、每个 MLP 输出；reader 包括后续 attention/MLP 输入以及最终 logits。边表示某个 writer 对下游 reader 的信息通路。与常见直接置零不同，作者采用 clean/corrupted prompt 配对，并在关闭边时用 corrupted prompt 上对应 writer 的 counterfactual activation 替代 clean activation，避免把读入分布推离数据流形。这样“边是否必要”更接近因果替换而不是粗暴破坏。

训练上只更新一个从零开始的 LoRA adapter，底座模型冻结。初始时所有边开放。每一轮先用因果重要性估计给当前存活边排序，然后剪掉一批最弱边；接着在新边掩码下训练 LoRA，使适配后的模型尽量复现原模型输出。这里的目标不是重新学任务标签，而是通过剩余通路逼近原模型 next-token distribution，因此它更像 constrained distillation。随后用 held-out controller 决定是否接受本轮剪枝：只有当目标行为准确率仍达标，且 off-target 语言建模能力没有明显恶化时，才保留这次删除，否则回退到上一状态。这个 accept/reject 机制很重要，因为它把“更小电路”约束在“行为保真 + 通用能力保真”之内，而不是单纯追求 sparsity。

作者把最终最小可接受电路记为 C1。与 frozen search 的区别在于：传统 EAP/EAP-IG/EAP-GP 之类方法只是在固定权重上估计边重要性；这里则允许通过低秩参数更新把行为迁移到更少边上。论文还专门做了一个对照：使用同样的搜索流程但不更新权重，结果明显更差，说明收益主要来自 reshaping behavior implementation，而不是搜索器本身更聪明。

另一个技术点是 fidelity 评估不只看任务准确率，还看与原模型输出分布的 KL，以及 condensed circuit 对原模型失败模式的预测能力。也就是说，作者知道“压缩后的小电路”可能只解释修改后的网络，因此专门测试它是否仍能描述原始未修改模型。这一点让方法不只是做出一个小模型，而是尽量保留对原模型机制的解释联系。19:T8f5,The method is an iterative prune-heal-backtrack procedure. A transformer is represented as a directed graph over residual-stream writers and readers. Writers include token embeddings, each attention head’s residual contribution, and each MLP output; readers include downstream attention/MLP inputs and final logits. Edges represent information flow from an upstream writer to a downstream reader.

A key design choice is how edges are ablated. Instead of zeroing an edge, the method uses paired clean/corrupted prompts and replaces the clean activation flowing through a closed edge with the corresponding activation from the corrupted prompt. This counterfactual replacement avoids pushing activations off-manifold, making edge necessity tests more causally meaningful than naive zero ablation.

Training updates only a freshly initialized LoRA adapter while keeping the base model frozen. Starting from all edges open, each round ranks surviving edges by causal importance, removes the weakest ones, and then trains the LoRA adapter to reproduce the original model’s outputs through the remaining graph. This is closer to constrained distillation than task finetuning: the goal is to match the original next-token distribution under a restricted internal pathway. A held-out controller then accepts the deletion only if both target-task accuracy and off-target language-modeling capability remain within tolerance; otherwise the method rolls back. The smallest accepted graph is denoted C1.

This differs sharply from frozen circuit search methods such as EAP-IG: those estimate importance under fixed weights, whereas Circuit Condensation allows low-rank adaptation to reshape where the behavior lives. The paper explicitly tests the same search loop without weight updates and finds that it loses badly, which isolates the gain to behavioral reshaping rather than better search alone.

Another strong point is the fidelity analysis. The authors do not stop at task accuracy on the adapted model. They also measure KL to the original model’s next-token distribution and test whether the condensed circuit predicts failure modes of the unmodified model better than size-matched frozen circuits. This is important because otherwise one could end up explaining only the modified network.1a:T6f6,The evaluation spans 4 behaviors, 8 models from 4 families, and 3 random seeds. The paper then shows why smaller circuits matter operationally. The condensed circuits make exhaustive subset tests, all-pairs ablations, and edge-level necessity checks feasible. Matched frozen circuits would require a median 55x more pairwise ablations, and subset enumeration is infeasible in all 19 matched cases reported. The condensed circuits also better predict where the original model will fail than frozen circuits of the same size. That is a strong indication that the method is not merely producing a compact explanation for the adapted model, but retaining explanatory contact with the original one.
