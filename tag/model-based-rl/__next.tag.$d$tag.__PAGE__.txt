9:T460,Dwell is a model-based RL framework that reframes a pretrained VLM as a recurrent latent transition operator in a POMDP. From histories of observations and actions, it infers a belief state b_t in the VLM’s latent space, predicts transitions and rewards in that space, and uses imagined latent rollouts to improve the policy.

Two implementation choices carry most of the novelty:
- Latent imagination over reconstruction: following joint-embedding style representation learning, it avoids synthesizing observations at every step and instead models predictable structure in a compact latent, reducing imagination cost.
- Dual adapters on one backbone: world/reward modeling and policy learning are instantiated as separate LoRA adapters attached to the same VLM backbone. This keeps a shared representation interface (mitigating latent incompatibility) while enabling parameter-efficient, partially decoupled online updates.
