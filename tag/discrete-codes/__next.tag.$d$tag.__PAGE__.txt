9:T44a,Abstract-CoT extends the tokenizer with a reserved abstract vocabulary and delimiter tokens. At inference time, the model generates a bounded abstract trace before producing the final answer. The main obstacle is cold start: the newly added tokens have random embeddings and no learned function. The training recipe has two stages. The first stage is a policy-iteration-style warm-up. It uses verbal CoT as supervision, but forces the answer to attend only to the abstract tokens rather than the full verbal rationale, making the abstract sequence an information bottleneck. The model is then self-distilled on its own generated abstract traces without access to the verbal CoT, and the process is repeated. The second stage applies GRPO with decoding constrained to the abstract vocabulary, refining the policy over abstract traces. Compared with Coconut-style continuous latent reasoning, this approach avoids architectural changes.
