18:T616,The method has a data side and a training side. The data side is called Refine. Starting from simple multimodal questions and successful candidate reasoning chains, it extracts an entity chain and then applies three controllable operators. Expand appends L evidence-supported hops after the original chain, turning the original answer into an intermediate node and forcing the agent to continue searching. Blur selects M internal nodes and attaches W retrievable but indirect facts to each, replacing explicit entity mentions with searchable clues and increasing disambiguation difficulty. Visualize selects N nodes near the end of the chain, retains up to P retrieved images per node, and injects visual evidence requirements so that solving the task requires both textual and visual retrieval. The pipeline also includes validation and filtering to reduce shortcuts from parametric knowledge or alternative evidence paths. On the training side, the paper uses joint RL and OPD. RL uses answer rewards plus lightweight behavior penalties to discourage redundant verification and premature stopping. OPD is not applied as full-trajectory hard distillation; instead, token-level teacher guidance is applied on the student’s own imperfect rollouts, with strength modulated by a shaped reward gap, and the OPD coefficient is exponentially annealed over training. The motivation is clear: pure RL gives only a coarse scalar signal, while full distillation can overwrite exploration with teacher behavior.
