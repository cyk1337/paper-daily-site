9:T442,The method builds on contrastive persona-vector extraction from prior activation-steering work. For each trait, natural-language positive and negative descriptions are used to derive a behavioral direction in activation space; interventions along that direction are then tested during generation. The main addition is the diagnostic taxonomy. A trait is labeled natural if it appears at baseline, steerable if it can be amplified by intervention, and intractable if the standard protocol cannot recover a usable direction. The authors construct a 53-trait inventory across clinician interaction, generic behavioral disposition, elementary education, and agentic task completion. They also study pairwise composition by applying two persona vectors jointly over all 171 unordered pairs from 19 generic traits, looking for clean composition, dominance, or collapse.
