另一个重要结果是泛化与边界。用原始预训练语料拟合的 novc 仍然保留主要收益，说明这个信号不完全依赖数学特定标签或任务定制。域外测试上，novelty 在 prose 上能降低 bits per byte；在 source code 和 synthetic execution traces 上则大致与 entropy 打平，无论是 bpb 还是 exact token accuracy 都没有明显优势。作者据此给出机制解释：computed results 这类位置的 surprisal 往往并不高，所以 entropy-based patching 会错过；而 novelty 在数学解答里常常恰好在“新结果串开始”的地方升高，因此能把 patch 打在计算结果附近。相反，在代码和执行轨迹里，真正关键的结构不一定表现为“新字节串开始”，所以 novelty 的优势消失。论文还分析了 boundary enrichment——SOMBRERO 一类 learned chunker 优化的指标——发现 entropy 在这个指标上反而最高，但它在 computed results 上最差，这说明 enrichment 这类局部统计目标并不等于对下游推理位置有帮助。作者也提到 learned chunker H-Net 可以自行恢复这类结构，暗示简单启发式和可学习 chunker 之间可能存在互补关系。19:T6ff,The proposed method is novelty patching, motivated by LZ77-style compression. If the upcoming bytes can be copied from recent context, they are old information; if they cannot, new information is entering the stream, and that is where a patch should start. Formally, for each position t, m_t is the length of the longest prefix of the future byte stream that appears within the previous 128 bytes, capped at 16. Novelty is defined as -m_t, so shorter matches mean higher novelty. Since m_t depends on future bytes, it cannot be used directly online. The authors therefore build a causal approximation: a lookup table indexed by the previous two bytes, storing the average value of -m_t observed under that context. The table is fit on 4 MB of raw text in seconds on CPU, with backoff to one-byte context and then the global mean for rare contexts. At inference time, patching is just a table lookup plus thresholding to hit a target patch rate.

Several design choices are notable. The method needs no labels, no reference model, and no loss-based ablation signal, making it cheaper than both entropy patching and the earlier dependence-table approach. It changes the routing principle from surprisal-seeking to novelty-seeking. The paper also evaluates two variants: nov, fit on GSM8K and MATH training solutions, and novc, fit on a raw pretraining-corpus sample, to test whether the effect depends on math-specific fitting. Baselines include BLT’s entropy threshold, the prior dependence table, and a hand-written syntax+entropy rule. Models are BLT-style systems trained from scratch with cross-attention pooling, a windowed local decoder, and 128-byte context, at 1M, 12M, and 50M parameters on a 1.9 GB math-heavy corpus, with matched patch rates around 11% and three seeds per setup.1a:T79b,The main gains appear only once the model is large enough to use the extra global compute effectively. At 1M parameters, all methods are near the floor on computed results, around 3–5%, and only the hand-written syntax rule noticeably improves final answers. At 12M, novelty patching separates clearly: final-answer accuracy reaches 71.5%, versus 46.6% for entropy and 56.7% for dependence; computed-result accuracy rises from 5.8% under entropy to 10.1%. At 50M, the gap becomes large: computed results reach 57.5%, compared with 20.7% for entropy and 35.1% for dependence; final answers reach 89.5%, compared with 59.9% and 78.2%. Entropy also shows high seed variance on computed results, suggesting that it is not just weak but unstable for this failure mode. The paper checks that gradient clipping at 50M is neutral for the entropy baseline, so the result is not an artifact of training stabilization.

Generalization results are mixed in an informative way. The corpus-fitted novelty table, built from raw pretraining text rather than math-solution data, preserves the main gain, which means the signal is not purely task-specific. Outside math, novelty lowers bits per byte on prose, but only ties entropy on source code and synthetic execution traces, both in bpb and exact token accuracy. The mechanism analysis explains why: computed results often have ordinary surprisal, so entropy misses them, while novelty tends to spike where a new result string begins in math solutions.
