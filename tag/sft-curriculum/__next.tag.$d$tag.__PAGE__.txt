5:T40d,The recipe has three stages. The first stage uses SFT to impose a more disciplined proof-search style. The authors curate long-form solution, self-verification, and self-refinement trajectories from math, science, coding, and instruction-following sources, ending with 338K filtered trajectories under 8K response tokens. A reverse-perplexity curriculum orders examples from those most mismatched to the initial policy to those more familiar to it. This is meant to inject unfamiliar proof behavior first, then consolidate without overly erasing existing competence. The second stage uses two levels of RL. Coarse RL uses verifiable prompts and binary outcome rewards in the RLVR style. Refined RL moves to proof quality, using a generative proof-level reward model, self-refinement prompts, and experience replay to retain rare successes on hard problems.
