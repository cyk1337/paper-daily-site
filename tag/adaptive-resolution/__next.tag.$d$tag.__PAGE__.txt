9:T546,The method has two stages. Stage one is REL-SFT. The authors build a 29.4K Reasoning-Evidence Localization chain-of-thought dataset, where each reasoning trace is aligned with page indices and normalized bounding boxes. Training is done across seven rendering resolutions, so the model learns to localize relevant evidence under compressed global views rather than simply reading everything at high fidelity. Stage two is GRPO. At inference, the model starts from low-DPI page images and iteratively generates a reasoning segment followed by an action: either answer directly or issue a region-enhancement call with a page index and bounding box. A tool then crops the aligned region from a higher-DPI rendering of the same page and returns it as an observation. The model incorporates that observation into subsequent reasoning until it answers or exhausts the call budget. A few design choices matter here: low- and high-resolution views share the same page coordinates, which avoids alignment drift; enhancement is local rather than full-page rerendering, which keeps added visual tokens under control; and policy learning must optimize not only correctness but also when to zoom, where to zoom, and when to stop.
