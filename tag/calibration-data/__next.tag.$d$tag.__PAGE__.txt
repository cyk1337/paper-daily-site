9:T439,The authors replace aggregate post-pruning accuracy with a capability-retention view over four dimensions: General, Commonsense, Code, and Math. They then profile 15 calibration sources using information metrics from Objective Information Theory. The strongest predictors are Distortion, operationalized through perplexity, and Aggregation, measured through repeated 4-gram patterns. Broader measures such as Scope, Variety, Granularity, and Mismatch are less reliable. Based on this analysis, the paper proposes two calibration constructions. The first is mix_balanced, which samples uniformly from capability-aligned pools under the same 128-sequence budget used by the baselines. The second is IGSP, a self-generation protocol that uses the base model and the evaluation taxonomy to create capability-balanced calibration data, while controlling perplexity bands and reducing repeated-pattern bias.
