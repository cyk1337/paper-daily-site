- Task: given a reasoning trace segmented into prefixes, extract prefix-safe observations o_t and predict eventual correctness y.
- Evidence interface: plug-in observation families including continuous scores (entropy/verifier/confidence), discrete codes (text markers, self-verification flags, hidden-cluster IDs), token-pooling probes, and latent-trajectory features.
- Calibration-first design: fit state-conditional likelihoods P(o_t | z_t) on held-out calibration splits (question-level). Default supervision propagates final labels to prefixes (weak supervision), with alternatives such as final-step calibration and EM variants.
- Online filtering: combine calibrated likelihoods with a simple transition model to produce a belief trajectory over time.
