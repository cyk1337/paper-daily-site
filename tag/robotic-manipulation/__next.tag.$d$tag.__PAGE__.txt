9:T67b,The framework has three main components. First, the LaST-R1 architecture. It is initialized from Qwen3-VL-4B and further pretrained on large-scale robotic manipulation datasets. At inference time, the model autoregressively generates latent reasoning tokens before producing actions, and those latent tokens condition a parallel action decoder. These tokens are not natural-language explanations; they are internal reasoning representations intended to capture physical dynamics, future state structure, and manipulation intent. To stabilize them, the authors anchor the latent reasoning space to global future representations from a vision foundation model, providing semantic and spatial priors so the latent variables do not drift into arbitrary hidden codes. Second, LAPO, or Latent-to-Action Policy Optimization. Instead of applying PPO-style optimization only to action probabilities, LAPO defines a joint step-level likelihood ratio over latent reasoning and action generation. This allows environmental reward to influence both the internal reasoning trajectory and the final action policy. The key benefit is better credit assignment: the reward arrives from interaction outcomes, but the update can still reach the intermediate reasoning process. Third, the adaptive latent CoT mechanism. Rather than fixing a constant reasoning horizon, the model adjusts the amount of latent reasoning based on task demands. Harder tasks can allocate more internal computation, while reactive tasks can use shorter reasoning for lower latency. The overall training recipe is pretraining on robotic data, task-specific SFT warm-up, and then online RL post-training.a:T547,From the provided full-text excerpt, the main confirmed results are as follows: the model is built on Qwen3-VL-4B and pretrained on multiple robotic manipulation datasets; on LIBERO, it reaches a 99.8% average success rate with one-shot warm-up, outperforming prior baselines; on four real-world complex tasks, LAPO improves over the warm-up policy by up to 44%, reaching a 90% average success rate across single-arm and dual-arm settings; after RL post-training, the system also shows zero-shot generalization to unseen objects, backgrounds, and lighting conditions. The experimental story appears to have three pillars.
