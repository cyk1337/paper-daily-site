a:T415,The paper proposes GRIP under the “Retrieval as Generation” paradigm: the retriever remains external, but the LM generates explicit control tokens during autoregressive decoding to trigger retrieval, rewrite queries, and terminate. GRIP augments the output space with four tokens: [RETRIEVE], [INTERMEDIARY], [ANSWER], [SOLVED]. These define two structured branches: continuing evidence acquisition via [INTERMEDIARY]→[RETRIEVE] (with a generated query), and completion via [ANSWER]→[SOLVED].
At the core is Self-Triggered Information Planning: the model repeatedly assesses information sufficiency; if sufficient it answers and stops, otherwise it emits an intermediary state, triggers retrieval with a reformulated query conditioned on the current reasoning context, incorporates retrieved passages, and loops. A structured training paradigm supervises multiple answerability types with distinct control-token patterns, teaching the model when to retrieve, how to query, and when to stop—without auxiliary controllers or classifiers.b:T430,For skim:
