The paper is stronger because it does not stop at introducing the method. It systematically compares three exploration families. First, repeated sampling, where all generations are drawn independently from the same starting point. Mechanistically, Hill Sampling treats the LLM as a conditional editor rather than a one-shot generator. The incumbent acts as a moving scaffold.
