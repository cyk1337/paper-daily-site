9:T6a5,The proposed Replayed-Prefix On-Policy Distillation (ReOPD) framework maintains an offline pool of teacher trajectories collected as a byproduct of teacher RL training, requiring no extra environment interaction. During training, prefixes are sampled from this pool; the student generates its own action at the target step, environment observations are read directly from stored trajectories instead of executing live environment calls, and the teacher only provides per-token distribution supervision at the current step rather than generating full trajectories. Theoretically, the gap between ReOPD and the ideal interactive OPD objective is decomposed into two terms: a student occupancy mismatch term from using non-student-generated prefixes, and a teacher reliability term from error when the teacher provides supervision on OOD student-induced histories, proving fully on-policy training is not universally optimal. Guided by this decomposition, multi-turn OPD is framed as reliability-aware prefix distribution design, using a geometric bridge between student and teacher prefix occupancy. A simple step-decay sampling schedule is used for implementation: early in training and for early interaction steps, more student-on-policy prefixes are used when the teacher-student gap is small and the teacher remains reliable; later in training and for late interaction steps, more replayed teacher prefixes are used to reduce noise from unreliable teacher supervision.
