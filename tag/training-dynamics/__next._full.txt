18:T402,The key method is parameter-trajectory crosscoding. Instead of fitting sparse dictionaries to activations, the method fits one sparse dictionary to the readout rows of all tokens across all checkpoints. Each token has a shared sparse code, while each feature has checkpoint-specific decoder directions and gates. This keeps feature identities aligned over training and avoids the unstable matching problem that would arise from training a separate sparse autoencoder at every checkpoint. Rows are centered and scaled per checkpoint, so feature norms track structural reorganization rather than only global norm growth. The second diagnostic is readout swapping: hold hidden states fixed and decode them with readouts from other checkpoints. Together with linear probes, this separates hidden-state availability from native readout expression.
