9:T4d2,MTRouter learns an offline outcome estimator over joint (history, model) representations.
- History embedding: serialize task block + interaction context; encode with a frozen text encoder ϕ into z_x. Context is truncated by a token budget (8192): always keep the task block and the most recent turns.
- Model embedding: represent each candidate model a with ψ(a), combining structured attributes (context limit, knowledge cutoff, pricing, etc.) and a learnable residual vector e_a to capture behavior not explained by metadata; residual is regularized to reduce overfitting.
- Joint embedding: combine z_x and z_a into a joint feature fed to a predictor of terminal episode outcome (score/accuracy).
- Training: supervised from logged trajectories, with an “error-aware adjustment” to stabilize learning from offline data (intended to mitigate noisy credit assignment from turn choice to terminal outcome).
- Inference: at each turn, score candidates and pick the model with the highest predicted terminal outcome, subject to a per-episode cost budget B and turn limit.
