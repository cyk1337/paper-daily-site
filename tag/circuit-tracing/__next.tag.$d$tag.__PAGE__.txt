9:T413,CIE-Scorer is built around internal-external discrepancy. Instead of tracing the full circuit over a long CoT, it first selects informative tokens within each reasoning sentence. It then performs circuit tracing only around those tokens and builds sentence-level computational graphs. These graphs are compressed into internal reasoning embeddings, which form an internal trajectory over the reasoning process. The external trajectory is obtained from hidden representations of the displayed reasoning sentences. A lightweight adaptor aligns the two latent spaces. Under a shared graph construction rule, the method builds an internal graph and an external graph, then computes their Fused Gromov-Wasserstein distance. This distance captures both node-feature mismatch and structural mismatch, and is used as the unfaithfulness score. Compared with text-only detectors, the method explicitly uses model-internal evidence.
