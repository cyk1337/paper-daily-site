b:T7d9,技术路线可以拆成四块。第一块是长上下文与深层信息流。Kimi Delta Attention 负责长序列混合，周期性插入 Gated MLA 层保留全局交互；这说明他们没有完全放弃 attention，而是用更便宜的序列混合主干配合稀疏的全局 attention 校正。Attention Residuals 则让每层可以选择性访问所有前层表示，缓解超深网络中的信息衰减与优化困难。第二块是超大规模稀疏 MoE。K3 使用 Stable LatentMoE，把 routed expert 空间扩到 896 个，每个 token 激活 16 个 expert，外加 shared expert。这里的难点不是 MoE 结构本身，而是极高稀疏度下的训练稳定性与负载均衡。报告提到用 normalization、SiTU-GLU 和 Quantile Balancing 来稳定路由与激活分布，目标是既扩大专家容量，又避免 router collapse、负载失衡和训练震荡。第三块是训练与数据 recipe。公开文本里没有把所有数据细节完全展开，但明确把 refined data and training recipes 作为 2.5× scaling efficiency 的组成部分，而不是把收益全部归因于架构。这一点很重要，因为大模型报告常把数据因素弱化。第四块是 post-training 与基础设施协同。RL 覆盖 general、agentic、coding 和多种 reasoning effort level，训练环境包括可验证搜索、专业知识工作、软件工程、kernel optimization、vision-in-the-loop 工具使用、persistent assistant workflow 等。不同领域和 effort 的策略最后通过 multi-teacher on-policy distillation 合并到统一模型。系统侧则分别为 KDA、2.8T MoE 预训练、百万 token agentic RL 和 serving 做了专门设计：如 fused kernels、KDA context parallelism、state-aware prefix caching、MoonEP 的静态 shape 与零拷贝通信、外部 KV 保留、partial rollout、可恢复 microVM sandbox 等。这里最值得注意的是，他们把“能否训练和运行长轨迹 RL”当成模型设计的一部分，而不是后处理问题。c:T848,The technical design has four major pieces. First is long-context and deep information flow. Kimi Delta Attention serves as the main long-sequence mixing mechanism, while Gated MLA layers are periodically interleaved to preserve global interaction. This suggests they do not fully abandon attention; instead they use a cheaper sequence-mixing backbone plus sparse global correction. Attention Residuals let each layer selectively access representations from all previous layers, which is meant to reduce information decay and optimization difficulty in very deep networks. Second is ultra-large sparse MoE. K3 uses Stable LatentMoE with 896 routed experts and 16 activated experts per token, plus shared experts. The challenge here is not the MoE idea itself but stable optimization and balanced routing under extreme sparsity. The report highlights normalization, SiTU-GLU, and Quantile Balancing as the stabilizers that keep routing and activation distributions under control. Third is the training and data recipe. The public text does not fully expose all data details, but it explicitly includes refined data and training recipes as part of the claimed efficiency gain rather than attributing everything to architecture. That is an important signal. Fourth is post-training and infrastructure co-design. RL spans general reasoning, agentic tasks, coding, and multiple reasoning-effort levels. Training environments include verifiable search, professional knowledge work, software engineering, kernel optimization, multimodal tool use, persistent assistant workflows, and autonomous execution. These specialized policies are later merged through multi-teacher on-policy distillation. On the systems side, the report introduces fused kernels, KDA context parallelism, state-aware prefix caching, MoonEP for perfectly balanced expert execution with static shapes and zero-copy communication, plus external KV retention, partial rollouts, and resumable microVM sandboxes for million-token agentic RL.
