- RSA with category prototypes: average document embeddings per category, build a cosine-distance RDM, and compute Spearman correlation with an expert symptom-relationship RDM. - Multi-baseline confound controls: compare geometry against affect (VAD), LIWC, stylistic, and topic-based baselines to attribute what drives alignment.
