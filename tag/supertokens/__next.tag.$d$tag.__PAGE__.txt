9:T4f1,Pipeline:
1) Collect model-native CoT traces by sampling reasoning outputs from the target model on OpenThoughts3 prompts.
2) Entropy-based framing: compute per-token conditional entropy H_θ(t_i|t_<i). The paper argues reasoning tokens split into (a) structural tokens—recurring multi-token phrases whose continuation becomes near-deterministic once started (low entropy), and (b) organic tokens—problem-specific content (math expressions, bindings, intermediate results) with higher entropy. They derive a compression ceiling showing that if the mean entropy inside merge spans is much smaller than log|V|, the achievable compression is approximately the fraction of tokens covered by merges.
3) Learn “reasoning supertokens” via cross-word BPE (SuperBPE) on the reasoning traces only, append them to the base vocabulary, and teach the model to use them via SFT. To limit distribution skew, n-gram counts are capped per sample. Training updates embeddings, LM head, and only a small subset of transformer layers to minimize disruption.
