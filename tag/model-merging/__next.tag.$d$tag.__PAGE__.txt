9:T4a7,ACLArena builds a fixed sequential pipeline rather than evaluating arbitrary task permutations. The order is Math → Search → E-commerce → Instruction Following. Math strengthens reasoning, Search uses a single tool, E-commerce involves up to 15 tools, and Instruction Following is introduced last for adherence and alignment. Sequential training is used as the diagnostic baseline. The analysis has two levels: a model-level view that tracks capability changes across stages, and a token-level view that studies how individual training signals shape behavior and optimization. The method comparison covers three practical strategies. On-policy distillation uses previous checkpoints as teachers on the student’s current state distribution. Self-distilled fine-tuning replays filtered specialist trajectories offline. Model merging reuses prior models directly in parameter space. The proposed Mixture of Low-Rank Experts first uses SDFT to learn valid behaviors across domains, then applies RL through stage-specific LoRA adapters. A router composes the resulting experts. The design reduces interference by avoiding a single dense parameter update path for all heterogeneous skills.a:T5c4,RLPF proceeds in four stages. First, parameters are grouped by semantic role rather than matched tensor by tensor. The groups include patch embedding, sequence mixing, feed-forward blocks, and normalization. This is necessary because attention projections and SSM transition parameters are neither shape-compatible nor functionally identical. Second, each group is projected into a shared low-rank latent space. The geometric choice is treated as a design hyperparameter, not as evidence that raw weights intrinsically live on a manifold. Third, fusion is performed in that latent space with component-dependent geometry: some groups use Euclidean averaging, while others use a Lorentz-model hyperbolic barycenter, implemented as a Fréchet mean under squared geodesic distance. The paper also adds a geodesic anchor objective to regularize the fused point relative to branch-specific representations and reduce instability in curved space. Fourth, the fused latent codes are decoded back into a ViT branch and an SSM branch, and a small learned gate combines their logits. This is the key conceptual point: the method does not collapse two models into one unified parameterization. It uses geometry-aware initialization plus lightweight training to build a retained two-branch hybrid. Compared with model soups, task arithmetic, or TIES, the novelty is less about the averaging rule itself and more about defining a role-based cross-architecture correspondence before fusion.b:T449,The evaluation covers CIFAR-10, Oxford-IIIT Pet, and ImageNet-1K. The most useful aspect of the experimental design is that it separates several effects that are often conflated: ImageNet results before fine-tuning are reported separately from final fine-tuned gated-hybrid performance; Euclidean versus Lorentz fusion is compared; and the contribution of parameter grouping and gating is ablated. The qualitative takeaways are: (1) geometry-aware fusion provides a usable initialization, but most of the final gain appears to come from retaining both branches and fine-tuning them; (2) different parameter groups prefer different geometries, and the paper does not claim hyperbolic fusion is universally better; (3) the learned gate is important for stable combination of branch outputs.
