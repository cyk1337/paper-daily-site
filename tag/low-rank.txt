First, it builds a canary task for procedure execution. Each synthetic SOP contains 10 ordered steps plus a conditional 11th step. The prompt includes both a target SOP and a distractor SOP, and the model must enumerate the exact ordered steps under two paraphrased conditions. Evaluation includes step recall and branch correctness, but the key metric is invented_x: the number of step strings drawn from the global SOP vocabulary that do not belong to the target SOP or its own conditional branch. This isolates fabricated procedure content rather than generic recall errors.

Second, the paper compares compression operators at matched perplexity dose. It contrasts coherent low-rank truncation, implemented as per-tensor SVD truncation, with magnitude pruning, and uses quantization as an additional control. The design is important: by matching perplexity as closely as possible, the authors try to hold overall damage constant and test whether behavior still differs by operator. Their central finding is that coherent low-rank error induces procedure invention while pruning at similar perplexity often does not. This leads to the mechanism claim that the relevant axis is coherence times rate, not damage magnitude alone.

Third, the paper proposes a cheap data-free detector, a two-axis coherence screen, to predict which compressed builds will fail the canary. The exact implementation details are not fully visible from the provided text, but it is described as a fixed-threshold pre-flight check operating across architectures without labeled safety data. The authors are careful to say that the fidelity probe itself is not the main claim; the stronger point is the dissociation between fidelity and safe agentic behavior, and then the conversion of that cautionary result into a more actionable screening method.1b:T72f,The experiments cover three model families: Mistral-7B, Qwen3-8B, and Llama-3.1-8B. The SOP canary is pre-registered with 24 SOPs and 3 random seeds, giving 144 paired events per arm, with seed-level reporting. The standard acceptance guards are WikiText-2 perplexity, zero-shot MMLU, and a data-free output-fidelity probe.

The main quantitative results are sharp. On Mistral-7B, an in-regime SVD build at only 1.069× baseline perplexity, comfortably inside the common 1.15× acceptance band, produces +1.729 invented steps per query over baseline, with a 95% CI of [1.076, 2.424]. The three seeds are consistently high at 2.06, 1.90, and 1.85. A magnitude-pruned build matched to the same perplexity gives -0.146 with CI [-0.375, +0.035], effectively near zero. On Qwen3-8B, an SVD build at 1.17× perplexity yields +0.208 [0.042, 0.375], while the damage-matched prune arm is exactly 0.000 [0, 0]. On Llama-3.1-8B, at 1.29× perplexity, SVD gives +1.285 [0.743, 1.882] whereas pruning gives only +0.069 [0.021, 0.125], an 18× gap.

A particularly strong dissociation appears within a single Qwen3-8B arm. The probe-allocated b98 SVD build wins a paired output-fidelity test and still fails the canary with +0.643 invented steps [0.171, 1.229]. So fidelity-up and behavior-broken can hold on the same tensors.

The mechanism section adds an important control. Moderate quantization can carry more raw perplexity damage than the failing SVD dose and still pass the canary, but when quantization is pushed to a similar error rate, around 1.05×, it also fails, with pooled clean-slice Δ of +0.10 [0.029, 0.20]. The authors therefore argue that the trigger is coherence times rate rather than perplexity damage alone. They also note that onset is early, within roughly a 2% perplexity dose, and appears distributed rather than localized.1c:T47c,For a skim, start with the blind-spot and dissociation section. For anyone working on compression or deployment, the main takeaway is not a single detector but a stricter acceptance logic: passing ppl, MMLU, and fidelity does not certify safe agentic execution.1d:T5c5,The method has three layers. First, the paper analyzes non-orthogonality from both tensor approximation and dot-product error perspectives. The key point is that the error of low-rank approximation composed with quantization is not just the sum of two independent errors; cross terms appear, so the composition cannot be treated as harmless stacking. Second, it studies ordering and derives that low-rank decomposition should be applied before quantization. The intuition is sensible: if quantization is applied first, the subsequent low-rank fit is performed on an already distorted matrix, so the principal subspace estimate itself is biased by quantization noise. If low-rank decomposition comes first, the dominant structure is extracted before discretization, and the later quantization acts on a more compact and regularized representation. Third, the paper introduces DAM, a Diagonal Adhesive Method, motivated by activation outliers. After low-rank decomposition, representation statistics can shift so that a few large-magnitude channels dominate block-wise quantization scales, reducing effective resolution for the majority of values. DAM keeps a diagonal-form corrective component to preserve scale information that is poorly captured by the joint low-rank-plus-low-bit approximation. First, AIR inherits the activation-aware profiling step from SVD-LLM(W). Hidden states X are collected on a calibration set, and a profiling matrix S is used to transform the weight matrix into a latent space, so the activation reconstruction objective becomes a low-rank approximation problem on the profiled weight W'. This preserves the computational convenience of SVD while making the objective more aligned with actual layer inputs.

Second, AIR constructs an element-wise influence matrix I from backward information. The framework is agnostic to the exact signal: Weight×Gradient, diagonal Fisher, and LRP-style relevance are all allowed. The default choice is AttnLRP, motivated by gradient-free propagation rules and better robustness around near-zero activations. Per-sample relevance maps are accumulated in absolute value over the calibration set and normalized to unit mean per layer. One of the paper’s useful findings is that, once AIR’s integration mechanism is used, the exact choice among reasonable backward signals matters much less than how the signal is incorporated.

Third, AIR defines a hybrid activation-and-influence-aware objective. Each element of the profiled residual is weighted by a factor of the form sqrt(1 + delta * I_ij). The additive all-ones anchor is important: low-influence entries are not discounted away; high-influence entries simply become more expensive to approximate poorly. This leads to an element-wise weighted low-rank approximation problem, which in general has no standard SVD closed-form solution. AIR therefore starts from the activation-aware optimum SVD(W', k), then performs a single rank-wise alternating least squares sweep. Each rank-1 component is updated in closed form, and the updates are ordered from the tail components toward the leading ones to avoid disturbing the dominant singular directions too much. The paper also provides a monotone-descent guarantee for this sweep.
