a:T485,The framework, Multi-Turn Certified Robustness (MTCR), models conversation as a State-Adversarial MDP. Dialogue history is mapped to a continuous state; at each turn an adversary chooses a user message from an epsilon-bounded perturbation set; the LLM responds according to its policy; and the state is updated. Safety is defined by a predicate over the state space. The central quantity is k-turn certified robustness: the worst-case probability of staying safe for k turns under any adaptive adversary. A naive certificate gives a lower bound of p^k, where p is the worst-case single-turn certified safety probability. MTCR tightens this through embedding-space mode decomposition, separately certifying intra-mode safety and inter-mode transitions. It also introduces (alpha, beta)-safety persistence, a structural condition under which the degradation can improve from p^k to beta^k when beta is larger than the worst-case single-turn bound, with a linear characterization 1 - k(1 - beta) for interpretable horizon estimates.
