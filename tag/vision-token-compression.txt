The central idea is to avoid writing all vision tokens into the downstream KV cache as-is; instead, it aggregates and compresses them during prefill under prompt guidance. The method has three steps. (1) Vision tokens are treated as graph nodes, and edges are defined by a feature divergence metric that estimates redundancy.
