9:T411,SpiralFovea is a parameter-free input-adaptive tokenizer. It converts the image to luminance, downsamples and quantizes it, then computes local Shannon entropy with a sliding window. The image is split into horizontal strips; within each active strip, the highest-entropy location is selected as an anchor, followed by radius-based deduplication to avoid collapsing all anchors onto one hotspot. Around each retained anchor, the method extracts multi-scale spiral rings: a central foveal patch plus three outer rings with increasing radius and patch size. For 224-pixel images, the schedule uses [(24,0),(28,18),(36,22),(48,26)], radii [0,26,58,96], and up to 29 patches per anchor. Out-of-bounds patches are filtered, leading to at most about 78 retained patches for most samples.
