19:T4bd,The authors model the space of decision-relevant situations as a topological space X, and a collection of contexts as an open cover {U_i}. Evidence on how a quantity contrasts between two contexts is represented as a real-valued 1-cochain ω on the nerve of the cover: an edge stores the contrast estimated on the overlapping population shared by two contexts, a triangle records the 3-way consistency condition. An agent transporting a claim across a chain of contexts is then performing path integration of this cochain. The core theorem states that conclusions are path-independent if and only if ω is exact (i.e., ω = δθ for some global assignment θ on vertices), and that disagreement between different paths is exactly the holonomy of the first Čech cohomology class [ω] ∈ H¹. Hodge decomposition splits evidence conflict into three orthogonal pieces: coboundary (fixable by recalibration), coexact (requires re-measurement), and harmonic (invisible to every local check; only remediable by refining the cover itself).
