9:T437,The framework has three stages. First, a Hybrid Disentangled VQ-VAE learns discrete action codes from adjacent egocentric video frames. A frozen DINOv2 encoder extracts spatial features, and a spatiotemporal Transformer models short-term frame transitions. The encoder uses two sets of learnable queries, one for action and one for background. These are quantized by two separate VQ bottlenecks, producing an action codebook and a background codebook. A mask-guided decoder encourages the action branch to focus on physically relevant motion while pushing camera motion and background changes into the background branch. Second, the learned action codes supervise VLM pretraining: the model maps observations and language instructions to high-level action intent. Third, during robot adaptation, the pretrained intent representation is combined with DINOv2 visual feedback and a flow-matching action head to produce continuous robot controls.
