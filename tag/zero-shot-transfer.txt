19:T59e,The paper proposes EmergentBridge, a lightweight embedding-level bridging framework that can be applied on top of a pretrained unified embedding model. Instead of aligning a new modality Mb only to an anchor embedding C, EmergentBridge synthesizes a proxy embedding for an already-aligned modality Ma from the anchor embedding (i.e., a predicted/constructed target \hat{x}^a derived from c). Training then aligns Mb jointly to (1) the anchor c and (2) the synthesized proxy \hat{x}^a, thereby strengthening Mb↔Ma relations without requiring paired (Mb,Ma) data.

The method is motivated via an InfoNCE analysis: the loss is decomposed into a positive alignment term and a negative log-partition term, and the gradient w.r.t. the modality embedding provides a geometric notion of the “anchor-alignment direction” (for cosine similarity with L2-normalized embeddings, it aligns with the anchor direction up to scaling/projection). Since proxy signals can be noisy and may interfere with anchor alignment, the paper introduces an orthogonal-subspace alignment regularizer: it constrains the additional proxy-driven alignment to lie in the subspace orthogonal to the anchor-alignment direction, using remaining degrees of freedom to absorb proxy constraints while preserving anchor performance.
