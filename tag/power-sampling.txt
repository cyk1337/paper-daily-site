19:T499,The paradox is traced to two distinct mismatches. First, coverage mismatch: Power Sampling applies exponentiation per-trajectory, which amplifies the weight of a single high-probability wrong trajectory while suppressing the collective support of multiple moderate-probability trajectories leading to the same correct answer, flipping the final majority decision. Second, dose mismatch: a fixed sharpening exponent α induces highly variable distribution shifts across different prompts, ranging from mild adjustment to near-complete distribution collapse on hard problems. The proposed Relative-Rank SoftSat fix addresses both issues: it replaces raw likelihood with per-prompt relative rank normalized to [0,1] to equalize sharpening strength across problems, and uses a saturating weighting function that boosts moderately ranked trajectories while capping gains for top-ranked trajectories, preventing a small number of dominant paths from absorbing excessive probability mass. 具体流程可以概括为：维护一个有界粒子群，每个粒子是一条部分生成的前缀；按 block 而不是单 token 推进若干步；对候选 block 做 proposal-corrected weighting；然后在边界处做 weighted selection / resampling。这里的权重不只看当前 block 的局部概率，还要乘上一个对未来可达 power-weighted mass 的估计，也就是 z_t 或其近似。作者把它称为 future-value selection potential。

从理论直觉上看，这相当于把“这个前缀现在看起来还行吗”改成“这个前缀后面还有没有希望接出高权重完整解”。这样可以避免两类有限预算下常见错误：一类是过早淘汰局部概率略低、但后续空间更好的前缀；另一类是过度保留局部很尖锐、但其实通向死路的前缀。

论文给了三种层次的实现。最基础的 APPS 只用粒子群和边界重采样，已经能体现 selection law。更强的版本在边界处用 rollout 暂时显化未来，估计下游质量。再进一步，作者把 rollout 信号压缩成一个轻量 boundary-level head，用便宜预测替代反复前瞻。这个 head 的角色很像 critic，但不是 RL 里那种基于外部奖励训练的 value model，而是服务于 power distribution 近似的 future-mass predictor。

作者还强调 APPS 与其他方法是互补而不是替代关系。和显式 rollout 的 scalable power sampling 相比，APPS 把主要创新点放在 selection-time guidance；和更强调 prefix-optimal proposal 的 Power SMC 相比，APPS 保持 proposal 简单稳定，把复杂度留给边界选择。这种分工很合理，因为在长上下文和紧 batch 条件下，频繁做大规模 rollout 或复杂 proposal 都会很贵，而 selection 是粒子法里最自然的全局控制点。1b:T985,The method, APPS, reframes finite-budget power sampling through the lens of Sequential Monte Carlo and places the main control mechanism at selection time rather than proposal time. That design choice is the key idea. Many particle methods focus on improving the proposal so each particle moves in a better direction. APPS argues that under a fixed compute budget, the more important decision is which prefixes deserve to keep receiving probability mass and compute.

Operationally, APPS maintains a bounded population of partial trajectories. These particles are advanced in token blocks rather than one token at a time. Candidate blocks receive proposal-corrected weights, and then a weighted selection or resampling step is performed at block boundaries. The crucial point is that the boundary weight is not based only on local block likelihood. It also includes an estimate of the future power-weighted mass reachable after committing to that block, namely z_t or an approximation to it. The paper calls this a future-value selection potential.

This changes the decision rule from “does this prefix look good locally?” to “does this prefix still have promising futures under the sharpened sequence-level target?” That helps avoid two common finite-budget errors: pruning prefixes that look slightly weaker locally but retain better downstream options, and overcommitting to prefixes that are locally sharp but globally dead-ended.

The paper presents this idea in layers. A minimal APPS already uses particle evolution plus boundary resampling to instantiate the selection law. A stronger version estimates downstream promise with explicit rollouts at boundaries. A further step compresses that rollout signal into a lightweight boundary-level head, so the algorithm can carry future information without repeatedly simulating the future. This head behaves like a critic in spirit, but it is not a standard RL value model trained on external rewards. It is a predictor of future reachable mass under the power target.

The paper also positions APPS as complementary to prior work. Compared with rollout-heavy scalable power sampling, APPS emphasizes selection-time guidance. Compared with proposal-centric Power SMC, it keeps the proposal simple and stable while making selection more informed.
