9:T468,The setup modifies DeepRD into a controlled next-step proof prediction task. Premises are written in natural language over entities and attributes, and the model must select or generate the next inference step that can lead to the goal. Lookahead is defined as the number of future proof steps the model must search through to identify the correct immediate step. Training supervises only the single next-step output, so the measured capability is internal computation during one forward pass rather than explicit CoT expansion. The curriculum jointly increases lookahead and the attribute vocabulary. This joint curriculum works better than increasing lookahead alone, likely because it prevents the task from remaining too templatic and makes the difficulty ramp smoother. The paper compares curriculum training with direct training at target depth, varies curriculum step size, tests several model sizes, and contrasts pretrained and randomly initialized models.
