19:T512,The framework is built on three core components: First, theoretical grounding: The order selection problem is formalized as minimizing the KL divergence between the data distribution and the distribution induced by the decoding procedure. A tractable upper bound on this intractable objective is derived, expressed in terms of the frozen denoiser's pathwise log-likelihood under teacher forcing. This bound yields a dense, model-self-aware reward signal that assigns credit to every unmasking decision: an optimal order selects the next token that maximizes the expected future log-likelihood of the remaining trajectory, rather than the token with highest local certainty. Second, Self-Aware Scheduling (SAS): A lightweight order policy is trained with Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) while keeping the base denoiser frozen. At each decoding step, the policy outputs a distribution over currently masked positions to select the next token to unmask. The policy is plug-and-play, and works seamlessly for both token-level any-order decoding and blockwise semi-autoregressive decoding.
