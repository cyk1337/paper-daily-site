A linear readout s(x)=ψ(F(x))+b determines the decision sign. For a matched pair (xA,xB), the paper proves three main results. First, deleting a subset of carriers collapses both inputs to the same unconditional output if and only if the removed mass is symmetric on the pair and no contrast remains outside the deleted subset. The induced error is deterministic, and all subsets satisfying the condition produce the same collapse. Second, patching carrier i shifts the readout by βiδi, with βi=ψ(vi) and δi=αi(xA)-αi(xB), whereas ablating carrier i shifts it by -βiαi(xB). These quantities do not bound each other. The paper constructs cases where every single-carrier patch flips the decision but no single-carrier ablation does. Third, for the non-independent case of an attention head and its own layer’s MLP separated by RMSNorm, the paper derives an explicit interaction error with a second-order remainder bound.
