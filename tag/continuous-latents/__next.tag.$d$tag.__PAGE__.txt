5:T456,TORM uses a two-stage training procedure. Each training instance contains sampled keyframes from the source video, a generated thought video built from a teacher reasoning plan, and the target answer sequence. In Stage I, a small number of latent tokens are inserted between the video-question context and the final answer. The model is trained with answer-token loss and a latent-state alignment objective that makes the latent hidden states encode features of the thought video. The latent segment is therefore grounded in question-relevant motion and state changes without requiring textual CoT supervision. In Stage II, the latent slots remain in the sequence, but supervision is applied only to the final answer, similar in spirit to Coconut-style latent reasoning. This encourages the model to consolidate the aligned latent behavior into an answer-oriented internal reasoning process. At inference time, the model does not generate thought videos, revisit frames, or call external visual tools.
