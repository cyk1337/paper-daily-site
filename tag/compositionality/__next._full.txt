Each source symbol maps to one or more globally unique multi-character right-hand-side strings. Global uniqueness makes primitive contraction unambiguous: any substring matching a right-hand side maps back to exactly one source symbol. The environment supports two inverse primitive operations. Expansion replaces a symbol with one of its right-hand sides. Contraction replaces a matching substring with its source symbol. Each step is serialized with the relevant position, span, and rule, which makes trajectory-level auditing possible.

A Transformer is pretrained from scratch on primitive rewrite chains. The generator starts from an initial word, repeatedly samples valid primitive expansions or contractions, appends the serialized action, and uses the resulting word as the next input. Pretraining exposes the model to local grammar-consistent dynamics, but not to the non-primitive shortcuts analyzed later. A contraction weight ρ controls the pretraining distribution; the default is ρ=4. Post-training uses a goal-directed contraction task. A prompt is produced by expanding a target symbol c* into a longer word w_prompt.
