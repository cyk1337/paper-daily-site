The reconstruction objective is not plain L2 error; it is a Hessian-weighted, loss-aware reconstruction error inspired by second-order PTQ. For a small set of candidate clipping ranges, it fixes the discrete code assignment, solves the dequantization parameters in closed form by saliency-weighted least squares, and chooses the candidate with the smallest loss-aware reconstruction error.
