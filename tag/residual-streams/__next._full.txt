18:T461,Hyper-Connections maintain n parallel residual streams. A sublayer reads them through a pre-map, applies attention or an FFN, writes the result back through a post-map, and transports the existing residual state through a learned matrix H_res. Standard HC leaves H_res unconstrained, so its singular values can cause exploding or vanishing transport. mHC projects H_res onto the Birkhoff polytope of doubly stochastic matrices, usually with Sinkhorn-Knopp iterations. This caps the largest gain at one, but does not lower-bound the smallest gain. The paper decomposes stream states into a mean component and inter-stream difference components, showing that doubly stochastic transport preserves the mean while shrinking differences. Identity HC avoids this shrinkage but removes cross-stream mixing. oHC instead constrains H_res to SO(n), the group of rotations. This keeps all singular values equal to one, so the transport is norm-preserving in every direction while still mixing streams.
