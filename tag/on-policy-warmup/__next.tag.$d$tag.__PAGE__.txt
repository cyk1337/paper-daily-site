9:T4cb,The proposed procedure is On-Policy Warmup. During warmup, the student interacts with the environment using its own policy. A fixed teacher then provides supervision on the states and histories actually visited by the student, including states reached after bad actions. The student is trained by on-policy distillation. After warmup, teacher queries stop and training switches to RLVR with verifiable outcome rewards. The theoretical analysis treats token or action generation as a finite-horizon trajectory distribution. Under shared environment dynamics, the cumulative reverse-KL distillation loss on student-visited states equals the KL divergence between the student and teacher trajectory distributions. Data processing then bounds the gap between their binary-verifier success probabilities. A competent teacher plus sufficiently small on-policy distillation loss gives a positive lower bound on the student’s initial success probability and an upper bound on the expected number of rollouts needed to discover a success.
