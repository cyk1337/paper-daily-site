18:Ta01,技术上最重要的是实验控制做得很严。作者使用参数化 SCM 生成世界：5 到 12 个节点，图结构在 Erdős–Rényi 与 scale-free 间随机，机制既有线性也有非线性，并用 confounding strength ρ 控制潜在混杂。测试域固定在高混杂 ρ=0.9，并筛出观测相关与因果效应符号相反的三元组，形成标准 Simpson trap。训练数据在线生成，避免固定语料记忆污染。每个样本由 4 条 evidence record 加 1 个 query 组成，evidence type 被系统拆成 obs、int、probe、obs_probe、none。这里 probe 很关键：它不是一般 intervention，而是直接对查询变量 X 做 do(X=v)，并观察所有变量响应，因此最接近回答目标查询所需的局部因果证据。

作者先做 α-mixture 扫描：训练时每个样本以概率 α 来自 interventional distribution，α 从 0 到 1 分六档。结果发现 corrected sign accuracy 没有随 α 单调上升，但 Simpson-slope 的幅度单调增大。于是作者提出 magnitude-direction duality：模型从干预数据里学到了响应强度，却没有学会在冲突证据下翻转方向。

接着，全文真正的主结果来自 evidence-type factor 分析。作者固定训练配方，尤其固定在 α=1.0 的纯干预训练，再在推理时只改变上下文证据类型，比较 pure obs、mixed、pure probes 等条件。结果显示，是否能恢复正确因果方向，主要由上下文里放的是什么证据决定，而且呈现剂量效应：观测证据越多，因果方向越被压制。随后用一组零训练成本的 inference-time intervention 去定位机制：增加 probe 数量不能解除压制；删掉观测记录则立刻恢复正确插值；只保留观测格式 token 但移除观测内容时，压制也会释放。这说明抑制不是训练阶段把能力学坏了，而是推理时被上下文内容门控掉了。作者把这种现象描述为“插值电路一直在参数里，但被上下文关掉”。

另外两个技术点也值得注意。其一，作者专门分析了 probe-based causal evaluation 的噪声地板：即便是完美插值器，单次采样也会有 26% 的符号错误，因此必须做 evidence averaging，否则容易把评测噪声误判成模型缺陷。其二，作者做了 0.93B 规模的 rate-level persistence 检查，以及 CLadder 审计与 sign randomization 分析，发现模型还学到一个正效应先验，而且这个先验有两层结构。这些补充实验让结论不只停留在小模型玩具现象。19:T433,For pretraining research, the contribution is less about a new model and more about correcting a data-mixture intuition. The paper shows that, at least in this controlled regime, the amount of interventional data in pretraining is not the operative variable for whether the model uses interventional evidence correctly. That matters for anyone thinking about causal pretraining, synthetic data curricula, or “higher-quality evidence” mixtures. A second contribution is conceptual: the bottleneck is not only whether causal evidence exists in training, but also whether the inference context activates or suppresses its use. This shifts attention toward context formatting, evidence composition, and conditional retrieval of learned circuits. Third, the evaluation methodology is unusually clean: online data generation, strict train/val/test world separation, computable causal ground truth, and an explicit analysis of the noise floor in probe-based evaluation. It is a negative result, but one that meaningfully constrains how to think about causal data in pretraining.1a:Ta0c,The technical setup is carefully controlled. Worlds are generated from parametric structural causal models with 5–12 nodes, either Erdős–Rényi or scale-free topology, mixed linear/nonlinear mechanisms, and a confounding-strength parameter rho controlling latent confounding paths. The evaluation domain fixes strong confounding at rho=0.9 and selects triples where observational correlation and causal effect have opposite signs, creating a clean Simpson trap. Training data are generated online, so there is no fixed-corpus memorization issue.

Each sample contains four evidence records plus one query. Evidence types are systematically varied: observational records, generic interventions, direct probes on the queried variable, mixed observation-plus-probe contexts, and no-context cases. The distinction between generic intervention and probe is important. Probe records intervene directly on X, the queried cause, and expose downstream responses, so they are the most task-aligned evidence for answering do(X=x)->Y.

The first analysis sweeps the intervention mixture ratio alpha during training. This falsifies the simple hypothesis that more intervention in pretraining should monotonically improve causal direction. Directional accuracy does not improve monotonically, but the estimated Simpson slope grows in magnitude. The authors interpret this as a magnitude–direction duality.

The stronger result comes from isolating the evidence type present at inference time. With training held fixed, including the alpha=1.0 condition, they vary only the context evidence type and show a contextual dose gradient: more observational evidence in context suppresses correct causal direction more strongly. A set of zero-training-cost inference-time interventions then localizes the mechanism. Increasing probe dose does not rescue the model. Removing observational records immediately restores the latent interpolation ability. Keeping observational-format tokens while removing observational content also releases suppression. This argues that the relevant switch is content-mediated and acts at inference time, not that the model failed to store the causal interpolation circuit during training.

A final methodological contribution is the analysis of probe-evaluation noise. Even a perfect interpolator incurs substantial single-shot sign error, so evidence averaging is necessary. The paper also includes larger-scale persistence checks at 0.93B and CLadder-style audits showing a learned positive-effect prior with layered structure under sign randomization.1b:T761,The main experiments come in five parts. First is the alpha sweep. Using a 25.7M GPT-style decoder with 8 layers, vocab size 41, block size 640, trained for 20k steps with batch size 64 and learning rate 6e-4, the authors vary the intervention fraction alpha across six levels from 0 to 1. Corrected sign accuracy is roughly 0.5/0.3/0.2/0.4/0.4/0.4, showing no monotonic gain from more intervention. In contrast, the Simpson-slope magnitude rises monotonically from 0.169 to 0.384.

Second is the 50-world evidence-type comparison. With training fixed, changing only the inference context shifts mean ratio_true from -0.288 in pure observational context to -0.092 in mixed context and +0.418 in pure probe context. Paired McNemar tests are highly significant, with p-values down to 1.1e-9.

Third is the inference-time mechanism test E1. Doubling the probe dose does not remove suppression. Erasing observational evidence raises ratio_true from about -0.09 to +0.56, close to the probe-only condition. This is strong evidence that the capability exists in the weights but is gated off by context.

Fourth is E5, which studies dose titration and content manipulation. One observational record barely suppresses, two dilute the causal signal, and four effectively capture the directional judgment. If observational-format tokens remain but observational content is removed, suppression disappears. The effect is therefore driven by content, not superficial formatting.

Fifth are robustness checks. The suppression pattern persists at 0.93B scale. The train/validation/test split is done over disjoint world domains, with additional novel-topology evaluation. CLadder audits reveal a learned positive-effect prior and suggest a two-layer structure under sign randomization. The paper is not a large-scale LLM systems study, but the experimental logic is strong and unusually well controlled.1c:T4f2,The main contribution is the Causal-Generative Dual-Judge (CGDJ), a benchmark and evaluation framework with two complementary modules. The first is Explicit Causal Perception, which tests whether a model can identify and articulate causal structure from visual evidence.
