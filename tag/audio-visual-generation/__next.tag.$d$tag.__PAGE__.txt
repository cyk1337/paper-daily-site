9:T4a7,JAVIS builds on Fun-Audio-Chat. The shared LLM runs at 5 Hz, while the speech refined head runs at 25 Hz. Instead of animating a face after waveform synthesis, the authors adapt the speech refined head with LoRA and turn it into a Joint Refined Head. Each 5 Hz LLM output is expanded into five conditioning vectors. The JRH autoregressively processes these vectors together with the embedding of the previous speech token, producing five hidden states for the current chunk. These states form the unified representation H_t. Two streaming branches consume H_t: the speech branch uses a frozen CosyVoice 3 speech decoder and vocoder to synthesize audio, while the motion branch uses an autoregressive Motion Decoder to generate continuous facial-motion chunks, which are rendered with a reference portrait. During training, the shared LLM is frozen, while the LoRA-adapted JRH and Motion Decoder are trained with both speech cross-entropy loss and motion loss. Compared with X-Streamer and Ex-Omni-2D, JAVIS avoids iterative generation of high-dimensional video latents and instead predicts compact motion controls before rendering, which is better aligned with streaming latency constraints.a:T521,From the available full-text excerpt (intro only), the approach is organized around three coupled issues and corresponding design choices:
1) Unified formulation: treat generation, reference-based synthesis, temporal extension, and editing—now extended to joint audio-video—as one conditional denoising family with varying condition/target configurations.
2) Explicit token roles: instead of implicit concatenation, attach role information to tokens so the model is told which segments are reconstruction targets, which are to be preserved, and which are guidance-only references.
3) Conditioning duality: implement both a semantic pathway (instruction following, reasoning over text + visual evidence) and a latent pathway (high-fidelity preservation of identity/texture/layout/motion). In audio-video, this must also handle modality absence and asymmetric roles per modality.
4) Data consistency & balance: emphasize truly aligned audio-video pairs and a task mixture that does not let data-rich objectives dominate.
