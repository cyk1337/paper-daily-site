1a:T479,Multi-token Residual Prediction (MRP) leverages the empirical fact that the logit change between consecutive denoising steps is small when only a few tokens are unmasked. Define the inter-step residual δ_t = ℓ_{t-1} − ℓ_t. Since δ_t has much smaller magnitude than ℓ_t, predicting δ_t is an easier target.
At inference, one backbone forward produces ℓ_t and hidden states h_t; a lightweight MRP head predicts a correction term from h_t so that ℓ_{t-1} can be approximated as ℓ_t + MRP(h_t) without a second backbone pass. This enables decoding additional tokens “as if” the model had advanced one more denoising step.
Two modes:
- Direct decoding: use corrected logits directly for faster multi-token reveals, accepting some approximation error.
- Speculative decoding: treat MRP as a drafter and verify with the backbone on the next step; accepted tokens are intended to be lossless, shifting compute from expensive backbone passes to a cheap proposal mechanism.
