17:T426,A minimal Turing-complete tape automaton F (Brainfuck family, five instructions) is fixed, and all ~1.5 billion programs of length L≤13 are exhaustively enumerated under a bounded universal (Levin–Solomonoff) prior. Because enumeration is exact, the map from program behavior to prior mass is a sufficient statistic, and the posterior predictive for any few-shot context is computed in closed form. To remove output-polarity bias, F is complement-symmetrized into sF; each task is paired with its bitwise complement twin, on which the optimum scores identically, so the original-twin gap isolates the model's own inductive bias. Ground-truth certification uses GPU-accelerated enumeration, an independent pure-Python reference, and exact cross-checks (byte-for-byte agreement of per-output program counts, witness re-execution, bit-identical posterior agreement). Models are scored with a bounded symmetric divergence against the optimal posterior, summarized by a difficulty-aware score and facet profile across induction, continuation, and abduction tasks.18:T582,Working in the category of measurable spaces with Markov kernels as morphisms, the paper builds a four-layer abstraction hierarchy and proves results level by level: (1) Core Bayesian transformer: defines only prior, likelihood, and posterior kernel, and establishes the joint-distribution identity via the disintegration theorem for finite kernels. (2) Semantic transformer: introduces an explicit internal update kernel and proposes the Bayes joint-semantics condition—composition of the update kernel with prior and likelihood recovers the symmetrized joint law—and proves that this condition implies the update kernel equals the posterior almost everywhere. (3) Full transformer block: formalizes the dataflow of input embedding, QKV projection, attention, residual/MLP, and readout as kernel composition, derives an explicit block-level Bayes formula through Radon-Nikodym differentiation, and proves its normalization. (4) Multilayer stack: defines the layer stack as ordered composition of hidden-state kernels and extends the proof to deep networks. The paper additionally proves that softmax attention defines a valid probability distribution, bridging the abstract kernel framework to standard attention implementations. All proofs depend only on kernel composition structure and the joint-semantics condition; no assumptions on depth, width, parameterization, or training procedure are
