The randomness is not i.i.d. noise; it is adapted along the trajectory using a tracked maximum of historical gradients, so that in expectation the method behaves like a preconditioned SGD step while still emitting ±1 per coordinate (preserving 1-bit communication and numerical robustness).

The theory is presented in two regimes:
- Online/convex optimization: the stochastic sign construction fixes the known non-convergence of SignSGD on non-smooth convex problems and achieves a sharp rate matching the lower bound.
- Non-convex optimization: the paper introduces refined stationarity measures that work under arbitrary norms (to better match problem geometry) and derives a sharp complexity bound claimed to avoid explicit dependence on dimension d, improving prior results by a factor on the order of d√d.
