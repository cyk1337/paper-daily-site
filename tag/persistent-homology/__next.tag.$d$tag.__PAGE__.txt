a:T40e,The method treats the contextual hidden states of prompt tokens at each transformer layer as a point cloud. It then computes finite zero-dimensional persistent homology, H0, for each layer. H0 tracks how disconnected components merge as the distance threshold grows, giving a compact view of the connectivity structure among token representations. Each layer is summarized by three descriptors: mean finite lifetime, normalized lifetime entropy, and the concentration ratio of the largest lifetimes. Concatenating these descriptors across layers gives a low-dimensional topology vector for the question. This vector is used for detection and also for steering. For steering, the method retrieves topologically similar positive and negative examples for the current query, constructs a local contrast direction in activation space, and applies the intervention during prefill or decoding.
