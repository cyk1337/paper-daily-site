9:T426,The framework has two parts: a data pipeline and a multimodal browsing agent trained on its output. Starting from a textual knowledge graph, UniBrowse builds connected evidence subgraphs around seed entities through multi-hop expansion. These graph-derived skeletons provide controllable reasoning paths. The pipeline then augments them with live web retrieval, adding open-web evidence and making the generated tasks closer to real browsing. It explicitly generates three information-flow patterns: text-only composition over textual evidence, image-to-text resolution from an initial visual clue into textual browsing, and text-to-image workflows where textual evidence guides later visual retrieval or verification. For data selection, the paper introduces exploration degree, a metric intended to capture the diversity of successful reasoning trajectories. Low-exploration instances are filtered out so that RL focuses on tasks with richer long-horizon decision signals.
