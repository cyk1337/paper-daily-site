A practical skim should take around 30 to 45 minutes.19:T4fd,3D-MoE combines (1) complementarity-guided offline placement, (2) a hardware fast path for residual hotspots, and (3) tiered online remapping.
1) Offline profiling + optimization: collect per-step expert load traces on representative mixed workloads, filter transient step noise, and infer persistent complementarity relations. Then perform a two-stage optimization: group experts whose loads offset over time, and map groups onto nearby NMP cores/tiles to keep weights local while stabilizing group-level load.
2) CMN (Collaborative Memory-Network): complementarity mostly helps at the group level; within-group transient hotspots remain. Since hot expert weights are pinned to a host core, naive helping would cause repeated remote weight fetches. CMN adds a lightweight memory-controller/NoC mechanism: the host loads a hot expert’s weight tile once from local DRAM and streams/broadcasts it to helper cores via a temporary broadcast path created by lightweight NoC reconfiguration (“load once, broadcast to many”), reducing redundant DRAM reads and packet overhead.
3) Tiered online correction: to handle workload drift without frequent global re-optimization, apply frequent low-cost local adjustments and reserve higher-cost reconfigurations for longer-term shifts.1a:T5aa,The proposed method is NR-SAE, a native-router sparse autoencoder. Each routed expert owns a small dictionary. When the frozen MoE router selects an expert for a token, the corresponding dictionary is opened; dictionaries for unselected experts are not scored. This differs from global SAEs over the summed MoE output and from approaches that learn a separate auxiliary router for the SAE.

The paper tests the factorization at two seams. In the reconstructive seam, the SAE receives a selected expert’s raw output yt,s and reconstructs it. In the predictive seam, a transcoder receives the common MoE input xt and predicts the selected expert’s output. The first setting tests whether expert outputs admit expert-local dictionaries; the second tests whether these dictionaries can capture the expert’s computation from its input.
