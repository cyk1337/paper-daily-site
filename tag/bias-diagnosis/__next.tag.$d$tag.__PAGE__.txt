1) Judge Datasheet protocol:
- A0 true-vacuum: empty/whitespace/identical-answer pairs to measure dark current (non-tie rate when tie is allowed).
- A1 controlled quality ladder: a constructively designed stimulus ladder (prefix-chain checklist with Pareto dominance, Δ0 controls, fillers, validity gates) to measure target sensitivity across increasing ΔQ.
- Criterion-shift probing: vary tie instructions (e.g., strict tie) to observe how the operating point moves—often reducing Δ0 false preferences while absorbing marginal ΔQ signals into ties.
2) Direction-stability decomposition (core technical novelty): raw same-quality non-tie rate (raw Δ0 FP) is decomposed into interpretable components:
- Stable cross-sensitivity (content-stable preference under order reversal).
- Positional false preference (slot-stable choice under order reversal → position bias).
- One-sided commit (only one order yields a non-tie → instability/near-threshold behavior).
- Residual conflict/other terms.
