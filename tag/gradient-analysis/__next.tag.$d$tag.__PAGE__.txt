9:T471,The method decomposes SFT into direction estimation and distance extrapolation. Let θ be the pre-SFT parameters and θI the ideal post-SFT parameters. Instead of localizing task-relevant neurons directly on θ, the method first performs a tiny update using 1% of the target training set for one epoch, obtaining θ′. This update is used to capture the initial task-induced optimization direction. The later shift Δθ = θI - θ is then treated as an accumulation of recursive updates starting from θ′, allowing the method to estimate which neurons or components will matter in the post-SFT model. The implementation follows the computational pattern of Attribution Patching: it collects activations and gradients with two forward passes and one backward pass, then scores neurons or components. Two pipelines are provided: fine-grained neuron-level estimation for precise parameter selection, and faster component-level estimation for larger models or tighter budgets.
