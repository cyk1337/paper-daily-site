Second, it shows that Transformers can implement these classes in context. The unifying view expresses next-token probabilities as linear functions of nonlinear features computed from past observations, which covers finite-window predictors, nonlinear feature predictors, and spectral-style methods. A small Transformer trained on a single HMM is used to validate that a finite-window-like predictor can emerge naturally. The attacks include GCG, AutoDAN, DeepInception, prefilling, and intent laundering. The key argument is the activation-cone blind spot: a gate based on alignment with a benign reference, cone, subspace, or null-space at a single layer can be fooled by activations that look benign under that geometry. The proposed fix is response-time probing. Instead of classifying only after the prompt, the system waits for the first N generated tokens, mean-pools their hidden states, and applies a linear probe to detect whether generation has entered an unsafe trajectory. 全文最有信息量的技术点是“collapse”概念。作者发现某些架构配置在训练早期其实形成过可观测信号，但随着训练推进，这个信号被抹掉；而另一些配置则能保留或恢复它。这说明 observability 不是静态结构属性，而是架构与优化轨迹共同作用的结果。也因此，论文把 architecture determines observability 说得比较克制：在同一 recipe 内，配置是决定因素；跨 recipe 时，配置与训练动态共同决定塌缩点。d:T780,The core methodological move is residualization. For each token, the authors compute cross-entropy loss, max softmax probability, and the L2 norm of the residual stream at a given layer. They fit an OLS model predicting token loss from max-softmax and activation norm, take the residual, and binarize it to define the probe target. Linear probes are then trained on frozen hidden states from each layer. Observability is measured by partial Spearman correlation, rho_partial, between probe predictions and token loss while controlling for max-softmax and activation norm. A stricter metric, r_OC, additionally controls for a trained output-side predictor based on the final-layer representation.

This matters because the paper is not satisfied with showing that hidden states contain information. It asks whether they contain information that is not recoverable from standard output-side confidence signals. The authors are careful about scope: observability here is linear readability under a specific protocol, not a claim about reasoning correctness, semantic truth, or what the model itself uses internally.

The experimental structure has three layers. First, a measurement layer: show that probe scores are substantially inflated if confidence controls are omitted. Second, a controlled architectural layer using the Pythia suite: under a shared training recipe, vary configuration and test whether observability systematically collapses. Third, an observational cross-family layer: compare GPT-2, Pythia, Qwen, Llama, Mistral, and others to show that scale alone does not explain the effect.

The most interesting technical idea in the paper is observability collapse. Some configurations form the signal early in training but later erase it, while others preserve or recover it. That makes observability a joint product of architecture and optimization trajectory rather than a static representational property.e:T7a7,The first important quantitative result is methodological. Across 13 models from 6 families, confidence controls absorb 57.7% of the raw probe signal on average. On GPT-2 124M, the raw Spearman correlation between a linear probe and token loss is 0.549, but only 0.282 remains after controlling for max-softmax and activation norm. So without residualization, one would overstate output-independent observability by roughly a factor of two.

The strongest evidence comes from the controlled Pythia experiments. Under the same training recipe, every tested run with the (24 layers, 16 heads) configuration collapses to rho_partial around 0.10, despite a 3.5x variation in parameter count and two Pile variants. By contrast, six other configurations land between 0.21 and 0.38. The output-controlled residual r_OC also vanishes at the same collapse points, which supports the interpretation that what disappears is the output-independent hidden-state signal itself, not just a fragile probe metric.

Checkpoint dynamics are especially informative. Both the collapsing and healthy configurations show the signal at the earliest measured checkpoint, but continued training erases it in the 24L/16H class while the healthier configuration recovers or maintains it. This shifts the interpretation from “some architectures never form the signal” to “some architectures fail to preserve it under training.”

The cross-family evidence extends the pattern. At roughly 3B scale, Qwen 2.5 and Llama differ by 2.9x in observability with non-overlapping probe-seed distributions. Mistral 7B and Llama 3.1 8B also diverge despite broadly similar decoder-only shapes. The paper therefore argues that scale is not predictive; configuration is.
