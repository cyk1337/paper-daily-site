c:Ta98,方法设计刻意做得很“干净”。训练管线只有三步：从某个 source model 以 BOS token 为起点无条件采样，得到 plain text synthetic corpus；用这些文本继续微调一个 base student；然后在一组下游 benchmark 上评估变化。整个过程中不引入任务提示、拒绝采样、答案校验、reward、偏好模型或外部数据过滤，因此任何收益都只能来自 student 与 synthetic signal 的相互作用，而不是额外监督。

核心分析框架有三部分。第一部分是 compatibility study。作者系统比较了 self-generated、same-lineage、different-recipe、cross-family 等 source-student 配对。以 Qwen student 为例，self data 最有效；同家族但更大的 Qwen2.5-7B 往往优于训练 recipe 已变化的 Qwen3-8B；跨家族如 LLaMA-3.2-1B 常常更弱甚至有害。这里的关键不是 source 绝对能力，而是 tokenizer、训练分布、建模习惯、内部表示结构等共同形成的“谱系相容性”。论文没有把 compatibility 简化成单一可观测量，而是把它当作一个经验上可验证、但未被简单 proxy 捕获的关系属性。

第二部分是对“intrinsic data quality”解释的反证。作者检验了几类直觉上应该有效的 proxy：student 对 synthetic corpus 的 mean NLL、synthetic corpus 与 benchmark 的语义接近度、增大 synthetic support 的规模等。结果显示，这些指标和真实 downstream gain 的对应关系很弱，甚至会出现“相同 NLL、相反收益”或“benchmark similarity 不同、收益相近”的情况。这个结果很重要，因为它说明自训练收益不是简单的 distribution matching，也不是“更像测试集就更好”。

第三部分是 latent capability resurfacing 的机制证据。作者不是只看 benchmark 分数，而是同时看 extractability。若收益只是 rehearse 了 source 生成的具体序列，那么 utility 上升通常会伴随更高的 verbatim extraction 风险。作者在 Pythia 控制实验里观察到相反现象：在合适的自训练区间，held-out exact-match extractability 明显下降，而 benchmark utility 保持甚至上升。这支持一种更分布式的解释：训练更新强化了参数中已有但未充分表达的任务结构，而不是记住更多具体句子。

此外，全文还讨论了 temperature 的作用。温度不是越高越好或越低越好，而是在 fidelity 与 coverage 之间做权衡：低温更贴近 source 的高置信模式，高温带来更广覆盖但也更噪。只有在兼容配对里，这个 trade-off 才能转化为稳定收益；在不兼容配对里，增加多样性并不能补救谱系错配。d:T8ed,实验结论比摘要里更完整，重点有四组。第一组是 source-student 配对实验。Qwen student 在 self-training 下收益最好；same-lineage transfer 次之；different recipe 和 cross-family 明显更差。作者特别强调一个反直觉点：较小但同谱系的 source，可能比更大但训练 recipe 改过的 source 更有用。这说明“teacher 越强越好”在这里不成立。

第二组是模型家族差异。Qwen 系列在 reasoning、math、code 上能得到更广泛收益；作者测试的 LLaMA student 收益更窄，主要集中在理解型 benchmark，对 structured generation 的改善不稳定。这一点支持“自训练放大预训练已支持的能力边界”而不是“补齐模型短板”。如果预训练里没有足够强的结构化生成基础，单靠 BOS-only synthetic text 很难补出来。

第三组是 proxy 失效实验。作者把 downstream gain 与 mean NLL、benchmark similarity、synthetic support size 等做对照，发现这些量都不能稳定排序不同 synthetic corpora 的效用。尤其是 mean NLL 这个结果很关键，因为它否定了一个常见想法：只要 student 觉得数据“顺口”，继续训练就会有帮助。论文给出的证据是，同样容易被 student 拟合的数据，可能带来相反的迁移结果。

第四组是 utility-extractability frontier。Pythia 控制实验中，compatible self-training 把点推向“更高 utility、更低 verbatim extractability”的区域。摘要里只说了趋势，正文强调下降是明显的，而不是边际波动。这组结果是全文最有机制含义的一部分，因为它把“不是简单记忆”从口头判断变成了可测现象。

从全文可见，作者更重视现象的稳健排序和机制辨析，而不是追求单个 benchmark 上的大幅绝对增益。参数规模和数据规模在正文中用于构造不同 source-student 关系，例如 Qwen2.5-0.5B student、Qwen2.5-7B source、Qwen3-8B source、LLaMA-3.2-1B source，以及 Pythia 系列的受控实验。合成数据来自 BOS-only unconditional sampling，且作者还考察了 temperature 和 synthetic support 的变化，但结论是扩大样本量本身带来的额外稳定收益有限，远不如 compatibility 重要。e:T449,The paper asks a deliberately minimal question: can a language model improve by continuing training on plain text sampled from a model with only a BOS token as input, with no prompts, no teacher annotations, no verifier, and no reward model? The answer is yes, but only under a compatibility condition between the source model and the student. The central claim is not that synthetic text has an intrinsic quality level that makes it useful or harmful. Instead, usefulness is relational. The paper formalizes this through the latent capability resurfacing hypothesis: pretraining already encodes capabilities that are only partially expressed by the base model, and weak self-training can resurface them when the synthetic corpus is compatible with the student. In incompatible settings, the same procedure does little or can even hurt. This is a sharp question for pretraining research because it probes what synthetic data is actually doing, where self-training breaks, and whether gains come from new information injection or from amplifying structure already present in the pretrained weights.f:T41c,The contribution matters in three ways. First, it adds an important qualifier to the model-collapse narrative. Recursive synthetic training is not uniformly bad; the outcome depends strongly on source-student compatibility. That shifts the discussion from intrinsic corpus quality to pairwise transfer geometry. Second, it provides a cleaner explanatory frame for several scattered observations from code self-distillation, reward-free reasoning self-training, and inference-time sampling: many gains may be better understood as resurfacing latent capabilities rather than creating new ones. That has direct implications for continual pretraining, synthetic data mixing, and the interface between pretraining and post-training. Third, it shows that common static proxies are poor predictors of synthetic utility. Source size, source capability, mean NLL under the student, and benchmark similarity all fail to reliably rank synthetic corpora. For anyone working on data curation, teacher selection, or synthetic filtering, that is a nontrivial warning.10:Ta8d,The experimental design is intentionally stripped down. A source model generates unconditional text from BOS. That synthetic corpus is then used to continue training a base student. The student is evaluated on downstream benchmarks. There are no prompts, no task formatting, no rejection sampling, no correctness filtering, no reward model, and no external supervision. This makes the setup unusually clean: if gains appear, they must come from the interaction between the pretrained student and weak self-generated signal.

The analysis has three main components. First is the compatibility study. The paper compares self-generated data, same-lineage transfer, different-recipe transfer, and cross-family transfer. For Qwen students, self-generated data works best; a same-lineage source such as Qwen2.5-7B transfers better than a differently trained but larger source such as Qwen3-8B; cross-family sources such as LLaMA-3.2-1B are weaker and can be harmful. The key point is that absolute source strength is not the right axis. Compatibility likely reflects a bundle of factors such as tokenizer alignment, training distribution, optimization recipe, and internal representational structure.

Second, the paper directly tests and rejects intrinsic-data explanations. It evaluates several plausible proxies: mean token NLL of the synthetic corpus under the student, semantic similarity between the synthetic corpus and benchmark distributions, and scaling the amount of synthetic support. None of these reliably predicts downstream gains. The paper explicitly shows cases where similar NLL leads to opposite transfer outcomes, and where benchmark similarity differs without corresponding utility differences. This is a strong argument against simple distribution matching stories.

Third, the paper provides mechanism-oriented evidence for latent capability resurfacing by jointly measuring utility and extractability. If gains were mainly due to rehearsal of specific generated sequences, one would expect better downstream performance to come with higher verbatim extraction risk. In controlled Pythia experiments, the paper finds the opposite in favorable regimes: held-out exact-match extractability drops while downstream utility is preserved or improved. That points to reinforcement of more distributed task structure rather than memorization of specific strings.

The paper also studies temperature as a coverage-fidelity trade-off. Lower temperature stays closer to high-confidence source modes; higher temperature broadens support but adds noise. This trade-off only becomes useful when the source-student pair is already compatible. More diversity does not rescue an incompatible pairing.11:T7e6,The empirical story has four important pieces. First, source-student pairing experiments establish the main ranking: self-training is strongest, same-lineage transfer is next, and different-recipe or cross-family transfer is weaker. A notable result is that a smaller but lineage-matched source can outperform a larger source trained with a different recipe. So stronger teacher does not imply better synthetic data in this setting.

Second, the paper compares student families. Qwen students show broader gains across reasoning, math, and code. The LLaMA student examined in the paper improves more narrowly, mostly on comprehension-style benchmarks, with little reliable gain on structured generation. This supports the claim that BOS-only self-training amplifies abilities already supported by pretraining rather than filling in missing structured capabilities.

Third, proxy-failure experiments show that mean NLL under the student, benchmark similarity, and synthetic support size do not reliably explain downstream gains. This is especially important for mean NLL, since it rules out a common intuition that data the student already finds easy should be safe and useful for continued training.

Compatible self-training moves the model toward better benchmark utility with lower held-out exact-match verbatim extraction. The paper presents this as a clear shift, not a tiny fluctuation. That is the strongest evidence in the paper against a simple rehearsal explanation.

The paper uses concrete source-student configurations such as Qwen2.5-0.5B as student, Qwen2.5-7B and Qwen3-8B as alternative sources, LLaMA-3.2-1B as a cross-family source, and Pythia models for controlled analysis.
