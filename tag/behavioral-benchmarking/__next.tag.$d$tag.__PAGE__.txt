9:T7c6,方法上有四个关键部件。第一是 rubric 构建。作者不是先拍脑袋定维度，而是通过 self-evolved discrimination-maximization 过程，让维度集合在迭代中稳定到 9 个行为维度，用来覆盖 emotional accompaniment 中更细的行为结构，例如 empathic reflection、advice restraint、safety calibration、persona stability、emotional trajectory tracking 等。这样做的目的，是避免单一总分把不同能力混在一起，也避免人为先验把量表限制死。

第二是四证书式 instrument trust。作者借 classical measurement theory，把评测有效性拆成四类证据：reliability，要求同一工具多次测量结果稳定；cross-instrument replication，要求不同架构、不同训练世代的 judge 在同一对象上给出相近排序；historical-footprint calibration，要求量表能识别已知架构代际变化带来的行为跃迁；pre-registered prediction，要求量表在未观测数据上做出事先承诺、可失败的预测。这套设计的重点，是把“validity”从“和某一组人一致”转成“能否在世界中稳定工作并经得起外部检验”。

第三是 judge 设计。作者使用 5-judge ensemble，覆盖 5 个模型家族，并刻意拉开 17 个月训练 cohort gap，以降低 judge 与 target 同源带来的循环性。文中还报告了 cross-timeline judges 的一致性，以及在 held-out 真实人类对话上的跨家族收敛情况。这里的核心不是 ensemble 本身，而是“异质 judge + 时间错位 judge”共同提供独立性证据。

第四是预注册与审计。作者在收集 Slice 2/3 数据前，预注册了 10 个可证伪假设和 11 个 forward predictions，并保留 4-artifact audit trail。这个部分很像严肃实验科学中的 protocol discipline，目的是防止在主观评测里事后挑结果、调 rubric、改解释。对 LLM 评测来说，这比单纯多跑几个 benchmark 更接近可审计测量。a:T7ac,The method has four main components. First, the rubric is not fixed a priori. The authors use a self-evolved, discrimination-maximization procedure and report that it stabilizes to a 9-dimensional rubric for emotional accompaniment. The dimensions include behaviors such as empathic reflection, advice restraint, safety calibration, persona stability, and emotional trajectory tracking. This matters because a single scalar score would collapse distinct behaviors and make regressions hard to detect.

Second, they introduce a four-certificate instrument-trust framework inspired by classical measurement theory. The four certificates are: reliability, meaning repeated measurements by the same instrument are consistent; cross-instrument replication, meaning judges from different architectural families and training cohorts converge on the same readings; historical-footprint calibration, meaning the instrument detects known architecture-era transitions as behavioral shifts; and pre-registered prediction, meaning the instrument makes falsifiable forecasts on data not yet collected. The key move is to shift validity away from “agreement with one rater group” toward “stability and external testability in the world.”

Third, the judge setup is deliberately heterogeneous. They use a 5-judge ensemble spanning five model families and a 17-month training-cohort gap. This is meant to reduce same-cohort circularity and provide a stronger independence argument than a single LLM-as-judge setup. The paper also reports agreement across timeline-separated judges and convergence on held-out real human conversations.

Fourth, the study is pre-registered and auditable. The authors commit to 10 falsifiable hypotheses and 11 forward predictions before collecting later data slices, and they maintain a four-artifact audit trail. This is important because subjective evaluation is especially vulnerable to post hoc rubric adjustment and selective reporting.b:T670,The empirical study evaluates 49 models across 8 families on emotional accompaniment. The most important result is not a leaderboard ranking but the discovery of dimension-level regressions that aggregate scores wash out.

The clearest example is advice_restraint. In the OpenAI lineage, GPT-5 drops by 1.87 points relative to GPT-4.1, which the authors describe as the largest single-step decline in their 24-month OpenAI arc. In the Anthropic lineage, Opus-4.7 drops by 0.629 relative to Opus-4.6. Yet the corresponding aggregate-score changes are about 0 and -0.12, small enough that a standard leaderboard view would likely dismiss them as noise. A directional Qwen3.5→Qwen3.6 result is also reported, but explicitly marked as n=2 and excluded from the headline regression claim.

On robustness, the headline regression preserves 95% of its magnitude under three independent user-proxy swaps, suggesting the effect is not an artifact of one prompt persona. Reliability metrics are strong: Krippendorff α_ord=0.91 and a 5-judge Spearman-Brown projected reliability of ρ_{K=5}=0.984. Agreement across timeline-separated judges is more moderate, with capability-graded ρ in [0.37, 0.62], but still sufficient to support replication of the main directional findings. On 74 held-out real human ESConv conversations unseen by the rubric, four judges from four families converge at ρ in [0.749, 0.850], which is the strongest external-generalization evidence in the paper.
