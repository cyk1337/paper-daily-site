1a:T967,方法上有三条主线。第一条是模型结构。AV-Flamingo 采用较典型的 encoder-LLM 组合，但关键在于时间轴上的联合表示。视觉侧使用 SigLip 编码图像或视频帧，再通过 Dynamic S2 一类的多尺度压缩模块把高分辨率视觉特征压成较紧凑的 token 序列；音频侧使用 AF-Whisper，并用 sliding-window 机制处理长音频，避免整段音频一次编码带来的长度和计算问题。随后引入 cross-modal interleaving and temporal alignment 模块，把视觉 token 与音频 token 按时间对齐并交错送入文本 LLM。这个设计与“分别做音频问答和视频问答，再把结果交给 LLM 汇总”的路线不同，它试图在输入层就保留时间同步关系，让 LLM 直接在统一序列上做推理。

第二条是数据。作者构建了 Audio-Visual-Skills（AV-Skills），总计约 700 万 caption 和 QA 训练实例，其中约 480 万是 QA。数据覆盖短视频和长视频，并且不是只做表层描述，而是刻意强调 temporal、compositional、cross-modal reasoning。这里的关键判断是：如果训练样本主要是“看见什么”或“听见什么”，模型学到的是感知标签映射；只有当标注要求模型同时利用时间顺序、事件组合和音画互证时，联合推理能力才有机会形成。换句话说，这个数据集的价值不只是规模，而是任务分布与能力目标更一致。

第三条是训练与推理格式。作者提出三阶段 curriculum：pre-training、mid-training、post-training。虽然给出的全文片段没有展开所有 mixture 细节，但从引言和方法描述可以看出，这三阶段对应能力逐步扩展：先建立短程感知与基础对齐，再扩展到长时程、多事件理解，最后通过 targeted post-training 强化复杂推理与指令跟随。与一次性混合所有数据直接训练相比，这种课程式设计更符合长视频能力的形成路径，因为长时程推理建立在稳定的局部感知和跨模态对齐之上。另一个值得注意的点是 TAVIT，即 Temporal Audio-Visual Interleaved Chain-of-Thought。它不是普通 CoT，而是把中间推理步骤显式绑定到时间戳，要求模型在 reasoning trace 中指出相关片段。这相当于给长视频推理增加了“时间 grounding”约束，减少模型只靠语言先验编故事的空间。1b:T843,The method has three main components. The first is the architecture. AV-Flamingo uses a vision encoder, an audio encoder, a temporal fusion module, and a text LLM backbone. On the visual side, it adopts SigLip and then applies a Dynamic S2-style spatial multi-scale compression module to reduce dense frame features into a compact token sequence. On the audio side, it uses AF-Whisper with a sliding-window extraction scheme so that long audio can be processed without collapsing everything into a single global representation. The important design choice is the cross-modal interleaving and temporal alignment module: audio and visual tokens are aligned along time and interleaved before entering the LLM. This differs from pipelines that solve audio and video separately and only merge at the answer stage.

The second component is data. The authors build Audio-Visual-Skills, a dataset with about 7 million caption and QA training instances, including about 4.8 million QA pairs. The dataset spans both short and long videos and is explicitly curated for temporal, compositional, and cross-modal reasoning. That is a meaningful distinction. Many multimodal datasets are large but mostly descriptive. Here the supervision is intended to force the model to use event order, audio-visual correspondence, and multi-step evidence aggregation.

The third component is training and reasoning format. The model is trained with a three-stage curriculum: pre-training, mid-training, and post-training. The paper frames this as a progression from short-range perception and alignment toward long-horizon multi-event reasoning, followed by targeted post-training for instruction following and reasoning. This is a more structured alternative to mixing all data at once. The paper also introduces TAVIT, a Temporal Audio-Visual Interleaved Chain-of-Thought format, where intermediate reasoning steps are explicitly grounded to timestamps.
