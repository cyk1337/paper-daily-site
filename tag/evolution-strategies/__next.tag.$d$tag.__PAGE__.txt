9:T864,方法上，这不是提出全新 ES 变体，而是对 ES 作为 reasoning post-training 工具做系统分析，并与 GRPO 做机制层面对照。ES 的基本过程是：对当前参数加随机扰动，得到一组 perturbed policies；每个扰动模型只做前向采样和 verifier 打分；再把 reward-weighted perturbations 聚合成更新方向。它绕开反向传播，因此显存占用低、并行性强。GRPO 则是在单一策略上采样多条响应，用组内相对优势构造 token-level policy gradient 更新。

作者的理论部分聚焦于“为什么 ES 可能有更高 Pass@K”。核心概念是 verifier-projected Jensen-Shannon diversity：不是看表面文本多样性，而是看 ES 群体在 verifier 视角下覆盖了多少不同的有效推理模式。论文论证这种群体多样性有助于 repeated sampling success，并且可以转化为更新后策略更高的 Pass@K。这个角度很关键，因为它把 ES 的优势从“无梯度优化也能训”转成“参数空间探索保留了解空间覆盖”。

经验分析部分主要有三块。第一，作者比较 ES 与 GRPO 的熵动态和 reasoning coverage，指出 GRPO 会出现 entropy collapse，而 ES 没有同样程度的收缩。第二，作者分析 ES 的参数变化分布，发现真正与任务相关的效果集中在少量较大幅度更新上，而大量参数变化在 perturbation cancellation 后功能贡献很小，因此 whole-model drift 不能直接等价为 widespread functional change。第三，作者系统扫超参数和估计器设计，结论包括：z-score reward normalization 是 ES 成功训练的关键；在 reasoning 的离散 reward 场景下，zeroth-order SFT 常用的 two-point estimator 并不带来优势；随着 pretrained model 规模增大，达到有效优化所需的 population size 反而下降。

论文还提出两个顺序组合方案 ES→GRPO 和 GRPO→ES，用来结合 GRPO 在 Pass@1 上的优势与 ES 在 Pass@K 上的优势。这不是特别复杂的算法创新，但很实用，因为它把两种更新机制看成对策略分布施加不同偏置的阶段性工具。a:T9c4,This is not primarily a new ES algorithm; it is a systematic mechanistic study of ES as a reasoning post-training paradigm, with GRPO as the main gradient-based comparator. In ES, the current parameters are perturbed to form a population of policies, each perturbed model is evaluated by forward-only sampling and verifier rewards, and reward-weighted perturbations are aggregated into an update estimate. This avoids backpropagation, making ES memory-efficient and easy to parallelize. GRPO, in contrast, samples multiple responses from a single policy and applies a token-level policy-gradient-style update based on relative advantages within the group.

The theoretical contribution centers on why ES may achieve higher Pass@K. The paper introduces verifier-projected Jensen-Shannon diversity, which measures diversity not at the surface-text level but in terms of distinct reasoning modes that matter under the verifier. The argument is that higher diversity across the ES population increases repeated-sampling success and can translate into higher Pass@K in the updated policy. This is an important framing shift: the advantage of ES is not just that it is zeroth-order, but that population-based parameter-space exploration preserves broader solution coverage.

Empirically, the paper analyzes three aspects. First, it compares entropy dynamics and reasoning coverage under ES and GRPO, showing that GRPO exhibits entropy collapse while ES does not collapse in the same way. Second, it studies the distribution of parameter changes under ES and finds that task-relevant effects are concentrated in a small subset of larger-magnitude updates, while much of the overall drift contributes little after perturbation cancellation. This supports the claim that whole-model drift is not the same as widespread functional change. Third, it performs a systematic sweep over hyperparameters and estimator variants, finding that z-score reward normalization is critical, that the two-point estimator commonly used in zeroth-order SFT offers no benefit under discrete reasoning rewards, and that the population size needed for effective optimization decreases as pretrained model scale increases.

The paper also proposes two sequential compositions, ES→GRPO and GRPO→ES, to combine GRPO’s strength on Pass@1 with ES’s strength on Pass@K. The idea is simple but useful: the two methods impose different biases on the policy distribution and can be staged rather than treated as mutually exclusive.b:T58b,From the full text provided, the main experiments are conducted on GSM8K and DeepScaleR, comparing ES against GRPO on Pass@1, Pass@K, entropy dynamics, capability retention, and hyperparameter sensitivity. The main empirical result is that ES improves Pass@1 while achieving higher Pass@K than GRPO, and it does so without the same degree of entropy collapse. In effect, GRPO tends to sharpen the policy around a smaller set of successful reasoning trajectories, whereas ES preserves a broader set of viable paths.

On capability preservation, the paper reports that under appropriate training settings, held-out capabilities remain largely intact. Combined with the parameter-change analysis, this supports the claim that previously observed forgetting under ES is better explained by training-set overfitting than by parameter drift per se.

The hyperparameter study yields several practical takeaways: z-score reward normalization is a key ingredient for successful ES training; the two-point estimator does not help in reasoning tasks with discrete verifier rewards; and larger pretrained models require smaller population sizes for effective ES optimization.
