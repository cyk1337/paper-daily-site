18:T55d,TokenDrift keeps the DDLM architecture and sampler fixed and adds a drifting-style refinement loss.

1) Differentiable lift from categorical outputs to continuous features: instead of sampling hard tokens, it computes expected token embeddings from the model’s per-position categorical distributions (soft-token embeddings). These are fed into a frozen semantic encoder to obtain continuous features.

2) Drifting in a frozen semantic space: in that feature space, it estimates an attraction–repulsion drift field V(y; P_data, P_theta), designed to be anti-symmetric so the signal vanishes at equilibrium (when model and data match). This preserves the fixed-point property that motivates drifting.

3) Stop-gradient drift target: it forms a drifted feature target y* (stop-gradient) and trains the DDLM to match y* (direct feature-space matching). The paper also explores alternative couplings—e.g., converting drift into a logit-space “mirror teacher”, and mixing with the original denoising loss—and reports that direct feature-space drifting yields the most stable balance between sample quality and entropy.
