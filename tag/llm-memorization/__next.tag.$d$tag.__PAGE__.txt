5:T45d,The main protocol is a leave-one-out cross-sequence linear probe. A probe is trained on activation pairs from memorized and non-memorized sequences, while one sequence is held out for testing. This tests whether the probe captures a shared memorization signature rather than idiosyncratic features of one sequence. The authors subtract a pure-distinguishability baseline and compare against a random-initialized model with the same architecture. They also perform a causal intervention by projecting out the fitted probe direction from the residual stream. This collapses the local probe signature while barely changing behavioral recall, suggesting partial separability between probe-readable structure and recall-producing structure. The proposed intervention, Probe-Geometry Alignment, aligns activations along the live probe readout direction at each depth. It is a one-dimensional, per-depth operation, unlike feature-matching distillation or concept-erasure methods that operate over the full model width. An adversarial extension iteratively augments orthogonal subspaces to handle attackers that refit probes.6:T489,The experiments cover a toy transformer, Pythia-70M, GPT-2 medium, and Mistral-7B. The reported memorization-specific cross-sequence gaps are +0.32 for Pythia-70M, +0.19 for GPT-2 medium, and +0.30 for Mistral-7B. A random-initialized Pythia model shows some shallow-layer structure, with a mean transformer-layer gap around +0.19, largely from token-identity propagation, but this drops to -0.04 at the deepest layer L6 where the pretrained signature peaks. Removing the fitted probe direction locally reduces the signature from +0.44 to -0.19, while behavioral recall changes by at most 0.19 nats in perplexity. PGA reduces the probe signal across scales: the toy model drops from 1.00 to 0.65 and further to 0.17 at depth 4; Pythia-70M reaches 0.07; Mistral-7B reaches 0.45; GPT-2 medium reaches 0.06 with MD-PGA at k=2. The method is tested against six adversarial probe variants. On five zero-shot capability benchmarks, per-task changes stay within 2.8 percentage points, with a mean accuracy change of +0.2 points.
