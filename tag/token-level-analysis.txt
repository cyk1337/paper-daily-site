18:T467,The proposed method is likelihood-array regression, or LAR. For each target token, a fixed scoring language model evaluates the token under a sequence of nested left-context windows rather than only under the full prefix. These evaluations form a structured array indexed by context length and relative token position, with additional features derived from the model’s predictive distribution. Arrays from documents of different lengths are aligned onto a common domain. LAR-1 learns additive contributions from individual cells to the class log odds and aggregates them over tokens and context lengths. LAR-2 adds within-path second-order features for the same target token, including same-context quadratic terms and products across different context lengths. Basis expansions and random squared projections produce finite-dimensional representations, which are estimated with ridge logistic regression. The theory analyzes the within-path quadratic model, decomposes finite-estimator error into approximation, regularization, estimation, and squared-projection components, and connects prediction error to AUC convergence.19:T867,方法由三部分组成。第一部分是 token-wise potential 的定义与估计。给定题目、当前推理前缀和标准答案，在每个 token 位置 t，从该前缀继续 rollout N 次，统计最终命中正确答案的比例，得到 pot_t。文中用 N=64，在整条推理链的每个 token 上都估计，而不是像 Bachmann 等工作那样只抽稀若干点。这样做的代价高，但换来了定位精度。

第二部分是 cliff token 的统计判定。作者不是简单要求 pot_{t-1}-pot_t 超过固定阈值，而是要求它至少超过 0.1，并且通过一侧 two-proportion z-test 的 95% 置信检验。等价地，阈值写成 0.1 + 1.645 * SE_t，其中 SE_t 由相邻两个位置的 rollout 成功率估计方差决定。这样在 pot 接近 0.5、方差较大的区域，判定门槛会更高；在 pot 接近 0 或 1 的区域，门槛较低。作者明确想避免把 rollout 噪声误判成 cliff，这比固定 0.2 或 30% drop 的规则更稳。

第三部分是 cliff taxonomy。作者根据该 token 是否是 greedy choice，以及该位置分布熵的高低，把 cliff 分成 deterministic、uncertain、sampled-off 三类。可以把它理解为三种不同失败机制：deterministic cliff 表示模型高置信地走错；uncertain cliff 表示模型自己也不确定，错误 token 出现在高熵竞争位置；sampled-off cliff 则更像采样偏离，模型的 greedy 也许不会错，但采样选到了坏分支。这个分类不是装饰性的，因为后面的干预和训练结果表明三类 cliff 的可修复性不同。

在干预上，作者做了两种关键验证。其一是删除第一个 cliff token 后重新采样，观察 pass@64 是否恢复；其二是保留 cliff token 但只重采样后续，观察是否仍然无法完全恢复。这个设计直接检验“cliff 是触发点而不是伴随现象”。其后又提出 Cliff-DPO：把 cliff 位置的好坏 token 构造成偏好对，分别在不同 cliff 子集上训练，比较哪类 token 监督真正有用。与一般 process supervision 不同，这里监督信号被压缩到单 token 位置，目标更尖锐。1a:T83d,The method has three main components. First, it defines token-wise potential. For a prompt, a partial reasoning prefix, and a ground-truth answer, the model rolls out N continuations from every token position and measures the fraction that end with the correct answer. This gives pot_t at each token t. The paper uses N=64 and computes this densely over the full trace, not just at a sparse subset of positions. The cost is high, but the localization is precise.

Second, it introduces a statistical criterion for cliff detection. A token is not labeled a cliff simply because pot_{t-1} - pot_t exceeds a fixed threshold. Instead, the drop must be at least 0.1 and pass a one-sided two-proportion z-test at 95% confidence. This yields an adaptive threshold of the form 0.1 + 1.645 * SE_t, where SE_t depends on the estimated variance of the rollout success rates at adjacent positions. The point is to avoid false positives in high-variance regions, especially around intermediate potential values.

Third, the paper proposes a taxonomy of cliff tokens: deterministic, uncertain, and sampled-off. The categorization uses whether the token is the greedy choice and the entropy of the local token distribution. Intuitively, deterministic cliffs correspond to confident but wrong decisions; uncertain cliffs arise at ambiguous, high-entropy positions; sampled-off cliffs reflect failures introduced by sampling away from a better-ranked continuation. This taxonomy is not cosmetic. It becomes central in the intervention and training results.

The causal validation is well designed. The authors delete the first cliff token and resample from that point, then compare this with keeping the cliff token fixed and only resampling the suffix. If recovery happens only when the cliff token is removed, that supports the claim that the token is a trigger rather than a post hoc marker. They then build Cliff-DPO by constructing preference pairs around cliff positions and training on different cliff subsets separately. Compared with generic process supervision, this is a much sharper token-level supervision signal.1b:T6fa,The study covers seven models and three math reasoning benchmarks: Qwen3-8B, Qwen3-4B, Qwen3-0.6B, Llama-3.1-8B, Llama-3.2-3B, Llama-3.2-1B, and Gemma-3-4B, evaluated on GSM1K, MATH500, and AIME 2025. Token-wise potential is estimated with 64 rollouts per position.

The most important result is the causal intervention. Deleting the first cliff token and resampling restores pass@64 to 1.0, while keeping that token and only resampling the continuation fails to fully recover. That is the strongest evidence in the paper: cliff tokens behave like actual failure triggers, not merely locations where failure becomes visible.

A second result is the cross-model taxonomy analysis. Deterministic cliffs appear relatively scale-invariant, whereas uncertain and sampled-off cliffs better reflect model-specific gaps or scale asymmetries. In practical terms, some errors persist even as models scale because they are confident misgeneralizations, while others are more about uncertainty or decoding-induced branch selection.

A third result comes from Cliff-DPO. Training on uncertain and sampled-off cliff subsets improves reasoning performance, while training on deterministic cliffs does not. The full excerpt does not include every hyperparameter or table entry, but the qualitative conclusion is already informative: token-level supervision helps when the failure is near a ranking boundary or caused by sampling, and helps much less when the model is confidently wrong.

The paper also reports that the adaptive threshold varies roughly from 0.18 to 0.24 depending on the local potential regime, with stricter thresholds in higher-variance middle regions.
