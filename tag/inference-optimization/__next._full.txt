18:T603,SPLIT models success as:
P(success) = P(at least one correct candidate exists) × P(it is selected | it exists),
and explicitly models the second term (selection), not just coverage.

Section 3 derives exact success formulas for two selectors widely used:
1) Uniform selection among verifier-accepted candidates.
2) Argmax selection by verifier score.

The formulas incorporate verifier true/false accept rates and three correlation sources observed in practice:
- Heterogeneous task difficulty (per-task solve rates vary widely).
- Shared blind spots across generators, modeled via a Gaussian copula.
- Correlated repeated verifier calls for the same candidate, modeled via a candidate-level acceptance propensity.

Section 4 estimates these ingredients from a small labeled pilot using empirical Bayes, including variants where verifier accuracy depends on task difficulty (to avoid biased pooling).

A planner then searches over configurations—generator choices, sample counts, verifier calls, thresholds—under a cost budget to maximize predicted success. The paper also provides a regret guarantee tied to differential (not absolute) forecast error (Proposition 4), explaining why approximate calibration can still yield near-optimal allocations.

Section 6.3 turns several empirical “folk observations” into testable mechanisms/conditions, including when majority-of-k re-verification reduces attainable precision and why false-positive rates can rise with stronger generators due to threshold calibration in scored verifiers.19:T4df,Across five allocation problems (real or simulated verifiers) plus an agentic threshold-selection setting, SPLIT’s chosen configuration is within 0.02–0.8 percentage points of the hindsight-optimal allocation on held-out tasks.

Fixed heuristics—“use the strongest model,” “sample more than you verify,” “add more judges,” and an adaptation of compute-optimal exponent rules—each lose at least 2.5 points in some realistic setting.

Concrete correlation/pathology evidence reported:
- On MATH, majority voting over Llama-3-8B-Instruct stays around ~43% while coverage reaches ~98%.
- Difficulty heterogeneity: an average 27% solve rate would imply 96% coverage at 10 tries under i.i.d., but measured per-problem rates yield 61%, with 44/128 problems almost never solved.
- Cross-generator correlation: per-problem solve rates of Llama-3-8B-Instruct and Gemma-7B correlate at 0.80.
- Judge correlation: about one-third of wrong MATH-500 answers fool at least 6 of 8 LLM judges.

Engineering/reproducibility: uses publicly released samples/verdicts, re-executes 80,403 CodeContests programs locally, reports paired significance tests, and includes a long-horizon evaluation on SWE-Bench Verified with a pool of 175 agent systems.1a:T7f6,核心思路是把 CODA（训练侧）提出的 GEMM-plus-epilogue 编程范式迁移到推理侧，并针对 prefill 与 decode 的不同约束分别设计。
- Prefill（FP8）路径：
  - 使用 persistent FP8 GEMM，并在 epilogue 里完成 RoPE、SwiGLU、残差更新、缩放/反缩放、RMSNorm 统计收集、以及把 K/V 直接写入 KV-cache（direct KV-cache placement）。
  - “延迟缩放（delayed scaling）”：在 producing epilogue 里直接写出 FP8 值，减少额外的精度转换与中间张量落盘。
  - RMSNorm 融合：推理权重固定，作者把 RMSNorm 的 gain 折叠进下一层投影权重（fold gain into weights），把逐行的 normalization factor 放到该投影的 epilogue 应用，从而避免显式 materialize 归一化后的激活张量。
  - Packed prefill：支持不同 request 长度的 batch，不用 padding，把“无效行”从 GEMM 行维度里剔除。
- Decode（INT4）路径：
  - 目标是减少权重与 KV-cache 的字节读取：投影权重 INT4，KV-cache 也维护 INT4 副本。
  - Hopper 没有 INT4 Tensor Core MMA；因此需要在进入 16-bit MMA 前展开 INT4。关键设计点是“在哪里展开”：
    - 权重：把 nibble 到 FP16/BF16 的转换尽量放在寄存器里，用短逻辑序列与 packed BF16 操作完成；group scales 在累加后再乘，避免 inner loop 里做标量乘。
    - KV-cache：按 MMA fragment 需要的布局存储（fragment-aligned packing），用 byte-indexed lookup 把 packed cache 值转成 FP16 pair。
  - Outlier-aware cache quantization：在量化前对 key 做 block-diagonal Hadamard transform，把大通道 outlier 能量“摊开”，降低 INT4 量化误差；同时对 query 与 attention output 做匹配变换，保证 attention dot product 在变换空间里保持一致（等价变换）。
- 评估与正确性：提供 layer-level 与 end-to-end 的数值误差检查，并用真实 Llama-3.1-8B checkpoint 在 WikiText-2 上跑完整 decode，测 perplexity 与误差累积。1b:T7c3,The work ports CODA’s GEMM-plus-epilogue programming model from training to inference, then specializes it for prefill vs decode.
- FP8 prefill path:
  - Persistent FP8 GEMMs with compile-time-selected epilogue policies.
  - Epilogues apply RoPE/SwiGLU, residual updates, scaling/restore, collect RMSNorm statistics, and write K/V directly into the KV cache (direct placement).
  - Delayed scaling: write FP8 outputs directly from the producing epilogue to reduce extra conversions and intermediate materialization.
  - RMSNorm fusion via weight folding: since inference weights are fixed, fold RMSNorm gain into the next projection weights before quantization; apply the per-row normalization factor in that projection’s epilogue, eliminating the explicit normalized activation tensor.
