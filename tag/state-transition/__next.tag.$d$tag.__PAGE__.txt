b:T4a2,EvoGUI consists of trajectory normalization plus three VQA-style diagnostics with mechanically derived labels.
- Normalized schema: consecutive screenshots (s_t, s_{t+1}), logged action a_t (click/type/select), optional value v_t (typed text/selected option), and metadata.
- T1 Temporal ordering: given K screenshots (K=3/4/5) from the same trajectory in shuffled order, predict the chronological order.
- T2 Inverse action/value: given (s_t, s_{t+1}), predict the action class; a stricter joint variant requires exact value recovery for type/select to prevent partial credit.
- T3 One-step successor discrimination: choose the logged adjacent successor among two candidates; distractors are sampled as cross-trajectory, same-domain, or long-skip (a later state s_{t+n}, n≥3) from the same trajectory. The paper explicitly notes that since distractors are log-sampled rather than execution-validated counterfactuals, T3 measures discrimination among provided candidates, not universal impossibility.
- Quality control: acknowledges GUI-specific ambiguity (async refresh, ads, loading) and applies manual filtering/validation steps (details are in the benchmark construction section).c:T44b,Orca encodes world signals X (in this version, primarily vision and language) into a latent state S = f_θ(X), and learns forward/backward evolution of states over time. Two factors are introduced:
- z_t for unobserved/implicit dynamics (physics, object properties, scene dynamics).
- c_t for observed/explicit conditions (instructions).

Training uses two complementary paradigms:
1) Unconscious learning: self-supervised learning from continuous videos to capture dense natural transitions, implemented as predicting next-step latents (latent-level temporal prediction rather than explicit semantic supervision).
2) Conscious learning: instruction- and event-conditioned learning using language-described events and VQA supervision to model sparse but meaningful transitions tied to decisions and outcomes.

A notable design choice is evaluation via readouts: the backbone is frozen during decoder post-training, and only lightweight readout modules are trained for text/image/action outputs.
