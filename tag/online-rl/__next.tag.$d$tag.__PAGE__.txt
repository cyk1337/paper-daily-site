第一类设计是降方差。ELBO surrogate 的问题不是不能算，而是不同时间步、不同噪声水平下估计方差很大，导致 reward-weighted ratio 更新极不稳定。作者针对这一点做了几件事：对 surrogate 做更合适的时间步采样与重加权；在 group 内做 reward 标准化，沿用 GRPO 的相对优势而不是单样本绝对回报；并把与预训练回归目标一致的局部误差形式拿来做 ratio 近似，减少 surrogate 与真实优化目标之间的错位。全文的主线是：只要 surrogate 的噪声被压住，ELBO 路线并不天然弱。

第二类设计是控制梯度步长。文章强调，扩散模型的 online RL 比 LLM 更容易因为单次更新过大而破坏采样轨迹分布，随后 reward 估计进一步恶化，形成正反馈。为此作者引入了类似 PPO/GRPO clipping 的更新约束，并配合额外的 step regularization，限制新旧策略在 surrogate 空间中的偏移。这里的关键不是简单“加个 KL”，而是让更新尺度与 surrogate 的统计性质匹配，否则会出现 reward 上升但视觉质量和多样性快速塌缩。

第三类设计是解耦优化与采样器。MDP 方法必须围绕一阶 SDE rollout 写目标，V-GRPO 则可以直接兼容 ODE sampler 和高阶 solver，因为它不依赖对整条随机轨迹做策略分解。这一点很重要：一旦 RL 目标不再绑在 rollout kernel 上，训练和采样可以分别选最合适的数值方案，工程自由度明显更高。

从方法定位看，V-GRPO不是提出新的 reward model 或新的 diffusion backbone，而是把“variational surrogate + GRPO + 一组必要的稳定化细节”组合成一个更简单的默认方案。它与 MixGRPO、BranchGRPO 的区别，不是再做更复杂的轨迹重构，而是回到更贴近预训练的目标，并把失败原因归结为优化统计问题。c:T8d9,V-GRPO builds a GRPO-style policy update on top of a tractable variational surrogate rather than the exact likelihood of the final generated sample or the full joint probability of a denoising trajectory. The key idea is simple: use an ELBO-connected local objective as a proxy for the intractable log-probability term required by policy-gradient RL, then make that proxy stable enough to support online optimization.

The main contribution is not the high-level formula alone, but the stabilization package around it.

First, the method reduces surrogate variance. In diffusion models, ELBO-related quantities can have highly uneven variance across timesteps and noise levels. If those noisy estimates are directly fed into reward-weighted ratio updates, training becomes unstable. The paper addresses this with a combination of better surrogate construction, timestep handling/reweighting, and GRPO-style group-relative reward normalization. The broader message is that ELBO surrogates were not inherently too weak; they were too noisy in prior implementations.

Second, it regularizes update size. The paper argues that denoising models are especially sensitive to overly aggressive online RL updates: once the denoising distribution drifts too far, reward estimation and sample quality can deteriorate together. V-GRPO therefore combines clipped ratio optimization with additional step control so that policy movement remains bounded in the surrogate space. This is conceptually similar to PPO-style trust-region behavior, but adapted to the statistical properties of diffusion surrogates.

Third, it decouples optimization from the rollout kernel. MDP-based methods are tied to first-order stochastic samplers because the objective is defined over the denoising trajectory. V-GRPO does not require that factorization, so it can work with ODE samplers and higher-order solvers. That is an important practical advantage: training and sampling no longer have to share the same restrictive numerical form.

Relative to methods such as MixGRPO or BranchGRPO, the paper does not add more trajectory engineering. It instead returns to a pretraining-aligned objective and argues that the missing ingredient was stable optimization, not a more elaborate RL formulation.d:T5ef,The empirical focus is multi-reward text-to-image alignment and a harder multi-stage, multi-reward setting. The experimental story is built around three axes rather than a single reward number: alignment quality, visual generation quality, and training stability. The most important comparisons are:
1. Against MDP-based GRPO variants, showing that once stabilized, the ELBO-surrogate route can outperform the supposedly stronger trajectory-based formulation.
2. Ablations on variance reduction and step regularization. Removing these components leads to noisier curves, weaker sample efficiency, and in some cases collapse or degraded visual quality.
3. Compatibility across sampler choices, supporting the claim that the method is not tied to first-order SDE rollouts.
4. Multi-reward experiments, where group-relative normalization helps when reward scales differ.
