18:T49c,The proposed score, progress advantage, is the log-probability ratio between the RL-trained policy and its reference policy for the same action under the same state: log π_RL(a_t|s_t) - log π_ref(a_t|s_t). The paper formulates multi-turn agent interaction as a stochastic token-level MDP, where the state contains the full history of generated tokens, observed tokens, tool outputs, and environment feedback. Under a reference-regularized RL objective, the optimal policy ratio recovers the optimal advantage function up to the expected scaling from the regularization coefficient. This extends earlier implicit-reward interpretations beyond deterministic reasoning traces, because the conditioning state now includes stochastic environment transitions. The method can be applied at token, action, or trajectory level by aggregating token log-ratios. It is also argued to apply to common RL procedures, including explicitly KL-regularized algorithms such as GRPO and clipping-based algorithms such as DAPO.
