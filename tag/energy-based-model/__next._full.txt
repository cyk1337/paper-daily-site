18:T44e,The method builds a discrete diffusion process using Glauber dynamics. The state space is a length-L token sequence. Glauber dynamics updates local coordinates and samples according to a stationary distribution defined by an energy function. The paper sets this energy function using a pretrained language model, with a distribution of the form p(x) proportional to exp(-f(x)). This is different from masked diffusion processes that diffuse toward a generic masking distribution or from discrete diffusion with simple uniform transition kernels.

The main implementation uses UL2-style pretrained models. UL2 covers causal generation and mask infilling, which makes it a natural fit for local resampling and conditional updates in the Glauber framework. The diffusion model is initialized from UL2 weights, augmented with time-dependent variables, and trained with a score-entropy loss adapted to the Glauber forward process. The appendix sketches how GPT-style models can be used as energy functions, but UL2 is the cleaner choice because it already supports both left-to-right and infilling behavior.19:T47c,The reported experiments cover unconditional generation, distributional quality, commonsense tasks, structured reasoning, and compute-normalized sampling. The model obtains better unconditional generative perplexity than prior discrete diffusion language models and is competitive with autoregressive models of similar size on generative and zero-shot perplexity. It also improves MAUVE scores and performs competitively with or better than prior diffusion models on Winogrande, PIQA, SIQA, and HellaSwag.

On structured reasoning tasks, the Glauber-UL2 model outperforms masked diffusion language models on Sudoku and Zebra puzzles without adding special training for robust token orderings. The compute-normalized best-of-N experiment uses a T5-Gemma 2B–2B backbone fine-tuned on reasoning and language tasks. A single Glauber-UL2 generation costs roughly 2L model invocations and matches or exceeds an AR model that generates two independent candidates under the same compute budget; the gap increases at N=2.
