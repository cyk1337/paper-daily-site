第一步是 fault injection。作者构造了一个 W8A8 参考实现，其中 INT8×INT8 到 INT32 的 accumulator 按设计是精确的，因此任何跨实现差异都只能来自 epilogue。然后他们从 Qwen3-1.7B 的 196 个捕获层中回放真实操作数，在这个参考流水线上注入 9 类 fault，形成一个预先定义的 check–fault 矩阵。最初是 63 个 cell，经过一个公开的两层 smoke run 后扩展到 77 个 cell，并只修正了 3 个预测，再锁定协议跑完整实验。这个预注册式设计的好处是，避免“看完结果再设计 fault”的自我强化。

第二步是分析为什么 tolerance-based conformance 会失效。作者的论点很清楚：对于 epilogue 中的 rounding mode 或中间精度差异，输出一旦变化，通常只会落到相邻的一个可表示 bfloat16 值上，也就是最多偏移一个 bfloat16 spacing；而一个 spacing 已经是允许合法浮点差异的最小容差。于是“合法实现差异”和“错误实现差异”落在同一个容差区间里，任何基于 tolerance 的检查都无法把两者分开。这不是阈值没调好，而是可分性不存在。

第三步是提出可部署的 power-of-two scale 方案。前作曾用 power-of-two scales 作为诊断探针，能恢复 bitwise 一致性，但 perplexity 恶化了 +157%，因此当时不能作为实际方案。本文指出那个结果是因为只改写了存储的 scales，却没有重新量化权重，属于 operand mismatch fault。作者这次做的是 requantized power-of-two checkpoints：把 scale 约束为 2 的幂，并重新量化权重，使 epilogue 的 scale 乘法变成精确的指数调整，从而消除 kernel 之间在浮点乘法与舍入路径上的自由度。这样，唯一真正严格的 bitwise check 才有可服务的 checkpoint 可用。

第四步是把 conformance suite 的能力边界讲清楚。结果不是“suite 很强”，而是“suite 在某些 fault 上完全无能为力”。这点写得比较诚实，也比单纯展示零误报更有价值。a:T915,The work extends the earlier “integer alibi” argument but turns it into a careful measurement study.

First, the authors build a fault-injection setup. They use a reference W8A8 pipeline in which the INT8×INT8 to INT32 accumulation is exact by construction, so any disagreement must come from the epilogue. They replay operands captured from 196 layers of Qwen3-1.7B and inject nine fault families with known ground truth. This yields a pre-specified check–fault matrix: initially 63 cells, then expanded to 77 after a disclosed two-layer smoke run that corrected only three predictions before the full run. That pre-registered structure matters because it prevents the suite from being evaluated only on faults it was implicitly designed to catch.

Second, the paper explains why tolerance-based conformance fails for epilogue behavior. If the fault is a rounding-mode change or an intermediate-precision change, the output, when it changes at all, typically moves to a neighboring representable bfloat16 value—at most one bfloat16 spacing. But one spacing is already the smallest tolerance that still admits legal floating-point differences across correct implementations. So legal variation and faulty variation overlap exactly where a tolerance check must operate. The limitation is structural, not a matter of choosing a better epsilon.

Third, the paper revisits power-of-two scales as a deployable mitigation. The companion paper had shown that power-of-two scales restore bitwise agreement, but the reported perplexity degradation was +157%, making the idea unusable in practice. This paper shows that the earlier degradation came from rewriting stored scales without requantizing the weights, which is itself an operand-mismatch fault. The new setup uses requantized power-of-two checkpoints: scales are constrained to powers of two and weights are requantized accordingly, so the epilogue becomes an exactly checkable exponent adjustment rather than a free floating-point multiply-and-round path.

Fourth, the paper is explicit about the limits of the conformance suite. The result is not “the suite works well everywhere,” but rather “the suite is exact where it applies and blind where tolerance cannot separate legal from faulty behavior.” That honesty is one of the stronger parts of the paper.b:T57f,There are two main experimental takeaways.

The first is the sensitivity study of the conformance suite. Against the corrected 77-cell prediction matrix, the suite achieves zero false positives and zero false negatives. But this is not the comforting result it might first appear to be. Four of the five epilogue fault classes are not caught by any check in any of their 4,704 cells. That is a strong negative result: the blind spot is systematic, not anecdotal. The explanation is the one-bfloat16-spacing overlap between legal and faulty outputs.

The second is the quality cost of power-of-two scales. The earlier +157% perplexity increase is shown to be an artifact of a bad probe construction—rewriting scales without requantizing weights. With proper requantization, the observed perplexity point estimates across three model sizes fall between -0.28% and +0.48%, while the two kernels become byte-for-byte identical. That changes the status of power-of-two scales from a diagnostic trick to a plausible deployment option.

On the setup side, using operands replayed from 196 captured layers of Qwen3-1.7B makes the fault study more realistic than purely synthetic tests.
