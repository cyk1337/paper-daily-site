Each model tokenizes the same text with its own tokenizer, and a window aligner constructs spans whose decoded text matches across models, producing aligned residual-stream activations. Each model’s encoder maps its activation into the common latent dictionary. For latent selection, the method normalizes selection scores across models, sums the normalized votes, and applies a joint TopK to choose one shared index set. During training, vote-source dropout selects the TopK indices from a randomly chosen single model with probability 0.5, which makes solo-model inference in-distribution.
