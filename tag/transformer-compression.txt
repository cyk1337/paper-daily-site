最醒目的结果是“方差不等于重要性”。在 GPT-2 上，block 3 之后 residual stream 的 90% 方差只需 768 维中的 2 维就能解释，说明从 PCA 角度看表示极度低维；但若真把激活投影到这些高方差子空间，perplexity 会严重恶化。Mistral 上用 CCA 比较 block 15 到 block 31 的预测方向时，top-128 PCA 方向与 top-128 CCA 方向只有 4% 重合，几乎不对齐；而 CCA 方向在各个维度预算下的 prediction R^2 都比 PCA 高 23%。扰动实验进一步表明，平均到 token 后，各维度敏感性近似均匀分布，所以虽然方差集中，真正的“重要性=方差×敏感性”并不集中在少数方向上。

关于 block 线性，作者报告 GPT-2 上 block 近似线性拟合可达 R^2≈0.95，Mistral 第 31 层也有 R^2=0.93，说明单层在局部数据流形上确实近似线性。单层替换也能工作：在 Mistral 7B 上，用一个 rank-773 的线性映射替换第 31 层，相当于用 6.3M 参数替代原本约 218M 参数的 block，held-out perplexity 只增加 1.71。但连续替换多个 block 会迅速失败：5 层串联替换后，R^2 从 0.94 掉到 0.60，perplexity 增加 151。作者把原因拆成两部分：残差连接中的误差累积，以及上游替换导致下游输入分布漂移。

量化部分的结论是，任何把权重先分解成多个因子再分别量化的方法，都会因为误差交叉项而比直接量化更吃亏。虽然全文片段没有给出完整 bit-width 表格，但作者的判断很明确：在相同比特预算下，direct quantization 严格优于 factored approaches。再加上中间层 prediction head 的结果，论文最终把注意力引向 adaptive per-token compute，而不是静态地把所有层都做同一种压缩。18:T711,This is a structural analysis paper rather than a single-method paper. The authors run more than 40 experiments spanning spectral compression, block-level function replacement, rotation-based quantization, activation analysis, and adaptive early exit.

For the variance-versus-importance question, they analyze residual-stream activations between blocks using PCA to measure how many components explain 90%, 95%, and 99% of variance. They then measure importance in two different ways. First, they perturb activations along PCA directions and observe the effect on next-token log-probability. Second, they use CCA to identify directions in an intermediate block that best predict a later block, especially the final block. This setup explicitly separates statistical energy from predictive utility.

For block linearity, they model each block’s residual contribution and fit a ridge-regression linear map to approximate it, reporting R^2 on calibration data. They then test both single-block replacement and sequential multi-block replacement. The key point is not that blocks are non-linear in a trivial sense, but that their apparent linearity is conditional: it holds on the original activation manifold induced by the true upstream blocks, and breaks once earlier blocks are replaced and the input distribution shifts.

For quantization, they analyze why factorized representations are fragile under quantization. If a weight matrix is decomposed into factors and each factor is quantized, cross-term errors accumulate, making the result worse than direct quantization at the same bit budget. The paper also compares behavior across scales instead of extrapolating from a small model to a larger one, and trains intermediate prediction heads to study whether some tokens can exit early with little loss.19:T824,The experiments use GPT-2 124M with 12 blocks and hidden size 768, and Mistral 7B with 32 blocks and hidden size 4096. Calibration uses 8,192 WikiText-2 tokens, with held-out samples for perplexity evaluation. The dataset is small, but the paper is after structural regularities rather than leaderboard numbers.

The most striking result is that variance and importance are largely decoupled. On GPT-2, after block 3, just 2 out of 768 dimensions capture 90% of residual-stream variance. Yet projecting activations onto those high-variance directions severely damages perplexity. On Mistral, the top-128 PCA directions and top-128 CCA directions between block 15 and block 31 overlap by only 4%, showing that dominant-variance directions are almost unrelated to directions that best predict downstream computation. CCA directions achieve 23% higher prediction R^2 than PCA directions at every tested dimensionality. Perturbation experiments further show that sensitivity is roughly spread across dimensions, so importance is broad even when variance is concentrated.

For block linearity, the fitted linear approximations achieve high local fit: about R^2≈0.95 on GPT-2 and R^2=0.93 for Mistral block 31. Single-block replacement can work surprisingly well. Replacing Mistral block 31 with a rank-773 linear map uses about 6.3M parameters instead of roughly 218M and increases held-out perplexity by only 1.71. But sequential replacement fails badly: after replacing 5 blocks, R^2 drops from 0.94 to 0.60 and perplexity rises by 151. The paper attributes this to additive error accumulation through residual connections and to activation distribution shift that invalidates downstream linear fits.

For quantization, the main conclusion is qualitative but strong: factorization before quantization amplifies error through cross-terms, so direct quantization dominates factored approaches at the same bit budget.
