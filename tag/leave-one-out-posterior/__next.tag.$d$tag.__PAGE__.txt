9:T474,The technical contribution has three parts. First, the authors rederive the UDM reverse bridge and show why the plug-in bridge parameterization targets a leave-one-out posterior rather than p(x0|xt). This issue is mostly hidden in masked diffusion, where the mask symbol makes the relevant conditioning structure simpler. In uniform corruption, the current noisy token is itself a vocabulary token, so using it directly in the clean-token proxy changes the optimal predictor. Second, the paper derives exact conversion rules between the denoising posterior, the leave-one-out posterior, and the score. This separates the choice of training objective from the choice of reverse-process parameterization: a denoiser can be trained with an ELBO-style objective, and a leave-one-out predictor can be trained with cross-entropy. Third, the authors introduce two absorbing-state reformulations, AUDM and MUDM. They preserve the UDM joint law while importing structural simplifications usually associated with masked diffusion.
