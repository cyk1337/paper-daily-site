9:T816,The core method is XTE, Cross-Modal Temporal Edits. Starting from an original video-text pair (V, y), the authors define a family of temporal edit operators A_k and matching caption rewrite functions g_k. On the video side, edits include clip reversal, event reordering, and temporal masking. On the text side, the caption is synchronously rewritten with temporal markers such as before, after, and reversed. The result is a paired temporal counterfactual: the edited video and edited text remain semantically aligned, while differing from the original pair only in chronology. That is the key design choice. Because the original and edited videos share nearly the same frames, static spatial cues are held constant, and the model is forced to rely on temporal structure rather than appearance shortcuts.

On the model side, ViTAL-X does not retrain a large video model from scratch. It starts from frozen image-text backbones such as OpenCLIP or SigLIP-2, then adds a shallow spatiotemporal transformer to model cross-frame dependencies, together with targeted LoRA fine-tuning. The trade-off is clear: preserve the strong static semantic priors learned from large-scale image-text pretraining, inject temporal modeling only where needed, avoid catastrophic forgetting, and keep training efficient.

The training objective is contrastive learning over XTE-generated hard negatives. The novelty is not the loss form itself, but the negative distribution. Standard video-text contrastive learning uses unrelated videos as negatives, which differ in actors, objects, and scenes, allowing the model to solve the task through static shortcuts. XTE replaces those with temporal counterfactuals of the same video, so static appearance is no longer sufficient.

The paper also introduces XTE-Bench, a diagnostic benchmark aimed specifically at temporal order, direction, and causality. That evaluation asset is important because standard benchmarks often let models score well through spatial bias, making it hard to verify whether temporal understanding has actually improved.a:T5d0,From the provided full-text content, the main empirical claims are threefold.
