a:T747,方法核心有两个表示层 readout，都是用“策略相关的反事实干预”来做校准目标。第一个 readout 作用在 prompt state 上，预测 closed-book sufficiency，也就是当前策略在不检索时是否已经足够回答；其补量就是 retrieval necessity。第二个 readout 作用在 answer commitment 阶段，估计 evidence reliance：把已检索证据删除后，答案是否会变化，用这种 answer-stage sensitivity 作为 operational grounding 的代理。

关键点不在于直接在线做反事实，而在于先用干预构造标签，再训练轻量 readout 去近似这些判断。这样 rollout 时只需读取 policy representation，就能得到 necessity 和 reliance 分数，避免了过程标注和额外 LLM 调用。

奖励设计是 correctness-first 且 normalization-aware 的。正确的 searched trajectory，只有在 necessity 高时，reliance credit 才有意义；正确的 direct trajectory，则拿 closed-book sufficiency 的信用；重复搜索会有有界惩罚。这个设计针对 GRPO 一类 group-relative 更新里的一个实际问题：如果一个 group 里样本都答对，纯 outcome reward 会让 advantage 退化到接近 0，训练无法区分“少搜、必要搜、证据驱动搜”和“乱搜但也答对”。Search-G1 用 shaped reward 在这些 tied groups 里恢复了可学习差异。

另一个重要机制是 periodic refitting。因为 readout 依赖 policy representation，而 RL 会持续改变表示空间，所以 necessity/reliance 估计器如果固定不动，很快就会失准。论文把 search-agent 训练看成 measurement-credit-adaptation 的闭环：定期用最新策略生成轨迹，重新做校准并重训 readout，让奖励测量始终跟着 policy knowledge boundary 走。这一点比静态 reward model 更贴近实际训练动态。b:T7ae,Search-G1 builds two representation-based readouts, both calibrated using policy-relative counterfactual interventions. The first readout operates on the prompt state and predicts closed-book sufficiency: whether the current policy could answer correctly without retrieval. Its complement is retrieval necessity. The second readout operates near answer commitment and estimates evidence reliance by measuring whether the answer would change if retrieved evidence were removed. This is framed as an operational proxy for grounding rather than a full causal certificate.

The key design choice is to use interventions for calibration, not for online reward computation. Counterfactual tests are run offline to create labels, and lightweight readouts are trained to predict those labels from the policy’s own representations. At rollout time, the reward can therefore be computed cheaply, without process supervision or extra model calls.

The trajectory reward is correctness-first and normalization-aware. Correct searched trajectories receive reliance credit only when retrieval was necessary. Correct direct-answer trajectories receive credit for closed-book sufficiency. Repeated search incurs a bounded penalty. This directly addresses a practical issue in group-relative RL such as GRPO: when all trajectories in a group are correct, pure outcome reward yields near-zero advantage and cannot separate efficient grounded search from redundant search. Search-G1 restores reward variation inside these tied groups.

A second important component is periodic refitting. Because the readouts depend on policy representations, and RL changes those representations over time, a frozen estimator will drift out of alignment. The paper treats training as a measurement-credit-adaptation loop: regenerate trajectories from the latest checkpoint, recalibrate the intervention targets, and refit the readouts so the reward remains aligned with the evolving policy boundary.c:T4dd,The evaluation spans four QA benchmarks and two model scales. The paper is not mainly about maximizing raw accuracy; it studies the trade-off among grounding, search usage, and task performance. A few setup numbers in the paper are already informative: on held-out NQ, a base Qwen2.5-3B answers 14% of questions correctly without retrieval, which means redundant search is a real issue; in HotpotQA, 79.9% of questions are bridge-type, and MuSiQue consists entirely of 2- to 4-hop questions, showing that evidence dependence is structurally common but still policy-relative.

Empirically, Search-G1 improves operational grounding and shortens trajectories while keeping accuracy competitive across benchmarks. The most useful part of the experimental section is the diagnostics. The paper checks estimator fidelity, asking whether the necessity and reliance readouts match the intervention-derived targets; reward tie-breaking, asking whether shaped rewards recover non-zero advantage in all-correct groups; and representation drift, showing that readout quality degrades without periodic refitting.
