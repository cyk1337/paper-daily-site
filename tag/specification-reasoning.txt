- Inputs: (1) the initial RFC proposal, (2) the project repository at the latest commit before the proposal, and (3) the entire prior RFC history (older proposals, discussions, accept/reject outcomes). Agents are restricted from accessing any information after the proposal date.

- Taxonomy: deficiencies are categorized using IEEE Std. 1028-1997 anomaly classes plus prior spec-quality work: Omission, Ambiguous, Inconsistent, Incorrect.
