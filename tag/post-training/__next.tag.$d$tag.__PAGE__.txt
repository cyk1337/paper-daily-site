9:T814,作者先做了一个 teacher-intervention pilot study，用来验证“token 级 teacher preference”是否真实存在。做法是固定 student 已生成的前缀，在某个 token 位置切入，让候选 teacher 接管一小段 continuation，然后再把控制权还给 student 完成后续生成。最后比较这种 student-teacher-student relay 相对纯 student 生成带来的正确率增益，把它当作该位置 teacher 适配度的代理指标。这个实验设计很关键，因为它不是只看 teacher 分布和 student 分布的静态 KL，而是看 teacher 在局部介入后是否真的改善最终结果。

基于这个观察，作者提出 MTA-OPSD。核心思路是构造一个 multi-teacher pool，而不是只用一个 teacher。teacher pool 的形成方式来自对 privileged reference 的不同暴露策略，例如不同截断方式、不同 exposure ratio。这样得到一组“信息量不同、尖锐程度不同”的候选 teacher。训练时，对 student rollout 中的每个 token，不再整句共享 teacher，而是先通过 majority voting 找到相对可靠的候选子集，再用 student-teacher KL 做进一步选择，给该 token 分配一个偏好的 teacher。摘要里提到“preferred teacher always exhibits a large student-teacher KL distance”，这点有些反直觉，因为通常会担心 KL 太大意味着 teacher 太难学；作者的解释更可能是：在有多数投票约束后，较大的 KL 代表 teacher 在该位置提供了更有区分度、也更有纠偏价值的信号，而不是无意义的尖锐偏移。

与已有 adaptive OPSD 的区别有两层。第一，适配粒度从 response-level 变成 token-level。第二，选择对象不只是“参考答案暴露多少”，而是从多个 teacher 中动态选一个最适合当前位置的 teacher。方法上仍属于蒸馏框架，没有引入复杂 RL credit assignment，也没有训练额外 gating 网络；这使它实现上相对直接，但也意味着计算成本主要来自多 teacher 前向和 token 级选择逻辑。a:T895,The paper first runs a teacher-intervention pilot study to test whether token-level teacher preference is real. The setup is simple but well targeted: keep a student-generated prefix, let a candidate teacher take over for a short continuation at a selected token position, then hand control back to the student to finish the response. The final accuracy gain of this student-teacher-student relay over pure student generation is used as a proxy for teacher suitability at that token position. This matters because it measures whether local teacher intervention actually improves the end result, rather than only comparing static distributional distances.

Motivated by that study, the authors propose Multi-teacher Adaptive On-policy Self-Distillation, or MTA-OPSD. Instead of one teacher, the method builds a pool of candidate teachers by varying how much of the privileged reference is exposed and how it is truncated. This creates teachers with different information content and different sharpness levels. During training, each token in a student rollout selects its own teacher from this pool. The selection procedure uses majority voting to identify a reliable subset of candidate teachers, followed by KL-based selection to pick the preferred teacher for that token.

The paper claims that the preferred teacher tends to have a large student-teacher KL distance. That is somewhat counterintuitive at first glance, since large KL is often associated with mismatch. The intended interpretation seems to be that, once majority voting filters out unreliable candidates, a larger KL can indicate a more informative corrective signal rather than an unhelpful overconfident one.

Relative to prior adaptive OPSD methods, the novelty has two parts. First, adaptation moves from the response level to the token level. Second, the model does not merely tune one teacher’s reference exposure; it dynamically chooses among multiple teachers for each token. The framework remains within distillation rather than RL, and it does not appear to require a separate learned gating network. - Judge: given (x, y), learns to discriminate whether y comes from the reference set or from the current generator’s on-policy samples, producing a “probability of being reference” score.
- Generator: optimized with RL (implemented on top of Prime-RL) to maximize the judge score.
No verifiers, no rubrics, no chain-of-thought traces—only outcome-level reference answers.
The central hypothesis is an implicit curriculum: once the generator matches superficial cues (length/format/lexical style), those cues stop separating the distributions, forcing the judge to rely on deeper task-relevant structure; stronger judges and longer co-training should therefore yield better rewards and better policies.
Key contrasts:
- vs RLVR: reward is learned from examples, not computed by a checker.
- vs frozen LLM judges: the judge is trained jointly, with a moving decision boundary.
- vs demonstration distillation / trace-based adversarial training: supervision is reference answers only, not reasoning trajectories.e:T476,Evaluated in five settings:
1) Three “verifiable but verifier-withheld” tasks: integer multiplication, code-based arithmetic-expression synthesis, and SMILES-to-formula translation. RAL with judge-only reward improves held-out correctness and in some settings matches or exceeds training with exact rewards (including variants that augment exact rewards with a generator-sized judge).
2) Two open-ended scientific generation tasks: biomedical abstract generation from in-domain references, and NIH grant abstract generation. RAL shifts outputs toward the target reference distribution without explicitly labeling the target domain, while maintaining semantic diversity; on NIH abstracts, outputs become harder to distinguish from real references for both LLM judges and human evaluators.

Adaptive Teacher Intervention first samples a full response from the student and scores it with the teacher under the same prefixes. It uses a sampled-token absolute log-probability gap, related to the K3 estimator, to measure local teacher-student disagreement. The response is split into short segments, and the earliest segment whose mean gap exceeds a threshold is selected. The preceding student prefix is kept, the divergent segment and its original suffix are discarded, and the teacher writes a bounded replacement segment. The student then continues generation from this revised prefix. Adaptive Supervision Selection monitors the regenerated continuation and removes persistently divergent suffix windows from the distillation loss. This differs from length control or token reweighting because it changes the conditional trajectory before later supervision is collected. The OPD objective remains reverse-KL-style token-level distillation, implemented with teacher log-probabilities over the student’s top-16 candidates and a clipped policy surrogate.10:T916,核心方法是定义并精确计算 leverage。给定锚点参数 θ0 和一个更新方向 Δ，作者考虑沿 αΔ 微小移动后，模型 next-token distribution 的 KL 变化。对 KL 做二阶展开，系数就是 Fisher 二次型 Δ^T F Δ，其中 F 是在固定评估语料上按 token 平均得到的 Fisher / Gauss-Newton 形式。作者不用有限差分，而是用一次 forward-mode Jacobian-vector product 精确算出每个方向的曲率 c(Δ)，再除以同一锚点、同一位移尺度下随机方向的平均曲率 c(null)，得到 leverage ρ = c(Δ)/c(null)。这样定义有几个优点：

1）它衡量的是“相对随机方向”的行为敏感度，而不是绝对 KL，因此能消掉参数重参数化和尺度的一部分影响；
2）它不需要真的沿方向 perturb 权重，因此没有有限差分在极小位移下的 cancellation floor；
3）它天然落在局部 Fisher 几何里，能把更新方向是否对准高敏感子空间这件事量化出来。

作者还定义了几种 null 和分解。isotropic null 给出标准 leverage；profile null 匹配各 tensor 的范数分布；sign-flip null 保持每个坐标的绝对值只随机化符号，用来分离“幅度放在哪些坐标”和“符号是否沿着正确协同方向”这两部分贡献。对应地，ρsign 可以理解为更新符号结构相对随机符号的价值，这在分析 DARE 随机丢弃并重缩放时尤其关键。

在实验设计上，作者既分析公开 released checkpoints，也做 controlled reruns。公开模型包括 OLMo-3、Tülu-3、Zephyr、OLMo-2 等多个阶段的 checkpoint。控制实验则在 0.5B 和 7B 规模上重跑 SFT 与 DPO，并严格使用 fp32 master weights，观察 leverage 如何随相对位移 relΔW 衰减。作者还重现 OLMo-2-1B 的 released DPO recipe，并做 recipe ablation，定位 leverage 水平来自哪些训练细节。

理论上，论文给出一个 noisy quadratic SGD 模型来解释为什么预训练窗口 leverage 会接近 1。直观上，在局部二次近似下，如果优化过程长期受梯度噪声驱动并在许多曲率方向上来回游走，那么累计位移虽然大，但在 Fisher 度量下并没有比随机方向更集中地对准高曲率子空间，因此单位位移带来的输出变化接近随机基线。11:T9f2,实验结果很扎实，且数字有辨识度。

第二组结果是 leverage 会沿训练轨迹被“花掉”。在作者控制的 SFT 和 DPO 训练中，无论 0.5B 还是 7B，只要从同一 anchor 往外走，ρ 都随着相对位移 relΔW 下降。SFT 的下降大致符合 relΔW^-0.7 到 relΔW^-1.7，DPO 则是 relΔW^-0.3 到 relΔW^-1.2。含义是：训练早期的更新方向更集中地对准高敏感子空间，越往后越接近把高 leverage 机会消耗完，后续更新的单位收益下降。

Given an anchor checkpoint θ0 and an update direction Δ, the authors consider the KL divergence between the anchor model and the model moved slightly along αΔ. A second-order expansion gives a Fisher quadratic form, Δ^T F Δ, where F is the token-averaged Fisher or Gauss-Newton matrix on a fixed evaluation corpus. Instead of estimating this curvature by finite differences, they compute it exactly with a forward-mode Jacobian-vector product, yielding c(Δ). They then normalize by the average curvature of random directions of the same size at the same anchor, c(null), and define leverage as ρ = c(Δ) / c(null).

It avoids the cancellation floor that makes tiny finite-difference KL estimates unreliable. And it naturally asks whether an update is aligned with high-sensitivity subspaces of the local Fisher geometry.

The paper also introduces several null models and decompositions. The isotropic null gives the default leverage. The profile null matches tensor-wise norms.
