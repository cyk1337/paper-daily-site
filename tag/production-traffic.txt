Uniform sampling preserves the production distribution but keeps many near-duplicate templates. Diversity-first sampling increases coverage but distorts the service and task mix. The paper introduces a template-aware sampler: mask variable spans, cluster near-identical normalized prompts with LSH, select diverse examples within each template using greedy max-min over variable spans, and allocate budget proportional to the square root of template count. Scoring is task-specific. Classification and information extraction, about 63.2% of traffic, use reference-based evaluation with gold answers generated by Kimi-K2.5 and checked by annotators. It performs a combined SFT stage, then trains separate GRPO experts for instruction following, function calling, and internal task-distribution alignment.
