b:T416,The method starts with a bi-level formulation where the outer problem chooses tokens to minimize validation loss and the inner problem trains the model on the selected data. Selection is represented by a relaxed vector alpha under a keep-ratio constraint, while the optimization procedure maps naturally to discrete extreme points. Instead of differentiating through the inner optimum and estimating inverse-Hessian terms, BLADE introduces the inner optimality condition as a constraint and moves it into a penalized single-level objective through Lagrange multipliers. This yields a first-order, Hessian-free update and leads to an excess-loss-like scoring rule based on the discrepancy between a proxy model and a reference model. The important design choice is that the reference model is dynamically synchronized with the proxy and incorporates validation information, rather than being trained once and frozen.
