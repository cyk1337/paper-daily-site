a:T4aa,RePro uses a forward-then-reflect rollout to generate two progress traces for the same episode.
- Forward pass: the agent interacts with the environment and emits an online progress estimate at each step along with the action.
- Reflect pass: after the episode ends and the final outcome is known, the model revisits the full trajectory and re-estimates step-wise progress retrospectively, producing a hindsight progress sequence that is assumed to be more reliable.
Training has two stages:
1) Retrospection Warmup: a small set of external-LLM demonstrations teaches the model the reflection format and basic behavior.
2) RePro-PO: RL with a composite reward that includes (a) retrospective progress shaping to provide dense per-step signals, (b) online–retrospective alignment to make online progress match the hindsight trace (reducing online noise), and (c) format regularization to prevent degenerate outputs / reward hacking.
