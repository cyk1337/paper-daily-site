First, it covers 21 general-purpose LLM judges across nine providers and three tiers, ranging from 8B-class systems to models above 100B parameters, including April 2026 frontier-generation systems. Second, it evaluates them on MT-Bench, JudgeBench, and RewardBench. MT-Bench contributes 2,391 pairwise comparisons with expert human labels; JudgeBench contributes 350 items across math, coding, creative writing, and analysis with objective correctness labels; RewardBench contributes 2,981 chosen-versus-rejected pairs with per-item position randomization. The bias-audit protocol uses paired AB/BA presentations and response-length analysis to estimate position and verbosity bias.
