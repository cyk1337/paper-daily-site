Standard PPO often relies on TD-style advantage estimates, where value approximation error can bias the update. GRPO removes the learned critic and instead normalizes returns within groups of rollouts. ABC takes a different route: it inserts learned advantage and value terms as control variates for the policy-gradient estimator while preserving unbiasedness. The critic is first trained on offline data to approximate the advantage and value functions of the target policy, and actor and critic are then updated with online sampled trajectories.
