Because different tokenizers produce different sequence lengths, per-position KD is ill-defined. The method uses deterministic dynamic-programming alignment to form paired chunks that decode to the same underlying text. Within each chunk, token probabilities are merged using the autoregressive chain rule to obtain chunk-level distributions. Second, it builds a sparse projection matrix W of shape |V_S| by |V_T|, mapping student-vocabulary probabilities into the teacher vocabulary space. H-KL uses top-ranked mappings under W and is better suited to cases where the common coverage is already reasonably good but exact matching is too conservative.
