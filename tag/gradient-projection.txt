18:T5da,The method has three components. First, dynamic leader selection. For each token, among the Top-K selected experts, one expert is sampled as the leader according to normalized routing scores rather than deterministically taking the top-scoring expert. This prevents one expert from always dominating the unmodified update path. Second, gradient accumulation. Each expert stores a recent history of output gradients as an orthonormal basis using Gram-Schmidt, forming a compact subspace memory of past update directions. Third, gradient projection. The leader keeps its original task gradient. Each follower removes the component of its output gradient that lies in the leader’s accumulated gradient subspace, keeping only the orthogonal component. As a result, co-routed experts processing the same token are discouraged from moving in highly redundant directions.

This differs cleanly from prior work. It does not redesign the router, does not require extra forward or backward passes, and does not enforce diversity through an auxiliary loss in parameter space or representation space. It acts directly on expert output gradients that already exist in the standard training pipeline. In that sense, it is a backward-time intervention rather than a new objective. The extra cost comes from maintaining per-expert gradient bases and performing projections.
