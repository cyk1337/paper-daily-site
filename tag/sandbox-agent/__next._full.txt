17:T816,方法核心是 BPO，Branching Policy Optimization。它不再为每个 prompt 独立采样 N 条完整轨迹，而是先采样一条 backbone trajectory，再在其中选若干 branch point，从这些中间状态恢复环境并展开多个 sibling rollouts，最后用同一父状态下兄弟轨迹的回报均值做 baseline。

具体流程分五步。1）先按当前策略跑出一条 backbone。2）在 backbone 上选 M 个分叉点。论文不是均匀选，而是用每步策略熵做 branch scheduler，优先把预算放在策略最不确定的位置。这样做的理由很直接：如果某一步策略已经很确定，再分叉的边际信息小；高熵位置更可能对应决策瓶颈。3）对每个 branch point，调用 sandbox snapshot/restore，从该状态 fork 出 K 个候选动作或 continuation。4）每个 fork 从该中间状态继续 rollout 到终止，得到 terminal reward。5）在更新时，对某个 sibling rollout 的 advantage，不再减去 prompt 下所有独立轨迹的均值，而是减去同一父节点下其他 siblings 的均值，形成 tree-structured sibling baseline，再配合 PPO-style clipped objective 做策略更新。

理论部分有两层。第一层是无偏性：只要 sibling baseline 不包含当前样本自身的回报，或按 leave-one-out 方式构造，优势估计对策略梯度仍无偏。第二层是方差上界：相较 GRPO 依赖 Var(R|s0)，BPO 的 sibling baseline 把方差压到与 E[Var(R|st)] 同量级，差值正是前缀解释掉的那部分 Var(E[R|st]|s0)。这正对应“共享前缀不该被当成独立样本噪声”。

和 VinePPO 的区别也值得看。VinePPO 也利用中间状态分叉，但更偏 critic / value estimation 语境；BPO 的重点是面向当前 LLM agent 常用的 baseline-only、critic-free 训练范式，把 branch topology 直接嵌进 GRPO/PPO 风格更新里，并强调 matched compute 下的方差收益。和 PPO/RLOO/GRPO 相比，BPO 改的不是 loss 形式，而是 rollout topology 与 advantage estimator 的条件化粒度。18:T857,The method is Branching Policy Optimization (BPO). Instead of sampling N independent full trajectories per prompt, BPO samples one backbone trajectory, chooses branch points along it, restores the sandbox at those states, expands multiple sibling continuations, and computes advantages using a sibling baseline within each branch family.

The pipeline has five steps. (1) Roll out one backbone trajectory under the current policy. (2) Select M branch points on the backbone. The paper uses an entropy-based scheduler rather than uniform sampling: branching budget is allocated to steps where the policy is most uncertain. This is sensible because branching at already-certain decisions adds little information, while high-entropy steps are more likely to be decision bottlenecks. (3) At each selected branch state, snapshot/restore is used to fork K alternative actions or continuations. (4) Each fork is rolled out to termination to obtain a final reward. (5) For policy updates, the advantage of one sibling rollout is computed against the mean return of its siblings under the same parent state, yielding a tree-structured sibling baseline, and then plugged into a PPO-style clipped objective.

The theory has two key parts. First, the estimator is unbiased as long as the sibling baseline is constructed without leaking the sample’s own return, e.g. via leave-one-out structure. Second, the variance bound improves over GRPO: instead of scaling with Var(R|s0), the relevant term becomes E[Var(R|st)] up to a K/(K-1) factor. The gap is exactly the prefix-explained component Var(E[R|st]|s0), which is the variance standard group baselines fail to remove.

It is also useful to compare BPO with VinePPO. VinePPO also branches from intermediate states, but it is more rooted in critic/value-estimation settings. BPO is tailored to the critic-free, baseline-only regime that has become common in LLM agent RL, and its main intervention is the rollout topology plus a state-conditional advantage estimator. Relative to PPO/RLOO/GRPO, the loss family is familiar; what changes is the granularity at which shared structure is exploited.19:T5ce,The experiments cover WebShop, ALFWorld, and SWE-bench Verified, with two backbones, and comparisons are done at matched return-sample budget or closely matched compute. The headline results are: BPO beats PPO, RLOO, GRPO, and VinePPO by 3.6 to 6.1 absolute points; it reaches the same final performance as GRPO in 0.62× the gradient steps; and it shows roughly half the empirical gradient variance. These numbers support the central claim that the gain comes from better rollout structure rather than simply more sampling.

The ablations are where much of the paper’s value sits. Increasing branch width K helps at first and then saturates, which suggests sibling comparison needs some horizontal diversity but not arbitrarily large branching. Branch count M shows a similar trade-off: too many branch points fragment the budget and reduce useful continuation depth. The entropy-based scheduler outperforms uniform and lowest-entropy branching, indicating that branch allocation is a real source of gain rather than a cosmetic choice. The paper also measures snapshot overhead explicitly and argues that, when checkpoint/restore is reasonably cheap, the variance-reduction benefit outweighs the systems cost.

The method appears best suited to sparse-reward, long-horizon tasks where early decisions strongly shape final success.
