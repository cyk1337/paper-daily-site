9:T412,TRACER uses a two-stage training recipe. First, supervised fine-tuning on real user dialogues teaches the model to generate user responses and to emit structured annotations: a rationale and an ordinal intent score. The intent score acts as an interpretable proxy for the user’s evolving commitment or engagement state. Second, the simulator is trained with multi-turn reinforcement learning while interacting with a fixed assistant. Rewards combine terminal outcome agreement and trajectory alignment against paired human logs. Dynamic time warping is used to align trajectories with different lengths and conversational pacing. A deviation-aware advantage modulation mechanism then uses local alignment errors, accumulated over discounted trajectory suffixes, to scale turn-level policy updates while preserving the direction of the session-level advantage.
