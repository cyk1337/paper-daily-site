1a:T8f5,方法由三部分组成。第一部分是 Strategy Nudging。做法很直接：在同一个问题输入前后，附加一些轻量的、启发式的策略级文本上下文，让模型分别沿不同 reasoning mode 采样。例如对数学题，可以提示代数法、几何法、分步验证、边界检查之类。关键点不在提示词本身多精细，而在于它把原本混在一起的推理空间显式分桶，让 rollout 不再只是从一个单峰分布里反复抽样，而是被迫访问多个潜在子空间。作者强调这和 oracle-guided 方法不同：它不提供正确解，也不提供中间步骤，只提供“往哪个方向想”的弱引导，因此保留了发现新解法的空间。

第二部分是 Inter-Intra group advantage。标准 GRPO 假设同一 prompt 下的一组 rollout 来自同一采样条件，因此组内 reward 的均值和方差可以拿来做相对 advantage 归一化。NudgeRL 里不同 rollout 来自不同 strategy context，reward 差异既包含轨迹质量，也包含 context 本身带来的系统偏置。如果仍按原始 group-wise normalization 做，credit assignment 会失真：某些策略组可能整体更难或更易，导致组内相对分数不能横向比较。作者因此把 advantage 估计拆成 intra-group 和 inter-group 两层，既保留同一策略组内部的相对排序，又引入跨策略组的校正，让更新信号能区分“这个 rollout 在本策略下好不好”和“这个策略整体值不值得学”。从问题设定看，这一步是方法成立的关键，不然 strategy nudging 只会把采样分布打散，却未必能稳定训练。

第三部分是 distillation-augmented objective。因为训练时 rollout 是在带 strategy context 的条件下采样，而测试时通常不会给这些 context，所以存在明显的 train-test mismatch。作者的处理是把在 context-forced exploration 中发现的有效行为蒸馏回 base policy，让基础模型在无额外提示时也能复现这些更优轨迹。这个设计很像“探索策略”和“部署策略”解耦：前者负责发现，后者负责吸收。相比直接把带 context 的 policy 当最终模型，这样更符合 RLVR 的实际使用方式，也减少了推理时额外 prompt 工程的依赖。1b:T840,The method has three main components. First, Strategy Nudging. For each prompt, the model is asked to generate rollouts under lightweight strategy-level textual contexts, such as hints that encourage different reasoning modes. The key idea is not that the prompts are highly informative, but that they partition the reasoning space into multiple semantically meaningful subspaces. Instead of repeatedly sampling from one dominant mode, the policy is nudged to visit alternative trajectories it would otherwise ignore. Unlike oracle-guided methods, these nudges do not reveal the answer or intermediate steps; they only bias the search direction, leaving room for novel successful trajectories to emerge.

Second, Inter-Intra group advantage. Standard GRPO assumes that rollouts for a prompt are sampled under the same condition, so within-group reward normalization is meaningful. Here, rollouts come from different strategy contexts, and reward variation mixes two factors: trajectory quality and context-specific bias. A naive group-relative normalization would therefore distort credit assignment. The proposed estimator separates intra-context comparison from inter-context calibration, so the update can capture both whether a rollout is strong within its own strategy group and whether that strategy is globally useful. This is likely the central technical piece: without it, strategy-conditioned exploration may diversify samples but produce unstable or misleading policy updates.

Third, a distillation-augmented objective. Training-time exploration is performed under explicit strategy contexts, but inference typically uses the base prompt only. That creates a train-test mismatch. The paper addresses this by distilling useful behaviors discovered under context-forced exploration back into the base policy. Conceptually, this decouples discovery from deployment: the conditioned policy explores, the unconditioned policy absorbs.
