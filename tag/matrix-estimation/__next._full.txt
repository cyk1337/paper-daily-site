核心重述是：MAGIC 能给出 J 的某一行（固定 q，对所有训练样本的影响）或某些向量-雅可比积/雅可比-向量积，但若要对每个 q 都跑一次反传穿越训练轨迹，成本随 k 线性爆炸。作者把“预算受限的多查询归因”表述为：在只能获得少量对 J 的线性测量（left/right vector products）的情况下，估计整个大矩阵。

论文区分了两个目标，并指出它们对应不同的最优测量策略：
1) reconstruction：尽量在 Frobenius 意义下重建 J（更偏向把大行范数的查询重建得更好）；
2) attribution：更像“每个查询一票”的平均归因质量（不希望被少数大范数查询主导）。作者强调这两者的差异不是表述细节，而会改变你应该如何分配测量预算。

在算法上给出两条路线，均建立在现有 metagradient 原语之上：
- Mage（Metagradient Approximation by Gram Eigendirections）：面向 reconstruction。直觉是用少量测量去捕捉影响矩阵的主能量子空间（类似从 Gram 矩阵的特征方向提取主成分），让重建误差主要落在尾部方向。
- Spell（Spherical Estimation for the Linear datamodeling score at Low budgets）：面向 attribution。它更强调对查询方向的“均匀覆盖”，用球面/各向同性的测量设计减少对行范数分布的偏置，从而在低预算下让“平均查询”的归因更稳。

与以往可扩展归因（近似 influence function、tracing、Shapley 近似）相比，这篇工作的关键差别不在于再造一个新的近似公式，而是：以 MAGIC 的精确 metagradient 为底座，把问题提升为“矩阵估计 + 测量设计”，并明确区分‘重建矩阵’与‘做归因评分’两种评价口径。19:T80a,They adopt the datamodeling setup: assign a continuous weight vector w over training points, run a fixed (randomness-fixed) training algorithm A to get parameters θ(w), and define each test query q as a scalar measurement φ_q(θ) (typically loss). The influence scores are the Jacobian entries ∂f_q/∂w_i, and stacking them yields a large influence matrix J ∈ R^{k×n} (k queries, n training points).

MAGIC-style exact influence can compute influences accurately by differentiating through the entire training trajectory, but doing this per query scales linearly in k. The key reframing is: multi-query attribution becomes an influence-matrix estimation problem from a limited number of linear measurements (left/right vector products obtainable via existing metagradient machinery).

A central conceptual contribution is separating two objectives:
- Reconstruction: recover J well in a global (Frobenius-like) sense, which implicitly prioritizes high-row-norm queries.
- Attribution: optimize attribution quality per query more uniformly, avoiding domination by a few large-norm rows.
They show these objectives imply different optimal measurement designs.

They then propose two estimators built on metagradient primitives:
- Mage (Metagradient Approximation by Gram Eigendirections) targets reconstruction by focusing measurements along dominant eigendirections of an associated Gram structure, effectively capturing the high-energy subspace of J.
- Spell (Spherical Estimation for the Linear datamodeling score at Low budgets) targets attribution by using more isotropic/spherical measurement strategies to improve average-per-query attribution under low budgets.
