9:T4d0,The paper defines an independent-update sampler. At each step, the network emits a distribution for each writable position; the scheduler selects a group R based only on such per-position distributions; the sampler then draws each position in R independently. This abstraction covers absorbing-state masked diffusion, remasking diffusion, and the terminal phase of uniform-state diffusion. The main theoretical claims are sharp. If the positions in R are conditionally dependent given the frozen tokens, the divergence incurred by that step is at least the total correlation of the group. No set of per-position distributions can certify group independence, because two joint distributions may share identical one-position marginals while allowing different combinations of values. The states created by a dependent parallel write are also states where the standard per-position masked cross-entropy objective does not determine the needed joint conditionals. A further reachability result shows that simply writing one position at a time can also fail under hard constraints when the rest of the sequence pins a token.
