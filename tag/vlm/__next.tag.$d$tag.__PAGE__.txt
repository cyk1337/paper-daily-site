具体做法分三步。

第一步是特征选择。作者先做 layer-wise analysis，研究 diffusion branch 在不同层、不同去噪阶段产生的表示，哪些对空间推理最有信息量，哪些注入后对原有语义路径干扰最小。这个分析是方法成立的前提，因为 diffusion 表示不是越多越好：早期层可能过于局部、后期层可能更偏生成目标，错误选择会把噪声或无关细节带进理解分支。

第二步是空间对齐与投影。DiFuse 从 UMM 原生 DiT 中抽取中间 spatial diffusion features，把它们投影到多模态 decoder 的 hidden space，并与视觉 token 保持空间对应关系。论文反复强调 spatial correspondence，因为作者的假设不是“扩散特征提供额外语义”，而是它们提供局部结构线索。如果在 token 对齐时打乱空间位置，收益会明显下降，这一点后面也被 feature shuffling 实验证实。

第三步是注入方式。作者不是直接替换视觉 token，也不是全层强融合，而是在选定的 decoder 层通过 residual adapters 把 diffusion 特征加到 visual-token states 上。这样做的目的，是把 diffusion 表示当作补充通道，而不是主导通道，尽量避免破坏原有 semantic pathway。论文特别关心 injection position：注入太早可能扰乱语义建模，太晚则来不及影响推理。作者通过系统分析找到干扰较小、收益较高的层位。

从方法取舍上看，DiFuse 和几类相关路线有明显区别。它不同于外接 geometry module 的方法，因为没有单独结构分支；不同于 auxiliary generation/reconstruction objective，因为没有额外训练目标；也不同于 test-time 先生成再理解的 pipeline，因为它直接复用 native diffusion states，而不是把生成过程当中间步骤。它更像一种表示重用与接口设计问题：在统一模型内部，把原本只服务生成的中间状态变成理解时可调用的结构先验。a:T990,DiFuse is deliberately lightweight. The diffusion branch and visual encoder are kept frozen. No external generator is introduced, and no auxiliary loss is added. During supervised fine-tuning, intermediate features from the model’s native diffusion transformer are injected into the understanding pathway.

The method has three main components.

First, feature selection. The authors perform a systematic layer-wise analysis over diffusion representations from different layers and denoising stages. The goal is to identify which states are informative for spatial reasoning and which can be integrated with minimal disruption to the original semantic pathway. This matters because diffusion features are not uniformly useful: some may be too local, some too generation-specific, and naive fusion can easily inject noise.

Second, spatial alignment and projection. DiFuse extracts intermediate spatial features from the native DiT, projects them into the hidden space of the multimodal decoder, and aligns them with visual tokens while preserving spatial correspondence. This is central to the paper’s hypothesis. The benefit is not supposed to come from generic extra capacity, but from transferring local structural cues. If spatial correspondence is broken, the gains should disappear, and the paper later tests exactly that.

Third, injection via residual adapters. Instead of replacing visual tokens or performing heavy fusion throughout the network, the method injects diffusion features into visual-token states at selected decoder layers using residual adapters. This treats diffusion states as a complementary channel rather than a dominant one, helping preserve the original semantic pathway. Injection position is a major design variable: too early may interfere with semantic abstraction, too late may be too weak to affect reasoning. The paper uses empirical analysis to find favorable insertion points.

Relative to related work, DiFuse differs from geometry-augmented multimodal models because it does not add a dedicated structural module; it differs from auxiliary generation/reconstruction approaches because it does not change the training objective; and it differs from generate-then-reason pipelines because it directly reuses native diffusion states rather than using generation as an intermediate procedure. The method is best understood as an interface design for internal representation transfer within a unified model.b:T82d,Experiments are centered on UniWorld-V1, with transfer validation on OmniGen2. The main result is that DiFuse consistently improves spatial reasoning over a matched SFT baseline while keeping general multimodal performance broadly comparable.

The empirical evidence is organized around four themes.

First, main-task improvements. The paper reports gains on spatially grounded reasoning tasks after adding DiFuse. The excerpt provided does not include exact numbers, so the tables in the full paper are needed for precise margins, but the qualitative claim is clear: the spatial gains are not obtained by obviously sacrificing broad multimodal competence.

Second, ablations on feature and layer selection. The authors compare diffusion features from different layers, different denoising stages, and different decoder injection positions. This is one of the most important parts of the paper because it answers which diffusion states are actually useful. The results indicate that usefulness is selective rather than monotonic: not every layer helps, and there is a clear sweet spot for injection.

Third, tests of spatial correspondence. Feature shuffling or position perturbation substantially weakens or removes the gains, showing that the improvement depends on preserving alignment between diffusion features and visual tokens. This supports the claim that the transferred signal is structural and spatial, not just an extra source of generic features.

Fourth, transfer across architectures. Similar trends on OmniGen2 suggest that the effect is not a one-off trick tied to a single model, but a more general property of unified architectures with native diffusion branches.

For a foundation-model reader, the most useful aspect is the experimental logic. The paper does not treat diffusion features as a black-box add-on; it uses layer selection, injection-position studies, and shuffling controls to argue that native generative states carry reusable spatial structure.
