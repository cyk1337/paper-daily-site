9:T528,Compute penultimate representations z(x;θ) on a probe set, form the empirical covariance Σ̂, take eigenvalues λk, normalize pk=λk/∑jλj, and define spectral entropy H=−∑k pk log pk; normalize by log d to obtain H~∈[0,1]. High H~ corresponds to near-isotropic representation energy; low H~ indicates concentration into a low-dimensional subspace.

The authors frame grokking as two phases: (I) rapid parameter-norm expansion while H~ stays high (memorisation plateau), followed by (II) a monotone decline (“entropy collapse”) after norm growth saturates. Generalisation reliably follows once H~ crosses a task-dependent threshold H~*. They report weak correlation between ||θ||2 and H~ (ρ≈−0.248), positioning entropy collapse as additional information rather than a reparameterization of norm dynamics.

They further propose a forecasting rule (power-law fit) to predict the grokking time from the entropy trajectory, and provide causal-style evidence via a representation-mixing intervention, with a norm-matched control to argue against ‘norm is the driver’ explanations.

Finally, they test architectural dependence: MLPs can show entropy collapse without grokking, supporting a “necessary but not sufficient” claim and highlighting Transformer inductive biases as a missing ingredient.a:T474,Main empirical setting: 1-layer Transformers on modular arithmetic tasks (Z/pZ, abelian) and S5 permutation composition (non-abelian).
- Seed-stable thresholding: across 10 seeds and three modular arithmetic tasks, H~*≈0.609 with 95% CI [0.595, 0.624]. In all runs, H~(t)≤H~* precedes reaching test accuracy ≥0.99, with mean lead time ≈1020 steps (95% CI [890, 1140]).
- Task dependence: for S5, the threshold shifts (reported H~*≈0.655), suggesting within-task stability but across-task variation.
- Independence from norm: Pearson correlation between ||θ||2 and H~ is weakly negative (ρ≈−0.248; paper reports CI/p-value), supporting the two-phase decomposition.
- Intervention: representation-mixing changes the entropy/generalisation timing; a norm-matched control indicates norm alone does not explain the effect.
- Architecture control: MLPs exhibit entropy collapse without grokking, establishing the indicator as necessary but not sufficient.
