5:T46d,The method has three parts. First, Rewrite-Driven Joint SFT trains the model to generate structured rewrites rather than CoT plus an answer. Images, videos, and visual documents are recaptioned; text queries are elaborated. The rewrite keeps retrieval-relevant entities, attributes, relations, and context, but avoids committing to a single summary. This is a better fit for general retrieval than CoT, because retrieval needs semantic coverage and discrimination more than explicit reasoning trajectories. Second, Cross-Mode Alignment aligns discriminative and generative embedding spaces through mutual contrastive learning: embeddings of the same query across the two modes are pulled together, while negatives are separated. This enables cross-retrieval and lets a system trade latency against semantic refinement. Third, Refine-RL uses discriminative embeddings as stable anchors. The process reward is based on whether the generative embedding improves the positive-negative similarity gap over the discriminative embedding.
