1a:T42c,The paper formalizes strong-to-weak scaffolding with a builder model and a fixed target model. For each benchmark, the builder receives a small validation split, 5% of the data, and must construct a scaffold that transfers to a hidden test split. The scaffold may include task routing, prompt templates, deterministic solvers, few-shot examples, verification passes, format enforcement, decomposition, or tools. The target model is not trained. This isolates transfer through inference-time structure rather than transfer through parameters. The empirical domain is Theory-of-Mind reasoning, where models must track nested beliefs, perspective changes, hidden information, and Bayesian goal inference. These tasks are difficult for small models but also expose structure that a scaffold can exploit.
