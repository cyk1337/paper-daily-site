1a:T71b,Wiola is still a decoder-only autoregressive LM, but each layer is modified in five places. First, WiolaRMSNorm adds a learned per-dimension offset before RMS normalization. The motivation is that pure rescaling cannot shift the effective zero point of the representation, so it may be insufficient against deep-layer collapse into a low-rank subspace. The overhead is tiny, and RMSNorm is recovered when the offset is zero. Second, SRPE extends RoPE-style 2D rotations into a helical 3D positional construction with a radial term. The goal is to encode token-, phrase-, and discourse-scale structure in one analytic form. Unlike standard RoPE, which is purely relative in the phase term, SRPE keeps relative-offset behavior while introducing controlled absolute-position dependence through the radial product. Third, GCLA gives each layer gated access to compressed summaries from the previous two layers. The summaries are mean pooled, and the gate is initialized near zero, so the model starts close to a standard stack and only learns to use cross-layer context when beneficial. Fourth, ATM performs dynamic greedy merging of semantically redundant adjacent tokens in the middle third of layers, then restores sequence length later so the external interface remains unchanged. The placement is deliberate: early merging risks damaging local pattern formation, while late merging can interfere with output alignment. Fifth, DSFF replaces a single FFN path with dual parallel streams intended to separate local and global feature extraction, followed by learned per-dimension fusion.
