9:T850,整体架构由三部分组成。第一部分是语义条件器：一个 multimodal LLM 编码文本，或联合编码文本与参考音频，再把层级 hidden states 聚合成 semantic condition。这样做的原因是，纯文本条件不足以表达说话人、风格、情绪、环境等信息，而直接让生成器从波形里自己抽高层语义又会让训练目标过重。第二部分是 AuK-VAE：在 speech、general audio、music 上联合训练的音频 VAE，提供共享 acoustic latent space，用于参考条件和最终波形重建。这里的设计选择很关键：如果只在语音上训练离散或连续 codec，编辑任务里遇到非语音声学事件、背景音或音乐片段时，latent 空间会过窄。第三部分是生成主干：hybrid rectified-flow Transformer。前段使用 dual-stream MMDiT blocks，让 semantic stream 和 acoustic stream 先交互但保留各自残差路径；后段切换到 unified single-stream DiT blocks，在融合后的序列上预测 rectified-flow velocity。这个结构比一开始就全融合更稳，因为生成和编辑任务对“保留已有声学信息”与“注入新语义信息”的要求不同，前期分流更容易控制条件注入强度。

训练分三步。预训练先做 generation-only warm-up，再联合 generation 和 editing，用共享 flow-matching objective 学统一映射。这样安排是因为编辑任务条件更复杂，若一开始混训，模型容易在基础可懂度和自然度上不稳。后训练分成两条线：编辑侧做 flow-based preference optimization，因为开放式编辑很难靠单一自动指标覆盖；生成侧做 Flow-GRPO，用内容正确性、说话人相似度、指令-风格一致性等自动奖励优化。最后再做蒸馏：通过 consistency initialization 和 task-routed Decoupled DMD，把 32 NFE teacher 压到 4-step、CFG-free 的 AuK-Flash。这里最有意思的是，作者没有把 RL 或 preference optimization 只当作后处理，而是把它们接到 flow 模型训练里，形成从统一预训练到偏好优化再到快速采样蒸馏的一整条链路。a:T961,The system has three main components. The first is a semantic conditioner: a multimodal LLM encodes text alone or text together with reference audio, and aggregates hierarchical hidden states into a semantic condition. This choice is deliberate. Text alone cannot fully specify speaker identity, style, emotion, or environment, while asking the acoustic generator to infer all high-level semantics directly from waveform conditioning would overload the generation objective. The second component is AuK-VAE, an audio VAE trained jointly on speech, general audio, and music to provide a shared acoustic latent space for both reference conditioning and waveform reconstruction. This matters because a speech-only codec space would be too narrow for editing tasks that involve non-speech acoustic events, background sounds, or mixed scenes. The third component is the generator backbone: a hybrid rectified-flow Transformer. It starts with dual-stream MMDiT blocks so that semantic and acoustic streams can exchange information while keeping separate residual pathways, then switches to unified single-stream DiT blocks that refine the fused sequence and predict rectified-flow velocity. Compared with immediate full fusion, this design is better aligned with the needs of mixed generation and editing tasks, where the model must balance preservation of existing acoustics against injection of new semantics.

Training is staged. Unified pretraining begins with a generation-only warm-up, then moves to joint optimization of generation and editing under a shared flow-matching objective. The warm-up is important because editing has more complex conditioning and can destabilize basic intelligibility and naturalness if introduced too early. Post-training splits into two branches. For editing, the authors use flow-based preference optimization because open-ended editing quality is hard to capture with a single automatic metric. For generation, they apply Flow-GRPO with automatic rewards for content correctness, speaker similarity, and instruction-style consistency. Finally, they distill the post-trained teacher using consistency initialization and task-routed Decoupled DMD, producing AuK-Flash, a 4-step, CFG-free student.
