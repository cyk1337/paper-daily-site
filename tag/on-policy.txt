18:T8bb,方法核心是 Interactive-Policy Distillation, IPD。它不是让学生完整生成、教师事后打分，也不是让教师始终主导轨迹，而是设计了一个 bidirectional propose-and-verify 状态机。学生和教师轮流扮演 proposer 与 verifier：当前 proposer 生成若干 token，verifier 判断当前前缀是否仍处在可接受区域；如果学生轨迹开始漂移，教师接管并给出一段纠偏 continuation；当轨迹重新回到学生能够承接的区域，再把控制权交还给学生。这样得到的训练样本是 mixed-source trajectory，其中一部分 token 来自学生，一部分来自教师。

在损失设计上，作者没有对整条混合轨迹统一套一个 KL，而是按 token 来源拆开。对学生自己提出的 token 位置，用 reverse KL，让学生在自己访问到的状态上向教师分布靠拢，但不过度覆盖教师所有 mode；这更接近 on-policy distillation 的目标，也更适合小模型在自身可实现的解空间里收缩分布。对教师接管时生成的 token 位置，用 forward KL，让学生直接模仿教师纠偏后的 continuation；这部分更像局部离线蒸馏。这个 source-split objective 是论文最有方法含量的地方，因为它把 on-policy 和 off-policy 蒸馏拼接在同一条轨迹里，而不是简单做数据级混合。

工程上，作者还实现了 fused inference engine，把 teacher 和 student 共置于一个 serving instance，分别维护 KV cache，并根据当前 proposer/verifier 角色调度请求。这个系统细节不是论文主创新，但它解决了双模型交替 rollout 的实际开销问题，否则 IPD 很容易因为 teacher intervention 过于频繁而失去训练效率。

和已有 OPD 的区别主要有三点。第一，传统 OPD 默认学生轨迹始终可被教师稳定评估，IPD 明确承认并处理 unanchoring。第二，已有方法常在样本级混合 on-policy/off-policy 数据，IPD 在 token 级别区分 supervision。第三，教师不是静态 labeler，而是交互式纠偏者。这个设计更像 DAgger 在语言模型蒸馏场景下的细化版本，但作者把它落到了 token-level KL 训练上，而不是只做行为克隆。19:T952,The core method is Interactive-Policy Distillation, or IPD. Instead of letting the student generate a full trajectory and querying the teacher afterward, or letting the teacher dominate the whole rollout, IPD uses a bidirectional propose-and-verify state machine. Student and teacher alternate between proposer and verifier roles. The current proposer emits tokens, and the verifier checks whether the trajectory remains within an acceptable region. If the student starts to drift, the teacher takes over and provides a corrective continuation. Once the trajectory returns to a region the student can plausibly continue from, control is handed back to the student. The resulting training sample is therefore a mixed-source trajectory, with some tokens proposed by the student and others by the teacher.

The loss is the most interesting part. Rather than applying one KL objective to the entire mixed trajectory, the paper splits supervision by token source. On student-generated positions, it uses reverse KL, which is more aligned with on-policy distillation: the student is trained on states it actually visits and is encouraged to concentrate on realizable modes instead of covering every teacher mode. On teacher-generated positions, it uses forward KL, effectively imitating the teacher’s corrective continuation. This source-split objective is the main methodological contribution because it combines on-policy and off-policy distillation within the same trajectory at token granularity, not just by mixing datasets.

The paper also includes a fused inference engine that co-hosts teacher and student in one serving instance, keeps separate KV caches, and schedules requests according to the current proposer/verifier roles. This is not the conceptual core, but it matters in practice because frequent teacher intervention would otherwise make rollout expensive.

Relative to prior OPD work, the differences are clear. Standard OPD assumes teacher supervision remains meaningful on student rollouts; IPD explicitly handles the case where that assumption breaks. Prior methods often mix on-policy and off-policy data at the sample level; IPD distinguishes them at the token level. And the teacher is not just a static labeler, but an interactive corrector. The overall flavor is reminiscent of DAgger-style correction, but adapted to token-level KL training for language models.1a:T683,The experiments focus on mathematical reasoning distillation across four teacher-student pairs. The main metrics are benchmark-averaged mean@8 and best@8 accuracy. The headline result is that IPD achieves the best average performance and leads on most individual benchmarks. The most concrete number given in the paper summary is on Qwen3-30B-A3B to Qwen3-1.7B-Base distillation: IPD surpasses the mean@8 of OPD trained for a full epoch, 279 steps, after only 60 training steps. That is a strong data-efficiency result.

The experimental story appears to support three claims. First, the gain is not merely from adding more teacher tokens, but from intervening when the student drifts, re-anchoring the trajectory, and then returning control.
