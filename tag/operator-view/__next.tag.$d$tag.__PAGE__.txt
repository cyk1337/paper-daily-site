9:T6a0,The paper models a token sequence as a vector field over the token-position graph. Each token position carries a feature fiber, and an attention update is written as a connection walk: a nonnegative walk matrix aggregates messages from neighbors, while each edge applies a linear transport map that moves features from the source fiber to the target fiber. Within this formulation, single-head attention is proved to be exactly a connection propagation step with constant transport. The walk is determined by attention weights, while the transport is shared across edges through the value/output linear maps.

The more important result is for multi-head attention. The paper shows that standard MHA can be rewritten exactly as a single edge-dependent connection walk. The effective transport on each edge is not fixed; it is an attention-gated mixture of the headwise transports induced by the value/output maps of different heads. This is the key structural clarification: MHA should not be interpreted only as separate heads followed by concatenation, but as one coupled operator whose edgewise transport depends on the attention pattern.

The paper then studies when the corresponding generator reduces to a random-walk connection Laplacian. This requires additional conditions, such as suitable normalization of the walk and constraints that make the layer update behave like a discrete propagation step. When those conditions hold, the layer dynamics align with diffusion on vector fields; when they do not, the operator is more general, potentially directed, asymmetric, and reaction-like.
