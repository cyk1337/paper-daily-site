9:T4f7,The method has two parts. FoldingCorpus is built from 1,200 proteins from the OpenFold monomer short-protein collection, split into 1,000 train, 100 dev, and 100 frozen-test proteins with cluster-disjoint partitions. It applies 12 deterministic structural operators to known coordinates, producing 14,400 question-answer records. The operators cover contacts, distance comparisons, segment orientation, center proximity, local direction, chirality, multi-constraint relations, and global-summary matching. Most answers are binary, two are three-way, and one is a 32-way textual summary match; each option is represented by a single vocabulary token. Fold2Reason freezes the base Qwen3.5-9B weights and trains LoRA parameters. The model reads a protein view with one marker per residue; coordinates are never given as input and are used only to define targets and losses. Discrete FoldingCorpus labels supervise the native language head. In parallel, a training-time workspace projects marker states to 256 dimensions, exchanges messages over local residue pairs and sampled long-range pairs, and feeds a frozen coordinate/distogram decoder.
