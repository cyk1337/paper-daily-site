18:T985,作者先把 training-free UE 系统化分成三类，再在统一实验框架下比较。

第一类是 verbalized uncertainty。模型直接用自然语言表达不确定性，文中研究了两种形式：一种是 abstention，让模型在不确定时输出 UNKNOWN；另一种是 numerical confidence，在答案旁边给出置信分数。优点是部署简单、对闭源模型也适用、几乎没有额外推理成本；缺点是完全依赖模型的自我校准能力。

第二类是 token-level uncertainty。作者采用自回归输出分布上的 entropy 和 negative log-likelihood，并对整段回答做 mean 与 max 聚合。mean 代表长度归一化的整体不确定性，max 试图抓住局部薄弱 token 的尖峰。文中一个重要点是：在 deterministic decoding 下，这类信号会明显退化；提高采样温度后，校准后的效果能恢复，甚至接近更昂贵的 semantic 方法。这个观察很关键，因为它说明 token-level UE 不是简单读取 logits 就够了，解码策略本身决定了你能不能看到有区分度的分布信息。

第三类是 semantic uncertainty。作者纳入 Semantic Entropy 及其多模态变体 VL-Uncertainty、VASE 一类方法。核心思想是多次采样得到多个回答，再通过双向 entailment 或扰动一致性把表面不同但语义相同的回答聚类，在语义簇上计算熵。这样可以避免把“同义改写”误判成高不确定性。代价也很明显：需要多次推理、额外 entailment 模型和聚类开销。

实验框架上，作者沿四个轴系统比较：模型家族、模型代际、模型规模、回答长度。模型覆盖 InternVL 与 QwenVL 两个家族，且各自包含两代和两个规模档，小模型约 2B，中等模型约 7B 到 8B。任务覆盖五个 VQA benchmark，包含多选、句子级和开放式回答。评估指标除了常见 AUROC、Coverage at Risk，还加入 CHDA。这个 calibrated hallucination detection accuracy 指标会先在一小部分数据上校准阈值，再看部署式检测效果，比纯离线排序指标更接近实际使用场景。

方法层面的贡献不在于发明新 UE 算法，而在于把不同范式放到同一坐标系里，并明确指出每类方法的适用边界。尤其是“输出长度决定哪一类方法更占优”这一点，解释了为什么过去很多结论彼此矛盾：它们常常只在短答案 VQA 上成立。19:Ta31,The paper organizes training-free UE into three families and evaluates them under one experimental protocol.

The first family is verbalized uncertainty. The model expresses uncertainty in natural language. The authors study two variants: abstention, where the model outputs UNKNOWN when uncertain, and numerical confidence, where it emits a confidence score alongside the answer. These methods are easy to deploy, work for both open and closed models, and add almost no inference cost. Their weakness is obvious: they rely entirely on the model’s self-calibration.

The second family is token-level uncertainty. The paper uses entropy and negative log-likelihood from the autoregressive output distribution, aggregated over the response by mean and max. Mean is intended as a length-normalized global uncertainty score, while max captures localized spikes from weakly grounded tokens. A key empirical point is that these signals degrade badly under deterministic decoding, but recover when estimated at higher temperature. That is an important observation because it shows that token-level UE is not just about reading logits; the decoding regime determines whether the uncertainty signal remains informative.

The third family is semantic uncertainty. The study includes Semantic Entropy and multimodal variants such as VL-Uncertainty and VASE. The idea is to sample multiple responses, cluster them by semantic equivalence using bidirectional entailment or perturbation-based consistency, and compute entropy over semantic classes rather than surface forms. This avoids treating paraphrases as uncertainty. The trade-off is higher cost: multiple generations, an entailment model, and clustering overhead.

The evaluation protocol is one of the paper’s main strengths. The comparison spans four axes: model family, generation, size, and response-length regime. The models come from two MLLM families, InternVL and QwenVL, each with two generations and two scales, roughly 2B and 7-8B. The tasks cover five VQA benchmarks spanning multiple-choice, sentence-length, and open-ended outputs. In addition to AUROC and Coverage at Risk, the paper uses CHDA, a calibrated hallucination detection accuracy metric that calibrates a threshold on a small subset before deployment-style evaluation. That makes the analysis more operational than standard ranking-only metrics.

The contribution is therefore not a new UE algorithm. It is a careful empirical map of where each UE family works, where it breaks, and why earlier conclusions from short-answer benchmarks do not transfer cleanly to broader MLLM settings.1a:T5fc,From the provided text, the confirmed setup includes two MLLM families, InternVL and QwenVL; two generations per family; two scale regimes, roughly 2B and 7-8B; and five VQA benchmarks covering multiple-choice, sentence-length, and open-ended response formats. The paper highlights four main findings. First, output length determines which UE family performs best; there is no single winner across all regimes. Second, token-level uncertainty collapses under deterministic decoding, but higher-temperature estimation restores calibrated signals that can rival much more expensive semantic methods. Third, verbalized self-uncertainty is unreliable in smaller and older MLLMs, but becomes much stronger in newer medium-scale models, with Qwen3-VL-Instruct called out explicitly. Fourth, even the better methods fail to achieve low risk at useful coverage, indicating that safe deployment of 2B to 8B MLLMs remains far from solved.

The excerpt does not include the full numeric tables, so it would be unsafe to quote exact margins. Still, the practical implications are already clear. For short-answer tasks, expensive semantic methods are not automatically justified. For long or open-ended outputs, plain token entropy is often unstable. Under mild assumptions that activated experts have comparable output norms and weak pairwise correlations, the RMS of the MoE output is largely governed by the sum of squared normalized routing weights. Reducing k removes lower-scored experts and re-normalizes the remaining weights, increasing this squared-weight concentration. This explains why smaller k amplifies the RMS and variance of the MoE output. To correct the mismatch, the paper proposes Layer-wise Distribution Alignment (LDA). Each model emission is mapped into one class using a fixed priority order: tool_call > refuse > ask > confirm > other. Tool calls are identified syntactically, while the other classes are detected through textual cues. The key metric is Gold Action Recall (GAR), computed at the case level with any-turn aggregation: if the model emits the gold action class at any turn in the case, the case counts as recalled. GAR ignores whether the full trajectory eventually passes. It only asks whether the model ever made the contextually appropriate action decision.

GAR is then paired with the benchmark’s conversation accuracy (Acc). If passing the benchmark truly requires taking the gold action, one should have Acc <= GAR. A violation, Acc > GAR, indicates grader masking: the benchmark passes trajectories that never emitted the required action class. Conversely, large slack, GAR >> Acc, indicates that the model selected the right action class but failed later in tool choice, arguments, or state progression. This decomposition turns a single end-to-end score into two approximately orthogonal failure modes.

To probe causality, the paper introduces two inference-time interventions. State-Reconciliation Intervention (SRI) targets missed tool calls by perturbing context when the gold action is tool_call but the model emitted none. Call-Reconciliation Intervention (CRI) targets unwarranted tool calls when the gold action is non-call. CRI has two variants: retry, which asks the model to re-decide and tests whether emissions move toward the gold class, and bypass, which avoids the unnecessary call without changing the emission itself. This is a strong design choice because it tests not only whether calibration is poor, but also whether it is plastic and whether fixing the decision layer actually improves trajectory outcomes.1f:T5dd,The main evaluation is on BFCL v3 multi-turn, with replication on tau-squared-bench retail and airline settings. The headline result is that some models with high aggregate accuracy have very low GAR on categories that require asking or refusing. The clearest example in the paper is xLAM: 71.8% aggregate accuracy, but only 19.5% GAR on miss_param, with a 64.5-point gap relative to size-matched peers, while BFCL’s state grader still marks many such cases as PASS. That directly supports the claim that aggregate accuracy hides action miscalibration.

The authors also run a 200-case manual audit and report inter-annotator agreement of kappa = 0.92, which strengthens confidence that the diagnostic is not an artifact of noisy labeling. The same pattern appears on a second benchmark, suggesting the issue is not specific to one grader implementation.

The intervention results are especially informative. First, calibration is plastic, but heterogeneously so: the same SRI perturbation can improve Acc for one model family and hurt another. Second, CRI cleanly separates two mechanisms. Retry often shifts emissions toward the gold action class, but tends to reduce final trajectory accuracy across nearly all families. Under an idealized conditional i.i.d. signal model, the paper derives a simple log-odds recursion and proves that the posterior process is a martingale. Second is the stopping decision.
