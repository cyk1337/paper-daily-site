9:T45a,The approach is continued-BPE tokenizer expansion. Starting from the 65K byte-level BPE tokenizer used by LFM2, the authors keep the existing merges fixed and append new merges learned on a multilingual corpus weighted toward under-tokenized languages, while retaining English and code to avoid losing compression on existing workloads. The expanded tokenizer has a 128K vocabulary. This construction gives two useful mapping properties: most old tokens survive as single tokens in the new vocabulary, and every newly added token has an exact decomposition into a sequence of old tokens. The model initialization follows this structure. Rows corresponding to carried-over tokens are copied directly. Rows for new tokens are initialized as the mean of the embeddings of their old-token decomposition. Adaptation then proceeds in two stages: first, train only the embedding-related parameters while freezing the model body; second, run full-model continued pretraining on a balanced multilingual mixture.
