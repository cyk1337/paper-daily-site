These curricula target different kinds of structure: hierarchical dependency tracking, latent transition rules and long-range interactions, reference-like declarative structure, and programmatic regularities. The experiments separate additive-compute from compute-matched training. In the additive setting, PPT is prepended before a fixed natural-language training budget. In the compute-matched setting, PPT replaces part of the natural-language training budget. The authors test data-constrained, data-unconstrained, and high token-to-parameter-ratio regimes. The setup includes 336M-parameter models and a larger 881M-parameter run trained on 50B tokens. Natural-language budgets include 50M, 100M, and 1B tokens, while PPT tokens are swept from 5M to 1B. In an NCA reproduction, the previously reported benefit appears under the original token-embedding initialization, but disappears when the embedding initialization is changed from N(0,1) to N(0,0.02).
