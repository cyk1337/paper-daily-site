9:T7f1,方法由四个部件组成。第一是参数分解视角。作者假设两个父模型 A、B 共享同一个 base model，后续专门化差异写成各自的 delta，merge 的目标不是直接平均完整权重，而是在 base 之上对 delta 做张量级重组。这比 model soup 式整体平均更细。第二是 MRI，Model-layer Response Importance。它给每个张量 T 打一个重要性分数，来源于两类信号：静态统计量和 probe 响应。静态部分聚合了归一化熵、方差、截断的 L2 norm；probe 部分则用一个小型校准集，比较 reasoning-conditioned activation 与 generic activation 的余弦距离，试图估计该张量对推理行为的敏感度。作者把两者按固定 α=0.5 混合，MRI 不是硬规则，而是 merge ratio 的软先验。第三是低维 genome 加进化搜索。Darwin 不直接为每个张量单独搜索一个系数，而是用 14 维 adaptive merge genome 编码 block-level 和 component-level 的合并策略，再通过 evolutionary search 做梯度无关优化。这一步的目的，是在极大的离散/连续混合 merge 空间里找到有效配置，同时避免参数维度过高导致搜索失效。第四是 MRI-Trust Fusion。作者没有让 MRI 完全决定 merge，也没有让进化搜索完全自由，而是引入一个可学习 trust 参数，把 MRI ratio 和 genome-derived ratio 融合成最终的 tensor-wise merge ratio。这样做的动机很清楚：诊断信号有偏差，纯搜索又容易样本效率差，二者结合更稳。除此之外，论文还加了 Architecture Mapper 处理异构父模型。它先在张量级建立对应关系，兼顾 tensor type、shape compatibility 和 depth alignment，再做数值重组。这一点使 Darwin 不局限于同架构 checkpoint soup，而是尝试跨家族 breeding。和 TIES、DARE、传统 model soup 相比，Darwin 的区别不在 merge kernel 本身多复杂，而在于它把“诊断先验 + 低维搜索 + 跨架构对齐”作为一个整体系统来设计。a:T4b2,Its value for pretraining research is indirect but real. The paper operationalizes a strong hypothesis: a nontrivial fraction of reasoning ability may already be formed during pretraining, while post-training often reorganizes or exposes that ability rather than creating it from scratch. Darwin is useful as an experimental probe for that hypothesis. Three implications matter. First, reasoning-relevant computation appears unevenly distributed across depth and tensor groups, which is relevant to selective updates, layerwise scaling, and mid-training interventions. Second, if merging specialized checkpoints works, then different post-training paths may be extracting complementary behaviors from a shared pretrained substrate rather than learning entirely separate capabilities. Third, cross-architecture breeding touches a deeper question about whether different model families converge to alignable functional decompositions. That matters for how we think about architecture choice versus objective choice in pretraining. In contribution scale, this is better viewed as an interesting post-training systems paper with pretraining implications, not as a direct change to the pretraining paradigm.b:T7d3,The method has four main pieces. First is the parameter decomposition view. Two parent models A and B are assumed to share a common base model, and their specialization is represented as deltas on top of that base. Darwin merges those deltas tensor-wise instead of averaging full weights, which is already more selective than model soups. Second is MRI, Model-layer Response Importance. For each tensor T, MRI combines static statistics and probe-based functional responses. The static term aggregates normalized entropy, variance, and capped L2 norm. The probe term uses a small calibration set and measures cosine distance between reasoning-conditioned activations and generic activations, aiming to estimate how sensitive that tensor is to reasoning behavior. The two are mixed with a fixed alpha of 0.5. MRI is used as a soft prior, not a hard merge rule. Third is a low-dimensional adaptive merge genome optimized by evolutionary search. Rather than assigning an independent coefficient to every tensor, Darwin encodes block-level and component-level merge decisions in a 14-dimensional genome and searches that space without gradients. This keeps the search tractable while still allowing nonuniform recombination. Fourth is MRI-Trust Fusion. Instead of fully trusting diagnostics or fully trusting search, Darwin learns a trust parameter that fuses MRI-derived ratios with genome-derived ratios into final tensor-wise merge coefficients. The motivation is straightforward: diagnostics are biased, but unconstrained search is sample-inefficient and unstable. The paper also introduces an Architecture Mapper for heterogeneous parents. It establishes tensor correspondences using tensor type, shape compatibility, and depth alignment before numerical recombination. Relative to model soups, TIES, or other selective merge methods, the novelty is less about the merge kernel itself and more about the system-level combination of diagnostics, low-dimensional search, and cross-architecture alignment.c:T659,From the provided full-text excerpt, the headline result is Darwin-27B-Opus reaching 86.9% on GPQA Diamond. The most important ablations are likely threefold. First, whether both parts of MRI matter: static statistics alone would reduce to a coarse magnitude heuristic, while probe-only signals may be noisy and calibration-set dependent. Second, whether the trust parameter actually helps balance diagnostics and search, rather than serving as a cosmetic interpolation term. Third, how much the Architecture Mapper contributes in heterogeneous settings, especially whether cross-architecture merging collapses without it. The role of the block/component-level genome also needs attention. The paper likely compares lower-capacity and higher-capacity encodings to show the trade-off between expressivity and search stability. The main caveat is that papers in this area are easy to overread from one benchmark number. The real question is robustness: does the gain hold across reasoning benchmarks, how sensitive is it to parent checkpoint choice, and does apparent accuracy improvement come with reduced faithfulness or transfer?
