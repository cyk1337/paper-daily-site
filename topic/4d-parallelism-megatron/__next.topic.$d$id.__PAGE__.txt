TP’s core communication is per-layer all-reduce / reduce-scatter / all-gather, with frequency scaling linearly with depth [Shoeybi2019Megatron]. MoE EP adds per-layer or per-block all-to-all, also a high-frequency primitive [Chowdhery2022PaLM]. Therefore TP/EP should default to the lowest-latency, highest-bandwidth inner ring (NVLink/NVSwitch); placing them across IB often turns “once per layer” latency accumulation into an end-to-end bottleneck.

In contrast, PP communication is stage-to-stage P2P; its frequency is set by stage count and microbatch scheduling, but it is not triggered per layer. This makes PP a better candidate for spanning IB. MegaScale codifies this hierarchy as a reproducible configuration—TP=8 within NVLink domains, PP over IB, DP/FSDP across pods—and reports 55.2% MFU for dense 175B at >10K GPUs [Jiang2024MegaScale]. The value is not that the number looks good, but that it provides a comparable debugging baseline: when MFU is far below the 40–55% band, first inspect mesh→topology mapping, collective implementations, and overlap before changing the model or blaming hardware [Jiang2024MegaScale].

8:T72e,把 SP 与 CP 统称为“长序列优化”会把工程路径带偏：SP 主要把 activation/重算压力从显存中移出，CP 则把 KV/attention 的数据分布形态扩展到多卡。Korthikanti et al. [Korthikanti2022SP] 的关键做法是：沿 sequence 切分部分 activation，并通过 all-gather/reduce-scatter 维持通信量级；再用 selective recomputation 将重算开销从约 36% 降到约 2% [Korthikanti2022SP]。因此，当 profiler 显示 attention 时间占比超过约 30% 时，SP 通常是“先做不后悔”的选择：它先解除显存与重算造成的 microbatch 约束，进而间接降低 PP bubble 与通信暴露。

但当 attention 占比继续上升（经验阈值 >50%）或 L≥32K，瓶颈会从“显存/重算”切换为“KV 必须分布式持有”。Ring Attention 沿 ring 传递 KV block，并与本地 attention 重叠 [Liu2023RingAttn]；Ulysses 用 head-axis all-to-all 组织 attention 的计算/通信 [Jacobs2023Ulysses]。两者都把 CP 提升为并行维度，而不是单卡 kernel 补丁：即使 FlashAttention-2/3 加速了单卡 attention [Dao2023FlashAttention2][Shah2024FlashAttention3]，一旦 KV 规模超过单卡可承载范围，跨设备 KV 分布与通信调度仍然必要。

USP 的贡献是把“选 Ring 还是 Ulysses”改写为“按拓扑参数选择通信形态”：由 head 数与 NVLink/IB 带宽比驱动选择或混合 [Fang2024USP]。这也说明 CP 应进入 mesh：一旦 CP 成为维度，拓扑映射（NVLink 内圈 vs IB 外圈）就能像 TP/PP 一样显式决策，而不是隐含在 kernel 中。当前公开证据仍缺少 Ring vs Ulysses vs USP/Untied-Ulysses 的系统性 breakpoint 图（按 L、heads、带宽比），因此更稳的阈值策略是用 attention 时间占比触发，而不是只看 L（见 open questions）。9:T80b,Treating SP and CP as the same class of “long-sequence optimizations” sends engineering down the wrong path: SP mainly moves activation/recompute pressure out of HBM, while CP moves the KV/attention data distribution across devices. The key move in Korthikanti et al. [Korthikanti2022SP] is to shard parts of activations along sequence while preserving communication order with all-gather/reduce-scatter, then use selective recomputation to cut recompute overhead from ~36% to ~2% [Korthikanti2022SP]. Therefore, when profiling shows attention time share above ~30%, SP is usually the “do it first” move: it removes microbatch limits caused by memory/recompute and indirectly reduces PP bubble and exposed communication.

When attention share rises further (a practical threshold is >50%) or L≥32K, the bottleneck shifts from “memory/recompute” to “KV must be held distributed.” Ring Attention passes KV blocks along a ring and overlaps them with local attention [Liu2023RingAttn]; Ulysses organizes attention compute/communication through head-axis all-to-all [Jacobs2023Ulysses]. Both promote CP to a parallel dimension rather than a single-device kernel patch: even if FlashAttention-2/3 accelerates single-device attention [Dao2023FlashAttention2][Shah2024FlashAttention3], once KV exceeds what a single device can hold, cross-device KV distribution and communication scheduling are still required.

USP’s contribution is to reframe “Ring vs Ulysses” as “choose the communication pattern by topology parameters,” with selection or mixing driven by head count and NVLink/IB bandwidth ratio [Fang2024USP]. This also explains why CP should enter the mesh: once CP is a dimension, topology placement (inner NVLink vs outer IB) becomes an explicit decision like TP/PP, rather than being buried inside kernels. Public evidence still lacks a systematic breakpoint map for Ring vs Ulysses vs USP/Untied-Ulysses (by L, heads, bandwidth ratio), so the more stable trigger is attention time share rather than L alone (see open questions).a:T694,PP debates used to stall at “are bubbles second-order.” A more reliable criterion is: when stage count P is large and microbatch count M is memory-limited, bubbles are a first-order loss; long context and large activations are often exactly what limits M. Zero Bubble Pipeline Parallelism gives a verifiable construction: split backward into input-grad and weight-grad stages and schedule them independently (ZB-H2), driving synchronous bubble to 0 under exact semantics [Qi2023ZeroBubble]. Thus, zero-bubble is closer to a “default schedule” than “extra complexity.”

Compared with earlier pipeline systems (e.g., PipeDream), the key is not async weights or consistency tradeoffs, but reordering after splitting the dependency graph, eliminating idle time under synchronous training semantics [Harlap2018PipeDream][Qi2023ZeroBubble]. Follow-up work further systematizes PP tuning: Pipeline Parallelism with Controllable Memory expresses schedules as composable building blocks and makes the memory-lifetime vs bubble tradeoff explicit [Qi2024ControllableMemoryPP]; Seq1F1B moves 1F1B’s unit to sequences, directly targeting pipeline efficiency under 32K–128K long context [Sun2024Seq1F1B].

In the 4D decision loop, a pragmatic default is: if PP is used, first treat zero-bubble as the baseline. If MFU is still below the healthy band, inspect whether PP partitioning conflicts with SP/CP-induced microbatch ceilings, and whether P2P has been mistakenly placed on outer topology (e.g., across pods).
