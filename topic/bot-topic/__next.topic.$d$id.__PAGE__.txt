Math-style reasoning usually has an automatic verifier and most errors live inside one chain of thought. Web, search, code-interpreter, and device-control tasks write actions into external state, and later observations change the policy distribution. The hard part of sparse reward is therefore not only sample efficiency; it is deciding which action should receive credit or blame.
