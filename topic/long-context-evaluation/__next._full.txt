After Kamradt 2023 made NIAH the de facto standard, nearly every ≥32K model from 2024 onward saturates NIAH near 100%, so treating its mean recall as the primary metric ranks models that are already tied at the ceiling [Kamradt2023NIAH]. RULER decomposes the task into retrieval, tracking, and aggregation pressures and exposes failure modes such as "nominal 128K performs like ~32K on compositional tasks," but the same 13 templates are now repeatedly visible in public SFT and distillation data, and by late 2025 frontier models showed signs of RULER itself saturating [Hsieh2024RULER]. For a pretraining team the actionable reporting format is to break the single number into four axes and co-report them: (a) length-stratified RULER + Loong + needle-threading covering retrieve / aggregate / reason [Hsieh2024RULER][Wang2024Loong][Roberts2024NeedleThreading]; (b) per-needle recall reported by position bin, not averaged [Liu2023LostInMiddle]; (c) decoding cost co-reported — KV footprint, decode latency, and sustained throughput at 128K, since GQA / MQA / MLA / sliding-window architectures show large latency gaps at equal recall [Ainslie2023GQA][DeepSeek2024MLA]; (d) train-eval contamination audits — rotate underlying templates every 4-6 months and publish n-gram overlap against public SFT mixes [Yuan2024LVEval][Kuratov2024BABILong].

Long-horizon agent evals — SWE-bench-Verified, Multi-SWE-bench, SWE-Lancer, METR Long-Horizon Tasks, terminal-bench, RepoBench — measure something different from NIAH/RULER: multi-turn tool use, repo navigation, planning, and retry policy [Chowdhury2024SWEBenchVerified][Xie2025MultiSWEBench][Anthropic2024SWELancer][METR2024LongHorizon][Liu2024RepoBench]. G1 · Kamradt 2023 把 single-needle 检索做成事实标准,现在更适合作最低门槛。机制是深度 × 长度 recall 热图,至今仍是长上下文报告的基础口径。但 ≥32K 模型在 NIAH 上从 2024 起几乎全数刷满 [Kamradt2023NIAH]。

G2 · Hsieh 2024 RULER 的价值是把 NIAH 的「能找到」拆成可控压力,而不是继续堆长度。相对 Kamradt 2023 NIAH,差异轴是 eval 口径: retrieve / variable tracking / aggregation / QA 四种受控压力,13 类任务模板;它修了 NIAH 的「单针 = 所有长上下文能力」偏差。代价是数据组成高度模板化,同一组 13 类模板在公开 SFT 数据里被反复暴露,2025 年底已经能在头部模型上看到 RULER 本身的饱和迹象 [Hsieh2024RULER]。

G3 · LongBench v2 [Bai2024LongBenchV2] 与 Loong [Wang2024Loong] 这一路线的早期形态是 An 2023 L-Eval（16K 量级、20+ 任务）[An2023LEval]；LV-Eval、HELMET、LongBench v2 都是它的延续。这里的判断是用真实任务对抗模板穿透。相对 Hsieh 2024 RULER，差异轴不在 loss，而在数据组成与 eval 口径：真实任务 + 人工验证答案，用非模板化问题降低模板记忆收益。代价是 scale 受限（LongBench v2 只有 503 题）与可分桶性下降，真实任务很难像 RULER 那样按 retrieve / aggregate / reason 干净分离。

G4 · LV-Eval [Yuan2024LVEval]、HELMET [Yen2024HELMET]、BABILong [Kuratov2024BABILong] 这一族的价值是把「长 ≠ 难」的混淆做成显式设计。相对 Hsieh 2024 RULER,差异轴是 eval 口径与 scale: LV-Eval 同一题 5 档长度,HELMET 7 个应用域 × 多个长度桶,BABILong 把 bAbI 已知最小 reasoning 链嵌入任意长 distractor;这一代把「length-stratified」从好习惯升级成默认报告口径。代价是评测矩阵单元数量爆炸,完整跑一次需要 12-30 小时 GPU。

G5 · MRCR / Latent List [Vodrahalli2024MRCR]、Counting-Stars [Song2024CountingStars]、NoCha [Karamcheti2024NoCha] 的价值是填上 NIAH 已饱和、SWE-bench 又过重的中间带。相对 Kamradt 2023 NIAH,差异轴是 eval 口径:从显式检索转到 latent 结构 reasoning 与多证据聚合;相对 SWE-bench,差异轴是 scale 与运行成本。这是当前(2025-2026)评测最缺、也最值得投资的位置。

**判断**:对 pretraining team,要做的不是选一代代替另一代,而是在 G2 / G4 / G5 各挑一个评测固定下来,用三条轴同时上报。NIAH 单独报告已经没有信号,但作为下界校验仍可保留(确认 ≥32K 还没满分以前不要声称长上下文能力)。19:Ta3d,Conclusion first: long-context evaluation has gone through five generations since 2023's single-needle NIAH, each fixing a specific failure mode of the previous one at the cost of harness complexity and contamination surface. Understanding this arc is what lets you judge which benchmarks are now near saturation.

G1 · Kamradt 2023 made single-needle retrieval the de facto standard. The depth × length recall heatmap remains the minimum reporting format, but ≥32K models saturate NIAH near 100% from 2024 onward [Kamradt2023NIAH].

G2 · Hsieh 2024 RULER decomposes "can find" into retrieve / variable tracking / aggregation / QA pressures across 13 task families. It fixes the NIAH bias of treating single-needle as the only signal, at the cost of structured templates — the same 13 templates have been exposed repeatedly in public SFT data, and by late 2025 RULER itself shows saturation on frontier models [Hsieh2024RULER].

G3 · LongBench v2 [Bai2024LongBenchV2] and Loong [Wang2024Loong] extend this lineage; its earlier form is An 2023 L-Eval (~16K, 20+ tasks) [An2023LEval], with LV-Eval, HELMET, and LongBench v2 as continuations. These benchmarks take a different route: realistic tasks with human-verified answers as a defence against template leakage. The cost is scale (LongBench v2 has only 503 items) and reduced bin-ability — realistic tasks are hard to cleanly factor into retrieve / aggregate / reason axes the way RULER can.

G4 · LV-Eval [Yuan2024LVEval], HELMET [Yen2024HELMET], BABILong [Kuratov2024BABILong] make the "length ≠ difficulty" confusion an explicit part of the design: LV-Eval runs the same instance at 5 lengths, HELMET reports 7 application domains across multiple length buckets, BABILong embeds bAbI's known-minimal reasoning chain inside arbitrary distractor. This generation upgrades length stratification from "good practice" to default reporting format. The cost is matrix-cell explosion — a full run takes 12-30 GPU hours.

G5 · MRCR / Latent List [Vodrahalli2024MRCR], Counting-Stars [Song2024CountingStars], and NoCha [Karamcheti2024NoCha] push pressure to latent-structure reasoning and multi-evidence aggregation, filling the gap between saturated NIAH and over-heavy SWE-bench. This is the most under-invested but highest-signal region as of 2025-2026.

**Judgement**: a pretraining team should not pick one generation over another — pick one benchmark from G2, G4, and G5 each and run all three. NIAH on its own has no signal left but is still worth keeping as a lower bound (do not claim long-context capability before saturating ≥32K on NIAH first).1a:Tab2,单一长上下文分数不适合作为 pretraining dashboard 的主指标。模型卡 / 公开 leaderboard 默认把长上下文压成一个数字,等价于把 capability、depth、length × position、cost 四条独立轴做随机投影。可控维度需要拆开报,否则定位不到训练数据、loss 或系统实现的问题。

**How to do it**. For a fixed length L, slice the context into at least 5 normalized-depth bins: [0, 0.2), [0.2, 0.4), [0.4, 0.6), [0.6, 0.8), [0.8, 1.0]. Repeat across length bins. The output is not "this model recalls 0.62 at 128K" but a 5×5 length × position recall matrix in which the U-shape's depth and position become comparable.

**Why per-needle rather than per-question**. When a question embeds multiple needles, scoring only correct / incorrect mixes retrieval signal across positions. Counting-Stars [Song2024CountingStars] decomposes multi-needle answers down to per-needle hits, which is currently the cleanest aggregation signal — it tells you "5 out of 8 stars found, 2 of the 3 misses are in the middle bin" rather than just "final count is wrong."

**Mechanism evidence attaches to behavior**. **The goldfish-loss position view**.
