15:T5a5,The first non-comparability is units: PPL is the exponentiated negative log-likelihood per token, and the tokenizer defines the token. A direct implication of Mielke et al. [Mielke2021TokenizationHistory] is that, for the same text, finer tokens often make per-token prediction “easier” and lower raw PPL, without implying stronger language modeling capability. Multilingual training/evaluation reports repeatedly hit this issue: BLOOM treats cross-language evaluation and data coverage as first-class concerns, but metric interpretation still requires a shared baseline over languages and segmentation.[BigScience2022BLOOM][McMillanMajor2022BigScienceCatalogue] The second non-comparability is objective mismatch: alignment stages optimize preferences or safety constraints, not next-token loss. Bai et al. [Bai2022RLHF] and Bai et al. [Bai2022ConstitutionalAI] show clear shifts along preference/harmlessness dimensions; Rafailov et al. [Gao2022RewardOveroptimization] warns that pushing a proxy to the extreme can hurt true preference quality, so stage 2 needs not only different metrics but also guardrails against single-metric overfitting. 17:T942,可执行的清单（带边界条件）：
1) 训练 dashboard 保留 loss/log PPL 作为最左侧信号，但只授权它做训练内闭环：early-stop、异常检测、数据混合/修剪、compute allocation。前提必须同时满足：同 tokenizer、同目标函数、同模型家族。[Kaplan2020ScalingLaws][Hoffmann2022Chinchilla]
2) 做 compute-optimal 拟合时，必须把训练 regime 写进实验卡：是否 overtrain、token budget cap、停训准则；输出必须包含拟合不确定性（例如对最优 token:param 比率给区间），不接受“单条曲线给单点最优”。[Porian2024ResolvingDiscrepancies][Gadre2024OvertrainingDownstream]
3) 任何跨 tokenizer/跨语言对外比较，禁止只报 raw PPL；最低要求：BPB 或信息归一化 + 语言均衡任务面板。若做不到 tokenizer-controlled，对外只给定性结论，不给精确排序。[Mielke2021TokenizationHistory][BigScience2022BLOOM][Liang2022HELM]
4) “是否继续训练/是否加算力”用逐任务缩放律而不是 PPL 代理：优先覆盖 5–15 个最关键任务，记录外推误差并把误差当成停训门槛的一部分。[Isik2024DownstreamScalingLaws][Bhagia2024TaskScalingLadders]
5) 遇到 inverse scaling/U-shape 的任务，不要用“平均分”掩盖；把这些任务标成 release blocker 或单独分桶，直到找到数据/提示/评测协议的可控解释。[McKenzie2023InverseScaling][Wei2022UShapedInverseScaling]

1) Keep loss/log PPL as the leftmost dashboard signal, but authorize it only for in-loop control: early stopping, anomaly detection, data mixing/pruning, compute allocation. Preconditions: same tokenizer, same objective, same model family.[Kaplan2020ScalingLaws][Hoffmann2022Chinchilla]
2) For compute-optimal fitting, write the training regime into the experiment card: overtraining or not, token budget caps, stopping criteria; outputs must include fit uncertainty (e.g., an interval for the optimal token:param ratio), not a single-point optimum from a single curve.[Porian2024ResolvingDiscrepancies][Gadre2024OvertrainingDownstream]
3) For any external comparison across tokenizers/languages, do not report raw PPL alone; minimum: BPB or information-normalized metrics plus a language-balanced task panel. 4) Use per-task scaling laws—not PPL proxies—for keep-training/scale-up decisions: prioritize 5–15 critical tasks, record extrapolation error, and treat error as part of the stopping threshold.[Isik2024DownstreamScalingLaws][Bhagia2024TaskScalingLadders]
5) For tasks showing inverse scaling/U-shapes, do not hide them behind an average score; mark them as release blockers or separate buckets until a controllable explanation (data/prompt/protocol) is found.[McKenzie2023InverseScaling][Wei2022UShapedInverseScaling]
6) Do not use PPL as a safety net for compression/pruning acceptance: require per-task regression tests and additionally monitor behavior-distribution drift (e.g., JS divergence or equivalent drift metrics), using drift as a rollback trigger.[KhanalCapone2024CompressionTasks]
