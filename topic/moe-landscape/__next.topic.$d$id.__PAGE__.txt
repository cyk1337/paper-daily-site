f:T47b,A tighter reading is that the DeepSeek template wins by moving MoE failures into early observable signals. Structurally, it uses fine-grained routed experts (start at 64–128; move to 256 only when capacity is insufficient) plus one shared expert to strip common patterns out of routed experts, reducing expert similarity and specialization failures.[Dai2024DeepSeekMoE][DeepSeekAI2024V2] For balancing, it replaces aux loss with bias EMA, decoupling balancing from the main gradients to avoid balancing at the expense of representation learning, while reducing implementation sensitivity.[Wang2024AuxFree][Qiu2025DemonsLBL][DeepSeekAI2024V3] For stability, it makes token drop, usage CV, and dead-expert counts hard gates in the first ~2000 steps: once these go unstable early, recovery usually requires large LR reductions, parallelism changes, or restarts—expensive and unpredictable.[DeepSeekAI2024V3][Fedus2021Switch][Lepikhin2020GShard] This also explains why z-loss stays: it addresses router-logit overflow as a numerical-stability issue, orthogonal to balancing objectives, and acts as a low-cost fuse.[Zoph2022STMoE][DeepSeekAI2024V3]10:T547,The core issue with auxiliary load-balancing loss is not the objective (more uniform usage), but that it writes the balancing signal into main gradients, producing two side effects.
