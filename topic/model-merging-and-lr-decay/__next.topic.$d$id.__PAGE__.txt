9:Tb5c,判断先说在前面:cosine / WSD cooldown 与 PMA / SWA / EMA 这一族 trajectory averaging 共享同一种 weighted-average 结构 — 「pretrain-time merging」不是什么孤立 trick,而是 Polyak-Juditsky 平均在这条线上的工程再包装: Izmailov 2018 SWA、Tarvainen 2017 Mean Teacher EMA、Kaddour 2022 LAWA、ByteDance Seed 2025 PMA、Meterez 2026 Anytime Pretraining 等都把 trajectory iterate 按一组权重平均后再评估或续训 [Izmailov2018SWA][Tarvainen2017MeanTeacher][Kaddour2022LAWA][Li2025PMAPretrain][Meterez2026Anytime]。它们与 cosine / WSD cooldown 互为对偶: cooldown 阶段每一步的有效贡献被 η_t 加权,等价于在 trajectory 上跑一次 EMA。Li 2025 PMA 在 411M 到 200B-MoE 的实测显示,在 constant-LR 阶段做 weighted average 可以「预测」cooldown 之后的 loss,这是这组对偶关系最直接的证据 [Li2025PMAPretrain]。Defazio 2024 Schedule-Free 与 Meterez 2026 Anytime 则把这条对偶推进到 horizon-free 设计: constant LR + tail averaging 在 1×–32× Chinchilla 上与 cosine 拟合的 envelope 几乎重合 [Defazio2024ScheduleFree][Meterez2026Anytime]。

Post-training 的 model merging 是另一类问题。Wortsman 2022 Model Soups、Ilharco 2023 Task Arithmetic、Yadav 2023 TIES、Yu 2024 DARE 处理的是跨独立 fine-tuning 路径的参数组合,不是 trajectory 内的加权平均 [Wortsman2022ModelSoups][Ilharco2023TaskArith][Yadav2023TIES][Yu2024DARE]。两类方法在 loss landscape 上作用不同: pretrain merging 沿同一 basin 的 SGD 轨迹做 EMA,post-train merging 跨 basin 做 task vector 加减;DARE / TIES 的稀疏化,就是为缓解跨 basin 时 sign 冲突带来的相互抵消。需要保留的 caveat 是,把两类方法混在一起,很容易得出「merging 总能涨点」或「merging 必然丧失多样性」这类过度泛化结论。

对 pretraining 团队,可执行口径是把 merging 当成两条独立信号源来用: (a) constant-LR 阶段的 PMA / EMA 既是 cooldown 之后 loss 的 proxy,也可直接用于 ablation,每改一次配方都不必重跑 cooldown [Li2025PMAPretrain][Sanyal2023EarlyAvg];(b) cooldown 末的 EMA 与 last-iterate 应同时上报,两者差距就是 averaging gain,通常在 0.02-0.08 validation NLL; (c) eval-loss 与下游 few-shot 不应假设同步,Hu 2024 MiniCPM 的「cooldown 解锁下游能力」与 Li 2025 PMA 的「constant 阶段 average 已经能预测 cooldown loss」对应的是不同机制,前者落在下游能力解锁,后者落在 loss 预测,因此实验上应把 LR cooldown 与 trajectory average 分别消融 [Hu2024MiniCPMWSD][Li2025PMAPretrain]; (d) Boyd 的 EWMM 与 Press 的 NumRec 条目给了 weighted average 的统计解释,窗口 / 衰减率就是 bias-variance trade-off 的调参项,不要把 SWA / EMA / LAWA 当成「玄学加成」 [Boyd2013EWMM][Press1992NumRec][Hansen2015NonParametrics]。a:Tbbb,Start from the structural claim: cosine / WSD cooldown and PMA / SWA / EMA-style trajectory averaging implement the same weighted-average geometry — pretrain-time "merging" is not an isolated hack but Polyak-Juditsky averaging under different wrappers (Izmailov 2018 SWA, Tarvainen 2017 Mean Teacher EMA, Kaddour 2022 LAWA, ByteDance Seed 2025 PMA, Meterez 2026 Anytime Pretraining all average trajectory iterates before evaluation or continued training) [Izmailov2018SWA][Tarvainen2017MeanTeacher][Kaddour2022LAWA][Li2025PMAPretrain][Meterez2026Anytime]. The part worth re-deriving every time is the duality with cosine / WSD cooldown: the η_t weighting along the cooldown tail is the same weighted average. Li 2025 PMA shows on dense models from 411M to 200B-MoE that a weighted average during the constant-LR phase *predicts* the loss after cooldown, which is the most direct experimental evidence of the duality [Li2025PMAPretrain]. Defazio 2024 Schedule-Free and Meterez 2026 Anytime push the duality to horizon-free designs: constant LR with tail averaging tracks the cosine envelope across 1×–32× Chinchilla [Defazio2024ScheduleFree][Meterez2026Anytime].

Post-training model merging is a different object. Wortsman 2022 Model Soups, Ilharco 2023 Task Arithmetic, Yadav 2023 TIES, and Yu 2024 DARE combine parameters across independent fine-tuning paths, not iterates within one trajectory [Wortsman2022ModelSoups][Ilharco2023TaskArith][Yadav2023TIES][Yu2024DARE]. The two operate on different objects in loss landscape: pretrain merging walks along one SGD basin doing an EMA; post-train merging crosses basins via task-vector arithmetic. The sparsification in DARE / TIES exists specifically to mitigate the sign interference that emerges when crossing basins. Conflating the two leads to over-generalisations like "merging always wins" or "merging must destroy diversity."

For a pretraining team the actionable framing is to treat merging as two independent signal channels: (a) constant-LR PMA / EMA is both a proxy for post-cooldown loss and an ablation lever (no need to re-run cooldown for every recipe change) [Li2025PMAPretrain][Sanyal2023EarlyAvg]; (b) at the end of cooldown, report EMA and last-iterate together — the gap is the averaging gain, typically 0.02–0.08 validation NLL; (c) do not assume eval-loss and downstream few-shot move together — Hu 2024 MiniCPM's "cooldown unlocks downstream capability" and Li 2025 PMA's "averaging during constant LR predicts cooldown loss" jointly imply that loss improvement and downstream unlock are separable mechanisms; the experimental design that decouples them is to ablate LR cooldown and trajectory averaging independently [Hu2024MiniCPMWSD][Li2025PMAPretrain]; (d) Boyd's EWMM and Press's Numerical Recipes give the statistical name to weighted averaging — tune the window / decay rate as a bias-variance trade-off, do not treat SWA / EMA / LAWA as folk magic [Boyd2013EWMM][Press1992NumRec][Hansen2015NonParametrics].b:Te90,结论先行:从 Polyak-Juditsky 1992 averaged SGD 起,model merging 分成两条常被混淆、但机制不同的演进线:trajectory-internal averaging(SWA / EMA / LAWA / PMA / Anytime)与cross-basin task-vector arithmetic(Soup / Task Arith / TIES / DARE / SALSA)。先把这两条线分开,后面的讨论才不会缠在一起。

**线 A · trajectory-internal averaging**。这条线始终是在一条 SGD 轨迹上做 weighted average,变的是窗口形状与权重,不是对象本身。Tarvainen 2017 Mean Teacher 把 EMA-on-weights 从优化 trick 变成 semi-supervised 的核心机制 [Tarvainen2017MeanTeacher];Izmailov 2018 SWA 用最后 25% iterate 的 uniform mean 建立 vision 基线 [Izmailov2018SWA];Kaddour 2022 LAWA 把 SWA 的尾段平均改成滑窗,支持在线使用 [Kaddour2022LAWA];Sanyal 2023 直接反驳 SWA 的「只看尾段」直觉,证明高 LR 阶段的间隔抽样平均更有效 [Sanyal2023EarlyAvg];Defazio 2024 Schedule-Free 把 tail averaging 写进优化器内核,不再依赖 LR scheduler [Defazio2024ScheduleFree];Li 2025 PMA 在 411M-70B dense 与 200B-MoE 规模上验证 constant-LR PMA 可以预测 cooldown 后的 loss [Li2025PMAPretrain];Meterez 2026 Anytime 给出 horizon-free schedule × averaging 的最优收敛理论 [Meterez2026Anytime]。caveat 也很明确:这条线讨论的是同一训练轨迹上的平均,差异轴是尾段 vs 滑窗 vs 全程、uniform vs EMA vs schedule,不是跨独立 fine-tune 路径的参数运算。

**线 B · cross-basin task arithmetic**。这条线始终是在独立 fine-tuning 路径之间做 task-vector 加减,变的是稀疏化策略与应用槽位。Wortsman 2022 Model Soups 把 averaging 从「同一轨迹」推到「同 init、不同 HP 的 fine-tune」,给出 uniform / greedy 两种 recipe [Wortsman2022ModelSoups];Dansereau 2023 复现实验后指出,增益强依赖 pretrained init 的 connectivity,ViT 有效,ResNet / EfficientNet 弱 [Dansereau2023ModelSoupCV];Ilharco 2023 把 Δθ_t = θ_FT − θ_pre 命名为「task vector」,定义加 / 减 / analogy 三种运算 [Ilharco2023TaskArith];Yadav 2023 在 task vector 数量 ≥ 4 时把性能下降定位为 sign 干扰,提出 TRIM-ELECT-DISJOINT [Yadav2023TIES];Yu 2024 DARE 进一步把 sparsification 简化成「random drop + 1/(1-p) rescale」,说明 fine-tune delta 在坐标维度上高度冗余 [Yu2024DARE];Chegini 2024 SALSA 把同样的 soup 思路推进到 RLHF 的 reference 模型槽位 [Chegini2024SALSA]。caveat 同样直接:这条线的对象是跨独立 fine-tune 的 delta,不是同一 SGD 轨迹上的 iterate 平均。

**两条线的连接点是 Wortsman 2022**。Model Soup 在表达上确实是桥:uniform / greedy 既可读成「同一 init 下的 trajectory ensemble」,对应线 A 的延伸,只是 trajectory 不再是 SGD 步而是多条 fine-tune;也可读成「task vector 的 0/1 selection」,对应线 B 的退化形式,因为所有 task vector 系数都是 0 或 1 [Wortsman2022ModelSoups]。机制上正因为有这层双重解释,soup 很容易被工程实践误用。caveat 是 Wortsman 2022 的适用前提一直是「同 init」;把这个前提丢掉,直接跨 init 求平均,失败后再归因为「averaging 不行」,归因就错了。

Keeping the two apart is the prerequisite for everything that follows.

**Line A · trajectory-internal averaging.** Tarvainen 2017 Mean Teacher promoted EMA-on-weights from an optimization trick to the core mechanism of semi-supervised consistency learning [Tarvainen2017MeanTeacher]; Izmailov 2018 SWA made "uniform mean of the last 25% iterates" a vision baseline [Izmailov2018SWA]; Kaddour 2022 LAWA turned SWA's tail mean into a sliding-window mean usable mid-training [Kaddour2022LAWA]; Sanyal 2023 inverted SWA's tail-only intuition by showing widely-spaced averaging during the high-LR phase is *more* effective [Sanyal2023EarlyAvg]; Defazio 2024 Schedule-Free folded tail averaging into the optimizer kernel and removed the LR scheduler [Defazio2024ScheduleFree]; Li 2025 PMA demonstrated at 411M-70B dense + 200B-MoE scale that constant-LR PMA predicts post-cooldown loss [Li2025PMAPretrain]; Meterez 2026 Anytime gave the optimal convergence theory for horizon-free schedules with weight averaging [Meterez2026Anytime]. The kernel does not change across this line — all are weighted averages along a single SGD trajectory; what changes is window shape (tail vs sliding vs whole-run) and weighting (uniform vs EMA vs schedule).

**Line B · cross-basin task arithmetic.** Wortsman 2022 Model Soups pushed averaging from one trajectory to multiple fine-tunes sharing the same init, with uniform and greedy recipes [Wortsman2022ModelSoups]; Dansereau 2023's replication showed gains depend strongly on pretrained init connectivity — strong on ViT, weak on ResNet / EfficientNet [Dansereau2023ModelSoupCV]; Ilharco 2023 named Δθ_t = θ_FT − θ_pre the "task vector" with add / subtract / analogy operations [Ilharco2023TaskArith]; the ≥ 4 task vector degradation was localized by Yadav 2023 TIES to sign interference, with the TRIM-ELECT-DISJOINT recipe [Yadav2023TIES]; Yu 2024 DARE further reduced sparsification to "random drop + 1/(1-p) rescale," showing fine-tune deltas are highly redundant per-coordinate [Yu2024DARE]; Chegini 2024 SALSA pushed the same soup idea into RLHF's reference-model slot [Chegini2024SALSA]. The kernel here also does not change — all are task-vector arithmetic across independent fine-tuning paths; what changes is the sparsification strategy and the slot of application.

**Wortsman 2022 is the connecting point.** Soup's uniform / greedy can be read either as "trajectory ensemble with same init" (extension of line A, where trajectory is a set of fine-tunes rather than SGD steps) or as "0/1 selection of task vectors" (degenerate case of line B, with all task-vector coefficients in {0, 1}). This dual reading makes soup the bridge between the two lines, but engineering-wise it is often over-generalised — the same-init premise is dropped, cross-init averaging then fails, and the failure is wrongly attributed to "averaging doesn't work."

**Judgement**: classify the merging literature by trajectory-internal vs cross-basin before anything else. Any argument that treats SWA and Task Arithmetic as the same kind of technique has already lost the critical distinction. **(1) 数学:η_t 加权 ≡ EMA**。SGD 更新 θ_t = θ_{t-1} − η_t·g_t,展开后 θ_T = θ_0 − Σ_{t=1}^T η_t·g_t。当 η_t 在 cooldown 段按 cosine / linear 衰减,后期 step 对最终 θ_T 的贡献会被 η_t 显著重加权(以最终时刻为锚的 effective weight 是 ∏_{s>t}(1 − η_s·…) 的级数)。这组 effective weight 与 EMA β = 1 − 1/N 的几何衰减权重在 spectrum 上几乎重合。Boyd 的 EWMM、Press 的 NumRec smoothing、Orbak 的 EWMA 控制图讲义都把这种「指数 vs 几何」的 bias-variance 权衡形式化 [Boyd2013EWMM][Press1992NumRec][Orbak2007EWMA]。Wang 2024 FWA 进一步把 SWA / EMA / LAWA 放进同一收敛框架,证明窗口 k ∈ [1, T/2] 都能拿到 log(T/k) 改进,且与 LR schedule 解耦 [Wang2024FWA]。边界也明确:这里说的是 weighted-average 结构上的对偶,不是逐步更新轨迹的逐点恒等。
