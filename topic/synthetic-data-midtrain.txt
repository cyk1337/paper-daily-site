1c:T5ae,To make collapse operational, first separate replacement from accumulation. MAD [MAD2023] gives the mechanism: once training data is recursively replaced by model-generated data, degradation appears first in tail distributions (mode dropping), because tail samples are scarce and are more easily averaged out during replacement. In contrast, [CollapseInevitable2024] gives a more engineering-facing boundary: as long as real data continues to accumulate (rather than being replaced), recursive training degradation is not inevitable. Together they define a robust red line: do not adopt “pure synthetic replacing the real pool.” The more industrially usable policy is to keep real data as a persistent anchor, use synthetic as an incremental layer for densification and coverage filling, and express pool update rules as auditable ratio and dedup policies.[MAD2023][CollapseInevitable2024] This also explains why “synthetic ratio can grow without bound” lacks public support: current evidence mainly shows that risk can be controlled under specific conditions (accumulate real, or verifier-strong subdomains), not that the claim holds for arbitrary tasks and generation methods.[DeepSeekMath2024][LetsVerify2023] For monitoring, average loss is insufficient; track tail coverage separately, including low-frequency languages/domains, long documents, rare formats, and whether they are squeezed out after mid-train.[Llama3Herd2024][DataCompLM2024]1d:T519,Public evidence supports a domain-split conclusion: synthetic scales more sustainably in math/code because correctness signals are harder and more automatable.[DeepSeekMath2024] connects executable verification to the synthetic pipeline, turning “sample quality” from teacher preference into decidable constraints; [RLTF2023] does the same with unit tests; [LetsVerify2023] uses step-level verification as process supervision to reduce local error accumulation in multi-step reasoning. In contrast, natural-language reasoning and agentic trajectories lack comparably strong verifiers: even stronger teachers can mistake preference for correctness, which narrows style and stably replicates error modes.[MAD2023][Cosmopedia2024] This does not mean NL cannot use synthetic; it means recipes should be framed as shaping: rephrase to preserve factual anchors, multi-style to resist narrowing, and continuous real-data accumulation as a tail anchor.[WRAP2024][CollapseInevitable2024] A pragmatic engineering boundary: when tasks provide executable/decidable feedback, allow higher synthetic ratios and allocate more mid-train compute; when feedback is mostly preference or weak proxies, keep synthetic for densification/coverage fill rather than as unbounded primary fuel.[DeepSeekMath2024][DataCompLM2024]1e:T77f,可操作清单（do/don’t）：

2) Don’t：在 backbone 明显 undertrained 时讨论 mid-train 或 synthetic 配方归因；先用 compute-optimal/训练轨迹把底座训到位，否则收益会与“补足基础训练”混在一起。[Chinchilla2022][ScalingLaws2020]
3) Do：把 synthetic 的默认角色设为 distribution shaping；优先选 rephrase 或 seed 约束生成，保留真实内容锚点，降低 hallucination 与文风收缩风险。[WRAP2024][Cosmopedia2024]
4) Do：把“更干净的数据”优先交给 filtering/ranking；只有当候选池 coverage 不足时才引入 synthetic 去补齐稀缺分布。[DataCompLM2024][RefinedWeb2023]
5) Don’t：不要做 replace real 的递归训练；数据池策略用 accumulate-not-replace，训练池中真实数据长期保留，经验下限先不低于 ~30%，并对长尾域单独做采样与评估看护。[MAD2023][CollapseInevitable2024]
