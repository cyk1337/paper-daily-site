1a:T62d,Attention optimization is often misread as “whose kernel is faster”, but the more stable engineering decomposition has four layers. Layer 1 is the dataflow of exact attention: FA1 [Dao2022FA1] casts the bottleneck as HBM I/O and uses tiling + online softmax to avoid materializing the L×L score matrix; the numerical mechanics of online softmax trace back to Milakov et al. [Milakov2018OnlineSoftmax], and the claim that exact attention need not use O(n^2) memory is algorithmically valid [Rabe2021NoQuadraticMemory]. Layer 2 is parallelism and scheduling: FA2 [Dao2023FA2] shows that, with unchanged math, work partitioning and parallel granularity can create near-2× throughput differences. Layer 3 is hardware mapping: FA3 [Shah2024FA3] integrates TMA, warp specialization, and FP8 tensor-core matmul into fused attention, moving the critical path from memory-bound toward compute-bound. Layer 4 is systems and semantics: serving bottlenecks often sit in decode parallelism and KV cache, where Flash-Decoding [Dao2023FlashDecoding] and FlashInfer [Ye2024FlashInfer][Ye2025FlashInferEngine] operationalize “chunk KV in parallel + reduce”; for variants, lift semantics first into FlexAttention [Dong2024Flex], and drop into specialized paths such as FlashMask [Wang2024FlashMask] or RingAttention [Liu2023RingAttention] only when mask/score semantics change tiling/memory access or require distributed blockwise execution. FA2 [Dao2023FA2] attributes most gains to work partitioning: more parallelism along the sequence dimension, lower non-matmul overhead, and better intra/inter-block load balance; these gains tend to transfer across models. FA3 [Shah2024FA3] binds gains more tightly to Hopper hardware features: TMA makes data movement more asynchronous, warp specialization decouples movement from compute, and FP8 tensor cores push matmul to higher throughput; this also explains why, under the same exact attention semantics, H100/H200 defaults lean toward FA3 while A100/H20 defaults lean toward FA2. By contrast, FlexAttention [Dong2024Flex] lifts variant expression into score_mod/mask_mod and lets torch.compile generate fused kernels, with the goal of shifting the cost of “writing variants” from CUDA maintenance to Python semantic maintenance. The real tradeoff is engineering economics: when training sits at a tight batch/seq margin and 10% throughput is cost, FA3’s generation-specific specialization is more likely to pay off; when mask/score variants iterate frequently and deployment spans hardware, FlexAttention’s unified entry point is steadier. The operational test is profiling: if attention is already near compute-bound (the Hopper case reported by FA3 [Shah2024FA3]), further peak chasing usually leaves only 10–20% marginal gains, and engineering effort more often moves to recomputation, parallelism strategy, or data pipelines. Conversely, if attention remains a dominant hotspot and hardware is fixed to Hopper, FA3’s bespoke implementation can still be the lowest-cost path.1d:T5ac,The shared premise in serving is that decode seriality and KV-cache read/write patterns set the real throughput ceiling; peak attention-kernel throughput is usually not the first bottleneck. Flash-Decoding [Dao2023FlashDecoding] gives a clean mechanism: in single-token decode, Q has a tiny batch dimension, and training-shaped kernels expose too little parallelism, leaving SMs idle; chunking KV along the sequence dimension, computing partial attention in parallel, then reducing restores usable parallelism. FlashInfer [Ye2024FlashInfer] consolidates these mechanisms behind a library interface, covering paged KV, GQA/MQA, multiple KV layouts, and prefill/decode transitions; the updated version positions it explicitly as an attention engine [Ye2025FlashInferEngine], where configurability matters more than single-shape peak. The trade-off differs from training: serving workloads are more fragmented (dynamic batch/seq, stochastic arrivals), so maintaining many hand-written kernels tuned for one shape is often a bad bargain. More reliable gains usually come from system strategy: reducing KV bandwidth with GQA [Ainslie2023GQA], or reducing the number of serial decode steps with speculative decoding [Leviathan2022SpecDec]. Therefore, unless profiling shows the attention kernel still dominates, investing engineering time in KV-cache representation, scheduling, and decoding strategy is usually steadier than chasing another 10% kernel peak.1e:T628,“O(L^2) attention is misaligned with long context” is an intuition, but current evidence does not support treating it as a universal default path. Second, architectural changes transfer poorly: Narang et al. The steadier engineering read is to treat them as structural options for specific regimes, not blanket replacements for dense attention.
