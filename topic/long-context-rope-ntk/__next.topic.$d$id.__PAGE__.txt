9:T6f0,Two pragmatic paths are clear. For existing models at 32K–128K: default to YaRN’s per-dim ramp + attention temperature, and budget explicitly for “a few-hundred-step long-sequence finetune + tiered acceptance on RULER/LV-Eval.” PI’s global interpolation is fine for bring-up/control, but it compresses high-frequency dimensions, reducing local-pattern resolution in ways that are hard to fully recover.[Peng2023YaRN][Chen2023PI][Hsieh2024RULER][Yuan2024LVEval]
For new/continual pretraining to 128K: set RoPE base directly near the target-window scale (often ~500000 in practice), then run a short-to-long curriculum (e.g., 8K→16K→32K→64K→128K) that mixes long-sequence tokens by stages; this matches the analysis that base limits low-frequency phase coverage and thus the learnable effective-length upper bound.[Dubey2024Llama3][Xu2024RoPEBaseBounds][Fu2024DataEngineering128K]
For 512K–2M targets: single global scaling starts to show per-dim mismatch; LongRoPE learns non-uniform per-dim scales via search and uses a larger finetune budget to fit errors by dimension, behaving like a more controllable engineering route.[Ding2024LongRoPE]

c:T782,For extending pretrained models to 32K–128K, the core trade-off is whether to sacrifice high-frequency local resolution to keep angles in-range. PI [Chen2023PI] globally interpolates all positions back into the trained range; it is fast for engineering bring-up, but also compresses high-frequency dimensions, effectively coarsening phase resolution for short-range relative shifts. In long-context tasks, this damage often appears as local-pattern confusion (variable names, formatting, short-span constraints); later fine-tuning struggles to fully recover because the high-frequency scale of the representation space has already been rewritten.

YaRN [Peng2023YaRN] is not mainly “another scaling formula”; it budgets interpolation by frequency band. The per-dim ramp mainly adjusts low-frequency dimensions so they keep enough phase coverage at long positions, while high-frequency dimensions stay close to the original scale to preserve local-pattern resolution. It also introduces attention temperature to counter entropy collapse in long-range attention logits (over-sharp or over-flat long-range logits both destabilize retrieval/aggregation); on RULER variable-tracking/aggregation/multi-hop chains, this more directly appears as effective-context growth across length tiers.[Hsieh2024RULER][Yuan2024LVEval]

A pragmatic engineering read is: PI is suitable as a control or quick bring-up; a shippable plan must hard-code acceptance on multi-tier length curves, not just the max-length needle.[Chen2023PI][Goldman2024IsItRetrieval] Goldman et al. [Goldman2024IsItRetrieval] should also shape acceptance design: many “long-context tasks” can be approximated by retrieval, and single-point needles are easier to game with prompt engineering or retrieval shortcuts. Retrofit acceptance should prioritize task families that require cross-segment aggregation and multi-hop tracing.[Hsieh2024RULER][An2023LEval]d:T66a,At 512K–2M targets, the main failure mode shifts from “angle overflow” to per-dim mismatch: different frequency bands develop different error shapes at extreme positions, and one ramp/scaling triggers two problems at once—low-frequency phase coverage remains insufficient, so far positions are less separable; high-frequency dimensions are over-compressed, so local patterns degrade.[Ding2024LongRoPE][Liu2023RoPEScalingLaws] Thus, smoothing formulas that look “good enough” at ≤128K can fail on million-token tails: the fix is not another turn of the same knob, but explicit modeling of how much extrapolation each dimension needs.

LongRoPE [Ding2024LongRoPE] operationally expands the hyperparameter space from a few global coefficients into a searchable/learnable per-dim scale pattern, then uses a larger finetune budget to fit dimension-level errors. It turns “extreme window” from a one-off trick into a tunable space: search for patterns that are stable on the target task family, then long-sequence finetune to realign attention distributions and representation geometry.

A frequently underestimated point: 512K+ shipping risk often comes from evaluations that are not “dirty” enough. Suites like ∞Bench [Zhang2024InfinityBench], Lifelong ICL/Task Haystack [Xu2024TaskHaystack], and NoCha [Karpinska2024NoCha]—longer, more distractive, and requiring cross-segment aggregation—surface “local degradation” and “long-tail recall failures” earlier. If you only use needles or retrieval-solvable tasks, both LongRoPE’s benefits and risks get underestimated.[Goldman2024IsItRetrieval][Hsieh2024RULER]e:T62a,Bypass routes share one pattern: move “long history” out of full attention—either by changing the architecture (SSM/sparse/recurrent) or by adding external memory/compression/retrieval. Mamba [Gu2023Mamba], Longformer [Beltagy2020Longformer], LongNet [Ding2023LongNet], RMT [Bulatov2023RMT], Landmark Attention [Mohtashami2023Landmark], and Activation Beacon [Zhang2024ActivationBeacon] deliver clear wins in complexity, memory, or throughput; system-side FlashAttention-2 [Dao2023FlashAttention2] and Ring Attention [Liu2023RingAttention] make “running long” operational.

But “running long” is not “reliably recalling.” Zoology [Arora2023Zoology] indicates that many efficient models still lag attention on recall-sensitive tasks. Kuratov et al. [Kuratov2024Needles11M] shows recurrent memory can recover facts in ultra-long haystacks that transformers miss, but these results also expose an acceptance trap: if the task is mostly single-point retrieval, bypass methods look strong; once tasks require cross-segment aggregation, multi-hop chains, or robustness to distractors, gaps reappear.[Hsieh2024RULER][Karpinska2024NoCha]

A more reliable shipping strategy is to treat bypass as a “system budget lever,” not the default replacement for native long context: within the same model family, first establish effective-context curves for native long-context or YaRN retrofit on RULER/LV-Eval/NoCha, then test whether retrieval/compression/memory reduces cost without degrading recall-heavy metrics.[Hsieh2024RULER][Yuan2024LVEval][Jiang2023LongLLMLingua]f:T836,可执行的清单（带边界与不要做的事）：
